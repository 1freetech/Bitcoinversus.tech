## Primary evidence, independent confirmation and chronology
- October 29, 2026: Vineyard Wind 1 reached full commercial operation. The actual project spokesperson Craig Gilvarg confirmed the date by email to WBUR, published November 6: https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/11/06/vineyard-wind-fully-running-offshore-wind-massachusetts . This is first-hand company confirmation reported by an established independent outlet; the developer had not posted its own distinct November commercial-operations release on its news page when checked.
- Massachusetts Division of Marine Fisheries November 2, 2026 mariner update: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massachusetts-division-of-marine-fisheries-offshore-wind , describes all Vineyard Wind turbines installed, previously commissioning and maintenance ongoing; the project spokesperson's October 29 report is the more specific full-commercial completion source. Earlier May 2026 state records show PPA activation April 27; full commissioning and earlier initial commercial supply must be distinguished.
- Vineyard Wind project description: https://www.vineyardwind.com/vineyardwind-1 , 62 GE Haliade-X 13 MW turbines, grid connections, offshore station, buried export cables. Project is routinely marketed and permitted as 800 MW, while 62 x 13 = 806 MW aggregate individual machine capacity; both are nameplate-type ratings, neither observed annual MWh.
- BOEM official project history: https://www.boem.gov/renewable-energy/state-activities/vineyard-wind-1 , July 2024 blade failure, manufacturer deviation, Jan 2025 revision approval and regulator operational release.
- Developer September 16, 2026 settlement with GE Vernova: https://www.vineyardwind.com/press-releases/2026/9/16-resolution-of-commercial-litigation . September statement 'all turbines capable' does not mean the October 29 commercial commissioning milestone had already occurred.
- Developer 2024 first-power chronology: https://www.vineyardwind.com/press-releases/2024/1/3/cip-avangrid-announce-first-power-from-nation-leading-vineyard-wind-1-project . First power to grid January 2, 2024.
