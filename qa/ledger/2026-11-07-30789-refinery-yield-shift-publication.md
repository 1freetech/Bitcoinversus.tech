## Primary source chronology and technical distinctions
- EIA analysis, published November 5, 2026: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68424 . Data from Petroleum Supply Monthly through August 2026. Gasoline refinery yield Jan-Aug averaged below 45%, below the 2021-2025 low in each month. Distillate yields above five-year average every month. Jet-fuel yield exceeded 12% for first time March, stayed at or above 12% through August.
- EIA monthly yield table https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pnp_pct_dc_nus_pct_m.htm. August 2026: finished motor gasoline 44.1%, distillate fuel oil 29.8%, kerosene-type jet fuel 12.0%. April 2026 jet fuel 12.5%.
- EIA October 22 refinery-utilization analysis https://www.eia.gov/TodayinEnergy/detail.php?id=68344. May-Sept 2026 utilization above 95% on average, highest for corresponding months since 1998. Utilization is not product yield; both distinct.
- Valero manufacturer educational resource https://www.valero.com/refining/oil-refining/refining-science-process . Explains crude boiling-point fractions, cracking, feedstock, configurations, diesel/jet production.
