## Original research and source status
- [Verified] ETH Zurich primary press release October 22, 2026: https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2026/10/one-small-leap-for-a-muscle-one-giant-step-for-robotics.html
- [Verified] Original peer-reviewed paper by Balciunaite, Michelis et al., Nature Communications, published September 11, 2026: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77655-1
- [Verified] Living mouse skeletal muscle cultivated on light-printed hydrogel scaffold; both computationally co-optimized with muscle placement for larger range of motion. Research team's optimized designs generated up to 11× range of motion compared with previous actuators of comparable muscle volume. Relative maximum, not population average.
- [Verified] Centimeter-scale ~1.5 cm actuators; reported 5 mm jump in one model, 6.2 mm/s walker in another, not a single actuator simultaneously achieving all observed task maxima.
- [Verified] Experimental robots still rely on nutrient solution; scaling requires oxygen/nutrient perfusion and managing variable biology, so no commercial deployment asserted.
