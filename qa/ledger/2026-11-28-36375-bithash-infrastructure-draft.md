<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>United Arab Emirates Bitcoin infrastructure provider BitHash is pursuing additional air-cooled and hydro-cooled mining capacity in the UAE and United States, even as the company expands toward enterprise computing. A <a href="https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/uaes-bithash-targets-ai-data-centers-eyes-250mw-project-in-oman/">November 27 report by Data Center Dynamics</a> describes the dual strategy. The company's <a href="https://bithash.ae/datacenter-construction">construction portfolio</a> separately identifies a hydro-cooled expansion at Jebel Ali, Dubai, with a stated electrical capacity of 6 MVA and eight kilometers of underground cabling. BitHash classifies the project as under construction, although it does not provide a dated commissioning report.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Electrical capacity and the power factor</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>A six-megavolt-ampere rating describes apparent electrical power rather than the real power available to mining machines. At an illustrative power factor of 0.95, a 6 MVA system carries 5.7 MW of real power. At a hypothetical facility power usage effectiveness of 1.05, roughly 5.43 MW could reach computing equipment. Neither assumption is a measured value for Jebel Ali. Transformer loading, protection settings, distribution losses and cooling demand determine the practical operating limit.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The distinction matters for equipment procurement. A 5.43 MW IT load operated at an illustrative 15 J/TH would provide approximately 362 PH/s of hashing capacity before downtime. The model describes a hypothetical configuration, not BitHash's actual hashrate or equipment order. Similar electrical distinctions appear in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/23/bitzero-namsskogan-norway-40mw-bitcoin-mining-110mw-transformers-2026/">Bitzero transformer expansion in Norway</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/23/ohio-bitdeer-massillon-bitcoin-mine-221mw-fully-online-2026/">Bitdeer's Massillon mining campus</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Cooling in a Gulf climate</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>BitHash describes a closed-loop liquid cooling system with external heat-rejection equipment for the Jebel Ali project. Hydro-cooled ASICs transfer heat from chips into circulating coolant through internal cold plates. Pumps move the coolant toward a heat exchanger or external cooling equipment, where energy is released to the environment. Unlike immersion systems, hydro-cooled miners normally retain their electronics outside the coolant stream.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>High outdoor temperatures constrain dry-cooler performance by reducing the temperature difference available for heat transfer. An operator must size the heat-rejection system for peak summer conditions, provide adequate coolant flow and maintain water chemistry, leak detection and pressure monitoring. Heat can also become a useful secondary output when a nearby customer has compatible thermal demand, as demonstrated by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/23/austria-hashlynk-ecohash-nine-bitcoin-miners-solar-heat-reuse-2026/">an Austrian mining heat-reuse project</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Oman remains a separate proposal</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The company's <a href="https://bithash.ae/blog/ai-colocation-and-institutional-bitcoin-mining-under-one-strategy">November strategy article</a> outlines a 250 MW Oman AI campus with a 2030 target. A <a href="https://bithash.ae/blog/ai-hpc-colocation-uae-tier-iii-data-centres-bithash">separate company publication</a> describes a potential 230 MW development around 2035. The disclosures do not establish whether both figures represent the same proposal. Neither version confirms secured financing, approved power connections or an operational campus.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>AI colocation and Bitcoin mining share requirements for power delivery and heat management, but network architecture, service obligations and hardware differ. Projects such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/26/soluna-project-annie-74mw-bitcoin-114mw-solar-texas-2026/">Soluna's proposed Project Annie</a> likewise illustrate why announced capacity must be distinguished from energized capacity. The next meaningful evidence for Jebel Ali would be an interconnection confirmation, commissioning tests, installed ASIC specifications and measured operating load. Until those details emerge, the project remains a company-reported construction initiative.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
