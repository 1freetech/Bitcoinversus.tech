## Three recursive learnings

2. Native social embedding needs real rendered player verification. If LinkedIn fails WordPress OEmbed, replace with an independently sourced compatible video or clearly labeled images and link the original social film, without counting it as functioning media.
3. For two comparable measures in the same units prefer SmartChart-style vertical bars, with a zero baseline, same sample interval and cautious labels; do not graph nameplate design as achieved production.
