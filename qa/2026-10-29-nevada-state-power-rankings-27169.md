Tract is land developer, not operating data center, per Nevada LCB https://www.leg.state.nv.us/App/InterimCommittee/REL/Document/33075 .
2. Tract South Valley, Storey County: **1,215 MW whole park ultimate potential**, 1,534 acres, seven parcels; official https://www.tract.com/project/south-valley/ . Actual first Fleet construction is ~**230MW total facility** with **200MW critical IT**, source https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/fleet-breaks-ground-on-data-center-outside-reno-nevada/ and https://www.nnbw.com/news/2026/jun/02/fleet-data-centers-breaks-ground-on-2-facilities/ . 144MW temporary gas generation proposed and conditionally approved; not additional IT demand. Separate Peru Ridge ~230MW is NOT included in South Valley's 1,215MW.
