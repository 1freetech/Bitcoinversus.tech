**#1 Nebius Independence AI Factory — up to 1,200 MW project power envelope**
- The developer's original March 3 2026 announcement https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-secures-approval-for-its-first-gigawatt-scale-ai-factory specifies up to 1.2GW. State primary release May 12 2026 https://ded.mo.gov/press-room/nebius-breaks-ground-gigawatt-scale-ai-factory-independence confirms first-phase groundbreaking.
- The current official project FAQ https://nebius.com/independencemo provides explicit split: **800 MW from municipal Independence Power & Light**, involving new private generation at the retired Blue Valley Power Plant connected to the Southwest Power Pool, and **400 MW of proposed on-site generation** for a future phase. 800+400=1200 is not 1200MW of currently active GPU load, not all presently energized; Nebius is still evaluating on-site generation. Existing municipal contract statements/ratepayer protection are developer and public-utility claims, not universal independent guarantee. 400-acre site, four planned buildings; up to five to seven years of phased work.
- **Important regression**: do not confuse 1.2GW planned total supply mix with full contracted grid demand, nor misstate emergency backup generators as continuously supplying 400MW.
