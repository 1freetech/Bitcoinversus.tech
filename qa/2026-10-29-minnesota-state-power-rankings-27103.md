Google official Feb 24 disclosure https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/data-center-pine-island/ actually describes separate **1,400 MW wind + 200 MW solar + 300 MW battery storage**, which sum to 1,900 MW supply-side nameplate resource capacities—not data-center load. Do not add energy storage or generation to site power. CUB puts proposed site demand at **780 MW**. Verified against independent CUB reporting and Google original release. Future power lint should flag wind+solar+storage rollups substituted for campus MW.
