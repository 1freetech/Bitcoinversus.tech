# Bitcoin Solo Mining Lands Three Blocks in 22 Hours

Published September 27, 2026.
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitcoin-solo-mining-lands-three-blocks-in-22-hours/

Three blocks attributed to NiceHash EasyMining landed within roughly 22 hours on September 25 and 26, delivering a combined 9.4474 BTC in block rewards and fees.

Block 968,495 landed at 04:12 UTC September 25 with 3.1336 BTC. Block 968,528 followed at 08:47 UTC with 3.1587 BTC. Block 968,621 arrived at 02:18 UTC September 26 with 3.1551 BTC.

The tracked hashrate was around 4 EH/s, so “solo” describes the payout structure rather than a single home ASIC. At that scale the tracker estimates a longer-term expected interval of roughly two days per block.

These blocks were mined around Bitcoin difficulty 132.76 trillion. The streak illustrates mining variance: hashrate buys probability, not certainty.
