# Cerebras and Gimlet Plan 100 MW of Wafer-Scale AI Inference

Cerebras Systems and Gimlet Labs plan roughly 100 MW of CS-4 wafer-scale AI inference capacity. Gimlet is a CS-4 launch partner and plans to make the first Cerebras-powered capacity available through Gimlet Cloud in 2027.
