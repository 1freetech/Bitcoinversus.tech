# CleanSpark Closes $2.276 Billion for 175 MW AI Campus

Published: September 25, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/cleanspark-closes-2-276-billion-for-175-mw-ai-campus/

CleanSpark closed **$2.276 billion** of 7.875% senior secured notes due 2031 to finance completion of its Sandersville, Georgia data center. The notes were sold at 98.5% of principal.

## A Bitcoin miner finances a 175 MW AI campus

Sandersville is designed around approximately **175 MW of critical IT load** under a 20-year lease with Anviran, a wholly owned Meta subsidiary. Project disclosures put contracted base-term payments at roughly **$6.6 billion**, with a 3% annual escalator.

## The debt carries a large fixed infrastructure bill

A 7.875% coupon on $2.276 billion implies approximately **$179.2 million of annual coupon interest** before amortization and other financing effects.

## Mining infrastructure becomes AI infrastructure

CleanSpark built its operating model around Bitcoin mining and says it controls more than **1.8 GW** of U.S. power, land and data-center infrastructure. Sandersville demonstrates how utility interconnections, substations, transformers, switchgear, cooling and networking developed by miners can be monetized for hyperscale AI.

## The energized megawatt is becoming the strategic asset

ASICs and GPUs can be replaced as technology advances. High-voltage grid connections, land, substations and permitted power capacity can remain productive across multiple generations of compute.
