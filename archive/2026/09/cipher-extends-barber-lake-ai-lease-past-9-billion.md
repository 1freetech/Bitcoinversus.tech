# Cipher Extends Barber Lake AI Lease Past $9 Billion

Published: September 25, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/cipher-extends-barber-lake-ai-lease-past-9-billion/

Cipher Digital has extended the contracted life of its 300 MW Barber Lake data center in Colorado City, Texas from 10 years to 20 years, adding approximately **$5.2 billion** of contracted revenue and taking the site's total above **$9 billion**.

The September 25 agreements pair an amendment to Cipher's existing Fluidstack lease with a binding commitment from a leading AI lab for an additional 10-year term.

## A 300 MW computing campus with a 20-year contract horizon

Cipher says Barber Lake's total contracted revenue rises from $3.8 billion to more than $9 billion. The additional AI-lab commitment is expected to generate roughly $5.2 billion over its 10-year term.

Barber Lake has 300 MW of gross capacity. Cipher's annual filing describes 244 MW allocated to Fluidstack in Phase I and the remaining 56 MW in Phase II.

## Delivery now runs into early 2027

Individual data halls are expected to be delivered from Q4 2026 through Q1 2027, with rent beginning as each hall is delivered.

Cipher will bear the first $359.3 million of costs above the initial budget. The tenant will reimburse 50% of costs beyond that threshold over the aggregate 20-year term.

## Bitcoin mining built the power-development skill set

Cipher emerged as an industrial Bitcoin miner, developing expertise in large electrical interconnections, substations, transformers, switchgear, networking and cooling. Those capabilities are now being monetized for AI/HPC tenants.

## Cipher still operates a major Bitcoin mine

Cipher's infrastructure portfolio lists its 207 MW Odessa site at approximately **11.6 EH/s**, demonstrating the hybrid direction emerging among major miners.

Barber Lake reinforces a central mining-infrastructure trend: long-lived access to land, high-voltage power and grid interconnection can support whichever compute workload offers the strongest economics.
