# Destro AI Raises $8 Million to Coordinate Warehouse Robots

Destro AI raised $8 million in seed funding to scale software that coordinates heterogeneous warehouse robots, people and workflows.

## Core facts
- Seed round co-led by Base10 Partners and Bonfire Ventures
- CoFound Partners also participated
- Funding will support enterprise deployments plus engineering and research
- MothershipOS coordinates multi-robot workflows at the warehouse level
- VisionOS handles robot intelligence trained on human demonstration data
- Destro positions the platform as robot-agnostic rather than tied to one hardware vendor
