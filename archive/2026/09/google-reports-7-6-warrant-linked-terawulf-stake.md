# Google Reports 7.6% Warrant-Linked TeraWulf Stake

Published: September 26, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/26/google-reports-7-6-warrant-linked-terawulf-stake/

Google LLC, XXVI Holdings and Alphabet filed a Schedule 13G on September 25 reporting beneficial ownership of **41,011,803 TeraWulf shares** issuable through warrants held directly by Google LLC, representing **7.6%** of the filing's calculated share base.

The warrants are currently exercisable or exercisable within 60 days. The disclosure therefore should not be read as Google buying 41 million common shares on September 25.

## What Google's 7.6% disclosure means

The Schedule 13G lists the reporting persons with shared voting and dispositive power over 41,011,803 shares. The ownership calculation uses 498,968,677 TeraWulf common shares outstanding as of July 31 plus the warrant shares.

## Lake Mariner connects Bitcoin mining infrastructure to AI

The warrants originate from Google's support for Fluidstack's Lake Mariner leases. TeraWulf previously disclosed more than 200 MW of AI-optimized capacity backed by $1.8 billion of Google credit support. A later 160 MW CB-5 expansion increased contracted Fluidstack capacity to roughly 360 MW and Google's total backstop to approximately $3.2 billion.

Lake Mariner was developed around utility-scale Bitcoin mining infrastructure and now supports high-density AI/HPC. TeraWulf has highlighted dual 345 kV transmission lines, water cooling and low-latency connectivity.

## TeraWulf's infrastructure mix keeps changing

In Q2 2026 TeraWulf reported $31.9 million of HPC lease revenue out of $44.8 million total revenue, about 71%, and 102 MW of revenue-generating critical IT capacity at Lake Mariner with another 336 MW under construction.
