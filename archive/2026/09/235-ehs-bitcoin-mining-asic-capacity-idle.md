# 235 EH/s of Bitcoin Mining ASIC Capacity Is Sitting Idle

Published: 2026-09-29
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/235-ehs-bitcoin-mining-asic-capacity-idle/
Featured media: 19406 — unique BitcoinVersus.Tech illustration generated for this story.

Luxor's August 2026 hashrate analysis estimates roughly 235 EH/s of Bitcoin ASIC capacity is outside active network production. The estimate compares approximately 1,150 EH/s of net ASIC capacity with roughly 915 EH/s of activity implied by August's average difficulty.

The figure includes uneconomic machines, deliberately curtailed fleets, hardware in transit and ASICs under maintenance. It is not a claim that 235 EH/s of equipment is broken.

## 235 EH/s Is a Large Reserve of Potential Competition

Idle hashrate is different from retired hashrate. Modern ASICs switched off for power-market events, relocation or repair can return when conditions improve. BitcoinVersus.tech recently reported that [Bitcoin difficulty reached 132.76T](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/bitcoin-difficulty-hits-132-76t-as-asic-efficiency-matters-more/).

## August's Hashprice Recovery Changed the Restart Math

Luxor says dollar-denominated hashprice increased 24.4% during August, from $31.63 to $39.33 per PH/s/day. Older 25–38 J/TH equipment still generated roughly $45/MWh on average, below Luxor's $48/MWh benchmark electricity cost.

That reinforces the importance of [PowerCompute's S19 XP refresh](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/powercompute-gets-39-more-hashrate-per-miner-swap/) and newer ASIC efficiency.

## Texas Can Put Curtailed Hashrate Back Into Play

Luxor notes the final Texas Four Coincident Peak is established in September. After the summer 4CP window ends, curtailed equipment can become a candidate to restart.

Related BitcoinVersus.tech coverage includes [Cipher Digital's 3.2 GW ERCOT power queue](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/cipher-digitals-3-2-gw-ercot-power-queue-turns-bitcoin-mining-sites-into-ai-capacity/) and [Bitdeer's 35 MW Project Kati deployment](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/bitdeer-project-kati-bitcoin-mining-35-mw/).

## October Has Historically Added Difficulty

Luxor reports that October has delivered positive difficulty growth every year since 2022, averaging about 4.38% per adjustment epoch and roughly 10% across the month. This is historical seasonality, not a forecast.

## Not Every Idle ASIC Will Return

The estimate does not predict all sidelined capacity will switch on. Different categories have different restart paths. BitcoinVersus.tech's examination of [Bitcoin testing estimated mining-cost levels near $85,000](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/26/bitcoin-tests-85k-mining-cost-after-280-days-below/) describes another part of the same economics.
