# Rocket Lab Launches 97th Electron Mission and 13th StriX Satellite

Rocket Lab completed its 97th Electron mission, delivering Synspective's 13th StriX synthetic aperture radar satellite into a 559-kilometer low Earth orbit.

The mission, “Owlright Owlright Owlright,” was Rocket Lab's 18th launch of 2026 and its 13th dedicated Electron launch for Synspective.
