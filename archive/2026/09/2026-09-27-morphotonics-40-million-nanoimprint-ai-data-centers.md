# Morphotonics Raises €40 Million to Push Nanoimprint Tech Into AI Data Centers

Dutch deep-tech company Morphotonics has raised more than €40 million in Series B funding as it scales large-area nanoimprint lithography for AR waveguides and expands into co-packaged optics for AI data centers.

The company says the funding will accelerate manufacturing technology for waveguides, CPO and next-generation photonic applications. TechCrunch reports that Morphotonics' next machine is expected to begin shipping in early 2027 and is designed for more than six million waveguides per year.

The data-center opportunity remains an expansion market: Morphotonics has not yet shipped machines into this segment, although it says customers have validated the technology.
