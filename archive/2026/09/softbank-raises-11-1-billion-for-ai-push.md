# SoftBank Raises $11.1 Billion for AI Push

Published: 2026-09-24T14:53:03

Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/24/softbank-raises-11-1-billion-for-ai-push/

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">SoftBank has raised <strong>$11.1 billion</strong> through dollar- and euro-denominated bonds as the technology conglomerate dramatically expands its investments across artificial intelligence infrastructure.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The transaction is the largest high-yield corporate bond sale globally on record, according to Reuters and LSEG data. A major purpose of the financing is funding the final $10 billion tranche of SoftBank&#8217;s $30 billion follow-on investment in <a href="https://openai.com/">OpenAI</a>. Once completed, SoftBank&#8217;s total investment in OpenAI is expected to reach approximately $64.6 billion.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But SoftBank&#8217;s strategy now reaches far beyond AI software.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The company is acquiring <a href="https://global.abb/group/en">ABB</a>&#8216;s robotics business for $5.4 billion and data-center investment company <a href="https://www.digitalbridge.com/">DigitalBridge</a> for $3.1 billion. Its broader AI strategy spans semiconductors, robotics, data centers and large language models.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The financing also connects directly with SoftBank&#8217;s enormous physical AI infrastructure ambitions. BitcoinVersus.tech previously covered the company&#8217;s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/05/07/softbank-and-doe-break-ground-on-500b-stargate-ai-data-center-ohio-campus/">Stargate AI data-center expansion</a>, part of an industry-wide race to secure computing capacity and electrical power for increasingly large AI systems.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The scale of the bond offering demonstrates another side of the AI infrastructure boom: building the next generation of computing requires enormous amounts of capital alongside chips, servers, power and data centers.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">SoftBank is positioning itself across nearly every layer.</p>
