# Hut 8 Beacon Point Clears Key ERCOT Power Step

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/hut-8-beacon-point-clears-key-ercot-power-step/

Hut 8's 1 GW Beacon Point AI data center in Nueces County, Texas, received a conditional Base Load classification in ERCOT's Batch Zero process, preserving a key path toward energization.

## Beacon Point clears a key Texas power checkpoint

Hut 8 said the classification is a step forward in Beacon Point's energization process. Independent reporting dated September 8 says ERCOT informed Hut 8's transmission provider that megawatts previously studied and allocated to Beacon Point remain allocated to the project.

## One gigawatt of utility capacity backs 704 MW of contracted IT load

Beacon Point has 1,000 MW of utility capacity under an interconnection agreement with AEP Texas. Hut 8 has two 15-year leases totaling 704 MW of critical IT capacity, carrying approximately $19.6 billion in aggregate base-term contract value.

The campus is designed around NVIDIA's DSX reference architecture.

## Bitcoin mining's power-first playbook

Hut 8's corporate materials describe Beacon Point as a site originally secured on an ASIC-compute thesis before being repositioned for hyperscale AI, illustrating the overlap between Bitcoin mining power infrastructure and AI data-center development.
