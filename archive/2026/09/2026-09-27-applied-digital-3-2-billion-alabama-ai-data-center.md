# Applied Digital Reveals $3.2 Billion Alabama AI Data Center

Applied Digital has confirmed that its previously undisclosed Delta Forge 2 AI Factory campus will be built near Brookwood in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama.

The newly disclosed location turns a previously anonymous 210 MW lease into a specific $3.2 billion Alabama infrastructure project. The campus is planned at roughly 1.26 million square feet across about 160 acres of a nearly 1,300-acre site, with operations targeted for 2028.

The 210 MW is contracted under a roughly 15-year take-or-pay lease with an unnamed high investment-grade U.S. hyperscaler, representing approximately $5.2 billion of base-term contracted revenue.

Applied Digital says construction employment should peak above 1,000 workers and the operating campus should support more than 100 full-time positions. The company committed $270 million in community-benefit payments over 20 years and projects about $131.5 million in education taxes over the same period.

The September development is distinct from the June lease announcement: Brookwood is now publicly identified as the host community, with a disclosed local footprint, investment estimate and community-benefit structure.
