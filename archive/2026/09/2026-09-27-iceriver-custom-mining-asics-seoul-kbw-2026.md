# ICERIVER Takes Custom Mining ASICs to Seoul

ICERIVER is bringing its ASIC chip-customization team to Korea Blockchain Week 2026 in Seoul from September 30 through October 1. The company offers algorithm-specific chip design, hardware optimization and manufacturing coordination to outside proof-of-work projects.

Story X embed: https://twitter.com/IceRiverMiner

## The custom-ASIC business has already shipped 3,000 machines

BGIN launched the business in May and said its first customer project produced 3,000 customized proof-of-work mining machines. 2,400 were delivered directly to the customer and 600 were allocated for ICERIVER retail distribution.

YouTube prototype teardown: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xNXLeYvfDDM

## BGIN is also developing Bitcoin silicon

BGIN completed tape-out of its 4 nm BT1 Bitcoin mining ASIC in March 2026 and reported first-pass silicon success. Prototype hardware has demonstrated real SHA-256 mining.

## ICERIVER mines more than Bitcoin

BGIN has developed mining hardware for Kaspa, Alephium and Aleo as well as Bitcoin. Its business includes mining-hardware sales, cryptocurrency mining, hosting and external ASIC engineering.
