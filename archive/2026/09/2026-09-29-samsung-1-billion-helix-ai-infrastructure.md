# Samsung Commits $1 Billion to Helix AI Infrastructure

Samsung Electronics and five affiliates are committing a combined $1 billion to Helix Digital Infrastructure. Samsung Electronics will invest $500 million, with five Samsung financial and construction affiliates providing the remaining $500 million.

The strategic relationship spans semiconductors, construction, energy storage, cooling and capital. Helix now has more than $11 billion of secured capital behind its integrated AI-infrastructure strategy.
