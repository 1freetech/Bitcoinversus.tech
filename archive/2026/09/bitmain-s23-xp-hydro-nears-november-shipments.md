## Summary

BITMAIN is moving its ANTMINER S23 XP Hyd. toward November 2026 shipments. The manufacturer lists the hydro-cooled SHA-256 ASIC at 600 TH/s, 5,340 W and 8.9 J/TH, with a listed futures price of $25.50/TH (about $15,300 per 600 TH/s unit).

The article explains how sub-9 J/TH efficiency affects large-scale Bitcoin mining infrastructure, including power density, cooling and fixed-megawatt capacity. It also notes that the specifications are manufacturer ratings for a futures product rather than BitcoinVersus.tech measurements from a shipping production unit.
