# Braiins Price Adapt Automates ASIC Power Targets as Free Access Ends

Published: 2026-09-29
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/braiins-price-adapt-automates-asic-power-targets/
Featured media: 19449 — unique BitcoinVersus.Tech Price Adapt / ERCOT control-room illustration.

Braiins is approaching a September 30 cutoff for free early access to Price Adapt, a Braiins Manager feature that changes Bitcoin miner power targets automatically as electricity prices, hashprice and ASIC efficiency move through the day.

Primary sources:
- https://braiins.com/blog/braiins-manager-price-adapt-automated-bitcoin-mining-profitability-optimization
- https://academy.braiins.com/braiins-manager/price-adapt

Supported Braiins OS machines can be overclocked, underclocked, left at their current target or paused. Stock-firmware and unsupported machines can still be paused and resumed, but they do not receive granular power-target optimization.

Braiins models the feature against historical ERCOT LZ_WEST day-ahead prices, seven-day trailing hashprice and miner efficiency curves. Its published modeled uplifts are scenarios rather than guaranteed field performance.
