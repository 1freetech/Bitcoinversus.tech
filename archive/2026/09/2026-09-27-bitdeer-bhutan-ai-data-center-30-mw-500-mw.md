# Bitdeer Plans 30 MW Bhutan AI Data Center With Path to 500 MW

Bitdeer disclosed a non-binding LOI on September 16, 2026 to develop an AI data center in Gelephu Mindfulness City, Bhutan. The initial target is 30 MW of renewable hydro/solar-powered capacity with a potential expansion pathway to 500 MW.

Bitdeer already lists 100 MW at Gedu and 500 MW at Jigmeling in Bhutan, both associated with carbon-free Bitcoin mining/data-center infrastructure. The Gelephu proposal extends that renewable-power strategy into AI compute.

The company has not yet disclosed the Gelephu GPU platform, customer, rack count, cooling architecture, capex or energization date. The 500 MW figure is an expansion pathway, not energized capacity.
