# German Farm Pairs 2 MW Solar With Hydro Bitcoin Mining

A farm near Hamburg, Germany, has paired ground-mounted solar generation with sealed 36-slot, 200 kW hydro-cooled Antminer cabinets to isolate ASIC hardware from agricultural dust.

APEXTO's September 23 case study says the site is currently limited to 2 MW of solar capacity and has deployed ten cabinets. It is pre-wired for six additional cabinets as the solar installation expands to 3.2 MW next year.

Each AP-A36 cabinet supports up to 36 hydro miners and integrates the rack, circulation pump, electrical switching, PLC controls and network equipment with an external 200 kW dry cooler. The published product specification lists up to 226 kW system load and 20 m3/h rated coolant flow.

APEXTO reports a 35% fan-noise reduction at the Hamburg deployment. This is a vendor case-study claim, not an independently audited acoustic measurement.

The case study does not disclose whether grid power, batteries, curtailment logic or ASIC underclocking are used outside peak solar hours, so the actual renewable-energy share and miner utilization cannot be established from the published information.
