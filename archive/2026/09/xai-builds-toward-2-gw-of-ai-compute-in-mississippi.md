# xAI Builds Toward 2 GW of AI Compute in Mississippi

Published: 2026-09-24T16:25:10

Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/24/xai-builds-toward-2-gw-of-ai-compute-in-mississippi/

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">xAI is building toward nearly <strong>2 gigawatts of AI computing power</strong> across its Greater Memphis and Southaven infrastructure, turning the Tennessee-Mississippi state line into one of the world&#8217;s largest concentrated AI compute projects.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Mississippi announced more than <strong>$20 billion</strong> of xAI investment in Southaven in January. The company&#8217;s MACROHARDRR data center is part of a broader cluster that xAI says will push its Colossus training infrastructure toward roughly 2 GW.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Power is becoming the defining engineering problem. Mississippi regulators approved permanent generation capable of supplying approximately <strong>1.2 GW</strong>, while xAI says temporary mobile turbines are being removed as the permanent power plant comes online.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At this scale, AI infrastructure begins to resemble the largest Bitcoin mining campuses: substations, generation, cooling, high-density computing equipment and enormous electrical interconnections become as important as the chips themselves.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The development follows the same infrastructure trend BitcoinVersus.tech highlighted in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/24/alibaba-targets-20-gw-of-data-center-capacity-by-2032/">Alibaba&#8217;s 20 GW data-center target</a>. The next phase of AI competition is increasingly being measured in megawatts and gigawatts.</p>
