# Mexico Gets 31.68 MW Hydro Bitcoin Mining Facility

APEXTO disclosed a completed 31.68 MW hydro-cooled Bitcoin mining installation in Monterrey, Mexico on September 21, 2026.

The unnamed enterprise operator uses twelve AP-HC40-A480 40-foot hydro-cooling containers rated at 2.64 MW each. Each provides 480 Antminer Hydro slots, for 5,760 ASIC slots across the site. APEXTO has not disclosed the exact ASIC models, operator identity or aggregate hashrate.

The sealed closed-loop architecture isolates miners from airborne industrial particulates and uses external dry coolers, stainless-steel headers, balance valves and coolant distribution equipment.
