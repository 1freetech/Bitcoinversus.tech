# AGM Group Advances Desktop Bitcoin Miner Toward Production

Published: September 23, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/23/agm-group-desktop-bitcoin-miner-production/

[AGM Group Holdings](https://agmhgroup.com/) is moving a jointly developed desktop Bitcoin mining device with HashBeaver from pilot production toward volume manufacturing and commercial sales. The compact node is intended for homes, offices and developers and uses a purpose-built application-specific integrated circuit dedicated to SHA-256 hashing.

The project entered pilot production on September 8. AGM says its next phase includes product testing, applicable certifications, manufacturing preparation and development of sales channels. The companies plan to align production batches and inventory with confirmed orders and actual sales-channel performance rather than treating pilot inventory as commercial revenue.

AGM is contributing system engineering, manufacturing quality management and supply-chain coordination. Earlier technical disclosures also assigned AGM work in system design, thermal engineering, hardware integration and reliability testing. HashBeaver is focused on ASIC development, algorithms, firmware optimization, mining-pool connectivity and the surrounding digital-infrastructure software.

The device's first-generation local ASIC compute is dedicated to Bitcoin SHA-256 mining. AGM explicitly says it will not perform general-purpose AI training or inference on the mining ASIC. Planned AI functions instead focus on operations, including recommendations based on temperature, power consumption and network conditions, anomaly detection and assistance with managing multiple devices.

Later software could add natural-language operations assistants that let users query device status, interpret alerts and receive operating recommendations. The companies are also evaluating cloud AI services accessed through companion software, keeping those workloads separate from the ASIC's Bitcoin hashing function.

Important hardware specifications remain unannounced. AGM and HashBeaver have not yet published validated hashrate, power consumption, noise, retail pricing or final delivery schedules. Those figures are expected after testing and certification progress, making the current milestone a manufacturing-readiness development rather than a completed product launch.

The desktop form factor places the project in a different segment from industrial container-scale mining fleets. Success will depend heavily on final efficiency, acoustics, thermal behavior, reliability and price because home and office miners operate under tighter noise, power and cooling constraints than large mining facilities.
