# Bitcoin Difficulty Hits 132.76T as ASIC Efficiency Matters More

Published: September 25, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/bitcoin-difficulty-hits-132-76t-as-asic-efficiency-matters-more/

Bitcoin mining difficulty jumped 4.16% on September 19 to **132.76 trillion**, tightening machine-level economics as operators deploy more efficient ASIC fleets.

Hashrate Index reported a seven-day network hashrate average near 946 EH/s and 30-day average near 934 EH/s on September 21.

## Difficulty directly squeezes ASIC output

When difficulty rises while Bitcoin price and transaction fees remain unchanged, each terahash earns less BTC. This raises the importance of joules per terahash.

## Hashprice remains near $40 per PH per day

September 25 dashboards showed hashprice around **$40/PH/s/day**. Hashrate Index's September 21 six-month forward market averaged about $38.96/PH/day.

At $40/PH/day, 1 PH/s generates roughly $40 gross daily revenue before electricity, pool fees, cooling, maintenance, downtime and financing.

## Efficiency decides how much power that revenue consumes

A 20 J/TH fleet needs about 20 kW per PH/s before facility overhead. A 10 J/TH fleet needs about 10 kW. At $0.05/kWh, machine-level electricity costs are approximately $24 and $12 per PH/day respectively.

## The next retarget could give miners some relief

Early estimates point toward a modest decline, but forecasts remain noisy until more of the 2,016-block epoch has elapsed.
