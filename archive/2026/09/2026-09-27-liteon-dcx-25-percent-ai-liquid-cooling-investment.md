# LITEON Takes 25% Stake in DCX to Pair AI Power With Liquid Cooling

LITEON Technology plans to acquire approximately 25% of Warsaw-based DCX Liquid Cooling Systems in a transaction valued at about $176 million.

The investment links LITEON's AI server power management, rack-level delivery and 800 VDC architecture with DCX coolant distribution units, facility distribution units, cold plates, dry coolers and immersion systems.

DCX currently markets ECDU systems from 0.6 to 2.6 MW and FDU systems from 5 to 8 MW. Its ECDU 1380/2600 H2 is specified for 1.38 to 2.6 MW of cooling capacity, flow up to 3,870 liters per minute and N+1 redundancy.

The companies say the partnership will focus on product development, engineering, manufacturing scale-up and global go-to-market work for integrated power-and-cooling platforms.
