# Cypherpunk Zcash Mining Fleet Reaches $125 Million Revenue Run Rate

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/cypherpunk-zcash-mining-fleet-reaches-125-million-revenue-run-rate/

Cypherpunk Technologies' Zcash mining fleet has moved from launch story to operating business. After ZEC rose sharply, outside analysis estimates the fleet near a $125 million annualized gross-revenue rate. The figure is revenue, not profit, and varies with ZEC price, network hashrate, difficulty, electricity, hosting and other costs.

## A $33.33 million ASIC fleet

Cypherpunk Mining launched in August through a $33.33 million equity-based transaction with Winklevoss Capital. The fleet brought roughly 4.2 GSol/s of Equihash hashrate online across U.S. facilities using Bitmain Z15 Pro machines.

Recent analysis estimates annualized gross revenue near $125 million. The estimate excludes electricity, hosting, equipment, pool and other operating costs.

## Network competition responds

The fleet represented about 18% of Zcash hashrate at launch. Its estimated share has since moved toward 15% as additional Equihash compute entered the network.

## Mined ZEC joins treasury

Cypherpunk's operating dashboard records 3,023.13 ZEC mined on August 31. The company combines ASIC mining production with a corporate ZEC treasury.
