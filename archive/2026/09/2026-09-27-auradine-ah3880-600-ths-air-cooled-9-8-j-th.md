# Auradine Ships 600 TH/s Air-Cooled Bitcoin Miner at 9.8 J/TH

Auradine's Teraflux AH3880 is shipping with a rated 600 TH/s hashrate, 9.8 J/TH efficiency and 5,880 W power draw in an air-cooled chassis. Auradine says the miner is designed in the United States and manufactured in Thailand.
