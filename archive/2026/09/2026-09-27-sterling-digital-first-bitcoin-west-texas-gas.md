# Sterling Digital Mines First Bitcoin From West Texas Gas

Sterling Digital recorded its first verified Bitcoin during live commissioning of its off-grid natural-gas-powered West Texas facility.

The September 2 regulatory update says two 2 MW Caterpillar natural-gas generators are delivering approximately 1.6 MW net power each to 420 ASIC mining servers. Once fully commissioned, the fleet is expected to provide about 193,500 TH/s, or 193.5 PH/s.

During the controlled live-load test, the site's generation system powered the ASIC fleet and the mined Bitcoin was deposited into Sterling's Coinbase custody account.

English YouTube interview: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IjLXUlJLOzk

Sterling has a five-year gas purchase agreement for up to 6,500 MMBtu per day from the WAHA pipeline in Martin County, Texas, expected to support up to 25 MW of computing capability.
