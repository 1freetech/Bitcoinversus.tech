# Bitcoin Miners Discover Their Most Valuable AI Asset Is Power

Published September 29, 2026.
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/bitcoin-miners-discover-their-most-valuable-ai-asset-is-power/

The most durable asset created by the Bitcoin mining boom may not be an ASIC at all. It may be the energized land, substations and grid access surrounding it. Reuters Breakingviews says the widening AI power shortage is putting a premium on infrastructure miners spent years assembling.

Reuters cites Morgan Stanley estimates for 68 GW of U.S. data-center power demand between 2026 and 2028 against projected supply roughly 38 GW lower. Bernstein previously estimated crypto miners control roughly 14 GW of operating and planned capacity.

Cipher Digital, TeraWulf and Hut 8 are increasingly developing powered shells, while IREN has pursued a more vertically integrated compute model. Reuters cites Jefferies research putting powered-shell revenue at as much as roughly $2 million per MW annually.
