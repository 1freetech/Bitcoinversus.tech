# XDOF Nears $1.2 Billion Valuation on Robot Training Data Boom

XDOF is reportedly in late-stage talks for a Series B financing that could value the robotics infrastructure startup at roughly $1.2 billion, less than three months after it emerged from stealth with a $70 million Series A.

The company is focused on the infrastructure around robot foundation models: teleoperation systems, data collection, robot operations, paired demonstrations and policy-training support.

TechCrunch reported that XDOF's annualized revenue is approaching $50 million and that 8VC is leading the discussions. The financing has not been announced as closed, so the final round size, valuation and investor terms could still change.

CEO Philipp Wu's June announcement said the company had raised $70 million and was open-sourcing ABC-130K, a large teleoperation dataset developed with collaborators from UC Berkeley, Carnegie Mellon, MIT and Amazon FAR.
