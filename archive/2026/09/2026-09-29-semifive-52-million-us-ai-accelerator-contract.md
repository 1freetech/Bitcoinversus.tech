# SEMIFIVE Wins $52 Million U.S. AI Accelerator Contract

South Korean custom-silicon company SEMIFIVE has signed a contract worth about $52 million with an unnamed U.S. AI fabless company to develop a next-generation data-center inference accelerator.

The chip is expected to exceed 500 mm², use LPDDR6 memory interfaces and PCIe Gen5 connectivity, and target tape-out in 2027. The program uses SEMIFIVE's end-to-end ASIC model spanning implementation, verification, packaging and manufacturing support.
