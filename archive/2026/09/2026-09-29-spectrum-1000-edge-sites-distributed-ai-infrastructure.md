# Spectrum Turns 1,000 Edge Sites Into Distributed AI Infrastructure

Spectrum is activating AI compute across a network that can reach more than 1,000 existing smaller edge facilities. The company says the footprint can place accelerated computing within roughly 10 milliseconds of 500 million connected devices.

Spectrum Edge Compute Infrastructure uses NVIDIA accelerated computing and is adding commercial relationships with CAST AI, HP and Hydra Host. World Wide Technology is demonstrating a robotics use case at SCTE TechExpo 2026.
