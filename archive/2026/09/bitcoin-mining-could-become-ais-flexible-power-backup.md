# Bitcoin Mining Could Become AI's Flexible Power Backup

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitcoin-mining-could-become-ais-flexible-power-backup/

CoinShares' September 22 industry update describes an emerging hybrid data-center architecture in which AI receives priority capacity while Bitcoin ASICs consume excess power and rapidly curtail when higher-value AI compute needs the electricity.

CoinShares says this could be especially useful with behind-the-meter generation, where Bitcoin mining can serve as a buyer of last resort and help keep generation assets utilized while AI demand fluctuates.

Its Q2 Bitcoin mining report estimates annualized AI profit around $1.5 million per MW under analyzed conditions versus roughly $500,000 per MW for Bitcoin mining.

The model remains emerging: CoinShares says it has not yet seen a major commercial contract explicitly implementing the shared AI-and-Bitcoin load structure.
