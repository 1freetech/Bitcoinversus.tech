# Atlantic HPC Takes 51 MW Bitcoin Mining Platform Toward Nasdaq

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/atlantic-hpc-takes-51-mw-bitcoin-mining-platform-toward-nasdaq/

Atlantic HPC Group has signed a definitive agreement to go public through a business combination with Aperture AC at a **$150 million pre-money equity value**.

## 51 MW of operating Bitcoin mining capacity

Atlantic reports 98 MW of utility-approved capacity across Oklahoma, Arkansas and Ohio: 51 MW classified as operating Bitcoin mining capacity and 47 MW under development for HPC, plus another 23 MW held for future development.

Its unaudited FY2026 figures include $28.6 million revenue, $4.4 million EBITDA, 1.9 EH/s hashrate and 303 BTC mined.

## Ohio mining site is part of the AI transition

Atlantic lists 20 MW of mining in Oklahoma City, 22 MW across two Arkansas sites and 9 MW allocated to mining in Alledonia, Ohio. The 9 MW Ohio block is currently offline while mining hardware is upgraded. The same location includes 26 MW of planned HPC development.

## Existing power connections are the core asset

Atlantic expects to provide powered-shell infrastructure while prospective HPC tenants supply cooling, racks, GPUs, servers, storage and networking. The strategy illustrates how utility interconnections and mining-site electrical infrastructure can become inputs to AI data-center development.

## Transaction

The combined company is expected to trade on Nasdaq as AHPC if the transaction closes. Closing is targeted for Q1 2027 and remains subject to shareholder, regulatory, exchange and other conditions.
