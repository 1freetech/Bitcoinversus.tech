# Grayscale Turns Its Bitcoin Miners ETF Into an AI Compute Fund

Published: 2026-09-30

Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/30/grayscale-bitcoin-miners-etf-ai-compute-gcpu/

WordPress Post ID: 19562
Featured Media ID: 19566

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Grayscale has turned a Bitcoin-miner equity fund into an AI-infrastructure fund, formally renaming the Grayscale Bitcoin Miners ETF (MNRS) as the Grayscale AI Compute ETF (GCPU).</strong></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3366332/0/en/grayscale-investments-introduces-grayscale-ai-compute-etf-ticker-gcpu-targeting-the-infrastructure-powering-ai-s-growth.html">The September 22 announcement</a> says GCPU now tracks the Indxx High Performance Computing Index and targets companies operating the physical infrastructure behind AI, including data centers, GPU cloud capacity and operators repurposing existing power and land.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>The change is more than a ticker swap: a fund built around Bitcoin-mining equities now follows a broader compute-infrastructure thesis.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">MNRS becomes GCPU</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Grayscale says the former MNRS fund and its underlying Bitcoin Miners Index were both renamed as part of the strategy change. The new index is designed around high-performance computing, AI cloud and accelerated-compute infrastructure rather than Bitcoin mining alone.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><a href="https://www.mutualfunds.com/news/2026/09/29/ai-trade-gets-a-three-way-split-this-week/">Independent ETF coverage</a> likewise describes GCPU as a rename of the existing Bitcoin Miners ETF rather than a new pool of capital, with the strategy shifting toward AI compute infrastructure effective September 22.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The transition fits a broader operational pattern already visible across mining. BitcoinVersus.tech recently examined how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitcoin-mining-could-become-ais-flexible-power-backup/">Bitcoin mining can act as flexible power infrastructure alongside AI loads</a>, particularly where miners already control energized sites and grid interconnections.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>GCPU reframes mining infrastructure as one branch of a larger market for energized compute capacity.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Bitcoin miners remain part of the AI-compute thesis</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Grayscale is not removing miners from the story. It says roughly half of the portfolio's target weight is aimed at companies built around GPU cloud and AI-hosting capacity, while the remainder includes companies with roots in other forms of high-performance computing, including Bitcoin mining, that have disclosed or executed transitions toward AI workloads.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><a href="https://x.com/Grayscale/status/2102381328880832751">The firm's launch post</a> makes the connection explicit: GCPU includes native data-center businesses plus operators repurposing existing power and land for AI, including Bitcoin miners.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/Grayscale/status/2102381328880832751","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"x","responsive":true} --><figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-rich is-provider-x wp-block-embed-x"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">https://twitter.com/Grayscale/status/2102381328880832751</div><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Grayscale's September 22 GCPU launch post says the portfolio includes AI-native data-center businesses and operators repurposing mining-era power and land for AI.</em></figcaption></figure><!-- /wp:embed -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>That transition is already occurring at operating sites. BitcoinVersus.tech reported that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/cango-starts-ai-gpu-compute-at-50-mw-bitcoin-mining-site/">Cango started AI GPU compute at a 50 MW Bitcoin-mining site</a>, demonstrating how an energized mining footprint can support a second compute workload.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>For investors, Grayscale is effectively treating grid access, data-center shells and power infrastructure as assets that can serve multiple compute markets.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Physical infrastructure is the investment target</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Grayscale argues that AI growth is constrained by physical compute capacity. Its launch material cites tight North American data-center availability and multi-year timelines for bringing new capacity online, while emphasizing the value of already energized infrastructure.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The same infrastructure logic is pushing mining-service companies beyond ASICs. BitcoinVersus.tech covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/luxor-bitcoin-mining-ai-infrastructure-2/">Luxor's expansion from Bitcoin mining into AI infrastructure</a>, including GPU and server sourcing alongside its established mining stack.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>GCPU does not invest directly in Bitcoin or other digital assets. According to Grayscale, any digital-asset exposure is indirect through companies in the index, and inclusion is based on high-performance-compute, AI-cloud or accelerated-compute activities rather than cryptocurrency ownership alone.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>The renamed fund therefore tracks a structural shift already underway in mining: the valuable asset may increasingly be the energized megawatt and the data-center infrastructure around it, not only the ASIC attached to it.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:separator --><hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" /><!-- /wp:separator -->
