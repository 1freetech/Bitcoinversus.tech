# Hut 8 Backs Texas Data Center Standards as Power-First Strategy Expands

Published: September 24, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/24/hut-8-texas-data-center-standards-power-first/

Hut 8 says it supports Texas' new data-center standards as the state tightens expectations around grid reliability, water conservation, community impact and infrastructure costs. The response is especially relevant because Hut 8 grew from Bitcoin mining into a broader energy and digital-infrastructure platform and is now developing one of Texas' largest AI data-center campuses.

Texas announced in August that Hut 8, Microsoft and Equinix had committed to comply with the state's data-center guidelines. Hut 8 separately said it welcomed the focus on grid reliability, local resources and community impacts.

## Hut 8's Power-First Model Moves Beyond Bitcoin Mining

Hut 8 describes its strategy as power-first. The company acquires and develops the electrical layer first, including powered land, interconnections, substations, switchyards and related systems, then builds digital infrastructure around that capacity. The approach grew out of capabilities closely associated with industrial Bitcoin mining: securing large blocks of power, constructing high-voltage infrastructure and operating energy-intensive compute.

The company's Beacon Point campus in Nueces County illustrates the scale of that transition. Hut 8 says the 525-acre site has 1,000 MW of utility capacity secured and is being developed specifically for AI workloads rather than cryptocurrency mining.

## Beacon Point Is Fully Commercialized

In July, Hut 8 signed a second 15-year lease for 352 MW of IT capacity at Beacon Point. The $9.8 billion base-term agreement doubled the same investment-grade tenant's contracted footprint to 704 MW and brought total base-term contract value for the campus to approximately $19.6 billion.

Across Hut 8's AI data-center portfolio, the company reported 949 MW of contracted IT capacity supported by 1,330 MW of utility capacity and approximately $26.6 billion of aggregate base-term contract value.

The infrastructure numbers matter for Bitcoin mining because they show how expertise developed around high-density mining power can migrate into a different compute market. ASIC mining and AI do not use identical facilities, but both require dependable utility capacity, transformers, switchgear, protection, distribution, cooling, networking and disciplined operations.

## Texas Standards Put Power and Water Under the Microscope

Texas' standards emphasize that large data centers should protect grid reliability, conserve water, respect surrounding communities and bear the infrastructure costs associated with their development. Those requirements are becoming more important as large-load interconnection requests expand across the ERCOT system.

Hut 8 says Beacon Point will not use municipal water for cooling. Its closed-loop cooling design is expected to circulate water through sealed piping, with the loop initially filled using water sourced outside Nueces County. The company says municipal water would be limited to ordinary building needs such as sinks and restrooms, estimated at roughly 10,000 gallons per day for each phase.

## A Bitcoin Mining Infrastructure Lesson

Hut 8's evolution demonstrates why power infrastructure can become more durable than any single generation of compute hardware. Bitcoin ASICs can be replaced as efficiency improves. AI accelerators will also cycle through new generations. A well-positioned interconnection, substation and utility relationship can support multiple generations and even multiple categories of computing.

For Bitcoin mining operators, the practical lesson is that site value increasingly depends on more than current hashrate. Interconnection quality, electrical architecture, land, cooling options, permitting, network connectivity and the ability to adapt a campus to future loads can determine whether a mining property remains useful as compute economics change.

Hut 8 continues to maintain direct exposure to Bitcoin mining while developing AI infrastructure. Its platform therefore offers a visible example of how mining companies can treat energy assets and data-center engineering as strategic infrastructure rather than merely the supporting equipment around ASICs.
