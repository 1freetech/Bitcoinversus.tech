# SolarEdge and Infineon Bring Microsecond Protection to 800 VDC AI Power

SolarEdge and Infineon are extending their collaboration into solid-state circuit breaker technology for 800 VDC AI and hyperscale data centers.

SolarEdge is leading the SSCB design while Infineon provides CoolSiC JFET technology. The companies say the protection devices are designed to interrupt DC faults within a few microseconds, addressing a key challenge between solid-state transformer conversion and the compute rack.

The underlying SolarEdge SST architecture is designed to convert 13.8–34.5 kV medium voltage directly to roughly 800–1500 VDC at greater than 99% claimed efficiency.

The companies have not disclosed commercial availability, pricing or deployment counts for the SSCB system, so the project remains a development program rather than a volume-shipping product.
