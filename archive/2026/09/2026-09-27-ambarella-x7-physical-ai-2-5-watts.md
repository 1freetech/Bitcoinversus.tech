# Ambarella X7 Adds Physical AI at 2 to 5 Watts

Ambarella introduced X7, its first standalone CVflow AI accelerator, on September 15, 2026. The sampling-stage chip is designed as a low-power co-processor for existing Arm and x86 systems rather than a replacement host platform.

X7 connects over PCIe Gen 3 x1 or USB 3.2 and operates in a 2-to-5-watt envelope in current customer designs. It supports CNNs, vision transformers, multimodal transformers and hybrid networks through Ambarella's third-generation CVflow architecture.

The XCalibur reference platform packages X7 as a 2280 M.2 card with LPDDR5 and PCIe throughput above 840 MB/s. Evaluation kits are available to qualified customers. Ambarella has not yet disclosed pricing, a headline TOPS figure or named its X7 OEM design customers.
