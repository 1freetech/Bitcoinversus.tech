# Bitcoin Miners Shift Megawatts From ASICs to AI

Published: September 25, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/bitcoin-miners-shift-megawatts-from-asics-to-ai/

Some of the largest publicly traded Bitcoin miners are physically reallocating energized megawatts, retiring ASIC hardware and converting mining campuses into high-density AI and HPC data centers.

CoinShares' Q2 2026 Bitcoin mining report estimates the listed sector's weighted-average ex-tax cash cost at about **$75,500/BTC** and says at least **35 EH/s** is scheduled to leave listed miners as several operators reduce or exit self-mining.

## IREN's AI revenue overtakes Bitcoin mining

IREN's June-quarter results show **$70.5 million** in AI Cloud Services revenue versus **$66.7 million** in Bitcoin mining revenue.

IREN recorded a **$450.4 million non-cash impairment**, primarily related to decommissioning Bitcoin mining hardware as sites are converted for AI Cloud growth, plus a $102.1 million reduction in the fair value of assets held for sale and $25.1 million in disposal losses.

CoinShares says IREN expects its transition from mining to AI to be substantially complete by December 31, 2026.

## TeraWulf is converting mining infrastructure too

TeraWulf's Q2 SEC filing records impairment charges related to shutting down mining infrastructure and ceasing Bitcoin mining operations to support its HPC transition. CoinShares reports two miner buildings at Lake Mariner were retired during H1 2026.

CoinShares calculates HPC lease revenue at about **71% of TeraWulf's $44.8 million quarterly revenue**. TeraWulf mined 179 BTC in Q2.

## The scarce asset is increasingly the energized site

Mining campuses can already control land, interconnection, substations, transformers, switchgear, fiber and large energized power blocks. CoinShares estimates U.S. interconnection queues around 2,600 GW and says data centers account for 87% of ERCOT's roughly 410 GW large-load queue.

## Not every major miner is abandoning SHA-256

Bitdeer continues investing in Bitcoin mining hardware and recently announced its Nevada SEALMINER factory. CoinShares identifies MARA, Riot, HIVE and Bitdeer as operators retaining greater flexibility to invest in mining.

This is producing two strategies: convert mining power infrastructure to AI/HPC, or continue improving SHA-256 economics with more efficient ASICs, energy and scale.

## Bitcoin ASICs are competing with GPUs for megawatts

Bitcoin ASICs and AI accelerators increasingly compete for the same resource: delivered electrical power. CoinShares estimates annualized AI profit around **$1.5 million/MW** versus approximately **$0.5 million/MW** for mining under the conditions analyzed, although Bitcoin price and hashprice can materially change that relationship.

Bitcoin mining helped build a generation of power-dense computing campuses. Some of those same substations, transformers and megawatts are now becoming foundations for AI infrastructure.
