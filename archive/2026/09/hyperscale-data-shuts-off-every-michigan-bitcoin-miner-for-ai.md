# Hyperscale Data Shuts Off Every Michigan Bitcoin Miner for AI

Published September 27, 2026.

Hyperscale Data switched off every Bitcoin miner at its Michigan data center effective September 1 as it prepares the campus for a California-based neocloud customer's AI infrastructure.

The initial deployment calls for 20 MW of critical AI compute capacity, with an option to scale to 52 MW. The agreement has a 10-year initial term plus two five-year extension options and is valued by Hyperscale Data at approximately $1.2 billion if it reaches its maximum term.

Hyperscale Data says the Michigan property could ultimately reach approximately 340 MW. The company continues Bitcoin mining operations in Montana and has separately announced plans for Patriot BTC.
