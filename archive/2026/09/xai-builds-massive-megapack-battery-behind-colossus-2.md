# xAI Builds Massive Megapack Battery Behind Colossus 2

Published September 27, 2026.
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/xai-builds-massive-megapack-battery-behind-colossus-2/

Satellite imagery reviewed by Canary Media shows 720 Tesla Megapack containers at xAI's Colossus 2 campus in Memphis. Depending on model, that implies roughly 2.8 GWh of storage and 720 MW to 1.4 GW of discharge power.

An xAI developer separately cited 3.3 GWh, while MLGW's CEO described roughly 2,000 MW behind the meter. The precise final rating is therefore not treated as confirmed.

xAI says batteries and onsite generation allow the campus to disconnect during emergencies or grid-strain events. This makes energy storage part of the operating architecture of the AI supercomputer rather than simply emergency backup.
