# HIVE Takes Swedish Bitcoin Mining VAT Fight to EU

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/hive-takes-swedish-bitcoin-mining-vat-fight-to-eu/

HIVE Digital Technologies has escalated a Swedish VAT dispute to the European Commission, raising a broader question about how European tax law classifies industrial Bitcoin hashrate services.

## The argument turns on what a miner actually sells

HIVE's Swedish subsidiaries Bikupa Datacenter AB and Bikupa Datacenter 2 AB dispute the Swedish Tax Agency's characterization of their operations as self-mining. HIVE maintains that the subsidiaries supplied computing capacity to an identified customer under contract and invoiced periodically.

The classification matters because input VAT recovery on ASIC equipment and other costs can depend on whether the underlying activity is treated as a taxable supply.

## ASICs, electricity and cooling make VAT classification expensive

Industrial Bitcoin mining is capital and energy intensive. ASICs, electrical infrastructure, cooling systems and power purchases make VAT treatment economically significant.

## The European Commission question

Dentons says Swedish courts declined to refer the classification question to the Court of Justice of the European Union. HIVE's counsel has asked the European Commission to examine Sweden's practice surrounding EU-law referrals under Article 267(3) TFEU. A separate request has sought EU consideration of the underlying VAT question.
