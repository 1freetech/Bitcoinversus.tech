# Private and Sovereign Miners Gain Bitcoin Hashrate Share

New September data indicates publicly listed miners are losing Bitcoin network share to private and sovereign operators while listed companies redirect power and capital toward AI infrastructure.

A September 25 dataset tracking 30 public miners estimated 416.1 EH/s, about 44.92% of global network hashrate, down 3.09 percentage points month over month.

Story X embed: https://twitter.com/FoundryServices

YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM4Hd1ZsQlk

CoinShares reported a weighted average ex-tax cash production cost near $75,500 per BTC for listed miners in Q2 and identified at least 35 EH/s scheduled to leave the listed cohort.

This shift means mining intelligence increasingly has to follow private companies, sovereign projects, hosting customers, energy producers and individual sites rather than relying on the largest public mining stocks.
