# Nuvoton Puts EIS Battery Diagnostics Into a 26-Cell Monitoring IC

Nuvoton Technology is adding electrochemical impedance spectroscopy directly to a 26-cell battery-monitoring IC. Samples of the KA85010UA are scheduled for January 2027.

Nuvoton says its proprietary inductor method reduces PCB temperature rise and power loss during EIS measurement by about 93% versus its conventional resistor method under its evaluation conditions. The IC is aimed at EVs, large-scale energy storage and reused-battery diagnostic systems.
