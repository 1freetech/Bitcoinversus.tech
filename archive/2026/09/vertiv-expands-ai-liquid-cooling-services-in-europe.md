# Vertiv Expands AI Liquid Cooling Services in Europe

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/vertiv-expands-ai-liquid-cooling-services-in-europe/

Vertiv is expanding its liquid-cooling field services in Europe, the Middle East and Africa through an agreement to acquire Ireland-based King Environmental Services, a specialist in fluid management, commissioning and load testing for high-density data centers.

## Vertiv adds liquid-cooling field expertise in EMEA

Vertiv announced on September 24 that KES will add fluid-system preparation, flushing, filtration, water treatment, commissioning, performance validation, thermal load testing and electrical load testing to its regional capabilities. KES is headquartered near Dublin and also operates from Frankfurt.

The transaction is expected to close in Q4 2026. Financial terms were not disclosed and Vertiv said the transaction is not expected to be material to its financial results.

## Why coolant quality becomes an uptime issue

Direct-to-chip liquid cooling moves heat away from CPUs, GPUs and accelerators through a liquid circuit. Contamination, trapped air, corrosion, incorrect chemistry and flow imbalance can reduce heat-transfer performance and contribute to equipment degradation.

KES works at the transition between construction and live operation, testing and balancing cooling loops before production compute is placed at risk.

## The thermal chain is becoming infrastructure

The acquisition follows Vertiv's 2025 purchase of PurgeRite in North America. KES extends that fluid-management model into EMEA.

This is relevant beyond AI. Bitcoin mining operators have experience with dense electrical loads, hydro and immersion systems. As mining campuses increasingly overlap with AI and HPC, commissioning around pumps, heat exchangers, water quality, flow, leak detection and heat rejection becomes a shared engineering concern.
