# Molex Packs 3,456 Fibers Into 1RU for AI Networks

Molex introduced VersaBeam Mini, an ultra-compact 16-fiber Expanded Beam Optical connector for high-density AI and hyperscale networks.

The 3.5 × 9 mm connector supports configurations of up to 3,456 fibers in a standard 1RU panel. Molex says it delivers three times the density of its existing VersaBeam 16 connectivity while keeping connections at 16-fiber granularity.

The expanded-beam interface is designed to reduce contamination sensitivity and maintenance overhead in dense optical fields. Molex targets co-packaged optics, optical backbones, front-panel switches and optical backplanes.

Limited customer sampling is planned for Q4 2026, with general production targeted for the first half of 2027.
