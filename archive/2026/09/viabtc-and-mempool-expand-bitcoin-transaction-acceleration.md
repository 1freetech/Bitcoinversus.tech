# ViaBTC and Mempool Expand Bitcoin Transaction Acceleration

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/viabtc-and-mempool-expand-bitcoin-transaction-acceleration/

ViaBTC has partnered with Mempool to widen access to Bitcoin transaction acceleration, connecting mining-pool block-building infrastructure with a service for eligible transactions delayed by congestion or low fees.

## ViaBTC puts mining-pool infrastructure behind transaction acceleration

ViaBTC says it will use its Bitcoin pool infrastructure, block-construction systems and transaction-processing capabilities to support eligible acceleration requests associated with Mempool. Existing ViaBTC users continue using the same website and app.

ViaBTC says its accelerator has handled more than 800,000 Bitcoin transactions since 2016. Commercial terms were not disclosed.

## Why a transaction gets stuck

A valid Bitcoin transaction can remain unconfirmed when demand for block space exceeds capacity or its fee rate is below competing transactions. Mining pools assemble candidate blocks and normally have an economic incentive to prioritize higher-fee transactions.

The accelerator changes transaction-selection priority rather than Bitcoin consensus rules.
