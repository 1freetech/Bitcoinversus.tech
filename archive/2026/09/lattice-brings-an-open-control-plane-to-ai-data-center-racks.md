# Lattice Brings an Open Control Plane to AI Data Center Racks

Published September 29, 2026.
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/lattice-brings-an-open-control-plane-to-ai-data-center-racks/

Lattice Semiconductor is preparing to demonstrate an open AI data-center control plane at the 2026 OCP Global Summit in San Jose. Lattice and AMI say the demonstrations will span secure platform firmware, rack-scale management and telemetry, flash-less boot, quantum-resistant security, AI-enabled sensor aggregation and open infrastructure management.

The architecture matters because modern AI racks combine accelerators, CPUs, networking, power shelves, cooling, sensors, firmware and management controllers from multiple vendors. Lattice and AMI are positioning a common control and management layer across those systems.

Lattice completed its $1.65 billion AMI acquisition in July 2026, adding platform firmware and infrastructure manageability to its low-power FPGA portfolio.
