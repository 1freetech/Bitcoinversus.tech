# Cango Starts AI GPU Compute at 50 MW Bitcoin Mining Site

Published: September 27, 2026
Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/cango-starts-ai-gpu-compute-at-50-mw-bitcoin-mining-site/

Cango has started commercial GPU compute services inside its 50 MW Bitcoin mining facility in Georgia. EcoHash completed infrastructure modifications for a dedicated AI section supporting up to 3 MW and put its first batch of GPU servers into commercial AI inference service.

## A Bitcoin mining site now has a dedicated AI section

Cango says the retrofit includes electrical, cooling and data-center infrastructure required for GPU compute. The dedicated section currently supports up to 3 MW, with room for future expansion.

## Why miners can move toward GPU infrastructure

Bitcoin mines and AI data centers use different computing hardware, network designs and cooling profiles, but both require large amounts of dependable electrical capacity. Existing substations, transformers, switchgear, land, fiber and operations teams can help mining operators adapt sites for GPU workloads.

## Cango already operates at Bitcoin-mining scale

Cango entered large-scale Bitcoin mining through major machine and infrastructure acquisitions. The Georgia project shows how that operating base can become a platform for adjacent high-density compute.

## EcoHash is starting with inference

EcoHash describes the initial workload as commercial GPU compute for AI inference, allowing the company to enter AI infrastructure incrementally rather than convert the entire mining campus at once.
