# Braiins OS 26.09 Adds Better ASIC Power and Startup Diagnostics

Braiins OS 26.09, released September 16, adds configurable startup cooldown, GUI log search, safer preheat behavior, PSU temperature logging for APW11/APW12/APW17 and fixes affecting hydro miners with Zynq control boards. Braiins OS 26.09.1 followed September 24 to fix intermittent T5 temperature-sensor errors that could halt mining for 60 seconds.

A Red Panda Mining S21 XP test reported approximately 268–270 TH/s at about 3,900 W on stock firmware and 270+ TH/s at roughly 3,740 W after Braiins OS autotuning. This is a single independent test, not a universal performance claim.
