<p><strong>Microchip has launched a compact gallium-nitride power converter designed to keep high-performance spacecraft processors supplied with stable power in radiation-heavy environments.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Announced October 14, 2026, the new <a href="https://www.microchip.com/en-us/about/news-releases/products/microchip-expands-space-power-portfolio-with-new-radiation-tolerant-gan-pol-converter"><strong>POL-05-20S-T</strong></a> is a radiation-tolerant point-of-load converter rated for up to 20A of continuous output. Microchip says the 25-gram surface-mount device measures 1.51 × 1.51 × 0.45 inches, accepts 4.75V to 13.2V input and provides an adjustable 0.7V to 5V output.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The device targets core power rails for FPGAs, microcontrollers, ASICs and other processing-heavy loads. Microchip specifies its electrical performance before and after a total ionizing dose of 50 krad and characterizes single-event effects to 43 MeV·cm²/mg. <a href="https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/microchip-launches-radiationtolerant-power-converter-for-space-93CH-2736128"><strong>Independent coverage</strong></a> also confirms that the converter is now available in production quantities.</p>

<p>The launch extends the wider shift toward GaN already visible in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/02/filtronic-spacex-68-million-starlink-gan-e-band-amplifiers/"><strong>Starlink radio hardware</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/renesas-650v-gan-megawatt-ai-data-center-power/"><strong>high-density power conversion</strong></a> and newer <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/xmax-hexa-creation-1200v-vertical-gan/"><strong>1200V GaN devices</strong></a>. This product is radiation-tolerant rather than proof that every GaN design is automatically suitable for space; mission qualification still depends on the complete system.</p>
