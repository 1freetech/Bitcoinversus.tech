## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shenzhen robotics startup DexForce is using simulated environments and synthetic data to train humanoid robots, aiming to reduce the cost and time required to teach machines real-world tasks. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/chinese-startup-dexforce-trains-humanoid-robots-using-virtual-data-slash-costs-2026-10-15/">Reuters reported on October 15</a> that the company builds virtual versions of physical environments, trains policies there, and then transfers the learned behavior onto real robots.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The approach attacks the same bottleneck behind the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/xdof-1-2-billion-robot-training-data/">robot-training data boom</a>: collecting enough useful demonstrations in the physical world is expensive and slow. DexForce is betting that simulation can generate far more task variation before a robot touches a real workcell.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><a href="https://en.dexforce.com/">DexForce describes its EmbodiChain and Real2Sim2Real systems</a> as an embodied-AI engine that simulates complex scenes, generates synthetic training data and transfers learned behavior back to physical machines. Its W1 Pro combines humanoid arms, dexterous end effectors, stereo vision and industrial control hardware.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Reuters says DexForce recently raised <strong>1 billion yuan, about $147 million</strong>, in Series B financing at a valuation above 10 billion yuan. The company says its technology is already being used in manufacturing and service deployments. The harder test is whether policies trained in virtual worlds remain reliable when lighting, objects, people and factory conditions change.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That challenge is also central to robots targeting <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/dyna-taku-dyna-2-1-full-workflows-physical-agent/">multi-step physical workflows</a> and to the wider <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/finance-mobileye-6-31-billion-valuation-physical-ai-robotaxi-humanoid/">physical-AI push across machines and autonomy</a>. Synthetic data can increase scale; real-world validation still decides whether the robot is useful.</p>
