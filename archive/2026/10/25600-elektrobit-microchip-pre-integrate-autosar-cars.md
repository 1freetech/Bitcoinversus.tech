<p>Elektrobit and Microchip Technology announced on October 15, 2026 that they are combining automotive hardware with pre-integrated AUTOSAR software to reduce the setup work required for software-defined vehicles. The <a href="https://www.microchip.com/en-us/about/news-releases/products/elektrobit-and-microchip-collaborate-on-pre-integrated-autosar-solutions">official announcement</a> says the package pairs Microchip automotive platforms with Elektrobit’s EB tresos software and tooling; <a href="https://www.marketscreener.com/news/elektrobit-and-microchip-technology-inc-collaborate-on-pre-integrated-autosar-solutions-for-next-ge-ce785dd3de8ef127">S&amp;P Capital IQ reporting</a> also confirmed the collaboration today.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The first implementation includes pre-integrated Microcontroller Abstraction Layer software plus EB tresos AutoCore OS and Generic modules on Microchip’s SAMA5 microprocessors. The companies say initial market work will emphasize high-growth automotive segments in Asia, including two-wheelers, while also supporting customers across Asia and North America.</p>

<p>AUTOSAR standardizes the software architecture used across electronic control units. Pre-integration does not make a vehicle autonomous by itself; it removes part of the repetitive foundation work so engineering teams can spend more time on application logic, validation and vehicle-specific features. Elektrobit is also applying an integration-as-code approach to automate repeatable Classic AUTOSAR configuration work.</p>
