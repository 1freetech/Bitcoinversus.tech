## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Vertiv used the 2026 OCP Global Summit to introduce an open <strong>DC Fault Manager</strong> reference architecture for 800 VDC AI data centers. The idea is to separate the millisecond-level job of interrupting a fault from the slower system-level work of deciding what happened, whether the affected section is safe, and when power can be restored.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On its <a href="https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/about/news-and-events/events/ocp-global-summit-2026/">OCP 2026 program page</a>, Vertiv says the design combines localized solid-state circuit breaker action with centralized post-fault analysis, safety interlocks, telemetry correlation and busway re-energization sequencing. The proposed standardization areas include fault-state messages, digital handshakes, breaker behavior classes, sampling expectations and reset sequences.</p>

<p><em>Vertiv explains why AI rack power is moving toward 800 VDC as rack density climbs.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The protection problem matters because direct current is harder to interrupt cleanly than conventional AC, especially as rack power moves toward hundreds of kilowatts and eventually megawatt-class systems. <a href="https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/08/17/siemens-and-reinhausen-turn-up-the-voltage-for-hungry-ai-racks/5288565">The Register has documented</a> the industry-wide move toward 800 VDC as vendors try to reduce conversion stages and current levels in dense AI racks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><em>Data-center operators are already debating how 800 VDC sidecars fit with UPS, protection and distribution architectures.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The proposal builds on a fast-moving 800 VDC ecosystem. BitcoinVersus.Tech has already tracked <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/54v-vs-800v-for-ai-racks/">the 54V-to-800V transition</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/xfusion-800v-power-liquid-cooling-ocp/">xFusion’s 800V rack infrastructure</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/delta-800-vdc-3mw-cooling-ocp-2026/">Delta’s 800 VDC and 3 MW cooling push</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The key caveat: this is a <strong>reference architecture and standardization proposal</strong>, not a finalized OCP standard. The signal is still important—fault coordination is becoming a first-class design problem as AI power systems move from tens of kilowatts toward megawatt racks.</p>
