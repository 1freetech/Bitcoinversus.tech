<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>FingerMotion has signed a <strong>$2.3 million agreement to acquire Newbit Technology</strong>, holder of rights and permits for a planned <strong>9.9 MW natural-gas-powered compute site</strong> in Alberta, Canada. The <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1602409/000149315226044880/form8-k.htm">September 29 SEC filing</a> confirms an agreement—not a completed acquisition or an operating 9.9 MW data center.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Newbit holds leases, permits, pipeline access and fixed site improvements at Brooks Campus #1.</li><li>Generators and other movable equipment are explicitly excluded from the transaction.</li><li>Closing is targeted by October 29, subject to a new gas supply agreement, consents and due diligence.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the Buyer Is Actually Getting</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The September 23 share-purchase agreement covers the County of Newell site, including development and electrical permits, environmental approvals, gas-metering infrastructure and existing foundations. Its purchase price is <strong>US$2.3 million</strong>, excluding applicable GST and possible adjustments.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>FingerMotion reported two $230,000 deposit installments and approximately $1.84 million due at closing. The prior natural-gas supply contract had ended; a replacement agreement and written change-of-control consent are among the closing requirements. Its <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1602409/000149315226047254/form10-q.htm">October 2026 quarterly filing</a> continued to describe the acquisition as pending.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Mining and AI Infrastructure Overlap</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Behind-the-meter generation means supplying computing equipment directly from power produced on-site instead of relying entirely on utility-delivered electricity. Our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/energy-behind-the-meter-power-explained-data-centers-onsite-generation/">behind-the-meter guide</a> explains the electrical model. An existing fuel connection and permits can have value even before servers or ASICs arrive.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>FingerMotion’s separate <a href="https://ir.fingermotion.com/newsroom/press-releases/detail/91/fingermotion-enters-edge-ai-inference-compute-market-through-strategic-mou-with-blueflare-energy-solutions-plans-to-proceed-with-first-behind-the-meter-site-pr1-in-alberta-under-non-binding-commercial-term-sheet">BlueFlare collaboration</a> contemplates pairing AI inference with flexible Bitcoin mining loads. But the Newbit filing does <strong>not</strong> establish that Brooks Campus #1 currently hosts either workload. For an operating comparison, see <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/microbt-20mw-natural-gas-bitcoin-mining-canada/">MicroBT’s reported 20 MW Canadian gas-mining deployment</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
