# Microsoft Unveils Maia 300 AI Chip

Published: 2026-10-13

Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/microsoft-maia-300-ai-chip/

WordPress Post ID: 24165
Featured Media ID: 24162

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Microsoft introduced Maia 300 on October 12, 2026, its third-generation custom AI accelerator for inference. The company says <a href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/12/microsoft-to-expand-azure-maia-system-to-multi-gigawatt-deployments/">production deployments will begin in 2027</a> and that Maia will become the primary inference system for Microsoft products and services as deployments expand to multi-gigawatt scale.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to Microsoft’s technical disclosure, each Maia 300 uses eight HBM4 stacks delivering 18.0 TB/s of peak memory bandwidth within a 950-watt TDP. A full rack is designed around 96 accelerators, 27.6 TB of HBM and roughly 167 kW of power.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Microsoft says Maia 300 can deliver 40% more tokens per megawatt than “leading GPU-based systems” and up to seven times the rack-scale token throughput of Maia 200. Those are vendor claims; Microsoft has not identified the specific GPU baseline for the 40% comparison, so independent testing will be needed before treating the efficiency figure as a general performance result.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-plans-huge-deployment-its-own-ai-chips-with-an-eye-saving-costs-2026-10-12/">Reuters reported</a> that Microsoft may deploy more than one million Maia chips in 2027, underscoring the scale of the internal-silicon strategy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The announcement fits a broader shift toward rack-level AI design. BitcoinVersus.Tech has recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/intels-new-ai-gpu-supports-up-to-480gb-of-memory/">Intel’s high-memory GPU D-Series</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/astera-labs-unveils-orion-rack-for-up-to-160-gpus/">Astera Labs’ modular Orion rack</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/delta-800-vdc-3mw-cooling-ocp-2026/">Delta’s 800 VDC power and megawatt-scale cooling work</a>.</p>
