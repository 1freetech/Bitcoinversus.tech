<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Applied Materials says it expects AI-driven chip investment to keep wafer-fab equipment spending elevated through the end of the decade.</strong> At its October 13 SEMICON West investor presentation, the company said it is targeting more than two percentage points of wafer-fab-equipment market-share gain from 2025 to 2030.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://investor.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-set-outperform-ai-drives-sustained-demand"><strong>official outlook</strong></a>, Applied said leading-edge logic, DRAM and advanced packaging are the main growth areas. It also expects its eBeam equipment portfolio to generate about $2 billion in 2026 revenue and service revenue to exceed $12 billion by 2030. Those figures are company targets, not guaranteed outcomes.</p>
