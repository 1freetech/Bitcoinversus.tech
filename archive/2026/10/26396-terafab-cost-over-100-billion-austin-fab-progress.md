<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Tesla and SpaceX's planned <strong>Terafab</strong> semiconductor complex is attracting attention for a new reason: its potential long-term price tag. During Tesla's October 21, 2026 earnings call, Elon Musk described a project that could ultimately require <strong>more than $100 billion</strong> in investment. That is a forward-looking estimate, not an announced final construction budget.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Changed This Week</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Tesla's <a href="https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tsla/transcripts/712902-q3-2026/">third-quarter earnings-call transcript</a> says the smaller Austin research fab is progressing, with machinery commissioning targeted for the first half of 2027. Musk hopes for a first test chip around May or June 2027 and useful volume output around the third quarter of 2028. He described the dates as aspirations, not guarantees.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Tesla's <a href="https://last10k.com/sec-filings/tsla/0001628280-26-066775.htm">October quarterly filing</a> independently confirms continuing construction at the Austin Fab and planned equipment commissioning in 2027. The filing does not establish that commercial-scale Terafab production has begun.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Semiconductor Manufacturing Costs So Much</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>A leading-edge chip factory needs lithography, deposition, etching, metrology, cleanroom infrastructure, high-purity water, dependable electricity and advanced packaging. Extreme-ultraviolet lithography systems are particularly costly and difficult to procure. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-teslas-terafab-targets-2028-volume-chip-production-musk-says-2026-10-20/">Reuters reported</a> that Musk thanked ASML and other suppliers for efforts to accelerate equipment deliveries, but did not directly confirm EUV orders.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The <a href="https://new.spacex.com/updates">August 6 SpaceX announcement</a> places the main planned complex in Grimes County, Texas, while the separate Austin research facility is intended to test processes first. SpaceX described a proposed factory exceeding <strong>100 million square feet</strong>, combining logic, memory, packaging and testing. That figure describes a plan—not finished floor space.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A $100 Billion Bet, Not a Finished Fab</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The companies envision chip supply for robotics, autonomous vehicles and space-based computing. Their stated one-terawatt ambition refers to eventual compute capacity, not the facility's confirmed electricity consumption or present production. Questions remain about process yields, equipment availability, financing, partner roles and when high-volume manufacturing can actually start.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>For context, our earlier coverage examined the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/21/tesla-spacex-terafab-2028-chip-production-timeline/">2028 chip-production target</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/tsmc-confirms-texas-team-terafab-talks/">TSMC's Texas discussions</a>, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/30/terafab-100-million-square-feet-giga-texas-scale/">proposed factory's enormous footprint</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/05/18/intel-and-tesla-partner-to-build-robotics-processors/">Intel's reported participation</a>. None of those announcements establishes a completed chipmaking line.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Bottom line:</strong> Terafab's updated cost outlook illustrates the capital intensity of advanced semiconductor manufacturing. The meaningful milestones to watch are installed equipment, functional test wafers, verified yields and sustained volume production—not just proposed investment or ambitious schedules.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
