<p><strong>Bitcoin Verse of the Day — October 14, 2026</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>“The one thing that's missing, but that will soon be developed, is a reliable e-cash. A method whereby on the Internet you can transfer funds from A to B without A knowing B or B knowing A.”</p><cite>Milton Friedman, 1999</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Milton Friedman said this in a February 10, 1999 interview, years before Bitcoin existed. He was not describing Bitcoin by name. He was identifying a missing capability of the internet: digital cash that could move directly between people without requiring the sender and receiver to know one another.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.hoover.org/plus/research/take-it-limits-milton-friedman-libertarianism-0">Hoover Institution</a> preserves the original <em>Take It To The Limits</em> interview. A transcript published by <a href="https://www.freedomandprosperity.tv/en/node/669">Freedom &amp; Prosperity TV</a> records the same e-cash passage. The exact interview excerpt is embedded below, followed by Hoover’s full archival recording.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin later arrived with a design explicitly called <em>A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System</em>. That history connects directly with BitcoinVersus coverage of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/11/02/bitcoin-whitepaper-anniversary/">Bitcoin whitepaper</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/18/bitcoins-beginning-satoshi-nakamotos-legendary-letter-to-wei-dei/">Satoshi Nakamoto’s letter to Wei Dai</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/24/the-21-laws-of-bitcoin-an-intellectual-guide-to-understanding-bitcoin/">The 21 Laws of Bitcoin</a>. Friedman’s verse matters because it captures the problem before Bitcoin supplied one answer.</p>
