<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/09/27/terawulf-secures-major-ai-hpc-hosting-deal-with-google-backed-fluidstack/">BitcoinVersus.Tech reported in September 2025</a> on TeraWulf’s move from Bitcoin mining toward large-scale AI and HPC hosting. That strategy has now expanded into a direct 20-year lease with Anthropic.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In a <a href="https://investors.terawulf.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001104659-26-080583/tm2619468d1_8k.htm">July 6 SEC filing</a>, TeraWulf said Anthropic will lease about <strong>401 MW of critical IT load</strong> at the Justified Data Campus in Hawesville, Kentucky. TeraWulf expects roughly <strong>$19 billion of contracted lease revenue</strong> over the initial term. Capacity is scheduled to arrive in phases beginning in the second half of 2027, reaching the full 401 MW in early 2028.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>TeraWulf also agreed to sell its 50.1% interest in the Abernathy joint venture for about $530 million. The company said the proceeds would be redirected toward AI infrastructure it owns directly. Its August quarterly filing still reported Bitcoin-mining revenue, so this is a diversification strategy rather than a complete exit from mining.</p>

<p>The buildout has continued. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered TeraWulf’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/bitcoin-mining-terawulf-muskie-kentucky-1gw-ai-hpc/">1 GW Muskie power expansion in Kentucky</a>. The broader pattern resembles <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/applied-digital-revenue-jumps-322-as-ai-factories-come-online/">Applied Digital’s AI-infrastructure expansion</a>: access to large blocks of power is becoming a major competitive asset for former mining-focused operators.</p>
