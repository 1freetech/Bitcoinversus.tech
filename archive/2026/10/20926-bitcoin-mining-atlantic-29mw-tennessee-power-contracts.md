# Bitcoin Mining: Atlantic Adds 29 MW Tennessee Power Contracts, With Energization Still Ahead

Live article: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/bitcoin-mining-atlantic-29mw-tennessee-power-contracts/

```html
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Atlantic HPC Group has acquired a Tennessee entity holding <strong>29 MW of contracted utility demand</strong>, expanding the Bitcoin miner’s development footprint into a fourth state. The acquisition closed September 23, 2026, and was announced September 30.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to the <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/30/3371786/0/en/atlantic-hpc-group-inc-acquires-valley-oasis-development-llc-adding-29-mw-of-contracted-power-to-platform.html">Atlantic and Aperture announcement</a>, Valley Oasis Development holds two 14.5 MW contracts with Dyersburg Electric System and a one-acre lease beside a substation. Contracted demand remains a development milestone, with delivery dependent on further work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Braiins’ field video below shows the physical power equipment behind operating Bitcoin mining. The West Texas gas-powered operation provides technical context; the footage does not depict Atlantic’s Tennessee site.</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What changed since Atlantic’s listing announcement</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitcoinVersus previously covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/atlantic-hpc-takes-51-mw-bitcoin-mining-platform-toward-nasdaq/">Atlantic’s proposed Nasdaq combination and 51 MW mining platform</a>. The Tennessee acquisition raises its disclosed contracted-capacity total from 98 MW to 127 MW and its development pipeline from 47 MW to 76 MW. The additional 29 MW belongs to the development portfolio, not newly energized mining or AI capacity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Half the contracted demand needs transformer upgrades</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2093524/000121390026104882/ea030716601ex99-2.htm">SEC-filed September investor update</a> says approximately 14.5 MW could use existing transformer capacity after Atlantic funds utility construction and the work is completed. Serving the remaining 14.5 MW requires replacement or upgrades to utility substation equipment at Atlantic’s cost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The presentation also discloses unpaid utility aid-to-construction, no obtained construction or operating permits, and no assured energization date. Approximately ten additional acres are under negotiation, with nonbinding terms. Those qualifications make the announcement a secured-site and power-contract transaction rather than evidence of a completed data center.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why miners should track delivery milestones</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For operators weighing ASIC mining against AI infrastructure, the useful asset is the route from a utility agreement to dependable electrical service. Land, funded construction, transformer availability and commissioning determine when a contracted megawatt can support revenue. A power contract alone cannot establish an AI facility’s cooling design, customer readiness or operating economics.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Atlantic’s disclosure adds a concrete case to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/ai-700-gw-power-queue-ghost-demand-data-centers/">the distinction between proposed power demand and build-ready capacity</a>. The next material updates would be payment of utility construction funding, permits, equipment work, binding land expansion and a delivery schedule.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/hut-8-beacon-point-clears-key-ercot-power-step/">Hut 8’s staged power-access progress</a>, each checkpoint should be reported separately. Atlantic has expanded its contractual footprint; the commercial value now depends on converting that footprint into usable compute infrastructure.</p>
```
