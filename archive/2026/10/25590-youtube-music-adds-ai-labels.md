<p><a href="https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-music-ai-labels/">YouTube said on October 14, 2026</a> that YouTube Music is rolling out labels for tracks created primarily with generative AI, including music where generative models produced the composition or vocals.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The label appears on the player page and can be tapped for more information. YouTube says it is working with music partners and using its own detection tools to identify qualifying tracks. The same labeling system is also planned for the main YouTube app.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/14/youtube-music-launches-new-labels-as-ai-generated-music-floods-streaming-platforms/">TechCrunch reports</a> that partners can declare tracks as fully generated by AI, partly generated by AI, or not generated by AI. The rollout gives listeners more context without changing the underlying song itself.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
