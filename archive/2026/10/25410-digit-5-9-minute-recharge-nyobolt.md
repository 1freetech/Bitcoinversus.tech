<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Agility Robotics has selected Nyobolt to supply the battery technology for Digit 5, giving the humanoid a claimed <strong>90 minutes of runtime from a 9-minute recharge</strong>. That is a 10:1 run-to-charge ratio, up from Digit 4’s 2:1 ratio.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The practical target is uptime. Agility says the new system is designed to let Digit work for <strong>more than 20 hours in a 24-hour day</strong>, which would make it easier to cover multiple warehouse or manufacturing shifts without long charging windows. Early access to Nyobolt-powered Digit 5 units is expected in the first half of 2027, with wider availability planned over the following six to nine months.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
