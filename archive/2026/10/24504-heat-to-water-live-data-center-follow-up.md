<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitcoinVersus.Tech first covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/11/01/u-s-army-turns-waste-heat-into-clean-water-for-soldiers/">AirJoule’s U.S. Army waste-heat-to-water project</a> in November 2025. Since then, the same core idea has moved much closer to full-scale deployment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>AirJoule has now commissioned its first full-scale Prime system, designed to produce up to <strong>2,000 liters of distilled water per day</strong>. In an <a href="https://airjouletech.com/2026/08/18/airjoule-technologies-and-the-net-zero-innovation-hub-for-data-centers-announce-strategic-collaboration-to-deploy-heat-to-water-technology-in-data-centers/">August 18 announcement</a>, the company and the Net Zero Innovation Hub for Data Centers said the unit would be deployed at a European data center to test whether low-grade server heat can drive water production in a live facility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>AirJoule says the first Prime unit is now on its way to Europe for validation inside a live data-center environment.</em></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Army work is still active. AirJoule’s latest <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1855474/000119312526350442/airj-20260630.htm">SEC filing</a> says the ERDC collaboration continues to focus on integrating the platform with tactical generator waste-heat recovery systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The big question now is whether the concept works economically at scale. If it does, waste heat could become useful twice: first as a by-product of computing, then as an energy source for producing water. That would add another option alongside <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/can-data-center-waste-heat-warm-homes-or-nah/">district heat reuse</a> and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/osdcec-008-data-center-water-systems-cooling-loops-towers-water-quality-blowdown-wue/">water systems already used inside data centers</a>.</p>
