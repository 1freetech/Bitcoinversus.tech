<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Eliyan has launched a new optical interconnect platform designed to move AI systems beyond the practical reach and power limits of copper.</strong> The company’s NuLight platform pairs two chiplets—Nexus and Oscar—to build modular links from 1.6 Tb/s OSFP configurations up to 12.8 Tb/s XPO.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://eliyan.com/press-release/eliyan-introduces-nulight/"><strong>Eliyan’s October 14 announcement</strong></a>, Nexus is a 3nm reverse-gearbox chiplet that converts 212G PAM4 electrical links into groups of 53G NRZ optical lanes. Oscar provides the electrical interface to microVCSEL and photodiode arrays. Eliyan says the architecture targets about 50 meters of optical reach and includes spare-lane repair that can reroute traffic if an optical lane degrades.</p>
