<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NVIDIA and Cisco announced on October 12, 2026, at the Open Compute Project Global Summit plans for Ultra-Dense Small Form Factor Pluggable, or USFP, a new optical-transceiver format designed for 400 Gb/s electrical signaling per lane and up to 12.8 Tb/s of overall capacity. The proposal targets AI-factory Ethernet networks that need more bandwidth without adding switch tiers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What happened</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to the <a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-cisco-introduce-new-pluggable-optics-form-factor-to-scale-ai-factories" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">NVIDIA announcement</a> and <a href="https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m10/nvidia-and-cisco-new-pluggable-optics-to-scale-ai-factories.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cisco’s release</a>, the first planned USFP transceivers would place 16 full-duplex lanes in an OSFP-sized footprint for switches and SuperNICs, with a path to 32 or more lanes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the evidence shows</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The companies say USFP can provide four times the front-panel bandwidth density and about 1.3 times the power efficiency of 200 Gb/s-per-lane pluggable optics. The design supports up to 100 watts of module power dissipation. TE Connectivity and Amphenol are working on cages, connectors and modules. These are vendor-stated design targets, not independent benchmark results.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The announcement follows related moves toward higher-density Ethernet, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/cisco-silicon-one-g302-51-2tbps-ai-racks/">Cisco’s 51.2 Tb/s G302 switch silicon</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/keysight-1-6t-ethernet-link-layer-retry-ocp-2026/">Keysight’s 1.6T Ethernet link-recovery testing</a>, and reports that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/lumentum-says-ai-optics-are-sold-out-through-early-2029/">AI-optics supply is already heavily committed</a>.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What remains unclear</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NVIDIA and Cisco did not announce pricing or a shipment date for USFP modules. They said they plan to create an open industry specification through a community-led multi-source project. Until that specification, qualification work and production hardware arrive, USFP remains a proposed ecosystem roadmap rather than a deployed standard.</p>
