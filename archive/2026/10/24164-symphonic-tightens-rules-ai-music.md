<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Independent music distributor Symphonic has updated its approach to generative AI, saying fully AI-generated releases should come from models that appropriately license or authorize the material used to train and operate them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an <a href="https://blog.symphonic.com/2026/10/12/symphonics-approach-to-generative-ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">October 12 policy update</a>, Symphonic said creators may be required to disclose whether music is AI-generated or AI-assisted, identify the tools or models involved, and show that they have the rights needed to distribute and monetize the work. The company also said acceptance by Symphonic does not guarantee delivery to every streaming platform because individual DSPs can impose their own restrictions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The policy puts more weight on provenance and licensing than a simple yes-or-no AI label. Symphonic says it is investing in fingerprinting, detection, provenance and validation tools while still relying on creator disclosure. It also says artist participation in AI dataset licensing remains opt-in rather than automatic.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That direction matches a broader shift already visible across the music business. BitcoinVersus.Tech has covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/culture-universal-music-elevenlabs-licensed-ai-remix-platform/">Universal Music and ElevenLabs building a licensed AI remix platform</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/culture-suno-v6-rebuilds-ai-music-around-licensed-songs-from-warner-bmg-and-believe/">Suno rebuilding its model around licensed catalogs</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/culture-youtube-music-ask-music-300-million-songs-conversational-ai/">YouTube adding more AI-driven music discovery</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Symphonic has also pursued paid, opt-in AI licensing. A <a href="https://www.wmg.com/news/sureel-ai-and-symphonic-distribution-unlock-fair-ai-licensing-revenue-for-symphonic-roster" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">July partnership with Sureel AI</a> was designed to let participating artists track and monetize authorized use of recordings, compositions and lyrics in AI systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The new rules do not amount to a ban on AI music. Symphonic explicitly says generative AI can have a legitimate role in creation. The practical change is that creators should expect more disclosure, more platform-specific routing and greater scrutiny of whether an AI tool has permission to use the material behind its models.</p>
