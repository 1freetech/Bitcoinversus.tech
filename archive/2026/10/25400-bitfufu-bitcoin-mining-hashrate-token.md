<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitFuFu and Finloop launched <strong>Hashrate Token (HASH)</strong> on October 15, a BTC-denominated on-chain note designed to give eligible investors economic exposure to Bitcoin mining output without directly owning ASICs or operating a mine.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/15/3381431/0/en/bitfufu-launches-btc-denominated-hashrate-token-hash-with-finloop.html">BitFuFu’s launch announcement</a>, the underlying hashrate is provided as a service and contributes to mining activity, with the resulting economic output forming the basis of BTC-denominated returns. Profit distributions are recorded on-chain, while access is limited by investor-qualification, jurisdiction, KYC/AML and other regulatory requirements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Months before HASH launched, BitFuFu publicly described <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bitfufu_bitcoin-mining-miners-activity-7364288215622565888-2ues">“asset innovation” around RWA structures backed by cloud hashrate</a>.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The product turns a direction BitFuFu had already been signaling into a live financial instrument. BitcoinVersus.Tech previously tracked the company’s rise above <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/23/bitfufu-mining-hashrate-tops-20-ehs/">20 EH/s of managed hashrate</a>, its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitfufuos-asic-firmware-power-market-control/">BitFuFuOS power-control strategy</a>, and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/11/economic-challenges-for-bitfufu-cloud-mining-clients/">economics facing cloud-mining customers</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitFuFu also filed the launch with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission in a same-day <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1921158/000121390026048533/ea028681601ex10-6.htm">Form 6-K exhibit</a>. Finloop supplies the token issuance and lifecycle infrastructure, including minting, burning, transfers and compliance tooling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important distinction is that HASH is <strong>not an ASIC and not direct ownership of a mining facility</strong>. It is a structured note whose economics depend on Bitcoin mining output. That means mining difficulty, fleet efficiency, fees, BTC price and regulatory constraints still matter; the launch documents do not establish guaranteed returns.</p>
