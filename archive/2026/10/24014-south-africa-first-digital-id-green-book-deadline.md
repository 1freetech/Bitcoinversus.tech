## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>South Africa has demonstrated its first working Digital ID prototype as the Department of Home Affairs prepares to retire the country’s long-running Green ID book.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an <a href="https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-leon-schreiber-home-affairs-quantum-leap-vision-future-government-01-oct">October 1 address</a>, Home Affairs Minister Leon Schreiber said production of the Green ID book will end on <strong>March 31, 2027</strong>. The document will stop being recognised as valid identification one year later, on <strong>March 31, 2028</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The planned Digital ID would live on a smartphone and operate alongside the physical Smart ID card. Home Affairs says biometric verification would be used for remote identity checks, while users would be able to see when their information is accessed. Participation is expected to be voluntary. Final regulations are still being prepared.</p>
