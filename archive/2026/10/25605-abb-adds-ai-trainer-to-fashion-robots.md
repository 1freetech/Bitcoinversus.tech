<p><a href="https://www.abb.com/global/en/news/139480/prsrl-abb-robotics-brings-industrial-grade-physical-ai-to-fashion-logistics">ABB Robotics says</a> its Fashion Inductor now includes AI Robot Trainer, creating what the company calls its first end-to-end vision-based industrial physical-AI solution. ABB says the upgrade can cut engineering and commissioning effort by up to 75% for fashion-logistics deployments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The system uses pre-trained fashion-specific vision models, rapid cell-level retraining and a digital twin tied into RobotStudio, Automatic Path Planning and the OmniCore controller. ABB lists throughput of up to 1,300 items per hour per cell and picking-accuracy targets above 99.5%.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The 75% reduction is ABB’s own stated figure, not an independently measured average. <a href="https://www.unite.ai/abb-robotics-adds-ai-robot-trainer-to-fashion-inductor-module/">Unite.AI’s coverage</a> separately reports the same vendor claim and notes that ABB plans to expand the AI Robot Trainer approach into additional vision-based robotics applications through Q1 2027.</p>
