<p><strong>Bitcoin Quote of the Day — October 14, 2026</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>“My hope is that it creates world peace or helps create world peace... you fix that foundational level and everything above it improves.”</p><cite>Jack Dorsey</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jack Dorsey made the statement during <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zwx_7XAJ3p0">The ₿ Word</a> panel on July 21, 2021. A transcript of the discussion places the passage at about <a href="https://ceointerviews.ai/interview/647459/">1:04:35</a>. Dorsey was answering a direct question about his hope for Bitcoin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>His argument was broader than a prediction about Bitcoin’s price. Dorsey said the existing monetary system consumes attention and resources that could otherwise be directed toward larger human problems. He described Bitcoin as a possible change at that underlying monetary layer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The claim is aspirational, not a demonstrated outcome: Bitcoin by itself cannot guarantee peace. But the reasoning connects with BitcoinVersus coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/bitcoin-separates-money-from-state/">separating money from state</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/bitcoin-is-freedom-money-bitcoin-verse-of-the-day/">Bitcoin as freedom money</a>, and the earlier idea that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/the-internet-needed-reliable-e-cash/">the internet needed reliable e-cash</a>.</p>
