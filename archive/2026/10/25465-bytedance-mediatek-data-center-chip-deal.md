## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>ByteDance is reportedly close to an agreement to buy MediaTek semiconductors for data-center use. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/bytedance-nears-chip-supply-deal-with-mediatek-data-centres-bloomberg-news-2026-10-15/">Reuters reported on October 15</a>, citing Bloomberg News, that the companies are nearing a supply deal. Reuters said it had not independently confirmed the agreement, so the transaction should still be treated as reported rather than final.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The potential deal matters because MediaTek has been expanding beyond consumer processors into custom AI infrastructure. That shift fits the broader move toward <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/networking-upscale-token-fabric-open-ai-network-nvidia-amd-rival-chips/">open networks that can connect multiple classes of AI accelerators</a> instead of assuming one chip architecture will dominate every workload.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><a href="https://www.mediatek.com/tek-talk-blogs/from-silicon-to-scale-inside-mediateks-ai-data-center-solution">MediaTek says its data-center business now spans custom ASIC design, advanced packaging, memory, high-speed interconnects and rack integration</a>. The company said in June that it had raised its 2026 AI Data Center ASIC revenue guidance to <strong>$2 billion</strong> and saw a $70 billion to $80 billion addressable market by 2027.</p>

<p>If the reported ByteDance deal closes, it would give MediaTek another path into hyperscale infrastructure while adding another supplier option for a large AI operator. The market is already producing specialized designs such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/semifive-52-million-us-ai-accelerator-contract/">SEMIFIVE’s custom accelerator work</a> and alternative compute architectures such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/30/efficient-computer-raises-97-million-to-scale-dataflow-chips/">Efficient Computer’s dataflow processors</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The key fact remains the status of the agreement: a deal has been reported, but neither company had publicly announced a completed transaction in the sources reviewed for this story.</p>
