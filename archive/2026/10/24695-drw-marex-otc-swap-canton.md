<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>DRW and Marex have completed what Canton and S&amp;P Dow Jones Indices describe as the first over-the-counter swap on Canton and the first OTC derivative tied to a fully tokenized benchmark.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.canton.network/canton-network-press-releases/derivnex-launches-industry-first-otc-swap-on-canton-with-drw-and-marex">October 13 announcement</a> says the trade was a total return swap between DRW and Marex executed through Derivnex. It referenced the S&amp;P iBoxx USD Liquid Investment Grade Index, which Kaiko tokenized on Canton.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important detail is what moved on-chain. The underlying corporate bonds were not tokenized. Instead, Kaiko created a tokenized representation of the benchmark data and supplied signed official index values used to value the swap. Derivnex represented the trade itself as a Canton smart contract so both counterparties can work from one synchronized record through the contract’s lifecycle.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">One Record Instead of Constant Reconciliation</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Traditional OTC swaps often require separate systems, bilateral messages and repeated reconciliation. The Canton model keeps the agreed trade terms and valuation workflow on a shared ledger while preserving bilateral privacy. <a href="https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/corporate-news/article/sp-dow-jones-indices-and-kaiko-enable-first-on-chain-otc-swap-referencing-a-tokenized-benchmark-executed-by-drw-and-marex-on-derivnex/">S&amp;P Dow Jones Indices</a> independently confirmed the structure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The transaction fits the broader move toward tokenized market plumbing. BitcoinVersus recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/finance-sec-five-year-tokenized-us-stocks-onchain-innovation-exemption/">the SEC’s path for tokenized U.S. stocks</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/solana-launches-dvp-settlement-program/">Solana’s delivery-versus-payment settlement program</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/bnb-chain-1-billion-tokenized-stocks-etfs/">BNB Chain passing $1 billion in tokenized stocks and ETFs</a>.</p>
