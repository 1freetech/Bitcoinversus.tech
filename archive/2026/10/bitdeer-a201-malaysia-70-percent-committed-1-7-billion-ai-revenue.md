# Bitdeer Locks $1.7 Billion of AI Revenue Before A201 Powers On

Published: 2026-10-02

Live: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/02/bitdeer-a201-malaysia-70-percent-committed-1-7-billion-ai-revenue/

WordPress Post ID: 19956
Featured Media ID: 19960

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Bitdeer has secured customer commitments for more than 70% of its 21.7 MW A201 AI data center in Johor Bahru, Malaysia before the facility has even been energized, attaching more than $1.7 billion in expected five-year revenue to a site scheduled to power on in the first quarter of 2027.</strong></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><a href="https://bitdeer.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-ai-further-expands-ai-cloud-services-take-commitments">The company's September 29 disclosure</a> says A201 is being built for liquid-cooled NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 systems, with part of the rack space designed to support the next-generation Vera Rubin platform. Bitdeer says it has already procured more than 100 GB300 NVL72 racks ahead of energization.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>The important number is not only 21.7 MW. More than 70% of that capacity is already associated with customer commitments before the building begins commercial operation.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A201 turns powered capacity into contracted compute</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Bitdeer grew primarily as a Bitcoin-mining infrastructure company, where power, cooling and energized data-center space determine how much SHA-256 compute can operate. A201 applies the same infrastructure discipline to dense GPU systems instead of ASIC miners.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The transition is visible across Bitdeer's own operating portfolio. BitcoinVersus.tech recently reported that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/23/bitdeer-bitcoin-mining-hashrate-79-9-ehs/">Bitdeer's self-mining hashrate reached 79.9 EH/s</a>, meaning the company is expanding AI infrastructure while still operating one of the industry's largest Bitcoin-mining fleets.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>A201 is therefore not a story about Bitdeer abandoning Bitcoin mining. It shows the company using its data-center development expertise to monetize a second class of compute.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">The customer commitments arrived before power-on</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The facility is expected to energize in Q1 2027. Bitdeer says the commitments already represent more than $1.7 billion in expected revenue over five years and lift its total expected AI Cloud revenue backlog to approximately $2.9 billion.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><a href="https://x.com/TheEnergyMag/status/2105043037277528136">A story-specific industry post</a> published after the announcement summarized the same capacity, contract and hardware milestones.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><a href="https://mugglehead.com/bitdeer-technologies-group-malaysia-a201-ai-offtake/">Independent reporting</a> adds an important qualification: A201's site lease predates the new customer commitments, and Bitdeer has not publicly identified the customers, deposit amounts or payment schedule in the September 29 release. The $1.7 billion figure is expected contract revenue, not revenue already earned.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>That distinction matters. The new development is the customer off-take commitment covering most of A201, not the original decision to lease and build the 21.7 MW site.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Johor is becoming a larger Bitdeer AI campus</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>A201 is not Bitdeer's only Johor project. BitcoinVersus.tech previously covered the company's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitdeer-secures-65-1-mw-johor-ai-data-center-expansion/">65.1 MW A202 expansion at the same Malaysia campus</a>. Together, A201 and A202 are expected to represent 86.8 MW of AI Cloud data-center capacity.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Bitdeer says A201 is purpose-built for liquid-cooled rack-scale systems. More than 100 GB300 NVL72 racks have already been procured, while part of the facility is intended to accommodate NVIDIA's Vera Rubin generation. That creates a hardware bridge between current Blackwell Ultra deployments and the next GPU platform.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>For data-center operators, the engineering challenge is not simply installing GPUs. Rack-scale AI requires dense power distribution, liquid cooling, networking and a commissioning sequence capable of bringing expensive compute online quickly after utility energization.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Bitcoin miners are increasingly being valued for infrastructure</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Bitdeer's A201 commitments arrive as investors increasingly separate the value of a mining company's Bitcoin production from the value of its land, substations, interconnections and operating expertise. BitcoinVersus.tech recently covered how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/30/grayscale-bitcoin-miners-etf-ai-compute-gcpu/">Grayscale converted its Bitcoin Miners ETF into an AI Compute ETF</a>, explicitly widening the investment thesis toward the infrastructure powering high-performance compute.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Bitdeer is a particularly direct example because both businesses remain active at once. Its Bitcoin fleet monetizes SHA-256 compute while its AI division signs longer-duration commitments around GPU infrastructure.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The company is targeting up to 350 MW of AI Cloud data-center capacity by Q1 2028 and says its active AI Cloud pipeline now exceeds $10 billion. Those targets remain forward-looking. A201's first physical test is still ahead: energization, deployment of the procured racks and conversion of contracted demand into operating revenue.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>For now, A201 demonstrates something concrete: Bitdeer has sold most of a 21.7 MW AI facility before power-on, using the same power-and-data-center foundation that made the company a major Bitcoin miner.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:separator --><hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" /><!-- /wp:separator -->
