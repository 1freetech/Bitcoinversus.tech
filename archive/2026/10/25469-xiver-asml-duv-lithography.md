## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dutch specialty foundry Xiver is adding an ASML PAS 5500 deep-ultraviolet lithography system to expand its photonics and microsystems manufacturing capability. <a href="https://xiver.com/xiver-expands-manufacturing-capabilities-with-new-asml-duv-lithography-tool/">Xiver announced the acquisition on October 13, 2026</a>, saying the tool will improve fine patterning, dimensional control, alignment and pattern fidelity for specialty devices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The PAS 5500 is a mature lithography platform, but mature does not mean irrelevant. Specialty photonics, MEMS and other high-mix manufacturing often need precise, repeatable patterning without the economics of leading-edge EUV. BitcoinVersus.Tech’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/osstc-009-photolithography-equipment-basics/">photolithography equipment primer</a> explains where steppers and scanners fit inside the wafer process.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Xiver says the PAS 5500 will complement its existing i-line tools and enable double-sided wafer patterning and sub-micron 3D micromachining. <a href="https://optics.org/news/xiver-picks-up-%E2%82%AC28m-debt-finance-to-aid-photonic-chip-production">Optics.org reports</a> that a <strong>€28 million</strong> debt facility from ABN Amro is supporting the company’s manufacturing expansion, including the DUV acquisition.</p>

<p>The purchase also shows why older DUV equipment continues to matter. The supporting ecosystem still includes excimer-laser infrastructure such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/gigaphoton-recycles-neon-arf-duv-lithography/">neon-dependent DUV light sources</a>, while advanced fabs are simultaneously pushing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/asml-tsmc-12-inch-photomasks-high-na-euv-half-field/">larger photomasks for High-NA EUV</a>. Different manufacturing problems need different tools.</p>
