<p>OpenAI said on October 13, 2026 that it discovered a sophisticated recording device hidden inside one of its offices and used its own models to help understand the device and trace it to a specific employee. In the company’s <a href="https://twitter.com/OpenAINewsroom/status/2110095403198849132">X statement</a>, OpenAI said it referred the matter to the FBI, which made an arrest, and that the employee was terminated.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/OpenAINewsroom/status/2110095403198849132]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/2c3b583a-3ddc-4920-a350-9698c119435c">Financial Times</a> identified the employee as Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, a Saudi national, and reported that the device was found behind a television in an executive conference room. Alfozan has been accused of secretly recording inside the company.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Calling him a “Saudi spy,” however, goes beyond what has been publicly established so far. The arrest and allegations concern a covert recording device; a foreign-intelligence motive has not been publicly proven. The FBI investigation remains ongoing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>OpenAI said it currently has no indication that the employee improperly accessed or removed model weights, source code or customer data. The company has not publicly explained exactly how its models helped identify the employee. The case adds another physical-security angle to a year in which BitcoinVersus.Tech has covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/07/28/openai-hacks-hugging-face-raises-new-questions-about-autonomous-ai/">OpenAI’s Hugging Face security incident</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/goodfires-inside-out-ai-monitors-watch-for-rogue-agents-before-they-act/">AI systems monitoring other AI agents</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/computer-security-thales-ai-cyberattacks-ai-defense-google-cloud/">AI-assisted cyber defense</a>.</p>
