<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>FPT and NAVER Cloud have signed a memorandum of understanding to expand AI Factory and GPU-as-a-service work across Asia-Pacific.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.navercorp.com/media/pressReleasesDetail?seq=10034740">NAVER’s official release</a> says the companies plan to combine GPU infrastructure, cloud technology and customer networks to pursue AI infrastructure opportunities in Korea, Japan, Vietnam and other APAC markets. Engineering teams are expected to work together on GPU optimization, inference performance and deploying AI agents in production environments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://fptsoftware.us/-/media/project/fpt-software/fso/news/ai-factory.webp?modified=20260212063054" alt="FPT technician working inside an AI data center"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>FPT’s AI Factory infrastructure. Source: FPT Software.</em></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261011402382/en/FPT-and-NAVER-Cloud-Form-Strategic-Alliance-to-Fuel-AI-Innovation-Across-Asia-Pacific-and-Beyond">FPT announcement</a> adds that NAVER Cloud’s HyperCLOVA X model is expected to be offered to FPT enterprise customers through FPT AI Factory. The releases do not disclose financial terms.</p>
