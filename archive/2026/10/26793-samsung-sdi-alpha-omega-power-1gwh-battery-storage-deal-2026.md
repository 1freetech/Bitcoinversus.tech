<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a href="https://news.samsungsdi.com/global/articleView?seq=468">Samsung SDI</a> announced October 26, 2026, that its U.S. subsidiary agreed to supply <a href="https://aoprenewables.com/">Alpha Omega Power</a> with 1 gigawatt-hour (GWh) of grid-scale batteries.</strong> Combined with a separate 2025 order, the developer has contracted 1.5 GWh. Delivery is planned by year-end 2026, ahead of projects expected to begin construction in 2027.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>The new 1 GWh contract raises the partners’ combined orders to 1.5 GWh.</li><li>Samsung's SBB 1.7 systems are intended for projects in ERCOT and WECC power markets.</li><li>Contracted energy capacity does not establish installed output, commissioning dates or actual grid dispatch.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Indiana manufacturing for American power markets</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The order covers the <strong>Samsung Battery Box (SBB) 1.7</strong>, a containerized energy storage system using nickel-cobalt-aluminum (NCA) prismatic cells. Samsung says the cells and modules will come from the <a href="https://starplusenergyus.com/about/">StarPlus Energy</a> factory in Kokomo, Indiana. The company has not disclosed pricing, project locations or commissioning dates.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":26791,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/samsung-sdi-alpha-omega-power-sbb17-official-product-2026.png" alt="Samsung SDI SBB 1.7 battery container and NCA battery with the two companies' logos" class="wp-image-26791" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Official SBB 1.7 product rendering accompanying Samsung SDI's October 26 announcement; not a photograph of delivered equipment.</em></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Alpha Omega Power plans projects across the <strong>Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT)</strong> and <strong>Western Electricity Coordinating Council (WECC)</strong> regions. Grid batteries can shift electricity across hours and help respond to variations in demand. That is relevant to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/27/centerpoint-70-7-billion-grid-investment-houston-14gw-demand-2026/">Texas grid growth</a>, but this contract alone does not establish how many megawatts can be discharged at once.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the SBB 1.7 specification means</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Samsung says SBB 1.7 stores about <strong>17% more energy per 20-foot container</strong> than SBB 1.5. It also advertises anomaly monitoring, module-level fire-suppression technology and a 25-year design life. These are manufacturer specifications, not evidence of decades of measured operation. In a <a href="https://news.samsungsdi.com/global/press/view?seq=313">2025 product launch</a>, Samsung cited 6.14 MWh per SBB 1.7 unit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":26792,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/samsung-sdi-sbb17-replus-2025-exhibition-company-photo.jpg" alt="Samsung SDI representatives standing behind scale models of SBB energy storage containers at RE+ 2025" class="wp-image-26792" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Company-supplied photograph of the SBB lineup at RE+ 2025; historical product context, not the 2026 Alpha Omega Power deal.</em></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The distinction between energy and power matters: <strong>GWh measures stored energy</strong>, whereas MW indicates a discharge rate. Actual duration depends on the project's power conversion equipment and operating design. As previous BitcoinVersus coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/batteries-are-now-cheaper-than-gas-peakers-in-43-markets/">battery economics</a> explains, storage costs and grid value depend on more than nameplate energy capacity. For this order, delivery, construction and energization remain separate milestones.</p>
