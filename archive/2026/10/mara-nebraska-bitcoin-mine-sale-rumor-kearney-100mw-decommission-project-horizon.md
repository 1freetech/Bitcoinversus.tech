<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>A rumor that MARA has Bitcoin-mining sites for sale in Nebraska appears to be mixing a real asset transition with a claim that is not yet publicly confirmed.</strong> BitcoinVersus found no public broker listing or MARA filing stating that its Kearney, Nebraska, Bitcoin mine is simply “for sale.” What is documented is substantial: the existing 100 MW cryptocurrency operation at Kearney is slated to be decommissioned as MARA and Starwood Digital Ventures redevelop the energized site into a much larger data-center campus.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The rumor is therefore <strong>directionally connected to something real</strong>, but the public record supports “redevelopment, possible asset contribution or powered-land sale, and mining relocation” more strongly than it supports “MARA listed its Nebraska mines for sale.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Project Horizon Will Replace the Existing Kearney Mining Operation</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kearney City Council unanimously approved Project Horizon in September. Local reporting says the multi-phase development at Tech oNE Crossing will replace the existing cryptocurrency mining operation. Phase 1 reuses the <strong>100 MW of power already allocated to the crypto facility</strong>, and the mining operation is expected to be decommissioned after that first phase becomes operational.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The official <a href="https://www.projecthorizonkearney.com/">Project Horizon site</a> describes a MARA and Starwood Digital Ventures campus and confirms that Phase 1 requires no new 100 MW power allocation because it will reuse electricity currently serving the adjacent Bitcoin miner. Future phases could expand the campus far beyond that initial load.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The “For Sale” Rumor May Be Coming From MARA’s Starwood Agreement</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MARA’s February 26 <a href="https://ir.mara.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000950142-26-000532/eh260744046_8k.htm">SEC filing on its Starwood strategic agreement</a> is probably the most important document for understanding why sale rumors can emerge.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The agreement covers a specified list of MARA’s existing U.S. Bitcoin-mining data centers outside existing third-party joint ventures. Starwood is tasked with development work, permitting, power arrangements, marketing, and securing hyperscaler tenants. Depending on how an individual property advances, it can be contributed into a joint venture or, under specific conditions, MARA can be required to sell its rights to the powered land to Starwood.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The filing also says that when a property transitions, MARA can either continue Bitcoin mining through a rent-free arrangement while it still has mining rights or be compensated to relocate its mining operation. After defined lockout periods, either side may also have rights that can ultimately force a property sale, subject to contractual protections.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is materially different from a conventional commercial listing with a broker, asking price, and buyer packet. But it does mean <strong>asset sales are explicitly contemplated inside MARA’s broader conversion strategy</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Kearney Is Exactly the Kind of Site MARA Wants to Revalue</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The core asset is not just the ASIC fleet. It is the combination of land, substations, utility relationships, transmission access, power contracts, fiber, roads, and a site that already consumes industrial-scale electricity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is the same thesis BitcoinVersus covered in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/25/bitcoin-miners-shift-megawatts-from-asics-to-ai/">Bitcoin Miners Shift Megawatts From ASICs to AI</a>. Building a new hyperscale data-center interconnection from scratch can take years. A Bitcoin mine can arrive with energized land already proven under real load.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MARA has been moving aggressively in that direction. BitcoinVersus recently reported that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/bitcoin-mining-mara-sold-23093-btc-1-6b-power-ai-infrastructure/">MARA sold 23,093 BTC as it rebuilt its strategy around power and AI infrastructure</a>, while another project involves a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/02/bitcoin-mining-mara-100-million-deposit-2gw-texas-campus/">2 GW Texas powered-land campus</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Nebraska Is Bigger Than One 100 MW Mining Load</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MARA’s Nebraska footprint also grew earlier this year. In January, the company acquired an additional operational data center in central Nebraska with <strong>42 MW of total capacity</strong> for roughly $25 million. MARA described that acquisition as adjacent to its existing Nebraska site, expanding the campus capacity by approximately 40%.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That detail may be another reason the market rumor refers to “sites” in the plural. But the public Kearney redevelopment documents specifically identify the existing <strong>100 MW cryptocurrency operation</strong> as the load to be replaced by the first Project Horizon data-center phase. BitcoinVersus has not found a public document saying both Nebraska assets are being marketed together for outright sale.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Kearney Approved the Conversion</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://southeast.newschannelnebraska.com/story/363530628/kearney-city-council-unanimously-approves-project-horizon-data-center">News Channel Nebraska reported on September 9</a> that Kearney City Council unanimously approved Project Horizon. The approved development is designed to replace the cryptocurrency operation, beginning with the reuse of its existing 100 MW electrical capacity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The broader plan is much larger than the current mine. Public planning materials describe a campus that could ultimately reach hundreds of megawatts as later phases secure additional power. In other words, Kearney is not simply losing a mining load. MARA and Starwood are attempting to turn the same energized property into a larger long-duration digital-infrastructure asset.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>This Does Not Mean MARA Is Leaving Bitcoin Mining</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MARA continues to describe Bitcoin mining as the foundation of its broader energy and digital-infrastructure strategy. The company’s model increasingly treats mining as an exceptionally flexible first tenant for energized land: ASICs can monetize power while a higher-value data-center customer is still being permitted, financed, or contracted.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That flexibility also explains why the transition does not necessarily mean every miner at Kearney disappears permanently. The Starwood agreement contemplates MARA relocating mining equipment when a property is converted and compensating the company when it loses the ability to keep mining at a particular site.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the network level, MARA remains part of a public-miner sector that BitcoinVersus recently found accounts for a substantial share of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/public-miners-42-percent-bitcoin-hashrate/">global Bitcoin hashrate</a>. The strategic change is increasingly about <strong>where a megawatt earns the highest return</strong>, not whether MARA abandons Bitcoin altogether.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>So Is the Nebraska Mine for Sale?</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Not in the ordinary publicly documented sense—at least not yet.</strong> BitcoinVersus has not found a public commercial listing, disclosed asking price, sale memorandum, or MARA announcement saying the Kearney Bitcoin mine is currently being marketed to outside buyers as a standalone mining facility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What the evidence does show is more nuanced and arguably more important:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>MARA owns major powered infrastructure in Kearney.</li><li>The existing 100 MW crypto operation is slated for decommissioning after Project Horizon Phase 1.</li><li>That 100 MW power allocation will be reused by the new data center.</li><li>MARA’s Starwood agreement explicitly allows property contributions, relocation compensation, and powered-land sales under defined conditions.</li><li>The Kearney redevelopment has already received city approval.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What Comes Next</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The rumor becomes a confirmed sale story only if a broker listing appears, MARA identifies a buyer, a property transfer is disclosed, or an SEC filing says the Nebraska powered land is being sold under the Starwood structure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Until then, the accurate headline is that <strong>MARA’s 100 MW Kearney Bitcoin-mining operation is on a path toward decommissioning and reuse as data-center power—not that MARA has publicly listed the mine for sale.</strong></p>
