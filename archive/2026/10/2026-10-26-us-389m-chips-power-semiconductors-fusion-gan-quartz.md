# U.S. Proposes $389 Million for Next-Generation Power Semiconductors

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The <a href="https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/10/department-commerce-announces-letters-intent-4-companies-389-million-rd">U.S. Department of Commerce announced</a> on October 22, 2026, four letters of intent for <strong>up to $389 million</strong> in CHIPS Act research funding. The proposed awards target power semiconductors for fusion energy and AI infrastructure, plus ultra-high-purity quartz essential to semiconductor manufacturing. <strong>These are proposed awards, not finalized or disbursed grants.</strong></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Four companies signed letters of intent totaling up to $389 million.</li><li>Fusion power electronics, vertical gallium nitride and semiconductor-grade quartz are the priorities.</li><li>The government would receive minority, non-controlling, non-voting equity interests if final agreements are reached.</li><li>Award amounts, schedules and technical outcomes remain conditional.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why These Devices Matter</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Helion requires precisely switched high currents to drive and recover energy from fusion magnets. Commonwealth Fusion Systems is developing electronics for plasma heating, diagnostics and plant control. Its earlier engineering update below illustrates its SPARC hardware; the video predates this funding announcement.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qM9hZxmJr9Q]<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Vertical Semiconductor proposes <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/25/vertical-semiconductor-73-million-gan-2-5-mw-ai-rack-2026/">vertical GaN technology for a claimed 2.5 MW AI rack demonstration</a>—a development target, not a demonstrated commercial rack. Sibelco's quartz research supports crystal-growth crucibles and precision manufacturing components.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Power conversion losses ultimately become heat. That makes semiconductor packaging, switching performance and supply materials relevant to data centers as well as experimental energy plants. See our reporting on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/renesas-100v-gan-ai-servers-robots/">GaN in servers</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/rittal-bosch-800v-power-ai-racks/">800 V rack power</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/semiconductors-asm-ai-angstrom-atomic-precision/">atomic-scale chip manufacturing</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
