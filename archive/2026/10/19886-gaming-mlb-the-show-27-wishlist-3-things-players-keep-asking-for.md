<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With MLB The Show 26 now the current game, the next annual entry is MLB The Show 27—and franchise players are already building the wishlist.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitcoinVersus.tech checked current community discussions, Reddit wishlist threads and Operation Sports coverage to look for requests that keep repeating rather than one-off complaints. This is not a scientific poll, but three themes surfaced again and again: smarter Franchise simulation, year-to-year save continuity, and true expansion/relocation tools built around a stronger Stadium Creator.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://newsletter.operationsports.com/p/mlb-the-show-27-wishlist-from-big-to-small" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Operation Sports' dedicated MLB The Show 27 wishlist</a> makes a similar broader point: the series has improved parts of its Franchise foundation, but players still want the mode to feel more alive, connected and persistent.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MLB The Show's own account <a href="https://twitter.com/MLBTheShow/status/2027096427181347225" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">highlighted the Franchise additions in The Show 26</a>, including its new front-office experience. That gives San Diego Studio a stronger base for what players are asking to see next.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. Smarter Franchise AI and a deeper baseball economy</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The loudest recurring request is not simply “add more menus.” Players want Franchise to behave more like a believable baseball organization over many seasons.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In a representative <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/MLBTheShow/comments/1vgobwz/what_are_your_hopes_for_mlb_the_show_27_to_make/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">MLB The Show 27 community wishlist discussion</a>, recurring asks include a revamped Franchise mode, online Franchise, three-team trades, cash considerations, draft-pick transactions, stronger player development, improved roster logic and more meaningful league news.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From a coding perspective, that means better decision models for CPU front offices. A general manager AI has to value major-league production, prospects, contracts, service time, positional need, competitive window and future roster flexibility at the same time. The difficult part is not making one realistic trade; it is keeping 30 organizations believable after five, ten or fifteen simulated seasons.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is the same long-horizon simulation problem BitcoinVersus.tech recently examined in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/gaming-madden-nfl-27-is-rewriting-the-logic-behind-franchise-simulation/">Madden NFL 27's Franchise economy</a>. Sports games increasingly need autonomous front offices that can reason across multiple seasons rather than simply react to today's roster rating.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. Bring back year-to-year saves</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The second request keeps resurfacing because Franchise is supposed to be a long-term mode: let players carry the same save into the next annual release.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For a player, the feature sounds simple—open the new game and continue. For a development team, it is a persistent-state migration problem. Every player ID, contract, draft class, custom roster, progression value, retired record, injury state, team setting and created asset has to survive changes to the next game's data model.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That requires versioned save schemas and migration logic, similar to upgrading a database without losing the information already stored inside it. If San Diego Studio changes how contracts, scouting or progression work in The Show 27, an old The Show 26 save still has to map cleanly into the new structure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The payoff is huge for simulation players because continuity changes the meaning of every decision. A prospect drafted in 2026 can become a veteran in 2034 without the player's universe being reset simply because a new retail version shipped.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. True expansion, relocation and Stadium Creator 2.0</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The third recurring cluster is customization at the league level: expansion teams, relocation, realignment and a less restricted Stadium Creator.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Community requests repeatedly mention movable bullpens, more flexible foul territory, improved wall textures, fewer prop restrictions, better uniform and logo tools, and the ability to build an expansion club rather than merely reskin an existing organization.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is much more than an art-tool request. True expansion would force the simulation engine to generate schedules for a changing number of teams, rebalance divisions, maintain playoff logic, create expansion drafts, redistribute player pools and keep historical records coherent after the league structure changes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Stadium creation also touches gameplay. Wall geometry, foul territory, bullpen placement and field dimensions influence collision detection, home-run logic, defensive routes and park effects. A more powerful creator therefore has to remain compatible with the baseball simulation underneath it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why these three keep coming back</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>All three requests are really asking for the same thing: persistence. Players want CPU teams to remember how to build a roster, saves to survive from one annual game to the next, and leagues to evolve instead of remaining frozen at 30 fixed organizations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is why the AI side matters. Smarter baseball simulation is not just better fielding reactions. It includes front-office behavior, prospect development, lineup construction, contract valuation and the thousands of decisions that happen between games.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitcoinVersus.tech recently looked at how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/gaming-college-football-27-update-shows-how-football-ai-chooses-who-to-block/">College Football 27 uses AI-style assignment logic during individual plays</a>. MLB The Show's next opportunity is to push that same systems thinking across entire seasons and organizations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Baseball is also becoming one of the most data-rich sports in the real world. Our recent look at <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/26/sports-statcast-can-now-measure-a-first-basemans-toughest-scoops/">Statcast's new first-base tracking metrics</a> shows how detailed modern baseball modeling has become. A future The Show can use that culture of measurement to make its virtual league feel deeper too.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The MLB The Show 27 wishlist, condensed</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1.</strong> Smarter Franchise AI with deeper trades, roster building and long-term team logic.<br><strong>2.</strong> Year-to-year saves that preserve Franchise and Road to the Show careers across releases.<br><strong>3.</strong> Expansion, relocation and a much more flexible Stadium Creator tied into league realignment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Presentation upgrades, richer league news and more varied animations also came up repeatedly in the discussions we checked. But if The Show 27 can make the league smarter, persistent and structurally customizable, it would address three of the clearest themes franchise players keep returning to.</p>
