<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin ASIC efficiency continues improving, but three often-quoted figures need careful labeling. BITMAIN rates its S23 Hyd. complete miner at 9.5 J/TH and S23 XP Hyd. at 8.9 J/TH. Bitdeer separately reports 6–7 J/TH for its SEAL04 chip under a low-voltage, ultra-power-saving test condition.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Three Different Disclosures</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BITMAIN unveiled the S23 Hyd. in May 2025 and announced the S23 XP Hyd. in August 2026. Bitdeer dates the SEAL04 chip release to Q4 2025. These events belong on a chronological timeline, but chip and whole-miner efficiency should not be treated as the same measurement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Important disclaimer: 6–7 J/TH is a chip-level validation result, not a verified production mining rig delivering 6.5 J/TH at the wall. <a href="https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-launches-sealminer-a4-series-bitcoin-mining-rigs">Bitdeer launched SEAL04-powered A4 rigs in April 2026</a>, with the A4 Ultra Hydro rated at 9.45 J/TH as a complete miner. The distinction is the measurement boundary, not whether SEAL04-powered hardware exists.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The newer BITMAIN S23 XP Hyd. is rated at 600 TH/s and 5,340 watts, or 8.9 J/TH. <a href="https://shop.bitmain.com/zh/product/detail?pid=000202608201636197889T3Y4SxC0660">Its store listing schedules shipment from November 2026</a>, so manufacturer specifications should not be confused with independently measured operating-fleet results.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the Charts Show</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The new chart marks Bitdeer's 6–7 J/TH laboratory chip range separately from the two complete BITMAIN machines. Using 6.5 J/TH as a midpoint is only a visual convenience; it is not the measured energy efficiency of a commercial Bitdeer mining rig.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A sigmoidal curve is not suitable for just three differently measured values. Such an analysis requires a larger, comparable dataset covering chip tests, production wall-plug readings, and operating conditions. The future milestone to watch is a reproducible sub-7 J/TH complete miner validated during real operation.</p>
