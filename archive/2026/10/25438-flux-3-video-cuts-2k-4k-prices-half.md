<p>The price change is easiest to understand per second. On the <a href="https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-video">official FLUX 3 Video page</a>, standard text- or image-to-video generation is listed at <strong>$0.40 per second for QHD/2K</strong> and <strong>$0.80 per second for UHD/4K</strong>. At those rates, a 10-second generation costs about <strong>$4 at 2K</strong> or <strong>$8 at 4K</strong>. Native audio is included.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>FLUX 3 is a multimodal model that works across video, audio, images and action prediction. Its video system supports text-to-video, image-to-video, editing, keyframes, multi-scene generation, multilingual dialogue and clip continuation. That broader multimodal direction overlaps with the shift we covered in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/neura-launches-multimodal-ai-model-for-robots/">NEURA’s multimodal robotics model</a>, where one model is expected to combine several forms of input rather than treat each one as an isolated pipeline.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For creators, lower high-resolution pricing changes how often a clip can be regenerated before production costs become restrictive. It also matters for applications that call video models through APIs, where <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/08/10/model-routing/">model routing</a> and per-request economics can determine which generator is selected. The same cost pressure is already reshaping other generative media categories, including the AI music systems we examined in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/culture-suno-v6-rebuilds-ai-music-around-licensed-songs-from-warner-bmg-and-believe/">Suno V6</a>.</p>

<p>The key limitation is wording: <strong>“up to 2× faster”</strong> describes a maximum improvement, not a guaranteed speedup for every prompt, resolution or workload. Black Forest Labs has also tied the 50% price reduction specifically to native 2K and 4K generation. The update is therefore best read as a serving-efficiency improvement with a direct price effect, rather than a new model release.</p>
