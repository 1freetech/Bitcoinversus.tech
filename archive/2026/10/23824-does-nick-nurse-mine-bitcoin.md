<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A rumor claims Philadelphia 76ers head coach Nick Nurse mines Bitcoin or runs mining servers. BitcoinVersus.Tech could not verify it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We searched direct interviews, official profiles and targeted web/social results. The <a href="https://www.nba.com/sixers/76ers-coaches/nick-nurse">76ers’ official Nick Nurse profile</a> identifies him as head coach and covers his background, but it does not say he owns or operates Bitcoin mining hardware. We found no first-party statement from Nurse or credible report confirming the claim. That does not prove he has never mined privately; it means the public claim currently lacks verifiable evidence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The clearest documented Bitcoin connection around the Sixers is former team executive Daryl Morey. In a <a href="https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/06/03/daryl-morey-on-crypto-and-nfts-its-the-start-of-a-major-major-trend">2021 CoinDesk interview</a>, Morey said he had accumulated Bitcoin by 2010–2011 and had given Bitcoin away on Twitter in 2011. Morey’s Bitcoin history should not be attributed to Nurse.</p>
