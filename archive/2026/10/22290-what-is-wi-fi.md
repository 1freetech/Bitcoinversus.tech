<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Wi-Fi is a way for devices to connect to a network without a cable.</strong></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Your phone, laptop, tablet, TV, game console, and many other devices can use Wi-Fi to talk to a nearby router or access point.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":22288,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} --><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/bitcoinversus_wifi_body_1200x675.jpg?w=1024" alt="Home Wi-Fi router connecting nearby devices including a laptop, phone, television, and game controller." class="wp-image-22288" /></figure><!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Is Wi-Fi the Internet?</strong></h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Not exactly.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Wi-Fi is the wireless connection between your device and a nearby network. The internet is the much larger network beyond your home or building.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>You can think of Wi-Fi like a short wireless bridge. Your phone crosses that bridge to reach your router, and the router usually connects you to the internet.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aL2___M5S9I","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true} --><figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aL2___M5S9I]</div><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Cisco gives a beginner-friendly introduction to Wi-Fi.</em></figcaption></figure><!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How Does Wi-Fi Work?</strong></h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Wi-Fi sends information through the air using radio signals.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Your router sends a wireless signal. Your phone or laptop receives it and can send information back. This happens so quickly that a webpage, video, or game can feel almost instant.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>That is why you do not need an Ethernet cable plugged into your phone to get online at home.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What Is the Router Doing?</strong></h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The router is the small box that helps move information between your devices and other networks.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>When you open a website on your phone, the request can travel over Wi-Fi to the router. The router sends it onward, and the answer comes back the same way.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>BitcoinVersus.Tech has a more detailed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/26/installing-and-configuring-basic-wired-wireless-soho-networks/"><strong>wired and wireless network overview</strong></a> if you want to go deeper later.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why Does Wi-Fi Get Weaker Farther Away?</strong></h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Radio signals get weaker as they travel.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Walls, floors, distance, and other wireless devices can also make the signal harder to receive. That is why Wi-Fi may be strong near the router but weaker in a far bedroom or garage.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Does Wi-Fi Mean “Wireless Fidelity”?</strong></h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>No. Wi-Fi is a name, not an acronym for “Wireless Fidelity.”</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>It became the familiar name used for wireless networking products that work with the Wi-Fi family of standards.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Easy Way to Remember It</strong></h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Wi-Fi lets nearby devices connect without a cable.</strong></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Your device talks to a router or access point through the air. That network can then connect you to the internet.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>That is the basic idea.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
