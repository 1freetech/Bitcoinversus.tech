<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>America’s AI infrastructure boom could become the largest major investment buildout in U.S. history relative to the economy, according to new Brookings research.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.brookings.edu/articles/financing-the-ai-buildout/">Brookings Papers on Economic Activity study</a> estimates spending on data centers, power systems, networking and specialized chips could total <strong>$10.3 trillion from 2025 through 2032</strong>, averaging <strong>3.63% of U.S. GDP per year</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/KobeissiLetter/status/2103563602200281411]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>The Kobeissi Letter compares Brookings’ AI estimate with earlier U.S. infrastructure booms.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Study author Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh says the comparable railroad boom averaged about 2.2% of GDP, highways and telecom about 1.1%, canals 0.66% and electrification 0.5%. He also cautions that historical estimates are not perfectly harmonized and that some railroad peak years exceeded 4% of GDP.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A newer <a href="https://www.nber.org/papers/w35865">NBER working-paper version</a> uses a different buildout assumption and reports nearly $9 trillion, or 3.2% of GDP annually, showing that the exact total depends on the scenario.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
