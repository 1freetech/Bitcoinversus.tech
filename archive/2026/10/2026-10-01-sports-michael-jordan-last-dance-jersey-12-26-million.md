<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A piece of Michael Jordan's final Chicago Bulls championship run has set another collecting record. His white No. 23 jersey from Game 3 of the 1998 NBA Finals sold for approximately <strong>₿145.6 ($12.26 million)</strong>, using a contemporaneous Bitcoin price near $84,200. Because Bitcoin moves continuously, the BTC figure is approximate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The result was confirmed by <a href="https://www.joopiter.com/departments/sports">JOOPITER</a>, Pharrell Williams' digital auction house, which lists the sale as a world auction record for an NBA game-worn jersey. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/sports/michael-jordans-last-dance-jersey-sells-record-1226m--flm-2026-10-01/">Independent reporting</a> also confirmed the $12.26 million result and that it surpassed the previous Jordan memorabilia record.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A jersey from the Last Dance</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Jordan wore the jersey on June 7, 1998, when Chicago beat the Utah Jazz 96-54 in Game 3 of the NBA Finals. Jordan scored 24 points as the Bulls took a 2-1 series lead on the way to their sixth championship in eight seasons.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">One bid, one enormous record</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The jersey carried a $10 million to $15 million estimate and ultimately realized $12.26 million. Its significance comes from more than Jordan's name: it is tied to a specific Finals victory during the season later immortalized as “The Last Dance,” and JOOPITER describes it as the only publicly known white game-winning jersey from the 1998 Finals to surface.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>CTV News also circulated the sale as a new record for Jordan memorabilia, reinforcing how quickly the auction result moved from the collectibles market into mainstream sports coverage.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Jordan's 1998 Finals run combines all three.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
