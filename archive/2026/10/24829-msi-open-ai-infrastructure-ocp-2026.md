<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MSI is expanding its open data-center and AI infrastructure lineup at the 2026 OCP Global Summit with new 21-inch Open Rack v3 systems, modular DC-MHS building blocks, liquid-cooled GPU servers, and a deskside AI system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://www.msi.com/news/detail/MSI-Showcases-Open-Data-Center-and-AI-Infrastructure-at-2026-OCP-Global-Summit-150055" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>October 14 announcement</strong></a>, MSI said its new ORv3 two-node platforms use AMD EPYC 9006 Series processors and support up to 600 W per node. The air-cooled CD281-S4091-X2 uses a 2OU design, while the liquid-cooled CD182-S6091-X2 fits two nodes into 1OU. Both use 48 V busbar power and PCIe 6.0.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The open-hardware angle matters because operators are trying to raise rack density without locking every generation to a completely new chassis. MSI’s DC-MHS portfolio spans AMD and Intel platforms, while its new rack-scale AI configuration adds liquid cooling, 400 GbE networking and dense GPU systems. That connects directly with the broader shift toward <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/kioxia-cm9-liquid-cooled-enterprise-ssds/"><strong>liquid-cooled infrastructure</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/hardware-hpe-proliant-gen13-256-core-amd-epyc-venice-pcie-6-ai-servers/"><strong>PCIe 6 servers</strong></a> and modular <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/osdctc-005-server-hardware-troubleshooting-post-bmc-logs-dimms-drives-psus-fault-isolation/"><strong>server management</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An independent <a href="https://www.storagereview.com/news/msi-ocp-2026-liquid-cooled-cg681-s6093-mgx-rack-32-rtx-pro-6000" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>StorageReview inspection of MSI’s OCP rack</strong></a> says the 48RU setup uses four liquid-cooled CG681-S6093 systems and can hold up to 32 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs when fully populated.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>MSI is also showing the XpertStation WS300, which brings a GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra system with up to 748 GB of coherent memory and dual 400 GbE networking to a desktop-sized platform. The larger story is not one product: it is the push to make AI infrastructure more modular from the desk to the rack.</p>
