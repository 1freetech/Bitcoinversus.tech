<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Siemens Energy CEO Christian Bruch says Saudi Arabia can connect AI data centers to electricity faster than the United States, arguing that speed-to-power is becoming a major competitive advantage in the AI infrastructure race.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an October 12 interview reported by <a href="https://www.arabnews.com/eye-on-ai/saudi-arabia-can-power-ai-data-centers-at-faster-pace-than-us-siemens-energy-ceo-3005885">Arab News via Asharq Bloomberg</a>, Bruch said even a six-month difference in energizing a data center can matter as AI capacity expands. The comparison is his assessment, not an independently verified ranking of U.S. and Saudi interconnection times.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hardware buildout is concrete. Siemens Energy is investing about $150 million to expand its Dammam site by roughly 60,000 square meters and add a factory for large power transformers. The project is expected to create about 130–150 jobs and supply equipment for Saudi Arabia’s 380-kilovolt transmission grid. <a href="https://www.siemens-energy.com/sa/en/home.html">Siemens Energy says</a> its Dammam footprint is scaling to about 75,000 square meters with more than 500 employees.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
