## Ranking method

Ranked by published peak sequential read speed; peak sequential write speed breaks ties. Performance varies by capacity, workload, cooling and platform.

## Ranking

7. Sabrent Rocket 5 — 14,000 MB/s read / 12,000 MB/s write
6. Crucial T705 — 14,500 / 12,700 MB/s
5. MSI Spatium M580 FROZR — 14,600 / 12,700 MB/s
4. Samsung 9100 PRO — 14,800 / 13,400 MB/s
3. Crucial T710 — up to 14,900 / 13,800 MB/s depending on capacity
2. WD_BLACK SN8100 — 14,900 / 14,000 MB/s
