<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>SK hynix is proposing a different way to build AI racks: put <strong>memory at the center</strong> instead of treating it as capacity permanently attached to individual CPUs and GPUs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the 2026 OCP Global Summit, SK hynix Senior Vice President Hoshik Kim presented its Memory-centric AI Rack System, or <strong>MARS</strong>. In an <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/skhynix-america-inc"><strong>official SK hynix America update</strong></a>, the company said the architecture uses CXL to connect CPUs and GPUs to a rack-level memory pool and enable zero-copy data sharing.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The idea is to reduce stranded memory. In a processor-centric rack, one server can run short while another has unused capacity. A shared pool lets systems allocate capacity where it is needed without adding another expensive accelerator just to gain more attached memory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://en.sedaily.ai/international/2026/10/13/sk-hynix-pitches-memory-centric-ai-rack-to-break-bottleneck"><strong>Seoul Economic Daily</strong></a> reports that SK hynix is supporting customized MARS testing with partners. That makes MARS an active architecture program, not evidence of a broadly shipping rack product yet.</p>

<p><em>A second discussion highlights the limits of CXL: it can expand and share capacity, but it does not match HBM bandwidth and is better viewed as a complementary memory tier.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The direction fits a wider shift already visible across the industry. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/marvell-structera-x-cxl-9tb-memory/">Marvell’s CXL chips supporting up to 9 TB of memory</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/astera-labs-leo-x-series-ai-memory-cxl-3-2/">Astera Labs’ CXL 3.2 memory platform</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/sk-hynix-expands-ai-memory-beyond-hbm/">SK hynix’s push beyond HBM</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MARS does not eliminate HBM. Instead, it reflects a growing effort to make memory capacity more shareable and to move less data across the network as AI inference becomes more memory-intensive.</p>
