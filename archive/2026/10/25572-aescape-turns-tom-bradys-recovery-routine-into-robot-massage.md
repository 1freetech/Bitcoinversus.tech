<p><a href="https://www.aescape.com/brady-protocol">Aescape has launched the Brady Protocol</a>, a 30-minute robotic deep-tissue recovery session co-developed with Tom Brady and now available across its massage-table network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The program targets the posterior chain from the calves through the traps. Before each session, Aescape’s system maps the user’s body, then its robotic arms follow the fixed recovery sequence while adapting stroke placement to the person’s proportions. Users can change pressure or pause the session in real time.</p>

<p><a href="https://athletechnews.com/tom-brady-aescape-ai-powered-massage-recovery-protocol/">Athletech News reports</a> that Brady helped shape the sequence, speed and pressure profile during development. Aescape says sessions start at $39, with pricing varying by location. The company frames the system as physical AI for repeatable bodywork rather than a replacement for clinical treatment.</p>
