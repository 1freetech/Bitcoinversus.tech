<p>Rice Robotics has launched <strong>TOMO</strong>, a wheel-based humanoid designed for classrooms and supervised STEM learning. <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/15/3381644/0/en/axonex-ai-group-joins-flair-s-embodied-ai-international-applications-hub-and-launches-education-humanoid-tomo.html">Axonex AI announced the robot on October 15</a> as Rice Robotics Hong Kong joined a new embodied-AI training and validation hub operated with Hong Kong’s FLAIR robotics center.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>TOMO has an expressive face, an interactive chest display and two-finger parallel grippers. The company says it is intended for interactive reading, programming, STEM exercises, supervised robotics exploration, light book handling and incident alerts. <a href="https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/axonex-ai-joint-venture-joins-hong-kong-embodied-ai-hub-93CH-4882956">Independent coverage of the announcement</a> confirms those stated classroom functions.</p>
