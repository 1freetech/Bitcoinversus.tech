<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A Delaware jury delivered a <strong>mixed verdict</strong> in the latest Qualcomm–Arm licensing fight, finding for Qualcomm on two claims while siding with Arm on four others.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Qualcomm said in its <a href="https://www.qualcomm.com/news/releases/2026/10/qualcomm-statement-on-trial-verdict-win"><strong>October 13 statement</strong></a> that the jury found Arm breached its Architectural License Agreement and intentionally disrupted Qualcomm’s business. Reuters reported that one interference finding involved Qualcomm’s relationship with Meta. The jury awarded Qualcomm <strong>$2 in nominal damages</strong>.</p>

<p><em>The hardware community quickly focused on the unusual split verdict and the symbolic $2 award.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Arm framed the result differently. In its own <a href="https://newsroom.arm.com/news/arm-qualcomm-legal-dispute"><strong>statement</strong></a>, Arm said the jury found for it on four claims and awarded only $1 on each of Qualcomm’s two successful claims. Arm also said the verdict supported its position that it acted in good faith under a separate technology license agreement.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>A second discussion highlights why both companies can point to parts of the verdict as wins.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ruling resolves the jury phase that BitcoinVersus.Tech covered when <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/qualcomm-arm-jury-deadlocks-as-chip-licensing-fight-heads-into-next-week/">deliberations deadlocked last week</a>. The broader dispute matters because Qualcomm’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/qualcomms-new-snapdragon-chips-bring-5-ghz-cpus-and-on-device-ai/">Oryon-based Snapdragon processors</a> compete in markets built heavily around Arm’s instruction-set ecosystem, while Arm itself is pushing newer designs such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/arm-c2-sme2-matrix-ai-mobile-cpu/">Arm C2</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The $2 award makes the immediate financial effect minimal, but the licensing findings could still matter as the companies continue negotiating technology access and royalties.</p>
