<p>Xbox has announced the <strong>Xbox Nano Controller</strong>, its smallest first-party controller yet. The compact gamepad is a little over <strong>5 inches long</strong>, weighs <strong>3.5 ounces</strong>, and still carries all <strong>18 standard gameplay inputs</strong>. Xbox says it is aimed at younger players, smaller hands, and anyone who wants a controller that is easier to carry. The company revealed the hardware in an <a href="https://twitter.com/xbox/status/2110400314931388482">official Xbox post on X</a> and detailed it on <a href="https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/14/xbox-nano-controller/">Xbox Wire</a>.</p>

<p>The Nano includes an <strong>internal rechargeable battery</strong>, a programmable <strong>Action Button</strong> for shortcuts such as screenshots and gameplay capture, plus <strong>Xbox Wireless and Bluetooth</strong>. Microsoft lists compatibility with Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Windows, Android and iOS. A USB-C cable is required for charging but is sold separately.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the United States, the <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/d/XBOX-Nano-Controller/8wg5vqp0x4g3">Microsoft Store</a> lists the controller at <strong>$49.99</strong> with a <strong>November 4, 2026</strong> release date. Preorders are open now.</p>
