<p><a href="https://twitter.com/Polymarket/status/2110396814965010937">Polymarket posted on October 14</a> that a “vibecoder” had reverse-engineered Microsoft Office with AI and released free alternatives to Word, Excel and PowerPoint. The core story is real, but the wording needs one correction: the ArtCraft team describes WordCraft, GridCraft and DeckCraft as <a href="https://github.com/storytold">clean-room reimplementations written from scratch in Rust</a>, not decompiled Microsoft binaries.</p>

<p>WordCraft targets the familiar Word workflow and DOCX files. GridCraft recreates an Excel-style spreadsheet environment with formulas, charts and PivotTables. DeckCraft is the PowerPoint-style presentation app. The repositories are open source, cross-platform and still early enough that compatibility, stability and real-world document fidelity remain important tests. <a href="https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/creator-free-adobe-suite-microsoft-office">Futurism reported on the project today</a> as the latest extension of the same AI-assisted software-building wave.</p>

<p>The bigger signal is not that Office has already been replaced. It is that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/vibe-coder-meme-goes-viral-again/">vibe coding</a> is moving from small demos toward larger desktop applications. BitcoinVersus.Tech has also tracked <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/google-play-private-ai-built-android-apps/">AI-built apps</a> and tools that let <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/coding-google-ai-assistants-live-official-docs-developer-knowledge-api-mcp/">AI coding assistants read live technical documentation</a>. The hard part now is less “can AI generate the interface?” and more whether these projects can survive years of edge cases, file-format quirks, security review and maintenance.</p>
