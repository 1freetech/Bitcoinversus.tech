<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Renesas has launched its first family of <strong>100V enhancement-mode GaN power transistors</strong>, targeting AI servers, humanoid robots, factory automation and industrial motor drives. The October 6 release expands the company’s GaN portfolio into lower-voltage power stages where switching speed, efficiency and compact size matter most.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The four new REXGaN devices span roughly <strong>5 mΩ down to 1.2 mΩ RDS(on)</strong>. Renesas says they deliver up to <strong>35% lower hard-switching figure of merit</strong> and up to <strong>63% lower soft-switching figure of merit</strong> than comparable GaN devices. Its technical page says the platform can cut switching losses by as much as <strong>70%</strong> and double power density versus conventional silicon MOSFETs in some designs.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
