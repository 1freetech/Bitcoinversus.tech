<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Baltimore beat Tennessee 24-18 on Sunday, but the Ravens left the stadium with a bigger question than the final score: Lamar Jackson’s left ankle. Jackson suffered the injury on a sack near the end of the first half, did not return, and later left M&amp;T Bank Stadium in a walking boot. <a href="https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/lamar-jackson-ankle-injury-ravens-titans-tyler-huntley">The Ravens’ official postgame report</a> said Head Coach Jesse Minter did not yet have a firm diagnosis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before the injury, Jackson was in control. He completed 15 of 20 passes for 222 yards and two touchdowns while adding 20 rushing yards, helping Baltimore build a 24-7 halftime lead. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/sports/ravens-qb-lamar-jackson-ankle-questionable-return-vs-titans--flm-2026-10-04/">Reuters reported</a> that Tyler Huntley replaced him for the second half, completing 8 of 9 passes for 63 yards as the Ravens held on without scoring again.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NFL Network insider Ian Rapoport <a href="https://twitter.com/RapSheet/status/2106845407552659503">reported on X that Jackson is believed to be day-to-day</a> and that the ankle will be monitored and treated during the coming week. That is encouraging, but Baltimore’s own postgame language remained cautious because the team had not yet completed its evaluation.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Baltimore Won, but the Second Half Looked Different</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Ravens entered halftime up 24-7 after one of Jackson’s sharpest halves of the season. Without him, the offense became far more conservative. Huntley protected the football and kept the operation functional, but Baltimore did not score in the final two quarters while Tennessee cut the margin to six points.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That immediate drop-off is why quarterback health changes an NFL team’s outlook so quickly. BitcoinVersus.Tech saw the same depth issue from another angle in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/sports-qb3-washington-mariota-kaliakmanis-injury/">Washington’s move to its third quarterback after Marcus Mariota went down</a>. One injury can turn a normal game plan into a survival exercise within minutes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Jackson’s Mobility Makes the Ankle Especially Important</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jackson’s value is not limited to designed runs. His ability to escape pressure changes protection rules, coverage spacing and the geometry of every broken play. Even a relatively short-term ankle problem can matter if it limits the burst and lateral movement that make those plays possible.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is also where modern football analysis is becoming more detailed. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/sports-nfl-ai-run-schemes-blocking-assignments-next-gen-stats/">the NFL’s AI system for identifying run schemes and blocking assignments</a>. Quarterback mobility adds another layer because a play that begins as a pass can become a scramble, an improvised throw or a designed read based on a single defender’s leverage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Week 4 already produced another major individual performance when <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/sports-ceedee-lamb-17-catch-career-day-cowboys-texans/">CeeDee Lamb caught 17 passes and scored the winning touchdown for Dallas</a>. Jackson’s afternoon was moving toward a similarly dominant headline before the ankle injury changed the story.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For now, the key point is uncertainty rather than panic. Baltimore won the game, Jackson was able to remain involved on the sideline for part of the second half, and the first national report described him as day-to-day. The next meaningful update will come when the Ravens determine how much swelling, instability or ligament involvement the ankle shows after further evaluation.</p>
