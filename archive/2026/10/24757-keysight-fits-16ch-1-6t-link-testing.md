<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Keysight has expanded its 1.6T Ethernet interconnect testing lineup with FITS-16CH, a 2RU system built to check the error performance of high-speed copper and optical links before they reach large AI data-center deployments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261014439659/en/Keysight-Expands-Active-and-Passive-1.6T-Copper-Interconnect-Validation-for-Scale-Out-Data-Centers" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">October 14 announcement</a>, Keysight said FITS-16CH provides 16 independent transmit and 16 independent receive channels for 53 Gb/s, 106 Gb/s and 212.5 Gb/s PAM4 signaling. The system can measure real-time bit-error-rate performance and forward-error-correction behavior across two groups of eight transmit and eight receive channels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The company says the platform supports active electrical cables, active copper cables, direct-attach copper, active optical cables and several optical-transceiver types. Keysight’s <a href="https://www.keysight.com/my/en/products/interconnect-test/functional-interconnect-test-solutions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">product documentation</a> lists model 941-1030 and says the chassis can be used for product development, quality assurance and manufacturing tests.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The timing matters because the surrounding ecosystem is moving quickly toward denser 1.6T links. BitcoinVersus.Tech has recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/keysight-1-6t-ethernet-link-layer-retry-ocp-2026/">Keysight’s 1.6T link-recovery work</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/marvell-extends-1-6t-ai-copper-links-beyond-six-meters/">Marvell’s longer-reach 1.6T copper links</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/ufispace-1-6t-liquid-cooled-ai-switch/">UfiSpace’s 1.6T AI switch</a>. FITS-16CH addresses a different part of that chain: measuring whether the physical interconnects can hold their error margins at the lane speeds those systems require.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>FITS-16CH is test equipment, not a bandwidth upgrade by itself. Its value will depend on how well it helps engineers find marginal cables, modules and lane behavior before those components are deployed at scale.</p>
