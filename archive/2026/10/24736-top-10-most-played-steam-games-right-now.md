<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Steam’s live player chart is unusually top-heavy this morning. At 6:15 a.m. on October 14, Counter-Strike 2 was above 1.18 million concurrent players, while Dota 2 and PUBG were both above 760,000.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These numbers come directly from <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/stats/stats/">Steam’s official game and player statistics page</a> and will continue changing throughout the day.</p>
