<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CoinShares has launched its first commercial OnChain Strategies deployment, letting Safe enterprise users access professionally managed stablecoin-yield strategies without handing custody of their assets to CoinShares.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/14/3380316/0/en/coinshares-launches-onchain-asset-management-business-through-strategic-partnership-with-safe.html">October 14 announcement</a>, CoinShares said the new model combines DeFi yield with returns from tokenized real-world assets. Safe provides the self-custody account layer, while Railnet supplies the on-chain operating infrastructure through Kiln’s integration with Safe.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The key distinction is custody. CoinShares designs the strategies and risk framework but does not take possession of client assets. Safe says its smart accounts are <a href="https://safe.global/">self-custodial smart-contract wallets</a>, so users retain control while allocating stablecoins into the managed strategies.</p>
