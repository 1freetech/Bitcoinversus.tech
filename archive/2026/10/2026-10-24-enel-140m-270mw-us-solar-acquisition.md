# Enel Completes $140 Million Purchase of 270 MW U.S. Solar Portfolio

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item --><li>Enel finalized its $140 million purchase of seven operating U.S. solar plants on October 2, 2026.</li><!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item --><li>The portfolio totals approximately 270 MWdc and produces roughly 400 GWh annually.</li><!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item --><li>The acquisition brings Enel into Virginia, North Carolina and South Carolina.</li><!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item --><li>Enel forecasts approximately $20 million in additional annual ordinary EBITDA; that is an estimate, not a reported result.</li><!-- /wp:list-item --></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Italian electricity group <a href="https://www.enel.com/media/explore/search-press-releases/press/2026/10/enel-finalizes-the-acquisition-of-a-portfolio-of-approximately-270-mw-of-solar-plants-in-the-united-states">Enel confirmed on October 2</a> that its Enel Green Power North America subsidiary completed the acquisition of seven operating photovoltaic plants from a U.S. utility. The portfolio adds approximately <strong>270 megawatts of direct-current (MWdc) solar capacity</strong> for a stated enterprise value of about <strong>$140 million</strong>. The seller was not identified in Enel's announcement.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Where the Solar Plants Are</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The <a href="https://www.enelnorthamerica.com/about-us/newsroom/search-press/press/2026/05/solar-plants-acquisition">original May 18 acquisition agreement</a> identifies two facilities in Virginia totaling more than 120 MWdc, one in North Carolina exceeding 90 MWdc, and four in South Carolina totaling approximately 50 MWdc. These are already operating plants, not newly announced construction projects.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Together, the assets average approximately <strong>0.4 terawatt-hours (400 GWh) annually</strong>, according to Enel. That is annual energy production, not an assertion that the projects continuously deliver 270 MW. Our earlier <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/energy-capacity-factor-explained-nameplate-efficiency-reliability/">capacity-factor explainer</a> explains why generation ratings and electricity output must be kept separate.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Enel Is Paying</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Enel reports a transaction value of approximately <strong>$140 million</strong> and an impact on group net financial debt of about <strong>$180 million</strong>, including lease-related debt. Those are different financial measures, not two purchase prices. Management expects an additional <strong>$20 million per year in ordinary EBITDA</strong>; actual future earnings may differ.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The company describes the purchase as a <em>brownfield</em> strategy: buying existing power assets rather than waiting for a new project to navigate land, permitting, construction and interconnection. Enel's <a href="https://www.enel.com/media/explore/search-news/news/2026/10/renewables-us-enel-strategy">October U.S. renewables strategy update</a> also discusses an additional 810 MW solar portfolio closed in September, a separate transaction.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Operating Solar Capacity Matters</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Solar investments sit within a broader electricity-market shift. Our prior report on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/energy-exus-715mw-solar-wisconsin-louisiana-data-centers/">Exus's 715 MW U.S. solar acquisition</a> examines another route into operating or advanced-stage assets. For power buyers, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/energy-what-is-power-purchase-agreement-ppa-wind-solar/">power purchase agreements</a> and the daily variability of solar output remain crucial considerations. Solar alone is not automatically 24-hour firm power, as explored in our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/can-solar-power-run-a-data-center-24-7-or-nah/">solar-powered data-center explainer</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The <a href="https://www.enelnorthamerica.com/about-us/newsroom/search-press/press/2026/10/solar-plants-acquisition-finalization">Enel North America closing statement</a> gives the acquisition's state-by-state distribution. The accompanying U.S. Department of Energy video explains how photovoltaic modules convert sunlight into electricity; it is background education, not footage of these specific acquired plants.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
