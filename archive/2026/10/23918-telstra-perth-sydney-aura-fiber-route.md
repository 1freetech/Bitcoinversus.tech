## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Telstra has switched on the Perth-to-Sydney route of its Aura Network, creating a new high-capacity fiber path across Australia between western and eastern digital hubs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://www.telstra.com.au/exchange/a-new-aura-network-route--a-bigger-opportunity-for-australia">October 7 announcement</a>, Telstra said the route passes through Kalgoorlie, Woomera, Port Augusta, Broken Hill, Dubbo and Orange before reaching Sydney. More than 9,000 kilometers of Aura fiber have now been built, with six routes ready for service.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The network is carrier and enterprise infrastructure rather than a residential broadband plan. It is designed to connect data centers, cloud regions and international cable gateways while providing more route diversity across the country. <a href="https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/telstra-expands-aura-network-with-launch-of-perth-sydney-route/">Data Center Dynamics</a> reports that Aura is planned to reach about 14,000 kilometers when completed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Route diversity matters because Australia also depends heavily on subsea links. The social post below documents a separate 2026 cable-fault investigation off Western Australia; it is included as regional infrastructure context, not as evidence about the Aura project.</p>
