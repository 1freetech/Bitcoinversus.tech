<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Paramount Pictures is developing a <strong>live-action film set in the Cyberpunk 2077 universe</strong>, with CD Projekt RED involved as a producer and veteran producer Lorenzo di Bonaventura attached to the project.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The news was first reported by Deadline and confirmed publicly by CD Projekt RED joint CEO Michał Nowakowski. Reuters reported that CD Projekt RED shares rose roughly <strong>3%</strong> in early Friday trading after the announcement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For now, the project is still early. No writer, director, cast, production start, or release date has been announced.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Has Actually Been Confirmed</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Paramount is developing the film with CD Projekt RED, while Lorenzo di Bonaventura is attached as producer. Di Bonaventura is best known for producing multiple Transformers films and other large-scale franchise projects.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nowakowski said Paramount shares CD Projekt RED’s “creative ambitions for the Cyberpunk franchise” and that more details will come later. CD Projekt RED told <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/paramount-develop-cyberpunk-2077-film-lifting-cd-projekt-reds-shares-2026-10-09/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Reuters</a> it had nothing to add beyond that statement.</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Night City Already Has Screen-Tested Material</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Cyberpunk 2077 is already unusually well positioned for a film adaptation because much of its identity is cinematic: dense urban production design, body modification, megacorporate politics, crime, surveillance, celebrity culture, and a cast built around morally complicated characters.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CD Projekt RED’s official launch trailer remains one of the clearest compact introductions to the tone Paramount is inheriting: Night City as a place where technology, ambition, violence, and personal identity constantly collide.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Franchise Is Much Bigger Than It Was In 2020</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Cyberpunk 2077 has now sold more than <strong>40 million copies</strong>, while the Phantom Liberty expansion has sold about <strong>15 million</strong>, according to figures cited by Reuters. The franchise has also expanded through Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, books, comics, merchandise, and a game sequel now in development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CD Projekt RED said in July that <strong>184 developers</strong> were working on the next Cyberpunk game. That gives Paramount a rare advantage for a game adaptation: this is not a dormant property being revived for a one-off movie. It is an active franchise with another major game already underway.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Biggest Unknown Is The Story</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The most important unanswered question is whether Paramount will adapt V and Johnny Silverhand directly or tell a new story elsewhere in Night City. CD Projekt RED has previously shown that the universe can support standalone characters through Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, so the film does not necessarily need to recreate the game’s main plot.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That may be the smarter route. Cyberpunk 2077 is built around player choice, branching relationships, and dozens of hours of exploration—things a two-hour film cannot reproduce directly. A new story could preserve the world without turning the movie into a compressed replay of the game.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For now, that remains speculation. What is confirmed is simpler: Paramount has entered Night City, CD Projekt RED is involved, and Hollywood now has one of gaming’s largest modern science-fiction worlds on its slate. <a href="https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/media/news/watch-the-cyberpunk-2077-launch-trailer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CD Projekt RED’s own archive</a> offers a useful reminder of how much visual and narrative material the studio has already built for the franchise.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
