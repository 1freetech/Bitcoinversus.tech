<p><a href="https://www.volvocars.com/us/media/press-releases/9FC694757A4F7641/">Volvo Cars announced</a> an expanded partnership with NVIDIA on October 15, 2026, aimed at bringing more capable automated driving into future Volvo vehicles. The companies say a next-generation Volvo will combine Volvo’s safety engineering with NVIDIA Hyperion sensor and compute architecture, Halos OS and the Alpamayo driving-software stack.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The planned system is intended to navigate from door to door across highways, busy urban streets and parking situations. Volvo says the rollout will gradually introduce “eyes-off” and “mind-off” driving. The announcement does not give a commercial launch date, regulatory classification or final vehicle model, so those capabilities should be treated as a development target rather than a feature available today.</p>

<p><a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/volvo-cars-and-nvidia-expand-partnership-to-advance-safe-automated-driving">NVIDIA’s release</a> says the expanded work builds on a collaboration that began in 2017. The core technical shift is toward a centralized software-defined vehicle stack where perception sensors, accelerated compute, operating software and driving models are designed to work together rather than as isolated driver-assistance features.</p>
