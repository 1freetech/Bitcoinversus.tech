<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Agricultural equipment manufacturer <strong>AGCO signed a commercial agreement with Agility Robotics on October 20, 2026</strong>, to pilot its bipedal Digit humanoid at the company’s <strong>Hesston, Kansas</strong>, factory. The <a href="https://investors.agcocorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/agco-and-agility-robotics-advance-smart-manufacturing-humanoid">company announcement</a> says the pilot is <strong>expected to begin in 2027</strong>; it does not report robots already performing work there.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Digit is expected to sort and stack components and position storage totes.</li><li>The agreement is signed, but its pilot start remains scheduled for 2027.</li><li>Neither company disclosed a robot count, contract price or measured results at Hesston.</li><li>The aim is to reduce physically demanding tasks while supporting factory employees.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>AGCO builds agricultural equipment under brands including Massey Ferguson and Fendt. Hesston’s assembly and materials-handling environment presents work where a robot could carry totes between human-designed stations. However, the announcement does not establish successful deployment or an independently measured productivity improvement.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Digit Would Do at Hesston</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Agility says Digit can combine autonomous mobility and manipulation with <a href="https://www.agilityrobotics.com/content/agco-and-agility-robotics-advance-smart-manufacturing-with-humanoid-robot-pilot-in-hesston-kansas-facility">Agility Arc fleet software</a>. Planned tasks focus on sorting parts, stacking materials and positioning totes. The announcement does not confirm which Digit generation AGCO will receive, so Digit 5 specifications should not be presented as already deployed in Kansas.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Until AGCO reports installed units, uptime or throughput, this is an <strong>agreement to test humanoid manufacturing automation</strong>, not proof of completed factory-wide automation.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
