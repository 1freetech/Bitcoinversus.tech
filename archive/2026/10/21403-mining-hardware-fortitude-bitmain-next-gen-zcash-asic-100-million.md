<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fortitude Mining has moved to the front of the line for Bitmain’s next generation of Zcash mining hardware. The company says it signed a letter of intent that gives it priority supply allocation for the unreleased machines and contemplates a purchase of up to <strong>₿1,170.16 ($100 million)</strong> at the BTC/USD rate used at publication.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important qualifier is that this is <strong>not yet a final equipment purchase</strong>. <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261005712271/en/Fortitude-Secures-Priority-Supply-Allocation-for-BITMAINs-Next-Generation-Zcash-Mining-Equipment-Announces-%24100-Million-Non-Binding-Purchase-Commitment-and-Additional-Upsizing-of-DCG-Credit-Facility-and-Available-ZEC-Funding-from-DCG"><strong>Fortitude’s announcement</strong></a> describes the commitment as indicative and non-binding. Final specifications and pricing for Bitmain’s next-generation Zcash ASICs have not been commercially released.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" style="font-family:ui-monospace,SFMono-Regular,Menlo,Monaco,Consolas,'Liberation Mono','Courier New',monospace">Fortitude Already Has a Large Bitmain Order in Motion</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is not Fortitude’s first large hardware commitment. BitcoinVersus previously covered the company’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/24/fortitude-9000-z15-pro-asic-expansion/"><strong>9,000-unit Bitmain Antminer Z15 Pro expansion</strong></a>, a definitive purchase aimed at materially increasing its Equihash capacity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That earlier fleet deal matters because the new agreement is about the generation after today’s Z15 Pro. <a href="https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-06-fortitude-expands-zcash-mining-push-with-up-to-100-million-equipment-commitment-417790"><strong>The Block reports</strong></a> that Fortitude secured priority access before the next-generation equipment has been commercially released, with shipments expected only after final specifications, pricing, and a binding purchase agreement are in place.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" style="font-family:ui-monospace,SFMono-Regular,Menlo,Monaco,Consolas,'Liberation Mono','Courier New',monospace">Why ASIC Access Matters in Proof-of-Work Mining</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/04/why-the-power-efficiency-index-only-applies-to-proof-of-work-coins/"><strong>Proof-of-Work mining</strong></a>, hardware access can change the economics before a machine ever reaches a rack. A newer ASIC can deliver more useful hash work per unit of electricity, while an operator that gets early allocation can deploy that efficiency before competitors refresh older fleets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Zcash uses Equihash rather than Bitcoin’s SHA-256 algorithm, so these machines are not interchangeable with Bitcoin miners. BitcoinVersus has previously compared the two networks in its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/08/30/bitcoin-versus-zcash-power-efficiency-theory-analysis/"><strong>Bitcoin Versus Zcash power-efficiency analysis</strong></a>. The engineering principle is similar even though the algorithms differ: chip efficiency, power cost, uptime, cooling, and network difficulty determine whether a fleet remains competitive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" style="font-family:ui-monospace,SFMono-Regular,Menlo,Monaco,Consolas,'Liberation Mono','Courier New',monospace">The Deal Still Has Real Execution Risk</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The headline number is large, but the current agreement leaves several variables unresolved. Bitmain has not publicly finalized the machines’ commercial specifications or pricing, and Fortitude can scale the contemplated purchase up or down subject to availability and Bitmain’s consent.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That makes this a hardware-supply story more than a completed-capex story. Fortitude has effectively reserved a place near the front of the queue. The value of that position will depend on whether the new ASICs deliver a meaningful efficiency gain once real specifications, shipment timing, pricing, and deployed performance become known.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" style="font-family:ui-monospace,SFMono-Regular,Menlo,Monaco,Consolas,'Liberation Mono','Courier New',monospace">What Comes Next</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next meaningful milestone is Bitmain’s formal product announcement. That should reveal the machine’s Equihash hashrate, wall power, efficiency, cooling requirements, and commercial pricing. Until then, the most important confirmed fact is that Fortitude has secured priority allocation and is prepared to commit significant capital if the final hardware meets its requirements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
