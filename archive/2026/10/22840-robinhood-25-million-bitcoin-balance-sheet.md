<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Robinhood has put <strong>$25 million of Bitcoin</strong> on its own balance sheet, moving the brokerage from simply helping customers trade BTC to holding the asset with corporate capital.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The disclosure came from Robinhood Senior Vice President and General Manager of Crypto and International Johann Kerbrat, who said the allocation reflects the company’s belief in crypto and is intended as a strategic asset-diversification move. Kerbrat also told <a href="https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-10-07-robinhood-adds-25-million-bitcoin-balance-sheet-417890">The Block</a> that the position is small relative to Robinhood’s overall size and is meant more as alignment with the Bitcoin ecosystem than as a wholesale treasury transformation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Position Is Less Than 0.5% of Robinhood’s Cash</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Robinhood reported <strong>$5.362 billion in cash and cash equivalents</strong> at June 30, 2026. Against that balance, a $25 million Bitcoin allocation equals only about <strong>0.47%</strong> of cash.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The company also reported <strong>$155 million in stablecoins</strong>. If cash and stablecoins are combined as a simple liquidity reference, the Bitcoin position equals roughly <strong>0.45%</strong> of that total.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is why this should not be confused with the aggressive treasury model used by companies built around repeated Bitcoin accumulation. BitcoinVersus just covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/metaplanet-sold-10000-bitcoin-bought-11000-back/">Metaplanet selling 10,000 BTC and buying 11,000 back</a> as part of a much more Bitcoin-centric balance-sheet strategy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">This Is Corporate Bitcoin, Not Customer Bitcoin</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The distinction matters. Robinhood already facilitates large amounts of customer cryptocurrency trading, but customer assets are not the same thing as Bitcoin owned by Robinhood itself. This new position exposes corporate capital directly to Bitcoin price movements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That makes the move strategically different from adding another trading pair or expanding wallet support. Shareholders now have a small amount of direct Bitcoin exposure embedded in Robinhood’s own balance sheet in addition to exposure through the company’s crypto business.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Robinhood’s Crypto Business Is Large but No Longer Dominant</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Robinhood’s second-quarter results show why the $25 million allocation is symbolically meaningful but financially modest. The company reported <strong>$100 million of cryptocurrency transaction revenue in Q2 2026</strong>, down <strong>38% year over year</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Crypto transaction revenue accounted for about <strong>8% of total net revenue</strong>, down from 16% a year earlier. At the same time, Robinhood’s event-contract, options, equity, credit-card and other businesses continued expanding, making the company materially more diversified than during earlier crypto cycles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That context makes the balance-sheet purchase more interesting. Robinhood is not buying Bitcoin because the entire company depends on BTC trading. It is adding Bitcoin even as crypto becomes a smaller percentage of total revenue.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Move Reverses an Earlier Capital-Allocation Debate</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Robinhood executives had previously questioned whether shareholders benefited from the company owning Bitcoin directly when investors could already buy BTC through Robinhood themselves. Management also had to weigh Bitcoin ownership against spending on engineering, acquisitions and new products.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The new allocation does not completely end that debate, but it answers it with a measured compromise: own some Bitcoin, keep the amount small, and preserve most capital for the operating business.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That approach also fits Robinhood’s longer Bitcoin history. BitcoinVersus covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/11/vlad-tenev-supports-u-s-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-at-white-house-summit/">CEO Vlad Tenev supporting a U.S. strategic Bitcoin reserve</a> in 2025. The company’s public stance toward Bitcoin has steadily moved from access, to infrastructure, to direct corporate ownership.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Robinhood Is Expanding Beyond Bitcoin Trading</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Bitcoin position sits inside a broader crypto expansion. Robinhood acquired Bitstamp, launched Robinhood Chain, expanded tokenized assets, and has continued building wallet and international crypto infrastructure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That makes the treasury move less like a standalone Bitcoin bet and more like another layer in a vertically expanding crypto business: brokerage access, exchange infrastructure, blockchain infrastructure, wallets, tokenization—and now a small corporate Bitcoin position.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the Numbers Show</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Robinhood crypto chief Johann Kerbrat says the company added $25 million of Bitcoin to its balance sheet.</li><li>The allocation equals about 0.47% of Robinhood’s June 30 cash and cash equivalents.</li><li>The company has not disclosed a recurring Bitcoin purchase schedule or long-term BTC allocation target.</li><li>Crypto transaction revenue was $100 million in Q2 2026, down 38% year over year.</li><li>Bitcoin is now both a customer-facing asset and a small corporate asset for Robinhood.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The strongest signal is not the size of the purchase. $25 million is tiny relative to Robinhood’s balance sheet. The important change is that one of the largest consumer financial platforms in the United States has crossed the line from selling access to Bitcoin to owning Bitcoin with its own money.</p>
