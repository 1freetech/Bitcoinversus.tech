<p><strong>Voyant Photonics is moving its silicon-photonics technology into AI data-center networking.</strong> The company said October 13 that it plans to develop a chip-scale optical circuit switching engine that networking vendors can integrate into larger switch platforms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/13/3379646/0/en/voyant-photonics-advances-into-ai-data-center-optical-circuit-switching-ocs-with-production-proven-silicon-photonics-technology.html"><strong>Voyant’s announcement</strong></a>, the design adapts a switching architecture already used in its FMCW LiDAR platform. Voyant says the solid-state fabric has no moving parts, can reconfigure in microseconds and has been engineered for trillions of switching cycles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><em>NextGenInfra explains how silicon-photonic optical circuit switching can reconfigure data-center paths much faster than traditional approaches.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.opencompute.org/projects/networking/optical-circuit-switching/"><strong>Open Compute Project</strong></a> now treats OCS as a dedicated networking subproject for AI, machine learning and high-performance computing. Unlike ordinary packet switching, OCS can keep traffic in the optical domain instead of repeatedly converting signals between light and electricity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The move arrives as optical networking becomes more central to AI infrastructure. BitcoinVersus.Tech has recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/tower-semtech-expand-sige-capacity/"><strong>expanded SiGe capacity for high-speed interconnects</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/nvidia-and-cisco-plan-12-8-tb-s-optics-for-ai-networks/"><strong>12.8 Tb/s pluggable optics</strong></a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/celestica-ds6005-102-4tbps-co-packaged-optics-ai-switch/"><strong>102.4 Tb/s co-packaged-optics switching</strong></a>. Voyant has not disclosed a production date, pricing or customer names for the OCS engine.</p>
