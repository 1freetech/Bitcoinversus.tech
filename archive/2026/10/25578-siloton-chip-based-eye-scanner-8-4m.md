## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>British photonics startup Siloton has secured a <strong>£8.448 million Innovate UK contract</strong> to accelerate GiraffeOCT, its handheld optical-coherence-tomography system built around a photonic chip smaller than a £1 coin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The two-year program will fund both in-clinic and at-home versions of the scanner plus clinical trials. <a href="https://www.ukri.org/news/winners-of-contracts-for-innovation-quantum-funding-announced/">UK Research and Innovation</a> describes Siloton’s approach as moving conventional OCT hardware from large, fragile optical assemblies onto a quantum-photonic chip. <a href="https://www.optics.org/news/siloton-secures-%C2%A38.4-million-to-translate-giraffeoct-into-clinics">Optics.org reports</a> that Siloton believes the contract could shorten development by as much as two years.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The engineering goal is straightforward: shrink retinal imaging enough that it can move beyond specialist hospital equipment and into clinics, community settings and eventually homes. The current award does not mean GiraffeOCT is already a fully deployed consumer medical product; it funds the development and trial work needed to move closer to that stage.</p>
