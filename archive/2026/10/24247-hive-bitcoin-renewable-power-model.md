<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>HIVE Digital Technologies says Bitcoin mining remains the cash-generating base of a broader renewable-power strategy even as the company shifts some of its electrical infrastructure toward artificial intelligence and high-performance computing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In a <a href="https://www.hivedigitaltechnologies.com/news/hives-buzz-hpc-appoints-ai-and-hpc-veteran-mark-volk-as-senior-vice-president-revenue-hive-surpasses-1-million-in-average-daily-revenue/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">September 10 operating update</a>, HIVE said its combined Bitcoin-hashrate and GPU-cloud businesses had exceeded $1 million in average daily revenue. Since August 21, the company said it had produced roughly 12 BTC per day while GPU-cloud operations generated about $100,000 per day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The numbers show why Bitcoin miners increasingly describe electricity infrastructure as the core asset. BitcoinVersus.Tech previously covered how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/bitcoin-miners-blueprint-ai-power-digital-power-network-expands/">Bitcoin miners are becoming a blueprint for flexible AI power infrastructure</a> as data-center developers compete for substations, interconnections and generation capacity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>HIVE describes that model as renewable energy flowing into digital infrastructure and then into sellable compute. In a separate <a href="https://www.hivedigitaltechnologies.com/news/hives-paraguay-ai-infrastructure-performance-validated-in-columbia-university-study-research-heads-to-neurips/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">company update on its Paraguay infrastructure</a>, HIVE said it has built around green-energy-powered data centers and highlighted its experience using demand response to help balance Sweden’s electrical grid.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The model is not limited to hydro. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently reported that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/microbt-20mw-natural-gas-bitcoin-mining-canada/">MicroBT says a 20 MW natural-gas Bitcoin mining system is operating in Canada</a>, showing how miners are experimenting with different generation and behind-the-meter designs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Cheap power alone is not enough; efficiency still determines how much Bitcoin can be produced from each megawatt. BitcoinVersus.Tech has also tracked <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitdeer-bhutan-ai-data-center-30-mw-500-mw/">Bitdeer’s hydro-backed infrastructure expansion in Bhutan</a>, another example of renewable power being treated as a reusable compute asset.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>HIVE’s figures are company-reported and preliminary, and the mix can change with Bitcoin price, network difficulty and GPU deployment. The broader point is narrower: for HIVE, Bitcoin mining still monetizes renewable electricity immediately, while the same land, substations and data-center expertise can increasingly be redirected toward longer-duration AI contracts.</p>
