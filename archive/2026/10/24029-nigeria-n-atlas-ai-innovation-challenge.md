## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nigeria is pushing its government-backed N-ATLAS language model from release into practical deployment through a national build challenge that closes October 12.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://ncair.nitda.gov.ng/naic/">National AI Innovation Challenge</a>, run through the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, NCAIR and ONDI, requires participants to submit a working technical product integrated with N-ATLAS rather than a proposal or research paper. The deadline is <strong>11:59 p.m. WAT on October 12, 2026</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NCAIR describes N-ATLAS as Nigeria’s first open-source multilingual large language model. It is fine-tuned from Llama-3 8B and supports Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and Nigerian-accented English, with automatic speech recognition for voice applications.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The challenge targets developer infrastructure, voice-first applications and sector-specific model tuning. Submissions also need real-world validation, technical documentation and a 3–5 minute demonstration video. N-ATLAS integration checks are scheduled for October 15–17, followed by a final showcase November 8–10.</p>
