<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Anousheh Ansari built one of the most extraordinary global careers in technology and exploration: electrical engineer, serial entrepreneur, commercial-space pioneer, International Space Station visitor and CEO of XPRIZE.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Her work is still active in 2026. On August 19, XPRIZE named Ansari as one of the judges for its $2 million Build with Gemini global AI competition. <a href="https://go.xprize.org/news/build-with-gemini-judges" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">XPRIZE described her</a> as a serial entrepreneur who co-founded and sold two technology companies before becoming the first female private space explorer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ansari’s technical career began with electronics and electrical engineering. She earned a bachelor’s degree in electronics and computer engineering from George Mason University and a master’s degree in electrical engineering from George Washington University. She then helped build technology companies in telecommunications and connected systems, eventually co-founding Prodea Systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Her family also helped change commercial spaceflight. They became title sponsors of the $10 million Ansari XPRIZE, the competition won by SpaceShipOne in 2004 after completing the required privately funded reusable spaceflights.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then Ansari made the transition from supporting spaceflight to experiencing it herself. On September 18, 2006, she launched aboard Soyuz TMA-9 toward the International Space Station. <a href="https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Human_and_Robotic_Exploration/International_Space_Station/ESA_experiments_with_spaceflight_participant_Ansari_to_ISS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ESA documented</a> her participation in four human-physiology experiments during the mission.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The flight made her the first female private space explorer, the first astronaut of Iranian descent and the first Muslim woman in space. She spent roughly 11 days on the expedition and became the fourth private explorer to visit space.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In 2018, Ansari became CEO of XPRIZE. The organization says that under her leadership it has launched hundreds of millions of dollars in incentive competitions across climate, health, AI and other major technical challenges.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
