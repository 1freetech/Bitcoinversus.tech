# Faraday Future Signs $200M Robotics Spinout Deal

## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Faraday Future has signed definitive agreements to transfer its robotics business to FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem Inc. in an all-stock deal that values the robotics operation at about $200 million. The <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1805521/000121390026109222/ea0308387-8k_faraday.htm">SEC filing</a> says the transaction agreement was signed October 12 and is expected to close by the end of October, subject to its remaining conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>FFR is valued at about $60 million on a fully diluted basis before the transaction. It plans to issue shares to Faraday Future at $2.246 each, roughly 50% above FFR's $1.50 closing price on October 12. The companies say the structure is intended to reduce dilution and separate the robotics operation into a listed vehicle.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://investors.ff.com/news-events/news-releases/">company announcement</a> also says FFR has secured up to $10 million in PIPE financing. Calling the resulting company the first U.S.-listed pure-play humanoid and bionic EAI robotics company is a company claim, not an independent market designation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The transaction arrives as robotics companies race to convert demos into repeatable businesses. BitcoinVersus.Tech has tracked <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/robotaxis-easiest-path-independent-robotics-business-fleet-operator/">robotaxis as a possible standalone robotics business</a>, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/robotics-humanoid-actuators-40-60-percent-hardware-cost-supply-chain/">cost pressure from humanoid actuators</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/robot-gyms-physical-ai-training-data-humanoid-robots/">robot-training data factories</a>. For FFR, the next test is whether the spinout can turn those robotics assets into sustained revenue rather than simply a new corporate structure.</p>
