<p><strong>Cisco introduced a new 51.2 Tb/s Silicon One G302 switching chip on October 12 for top-of-rack AI networking.</strong> The chip uses 200G SerDes, supports Cisco 1.6T optics, and is designed to integrate with direct-to-chip liquid cooling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m10/cisco-advances-scalability-and-openness-at-ocp-2026.html">OCP 2026 announcement</a>, Cisco said the G302 is built for top-of-rack switches that aggregate server traffic before sending it into the wider data-center fabric. Cisco plans to begin shipping the chip before the end of 2026. The company also said its 102.4T G300 is now sampling and the first G300-powered systems are shipping.</p>

<p>The announcement fits Cisco’s larger push toward faster Ethernet fabrics for AI. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/nvidia-cisco-usfp-12-8tbps-ai-network-optics/">Cisco and NVIDIA’s planned 12.8 Tb/s USFP optics</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/networking-cisco-adds-supermicro-rack-scale-systems-to-nvidia-ai-factory/">Cisco’s rack-scale work with Supermicro and NVIDIA</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/broadcom-ocp-2026-tomahawk6-102-4t-800g-nic-cpo-ai-networking/">Broadcom’s 102.4 Tb/s OCP networking push</a>.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.unite.ai/cisco-announces-g302-silicon-and-usfp-optics-with-nvidia-at-ocp-2026/">Independent coverage from Unite.AI</a> notes that Cisco is positioning the G302 as part of a broader open AI infrastructure stack spanning switching silicon, optics, SONiC and multi-site fabrics. The key point is simple: as GPU racks get denser, the switch at the top of each rack has to move more data while fitting inside tighter power and cooling limits.</p>
