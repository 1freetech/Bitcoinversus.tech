<p><strong>IREN’s unnamed “leading frontier AI lab” customer may be Anthropic.</strong> <a href="https://www.barrons.com/articles/iren-stock-anthropic-ai-c48b758a">Barron’s reported October 12</a>, citing The Information, that Anthropic has entered a multi-year cloud-services agreement with IREN as it accelerates data-center capacity for Claude.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Confirmed:</strong> IREN disclosed in its <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000051/irenreportsfy26results.htm">August 27 SEC-filed FY26 results</a> that it had signed a new multi-year AI Cloud contract with a “leading frontier AI lab.” The filing did <strong>not</strong> name the customer. It also said 2026 capacity was largely sold out, with about <strong>$4 billion of contracted annualized run-rate revenue</strong> tied to 2026 capacity and roughly $1 billion operating at the time.</p>

<p><strong>Reported, not confirmed:</strong> neither IREN nor Anthropic has publicly identified Anthropic as that customer. Today’s reporting therefore narrows the mystery but does not make the attribution official.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The mining connection is significant. IREN’s FY26 filing showed <strong>$578.2 million</strong> of Bitcoin-mining revenue for the year, but Q4 AI Cloud revenue reached <strong>$70.5 million</strong>, slightly above mining’s $66.7 million. The company also recorded large non-cash impairments as it decommissioned mining hardware and redirected powered sites toward GPU deployments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If Anthropic is confirmed, it would add another major AI customer to an infrastructure platform already serving Microsoft and other AI developers. It would also reinforce a larger mining-industry trend: the most valuable asset at some Bitcoin mines may now be the <strong>power connection and data-center footprint</strong>, not the ASIC fleet.</p>
