<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bain &amp; Company says the AI industry may need to generate roughly <strong>$6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031</strong> to support the infrastructure now being built for AI.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That does not mean Bain is saying today’s spending is automatically wasteful. Its <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/global-ai-market-could-hit-6-trillion-annually-by-2031-through-unlocking-value-and-innovation--bain--cos-7th-global-technology-report-302891178.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">2026 Global Technology Report</a> argues that the buildout can work economically—but only if AI creates much larger new revenue streams.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The revenue gap is the key question</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bain estimates existing consumer and enterprise AI products could generate about <strong>$1.2 trillion to $1.8 trillion</strong> annually by 2031. That leaves roughly <strong>$4.2 trillion</strong> that would need to come from newer markets such as AI-powered search and advertising, autonomous vehicles and industrial systems, robotics and digital twins, drug discovery, energy and products that do not yet exist at scale.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The report assumes annual AI infrastructure investment could reach about <strong>$1.5 trillion</strong> by 2031, including data centers, compute capacity, GPUs, memory and networking. Bain’s calculation assumes capital spending remains around one-quarter of industry revenue.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">So is AI overspending?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The answer is still unresolved. Bain’s number is a forward-looking economic requirement, not proof of a bubble. The risk is that infrastructure is being built faster than revenue-producing AI markets can mature. The upside case is that entirely new products close the gap.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
