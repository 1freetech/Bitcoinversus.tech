# Bitdeer SEAL04's 6–7 J/TH Claim Is Real, But the A4 Miner Runs at 9.45 J/TH

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Bitdeer's SEAL04 chip really did demonstrate approximately 6–7 joules per terahash (J/TH)</strong>, according to the company's formal investor disclosures. But the much-discussed result belongs to a <strong>chip-only laboratory test</strong>, not a complete Bitcoin mining machine operating at the wall.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On <strong>November 10, 2025</strong>, Bitdeer said early SEAL04 samples reached 6–7 J/TH in <em>low-voltage, ultra-power-saving mode</em>. It later identified two development paths, SEAL04-1 and SEAL04-2. The first improved on its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/06/24/bitdeer-achieves-9-7-j-th-efficiency-with-seal03-bitcoin-mining-chip/">SEAL03 chip's approximately 9.7 J/TH laboratory result</a>; development of the second design continued. The initial 2025 ambition of roughly 5 J/TH was not the reported SEAL04-1 test result.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">From SEAL04 Silicon To A4 Machines</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What happened after testing? On <strong>April 7, 2026</strong>, Bitdeer <a href="https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-launches-sealminer-a4-series-bitcoin-mining-rigs">launched the SEALMINER A4 family using SEAL04 chips</a>. Its flagship <strong>A4 Ultra Hydro</strong> is manufacturer-rated at <strong>886 TH/s, 8,372.7 W and 9.45 J/TH</strong>. The <strong>A4 Pro Hydro</strong> and <strong>A4 Pro Air</strong> are both rated at 10.9 J/TH. Bitdeer's published launch tolerances allow efficiency to vary by ±5%.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>6–7 J/TH:</strong> SEAL04 chip samples, low-voltage laboratory mode.</li><li><strong>9.45 J/TH:</strong> A4 Ultra Hydro, complete machine nameplate rating.</li><li><strong>10.9 J/TH:</strong> A4 Pro Hydro and A4 Pro Air machine ratings.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The approximately <strong>6.5 J/TH midpoint</strong> is useful when plotting the chip result, but it is not a separately certified product specification. Differences between chip and miner numbers reflect <em>both</em> operating conditions and system-level power demands, including power conversion, controls and board losses. The 2.95 J/TH gap should not be labeled entirely as overhead without matched chip operating data.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What This Means For 1 J/TH</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The result strengthens evidence of ongoing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/21/bitcoin-asic-architecture-1-j-th-2033-2046-efficiency-slowdown/">ASIC power-efficiency progress</a>, but it does not establish that 1 J/TH is imminent. Comparing Bitdeer's lab chips directly with a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/top-10-most-efficient-bitcoin-mining-machines-2026-j-th/">ranking of complete miners</a> would overstate their advantage. The next decisive evidence would be reproducible chip measurements at disclosed voltage, frequency and hashrate, followed by independently observed whole-machine power consumption.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Verdict:</strong> The 6–7 J/TH <a href="https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-reports-unaudited-financial-results-third-quarter-2025">claim is supported by Bitdeer's filings</a>. The market-ready reference point is its 9.45 J/TH A4 Ultra Hydro. Both values matter, provided they remain correctly labeled.</p>
