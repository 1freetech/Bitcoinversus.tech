<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Changpeng Zhao and Michael Saylor have independently landed on the same idea again: autonomous AI will need autonomous money. In CZ’s <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FImugb78BP4">April 9 TBPN interview</a>, the Binance founder said AI will use crypto for payments because software agents cannot complete human bank KYC and blockchain works across borders.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Saylor reached a similar conclusion from a Bitcoin-first direction. In his <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sSKZelY0Yc">September Freedom Tech DC discussion</a>, he argued that AI agents will need digital money, digital assets and financial rails that operate continuously. BitcoinVersus.Tech <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/michael-saylor-ai-agents-digital-money-bitcoin/">documented that argument separately</a> one day ago.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the second such convergence in our archive. In April 2025, BitcoinVersus.Tech noted that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/12/great-minds-think-alike-bitcoin-leaders-michael-saylor-and-changpeng-zao-share-similar-philosophy-despite-ongoing-global-trade-war/">CZ and Saylor independently framed chaos as opportunity</a>: CZ wrote that chaos and opportunity often arrive together, while Saylor said Bitcoin is powered by chaos.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That repetition is what we mean by <strong>global collective intelligence</strong>. CZ built a broad crypto ecosystem; Saylor has centered his strategy on Bitcoin. Their backgrounds, businesses and methods differ, yet both keep identifying similar pressure points when technology changes the economic system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The claim is analytical, not ideological. Two people agreeing twice does not prove universal Bitcoin consensus. But independent convergence matters—especially as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/shopify-opens-checkout-to-browser-based-ai-agents/">AI agents are already entering commercial checkout</a>. Collective intelligence does not require coordination. Sometimes people studying the same system long enough simply begin seeing the same constraints.</p>
