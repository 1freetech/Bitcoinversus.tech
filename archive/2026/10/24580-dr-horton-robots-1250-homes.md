<p>D.R. Horton is moving physical AI into mainstream U.S. homebuilding. Promise Robotics said on <strong>October 13, 2026</strong> that the companies signed a multi-year agreement giving the homebuilder access to its Factory-as-a-Service platform for the production of <strong>more than 1,250 homes</strong>. The <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/promise-robotics-expands-physical-ai-production-infrastructure-for-us-homebuilding-through-multi-year-commercial-agreement-with-dr-horton-302906542.html">company announcement</a> says Promise is also expanding its U.S. network into Phoenix.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The system takes existing house designs, converts them into production plans and robot instructions, coordinates factory work, and links that process to onsite installation. Promise says builders can add automated production without owning the factory infrastructure themselves.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An <a href="https://www.housingwire.com/articles/promise-robotics-dr-horton-factory/">independent HousingWire report</a> says the agreement covers factory-built framing for 1,250-plus homes. The move pushes the same physical-AI trend BitcoinVersus.Tech has tracked in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/ubitium-14-million-physical-ai-processor/">robotics-focused processors</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/lantronix-spotter-autonomous-drones-robots/">autonomous robot computers</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/nvidia-robots-assemble-gb300-ai-hardware/">robots assembling AI hardware</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important distinction is that this is not a promise to finish 1,250 complete houses autonomously. The disclosed agreement covers production support through robotic manufacturing and related factory workflows. D.R. Horton still relies on conventional onsite construction for the rest of the build.</p>
