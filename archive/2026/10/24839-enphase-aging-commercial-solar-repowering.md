<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Enphase Energy is pushing a new use for commercial microinverters: replacing failed or aging string and central inverters while keeping the existing solar panels and racking in place.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an <a href="https://enphaseenergy.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enphase-energy-highlights-projects-bringing-new-life-aging" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>October 14 announcement</strong></a>, Enphase highlighted U.S. repowering projects using its IQ9 Commercial Microinverters. At Furman University in South Carolina, Detail Solar upgraded the first 30 kW of a planned repowering for a 988 kW array whose legacy string-inverter architecture was underperforming. In Pennsylvania, Endless Energy is repowering a 240 kW AC system at Peirce Welding and Assembly with 561 IQ9N-3P units.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The IQ9N-3P and IQ9S-3P support 480 V three-phase systems, while the IQ8P-3P serves 208 V sites. Enphase says its 2-to-1 configuration can pair two older modules with one microinverter, helping match slightly degraded panels to newer electronics without replacing the entire array.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hardware itself is also notable. <a href="https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2026/01/enphase-begins-distributing-commercial-microinverter-nationwide/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Solar Power World</strong></a> reported earlier this year that the IQ9N-3P uses gallium nitride power electronics, supports 480Y/277 V commercial grids, reaches up to 97.5% efficiency, and can support modules up to 600 W.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The shift is essentially a maintenance strategy for aging solar fleets. BitcoinVersus.Tech has previously explained how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/03/21/solar-engineer-training-solar-inverters/"><strong>solar inverters</strong></a> convert panel output into usable AC power, how a centralized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/03/24/power-electronics-string-inverter/"><strong>string inverter</strong></a> architecture works, and how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/02/28/energy-operations-solar-cells/"><strong>solar cells</strong></a> generate the DC electricity those inverters process. The new repowering projects show that the panels can outlive the electronics around them.</p>
