<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitFuFu and Hong Kong-based Finloop have launched <strong>Hashrate Token (HASH)</strong>, an on-chain note designed to give eligible investors BTC-denominated exposure to the economic output of Bitcoin mining.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/15/3381431/0/en/bitfufu-launches-btc-denominated-hashrate-token-hash-with-finloop.html">BitFuFu’s October 15 announcement</a>, the underlying hashrate is provided as a service and contributes to professionally managed mining activity. The resulting economic output forms the basis for the note’s BTC-denominated returns, while profit distributions are recorded on-chain.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/BitFuFuOfficial/status/2014322027264626826]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>BitFuFu had already been publicly discussing asset tokenization as part of its broader financial-infrastructure strategy.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The distinction matters: HASH does <strong>not</strong> represent direct ownership of ASICs, a mining site or physical hashrate infrastructure. It is a tokenized note linked to mining economics. BitFuFu’s <a href="https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/FUFU/6-k-bitfufu-inc-current-report-foreign-issuer-3a94c5927db3.html">October 15 Form 6-K</a> also states that the product is limited by investor-qualification, jurisdictional and compliance requirements and is not generally offered to U.S. persons.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PJS4tzQuZQU]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>BitFuFu’s VP of investor relations discusses where hosted and cloud hashrate fit into the changing Bitcoin-mining market at Bitcoin 2026.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The launch follows BitFuFu’s broader effort to turn mining infrastructure into more programmable financial products. BitcoinVersus.Tech has previously tracked the company’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/23/bitfufu-mining-hashrate-tops-20-ehs/">20 EH/s-scale mining platform</a>, its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/bitfufuos-asic-firmware-power-market-control/">BitFuFuOS power-market controls</a>, and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/11/economic-challenges-for-bitfufu-cloud-mining-clients/">economics of its cloud-mining model</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>HASH pushes that model one step further: instead of selling only hosted compute or cloud-mining access, BitFuFu is packaging the economic output of managed mining into an on-chain financial instrument.</p>
