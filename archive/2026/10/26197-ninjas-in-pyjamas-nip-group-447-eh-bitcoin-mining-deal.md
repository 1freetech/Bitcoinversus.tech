<p><strong>October 23, 2026 | Bitcoin Mining · Gaming · Digital Infrastructure</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>NIP Group</strong>, the Nasdaq-listed parent of esports organization <strong>Ninjas in Pyjamas</strong>, announced on October 20 that it signed a deal to acquire Bitcoin mining machines rated at approximately <strong>4.47 exahashes per second (EH/s)</strong>. The machines already operate at third-party hosting facilities, but the transaction has <strong>not yet closed</strong>. This is the company's third mining acquisition tranche, not its first entry into mining.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>The proposed purchase covers <strong>4.47 EH/s</strong> of operating mining hardware.</li><li>After closing, NIP expects a combined mining fleet of approximately <strong>13.85 EH/s</strong>.</li><li>Management forecasts <strong>195 BTC monthly at full deployment</strong>; this is not reported production.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How the Purchase Works</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to the company's <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1966233/000149315226047700/ex99-1.htm">SEC-filed announcement</a>, NIP would issue sellers up to <strong>247,091,740 Class A ordinary shares</strong> for the equipment. The agreement is dated September 24, 2026; closing remains subject to customary conditions. Shares, rather than cash, are the stated consideration. That makes potential dilution an important question for shareholders.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The projected <strong>13.85 EH/s</strong> represents 13.85 million terahashes per second of SHA-256 mining capacity. It is <em>not</em> an electrical-power measurement. The announcement does not specify the ASIC models, fleet efficiency in J/TH, or site power demand, so an accurate megawatt or electricity-cost estimate cannot be derived from the announced hashrate alone. Our earlier <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">Power Is Money</a> analysis examines the energy-economics relationship, while a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/proof-of-work-bitcoin-podcaster-builds-custom-dashboard-for-bitaxe-community/">2024 Bitaxe monitoring story</a> illustrates why operators track power and performance together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What 195 BTC a Month Really Means</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NIP <a href="https://ir.nipg.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nip-group-bitcoin-mining-operations-produce-1514-btc-initial/">previously reported</a> approximately <strong>151.4 BTC over September–November 2025</strong>. Its new estimate of 195 BTC <em>per month</em> assumes full deployment and is sensitive to network difficulty, hashrate, uptime, and operating conditions. It should not be represented as Bitcoin already mined or held in treasury. Our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/22/bitdeer-leads-september-2026-bitcoin-mining-production-ranking/">September production comparison</a> distinguishes actual mined BTC from fleet projections.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">From Esports to Computing Infrastructure</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The company's <a href="https://ninjasinmining.com/">Ninjas in Mining division</a> presents Bitcoin mining as an infrastructure business alongside its entertainment operations. Executives say some capacity may eventually support AI and high-performance computing. However, ASIC ownership does not automatically provide GPU-ready AI servers or contracts: the equipment and operating requirements differ. For context, BitcoinVersus.Tech recently compared <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/21/nvidia-dsx-pod-14-day-ai-factory-bitcoin-mining-container-comparison/">modular AI factories with mining infrastructure</a>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Comes Next</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The key tests are confirmation that the transaction closed, realized hashrate, Bitcoin mined, energy and hosting costs, and whether the share-funded expansion improves operating performance. Until those figures are disclosed, <strong>13.85 EH/s and 195 BTC per month remain company expectations</strong>, not audited results. For more company context, see <a href="https://ir.nipgroup.gg/news-releases/news-release-details/nip-group-expands-bitcoin-mining-capacity-third-tranche-asset">NIP Group's October 20 release</a>.</p>
