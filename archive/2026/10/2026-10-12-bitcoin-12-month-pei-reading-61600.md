# Bitcoin’s 12-Month PEI Reading Lands Near $61.6K

**Published:** 2026-10-12
**Live article:** https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/bitcoin-12-month-pei-reading-61600/

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/05/power-efficiency-index-explained/">Power Efficiency Index</a> (PEI) now gives a different signal from the longer 18-month reading. Using same-date network hashrate readings of <a href="https://maketo.com/bitcoin/hashrate">1,024 EH/s on October 11, 2025 and 1,024 EH/s on October 11, 2026</a>, plus leading announced ASIC efficiency improving from 9.5 to 8.9 J/TH, the 12-month PEI is <strong>1.067×</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>PEI = (1,024 ÷ 1,024) × (9.5 ÷ 8.9) = 1.067×.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The model’s Physical Price is the lowest observed BTC price inside the window: <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BTC-USD/history/">$57,747.77 on July 1, 2026</a>. Applying PEI produces a Relative Price of <strong>$61,640.88</strong>. Bitcoin was trading near <strong>$83.9K</strong> on October 12, putting market price roughly <strong>$22.2K, or 36.1%, above</strong> the PEI reading.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That reverses the direction of our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/23/18-month-power-efficiency-analysis-suggests-bitcoin-still-undervalued/">18-month analysis</a>, where market price sat below PEI. The reason is straightforward: over this 12-month slice, network hashrate finished almost exactly where it began while mining efficiency improved only modestly. The selected measurement window matters.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The 8.9 J/TH input is still manufacturer-rated performance for the announced <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/21/bitmain-antminer-s23-xp-hyd-8-9-j-th-bitcoin-mining/">S23 XP Hyd.</a>, not broad field validation. A short launch video from WDMS shows the machine: <a href="https://twitter.com/Yesmining_Ethan/status/2093298490306961437">original X post</a>.</p>
