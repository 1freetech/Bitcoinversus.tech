<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Character.AI has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy as it tries to sell the business while keeping its chatbot platform online.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/">October 12 update</a>, Character.AI said the court-supervised sale should finish by late November or early December. The company also warned that if no workable bid emerges, the platform could be wound down.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/wongmjane/status/2109881444793548972]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Jane Manchun Wong highlighted the Chapter 11 filing shortly after the company’s announcement.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/characterai-files-bankruptcy-agrees-sell-some-assets-disney-20-million-2026-10-13/">Reuters reports</a> that Disney has agreed to pay $20 million for certain assets and a non-exclusive perpetual license to Character.AI intellectual property. Character.AI cited legal, regulatory and operational pressures; the service remains available and subscriptions continue for now.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>WFAA previously covered Character.AI’s legal pressure and settlements involving teen-safety allegations.</em></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The collapse lands amid broader scrutiny of AI products, from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/americans-wary-ai-rapid-growth/">public concern about rapid AI growth</a> to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/bank-regulators-agentic-ai-scrutiny/">regulatory attention on agentic AI</a> and new work on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/goodfires-inside-out-ai-monitors-watch-for-rogue-agents-before-they-act/">monitoring risky AI behavior</a>.</p>
