<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Yes—but barely.</strong> Small modular reactors are no longer purely future technology, but they are not yet something most utilities or data centers can simply order and connect to the grid.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://aris.iaea.org/Publications/">International Atomic Energy Agency</a> lists Russia’s floating Akademik Lomonosov plant and China’s HTR-PM as operating SMR projects. Akademik Lomonosov uses two 35 MW electric reactor modules and has been in commercial operation since 2020. China’s HTR-PM entered commercial operation in 2023.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/NTI_WMD/status/2035002600353841339]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Nuclear Threat Initiative highlighted a major remaining problem in 2026: turning reactor designs and policy support into repeatable commercial deployment.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Western market is further behind. Canada’s BWRX-300 at Darlington is now under nuclear construction, but it still needs an operating licence before fuel loading and commercial operation. In the United States, the <a href="https://www.nrc.gov/about-nrc/news-releases/2025/nrc-approves-standard-design-nuscale-us460-small-modular-reactor">NRC approved NuScale’s US460 design</a> in May 2025, but that approval is not a licence to build a specific plant.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nDF7Nln5e-0]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>The World Nuclear Association explains which SMRs are operating, under construction, and moving through deployment.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So the answer has changed since BitcoinVersus.Tech first covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">SMRs in 2024</a>. They exist. They generate electricity. But for a U.S. data center considering <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/can-small-modular-reactors-power-data-centers-or-nah/">SMR power</a>, commercial availability is still measured in licensing and construction timelines—not delivery dates. The recent <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/historic-reactor-salt-texas-molten-salt-reactor/">Texas molten-salt reactor work</a> shows how quickly the pipeline is expanding, but most Western projects remain pre-operation.</p>
