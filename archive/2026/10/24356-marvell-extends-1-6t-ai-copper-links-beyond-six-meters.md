<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Marvell has launched a new generation of AI-rack connectivity chips that pushes <strong>1.6 Tbps active electrical cable links beyond six meters</strong> while adding a separate <strong>3.2 Tbps retimer</strong> for on-board and backplane connections.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1039/marvell-bolsters-connectivity-portfolio-with-new-3-2t-protocol-aware-retimer-and-second-generation-1-6t-aec-dsp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">October 13 announcement</a> introduces the second-generation Alaska A 1.6T AEC DSP and Alaska E 3.2T protocol-aware retimer. Both target 224G-per-lane AI scale-up networks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The copper reach doubles</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Marvell says the 3nm Alaska A chip supports eight 224G PAM4 lanes, consumes 12.8 W and can carry 1.6T links for more than six meters—roughly double the first-generation reach. The company also claims an 8 W power saving versus comparable 5nm designs using its stated test assumptions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Alaska E retimer delivers 3.2 Tbps through 16 lanes of 224G PAM4 and supports Ethernet, UALink and ESUN. Marvell positions it for short, extremely high-bandwidth links between XPUs, switches and backplanes inside dense AI systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
