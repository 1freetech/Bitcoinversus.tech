<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>NVIDIA and Cisco announced plans on October 12 for a new pluggable optical format aimed at next-generation AI networks.</strong> The Ultra-Dense Small Form Factor Pluggable, or USFP, is designed for 400Gb/s-per-lane electrical signaling and a path to as much as 12.8Tb/s of aggregate capacity. This is a specification effort, not a shipping product announcement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-cisco-introduce-new-pluggable-optics-form-factor-to-scale-ai-factories">NVIDIA’s announcement</a>, the first planned transceivers would use 16 full-duplex lanes in an OSFP-sized footprint, with future expansion to 32 lanes. NVIDIA and Cisco say the design could deliver 4× the bandwidth density and about 1.3× the power efficiency of 200Gb/s-per-lane pluggables.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The goal is to push more bandwidth through each switch face without forcing operators to add another network tier. BitcoinVersus.Tech has been tracking the same pressure through <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/astera-labs-pcie-7-50-meter-optical-links-ai-racks/">Astera Labs’ 50-meter PCIe 7 optical links</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/networking-cisco-adds-supermicro-rack-scale-systems-to-nvidia-ai-factory/">Cisco’s rack-scale NVIDIA AI infrastructure work</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/networking-ciena-ai-network-services-revenue-optical-upgrades-urgent/">Ciena’s AI-network upgrade findings</a>.</p>

NVIDIA and Cisco plan to pursue an open industry specification with partners including TE Connectivity and Amphenol.</p>
