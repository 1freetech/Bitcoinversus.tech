<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>ASML says demand tied to the artificial-intelligence buildout is now running ahead of the chip-tool capacity it currently plans to have available in 2027 and 2028.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On October 14, 2026, ASML <a href="https://www.investor.asml.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asml-reports-eu11.7-billion-total-net-sales-and-eu5.0-billion-net-income-q3-2026"><strong>reported</strong></a> record third-quarter net sales of €11.7 billion, a 57.5% gross margin and 113 new lithography systems sold. The company also raised the low end of its full-year sales outlook to €44 billion–€45 billion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CEO Christophe Fouquet said customers are increasing short- and medium-term equipment forecasts primarily because of their expectations for AI-related investment. Some customers are already expressing interest in orders for 2029 and 2030. ASML now says its currently planned supply for both 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet customer demand, and it is studying ways to expand 2028 capacity beyond the previously announced 30% increase.</p>
