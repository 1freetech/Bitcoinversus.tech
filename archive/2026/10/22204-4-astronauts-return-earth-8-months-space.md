<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Four astronauts are safely back on Earth after spending nearly eight months in space aboard the International Space Station.</strong> NASA’s SpaceX Crew-12 splashed down Thursday morning in the Pacific Ocean off the coast of Los Angeles aboard the SpaceX Crew Dragon <strong>Freedom</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The crew included NASA astronauts <strong>Jessica Meir</strong> and <strong>Jack Hathaway</strong>, ESA astronaut <strong>Sophie Adenot</strong>, and Roscosmos cosmonaut <strong>Andrey Fedyaev</strong>. NASA said they landed at <strong>8:34 a.m. PDT</strong> on Oct. 8 after a 237-day mission that carried them more than 100 million miles around Earth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Who Came Home</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Crew-12 was made up of Meir, the mission commander; Hathaway, the pilot; Adenot of the European Space Agency; and Fedyaev of Roscosmos. Their SpaceX Dragon spacecraft launched in February and carried them to the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/space-crew-13-iss-record-seven-hours-55-minutes/"><strong>International Space Station</strong></a>, where they spent about 235 days living and working in orbit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":22202,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NASA’s <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-spacex-crew-12-splashes-down-sets-briefing-to-discuss-mission/"><strong>official mission release</strong></a> says the four completed more than 3,792 orbits around Earth. Meir and Fedyaev were both on their second spaceflight, while Hathaway and Adenot completed their first.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Splashdown</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dragon Freedom undocked from the space station on Wednesday and spent a little more than a day heading home. After its deorbit burn, the capsule entered Earth’s atmosphere and descended under parachutes before splashing down in the Pacific near Los Angeles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>SpaceX recovery teams secured the capsule and lifted it aboard the recovery ship before helping the crew out for medical checks. The safe return completed another round-trip flight for NASA’s Commercial Crew Program, which uses private spacecraft to carry astronauts to and from low Earth orbit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>They Did a Lot More Than Wait to Come Home</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The mission included hundreds of hours of science. NASA says the crew worked on stem-cell production, on-demand IV fluid generation for future deep-space missions, nutrition research, blood-flow studies, and research into how pneumonia-causing bacteria can contribute to long-term heart damage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Meir completed four spacewalks during the mission, bringing her career total to seven. Adenot completed three and became the first French woman to perform a spacewalk.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Crew-13 Is Already on the Station</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The handoff was already underway before Crew-12 left. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/space-crew-13-iss-record-seven-hours-55-minutes/"><strong>Crew-13 reached the ISS in a record 7 hours and 55 minutes</strong></a> earlier this month and is now continuing the station’s research mission.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>NASA has also continued expanding its partnership with SpaceX for crew transportation. BitcoinVersus.Tech previously covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/20/nasa-spacex-three-crew-flights-946-million-iss-contract/"><strong>NASA adding three more SpaceX crew flights</strong></a> under its Commercial Crew Program.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>A Long Mission Ends Safely</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The return closes a long but productive mission for the four-person crew. <a href="https://apnews.com/article/3d4ca5874f95fe94afc037b2443f184c"><strong>The Associated Press</strong></a> reported that the astronauts were especially eager to get back to their families and simple things they had gone months without—fresh air, showers, and being outside.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For Crew-12, the hardest part is now over: the capsule is back, the crew is safe, and eight months of work in orbit is officially in the books.</p>
