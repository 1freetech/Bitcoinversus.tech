<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hesai has launched Kosmo, a 3D world data engine designed to turn real environments into reusable training data for robots and physical-AI models.</strong> The system combines the Kosmo One spatial camera with the Kosmo World cloud platform, giving developers a way to capture, reconstruct and manage detailed 3D spaces.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://www.hesaitech.com/9974-2/"><strong>October 14 launch announcement</strong></a>, Hesai says Kosmo combines lidar, high-resolution imaging and 3D Gaussian Splatting. The company says a typical 100-square-meter indoor space can be scanned in about 15 minutes, while reconstruction can be up to five times faster than comparable workflows under similar conditions.</p>
