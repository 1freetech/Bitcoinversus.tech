<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Large distributed denial-of-service attacks are becoming much more common, according to new threat data released October 14.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.netscout.com/threatreport"><strong>NETSCOUT’s first-half 2026 report</strong></a> says its ATLAS system recorded more than 9.1 million DDoS attacks across 209 countries and territories from January through June. Attacks exceeding either 1 Tbps or 1 Gpps increased 1,286% year over year. NETSCOUT also recorded its first IPv6 attack above 1 Tbps, while active IPv6 mitigation increased more than 300%.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The report also says direct-path techniques represented 64.4% of observed attacks and that events lasting one minute or less increased from 1.6% of attacks in the second half of 2025 to 3.1% in the first half of 2026.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The direction is supported by separate industry data. <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/ddos-threat-report-2026-h1/"><strong>Cloudflare reported</strong></a> 935 network-layer attacks above 1 Tbps during the same six-month period, including 805 in the second quarter alone. The two companies measure different networks and use different methodologies, so their total attack counts should not be compared directly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For defenders, the trend makes basic architecture more important: <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/what-is-anycast/"><strong>Anycast</strong></a> can spread traffic geographically, a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/what-is-a-cdn/"><strong>CDN</strong></a> can add distributed capacity, and resilient <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/osntc-006-dns-basics/"><strong>DNS</strong></a> prevents one overloaded service from becoming a wider outage.</p>
