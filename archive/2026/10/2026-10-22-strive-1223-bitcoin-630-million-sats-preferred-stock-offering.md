<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin treasury company <strong>Strive, Inc. (Nasdaq: ASST)</strong> acquired <strong>1,223 BTC</strong> for approximately <strong>$100 million</strong> during October 14–16, 2026, then announced the completed <strong>$630 million</strong> initial offering of its SATS preferred stock on October 20. These are separate transactions: the new offering's proceeds should not be mistaken for Bitcoin already purchased.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li><strong>1,223 BTC</strong> purchased at an average of $81,734 per coin, including fees.</li><li><strong>36,818 BTC</strong> held as of October 16, after 4,045 BTC in purchases that week.</li><li><strong>$630 million gross</strong> raised through 7 million SATS preferred shares priced at $90 each.</li><li><strong>15% annual dividend rate</strong> calculated against $100 stated value per SATS share, subject to declared-payment terms.</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Bitcoin Holdings Reach 36,818 BTC</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Strive's <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1920406/000162828026066222/asst-20261019.htm">October 19 Form 8-K</a> shows that the 1,223 BTC purchase followed an earlier <strong>2,822 BTC</strong> acquired on October 12–13. Together they total <strong>4,045 BTC</strong> for the week, not 4,045 BTC plus another 1,223. The holdings increased from 32,773 BTC on October 9 to 36,818 BTC on October 16. Cash and cash equivalents totaled $317.5 million on October 16, before the SATS closing.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What The $630 Million Offering Means</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://investors.strive.com/news-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/Strive-Announces-Nasdaq-Listing-of-SATS-and-Closing-of-Upsized-Initial-Public-Offering/default.aspx">Strive's October 20 closing announcement</a>, investors bought <strong>7 million shares</strong> of 15% Series A Perpetual SATS Preferred Stock for $90 apiece. The 15% rate applies to each share's <strong>$100 stated amount</strong>, implying $15 in annual accrual per share, or $105 million across all offered shares; actual dividend payments are governed by the security's terms and board declarations. Scheduled business-day dividend dates begin November 9.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The company says <a href="https://investors.strive.com/news-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/Strive-Announces-Pricing-of-Upsized-Initial-Public-Offering-of-SATS-Stock/default.aspx">net proceeds may fund</a> Bitcoin or Bitcoin-related investments, other income-generating assets, working capital, or repurchases. It has not specified how much of the $630 million will buy Bitcoin. SATS is junior to Strive's SATA preferred stock but senior to common shares in the stated capital structure.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Growing Corporate Treasury Strategy</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The purchase extends Strive's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/strive-buys-3311-bitcoin/">earlier 3,311-BTC accumulation report</a>. The company joins a longer financing trend explored by BitcoinVersus.Tech in its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/microstrategy-plans-2-billion-equity-offering-to-purchase-bitcoin/">2024 coverage of a $2 billion equity-financed Bitcoin plan</a> and its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/16/boyaa-interactive-bets-big-on-bitcoin-with-100-million-investment-plan/">2023 report on Boyaa's $100 million Bitcoin investment plan</a>. See also the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/18/top-7-largest-public-company-bitcoin-treasuries-2026/">2026 public-company treasury ranking</a> for broader context.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Preferred equity avoids some conventional debt maturities but still creates financing costs and dividend expectations. Investors must weigh Bitcoin price swings, common-share dilution, preferred-share seniority and available liquidity. <strong>Bottom line:</strong> the disclosed 1,223-BTC purchase is complete, while any additional Bitcoin buying funded by SATS remains a future possibility.</p>
