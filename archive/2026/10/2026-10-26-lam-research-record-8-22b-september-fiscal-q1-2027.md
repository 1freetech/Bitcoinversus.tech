# Lam Research Reports Record $8.22 Billion Quarter as Chip Equipment Demand Surges

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Lam Research reported a record $8.22 billion in quarterly revenue on October 21, 2026, for the period ended September 27.</strong> Revenue rose <strong>22% sequentially</strong> from $6.72 billion and approximately <strong>54% year over year</strong>, according to its <a href="https://investor.lamresearch.com/2026-10-21-Lam-Research-Corporation-Reports-Financial-Results-for-the-Quarter-Ended-September-27%2C-2026">official earnings release</a> and <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707549/000070754926000052/lrcx-20261021.htm">SEC Form 8-K</a>. The company credited AI-driven demand for more sophisticated semiconductor manufacturing equipment.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Quarterly revenue reached $8.22 billion, up 22% from June 2026.</li><li>New wafer-fabrication systems generated $5.73 billion; support and related revenue contributed $2.49 billion.</li><li>GAAP operating margin reached 40.0%, with diluted earnings of $2.19 per share.</li><li>Lam forecasts December-quarter revenue of $8.6 billion, plus or minus $400 million, while warning of near-term supply constraints.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the Results Show</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:table {"hasFixedLayout":false} --><figure class="wp-block-table"><table><thead><tr><th>Metric</th><th>June 2026</th><th>September 2026</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Revenue</td><td>$6.72B</td><td>$8.22B</td></tr><tr><td>GAAP operating margin</td><td>37.4%</td><td>40.0%</td></tr><tr><td>GAAP diluted EPS</td><td>$1.81</td><td>$2.19</td></tr></tbody></table><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Source: Lam Research, September 2026 quarterly results; June comparison reported by the company.</figcaption></figure><!-- /wp:table -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The $5.73 billion systems figure includes advanced deposition, plasma etch, cleaning and related fabrication equipment. These tools build intricate transistor structures, multilayer memory and vertical interconnects. Lam's <a href="https://www.lamresearch.com/products/our-processes/etch/">etch technology guide</a> explains why high-aspect-ratio plasma processing is increasingly important as chip structures become taller and denser.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Manufacturing Capacity Matters</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Lam expects December-quarter revenue of approximately $8.6 billion, with a $400 million range. During its <a href="https://investor.lamresearch.com/events?item=105">October 21 investor webcast</a>, management said the outlook would have been higher without certain component supply bottlenecks. It also flagged cleanroom availability as a constraint as chipmakers expand. Those are forward-looking assessments, not guaranteed production or sales.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Comes Next</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Lam's stronger sales indicate substantial demand for tools used to manufacture AI processors and memory, but quarterly revenue is not a direct measure of global chip output. The December guidance and management's preliminary 2027 forecasts depend on customer spending, component delivery and factory readiness.
