## Gutenberg source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>D-Wave has delivered a 4,400-plus-qubit Advantage2 annealing quantum computer to Lombardy for installation at Villa Saporiti in Como, giving Italy its first on-premises system of this type. In its <a href="https://www.dwavequantum.com/company/newsroom/press-release/d-wave-delivers-italy-s-first-annealing-quantum-computer/">October 15 announcement</a>, D-Wave said the machine will operate within the Q-Alliance ecosystem under the name VoltaB1.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The installation follows a €10 million agreement with Swiss Quantum Technology SA. SQT acquired 50% of the system’s capacity for five years and holds an option to purchase the full machine. Once operational, Advantage2 will also be reachable through D-Wave’s Leap cloud service. <a href="https://quantumcomputingreport.com/d-wave-delivers-italys-first-annealing-quantum-computer-to-lombardy-infrastructure-hub/">Quantum Computing Report</a> independently confirmed the delivery and agreement structure.</p>

<p>Advantage2 uses quantum annealing rather than the gate-model approach pursued by many other systems. BitcoinVersus.Tech has followed both paths through <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/d-wave-gate-model-quantum-simulator-error-aware-developers/">D-Wave’s gate-model simulator</a>, its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/dwave-skywater-us-quantum-chip-manufacturing/">extended U.S. quantum-chip manufacturing deal</a>, and the photonic architecture behind <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/orca-launches-pt-3-quantum-computer/">ORCA’s PT-3 quantum computer</a>.</p>

<p>The system is scheduled for formal inauguration on October 16. For Italy, the significance is less about owning a quantum computer as a symbol and more about placing production-grade hardware close to universities, companies and researchers who can test optimization and materials workloads directly. That gives the country a local base for training, application development and industrial experimentation instead of relying only on remote access to overseas hardware.</p>
