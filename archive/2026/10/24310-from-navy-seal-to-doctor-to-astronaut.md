<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jonny Kim has already lived several careers that would normally stand alone. He enlisted in the U.S. Navy after high school in 2002, became a Navy SEAL, and served as a combat medic, sniper, navigator and point man during more than 100 combat operations. His decorations include the Silver Star and Bronze Star with Combat “V.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kim then changed direction without leaving public service. He earned a mathematics degree from the University of San Diego and graduated from Harvard Medical School in 2016, followed by emergency-medicine training in Boston. <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-astronaut-jonny-kim-departs-agency-to-continue-military-service/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">NASA’s official biography of his departure</a> records the unusual sequence: SEAL, physician, naval officer and eventually astronaut.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At 33, Kim was selected for NASA’s 2017 astronaut class. While serving at NASA, he added another qualification: in 2023 he earned his naval aviator “Wings of Gold,” becoming dual-designated as a naval aviator and flight surgeon.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>His first spaceflight finally came on April 8, 2025. Kim launched aboard Soyuz MS-27 and served as a flight engineer on Expeditions 72 and 73. <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-astronaut-jonny-kim-crewmates-return-from-space-station/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">NASA recorded 245 days in space</a>, 3,920 Earth orbits and nearly 104 million miles before the crew landed in Kazakhstan on December 9.</p>

<p>Kim’s flight came during a busy period for the station. BitcoinVersus.Tech has recently covered the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/final-iss-solar-arrays-crs-35/">final ISS roll-out solar arrays</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/nasa-is-now-taking-bids-for-the-space-stations-that-come-after-the-iss/">NASA’s search for commercial stations to follow the ISS</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/4-astronauts-return-earth-8-months-space/">another crew returning after eight months in orbit</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On August 27, 2026, Kim left NASA after nearly a decade with the agency. He returned to active Navy service as a lieutenant commander, with NASA saying he would finish the remainder of his military career in naval aviation training. The astronaut chapter ended, but the career did not.</p>
