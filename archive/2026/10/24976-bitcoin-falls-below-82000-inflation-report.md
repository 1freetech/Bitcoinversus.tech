<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin fell below $82,000 Wednesday after September U.S. inflation came in hotter than markets expected, adding pressure to risk assets and forcing traders to reassess the Federal Reserve’s next move.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm">U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics</a> said the Consumer Price Index rose 0.6% in September and 3.7% from a year earlier. Gasoline jumped 9.1% for the month and accounted for more than half of the overall increase. Core CPI rose 0.3% monthly and 2.6% annually.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin traded around $81,900 during the afternoon after moving above $84,000 earlier in the session. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-10-14/">Reuters reported</a> that the dollar strengthened, Treasury yields rose and stocks fell after the report, while market-implied odds of an October Fed rate increase climbed sharply.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan <a href="https://twitter.com/Matt_Hougan/status/2110356825866666041">said on X</a> that he initially expected crypto to fall more sharply after seeing the inflation data, making the market’s comparatively limited reaction notable.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Hougan’s reaction highlights the tension between hotter inflation and Bitcoin’s relatively contained initial decline.</em></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The connection runs through <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/why-do-interest-rates-exist/">interest rates</a>: higher expected rates can strengthen the dollar and raise yields, making speculative assets less attractive at the margin. BitcoinVersus.Tech previously tracked <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/10/core-cpi-falls-to-lowest-level-since-2021/">core inflation’s earlier decline</a> and, more recently, the large swings in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/bitcoin-etf-flows-swung-920-million-one-week-october-2026/">Bitcoin ETF flows</a>.</p>
