<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adani Enterprises and Jabil signed a <a href="https://www.adani.com/newsroom/media-releases/adani-enterprises-jabil-sign-mou-for-50-50-data-centre-hardware-joint-venture">non-binding memorandum of understanding on October 13</a> for a proposed 50:50 joint venture to manufacture AI and data-center hardware in India. The companies say the platform would target <strong>multi-gigawatt</strong> manufacturing capacity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The proposed portfolio reaches well beyond servers. Adani and Jabil say it could include AI racks, storage, networking gear, power-distribution units, liquid-cooling systems and transformers, plus installation and commissioning. That matters because BitcoinVersus.Tech has already tracked how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/ai-demand-driving-more-power-lines/">AI demand is driving more power infrastructure</a>, while <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/credo-1-6t-zeroflap-light-cable-ai-links/">1.6T optical links</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/microsoft-maia-300-ai-chip/">custom AI chips</a> are pushing the rest of the rack forward.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The wording is important: this is still a proposal. The venture remains subject to definitive agreements, approvals and customary conditions. A <a href="https://www.financialexpress.com/business/industry-adani-group-jabil-inc-sign-mou-for-hardware-jv-4360592/">same-day Financial Express report</a> likewise describes the deal as an MoU rather than a completed joint venture.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->

<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>NDTV Profit explains the original Adani-Jabil AI infrastructure alliance announced in June, which the October 13 MoU now advances toward a proposed 50:50 manufacturing venture.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If completed, the significance is vertical integration: AI compute hardware, networking, cooling and power equipment would be assembled inside the same manufacturing ecosystem. The companies estimate India could reach <strong>5–8 GW</strong> of data-center capacity by 2030, but that figure is an industry forecast, not a guaranteed outcome.</p>
