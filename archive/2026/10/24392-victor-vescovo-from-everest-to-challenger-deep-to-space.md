<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Victor Vescovo has built one of the most extraordinary exploration résumés of the modern era: retired U.S. Navy commander, private-equity investor, Seven Summits climber, polar explorer, deep-ocean pilot and space traveler.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>His record spans the vertical extremes of the planet. Vescovo reached the summit of Mount Everest in 2010, later completed the Explorers Grand Slam by climbing the highest peak on every continent and traveling to both the North and South Poles, and then turned his attention downward.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Between 2018 and 2019, Vescovo led the Five Deeps Expedition, piloting the submersible <em>Limiting Factor</em> to the deepest point in each of Earth’s five oceans. At Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, he descended nearly seven miles below sea level. <a href="https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/709910-first-person-to-complete-the-explorers-extreme-trifecta-male" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Guinness World Records</a> recognizes him as the first person to reach Earth’s highest point, its deepest point and space.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>His deep-ocean career kept expanding. By July 2022, Vescovo had reached Challenger Deep 15 times, another Guinness record. The repeated dives also supported scientific mapping and helped refine measurements of the trench’s depth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then he went higher than Everest. On June 4, 2022, Vescovo flew aboard Blue Origin’s NS-21 mission and crossed the Kármán line. <a href="https://www.blueorigin.com/news/new-shepard-ns-21-mission-announcement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Blue Origin identified him</a> as an explorer, co-founder of private-equity firm Insight Equity and retired U.S. Navy commander.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That made Vescovo the first person to complete what Guinness calls the Explorers’ Extreme Trifecta: Everest, Challenger Deep and space. His résumé belongs beside <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/from-spacewalk-to-challenger-deep/">Kathryn Sullivan’s path from spacewalk to Challenger Deep</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/from-marine-mechanic-to-doctor-to-astronaut/">Story Musgrave’s career from Marine mechanic to doctor to astronaut</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/from-navy-seal-to-doctor-to-astronaut/">Jonny Kim’s journey from Navy SEAL to doctor to astronaut</a>. Few careers cover more literal ground.</p>
