<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>More than two tons of historic molten-salt reactor material from Oak Ridge National Laboratory has reached Texas for use in a new research reactor.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>ORNL <a href="https://www.ornl.gov/news/transfer-lithium-7-salt-accelerates-molten-salt-reactor-deployment">transferred</a> lithium-fluoride and beryllium-fluoride salt, known as FLiBe, from its 1960s Molten Salt Reactor Experiment inventory to Natura Resources. The salt contains lithium-7 enriched to <strong>99.99%</strong>, which helps limit neutron absorption and tritium production.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/GovNuclear/status/1820971159858401619]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>The U.S. Department of Energy’s nuclear office previously showed ORNL hardware used to study gas behavior inside molten-salt reactor systems.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Abilene Christian University <a href="https://acu.edu/2026/09/21/historic-molten-salt-reactor-coolant-arrives-at-acus-next-lab/">said the shipment arrived</a> for its NEXT Lab and Natura’s MSR-1 project. The Nuclear Regulatory Commission lists the planned reactor at up to <strong>1 MW thermal</strong>; it is a research reactor and is not designed to generate electricity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uHT-w2x6dDg]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>ORNL demonstrates remote maintenance concepts developed during the original molten-salt reactor program.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If the project reaches operation as planned, it would be the first U.S. salt-fueled reactor since Oak Ridge’s original experiment shut down in 1969. The project connects directly to BitcoinVersus.Tech’s coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">small modular reactors</a>, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/energy-westinghouse-evinci-microreactor-1011c-criticality-test/">eVinci microreactor</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/02/energy-deployable-unity-nuclear-battery-dome-test/">deployable nuclear batteries</a>.</p>
