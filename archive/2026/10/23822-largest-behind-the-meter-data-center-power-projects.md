<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>AI data centers are increasingly bringing their own electricity. Ranked by announced dedicated or on-site generation capacity, these are seven of the largest publicly documented projects BitcoinVersus.Tech could verify as of October 11, 2026. Capacity is planned or permitted—not necessarily operating.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. Fermi Project Matador — 11 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://fermiamerica.com/fermi-america-unveils-plan-for-largest-u-s-energy-and-data-complex/">Fermi America</a> plans an 11 GW private-grid AI and energy campus near Amarillo, Texas, combining gas, nuclear, solar and storage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. Pacifico GW Ranch — 7.65 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.pacificoenergy.com/gw-ranch">GW Ranch</a> in Pecos County has approval for up to 7.65 GW of gas generation plus batteries and solar for hyperscale data centers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Nexus Hubbard — about 7.2 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nexus’ Texas permit filings describe 31 turbines and 24 gas engines totaling roughly 7.2 GW beside a hyperscale data-center campus.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. Project Star — 6.47 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/data-centers-project-star-encinal-texas-22-3-billion-5-gw-ai-campus/">Project Star</a> pairs 6.47 GW of planned gas generation with an adjacent 5 GW Related Digital campus in Encinal, Texas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. Poolside Project Horizon — 2 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The proposed Pecos County campus was designed around 2 GW of behind-the-meter gas generation, though the project’s development status has become uncertain.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">6. Microsoft Pecos — 2 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/06/22/powering-the-next-wave-of-ai-expanding-capacity-with-our-new-datacenter-in-pecos/">Microsoft</a> says its new Pecos campus will add about 2 GW of data-center capacity and launch with co-located behind-the-meter gas power.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">7. Alpha Digital Campus — 2 GW</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>PowerBridge’s planned Reeves County campus targets up to 2 GW using co-located generation for future hyperscale and AI tenants.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
