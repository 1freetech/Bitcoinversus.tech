<p><strong>Bitcoin Verse of the Day — October 14, 2026</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>“We want Bitcoin to be a system where anyone in the world can send money to anybody. That is the whole point.”</p><cite>Gloria Zhao</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gloria Zhao said this during her <a href="https://btctranscripts.com/adopting-bitcoin/2021/2021-11-16-gloria-zhao-transaction-relay-policy">Transaction Relay Policy for L2 Developers</a> talk at Adopting Bitcoin 2021. The line appears near the beginning of the presentation, where she explains the design goals behind Bitcoin’s transaction relay system: accessibility, censorship resistance, security and incentive compatibility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Zhao’s framing is direct. Bitcoin is not only a ledger for holding value; its payment system is supposed to remain open enough that a person can broadcast a transaction without needing approval from a bank, company or central operator. That goal is why technical details such as mempool policy, node accessibility and transaction relay matter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The quote connects with BitcoinVersus coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/the-internet-needed-reliable-e-cash/">reliable internet e-cash</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/bitcoin-is-freedom-money-bitcoin-verse-of-the-day/">Bitcoin as freedom money</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/proof-of-work-is-based-on-physics-bitcoin-verse/">Bitcoin’s proof-of-work foundation</a>. The technical point is simple: global payments only work as advertised if ordinary users can still reach the network.</p>
