## Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>More than <a href="https://www.itu.int/digital-resilience/submarine-cables/">99% of international data exchange</a> travels through submarine telecommunications cables, according to the International Telecommunication Union. Satellites matter, but most traffic crossing oceans still moves through fiber on the seafloor.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A <a href="https://www.businessinsider.com/inside-americas-only-internet-sea-cable-maker-supplying-google-2026-10">Business Insider report</a> published October 12 says SubCom, based in Newington, New Hampshire, is the only American company capable of both manufacturing complete long-haul subsea telecom cable systems and laying them at sea.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That claim is narrower than saying SubCom is the only U.S. company making any type of undersea cable. The distinction is the ability to build and deploy a complete submarine telecommunications system. <a href="https://www.subcom.com/documents/SubCom_of_Newington_plans_multi-million-dollar_expansion-20JAN2026.pdf">SubCom says</a> it has built more than 200 underwater systems and became the first company to deploy 1 million kilometers of subsea cable in 2025.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>TeleGeography estimates more than 1.5 million kilometers of submarine cable were in service globally in early 2026. Its current map tracks 607 in-service systems and another 102 planned.</p>

<p>The manufacturing bottleneck fits a broader trend BitcoinVersus has covered in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/08/24/data-center-cabling-fundamentals-101-everything-a-technician-needs-to-know/">data-center cabling</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/24/1-6t-ethernet-data-center-deployment/">1.6T Ethernet</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/networking-att-corning-fiber-supply-field-installation/">AT&amp;T and Corning’s U.S. fiber expansion</a>: cloud and AI growth ultimately depends on physical networks that still have to be manufactured, installed and maintained.</p>
