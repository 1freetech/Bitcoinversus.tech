## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>IonQ agreed on October 12 to acquire cybersecurity company Elisity in an all-stock transaction valued at up to <strong>$1.3 billion</strong>, expanding the quantum-computing company deeper into enterprise security.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-to-acquire-elisity-to-accelerate-zero-trust-for-the-quantum-era">IonQ’s announcement</a>, the deal includes up to $150 million in performance-based earnouts. The companies expect the transaction to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approval and other closing conditions. The <a href="https://www.wsj.com/business/deals/quantum-computing-company-ionq-to-buy-cybersecurity-firm-for-up-to-1-3-billion-65af65bd">Wall Street Journal</a> also reported the agreement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Elisity develops Zero Trust and identity-based microsegmentation software designed to limit which users, devices, workloads and AI agents can communicate across a network. IonQ says Elisity has more than 100 customer deployments; that figure is a company claim.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The acquisition would combine that network-security platform with IonQ’s post-quantum cryptography and quantum key distribution products. It does not mean quantum computers are currently breaking mainstream encryption; the strategy is aimed at current attacks and longer-term cryptographic migration.</p>
