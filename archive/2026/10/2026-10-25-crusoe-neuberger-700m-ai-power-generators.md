# Crusoe Secures $700 Million to Finance AI Data-Center Power Equipment

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Crusoe and Neuberger Specialty Finance announced a $700 million equipment-backed financing facility on October 22, 2026, to support power generation for Crusoe-owned and -built AI data centers.</strong> The loan facility is secured by recently ordered generators, reinforcing the importance of securing electricity infrastructure before high-density computing sites come online.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>The $700 million facility finances power-generation equipment, not completed operating capacity.</li><li>Recently ordered generators serve as collateral for the transaction.</li><li>Crusoe says the equipment will supply behind-the-meter electricity to its AI facilities.</li><li>The announcement does not disclose which generator orders secure the loan, financing terms or how many megawatts will result.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Behind-the-Meter Power Matters</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Behind-the-meter generation produces electricity on a customer's side of its utility meter, potentially reducing reliance on delayed grid connections. For an AI data center, however, installing generator equipment still requires fuel supply, electrical protection, switching, controls, emissions compliance and commissioning. <a href="https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/neuberger-and-crusoe-announce-700-million-facility">Crusoe's joint announcement</a> identifies gas generators as the planned equipment but does not claim they are already online.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The companies describe the financing as an asset-based facility, meaning equipment supports the borrowing. That differs from treating the entire data-center campus—or its GPUs—as the collateral. Neither party published the interest rate, term or a schedule for the equipment's delivery.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Energy Infrastructure Before AI Capacity</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Crusoe previously announced a <a href="https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/bergen-engines-signs-approx-750mw-u-s-power-agreement-with-crusoe-for-ai-data-centers">roughly 750 MW Bergen Engines agreement</a>, comprising a 438 MW contract and a 310 MW letter of intent. Those earlier orders illustrate its power strategy, but the latest release does not confirm whether they are pledged to this financing.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Crusoe leaders discuss the relationship between energy, data centers and cloud computing in this earlier company video. It explains the strategy rather than the October 22 transaction.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
