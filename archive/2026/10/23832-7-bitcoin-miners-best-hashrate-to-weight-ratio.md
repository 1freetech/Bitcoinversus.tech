<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Hashrate usually gets compared with watts, dollars, or joules per terahash. Weight is another useful engineering metric: how much SHA-256 throughput fits into each kilogram of hardware.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Using published manufacturer hashrate and net-weight figures for model variants we could verify, these seven machines had the highest ratios. The calculation is simple: <strong>TH/s ÷ net kilograms</strong>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} --><ol class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Antminer S23 Hyd.</strong> — 580 TH/s, 13.0 kg — <strong>44.6 TH/kg</strong></li><li><strong>Antminer S23 XP Hyd.</strong> — 600 TH/s, 15.8 kg — <strong>38.0 TH/kg</strong></li><li><strong>Antminer S21 XP Hyd.</strong> — 473 TH/s, 13.8 kg — <strong>34.3 TH/kg</strong></li><li><strong>Antminer S21 XP+ Hyd.</strong> — 500 TH/s, 14.8 kg — <strong>33.8 TH/kg</strong></li><li><strong>Antminer S21e XP Hyd.</strong> — 430 TH/s, 13.8 kg — <strong>31.2 TH/kg</strong></li><li><strong>Antminer S21j XP Hyd.</strong> — 495 TH/s, 16.2 kg — <strong>30.6 TH/kg</strong></li><li><strong>Antminer S23e U2H</strong> — 865 TH/s, 29.0 kg — <strong>29.8 TH/kg</strong></li></ol><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The striking part is that every machine in this top seven uses hydro cooling. Removing large onboard air-cooling hardware lets manufacturers pack unusually high compute density into the miner chassis.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
