## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://orcacomputing.com/orca-computing-launches-pt-3-the-first-quantum-computer-built-for-commercial-quantum-advantage/">ORCA Computing launched PT-3 on October 15, 2026</a>, putting its third-generation photonic quantum computer into service at Digital Realty’s Innovation Lab in London alongside conventional accelerated computing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The system is notable because it is designed for an ordinary data-center environment rather than a specialized cryogenic facility. ORCA says PT-3 runs at room temperature, draws about <strong>6 kW across two standard racks</strong>, and can be upgraded in the field. That puts it directly into the broader contest BitcoinVersus.Tech examined in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/quantum-computing-industry-no-winning-architecture/">The Quantum Computing Industry Still Has No Winning Architecture</a>.</p>

The publication also reports an approximate system weight of 800 kg.</p>

<p><em>ORCA explains how its rack-mounted, room-temperature photonic quantum systems integrate with classical computing infrastructure.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>ORCA says a hybrid extension of NVIDIA’s Proteína protein-design model improved the ratio of useful proteins by <strong>20%</strong> versus its classical baseline. On a 25,755-variable energy-scheduling benchmark, the company says PT-3 ran its hybrid workflow in about an hour, more than <strong>12× faster</strong> than its predecessor at the same solution quality. Those are vendor-reported results, not a blanket proof that PT-3 outperforms classical computing across workloads.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The launch arrives as other photonic approaches move toward production, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/xanadu-globalfoundries-quantum-photonic-chips-manufacturing-2026/">Xanadu and GlobalFoundries’ quantum-photonic manufacturing work</a>, while large-scale projects such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/psiquantum-150-million-us-loan-quantum-factory/">PsiQuantum’s planned U.S. quantum factory</a> pursue a different route to useful machines.</p>
