<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Richard Garriott built imaginary worlds first. Then he started exploring the real one.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before spaceflight and deep-ocean expeditions, Garriott was already helping define an industry. He began developing computer games as a teenager and created the <em>Ultima</em> series, one of the foundational franchises in computer role-playing games. The <a href="https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/computer-games/16/203">Computer History Museum</a> describes <em>Ultima Online</em> as an early massively multiplayer online role-playing game and credits the broader series with decades of influence on computer gaming.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU90N4Cbj10","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LU90N4Cbj10</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Garriott and his brother Robert founded Origin Systems in 1983. The studio expanded the <em>Ultima</em> universe and later became part of Electronic Arts. Decades later, the influence of those games is still visible in open-world RPGs and online virtual worlds.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then Garriott took the word “exploration” literally.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On October 12, 2008, he launched aboard Soyuz TMA-13 and traveled to the International Space Station. <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/history/space-station-20th-space-flight-participants/">NASA records</a> that Garriott spent 10 days aboard the station, conducting educational outreach and photographing Earth. He also became the first American second-generation space traveler; his father, Owen Garriott, had flown on Skylab and the Space Shuttle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That places him alongside other remarkable private-space pioneers such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/anousheh-ansari-from-engineer-to-tech-founder-to-space-explorer/">Anousheh Ansari</a>, whose engineering and entrepreneurial career also eventually led to orbit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znF-8xuN0J0","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znF-8xuN0J0</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Space was not the end of the journey. Garriott traveled to the North and South Poles, visited the wreck of the <em>Titanic</em>, and in 2021 descended to Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench—the deepest known point in Earth’s oceans.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That descent put him in another tiny group of explorers whose careers span the most extreme places on Earth. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/victor-vescovo-from-everest-to-challenger-deep-to-space/">Victor Vescovo</a> helped make modern full-ocean-depth exploration possible, while <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/from-spacewalk-to-challenger-deep/">Kathryn Sullivan</a> became the first person to both walk in space and reach Challenger Deep.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The thread connecting Garriott’s career is not simply adventure. It is the habit of building a world, learning its rules and then pushing beyond the boundary that everyone else accepted.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>He did it first with software.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Then he did it with the planet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
