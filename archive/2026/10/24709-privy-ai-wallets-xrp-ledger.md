<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Privy is bringing programmable wallet infrastructure to the XRP Ledger, giving developers a simpler way to build embedded crypto payments and AI-agent transactions with XRP, RLUSD and other XRPL assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://privy.io/blog/building-payments-and-agentic-commerce-with-the-xrp-ledger">October 13 announcement</a>, Privy said developers will be able to create XRPL wallets programmatically, sign transactions and give users portable wallets without forcing them to manage a separate crypto app. Server-side wallets can also let software agents hold funds and pay for services through x402.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/14/ripple-adds-stripe-owned-wallets-to-xrp-ledger-as-ai-payments-pass-14-million">CoinDesk reported</a> that Ripple and Privy see the same infrastructure supporting machine-to-machine payments, while noting that new XRPL wallets still need the network’s minimum reserve before they can transact.</p>
