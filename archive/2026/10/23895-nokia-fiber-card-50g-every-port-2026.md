## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nokia announced a new <a href="https://www.nokia.com/newsroom/nokia-launches-flexible-50g25g10gpon-solution-to-accelerate-ai-ready-fiber-broadband/">16-port fiber broadband line card</a> on October 12, 2026. The company says each port can support GPON, 10G, 25G and 50G passive optical network (PON) technologies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The design would let internet providers introduce faster services on existing fiber infrastructure without committing every port to one generation of equipment. Nokia says the card uses its Quillion chipset and multi-PON optical modules, with general availability planned for <strong>2027</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Nokia also claims up to four times lower upstream latency and improved encryption and authentication. Those are vendor performance claims, not independently verified results. A <a href="https://www.telecoms.com/telecoms-infrastructure/nokia-adds-50g-pon-to-its-multi-pon-line-card">Telecoms.com report</a> notes that operators could add capacity without replacing an entire network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The optical-component ecosystem is developing too. Semtech's <a href="https://twitter.com/SemtechCorp/status/1904609270823371225">earlier 50G PON chipset announcement</a> is a separate example of work on next-generation access equipment, not a Nokia partnership announcement.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Higher access speeds are only part of network performance. Fiber providers must also manage <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/12/07/fiber-optic-training-splitters-vs-taps/">optical splitters</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/12/16/fiber-optic-training-wdm-wavelength-division-multiplexing/">wavelengths</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/osfotc-001-fiber-optic-safety-handling-inspection-cleaning-basics/">fiber installation quality</a>. The 50G figure describes a PON technology's capacity, not a promise that every customer will receive 50 Gbps.</p>
