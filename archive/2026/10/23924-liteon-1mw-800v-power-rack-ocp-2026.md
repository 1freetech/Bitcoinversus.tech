<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>LITEON is using OCP Global Summit 2026 to push its 800V DC power architecture deeper into megawatt-scale AI infrastructure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/liteon_powering-the-next-generation-of-ai-data-centers-activity-7511340447420542976-qaAT">official OCP announcement</a>, LITEON says it will showcase an 800V DC architecture spanning power supply, thermal management, mechanical design, software control and system integration. The company is also scheduled to present a session titled “1MW Power Rack Solution for AI Data Center.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The direction fits a broader industry shift. BitcoinVersus has recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/delta-800-vdc-3mw-cooling-ocp-2026/">Delta’s 800V DC and 3 MW cooling push</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/semiconductors-microchip-navitas-800v-6v-ai-rack-20kw-converter/">800V-to-6V rack conversion</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/data-centers-quantumscape-solid-state-battery-1mw-ai-racks-powerblock/">1 MW AI-rack power systems</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>LITEON previously detailed the same architecture around NVIDIA Vera Rubin, saying higher-voltage DC can reduce current, simplify power delivery and support denser AI racks. That remains a company claim rather than an independent efficiency benchmark. <a href="https://www.liteon.com/en/news/press-center/content/liteon-nvidia-gtc-2026">LITEON technical background</a></p>
