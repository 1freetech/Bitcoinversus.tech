<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google has expanded <strong>Gemini 4 Argon</strong> beyond its limited cybersecurity rollout, making the frontier model available to paying developers and businesses and to Gemini Pro and Ultra subscribers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The timing matters. Google first <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">announced Argon on September 30</a>, but access initially went to trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program. The broader commercial rollout arrived October 13.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Argon is built for</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Google positions Argon for long-horizon work in software engineering, finance, legal research, cybersecurity and science. It supports a <strong>1 million-token output limit</strong>. Google reports a 77.9% score on DeepSWE v1.1, 51.3% on AutomationBench and a tied-first 68% on CWE-bench v1 for vulnerability remediation. Those benchmark figures are Google-reported results, not independent BitcoinVersus.Tech testing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Google is also expanding Argon access through its Gemini app, AI development tools and AI Mode in Search.</p>
