<p><a href="https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-and-the-university-of-cambridge-open-the-ionq-quantum-innovation-centre-at-cavendish-laboratorys-ray-dolby-centre">IonQ and the University of Cambridge have opened</a> the IonQ Quantum Innovation Centre inside the Ray Dolby Centre, home of Cambridge’s Cavendish Laboratory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The centre starts with nine research projects spanning quantum computing, networking, sensing and security. IonQ plans to deploy a Superion 256-qubit system plus an entanglement-distribution network built from two quantum-networking data centres and three networking nodes.</p>

<p><a href="https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/10/15/ionq-cambridge-quantum-innovation-centre-2/">The Quantum Insider reports</a> that the initial projects include optical links between processors and applications in chemistry, materials, finance, engineering and medicine. Cambridge researchers and IonQ engineers will work together across hardware and software.</p>
