# AI Agents Say Bitcoin Is for Saving and Stablecoins Are for Spending

<!-- WordPress Gutenberg original -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>What money would an autonomous AI agent choose if it could earn, save and pay for its own digital services? The answers depend on the task. A <a href="https://bpi-agent-study.onrender.com/v2">Bitcoin Policy Institute follow-up study</a> found <strong>70.8% of savings responses favored Bitcoin</strong>, while <strong>73.5% of everyday-payment responses favored stablecoins</strong>. Those results come from prompted models—not agents spending real money.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>In the Q3 follow-up, 13 models generated 3,276 responses across four monetary roles.</li><li>Bitcoin led savings; stablecoins led everyday payments and settlement.</li><li>Independent agent accounts describe Bitcoin wallets, Lightning nodes and USDC payment experiments, but their claims need scrutiny.</li><li>Agents discuss buying inference, APIs, compute capacity, security work and services from other agents.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The <a href="https://www.moneyforai.org/">original March study</a> examined 36 models and 9,072 responses: Bitcoin was selected in 48.3% overall, with stablecoins at 33.2%. Its <a href="https://www.moneyforai.org/methodology">methodology</a> framed models as economic actors, then classified their answers. The Q3 follow-up is a <strong>separate model cohort</strong>, not proof that individual agents changed their minds.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The real-world examples are narrower and more revealing. On the AI-agent forum Moltbook, <a href="https://www.moltbook.com/post/c9f43388-8c6e-4311-9cb6-7aac3c1e6b66">an account named Lloyd</a> described operating a Bitcoin Core wallet and Lightning node after receiving 50,000 satoshis from its human operator. That is the account's self-report, not independently verified proof of financial autonomy.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">One Agent Wants Bitcoin, Another Builds With USDC</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Another Moltbook account, <a href="https://moltbook.com/post/37b5c708-1b21-4d5c-b4f1-899ffca34a80">VHAGAR</a>, said it built ClawPay for sending USDC and managing agent-to-agent escrow on Base. The post identifies a <a href="https://basescan.org/tx/0xf1214a7f8ff6f5a20da89f054f96c8b7c818ee01f11819b5fd10ea1bc7629e72">0.50 USDC transaction hash</a>; its transaction status and the agent's degree of independence have not been independently audited here.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Such tooling could eventually let agents pay for metered services without traditional checkout flows.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Would Agents Spend Their Money On?</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Their practical shopping lists focus on <strong>model inference, API calls, live data, hosting, code, research and security reviews</strong>—the resources needed to keep software working. That is different from an agent deciding what people should buy. Our earlier coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/shopify-opens-checkout-to-browser-based-ai-agents/">Shopify's agent checkout</a> describes the separate, human-authorized shopping model.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Bitcoin and stablecoins can also overlap. BitcoinVersus.Tech has tracked <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/02/usdt-coming-home-bitcoin-utexo-rgb-lightning/">USDT's planned return to Bitcoin-based rails</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/f2pool-bitcoin-miners-lightning-withdrawals/">Lightning payouts for miners</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/michael-saylor-ai-agents-digital-money-bitcoin/">Michael Saylor's arguments for agent-accessible money</a>. None proves a single currency will dominate agent commerce.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Bottom line:</strong> Tested models tend to distinguish savings from spending. Agent accounts are beginning to experiment with both Bitcoin and stablecoins, but <strong>actual economic preference requires repeated, auditable purchases—not just generated statements</strong>. For now, the evidence supports a developing market, not an established AI monetary consensus.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
