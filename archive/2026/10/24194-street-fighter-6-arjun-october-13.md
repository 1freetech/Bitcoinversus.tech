<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Street Fighter 6 is adding Arjun on October 13 as the second playable fighter in its Year 4 lineup, bringing a slower, power-focused style built around yogic breathing techniques and heavy attacks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://steamcommunity.com/games/1364780/announcements/detail/676256891013169833" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official gameplay announcement</a>, Capcom describes Arjun as a former police officer in India who was framed after his partner was killed. His combat design emphasizes large strikes, recoverable damage and breathing-based abilities rather than speed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Arjun arrives after Yasmine in the Year 4 roster. BitcoinVersus.Tech previously covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/gaming-capcom-rex-re-engine-ai-assisted-game-development/">Capcom’s work on AI-assisted game development</a>, part of a broader effort to modernize the tools behind its games while continuing to expand long-running franchises.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The technical side of Capcom’s pipeline is also changing. BitcoinVersus.Tech reported that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/gaming-capcom-rex-re-engine-ai-generation-game-engine/">the company is evolving RE Engine through its REX initiative</a>, which is intended to support increasingly complex production requirements across future titles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://us-store.captown.capcom.com/blogs/news/0000021" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Capcom’s Year 4 pass listing</a> says Arjun is part of a four-character package with Yasmine, Tifa and Bosch. Owners of the Year 4 Character Pass or Ultimate Pass receive the fighters as they unlock, while individual characters can also be obtained separately in the game.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The release also lands during a busy stretch for large-scale game technology. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently looked at <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/02/gaming-ace-combat-8-unreal-engine-5-100-player-air-base/">Ace Combat 8’s Unreal Engine 5 multiplayer systems</a>, showing how major publishers are simultaneously pushing character design, online systems and engine technology.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Arjun’s launch is a roster update rather than a new game or engine release. The important change for players is straightforward: Street Fighter 6 gains another distinct combat archetype, while Year 4 continues toward Tifa and Bosch after Yasmine and Arjun.</p>
