<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>NEURA Robotics has introduced Neuron AI, a multimodal foundation model designed to give robots a broader understanding of the physical world.</strong> The company says the model combines 3D vision, touch, audio, robot state, language and memory so machines can act on information that goes beyond a camera feed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to the <a href="https://www.streetinsider.com/Business+Wire/NEURA+Robotics+Introduces+Neuron+AI%3A+The+Brain+of+the+Physical+World/27182600.html"><strong>October 14 launch announcement</strong></a>, Neuron AI uses a Perception-Language-Action architecture and is trained with real-world robot data, simulation and human demonstrations. NEURA says the same model can operate across five of its robot embodiments and can be fine-tuned for customer tasks.</p>

The important shift is that Neuron AI attempts to connect those signals with memory and language inside one model rather than treating perception and action as separate pipelines.</p>
