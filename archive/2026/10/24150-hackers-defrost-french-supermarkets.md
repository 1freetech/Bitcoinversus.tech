<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hackers remotely disrupted refrigeration systems at hundreds of supermarkets across France, forcing stores to discard fresh and frozen food after refrigerators and freezers were pushed into defrost conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crq5nzxv9q4yo">BBC News reported</a> that major French grocery chains were affected, including Intermarché and Coopérative U. Reuters separately reported temperatures in some refrigeration systems rising as high as <strong>50°C (122°F)</strong>, with food losses and fires reported at affected locations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Attack Reached the Physical World</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This was not simply a website outage or stolen-data incident. The attackers reportedly manipulated operational technology that controls supermarket refrigeration. That makes the event closer to an industrial-control-system attack than a typical retail breach. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/ai-reaches-scada-systems-booz-allen-ot-test/">AI testing against SCADA systems</a>, explained the difference between <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/hmi-vs-scada/">HMI and SCADA</a>, and reviewed how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/osdcec-005-data-center-monitoring-controls-bms-epms-dcim-snmp-modbus-alarms-trending/">Modbus and monitoring controls connect physical infrastructure to software</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Older Refrigeration Controllers Are Under Scrutiny</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The refrigeration systems at the center of the investigation use Danfoss control equipment. Danfoss has previously published security advisories for the AK-SM 8xxA family, including an authentication-bypass flaw, and has repeatedly advised customers to keep supported systems on current firmware. Its <a href="https://www.danfoss.com/en/service-and-support/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure/danfoss-security-advisories/dsa-2025-03-01/">2025 security advisory</a> says versions before 4.2 were affected by CVE-2025-41450.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That does <strong>not</strong> establish which vulnerability the attackers used. Public reporting has not yet identified a confirmed intrusion path, and no responsible attacker has been publicly established. France's national cybersecurity agency ANSSI and the Paris prosecutor's office are investigating.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The incident is a practical warning about internet-connected operational technology: when software controls temperature, power, pumps or other physical processes, a cyberattack can damage inventory and equipment even when no customer database is touched.</p>
