<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Chery Group’s robotics brand AiMOGA says it will debut a new home robot at the 2026 Chery International User Summit. In its <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/13/3380004/0/en/car-robot-synergy-real-world-impact-aimoga-product-launch-to-be-held-during-2026-chery-international-user-summit.html">October 13 announcement</a>, the company described the machine as a household-focused robot built for everyday needs and natural interaction. AiMOGA has not yet disclosed specifications, price or a shipping schedule, so the launch should be treated as an upcoming product debut rather than a proven consumer deployment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>AiMOGA says its broader robot lineup has passed 3,000 cumulative global deliveries across more than 60 countries and regions. That scale is not just a company claim: <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/cherys-robot-unit-eyes-ipo-targets-overseas-market-police-robots-2026-08-19/">Reuters reported in August</a> that AiMOGA had delivered more than 3,000 robots globally while preparing for a possible IPO.</p>

<p>The bigger story is the crossover between automotive engineering and embodied AI. BitcoinVersus.Tech has tracked robots entering <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/dr-horton-robots-1250-homes/">home construction</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/doug-field-mind-robotics-president/">industrial AI through Mind Robotics</a>, and the growing push for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/10/nvidia-halos-humanoid-robot-safety/">humanoid safety systems</a>. AiMOGA’s next step moves that same race directly toward the home.</p>
