<p><strong>Tower Semiconductor and Semtech are expanding dedicated silicon-germanium manufacturing capacity for AI data-center interconnects.</strong> The companies said October 13 that Semtech will invest to secure additional SiGe capacity across Tower fabs in the United States, Israel and Japan under a new multi-year agreement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The deal extends a 20-year partnership. According to <a href="https://towersemi.com/2026/10/13/10132026-2/"><strong>Tower’s announcement</strong></a>, Semtech uses Tower’s SiGe process for transimpedance amplifiers, laser and modulator drivers, linear equalizers and other high-speed analog parts used in optical and copper links. The companies say the added capacity is intended to support 800G and 1.6T products while preparing for 3.2T interconnects.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.semtech.com/products/signal-integrity/data-center"><strong>Semtech’s data-center portfolio</strong></a> includes analog optical and copper interconnect products for 400G, 800G and 1.6T systems. The company has also been publishing technical explainers on lower-power optical connectivity as AI clusters move toward denser links.</p>

<p>The announcement fits a wider shift toward denser AI networking already visible in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/nvidia-and-cisco-plan-12-8-tb-s-optics-for-ai-networks/"><strong>400G-per-lane optics</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/celestica-ds6005-102-4tbps-co-packaged-optics-ai-switch/"><strong>102.4 Tb/s co-packaged-optics switches</strong></a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/broadcom-ocp-2026-tomahawk6-102-4t-800g-nic-cpo-ai-networking/"><strong>Broadcom’s 102.4T networking roadmap</strong></a>. The agreement itself does not disclose the investment amount or the exact volume of new wafer capacity.</p>
