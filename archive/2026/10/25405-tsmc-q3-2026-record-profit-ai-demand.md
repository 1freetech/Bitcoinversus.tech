## Final Gutenberg Source

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>TSMC reported a record third-quarter profit on October 15, 2026 as demand for advanced chips continued to accelerate. The company’s <a href="https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q3">official Q3 results</a> show revenue of NT$1.49424 trillion (US$46.72 billion) and net income of NT$763.06 billion. Revenue rose 50.9% year over year, while net income and diluted earnings per share increased 68.7%.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The manufacturing mix also shows how quickly leading-edge production is moving. TSMC said 2-nanometer chips accounted for 8% of quarterly wafer revenue, 3nm for 33%, 5nm for 28% and 7nm for 10%. Combined, 7nm-and-below technologies represented 79% of wafer revenue. That adds important detail to BitcoinVersus.Tech’s earlier report on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/semiconductors-tsmc-hits-46-71b-record-quarter-as-ai-chip-demand-surges/">TSMC’s September revenue surge</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
