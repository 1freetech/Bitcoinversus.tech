<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>TSMC reported a record third-quarter profit on October 15, 2026 as demand for advanced chips continued to accelerate. The company’s <a href="https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q3">official Q3 results</a> show revenue of NT$1.49424 trillion (US$46.72 billion) and net income of NT$763.06 billion. Revenue rose 50.9% year over year, while net income and diluted earnings per share increased 68.7%.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The manufacturing mix also shows how quickly leading-edge production is moving. TSMC said 2-nanometer chips accounted for 8% of quarterly wafer revenue, 3nm for 33%, 5nm for 28% and 7nm for 10%. Combined, 7nm-and-below technologies represented 79% of wafer revenue. That adds important detail to BitcoinVersus.Tech’s earlier report on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/semiconductors-tsmc-hits-46-71b-record-quarter-as-ai-chip-demand-surges/">TSMC’s September revenue surge</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>TSMC guided fourth-quarter revenue to US$52.3 billion–US$54.3 billion, with gross margin expected at 65%–67%. <a href="https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/tsmc-q3-profit-jumps-69-record-beating-expectations-2026-10-15/">Reuters reported</a> that the result was TSMC’s sixth consecutive record quarterly profit, while also noting management’s more cautious stance heading into 2027.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The numbers reinforce the infrastructure pressure already appearing elsewhere in the chip supply chain. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/asml-ai-demand-outrunning-supply/">ASML’s warning that AI demand is outrunning supply</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/globalfoundries-two-new-york-chip-fabs-japan/">GlobalFoundries’ planned New York fab expansion</a>. TSMC’s quarter does not prove that every AI investment will pay off, but it is strong evidence that demand for advanced foundry capacity remains exceptionally high.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
