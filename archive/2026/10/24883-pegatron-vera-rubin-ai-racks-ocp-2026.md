<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pegatron is using the 2026 Open Compute Project Global Summit to show how next-generation AI hardware moves from a reference design into a production-ready rack.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pegatron-brings-ai-infrastructure-from-design-to-deployment-at-ocp-global-summit-2026-302908444.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">October 14 announcement</a>, Pegatron said its OCP lineup now spans rack-scale AI, compute and storage systems built around ORv3 busbars, DC-MHS modular hardware, OCP 3.0 network adapters and liquid cooling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The centerpiece is the <strong>RA4803-72N3</strong>, a fully liquid-cooled NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 rack with <strong>72 Rubin GPUs</strong>, <strong>36 Vera CPUs</strong> and sixth-generation NVLink. Pegatron is also showing the AS210-2T1-8H3, a 2U Rubin NVL8 system, plus rack configurations with up to 64 or 72 GPUs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The important part is not only the GPU count. Pegatron says its manufacturing workflow connects digital-twin simulation, automated production, thermal validation and rack-level run-in testing before deployment. That follows the same industry shift BitcoinVersus.Tech has covered in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/msi-open-ai-infrastructure-ocp-2026/"><strong>MSI’s open AI infrastructure</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/xfusion-800v-power-liquid-cooling-ocp/"><strong>xFusion’s power and cooling work</strong></a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/wiwynn-ayar-guc-live-cpo-ai/"><strong>Wiwynn’s live optical compute system</strong></a>.</p>
