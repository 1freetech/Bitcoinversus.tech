<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hood Warfare 2</strong> has turned into one of Steam’s strangest breakout shooters of the week. Trapstar Games released the Chicago-set multiplayer FPS on October 8, and its player count has accelerated far beyond the roughly 4,400-concurrent-player snapshot circulating earlier this week.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As of October 14, a <a href="https://ragequit.gg/steam-charts/hood-warfare-2/">live Steam tracker</a> recorded an all-time peak of 15,258 concurrent players. The <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/5155360/Hood_Warfare_2/">Steam store</a> currently lists the game at $5.39 during its introductory discount, with 79% of more than 1,100 user reviews positive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The comparison players keep making is Call of Duty, but that is community shorthand rather than an official connection. Hood Warfare 2 uses familiar multiplayer-FPS ideas—custom loadouts, perks, killstreaks and objective modes—then reframes them around fictional gang warfare. Modes include Turf War, GangBang, Chaos, Hit A Lick, Destroy The Block and Chain Snatch.</p>

<p>The game is built in Unreal Engine 5 and includes weapon attachments such as sights, magazines, grips and suppressors. Its sudden rise fits a broader Steam environment where inexpensive games can spread extremely quickly through clips and social posts.
