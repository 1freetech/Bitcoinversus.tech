<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Strategy Executive Chairman Michael Saylor says AI agents will force finance to become as programmable and always-on as the software using it. In a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sSKZelY0Yc">Freedom Tech DC discussion with Bitcoin Policy Institute Executive Director Conner Brown</a>, Saylor argued that AI will automate jobs and business models while making new products easier to build.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>His policy answer is cheaper capital formation, more digital-dollar competition, and stronger rights to custody and transfer digital assets. His clearest line was that <strong>“freedom starts with the bill of rights.”</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin sits at the center of that framework. He said, <strong>“Bitcoin is the greatest of the digital commodities,”</strong> and argued banks should be able to custody BTC and extend credit against it under workable rules. That fits a policy arc BitcoinVersus.Tech has tracked from the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/08/michael-saylor-attends-white-house-digital-assets-summit/">White House digital-assets summit</a> to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/06/microstrategy-plans-to-become-the-first-bitcoin-bank/">Strategy’s Bitcoin-focused financial ambitions</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The most forward-looking section concerns autonomous software. AI agents can research, negotiate and buy continuously, while traditional finance still relies on human identities, business hours and manual approvals. <strong>“The banks won't give them a bank account,”</strong> Saylor said. He expects agents to favor digital wallets, programmable payments and digitally native assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That idea is becoming less theoretical. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/shopify-opens-checkout-to-browser-based-ai-agents/">Shopify opening checkout to browser-based AI agents</a>. Saylor’s argument goes further: digital intelligence, digital money and digital capital could converge into one economic stack.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>In a <a href="https://www.michael.com/articles/prescriptions-for-prosperity-in-the-digital-economy">follow-up policy paper</a>, Saylor urged regulators to protect rights to create, issue, custody, transfer and use digital assets. His market-growth projections remain forecasts, not guarantees.</p>
