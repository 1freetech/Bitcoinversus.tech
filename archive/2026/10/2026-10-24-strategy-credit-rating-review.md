<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Strategy, the Bitcoin treasury company led by Executive Chairman Michael Saylor, remains rated <strong>B- with a stable outlook</strong> by S&amp;P Global Ratings, according to an October 16, 2026 rating affirmation reported October 19. The assessment comes as Strategy expands its dollar liquidity and Bitcoin-backed digital credit products. The X trending discussion refers to a credit review, but the accessible evidence does not establish a separate formal “four-month review.”</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2>Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul><li>S&amp;P’s B- issuer rating remains below investment grade.</li><li>Strategy reports 848,517 BTC as of October 18, 2026.</li><li>Its latest dashboard lists a $4.833 billion USD Reserve and $447 million of additional USD Cash.</li><li>Strategy’s proprietary BTC Credit metric is not an S&amp;P credit rating.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2>Liquidity and Debt Under Review</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Strategy’s <a href="https://www.strategy.com/">official dashboard</a> lists $6.714 billion of debt and $13.557 billion of preferred securities, alongside its cash resources. Its October 19 update also expanded the authorized digital credit securities repurchase program to $3 billion. The larger authorization is a maximum program size, not proof that the entire amount has been spent.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Liquidity matters because interest and preferred dividends generally require dollar payments even though Bitcoin dominates Strategy’s treasury assets. The company says its USD Reserve is earmarked for those payments. The <a href="https://www.strategy.com/notes">official methodology notes</a> caution that internally calculated duration and Bitcoin credit measures are illustrative, not guarantees of solvency.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2>A Different Credit Calculation</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>On October 19, Saylor reported <strong>3.4 years of USD Duration</strong> and <strong>53 basis points of STRC BTC Credit</strong>, assuming 10% annual Bitcoin appreciation, 40% volatility and a Bitcoin reference price of $81,468. Those assumptions can materially change the model output. They are separate from S&amp;P’s independent B- rating. See <a href="https://www.strategy.com/credit/pricing">Strategy’s credit-pricing methodology and disclaimers</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The company has also described its Bitcoin and digital-credit approach in <a href="https://www.michael.com/articles/how-strategy-engineers-digital-credit">Saylor’s September 29 essay</a>. For related coverage, read our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/16/michael-saylor-bitcoin-digital-credit-xapo-summit/">Xapo Summit digital-credit report</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/23/michael-saylor-strategy-bitcoin-daily-dividends-ai-interview/">October interview recap</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2>What Investors Should Watch</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The important questions are whether Strategy maintains liquidity, manages its debt and preferred-dividend obligations, and retains access to capital markets during Bitcoin price declines. Its next scheduled quarterly results on October 29 could provide additional information. A future S&amp;P upgrade is possible but has not been confirmed.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
