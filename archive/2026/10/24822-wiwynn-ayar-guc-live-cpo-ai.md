<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Wiwynn, Ayar Labs and Global Unichip Corp. are showing a live co-packaged-optics system at the 2026 Open Compute Project Global Summit, moving the technology from architecture diagrams into a working compute platform.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://www.wiwynn.com/news/wiwynn-ayar-labs-and-guc-advance-co-packaged-optics-with-live-demonstration-of-integrated-optical-compute-system-at-ocp-global-summit-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>October 13 announcement</strong></a>, Wiwynn said the system integrates a GUC ASIC with Ayar Labs’ TeraPHY optical engines and SuperNova external light source. The demo streams health telemetry from optical engines inside the multi-chip package while the tuning system tracks thermal and wavelength drift.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to the companies, eye height, optical modulation amplitude and extinction ratio remain stable while the system compensates for temperature changes. The ORv3-ready design also includes detachable fiber-array units and in-chassis fiber routing, features aimed at making optical compute systems easier to manufacture and service.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ayar Labs’ <a href="https://ayarlabs.com/products/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>CPO platform</strong></a> places optical I/O directly beside compute silicon so large GPU clusters can move data without relying entirely on long high-speed copper links. BitcoinVersus.Tech has also covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/eliyan-12-8t-optical-chiplet-platform/"><strong>Eliyan’s 12.8T optical chiplet platform</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/salience-labs-125m-ai-optical-switches/"><strong>Salience Labs’ optical-switch expansion</strong></a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/keysight-fits-16ch-1-6t-link-testing/"><strong>1.6T interconnect testing</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The OCP demonstration is not a production benchmark, but it is a meaningful integration milestone: the companies are showing that optical engines, light sources, telemetry, cooling and serviceability can operate together inside a real compute system.</p>
