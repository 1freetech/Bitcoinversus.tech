<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>NBA Commissioner Adam Silver says the league could begin using a chip-equipped “smart ball” for officiating as soon as 2027.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Silver told <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/11/commissioner-adam-silver-nba-could-launch-smart-ball-tech-next-year.html">CNBC</a> that the NBA is working with Wilson on a basketball containing a Bluetooth sensor that weighs about <strong>1 gram</strong>. The sensor can track movement, spin and changes in trajectory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The key update is timing. BitcoinVersus.Tech <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/sports-nba-smart-basketball-referee-wrist-wearables/">reported the NBA’s preseason smart-ball tests on October 5</a>, when the league said the data was being collected only for research. Silver now says the technology could move into actual officiating next year.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><em>NBA fans are already debating the smart-ball proposal on Reddit.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Silver said the league has tested chipped and standard balls in the G League, Summer League and preseason games, and players could not tell which ball contained the sensor. The <a href="https://apnews.com/article/d6fa30f7f8db2abeb2e092c884c3aad2">Associated Press</a> previously reported that Wilson’s connected ball was being evaluated for possible future real-time officiating use.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One use case is out-of-bounds reviews: a sudden change in spin could help determine whether a player touched the ball. Silver also described consumer versions that could analyze shooting angle and rotation. That idea resembles other sensor-heavy sports systems BitcoinVersus.Tech has covered, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/01/sports-tdks-smart-javelin-turns-every-throw-into-real-time-sensor-data/">TDK’s smart javelin</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/sports-ibm-us-open-serve-quality-limb-tracking-ai/">IBM’s US Open tracking tools</a>.</p>
