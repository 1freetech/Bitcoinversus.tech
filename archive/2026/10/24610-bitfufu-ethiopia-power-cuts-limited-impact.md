<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitFuFu says the impact from Ethiopia’s power curtailment is now largely reflected in its operating results.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In its <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/fr/news-release/2026/10/13/3380213/0/en/bitfufu-announces-september-2026-bitcoin-production-and-operational-updates.html">October 13 operating update</a>, the miner reported 167 BTC produced in September, down 4.0% from 174 BTC in August. Total hashrate under management fell from 20.6 EH/s to 19.4 EH/s, while managed power capacity declined from 344 MW to 324 MW.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[tweet https://twitter.com/AfricaFirsts/status/2015069721465671828]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Ethiopia is still expanding its hydroelectric system, including the Koysha project. That broader grid build-out matters because Bitcoin mining there remains highly dependent on hydropower availability.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The more interesting number is self-mining. BitFuFu produced 98 BTC from its own machines, up from 88 BTC in August, even as cloud-mining production dropped to 69 BTC from 86 BTC. Daily production stayed flat at 5.6 BTC, fleet efficiency remained 16.7 J/TH, and holdings rose to 1,388 BTC.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->
[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqie7bWQHPk]
<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Al Jazeera’s report from Ethiopia explains why abundant hydroelectric power attracted Bitcoin miners to the country in the first place.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>BitcoinVersus.Tech <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/27/ethiopia-cuts-bitcoin-miners-23-percent-contracted-power/">reported in September</a> that Ethiopia had cut miners to 23% of contracted capacity amid drought pressure. BitFuFu now says the affected Ethiopian investment is a limited part of its asset base and expects only minimal additional impact. That update follows our earlier coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/ethiopian-electric-power-generates-over-25-million-from-data-mining-companies/">mining revenue for Ethiopian Electric Power</a> and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/04/26/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia-2/">new mining facility build-out in Ethiopia</a>. A <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/shoreafrica_ethiopia-has-slashed-power-supplies-to-bitcoin-activity-7505584518247038976-9X07">September grid report</a> provides additional context on the curtailment.</p>
