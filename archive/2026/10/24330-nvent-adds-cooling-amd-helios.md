<p><strong>nVent and AMD are adding a rack-level cooling option to the AMD Helios AI platform.</strong> The companies said October 13 that nVent will integrate a DC-powered rear-door heat exchanger with the double-wide Helios rack and show the design at the OCP Global Summit in San Jose.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/13/3379630/0/en/nvent-and-amd-collaborate-on-cooling-for-amd-helios-rackscale-solution.html"><strong>nVent’s announcement</strong></a>, the dual rear door is designed to remove heat that remains after direct-to-chip liquid cooling. nVent says the unit can return cooled air to the room while using temperature, humidity and differential-pressure sensors to control fan speed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>AMD explains how Helios is designed as rack-scale infrastructure rather than a collection of separate servers.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.amd.com/en/products/rackscale-solutions/helios.html"><strong>AMD lists Helios</strong></a> as a 72-GPU rackscale reference design using Instinct MI455X accelerators, EPYC “Venice” CPUs and Pensando networking. The new cooling option extends an ecosystem already focused on dense, open rack infrastructure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The announcement follows broader efforts to cool denser AI racks. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/13/study-liquid-cooling-cuts-pue-17-vs-air-cooling/"><strong>study finding lower PUE with liquid cooling</strong></a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/wiwynn-tests-diamond-cooling-ai-chips/"><strong>Wiwynn’s diamond-cooling work</strong></a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/09/amd-pushes-for-more-samsung-hbm4-as-mi455x-and-helios-ramp/"><strong>the MI455X and Helios ramp</strong></a>. The nVent release does not disclose pricing or a deployment date.</p>
