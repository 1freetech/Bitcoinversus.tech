<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>“Bitcoin is, very simply, separation of money and state.”</p><cite>Alex Gladstein</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Alex Gladstein gave this line during a 2023 episode of <a href="https://www.thegreatsimplification.com/episode/72-alex-gladstein">The Great Simplification</a>. The official transcript places it at about <strong>1:25:17</strong>, during a discussion about digital cash, privacy and peer-to-peer payments. The original video is embedded below.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gladstein’s phrase is a political and monetary argument, not a claim that Bitcoin removes governments from economic life. Taxes, regulation and legal systems still exist around Bitcoin. His point is narrower: Bitcoin’s issuance rules and transaction network are not operated by a central bank, and users can transact directly over an open protocol.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That framing connects with earlier BitcoinVersus coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/bitcoin-is-freedom-money-bitcoin-verse-of-the-day/">Bitcoin as freedom money</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/report-by-nydig-claims-bitcoin-is-technically-freedom-of-speech-protected-by-1st-amendment/">Bitcoin and freedom of speech</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/global-bitcoin-adoption-machankura-enables-bitcoin-transactions-without-internet/">Bitcoin payments in Africa through Machankura</a>. Gladstein has continued making the broader human-rights case for Bitcoin through the <a href="https://hrf.org/latest/why-bitcoin-is-freedom-money-hrf-in-the-journal-of-democracy/">Human Rights Foundation</a>.</p>
