<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Open Compute Project says AI data-center power density is now on a path to reach and eventually exceed <strong>1 MW in a single rack</strong>, pushing the industry toward new power, cooling and grid designs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In an <a href="https://www.opencompute.org/news/realizing-the-ocp-open-data-center-ecosystem-vision/">October 12 update</a> published as the 2026 OCP Global Summit opened in San Jose, the foundation said its community has established a “clear path” to 1 MW racks while developing standards that extend from the chip to the utility grid. OCP's roadmap includes <strong>800 VDC</strong> distribution today and an eventual evolution toward <strong>1,500 VDC</strong>, alongside solid-state transformers, energy-storage guidance and specifications for how hyperscale facilities should ride through grid disturbances.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The direction is already visible in hardware. BitcoinVersus.Tech recently covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/liteon-1mw-800v-power-rack-ocp-2026/">LITEON's 1 MW 800 V power rack</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/delta-800-vdc-3mw-cooling-ocp-2026/">Delta's 800 VDC and 3 MW cooling systems</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/rittal-bosch-800v-power-ai-racks/">Rittal and Bosch's 800 V AI-rack power work</a>. Those systems show how rack power, liquid cooling and facility electrical design are increasingly being engineered as one system rather than separate layers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>OCP also cited a new IDC impact study forecasting that more than <strong>$890 billion</strong> of IT equipment based on OCP-recognized designs could change hands in 2030, with OCP penetration exceeding 50% of the applicable market. That is a forecast, not booked revenue, and the 1 MW figure is a technology roadmap rather than a claim that typical racks operate at that level today.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A separate <a href="https://www.opencompute.org/news/explore-22-tracks-at-the-2026-ocp-global-summit/">OCP summit overview</a> shows how central the issue has become: its Rack &amp; Power track specifically covers 800 VDC, direct-to-server power, 1 MW+ racks, HVDC grounding, backup technologies and AI power-oscillation filtering.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The larger shift is architectural. As rack density climbs, power conversion, cooling, networking, physical rack design and grid interaction increasingly have to be co-designed. OCP's newest roadmap suggests the industry is moving from optimizing individual servers toward engineering the entire data center as one connected electrical and thermal system.</p>
