<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>SpaceX Super Intelligence</strong> announced a new unified subscription in an <a href="https://twitter.com/SpaceXSI/status/2109783335296008414">X post on October 12, 2026</a>, bringing Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and paid X features under one plan.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The company’s <a href="https://x.ai/news/x-subscription">official announcement</a> says The X Subscription has a free tier plus paid plans at $8, $30, $100 and $200 per month. Grok and Cursor draw from a shared usage pool, while third-party models inside Cursor are billed separately on eligible tiers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The bundle connects several trends BitcoinVersus.Tech has been tracking: <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/03/xai-team-bots-shared-ai-coworker-whole-teams/">Grok becoming a shared AI coworker</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/gaming-unity-grok-build-30-first-party-engine-skills/">Grok Build moving deeper into development workflows</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/coding-google-ai-assistants-live-official-docs-developer-knowledge-api-mcp/">AI assistants gaining direct access to developer tooling and documentation</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Cursor’s own <a href="https://cursor.com/help/the-x-subscription/overview">support documentation</a> independently confirms the shared Grok-and-Cursor usage model and says switching is optional for existing customers. Current subscribers can keep their present plans unless they choose to move.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What remains to be seen is whether one combined subscription changes how users split their time between social media, general-purpose AI and coding agents. The confirmed change today is simpler: tools that previously lived behind separate subscriptions can now be purchased as one package.</p>
