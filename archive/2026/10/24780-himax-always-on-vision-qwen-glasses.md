<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Himax’s WiseEye AI processor is now powering always-on vision in Alibaba’s latest Qwen Glasses N1 series.</strong> Himax said October 14 that its HX6538 chip lets the glasses continuously sense their surroundings while keeping the main host processor asleep until it is actually needed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/14/3380540/8267/en/himax-wiseeye-ultralow-power-ai-adopted-in-alibaba-s-latest-qwen-glasses.html"><strong>Himax’s announcement</strong></a>, the system combines on-device AI recognition with tiered visual wake-up. That matters for lightweight glasses because constant camera and AI processing can drain a small battery quickly. Moving basic perception onto the low-power WiseEye chip lets the glasses stay aware without running the full application processor all the time.</p>
