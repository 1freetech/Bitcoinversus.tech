<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Bitcoin Verse of the Day — October 14, 2026</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>“Bitcoin is a shining city in cyberspace, and you can go there once you understand it.”</p><cite>Michael Saylor</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This verse works because it turns a technical network into a simple image: a place people can choose to enter once they understand what the system offers. Saylor was speaking about cryptography, decentralization and the ability to hold value outside a single company, bank or government.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The quote comes directly from Saylor’s <a href="https://www.atlassociety.org/fr/post/gala-2022-michael-saylor-keynote-speech">2022 Atlas Society keynote</a>. A time-coded transcript at <a href="https://saylorcorpus.hopium.studio/transcript/ZcjFrIMw2sI/">SaylorCorpus</a> places the line at about <strong>11:14</strong>. The original Atlas Society video is embedded below so the verse can be heard in its full context.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The metaphor also connects with earlier BitcoinVersus work on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/04/04/god-in-cyberspace-the-political-history-behind-the-christian-reformation-and-the-bitcoin-revolution-2/">Bitcoin and cyberspace</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/report-by-nydig-claims-bitcoin-is-technically-freedom-of-speech-protected-by-1st-amendment/">Bitcoin and freedom of speech</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/24/the-21-laws-of-bitcoin-an-intellectual-guide-to-understanding-bitcoin/">The 21 Laws of Bitcoin</a>. The common thread is voluntary participation in an open monetary network rather than the price of bitcoin on any single day.</p>
