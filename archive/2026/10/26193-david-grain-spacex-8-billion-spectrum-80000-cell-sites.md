<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Billionaire entrepreneur <strong>David Grain</strong>, founder of <strong>Grain Management</strong>, is at the center of SpaceX’s next wireless-network expansion. His firm agreed on October 8 to sell its nationwide 800 MHz spectrum portfolio to SpaceX in a transaction <a href="https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/spacex-to-acquire-spectrum-that-enables-starlink-mobile-services-4939769">reported at approximately $8 billion</a>. The companies have not disclosed the price, and the purchase still requires federal approval.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why David Grain's Spectrum Business Matters</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Grain founded the communications-infrastructure investment firm in 2007. In August 2026, it acquired the 800 MHz licenses from T-Mobile through an exchange involving cash and 600 MHz spectrum. The October 8 <a href="https://graingp.com/grain-management-announces-definitive-agreement-to-sell-nationwide-800-mhz-spectrum-portfolio-to-spacex/">Grain Management announcement</a> confirmed an agreement to transfer the entire nationwide portfolio to SpaceX, subject to FCC approval and other closing conditions.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->[tweet https://twitter.com/SpaceX/status/2108291133025698301]<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>SpaceX’s original announcement outlines its ambition to become a major U.S. mobile carrier; it does not disclose the purchase price.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Spectrum and the Towers Are Different Pieces</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The portfolio includes up to <strong>14 MHz of paired 800 MHz spectrum</strong>, a low-frequency coverage layer that generally travels farther and penetrates obstacles more effectively than higher bands. SpaceX wants to combine terrestrial service with satellite direct-to-device connections. Our earlier report explains <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/starlink-mobile-800-mhz-spectrum-indoor-phone-coverage/">what the 800 MHz licenses could mean for indoor coverage</a>. Reliable satellite-to-phone performance inside buildings remains a difficult engineering task.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->[youtube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8zS2rU-URo]<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><em>T-Mobile and SpaceX’s original direct-to-cell presentation provides historical context for the satellite-to-phone approach, not confirmation of the new spectrum deal.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Reported Plan for 80,000 Cell Sites</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The latest development came on <strong>October 22</strong>, when <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-seeks-access-80000-us-cell-sites-wireless-network-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-10-22/">Reuters, citing Bloomberg</a>, reported that SpaceX was seeking access to at least <strong>80,000 U.S. cell sites</strong>. Crown Castle was identified as a potential infrastructure partner. These are reported negotiations—not 80,000 leased or constructed sites. Towers, radios, backhaul, permits and network integration would require considerable investment.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:shortcode -->[tweet https://twitter.com/elonmusk/status/2108296168094544099]<!-- /wp:shortcode -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the $8 Billion Does Not Mean</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The reported purchase price is a value for <strong>Grain Management’s spectrum assets</strong>, not David Grain’s personal payout or the transaction’s net profit. Investors, acquisition costs and other deal terms matter. BitcoinVersus.Tech previously covered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/starlink-major-us-phone-carrier/">Starlink’s wider wireless-carrier ambitions</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/02/starlink-systems-deployed-to-restore-connectivity-in-flood-stricken-poland/">Starlink’s emergency-connectivity role</a>. The next checkpoints are FCC approval and actual infrastructure agreements.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list --><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Grain Management agreed to sell its nationwide 800 MHz licenses to SpaceX; the reported $8 billion price is unconfirmed by the parties.</li><li>SpaceX is reportedly seeking access to at least 80,000 U.S. cell sites, but leases have not been announced.</li><li>Regulatory clearance and network buildout remain essential steps before the proposed mobile strategy is realized.</li></ul><!-- /wp:list -->
