<p><strong>Bitcoin Verse of the Day — October 14, 2026</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>“Cryptocurrencies are the first step to a world with a global programmable money.”</p><cite>Neha Narula</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Neha Narula delivered this line in her 2016 TED talk, <em>The Future of Money</em>. In the original recording, the sentence begins at about <strong>11:58</strong>. Seconds later, she explains the idea in practical terms: programmable money could let someone pay another person securely without signing up, asking permission, converting currencies or worrying about funds getting stuck.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.ted.com/talks/neha_narula_the_future_of_money">TED archive</a> identifies the talk as a May 2016 TED@BCG Paris presentation. <a href="https://www-prod.media.mit.edu/articles/the-future-of-money/">MIT Media Lab</a> also archived the talk through its Digital Currency Initiative. A time-coded transcript places the verse at <a href="https://www.english-video.net/v/en/2554">11:58</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For Bitcoin, the verse points to a core idea: money can operate as software on an open network rather than only as an entry inside a bank’s system. That connects directly with BitcoinVersus coverage of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/11/02/bitcoin-whitepaper-anniversary/">Bitcoin whitepaper</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/18/bitcoins-beginning-satoshi-nakamotos-legendary-letter-to-wei-dei/">Satoshi Nakamoto’s letter to Wei Dai</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/24/the-21-laws-of-bitcoin-an-intellectual-guide-to-understanding-bitcoin/">The 21 Laws of Bitcoin</a>.</p>
