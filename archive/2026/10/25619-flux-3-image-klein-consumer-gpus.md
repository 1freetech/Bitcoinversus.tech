<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Black Forest Labs released <strong>FLUX 3 Image Klein</strong> on October 15, 2026, opening the weights for a 5.6-billion-parameter model built for image generation, editing and multi-reference composition. The <a href="https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein">official release</a> says Klein supports native 4K output, bounding-box layout control and as many as 10 reference images in one generation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Klein ships as a base checkpoint for fine-tuning and research plus a distilled 16-step version for lower-latency inference. Black Forest Labs also provides FP8, INT8 and NVFP4 variants. <a href="https://venturebeat.com/technology/black-forest-labs-opens-flux-3-image-klein-weights-with-free-commercial-use-limited-to-smaller-businesses">VentureBeat reports</a> that the INT8 build can run on systems with 8 GB of GPU memory, putting a capable modern image model within reach of many consumer PCs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The release extends the same FLUX 3 family covered in our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/flux-3-video-cuts-2k-4k-prices-half/">FLUX 3 Video pricing update</a>. It also fits the broader move toward local inference and lower deployment costs discussed in our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/08/10/model-routing/">model-routing explainer</a> and our coverage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/nvidia-europe-ai-science-tools-compute/">NVIDIA opening more AI tools to European researchers</a>.</p>

<p>The important distinction is that “open weights” is not the same as unrestricted open-source software. Klein is released under the FLUX Kommunity License, with separate terms for larger commercial deployments. For developers, however, the technical shift is clear: high-resolution generation, precise editing and reference-driven composition are moving onto smaller hardware footprints.</p>
