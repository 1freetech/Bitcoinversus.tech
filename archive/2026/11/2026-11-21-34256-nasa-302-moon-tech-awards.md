# NASA Details 302 Moon-Tech Projects Worth Up to $171 Million

## Primary source chronology and deduplication
- NASA November 20, 2026 portfolio recap: https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/rtmd/new-nasa-seed-funding-primes-small-businesses-for-innovation/

- NASA Phase II 98 project selection announcement, July 21, 2026: https://www.nasa.gov/sbir_sttr/phase-ii/
- 51 + 121 + 32 Phase I selections = 204; 204 + 98 Phase II selections = 302. No implication that every selection was announced November 20. NASA ceiling up to $171M, NOT actual expenditure.
- Differentiated from earlier BitcoinVersus Moon Base stories on NASA power towers, solid-state batteries, communications and CLPS framework. Site searches for “NASA seed funding” and “SBIR” found no duplicate article about the 302-project collection.

## Science and engineering
- Opterus Research and Development phase II rollable photovoltaic arrays could scale toward 50 kW. Design target, not verified lunar electrical production.
- Intelligent Fiber Optic Systems Corp phase I 10-foot arm combining visual, tactile, temperature and force sensing.
- Elementium Materials phase I design goal to operate at minus 76°F / minus 60°C without active heating; not a deployed battery, and no published site-level savings.
- Slate Geotechnical Consultants and Colorado School of Mines STTR Phase I investigations of two reusable lunar-regolith landing pad preparation approaches; methods under evaluation.
- Funding stages separate feasibility from prototype demonstration. Neither alone implies flight qualification.
