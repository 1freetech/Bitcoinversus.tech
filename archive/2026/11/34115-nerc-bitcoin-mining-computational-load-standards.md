# Bitcoin Mines Face New US Grid Reliability Standards

Final post-publication Gutenberg source is preserved [here](./34115-nerc-bitcoin-mining-computational-load-standards.gutenberg.html). WordPress hosts the original featured cover and donation QR.

## Verification record

- NERC petition submitted to FERC on November 16, 2026; APPA clause summary published November 17, 2026.
- CLO-001-1 addresses interconnection, studies and modeling; CLO-002-1 addresses operational data, communications and operating instructions; CLO-003-1 addresses protection coordination and disturbance monitoring.
- Proposed facility scope: at least 50 MW connected computational load and at least 100 kV supply equipment connection. These are proposed thresholds, not blanket requirements for all mining sites.
- Rules awaiting FERC approval at reporting date; November submission follows October 21 registry petition.
- NERC reviewed 26 significant cryptocurrency mining load-reduction events from January 2023 through September 2025. Reviewed events included loads dropping 17% to 95% relative to their pre-disturbance demand.
- PJM reported nearly 4,000 MW of data-center demand disconnected during July 22, 2026 disturbance; not documented as Bitcoin mining sites.
