# Opera Picks Stripe’s Privy for 20 Million MiniPay Wallets

Final editorial source: [Gutenberg HTML](./33988-opera-minipay-stripe-privy.gutenberg.html). Source was read from published WordPress post **33988** and archived byte-for-byte.

## Verified event and distinctions

- Opera/MiniPay announced **November 19, 2026** that Privy, acquired by Stripe in **2025**, would underpin MiniPay wallet authentication and key management.
- Global rollout **will start during 2026**. No source confirms migration of all existing activated wallets had completed at announcement.
- Opera reports **more than 20 million cumulative wallet activations** and **more than 600 million lifetime transactions**. Neither is a monthly active user count; cumulative activations do not establish unique humans.
- Privy supports email, social identity, passkey and wallet sign-in, shared balance/history across supported platforms, and exportable wallet keys. Preserving export rights does not eliminate identity and recovery risks.
- Link by Stripe **deposits** were announced **November 12, 2026** for eligible US/EU users. Link top-ups and the Privy wallet migration are separate products and launch stages.
- Third-party financial services are available through partners. MiniPay itself reports it is a self-custodial wallet and does not directly provide exchange or remittance services.
