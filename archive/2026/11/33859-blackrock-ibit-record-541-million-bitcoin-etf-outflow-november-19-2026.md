# BlackRock Bitcoin ETF Hits Record $541 Million Daily Outflow

*Verbatim published WordPress Gutenberg body with canonical volunteer footer, original 5,000-satoshi donation QR, referenced video embeds and data sources.*

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF recorded its <strong>largest single-day net withdrawal</strong> on Thursday, November 19, 2026. <a href="https://farside.co.uk/btc/">Farside Investors</a> reported <strong>$541.1 million leaving IBIT</strong>, while <a href="https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/61789">SoSoValue's more precise figure was $541.08 million</a>. The withdrawal exceeded the fund's prior $528.3 million daily record from January 30, according to <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/markets/blackrock-ibit-record-single-day-outflow">contemporary reporting</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Across all tracked U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs, net redemptions totaled approximately <strong>$582.3 million</strong>. BlackRock's product accounted for about <strong>92.9%</strong> of the total. The figures show investor withdrawals from a fund sponsored by BlackRock, not evidence that the asset manager liquidated $541 million from a proprietary corporate Bitcoin treasury.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A third consecutive outflow day</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>IBIT recorded $76.5 million in net inflows on Monday, November 16, before reversing to $209.5 million of outflows Tuesday and $148.7 million Wednesday. Thursday's record brought the <strong>three-session withdrawal total to $899.3 million</strong>, based on the sum of the three negative daily flow figures. One positive session did not reverse the broader selling trend.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Other funds did not all move together. On November 19, Morgan Stanley's MSBT took in $49.6 million, while Fidelity's FBTC lost $45.9 million, ARK 21Shares' ARKB lost $31.3 million and Grayscale's GBTC lost $13.9 million. The inflow into MSBT partly offset withdrawals elsewhere. Farside's historical table shows a larger single-fund negative day for Grayscale, so the new record applies to <strong>IBIT specifically</strong>, not every Bitcoin ETF in history.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why an ETF outflow is not BlackRock selling its own Bitcoin</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Shareholders can sell IBIT shares on an exchange, but ordinary share trades between investors do not automatically require the trust to dispose of coins. Authorized Participants can create or redeem large share baskets when fund demand changes. Under the <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1980994/000143774926006058/bit20251231_10k.htm">trust's SEC-filed operating rules</a>, redemptions may involve Bitcoin sales or deliveries arranged through trading counterparties to settle fund obligations.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The distinction is important because BlackRock sponsors and administers the product on behalf of investors. Its role differs from a corporation selling its own Bitcoin holdings to fund operations. The daily fund-flow statistic does not disclose each final Bitcoin transaction, wallet transfer, execution price or investor identity. Our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/10/blackrock-bitcoin-self-custody-ibit-swaps-record-shares-fall-november-2026/">report on IBIT custody conversions</a> explained why large in-kind exchanges and falling shares outstanding can occur together.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the record means for Bitcoin</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Fund outflows can reflect selling pressure, portfolio rebalancing, hedges or institutional liquidity decisions. The flow table alone cannot establish what caused the withdrawals or prove that a particular Bitcoin price movement was driven by ETF redemptions. Other sources of supply and demand, including spot exchanges, derivatives, miners and long-term holders, remain important.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>For comparison, our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/07/bitcoin-etfs-lose-1-28-billion-week-blackrock-ibit-outflows-november-2026/">November 6 weekly ETF analysis</a> documented $1.28 billion of sector withdrawals. A separate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/05/bitcoin-etfs-gain-74-6-million-as-fidelity-leads-blackrock/">November 4 inflow day</a> showed investors continuing to favor some competing funds. Our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/03/bitcoin-etfs-lose-1-04-billion-two-sessions-november-2026/">two-session withdrawal analysis</a> provides additional context for the longer month.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The next trading sessions will determine whether the record becomes part of a sustained redemption streak or a short-lived imbalance. The verified development is a new IBIT withdrawal record, not a declaration that BlackRock has abandoned Bitcoin.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
