# BitFuFu Revenue Falls 79% Even as Its Own Bitcoin Mining Grows

The [exact Gutenberg editor source](./34029-bitfufu-q3-revenue-self-mining.gutenberg.html) is archived byte-for-byte from **published** WordPress post #34029.

## Primary evidence and dates

- November 20, 2026 at 06:30 ET, BitFuFu disclosed unaudited Q3 2026 results for quarter ended September 30, 2026.
- SEC Exhibit 99.1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1921158/000121390026122400/ea031259601ex99-1.htm
- Company-issued release: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/20/3407337/0/en/bitfufu-reports-unaudited-third-quarter-2026-financial-results.html
- Q3 2026 total revenue $37.7M versus Q3 2025 $180.7M, reported decline 79.1%.
- Revenue segments Q3 2025 → Q3 2026, USD million: cloud mining 122.9 → 13.8; own BTC mining 20.1 → 18.4; equipment sales 35.8 → 2.0; hosting and other 1.9 → 3.5. Segment sum matches disclosed consolidated totals.
- Own-mined BTC Q3 2025 174, Q3 2026 258, growth (258/174−1) = 48.2759%, rounded +48.3%. Customers mining on rented cloud capacity produced 1,033 BTC in Q3 2025 and 195 in Q3 2026; customer BTC not company self-mined BTC.
- Managed hashrate year-end-quarter snapshots September 30, 2025 36.0 EH/s and September 30, 2026 19.4 EH/s, a 46.1% decline. Manufacturer-specification total includes temporarily offline energized machines and is not a delivered mining-pool work measurement.
- Installed/secured power capacity declined 624 → 324 MW. Average hashrate allocated to self-mining grew 50.9% per company statement.
- Average BTC price in the quarter dropped from about $114,500 (Q3 2025) to $71,000 (Q3 2026), per the company.
- Q3 2026 net income was $24.9M but included a $36.5M fair-value gain related to digital assets. Do not characterize accounting gains as Bitcoin mining cash earnings.
- $2.05M provision attributed to prepaid Ethiopia mining-site acquisition uncertainty. No inference of closure or final transaction settlement.
- October 2026 company update: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1921158/000121390026116602/ea031104401ex99-1.htm
