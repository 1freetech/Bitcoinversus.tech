# ESA Awards Over €102 Million for a Space Debris Catcher

The [companion Gutenberg source](./33964-esa-clearspace-1-debris-catcher.gutenberg.html) is the byte-for-byte published WordPress editor readback for post **33964**.

## Key verified reporting

- **November 17, 2026**: ESA signed an implementation/build contract with OHB System AG valued at **more than €102 million**. Contract figure does not represent final mission cost or cost per debris object.
- The current target is **Proba-1**, a **95 kg** ESA spacecraft launched in **2001**, measuring about **0.6 × 0.6 × 0.8 metres**.
- The capture concept uses four articulated arms after autonomous rendezvous, rotation characterization, synchronization and collision avoidance.
- The original planned target, the VESPA payload adapter, was damaged by orbital debris in 2023; ESA replaced it with Proba-1.
- ESA's **November 17, 2026** announcement states a **2030** scheduled launch. Older ESA pages and documents show 2029 or earlier dates; latest construction release supersedes those timelines.
- No active debris capture has yet occurred with ClearSpace-1. Images and videos are historical simulations or educational documentary material.
