# Vuzix Makes Full-Color AI Glasses Under 40 Grams

*Verbatim WordPress Gutenberg body including source-attributed archival media and unchanged canonical volunteer footer.*

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Smart glasses may be getting lighter without giving up color. On <strong>November 18, 2026</strong>, Vuzix introduced <a href="https://ir.vuzix.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/2189/vuzix-introduces-ultralite-color-oem-platform-for">Ultralite Color</a>, a <strong>sub-40-gram</strong> reference design combining full-color microLED waveguide displays in both lenses. The system is aimed at eyewear manufacturers developing their own AI-enabled glasses, not consumers looking to buy a finished pair immediately.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Vuzix calls the platform an OEM starting point, with microphones for voice commands and a wireless connection to a smartphone or another computing device. The initial design intentionally <strong>omits cameras and speakers</strong>. No retail price, field of view, resolution or independently measured battery runtime was announced. The company plans a CES 2027 demonstration in Las Vegas, January 6 through 9.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">How a full-color image fits inside transparent lenses</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>A microLED light engine produces tiny red, green and blue light patterns. Waveguide optics direct the image through thin transparent material toward the eye while allowing the wearer to see the surrounding environment. A binocular arrangement places display information in both eyes rather than showing a single green notification near one lens.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Vuzix says its <strong>Incognito</strong> optical treatment reduces light escaping forward, making displayed information less conspicuous to other people. Electrochromic tinting changes lens darkness electrically to help maintain contrast as ambient lighting varies. Both are manufacturer-described capabilities; without measured brightness, optical efficiency and outdoor testing, the real-world trade-offs remain uncertain.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The company already sells or develops other smart eyewear platforms. Its prior <a href="https://ir.vuzix.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/2174/vuzix-to-showcase-its-latest-advanced-ai-driven-smart">Display Week demonstrations</a> used both microLED and liquid-crystal-on-silicon engines, depending on the product. The new Color reference should not be confused with those older prototypes.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Vuzix left cameras and speakers out</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Cameras, speakers, batteries and onboard processors take space and consume power. Leaving two of those components out can simplify a frame, while using a phone for processing shifts computing and connectivity work away from the face. Microphones still enable voice interaction, so a camera-free design does not eliminate privacy, data-collection or recording concerns.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>An OEM could change the configuration before selling a branded product. Vuzix explicitly says partners may explore additional hardware, and it disclosed <strong>no signed customer orders or mass-production commitments</strong> with the launch. Our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/culture-smart-glasses-privacy-norway-new-york-courts-restrictions/">smart-glasses privacy report</a> explains why device features and local restrictions matter.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Lightweight glasses still face a commercial test</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>A working reference design does not determine whether brands can manufacture durable prescription-compatible eyewear profitably. Optical yield, battery packaging, frame fit, thermal performance, visibility in daylight and consumer willingness to pay all influence adoption. Vuzix says Ultralite Color received a CES 2027 Fashion Tech Innovation Award honor, recognizing the design rather than proving market demand.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Competitors are pursuing different architectures. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/15/samsung-google-gemini-intelligent-eyewear-warby-parker-gentle-monster-pricing-december-2026/">Google and Samsung's branded AI glasses</a> target an upcoming retail launch, while <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/01/xreal-aura-android-xr-glasses-1099-launch-2026/">XREAL's Android XR glasses</a> follow a more immersive display strategy. Our reporting on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/14/himax-always-on-vision-qwen-glasses/">Himax-powered wearable vision</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/01/verisilicon-gltiny-3d-gpu-wearable-graphics-low-memory-2026/">low-memory wearable graphics chips</a> shows how component choices shape the final experience.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The next meaningful milestones are an in-person CES demonstration, independently measured optics and battery performance, and a named partner shipping branded glasses. Until then, Ultralite Color is a promising engineering platform, not a commercially proven consumer headset.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
