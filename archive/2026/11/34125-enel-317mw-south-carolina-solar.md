# Enel Agrees to Buy 317 MW of South Carolina Solar

[Exact published Gutenberg editor source](./34125-enel-317mw-south-carolina-solar.gutenberg.html), read back from published WordPress post **#34125**.

## Sources and checked dates

- **November 20, 2026:** Enel agreed through Enel Green Power North America and EGPNA Project Holdco 7 to acquire 100% of energyRe South Carolina photovoltaic projects totaling **317 MWdc**. Agreement is signed, but closing has **not** occurred as of the announcement.
- Official Enel 1-page primary press statement: https://www.enel.com/content/dam/enel-common/press/en/2026-november/PR-photovoltaic-plants-in-South-Carolina-USA.pdf . The PDF was inspected, including the rendered page screenshot.
- Official energyRe seller release: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/energyre-sells-a-portfolio-of-south-carolina-solar-projects-totaling-317-mwdc-to-enel-green-power-north-america-302947038.html . Seller remains construction manager until commercial operation.
- Expected project average annual generation **exceeds 540 GWh**, i.e. **more than 540 million kWh**. A 540 GWh lower bound divided by 365 days is approximately 1.48 GWh per day; divided by 8760 hours is about 61.6 MW average energy production, not a separate DC nameplate or AC export rating.
- Estimated transaction impact to Enel group net financial debt **~$255 million** after completion and entry-to-service adjustments. This amount is **not explicitly disclosed as the purchase price**.
- Estimated annual EBITDA contribution **~$30 million** once plants are operating, not booked or achieved earnings.
- Company reports North American renewable portfolio of wind, solar and storage **~13.5 GW following closing**. Battery capacity included in a company portfolio figure does not imply battery storage is a primary energy source.
- Planned close in **Q1 2027**, subject to U.S. antitrust, foreign investment and other customary approvals. Forecast completion is neither regulatory approval nor operational start.
- Previous distinct Enel portfolios:
  - **September 10, 2026** completed purchase of **810 MW** U.S. solar plants with expected production of around **1.6 TWh (1,600 GWh)** per year: https://www.enel.com/media/explore/search-press-releases/press/2026/09/enel-closed-the-acquisition-of-810-mw-portfolio-of-solar-plants-in-the-united-states
  - **October 2, 2026** completed purchase of **~270 MW** operating solar portfolio with expected production of **~0.4 TWh (400 GWh)** per year: https://www.enel.com/media/explore/search-press-releases/press/2026/10/enel-finalizes-the-acquisition-of-a-portfolio-of-approximately-270-mw-of-solar-plants-in-the-united-states
- Distinguish **MWdc panel nameplate rating** from actual AC generation, grid interconnection and annual energy GWh. The article makes no unsupported per-project commissioning date, site count, exact address, final purchase price, PPA or storage claim.
