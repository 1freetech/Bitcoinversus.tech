# Scientists Cut Battery Formation Time 40% With a Modest Life Tradeoff

Canonical: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/20/ukbic-battery-formation-40-percent-faster-six-percent-cycle-life-2026/

## Primary evidence
- UKBIC November 18, 2026 official research announcement: https://www.ukbic.co.uk/ukbic-research-shows-battery-cell-formation-can-be-accelerated-by-40-with-only-a-modest-impact-on-cycle-life
- UKBIC November 17 technical paper summary, Kenneth Nwanoro et al: https://www.ukbic.co.uk/optimising-cell-formation-impact-of-formation-protocols-on-cell-manufacturing-cost-and-performance
- UKBIC 2022 manufacturing energy background: https://www.ukbic.co.uk/white-paper-pathways-to-reduce-energy-consumption-in-lithium-ion-battery-cell-manufacturing
- Study compared standard F1 against 13 experimental protocols on 4.8 Ah 21700 NMC811/Gr-SiOx cells, cycling at C/3 until 80% SOH.
- Experimental findings as reported by UKBIC: up to 40% shorter formation duration; up to 40% lower formation-stage energy use; approximately 6% cycle-life reduction against best-performing standard reference. Do not generalize to pack lifespan, charge range or all chemistries.
- 15–32% non-material conversion-cost share and £1.50–£3.75/kWh potential savings are reported industry-scale estimates, not measured realized commercial savings.
- A 12% reduction for a process accounting for 30% of total route time (30% x 40%) is conditional arithmetic with unchanged remaining steps and no queue effects; not a UKBIC measured outcome.
