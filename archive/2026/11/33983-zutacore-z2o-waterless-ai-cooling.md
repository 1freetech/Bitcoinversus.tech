# AI Data Center Cooling Without Water or Chillers?

Exact post-publication Gutenberg editor source is preserved in [the companion file](./33983-zutacore-z2o-waterless-ai-cooling.gutenberg.html). The original cover is WordPress media #33986 and original chart is media #33977.

## Verified facts and analytical distinctions

- ZutaCore and Munters unveiled Z₂O on November 16, 2026, at SC26.
- The unified dielectric two-phase loop is intended to displace chilled-water equipment and cooling towers in the cooling reference design.
- Vendor-modeled cooling pPUE: Z₂O 1.030; chilled-water reference 1.115. These are not independently measured full-facility PUE values.
- At a hypothetical 1 MW continuous IT load, modeled cooling electrical overhead is 30 kW versus 115 kW, an 85 kW difference and 73.9% reduction.
- Illustrative 24/7 full-year projected difference: 85 kW × 8,760 h = 744,600 kWh, or 744.6 MWh. No actual deployed-site annual saving verified.
- Reference model: hourly Dulles, Virginia climate bins, 43°C saturation temperature, 80/20 liquid/air split, 100% IT load.
- Waterless refers to cooling-system water use, not all upstream electricity or equipment water impacts.
