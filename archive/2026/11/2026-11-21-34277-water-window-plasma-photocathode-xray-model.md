# Scientists Model a Compact X-Ray Laser for Studying Cells

Canonical URL: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/21/water-window-x-ray-laser-plasma-photocathode-simulation-november-2026/

## Distinct follow-up angle
- Original BitcoinVersus article id 34142: Berkeley Lab experimentally observed >90% shot lasing and >1000-fold optical gain at 420 nm; actual 2025 paper, DOE 2026 retrospective.
- BitcoinVersus follow-up id 34218: Berkeley Lab / TAU Systems experimentally operated an LPA-driven FEL >8 hours unattended, April 2026 paper, at 420 nm.
- New November 5, 2026 Nature Communications paper: Strathclyde / HHU / ELI team computationally models 2.3–4.4 nm water-window X-ray output from a separate plasma photocathode + beam-driven wakefield design. Not a measured X-ray laser or simply an upgrade to the Berkeley apparatus.
