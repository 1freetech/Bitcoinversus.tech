## Facts and reporting status

- SCinet at SC26 in Chicago operated in the November 14–20, 2026 conference period; official SC26 conference dates were Nov. 15–20.
- Internet2 announcement of November 9, 2026: 19.6 Tbps planned wide-area network capacity for SC26.
- SCinet official history: 13.72 Tbps was delivered across 30 connections in SC25, 2025.
- Arithmetic: 19.6 minus 13.72 = 5.88 Tbps; 5.88 / 13.72 = 42.86% theoretical planned capacity growth, rounded 42.9%.
- Internet2 projected contribution: 7.6 Tbps = 1.6 Tbps for conference links + 6 Tbps research paths, or about 38.8% of 19.6 Tbps overall plan.
- University of Illinois SC26 report: approximately 240 SCinet volunteers, eight countries, 28 states, 109 organizations.
- The comparison charts aggregate WAN link capacity, not observed aggregate traffic and not one single link speed.
- Planned SC26 capacity was not independently confirmed from after-event final operating measurements.
