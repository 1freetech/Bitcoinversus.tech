# Is This the End of Bitcoin? What the November Crash Really Means

Exact WordPress post-publication Gutenberg editor source is preserved in [the companion file](./34136-is-this-the-end-of-bitcoin-crash-analysis.gutenberg.html). Original cover and chart binaries remain on WordPress.

## Key verified facts

- Reuters reported Bitcoin $55,830 at 11:13 p.m. US Eastern time November 20, 2026, corresponding to 8:13 p.m. Pacific. The item URL date is November 21 in UTC.
- October 6, 2025 Bitcoin peak near $126,210; from there to $55,830 represents ~55.8% loss. The $55,830 is a timestamped quote and not an official daily settlement.
- Farside Investors fund-flow totals in USD millions: Nov 13 -185.4, Nov 16 +7.9, Nov 17 -239.9, Nov 18 -306.5, Nov 19 -582.3. Five-session net: -1,306.2 million dollars.
- November 19 IBIT net outflow: $541.1m. Not a sale from BlackRock's corporate treasury.
- Farside Nov 20 row had missing fund fields at research cutoff, so excluded from the chart and summation.
- CoinDesk reported approximately $1.5bn crypto-wide rolling 24-hour derivatives liquidations around November 20; not Bitcoin-only and not proof of a single causal mechanism.
- CryptoQuant's Nov 19 realized price estimate: $53,478, historical snapshot and not a guaranteed floor. Trader-cost-basis band around $71,700, not recovery confirmation.
- Bitcoin's consensus network is distinct from price. A functioning network cannot guarantee future investment profits, while prices can fall without halting mining or consensus.
- Analytical scenarios: deeper drawdown; prolonged stabilization; conditional recovery. No deterministic target is asserted.
