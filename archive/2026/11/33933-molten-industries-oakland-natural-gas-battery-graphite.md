# Oakland Plant Turns Natural Gas Into Battery Graphite

Source-of-truth: [exact WordPress Gutenberg editor readback](./33933-molten-industries-oakland-natural-gas-battery-graphite.gutenberg.html), preserved byte-for-byte from published post **#33933**.

## Verified research

- Molten Industries press release dated **November 18, 2026** states the Oakland demonstration facility is commissioned and operating. Capacity is **300 metric tons of battery anode graphite annually**, not actual measured full-year output.
- Molten states **more than 95% conversion/yield** and **4.5 kWh/kg** electricity, treated as company claims lacking independent full-plant audit. Methane pyrolysis splits methane into solid carbon and hydrogen.
- Avoid treating zero direct carbon dioxide from oxygen-free methane reaction as zero lifecycle greenhouse-gas emissions; methane production, leakage, and electricity matter.
- USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 reports estimated **79,000 metric tons of U.S. natural graphite imports in 2025** versus **53,000 in 2021**, with no U.S. natural graphite mine production reported for 2025. Raw natural graphite imports are not equivalent to synthetic/methane-derived battery-anode graphite.
- March 2026 POSCO Future M agreement concerns development and processing, not proven bulk deliveries.
