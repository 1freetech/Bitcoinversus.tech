## Reported facts, event timing and limitations

- **November 20, 2026 at 10:50 p.m. ET (7:50 p.m. Pacific; 03:50 UTC November 21)** Reuters quoted Bitcoin at **$56,813.68**, down 3.57% in its last reporting window. Reuters story URL: https://www.reuters.com/business/bitcoin-falls-357-56814-2026-11-21/ . **Do not interpret 3.57% as a full-day percentage or $56,813.68 as a final session close.**
- Reuters story is **dated November 21 in UTC**, but describes a **Friday November 20 trade in U.S. time zones**. The title and main article clearly identify that fact.
- Reuters' earlier November 20 news update quoted **$59,381** at approximately **11:25 a.m. ET**: https://www.reuters.com/technology/bitcoin-falls-below-60000-level-2026-11-20/ .
- CoinDesk update published **November 20 at 11:10 p.m. ET** reported BTC **below $57,000**, lowest since **September 2024**, and a broad multi-asset selloff: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/21/bitcoin-tanks-below-usd57-000-for-the-first-time-in-two-years-and-its-not-alone .
- CoinDesk Friday-afternoon reporting estimated **roughly $1.5 billion in rolling 24-hour crypto derivatives liquidations from CoinGlass**, and mining-related stocks with Friday-session declines of approximately 12%–15% for several firms: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/20/bitcoin-plunges-below-usd59-000-as-rush-to-cash-puts-new-bear-market-lows-back-in-play . This is **a source-reported time-sensitive rolling estimate**, not an independently audited final-day total.
- July 1, 2026 price low **$57,754.25**, reported by Barron's on November 16: https://www.barrons.com/articles/bitcoin-prices-strategy-coinbase-f7d3191e .
- Prior BitcoinVersus.Tech **November 8** source describes a **$73,708 reported low**: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/08/bitcoin-73708-low-2026-mining-reward-usd-value-difficulty/ . Change to Nov20 Reuters quote = **(56,813.68 / 73,708 - 1) × 100 = -22.92%**. Change below July reference = **(56,813.68 / 57,754.25 - 1) × 100 = -1.63%**. Figures are comparing specific reported reference quotes and intraday lows, **not equivalent daily settlement prices**.
- Earlier Nov. 19 IBIT net withdrawal **$541 million**, documented in prior publication: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/20/blackrock-ibit-record-541-million-bitcoin-etf-outflow-november-19-2026/ . The article does not claim ETF outflows alone caused the crash.
- Risks and Fed commentary are reported interpretations, not established single-cause explanations. Do not imply a Bitcoin protocol error or an ASIC technological change caused the market drop.
- Mining energy worked example: At **1 PH/s** and **12 J/TH**, machine draws **12 kW**, consumes **288 kWh/day**. At $0.06/kWh, ASIC direct electricity **$17.28/day**. Using a **separate earlier-Friday hashprice snapshot of $27.79 per PH/s-day** yields $10.51 in remaining gross revenue before other costs, **not a measured current margin at BTC $56,813.68**. The later spot drop may alter actual hashprice, also dependent on fees/difficulty, so no new hashprice is inferred.
- A **$86,000/BTC JPMorgan modeled production cost** does not set a market price floor or uniform company-level shutdown threshold. Refer to the distinct November 20 report: https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/20/jpmorgan-bitcoin-mining-cost-86000-versus-electricity-2026/ .
