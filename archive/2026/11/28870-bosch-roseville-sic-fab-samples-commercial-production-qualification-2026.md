## Archived WordPress Gutenberg content

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>ROSEVILLE, Calif. — Bosch's <a href="https://us.bosch-press.com/pressportal/us/en/press-release-31424.html">July 13, 2026 manufacturing update</a> confirmed that its California semiconductor plant had begun sample production of silicon-carbide chips. The company expected commercial production later in 2026, but the primary disclosures reviewed on November 3 do not establish completed customer qualification, full-volume shipments or a sustained factory yield. Those operating milestones matter as Bosch prepares a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/03/bosch-semiconductors-division-2028-five-billion-sales-2030/">dedicated semiconductor division for January 2028</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The distinction is substantial: making trial chips proves a process can produce devices; manufacturing customer-qualified chips reliably and at a competitive cost requires repeatable yields, testing, packaging and delivery. Bosch acquired the existing Roseville fab in 2023 and is converting it into a 200-millimeter silicon-carbide power-device facility. The U.S. plant adds geographic diversity alongside Bosch's established Reutlingen operation in Germany.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The investment and the production gap</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/07/department-commerce-announces-direct-funding-agreement-bosch-225-million">U.S. Commerce Department announced a signed direct-funding agreement</a> on July 13 for up to $225 million in CHIPS Act incentives supporting Bosch's approximately $2 billion Roseville conversion. An authorized maximum is not proof that every dollar has been paid. Federal support can lower project financing pressure, but it does not independently verify wafer throughput, device quality or profitable production.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bosch says Roseville has new cleanroom infrastructure and specialized equipment for high-voltage silicon-carbide MOSFETs. The devices help regulate and switch electricity in electric vehicles, charging systems and industrial equipment. Unlike a central processor, a SiC power transistor does not run AI models; it may help reduce energy losses in power conversion systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why the 200-mm wafer transition matters</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to <a href="https://www.bosch-semiconductors.com/stories-and-events/boschs-global-sic-semiconductor-manufacturing-network/">Bosch's manufacturing-network report</a>, Reutlingen stopped producing its older 150-mm SiC wafers in May 2026, moving entirely to 200-mm SiC production. A 200-mm circular wafer has roughly 78% more gross surface area than a 150-mm wafer, before edge losses, die geometry and defects. Greater potential wafer area is not a measured 78% rise in saleable chips or factory output.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The company describes Roseville and Reutlingen as a future two-fab supply strategy, with manufacturing practices shared between German and American engineering teams. Bosch's <a href="https://www.bosch-semiconductors.com/stories-and-events/two-fabs-one-goal-reliable-sic-supply/">two-fab production account</a> also identifies sample chips, product qualification and a planned commercial ramp as separate steps. For chip purchasers, two locations can support regional sourcing, provided both deliver qualified devices on schedule.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Silicon-carbide production is becoming more competitive. BitcoinVersus.Tech has reported on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/wolfspeed-premium-200mm-silicon-carbide-wafers/">Wolfspeed's 200-mm SiC wafers</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/09/29/infineon-and-eaton-push-silicon-carbide-into-800-vdc-ai-power/">Infineon and Eaton's SiC work for 800-V data-center power</a>. Meanwhile, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/renesas-100v-gan-ai-servers-robots/">Renesas' GaN server and robotics devices</a> illustrate alternative power-semiconductor approaches; GaN and SiC cannot be treated as interchangeable without considering voltage, frequency, thermal conditions and cost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The practical scorecard for Roseville is now straightforward: public evidence of commercial start-up, qualification results, repeatable yields, customer shipments and utilization. Bosch's new division may sharpen sales and foundry strategy by 2028, but manufacturing results will determine whether the California plant adds cost-effective capacity rather than only announced investment.</p>
