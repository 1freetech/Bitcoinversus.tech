<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/25/fidelity-digital-assets-officially-acknowledges-bitcoins-potential/">Fidelity</a> Investments' Global Macro Director Jurrien Timmer has expressed a strong vote of confidence in Bitcoin.
