<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/02/the-digital-ides-of-march-bitcoins-defining-moment/">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC) has initiated charges against the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/08/gary-gensler-continues-to-challenge-crypto-assets-crypto-community-pushes-back/">crypto trading platform</a> Kraken. The SEC's action highlights its commitment to ensuring compliance with securities laws.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Since 2018, Kraken has reportedly engaged in multi-million dollar operations that may have skirted the stringent requirements set by securities regulations. The SEC emphasizes that Kraken's amalgamation of services typically offered by exchanges, brokers, dealers, and clearing agencies necessitated official registration, which the platform did not pursue. This oversight led to a lack of standard investor protections, such as SEC oversight and clear records, potentially putting investors at risk.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The complaint further notes Kraken's practices that could endanger customer assets, including the mixing of company and customer funds. Such actions, while concerning, highlight the SEC's vigilance in scrutinizing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/20/xrp-value-rises-as-sec-drops-allegations-against-ripple-leaders/">crypto entities</a> to ensure they adhere to the same standards that have long governed traditional finance. The regulatory body's pursuit of remedies like injunctive relief and profit disgorgement reflects its dedication to safeguarding the sector's integrity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The case against Kraken comes amidst broader regulatory scrutiny of the crypto market, as the SEC aims to fortify investor confidence and stabilize the foundational aspects of this innovative financial frontier. The move is in line with the SEC's recent actions towards other major platforms like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/21/analyzing-the-coinbase-legal-tussle-with-the-sec/">Coinbase</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/30/binance-ceo-forecasts-bright-future-for-bitcoin-post-halving/">Binance</a>, reinforcing the message that compliance is paramount.</p>
