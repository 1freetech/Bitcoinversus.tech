<p style="text-transform:none"><em><strong>Bitcoin is the Modern Manifestation of Ford's 'Energy Currency</strong></em>'</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nearly a century ago, Henry Ford <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/1921/12/06/archives/mr-fords-energy-dollar.html">cast a vision</a> that would challenge the financial status quo. Published in the New York Tribune on December 4, 1921, Ford's revolutionary idea was to replace gold with an "energy currency" in a bold move to disrupt the banking elite's grip on global wealth and cease the endless cycle of wars. The ambitious plan called for the construction of "the world’s greatest power plant," not merely a marvel of engineering, but the cornerstone of a new currency system. The promise of this idea lay dormant, a historical footnote, until the advent of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/13/block-ceo-jack-dorsey-reveals-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining-with-the-mdk-hashboard/">Bitcoin</a> - a digital currency that remarkably echoes Ford's foresight.</p>

In this light, Bitcoin could be seen as an "energy currency," its value derived from the electrical energy consumed in its creation and management.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The parallels between Ford's vision and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/15/bitvm-enables-smart-contracts-on-bitcoin/">Bitcoin's functionality</a> are striking. Both concepts share a common ground - the ambition to democratize wealth and to provide a counterbalance to the traditional financial systems which have, in many ways, dictated global economics and politics. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/10/greg-foss-predicts-bitcoins-potential-rise-to-2-million/">Bitcoin's ascent</a> in the world of finance is a testament to the enduring relevance of Ford's century-old insight. As Bitcoin continues to gain acceptance, not just as an investment but as a genuine medium of exchange, it stands as a modern-day realization of Ford's energy currency - with the potential to pacify economic disparities and perhaps, in some ways, to lessen conflict.</p>
