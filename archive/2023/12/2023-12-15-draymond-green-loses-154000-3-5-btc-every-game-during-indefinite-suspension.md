<p style="text-transform:none">Draymond Green, a star player for the Golden State Warriors, has been suspended indefinitely by the NBA following an on-court altercation. This incident occurred during a game against the Phoenix Suns on December 12, 2023, where Green was ejected for striking center Jusuf Nurkic in the face. The league's decision to suspend Green indefinitely was influenced by his "repeated history of unsportsmanlike acts," and he will need to meet certain league and team conditions before returning to play.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Green will be docked $153,941 per game if the suspension is fewer than 20 games, and $202,922 per game if it exceeds 20 games. This suspension follows his third ejection in just 23 games this season. During a post-game press conference, Green stated that his intention was to sell the foul and not to harm Nurkic.</p>
