<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/30/binance-ceo-forecasts-bright-future-for-bitcoin-post-halving/">Binance</a> announced that it will stop supporting all Binance USD (BUSD) products December 15, 2023. This decision follows Paxos, the issuer of BUSD, halting the minting of new coins. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-admin/post.php?post=2730&amp;action=edit">Binance</a> advised its users to withdraw or convert their existing BUSD on the exchange into other assets before December 15. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">From December 31, the platform will disable withdrawals for BUSD, and existing balances will be automatically converted into First Digital USD (FDUSD) for certain users​​. This move is part of Binance's larger strategy to wind down services for its native stablecoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The exchange had previously announced plans to gradually stop support for BUSD before February 2024, beginning with the cessation of borrowing and lending services for the stablecoin in October​​. The decision to halt support for BUSD came amidst regulatory scrutiny. In February, the United States Securities and Exchange Commission suggested that BUSD was an unregistered security, leading to a Wells notice issued to Paxos. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Furthermore, the New York Department of Financial Services ordered Paxos to halt the issuance of BUSD​​. This news follows a significant settlement between U.S. authorities and Binance, along with its former CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/30/binance-ceo-forecasts-bright-future-for-bitcoin-post-halving/">Changpeng Zhao</a>, in November. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">They were required to pay $4.3 billion, and Zhao resigned as CEO as part of the settlement​​. Before August, BUSD was one of the largest stablecoins by market capitalization, reaching over $23.3 billion in November 2022. However, its market cap had fallen to approximately $1.7 billion at the time of the report, marking a decline of more than 92% in 12 month.</p>
