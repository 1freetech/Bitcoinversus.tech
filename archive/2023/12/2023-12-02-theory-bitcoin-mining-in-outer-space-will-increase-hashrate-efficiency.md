<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Is Bitcoin mining possible in outer space?  NASA's 2021 <a href="https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/hardware/space-final-frontier-data-centers#:~:text=The%20HPE%20Spaceborne%20Computer%2D2,to%20run%20actual%20production%20workloads." target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">venture</a> - which deployed the HPE Spaceborne Computer-2 to the International Space Station - showcased that servers can effectively operate in the vast expanse beyond our atmosphere.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><em>Bitcoin Mining In Space will Be expensive</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Undertaking industrial-scale <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin mining</a> in space is no small feat, both technically and financially.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the idea of space-based Bitcoin mining has potential, executing such a vision presents some challenges. Even at its most economical, launching equipment to space costs a staggering $2,719 per kilogram according to a <a href="https://thespacereview.com/article/3740/1">report</a> by thespacereview.com. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Beyond the financial burden of sending mining machinery into orbit, one would also need to deploy a self-enclosed solar array power loop to sustainably power the machines. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Furthermore, the unpredictable nature of space machinery demands robotic automation capable of conducting repairs and replacing parts. And even with advanced robotics, there's a fundamental constraint: <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">Bitcoin mining operations</a> traditionally thrive in locations where humans can directly intervene, maintaining and troubleshooting the machinery in real-time. Bringing this industry to space would fundamentally alter this dynamic, adding layers of complexity and risk. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Such an endeavor demands significant investment, placing it squarely within the purview of nation-states or major corporations. It's conceivable that a combined corporate-state initiative, involving giants like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Coinbase</a>, SpaceX, OPEC, Apple, or Google, could pioneer this space. Given the advanced infrastructure required, it's anticipated that the hashrate of these celestial mining machines would surpass 1,0000 TerraHash per second - or 1 PetaHash.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Space: A Bitcoin Miner's Utopia?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The environment of outer space offers unique advantages for Bitcoin mining. Servers operating in space are governed by the same physical laws as on Earth, yet the conditions are vastly different. Furthermore, the cold void of space provides an ideal temperature for Bitcoin machines, allowing for faster processing without the threat of overheating.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the appeal of mining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/25/fidelity-digital-assets-officially-acknowledges-bitcoins-potential/">Bitcoin</a> in space, the practical challenges and costs involved make it a complex endeavor, nonetheless.</p>
