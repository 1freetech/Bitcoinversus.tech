<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">Bitmain</a>, a leading manufacturer of cryptocurrency mining hardware, has taken a significant step forward in product durability by applying a protective conformal coating to the temperature sensors of its hashboards. This advancement is poised to enhance the reliability and lifespan of mining equipment, a crucial factor in the competitive world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/14/bitcoin-mining-is-shaping-renewable-energy-frontiers/">Bitcoin mining</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Conformal coatings are essential in safeguarding circuit boards against a variety of environmental stressors, including moisture, thermal shock, and contamination. By integrating this technology, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/12/analyzing-centralization-and-monopolies-in-the-bitcoin-industry/">Bitmain's hashboards</a> are expected to withstand harsh conditions better, ensuring longer uptime and more consistent performance — a vital aspect for miners where every second of productivity counts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The production manager of Kaboom Racks, known as KaboomracksJake, commended the move, saying, "The K-Pro may have the soldered heat sink design but hey Bitmain is now putting a protective coating over the temp sensors at least.” The implication is clear: Bitmain is committed to innovation that translates into real-world benefits for its customers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">With the enhanced durability offered by the protective coating, miners can expect their hardware to operate more efficiently for extended periods. This improvement is not just a technical upgrade but also a strategic advantage in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin mining industry</a>, where prolonged equipment longevity can significantly impact profitability.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
