<p style="text-transform:none">In an era of groundbreaking developments in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/30/robots-will-eventually-replace-the-human-workforce/">artificial intelligence</a> (AI), the digital currency Bitcoin has been hailed by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/19/decoding-bitcoin-with-max-keiser-spotlight-on-blackrock-and-spot-etfs/">Bitcoin Activist Max Keiser</a> as humanity's hope amidst the rise of potentially threatening AI advancements. Keiser's comedic yet poignant declaration came in response to concerns about a new model of AI, known as Q*, which some fear could become a danger to human society.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/27/intel-elon-musk-x-com-steps-into-the-future-with-bitcoin-integration/">X.com</a> post, Keiser exclaimed, "Only #Bitcoin can save us!" This statement quickly captured the attention of the Bitcoin community and tech enthusiasts alike. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/29/openai-ceo-sam-altman-highlights-bitcoins-crucial-role-in-technological-innovation/">Sam Altman</a> found himself briefly ousted after a group of researchers sent a letter to the board of directors. The letter, whose details remain undisclosed, warned of a powerful AI discovery that could pose a serious risk to humanity. Although the specifics of the AI's capabilities could not be independently verified by Reuters, the concern was significant enough to catalyze major upheaval within the company.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The developments at OpenAI have sparked a larger discussion about the power and potential dangers of generative AI. Experts in the field are exploring the frontiers of AI development, particularly in mathematics, where achieving the ability to compute the one correct answer could signal reasoning powers similar to human intelligence. Such advancements could lead to breakthroughs in scientific research and beyond.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the idea that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/26/gradually-then-suddenly-how-ai-and-robots-are-reshaping-the-workforce/">AI</a> could one day outthink humans is not without its <a href="https://wordpress.com/post/bitcoinversus.tech/2601">critics and doomsayers</a>. The debate about the existential risks posed by AI has been ongoing, with some fearing that highly intelligent machines might one day conclude that humanity is expendable.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Keiser's comment may have been made with a touch of humor, but it reflects a serious consideration of Bitcoin's role in a future dominated by AI. As Altman himself remarked on the thrill of pushing the frontiers of discovery, it's the merging of these technological advancements that could define the next era of human achievement.</p>
