<p style="text-transform:none">HIVE Digital, a Canadian <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/15/pennsylvania-greenlights-bitcoin-mining-with-revised-bill/">Bitcoin mining</a> giant, has <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2023-11-14/hive-bitcoin-mining-efficiency-s19k">taken a decisive step</a> to enhance its operational efficiency. On November 14, 2023, the company announced a significant investment aimed at amplifying its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin mining capabilities</a>. This strategic move involves the acquisition of 4,800 units of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">Bitmain’s Antminer</a> S19k Pro, a move that promises to infuse the firm’s mining fleet with cutting-edge efficiency.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This recent purchase is expected to increase their computing power by approximately 652 PH/s while aiming to transform their energy efficiency from 38J/TH to an impressive 23J/TH. The new equipment, scheduled for delivery within the next month, will be a substantial upgrade for HIVE, which has also added another 3,100 units of the S19XP model to its arsenal over the past six months.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's proactive approach comes at a critical time. With the cost of bitcoin production reaching over $22k per BTC mined during Q3, HIVE found itself grappling with efficiency challenges, marking it as one of the public mining companies with higher production costs. The forthcoming bitcoin halving event next year adds further urgency to this upgrade. Without significant enhancements to efficiency or reductions in energy costs, HIVE could potentially mine at a gross loss.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In response to these financial pressures, HIVE has adopted a strategy of liquidity over accumulation. The Q3 earnings report revealed the company's decision to sell all bitcoin mined since July along with 200 BTC from its reserves. This approach has allowed the company to fund its operational and capital expenditures without diluting shareholder value through the issuance of new shares or resorting to financial leverage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">HIVE stands as the fifth-largest bitcoin holder among its publicly traded peers, maintaining control of about 1% of the computational power (hashrate) on the network. </p>
