<p style="text-transform:none">Essentially, Finance experts are wary of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/17/fedimint-to-take-center-stage-with-obi-nwosu-at-africas-premier-bitcoin-conference/">Bitcoin</a> because it changes the game — it cuts out the middleman, allowing people to trade directly with each other. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">EuroDale, a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">Bitcoin expert</a> on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/23/blackrocks-indirect-bitcoin-investment-through-microstrategy/">x.com</a>, puts it plainly: professionals may resist Bitcoin because it affects their bottom line. He says, "It is nearly impossible to make someone understand <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/04/bitcoin-and-the-history-of-the-u-s-dollar/">Bitcoin</a> when their salary relies on not understanding it."</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Adding validation to the tweet, Haakon Haave <a href="https://twitter.com/haakon_hv/status/1729117171547259152">shared</a> his personal journey with Bitcoin on social media. He remarked, "I was working for a bank when I first understood <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/13/block-ceo-jack-dorsey-reveals-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining-with-the-mdk-hashboard/">Bitcoin</a>. Now, I no longer work for a bank," suggesting a profound shift in his career path after realizing the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/25/bitcoins-growth-potential-spotlighted-on-x-com/">potential of Bitcoin</a>.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Despite some pushback, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/27/morgan-stanleys-bullish-bitcoin-prediction/">Bitcoin</a> is gaining traction. It promises to make finance fairer and offer more transparency. It's not just for tech enthusiasts anymore; it's going mainstream. Business owners and traditional financial institutions are getting on board with Bitcoin's secure, decentralized approach, which avoids the weaknesses of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/04/bitcoin-and-the-history-of-the-u-s-dollar/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">traditional finance</a>.</p>
