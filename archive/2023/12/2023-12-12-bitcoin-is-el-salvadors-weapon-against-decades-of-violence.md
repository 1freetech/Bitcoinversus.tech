<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin's role in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">El Salvador's </a><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">resurgence</a><br>has been nothing short of remarkable. For years, the nation was plagued by violence and corruption, with its people suffering under the weight of organized crime. The economy was in shackles, and hope was a scarce commodity. However, two years after <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/07/alliance-bernstein-predicts-bitcoin-fund-management-is-a-650-billion-opportunity/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin</a> was adopted as legal tender, the landscape has drastically changed. The crime that once seemed insurmountable has been significantly reduced, breathing new life into the country and giving back the people their homeland.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/19/decoding-bitcoin-with-max-keiser-spotlight-on-blackrock-and-spot-etfs/">Max Keiser</a> recently highlighted El Salvador's achievements in a compelling online discussion. He drew parallels between the country's past struggles and ongoing global conflicts, emphasizing the peace and stability that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/28/bitcoin-miners-aid-texas-during-heatwave-curtail-energy-consumption-by-90/">Bitcoin</a> has helped foster. Keiser's statements echo a growing sentiment that Bitcoin is more than just a financial asset; it's a tool for societal change.</p>
