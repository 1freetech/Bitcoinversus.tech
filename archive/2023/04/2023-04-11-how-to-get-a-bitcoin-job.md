<p>To the aspiring bitcoiner,&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here’s some advice on how to get a job in the bitcoin sector:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1. Understand your purpose. </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The truth is that some people don’t understand the importance of the first and only digital monetary network in which you can exchange value on a global scale instantaneously without the need for permission from a third party, and they never will. There is an unfortunate reality that most will scoff at the idea of an independent monetary network equipped with an immutable ledger and a 24-hour automated accounting system that requires no trust to verify. And they continue on in a deep slumber through the Great Financial Awakening.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You won’t be able to save everyone.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So come to your own conclusion as to why the bitcoin industry is particularly important to you and your future.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>2. Keep your conviction consistent</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After realizing why the bitcoin industry is important to you, continue to apply to job openings. Attending a bitcoin event or conference is a great place to network. Most of the pioneers and innovators in the space attend events and conferences throughout the year and you can usually find bitcoin companies in need of diamond hands to work at their bitcoin mining facilities.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin companies need hardware and software engineers as well as facility technicians. Recently, I’ve discovered that there is a shortage of specialists within the bitcoin space and that is a result of people being unaware of what is going on.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>3. Never give up.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Applying to jobs can be stressful(due to its volatility) and the industry can be daunting because of the learning curve, but this is a long-term investment of your time and/or money. Do not grow weary of the task at hand. You got this. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sincere Regards,</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Matthan</p>
