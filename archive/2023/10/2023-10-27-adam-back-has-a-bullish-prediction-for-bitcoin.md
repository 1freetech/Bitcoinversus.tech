<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>Adam Back Predicts $100,000 Milestone by 2024</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A renowned name in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/unpopular-opinion-gary-gensler-is-the-perfect-fit-for-crypto-oversight/">cryptocurrency</a> world, Adam Back is the CEO of Blockstream, a leading company in blockchain technology. Credited with the invention of the "proof-of-work" system used by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/02/the-digital-ides-of-march-bitcoins-defining-moment/">Bitcoin</a>, his expertise is revered in crypto circles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ec7aFp2PgqE">recent appearance</a> on Bloomberg, Adam Back, a major player in the Bitcoin community, shared his confidence in the digital currency. He emphasizes a potential price appreciation, anticipating that Bitcoin will touch the $100,000 mark before its 2024 Halving event.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
