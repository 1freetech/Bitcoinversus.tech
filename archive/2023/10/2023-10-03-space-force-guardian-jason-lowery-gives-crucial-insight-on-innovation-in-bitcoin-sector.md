<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lowery distinguishes two categories within the technological sphere: technological rhetoric and real innovation. Technological rhetoric is characterized by buzz, flashy headlines, and promises of disruptive change devoid of substantial evidence or actualization. It often masquerades as innovation through catchy jargon and hyped-up statistics, lacking the depth or comprehensive understanding of the original concepts it attempts to replicate.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Conversely, real innovation is important, entailing not merely the development of new technologies or processes but fundamentally reimagining and transforming existing paradigms. It’s about creating tangible value, often addressing unrecognized problems, necessitating a deeper level of understanding and appreciation.</p>

Bitcoin’s inception and evolution epitomize the transformative potential of genuine innovations, serving as a testament to the capability of real advancements to reshape our world.</p>
