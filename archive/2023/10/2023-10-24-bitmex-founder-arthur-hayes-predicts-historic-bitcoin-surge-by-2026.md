<p style="font-size:1rem"><strong><em>Unprecedented Growth Ahead for Bitcoin?</em></strong></p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Hayes laid out a detailed rationale behind his optimistic projection:</p>

<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Bitcoin as a Store of Value:</strong> With the increasing adoption rate, Bitcoin is now being widely regarded as a trusted store of value.</li>
