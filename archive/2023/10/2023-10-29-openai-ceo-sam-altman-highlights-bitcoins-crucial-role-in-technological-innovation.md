<p style="font-size:1rem;text-transform:none"><em><strong>Sam Altman Acknowledges Bitcoin on Joe Rogan's Podcast </strong></em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During a recent appearance on <a href="https://www.blackbitcoinbillionaire.com/untitled-16/">The Joe Rogan Experience</a>, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman emphasized the importance and transformative potential of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/13/the-real-merge-the-moment-bitcoin-integrates-with-the-gaming-industry/">Bitcoin</a>. Altman, who is also the founder of Worldcoin, described Bitcoin as a “super logical and important step on the technology tree” of human progress.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Altman's enthusiasm for the digital currency was evident throughout the conversation. Yet, he also raised concerns regarding governmental attitudes toward it. He revealed, “The US government is trying to control #Bitcoin,” suggesting a potential clash between regulators and the growing crypto industry.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The podcast touched on a range of pertinent issues, from the regulatory crackdown post FTX's collapse to the role of corruption and how Bitcoin could shape society's future. Altman also expressed his disappointment regarding the regulatory backlash following FTX's unfortunate incident.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the challenges, his optimism for the digital currency remained unshaken. He asserted his opposition to Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), emphasizing a belief in the decentralized promise of crypto technologies like Bitcoin.</p>
