<p style="font-size:1rem;text-transform:none"><strong><em>El Salvador's Renewable Energy Pioneering Bitcoin Mining</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a groundbreaking initiative, El Salvador, through a collaboration between Volcano Energy and Luxor Technology, has rolled out its first-ever <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/" target="_blank">Bitcoin mining</a> pool named 'Lava Pool.' Setting itself apart, this venture leans into its green ethos – being powered entirely by renewable <a href="https://x.com/1BitcoinVersus/status/1604676826349068288?s=20">geothermal energy</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"1rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:1rem;text-transform:none"><strong><em>A Sustainable Mining Endeavor</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Lava Pool project, initiated in June 2023 and operational earlier this month, seeks to increase <a href="http://bitcoinversus.tech/?p=1060">Bitcoin's hashrate</a>. In layman's terms, the hashrate refers to the computational power dedicated to mining and processing Bitcoin transactions. The use of renewable geothermal energy showcases El Salvador's dual commitment: spearheading technological innovation and embracing sustainability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin Magazine <a href="https://twitter.com/bitcoinmagazine/status/1709576587232866692?s=51&amp;t=SuCbxtr_gZDMxWNSTFJTVA">highlighted</a> the country's new stride on x.com, "JUST IN: The first El Salvador-based #Bitcoin mining pool just went live 🇸🇻". This echoes El Salvador's consistent push towards the frontier of cryptocurrency innovation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="font-size:1rem;text-transform:none"><strong><em>Decentralization and Energy</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lava Pool <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2023/10/04/the-first-bitcoin-mining-pool-from-el-salvador-is-here/#:~:text=Contributor&amp;text=After%20launching%20their%20bitcoin%20mining,Its%20name%20is%20Lava%20Pool.">aims to reinforce </a>the national energy grid. By increasing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/08/prediction-bitcoin-hashrate-to-hit-new-all-time-highs-by-year-end/">Bitcoin's hashrate</a>, the country not only bolsters its cryptocurrency infrastructure but also ensures that this progression doesn't come at the planet's expense. It's a practical demonstration of how sustainability and technology can coexist.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"1rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:1rem;text-transform:none"><strong><em>A Legacy with Bitcoin</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In June 2021, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">El Salvador</a> etched its name in the annals of history by becoming the first country globally to recognize Bitcoin as legal tender. This wasn't just an economic decision; it was a signal of the nation's forward-thinking approach, aligning itself with the future of decentralized digital currencies.</p>
