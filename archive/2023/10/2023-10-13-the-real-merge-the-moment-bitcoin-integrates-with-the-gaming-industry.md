<p style="text-transform:none">Microsoft's gaming behemoth, Xbox, is diving into the world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/23/nodal-power-turns-landfill-methane-into-green-energy-for-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a>. A <a href="https://twitter.com/btc_archive/status/1708821607936840190?s=51&amp;t=SuCbxtr_gZDMxWNSTFJTVA">tweet</a> by @BTC_archive, citing the leaked roadmap, stated, "NEW: Microsoft is building a #Bitcoin and crypto wallet into Xbox. XBox has +120 million monthly active users!"</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By mere numbers alone, integrating a Bitcoin wallet into a major video game console like Xbox could provide a significant boost to Bitcoin's scaling efforts, bringing Bitcoin closer to mainstream daily use.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Coinpedia, this <a href="https://coinpedia.org/news/microsofts-game-changing-move-cryptocurrency-integration-in-xbox-platforms/">groundbreaking news</a> stems from Microsoft’s <a href="https://files.cand.uscourts.gov/files/23-cv-02880_FTC_v_Microsoft/PX7011%20(Redacted).pdf">confidential documents </a>that were unintentionally revealed amidst their ongoing legal skirmish with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC). These documents, uncovered on the renowned ResetEra gaming forum, showcased the Xbox roadmap for May 2022, hinting at the upcoming introduction of crypto wallet support.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Though the specifics of this new feature remain undisclosed, the potential implications of such a union are enormous. Not only does this confirm the long-standing speculation of major corporate giants like Microsoft taking a keener interest in Bitcoin and its applications, but it also showcases a budding synergy between the gaming and cryptocurrency sectors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

This could well be the dawn of a new era where gaming consoles aren't just for play, but also for pay.</p>
