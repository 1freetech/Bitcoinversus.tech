<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In a recent YouTube<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bNbN73xQNKQ"> video</a> by VoskCoin, Dennis Wiltshire, CEO of BMA Enterprises, shared an inside look into the groundbreaking Immersion <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/23/nodal-power-turns-landfill-methane-into-green-energy-for-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining farm</a> operated in partnership with Aqil and Colby of BMA. The innovative facility, based in Virginia, utilizes liquid cooling to enable Bitcoin miners to mine more BTC per day while using less electricity, ultimately enhancing the longevity of ASIC mining rigs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The facility, conceived by Aqil during a period of boredom, eventually materialized through collaboration with Dennis Wiltshire and Colby from BMA Enterprises. Together, they took on the responsibility to operate, maintain, and expand the immersion Bitcoin mining farm. The core of the operation is the liquid-cooled Bitcoin mine solution developed by DCX. This system, comprising immersion tanks, immersion fluid, and a radiator the size of a tractor-trailer, effectively cools approximately 500 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/29/bitcoin-mining-hardware-prices-surge-amid-hashrate-increase/">Bitmain Antminer S19</a> Bitcoin mining rigs.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the video tour, Drew Vosk of VoskCoin delves into the various aspects of the BTC immersion mining farm. He learns about the utilization of miners, the braiins OS, Dennis’ custom software monitoring solution, and the process for repairing BTC miners that break and stop working. The video provides valuable insights into the inner workings of the immersion cooling facility and highlights the benefits of this innovative approach to Bitcoin mining.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By using liquid cooling, the facility can significantly increase the efficiency of the mining rigs. Traditional air-cooled mining farms face challenges with heat dissipation, resulting in higher electricity consumption and reduced equipment lifespan. However, immersion cooling tackles these issues by submerging the mining rigs in a special coolant fluid that absorbs the heat generated during mining. This innovative solution not only lowers electricity consumption but also extends the lifespan of the mining rigs, making it an environmentally friendly and cost-effective approach to Bitcoin mining.</p>
