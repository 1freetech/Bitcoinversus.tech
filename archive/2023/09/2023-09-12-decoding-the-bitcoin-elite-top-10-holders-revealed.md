<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The ownership of Bitcoin can be difficult to track due to the pseudonymous nature of Bitcoin transactions. Each transaction is linked to a unique digital address, and while some entities reveal their ownership of certain addresses, others prefer to remain anonymous.
