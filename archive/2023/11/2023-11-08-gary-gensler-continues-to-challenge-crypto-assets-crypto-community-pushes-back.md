<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/02/crypto-assets-deemed-securities-by-federal-judge-amplifying-cryptocurrency-regulatory-uncertainty/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC) has taken a <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2023/10/09/sec-says-crypto-assets-have-no-inherent-value-in-new-court-motion-against-coinbase/#:~:text=The%20U.S.%20Securities%20and%20Exchange%20Commission%20(SEC),they%20cannot%20generate%20profits%20on%20their%20own.">firm stance</a> against the inherent value of digital assets, particularly Bitcoin. According to the SEC, these digital assets don't possess any innate value, as they're incapable of generating profits independently.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This argument leans heavily on the Howey test, a traditional legal metric used to ascertain whether a transaction is deemed an investment contract. This test has frequently been employed in crypto-related legal debates, given the gray area surrounding the status of many digital assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Interestingly, the SEC went a step further, pointing fingers at specific altcoins like Solana and MATIC, labeling them as unlicensed securities. This action can be seen as an attempt by the regulatory body to clarify its position and perhaps exert more control over the rapidly evolving crypto market.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Paul Grewal, Coinbase's chief legal officer, was quick to retaliate against these claims. "The assets we list on our platform are not securities and are not within the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/unpopular-opinion-gary-gensler-is-the-perfect-fit-for-crypto-oversight/">SEC's jurisdiction</a>," he stated, denouncing the SEC's motion as nothing new.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the debate continues, one thing is clear: The crypto community will not back down without a fight, especially when the value and legitimacy of assets like Bitcoin are questioned. With both parties standing their ground, the coming months promise further intriguing developments in the ongoing tussle between crypto exchanges and regulators.</p>
