<p style="font-size:1rem"><em><strong>FTX's Controversial Code Alteration</strong></em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"0.9rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:0.9rem;text-transform:none">The recent revelations by FTX co-founder Gary Wang have sent shockwaves throughout the crypto community, shedding light on the underbelly of exchange operations and igniting debates about transparency and trust. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"0.9rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:0.9rem;text-transform:none">During Gary Wang's testimony at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUG506UMZOY&amp;t=13s" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Sam Bankman-Fried</a> (SBF) trial, Wang <a href="https://bitcoinnews.com/ftx-co-founder-gary-wangs-testimony-sbf/">shared</a> his first-hand account, detailing how he allegedly altered the FTX code under the guidance of Bankman-Fried.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"0.9rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:0.9rem;text-transform:none">These alterations, spanning from 2019 to 2022, enabled Alameda Research—an affiliated hedge fund—to borrow infinite amounts from unsuspecting FTX customers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"1rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:1rem;text-transform:none"><strong><em>Unveiling Alameda's Deficit with FTX</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none","fontSize":"0.9rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:0.9rem;text-transform:none">Wang's revelations didn't end with the code modifications. Wang also stated he found out in 2021 that Alameda was running a staggering $200 million deficit with FTX. The revelation, which took him aback, was promptly shared with Bankman-Fried, adding more depth to the controversy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
