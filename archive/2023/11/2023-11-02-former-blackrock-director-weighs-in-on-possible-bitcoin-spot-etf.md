<p style="text-transform:none">A <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2023/10/05/blackrock-insider-primes-crypto-for-a-huge-177-wall-street-earthquake-that-could-hit-the-price-of-bitcoin-ethereum-and-xrp/#:~:text=A%20former%20BlackRock%20managing%20director%20has%20predicted,SEC%20approves%20a%20long%2Dawaited%20bitcoin%20spot%20ETF.">recent statement</a> by a former <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/10/theory-what-if-blackrock-controlled-bitcoin-mining/">BlackRock</a> managing director has ignited renewed optimism within the crypto community. The potential approval of a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/08/the-dawn-of-bitcoin-etfs-why-the-sec-might-approve-multiple-applications-together/">Bitcoin spot ETF</a> by the SEC, might just be around the corner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The transformative world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/11/bitcoiner-declares-bitcoin-is-the-apex-predator-of-money-in-viral-tweet/">Bitcoin</a> may soon be getting a major boost. According to a former managing director at BlackRock, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/unpopular-opinion-gary-gensler-is-the-perfect-fit-for-crypto-oversight/">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC) is poised to greenlight a Bitcoin spot ETF in just a few months. If these predictions hold, the crypto market could witness a staggering influx of up to $17.7 trillion from institutional entities.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">A Bitcoin ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) mirrors the price of Bitcoin, offering investors a method to buy into the cryptocurrency without actually purchasing the digital coin itself. Current Bitcoin ETFs are largely anchored on futures contracts, but the crypto community has been eagerly awaiting the launch of spot ETFs, which would track Bitcoin's real-time price.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With giants like BlackRock, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/11/ark-investment-ceo-cathie-wood-anticipates-parabolic-move-with-bitcoin-in-convergence-with-ai-boom/">Ark Investment Management</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/06/grayscale-urges-sec-to-approve-bitcoin-etf/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Grayscale</a> all vying for SEC approval of their respective Bitcoin ETF applications, the stakes are definitely high. </p>
