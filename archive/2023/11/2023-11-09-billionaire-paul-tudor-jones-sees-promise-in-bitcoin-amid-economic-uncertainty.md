<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Hedge Fund Tycoon Highlights Bitcoin and Gold as Robust Assets</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Billionaire and hedge fund magnate, Paul Tudor Jones, has expressed a growing affinity for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/15/bitvm-enables-smart-contracts-on-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> (BTC) and gold. In a recent interview with CNBC, Jones explained why these two assets are at the top his list, particularly in the face of looming economic challenges.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Jones indicated that the combination of impending <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/14/ceo-nancy-davis-calls-the-fed-panzis-in-latest-cnbc-interview/">Fed rate hikes</a>, potential recession, and emerging geopolitical disputes makes Bitcoin a compelling choice. He believes Bitcoin, together with gold, will occupy a more significant portion of investors' portfolios than historically observed.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">With the shadow of a potential equities correction hanging overhead, alternative assets like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">Bitcoin</a> are increasingly catching the eyes of investment experts. Jones' endorsement of Bitcoin, in this context, reflects a broader trend of interest in Bitcoin as a stabile asset.</p>
