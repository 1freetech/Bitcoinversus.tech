<p style="text-transform:none">The trial of Sam Bankman-Fried concluded with the jury delivering a guilty verdict on all seven charges, including wire fraud and money laundering conspiracies. This outcome marks the end of a saga that saw FTX, once a thriving crypto exchange, crumble amid allegations of financial corruption.</p>
