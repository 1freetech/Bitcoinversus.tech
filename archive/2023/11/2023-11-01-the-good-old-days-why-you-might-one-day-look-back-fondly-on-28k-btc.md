<p style="font-size:12.5px"><em>The bitcoin bull market is here. It’s happening again and it’s happening faster than we think. And so, as we anticipate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/02/the-digital-ides-of-march-bitcoins-defining-moment/">Bitcoin's next big move</a>, some, like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a> Chairman <a href="http://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/20/michael-saylor-shares-insight-on-bitcoins-impact-at-btc23-conference-in-germany/">Michael Saylor</a> reminisce about the good old days.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin</a> seemed to be taking a nostalgic pause, hovering in the sweet spot between $26,500 and $28,000 before Monday, October 23rd. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">And then - just like that - bitcoin’s trading at 35,000. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"I Wish it didn’t happen so fast. You don’t want it to happen that fast. These are the days where you can work at a job at McDonald's and buy Bitcoin. In ten years, you’re gonna realize it was the most lucrative thing you’ve ever done in your life," says Saylor, emphasizing the golden opportunity presented in this bear market.</p>
