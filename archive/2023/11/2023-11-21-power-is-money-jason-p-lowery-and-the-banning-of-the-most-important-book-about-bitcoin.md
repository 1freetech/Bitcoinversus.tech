<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none"><em><strong>Power is Money</strong> is a 3-part series of articles that examines the relation between the Mastery of Energy and the dynamics of Bitcoin and economic progress, integrating concepts from </em>science<em> to offer a fresh perspective on global prosperity and value.</em></p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size:clamp(0.875rem, 0.875rem + ((1vw - 0.2rem) * 0.196), 0.9rem);"><em><strong>Embracing Bitcoin As The Next Biological Construct</strong></em></h4>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">The financial and Bitcoin spheres were recently taken by storm with Jason P. Lowery&#8217;s riveting take on the strategic implications of Bitcoin in his book, &#8220;Softwar.&#8221; At its core, power projection is a fundamental aspect of all living beings&#8217; communication. By adopting Bitcoin, humanity is diving deep into the subsequent stage of this inherent biological construct.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">A profound excerpt from &#8220;Softwar&#8221; eloquently encapsulates the dynamic of power:</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">&#8220;Entrust sapiens with abstract power&#8230; they will find a way to exploit that logic for their own personal advantage&#8230; Logical constraints can’t prevent the exploitation of logical constraints.&#8221;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">Lowery&#8217;s thought process suggests that traditional labels that we give eachother- king, president, governor &#8211; are all essentially unreal labels that we give to create hierarchical constructs. In reality, these constructs are usually exploited for a myriad of reasons, including monetary gain. While Bitcoin may have been invented to be a monetary asset, Lowery posits that Bitcoin is the next evolution in modern warfare (power projection). </p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">Lowery&#8217;s work isn&#8217;t just another addition to the finance bookshelf. Its originality and depth have sent shockwaves, even unnerving a considerable chunk of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin community</a>.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">In &#8220;Softwar&#8221;, Lowery articulates:</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">&#8220;Recall how computer program design represents an exercise in abstract thinking&#8230; They forget that just because multiple people decided to use the same abstract terms&#8230; doesn’t mean that these descriptions are objectively true.&#8221;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">Just as computer programming demands abstract thinking, so too does the conceptualization of Bitcoin as a form of currency. However, beyond its apparent role as money, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/19/theory-bitcoin-could-be-the-catalyst-for-mega-skyscrapers-like-the-x-seed-4000/">Bitcoin</a> represents a more complex evolution in the dynamics of power projection, akin to an advancement in modern warfare. The consensus around Bitcoin being merely digital money is a simplification; this viewpoint overlooks the possibility that its design and decentralized nature may embody a new strategy in economic and geopolitical influence. Thus, while multiple parties may agree on Bitcoin&#8217;s monetary utility, this shared understanding doesn&#8217;t fully capture its potential as an instrument of power in the digital age</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin&#8217;s gravity is pulling in more than just financial institutions. Realizing the strategic advantage of Bitcoin mining facilities for power projection, nations worldwide are vying to establish these facilities within their territories. We once believed &#8220;money is power,&#8221; but today&#8217;s world is revealing that &#8220;power is money,&#8221; a transformation evident with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">Bitcoin&#8217;s growing global influence</a>.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading">The Controversy Surrounding &#8220;Softwar&#8221;</h4>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">Recent developments have seen Lowery facing institutional pressure, compelling him to retreat from public discussions about his groundbreaking book. He recently stated on his x.com account:</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">“For those asking what&#8217;s been going on w/me, I was ordered to take SOFTWAR down &amp; asked to stop talking about the subject publicly.&#8221;</p>

<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-rich is-provider-twitter wp-block-embed-twitter"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper"><a href="https://twitter.com/jasonplowery/status/1684637080029196288?s=51&#038;t=SuCbxtr_gZDMxWNSTFJTVA" rel="nofollow">https://twitter.com/jasonplowery/status/1684637080029196288?s=51&#038;t=SuCbxtr_gZDMxWNSTFJTVA</a></div></figure>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="text-transform:none">Jason P. Lowery&#8217;s thesis has emerged as a landmark work in the discourse on Bitcoin&#8217;s role in regards to its strategic significance. Its out-of-print status and the fact that copies are trading for upwards of $15,000 on sites like eBay underline its significance. This rare thesis was removed from retail in July 2023 per the request of military, adding to its allure and demand among collectors and Bitcoin advocates. </p>
