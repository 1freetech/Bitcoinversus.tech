<p style="text-transform:none">In a decisive step signaling the growth and global acceptance of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/13/the-real-merge-the-moment-bitcoin-integrates-with-the-gaming-industry/">Bitcoin</a>, Marathon Digital has announced the initiation of a fresh <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin mining</a> site in Abu Dhabi. The powerhouse behind this venture? None other than the Sovereign Wealth Fund of Saudi Arabia.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Current costs of mining a single Bitcoin hover between $16,000 to $18,000. This figure is set to double by May, leading to speculations around <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">market consolidations</a> if Bitcoin's price does not maintain near the $40,000 threshold.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Further shedding light on the industry's landscape, Thiel stated, “I spent a lot of time in D.C. and New York talking to regulators and Legislators. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/24/bitcoin-vs-xrp-technical-analysis-reveals-bitcoin-is-supreme/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin and crypto</a> have bifurcated in an interesting way. Institutional investors and regulators view Bitcoin as a commodity. This is an asset class that is maturing. It’s fully decentralized. There isn’t a central controlling actor that can do anything with it. And Bitcoin never issued bitcoins, if you would, to raise capital for itself.”</p>
