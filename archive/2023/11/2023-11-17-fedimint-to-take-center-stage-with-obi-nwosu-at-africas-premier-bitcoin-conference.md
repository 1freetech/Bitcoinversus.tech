<p style="font-size:0.9rem"><strong><em>Bitcoin Brightens Africa: Fedimint will Take Center Stage at the Africa Bitcoin Conference</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Africa Bitcoin Community</a> recently announced that Obi Nwosu, the Co-founder and CEO of Fedi, will grace the stage as a key speaker at the upcoming <a href="https://www.afrobitcoin.org/">Africa Bitcoin Conference</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The first Africa Bitcoin conference took place in Accra, Ghana from December 5-7, 2022, with Jack Dorsey as the main highlight. It boasted over 70 speakers and attracted a global audience of over 1,000 people, plus coverage from more than 40 media outlets. the second edition returns to Accra from December 1-3, 2023.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A recognized name in the domain, Obi is at the forefront of global Bitcoin adoption technology and also a fervent advocate for Fedimint. His association with ₿trust, a non-profit entity lead by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/13/block-ceo-jack-dorsey-reveals-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining-with-the-mdk-hashboard/">Jack Dorsey</a> and Jay-Z, further solidifies his position in the ecosystem.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Fedimint operates akin to a custodial lightning wallet, such as the Wallet of Satoshi, but with an upper hand in terms of user privacy and native backup mechanisms. Multiple federated mints are expected to be rolled out across the globe in various community sizes. The beauty of the <a href="https://fedimint.org/docs/GettingStarted/What-is-a-Fedimint#:~:text=A%20Fedimint%20%2D%20or%20%22Federated%20Mint%22%20is,is%20natively%20interoperable%20with%20Bitcoin's%20Lightning%20Network.">Fedimint model</a> lies in its flexibility; it could be perceived as a community bank for a small locality with a booming circular economy or as a myriad of Fedimints operating on the lightning network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The open protocol ushers Bitcoin enthusiasts to form communities, aiding each other in custody, backup solutions, and making lightning payments via a privacy-centric wallet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Fedimint's comprehensive structure consists of functional components, namely accounts, custody &amp; redemption, backup &amp; recovery, transaction processing, and LN Gateway. These functionalities are bolstered by three main personas: the Guardian, User, and Lightning Gateway. A deep dive into the inner workings reveals a sophisticated blend of technology and community-driven ethos, ensuring a seamless and safe <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/15/bitvm-enables-smart-contracts-on-bitcoin/">Bitcoin experience</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With an anticipated boom in Fedimint compatible wallet software like Fedi, the future looks promising for both novice and seasoned Bitcoin users. The initial aim of Fedimint is to offer an ultra-private, federated custodial wallet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The future of Fedimint and its potential impact on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/07/nigeria-emerges-as-a-global-leader-in-bitcoin-awareness/">Africa's Bitcoin landscape</a> is a narrative eagerly awaited, and the Africa Bitcoin Conference promises to be a pivotal moment in this unfolding story.</p>
