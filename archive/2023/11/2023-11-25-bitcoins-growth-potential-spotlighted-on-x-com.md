<p style="text-transform:none">Such comparisons emphasize <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Bitcoin's untapped potential</a>. <p><strong>The "Escape Velocity" of Bitcoin</strong> </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">Max Harvey</a>, another x.com contributor, offers a compelling perspective: “Bitcoin has probably already reached <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">escape velocity</a>. The higher the price goes, the more liquid, desirable, and secure it becomes. The more liquid, desirable, and secure it becomes, the higher the price goes. This feedback loop is unique to the digital asset Bitcoin, and will probably repeat forever, which means the price is probably going to go up forever.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Harvey's statement underscores <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Bitcoin's unique growth dynamics</a>. The continuous upswing in its value reinforces its liquidity, desirability, and security, laying the foundation for an ever-expanding growth cycle.</p>
