<p style="text-decoration:none;text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://www.thebtcsource.com/this-guy-is-building-the-amazon-of-bitcoin-featuring-lawrence-bahr/">recent episode</a> of The Bitcoin Source, host Dawdu M. Amantanah sat down with Lando Bahr, the CEO of ShopInBit. Lando, also known as Lando Rothbardian, offered insights into the meteoric rise of ShopInBit, Europe's leading <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/23/nodal-power-turns-landfill-methane-into-green-energy-for-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a> online marketplace.<br /></p>

<p style="text-decoration:none;text-transform:none">Boasting a staggering inventory of over 800,000 products, ShopInBit not only allows customers to purchase using BTC but also provides a unique concierge service. From obtaining hard-to-find items to booking once-in-a-lifetime vacations, ShopInBit seems poised to redefine online shopping. As put forth in the interview description, ShopInBit invites everyone to "Experience the Amazon of Crypto."</p>

With its vast product range, BTC payment options, and unmatched concierge services, ShopInBit embodies the future of a Bitcoin-centric shopping experience.</p>
