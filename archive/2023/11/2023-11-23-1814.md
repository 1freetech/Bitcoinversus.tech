<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Berkshire Hathaway CEO Warren Buffett has raised eyebrows with one of his latest investment moves. The Oracle of Omaha, once a stern critic of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">Bitcoin</a>, has inadvertently become a benefactor of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/02/the-digital-ides-of-march-bitcoins-defining-moment/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin revolution</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nubank, a Brazilian startup bank, <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/buffett-backed-nubank-posts-record-revenue-strong-user-growth-2023-08-15/">stands out</a> not just for its digital banking solutions but also for enabling its users to engage in bitcoin and other cryptocurrency trades. But Nubank didn't just stop at facilitating trades; they launched their <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/24/bitcoin-vs-xrp-technical-analysis-reveals-bitcoin-is-supreme/">cryptocurrency</a>, nucoin, affirming their stronghold in the crypto domain. This progressive approach made Nubank a star performer on the stock market, eclipsing the returns of Buffett's other heavyweight investments like Amazon, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/bitcoin-appreciates-over-time-relative-to-the-iphone/">Apple</a>, and Coca-Cola.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For many in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">Bitcoin world</a>, Buffett's name often rings a bell for his stark criticism rather than support. His past remarks have labeled Bitcoin as a mere "gambling token" and even went as far as dubbing it "rat poison squared." However, despite his reservations, Berkshire Hathaway's strategic moves seem to be singing a different tune.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Buffett, poured $500 million into Nubank in June 2021, right before the bank's buzzworthy IPO. This bet on Nubank amplified as the bank's shares surged, prompting Buffett to invest another $250 million during the IPO. Today, that initial $500 million stake is currently valued at $840 million, underscoring a lucrative venture into the Bitcoin-friendly financial space.</p>
