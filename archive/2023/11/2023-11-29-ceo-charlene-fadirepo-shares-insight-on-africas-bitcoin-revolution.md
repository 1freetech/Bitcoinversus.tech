<p><em><strong>Charlene Fadirepo is Amplifying Bitcoin's Voice in Nigeria and Beyond</strong></em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a conversation with Dawdu M. Amantanah of The Bitcoin Source, Charlene Fadirepo provided invaluable insights into the rapidly evolving world of Bitcoin in Africa. Fadirepo, Guidefi's founder and CEO, has emerged as a vibrant advocate for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin in Africa</a>.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">With Guidefi, Charlene has addressed the pressing need for financial inclusivity, simplifying the journey for women and professionals of color to identify suitable financial advisors. Venturing further, the Guidefi Academy now presents courses dedicated to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/28/bitcoin-miners-aid-texas-during-heatwave-curtail-energy-consumption-by-90/">Bitcoin</a> and cryptocurrency investments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Charlene's endeavors in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">Bitcoin</a> arena span beyond entrepreneurship. As a sought-after Bitcoin consultant, she assists businesses and non-profit sectors in harmoniously integrating Bitcoin strategies. Boasting a role as a Blockchain &amp; Cryptocurrency Technical advisor for Georgian College, Charlene's voice is increasingly significant in the spheres of financial empowerment and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/17/fedimint-to-take-center-stage-with-obi-nwosu-at-africas-premier-bitcoin-conference/">Bitcoin's role in Africa</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Before her voyage with Guidefi, Charlene fortified the U.S. financial system at the Federal Reserve and offered critical advice to Wall Street banks with PwC in the aftermath of the financial crisis.</p>
