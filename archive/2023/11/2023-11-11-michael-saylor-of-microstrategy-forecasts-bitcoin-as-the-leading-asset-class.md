<p style="text-transform:uppercase"><strong><em>Bitcoin is an Authentic Asset Class</em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With traditional financial institutions slowly gravitating towards the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/10/greg-foss-predicts-bitcoins-potential-rise-to-2-million/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin</a> realm, Saylor's recent statement acts as a testament to Bitcoin's burgeoning potential. The influential entrepreneur believes that the digital currency's recognition as a unique asset class is not just imminent, but transformative.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During a recent interview, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/20/michael-saylor-shares-insight-on-bitcoins-impact-at-btc23-conference-in-germany/">Michael Saylor</a> was quoted saying, "Bitcoin is an asset class and that's a major revolution. If Fidelity and BlackRock and if 10 other ETF issuers all agree that Bitcoin is an asset class, it should be 10x to 100x bigger than it is right now."</p>

<p style="font-size:1rem"><strong><em>A Price Revolution on the Horizon? </em></strong></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The impact of a Bitcoin exchange-traded fund (ETF) on the currency's valuation has been a topic of considerable debate in the crypto community. Saylor's recent comments bring to light the enormity of this potential impact. He suggests that the establishment of an ETF could magnify Bitcoin's value from 10 to 100 times its current value, potentially placing each coin in the impressive range of $250K to an astounding $2.5M.</p>
