<p style="text-transform:none">In its young lifetime, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">Bitcoin</a> has seen its share of ups and downs. But as 2023 unfolds, the cryptocurrency demonstrates a new phase of growth and stability, rivaling traditional market giants.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Most Bitcoin price charts tell a story of either growth or volatility. However, a unique perspective emerges when we combine the two. In its first 13 years, Bitcoin became notorious for its dramatic price swings. But since the fall of 2023, a new narrative is being written. Today, Bitcoin's volatility has subdued, making it less volatile than the S&amp;P 500. This newfound stability is partly because about 40% of Bitcoin holders are now long-term investors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">2023 has been a pivotal year for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/23/blackrocks-indirect-bitcoin-investment-through-microstrategy/">Bitcoin</a>. Starting in January, the cryptocurrency began a remarkable journey, rising by 83% to touch $31,035 by April 10. This wasn't just a regular price increase; it represented Bitcoin breaking the formidable $30,000 resistance level.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">From April to July, Bitcoin confidently traded around the $30,000 mark, reaching out to almost $32,000. The positive sentiment was buoyed by news of major financial institutions like BlackRock and Fidelity applying for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/30/landmark-ruling-opens-doors-for-future-bitcoin-etfs/">spot Bitcoin ETFs</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">But the journey wasn't without its obstacles. Mid-August saw Bitcoin's price drop from $29,000 to a two-month low of $26,000 within just 12 hours. This sharp decline, the biggest one-day drop since November 2022, was linked by some to reports of Elon Musk's SpaceX selling its Bitcoin stash.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/02/bitcoin-posts-gains-in-september-igniting-hopes-for-a-bullish-october/">September</a> too had its moments, with Bitcoin approaching the $25,000 line but never truly breaking it. Looking back, the cryptocurrency's price danced between $26,000 and $29,500, even surpassing the $30,000 mark to 34,000. Yet, the key takeaway remains: Bitcoin closed October with 25%+ gains.</p>
