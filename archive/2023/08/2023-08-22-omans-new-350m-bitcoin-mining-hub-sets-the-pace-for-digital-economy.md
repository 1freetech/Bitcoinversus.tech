<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Salalah Free Zone Emerges as Oman’s Epicenter for Bitcoin Mining and Data Hosting</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As global economies steadily turn their gaze towards<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/11/bitcoin-vs-paypal-new-company-ventures-verify-bitcoins-significance/"> digital currencies </a>and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/21/revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-how-ai-and-quantum-computing-meet/">blockchain technologies</a>, Oman asserts its presence on the world stage. In a <a href="https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1141405/business/oman-attracts-370-million-investment-in-new-data-hosting-crypto-mining-centre">report</a> by the Oman Daily Observer,  The Ministry of Transport, Communications, and Information Technology (MTCIT) has unveiled a groundbreaking <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">Bitcoin mining</a> and data hosting center, nestled in the heart of the Salalah Free Zone. This move cements Oman's position as a burgeoning hub for cryptocurrency activities, building on the momentum from a similar venture announced last year.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Second Cryptocurrency Mining Hub in Play</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Following the inauguration of the $390 million facility in November 2022, the latest center marks Oman’s second massive investment in the cryptocurrency domain. With a combined worth of $740 million in these projects, the nation is paving the way to not only fortify its digital economy but also open doors to countless job opportunities for its citizens.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Exahertz International at the Forefront</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Driving this transition is Exahertz International. Responsible for setting up these state-of-the-art centers, the company already has over 2,000 operational machines fueling this ambitious endeavor. Their plan is audacious; by the close of the year, the infrastructure will be augmented by an additional 15,000 devices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CEO Sam Fedows of Exahertz International holds an expansive vision. Following a triumphant trial run in the Salalah Free Zone, he accentuates the possibility of Oman witnessing an explosion of blockchain-based hubs. He points to Oman's rich resources - both energy and labor - as factors propelling this vision into a reality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Global Reach and Future Prospects</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As Oman strides confidently in the crypto arena, other nations, like Kyrgyzstan, are making similar advancements. With a $20 million investment alongside the Kambar-Ata-2 Hydro Power Plant, Kyrgyzstan showcases the universal tilt towards sustainable energy sources in cryptocurrency mining.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Looking ahead, Exahertz International is eyeing expansion in pivotal regions such as the Governorates of Al Jabal Al Akhtar and Al Dakhiliya, emphasizing broader access to their avant-garde facilities.</p>
