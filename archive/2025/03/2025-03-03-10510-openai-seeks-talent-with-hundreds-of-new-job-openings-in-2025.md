<p style="text-transform:none">​OpenAI is actively expanding its global workforce, with 312 job openings across various departments as of March 2025. The organization's <a href="https://openai.com/careers/search/">careers page</a> lists positions in engineering, research, operations, and policy, reflecting its commitment to advancing artificial intelligence responsibly. ​</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">In a strategic move to bolster its presence in the Asia-Pacific region, OpenAI <a href="https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-to-open-asia-pacific-hub-in-singapore-3f26eb32">announced </a>plans to establish a new office in Singapore. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This hub aims to enhance collaborations with governments and businesses, offering robust support to customers in the area. The expansion underscores OpenAI's dedication to tapping into local talent and strengthening its global footprint. ​</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">OpenAI's <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/trump-announces-usd500b-ai-joint-venture-with-oracle-openai-and-softbank-8778027">growth strategy</a> also includes significant partnerships to enhance AI infrastructure. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The competitive landscape for AI talent is intensifying, with companies vying for top researchers and engineers. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">OpenAI's recent hiring of three senior engineers from Google DeepMind to join its Zurich office exemplifies this trend. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These strategic hires are set to advance OpenAI's work in multimodal AI, integrating capabilities across various mediums such as images and audio. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">OpenAI's global expansion includes plans for new offices in cities like New York, Seattle, Brussels, Paris, and Singapore, furthering its presence beyond its headquarters in San Francisco. </p>
