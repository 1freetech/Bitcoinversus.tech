<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/25/coinbase-completes-first-ai-to-ai-crypto-transaction/">Coinbase</a> CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/coinbase-envisions-crypto-driving-20-of-the-global-gdp/">Brian Armstrong</a> attended the first-ever White House <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/08/michael-saylor-attends-white-house-digital-assets-summit/">crypto summit</a> and praised President Donald Trump’s efforts to revitalize the industry. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In an interview with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/microstrategys-bitcoin-bet-pays-off-says-yahoo-finance/">Yahoo Finance</a> Senior Reporter Jennifer Schonberger, Armstrong stated, </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"President Trump really breathed life back into this industry over the last few years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">We really felt like we were being unlawfully attacked by the prior administration... The president has taken a leadership role here and decided to bring it back to the US, and it's already having positive results."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The comments come as the U.S. government explores policies to support digital assets and blockchain innovation. The White House summit signals a shift in regulatory sentiment, with industry leaders welcoming a more pro-crypto stance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Armstrong’s <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com">remarks </a>highlight growing confidence in the U.S. as a competitive hub for crypto firms following years of regulatory pressure.</p>
