<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent development, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/bitcoins-future-price-according-to-industry-experts/">Tyler Winklevoss</a>, co-founder of the cryptocurrency exchange Gemini, <a href="https://followin.io/en/feed/16632362">expressed reservations</a> about including certain digital assets in the proposed U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/03/adam-backs-post-on-twitter-reveals-true-sentiment-of-bitmerica-regarding-trumps-strategic-crypto-reserve/">Crypto Strategic Reserve</a>. He emphasized that only Bitcoin meets the necessary criteria for such a reserve. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Winklevoss elaborated that an asset suitable for a strategic reserve should embody characteristics of hard money and serve as a proven store of value. He suggested that many existing crypto projects do not meet these standards, reinforcing his stance that Bitcoin is the sole digital asset currently fitting this role. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10620,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-3.png?w=597" alt="" class="wp-image-10620" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Similarly, Cameron Winklevoss, Tyler's twin brother and fellow co-founder of Gemini, echoed these sentiments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He <a href="https://www.mitrade.com/insights/news/live-news/article-3-672590-20250304">described</a> Bitcoin as the only asset that qualifies as a store of value reserve asset, with Ethereum potentially being an exception due to its unique properties.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://bsky.app/profile/bitcoinversus.bsky.social/post/3lfxg2mzcs22l","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"bluesky-embed"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Winklevoss twins' comments come in response to President Donald Trump's recent announcement of a "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/03/strategy-chairman-michael-saylor-advocates-for-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">Crypto Strategic Reserve</a>," which aims to position the United States as the "Crypto Capital of the World." The proposed reserve includes cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana, and Cardano. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the inclusion of assets like XRP and Cardano has sparked debate within the crypto community. Critics argue that these assets lack the real-world utility and adoption seen with Bitcoin and Ethereum, questioning their suitability for a national reserve. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Market reactions to the announcement have been mixed. While Bitcoin experienced a surge following the news, it later faced a pullback, reflecting the market's cautious stance amid ongoing discussions about the reserve's composition and implications.</p>
