<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/politics-donald-trump-proposes-crypto-advisory-council/">President Donald Trump</a> announced <a href="https://www.businessinsider.com/crypto-trump-strategic-reserve-bitcoin-ethereum-ripple-solana-cardano-2025-3">the establishment</a> of a U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/08/bitcoin-strategic-reserve-bill-proposal-by-wyoming-senator-cynthia-lummis/">Crypto Strategic Reserve</a>, aiming to position the United States as the "crypto capital of the world." The reserve is set to include major cryptocurrencies such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/22/gamestop-explores-bitcoin-investment-amid-strategic-shift/">Bitcoin</a> (BTC), <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">Ethereum</a> (ETH), <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ripple-ceo-predicts-inevitable-approval-of-xrp-etf/">Ripple</a> (XRP), <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/21/hackers-use-mcdonalds-instagram-for-grimace-crypto-scam/">Solana</a> (SOL), and Cardano (ADA). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The announcement led to a significant surge in cryptocurrency markets. Bitcoin's price rose by over 10%, reaching approximately $94,000, while Ethereum saw a 13% increase, trading around $2,516. Other included cryptocurrencies, such as XRP, SOL, and ADA, also experienced notable gains. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the inclusion of multiple cryptocurrencies in the reserve has sparked debate within the crypto community. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Prominent figures, including Bitcoin maximalists, argue that the reserve should focus solely on Bitcoin due to its established position and decentralized nature. Some have criticized the inclusion of other tokens, referring to them as "sh*tcoins," and expressing concerns over their long-term value and centralization aspects. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Adam Back, CEO of Blockstream and a notable figure in the Bitcoin community, has previously highlighted President Trump's significant holdings in Bitcoin, suggesting a strong personal interest in the cryptocurrency. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The establishment of the Crypto Strategic Reserve marks a pivotal shift in U.S. financial policy, reflecting a growing acceptance of digital assets at the federal level. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the initiative progresses, discussions about the composition and management of the reserve are expected to continue, influencing the future trajectory of the cryptocurrency market.​</p>
