<p style="text-transform:none">The song features a notable collaboration with Jadakiss, adding a distinctive edge to the composition.​</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">DJ Premier's production on "Rite Where U Stand" is a testament to his ability to blend classic soul elements with gritty hip-hop beats. </p>
