<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the ever-evolving world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">cryptocurrency mining</a>, the Bitmain <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/theory-antminer-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">Antminer S21e XP Hyd 3U</a> emerges as a dominant force, delivering a staggering 860 Th/s on the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">SHA-256 algorithm</a>, compared to the IceRiver AL3's modest 15 Th/s on the Blake3 algorithm. The computational power difference is a mind-blowing 5,300%, making <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/theory-antminer-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">Bitmain’s latest machine</a> the clear winner for Bitcoin mining enthusiasts.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The IceRiver AL3, with a power <a href="https://pool.kryptex.com/articles/iceriver-al3-en">consumption</a> of 3,500W, is primarily focused on mining Alephium (ALPH) using the Blake3 algorithm. While profitable in its own right, generating approximately $48.66 per day, it caters to a niche mining audience​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This machine stands out for miners interested in diversifying away from Bitcoin into other proof-of-work coins like Alephium​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the Antminer S21e XP Hyd 3U takes mining to another level, specifically for Bitcoin, where SHA-256 reigns supreme. With 11,180W of power consumption, the Antminer produces roughly $28.78 per day in mining rewards. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While its profitability may seem lower, the real advantage lies in its massive computational output, which dominates the Bitcoin mining sector. Its advanced liquid cooling system and optimized efficiency allow it to maintain peak performance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This showdown highlights the computational gap between the two miners. For those focused on Bitcoin, the Antminer S21e XP Hyd 3U is the unrivaled choice, whereas the IceRiver AL3 serves a smaller but growing segment interested in alephium mining. The key takeaway? </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">When it comes to raw power, the Antminer S21e XP Hyd 3U leads by a significant margin, making it the go-to machine for large-scale <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/12/swan-bitcoin-launches-mining-operations-and-eyes-public-listing/">Bitcoin operations</a>.</p>
