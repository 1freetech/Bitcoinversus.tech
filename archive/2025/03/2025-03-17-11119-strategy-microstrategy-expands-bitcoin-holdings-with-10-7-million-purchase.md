<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/03/strategy-chairman-michael-saylor-advocates-for-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">​Strategy</a>, formerly known as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/microstrategy-to-raise-700-million-for-bitcoin-acquisition/">MicroStrategy</a>, has <a href="https://www.ainvest.com/news/strategy-expands-bitcoin-holdings-130-btc-market-fluctuations-2503">expanded</a> its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a> by acquiring an additional 130 BTC. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This purchase, valued at approximately $10.7 million, was executed at an average price of $82,981 per BTC between March 10 and March 16, 2025. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to the company's latest SEC filing, the acquisition was funded through the sale of 123,000 shares of its 8.00% Series A perpetual preferred stock (STRK), generating approximately $10.7 million in net proceeds. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Notably, no Class A common stock was sold during this period. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://www.ainvest.com/news/strategy-expands-bitcoin-holdings-130-btc-market-fluctuations-2503">recent purchase</a> underscores Strategy's ongoing commitment to its Bitcoin acquisition strategy. The company now holds approximately 499,226 BTC, representing about 2.3% of the total Bitcoin supply. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In February 2025, Strategy completed a $2 billion offering of 0% convertible senior notes due 2030, with net proceeds intended for general corporate purposes, including further Bitcoin acquisitions. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Michael Saylor, co-founder of Strategy, has been a vocal advocate for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/22/gamestop-explores-bitcoin-investment-amid-strategic-shift/">Bitcoin</a>, positioning the company as a major institutional holder of the cryptocurrency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This strategic approach has significantly influenced the company's market valuation and stock performance. ​</p>
