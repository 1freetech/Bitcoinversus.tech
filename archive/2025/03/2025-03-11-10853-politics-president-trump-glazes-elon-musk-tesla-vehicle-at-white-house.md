<p style="text-transform:none">President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump</a> showcased several Tesla models at the White House on Tuesday afternoon, <a href="https://www.theverge.com/tesla/627894/tesla-stock-sales-protest-musk-trump-doge">expressing his intention</a> to purchase a Tesla vehicle. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This event occurred amid a significant decline in Tesla's stock value, which has fallen by 50% since December 2024. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Standing alongside <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/politics-donald-trump-announces-elon-musk-will-lead-government-efficiency-commission/">Tesla CEO Elon Musk</a>, President Trump inspected various Tesla models, including the Model S, Cybertruck, and Model Y, displayed on the White House lawn. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He praised Musk's dedication to innovation and <a href="https://newrepublic.com/post/192623/donald-trump-elon-musk-tesla-commercial-white-house">announced plans</a> to acquire a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/elon-musks-optimus-robot-set-for-mass-adoption-at-20k-30k-price/">Tesla</a>, stating, "I'm going to buy because, number one, it's a great product... And number two, because this man has devoted his energy and his life to doing this."​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tesla's <a href="https://m.economictimes.com/news/international/us/tesla-stock-price-drop-on-march-11-2025-why-tsla-shares-fell-elon-musks-response-and-future-outlook/articleshow/118893552.cms">recent stock decline</a> has been attributed to several factors, including weak global sales performance and concerns over Musk's involvement in the U.S. Department of Government Efficiency. In Germany, Tesla's deliveries plummeted by 59%, marking the lowest figures since 2021, while sales in China decreased by 11.5% year-over-year. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite these challenges, Musk <a href="https://m.economictimes.com/news/international/us/tesla-stock-price-drop-on-march-11-2025-why-tsla-shares-fell-elon-musks-response-and-future-outlook/articleshow/118893552.cms">remains</a> optimistic about Tesla's future. He acknowledged the recent stock downturn but reassured investors by stating, "It will be fine," during a press briefing. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">President Trump's <a href="https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/trump-hater-of-evs-says-hes-buying-a-tesla-66553ef8">public endorsement</a> of Tesla and Musk comes at a critical time for the company. The President's support aims to bolster confidence in Tesla amid market volatility and public protests against Musk's political associations. ​</p>
