<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Fred Thiel, CEO of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a> (MARA), recently <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2Xeavooa8c">acknowledged</a> the Trump administration's efforts in promoting <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/05/uk-to-recognize-cryptocurrency-as-personal-property/">cryptocurrency adoption</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In an interview with Fox Business, Thiel <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2Xeavooa8c">highlighted</a> how current policies have positioned the United States to become a dominant force in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/15/energy-bitcoin-mining-shutdown-in-norway-causes-power-bills-to-surge-by-300-per-household/">Bitcoin mining</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/california-dmv-digitizes-car-titles-with-blockchain-technology/">blockchain technology</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Thiel, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin miners</a> possess the unique advantage of being able to "shut off anytime without affecting operations," providing a substantial benefit by allowing power generators to effectively balance electricity supply on the grid.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/13/marathon-digital-invests-100-million-in-bitcoin/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a> is a leading Bitcoin mining company that has significantly expanded its operations in recent years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In November 2024, the company <a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1375/mara-adds-372-megawatts-of-capacity-in-ohio-with-152-megawatts-operational-and-220-megawatts-in-development?utm_source=chatgpt.com">acquired two operational</a> data centers in Hannibal and Hopedale, Ohio, adding 222 megawatts of interconnect-approved capacity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, Marathon <a href="https://www.mara.com/posts/mara-announces-25-megawatt-micro-data-center-project-powered-by-excess-natural-gas-from-oilfields">initiated</a> the development of a 150-megawatt facility in Findlay, Ohio. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These expansions have increased the company's total compute capacity by over 70%, bringing its total nameplate capacity to nearly 1.5 gigawatts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In its pursuit of sustainable mining solutions, Marathon launched a 25 MW data center operation powered exclusively by excess <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/natural-gas-finds-new-value-in-bitcoin-mining-operations/">natural gas</a> from oilfield production. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative, in partnership with NGON, aims to transform excess gas into electricity for on-site data centers, thereby reducing methane emissions and improving the sustainability of oil production. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Furthermore, Marathon has embarked on a pilot project in Utah, utilizing methane gas generated from landfill waste to power <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin mining operations</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This project <a href="https://www.mara.com/posts/marathon-digital-ceo-on-testing-btc-mining-with-methane-gas-from-waste-landfill">reflects</a> the company's commitment to integrating renewable energy sources into its mining activities, aligning with broader environmental goals. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Trump administration's pro-crypto stance has further bolstered the industry's prospects. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Recent <a href="https://www.cryptoninjas.net/news/trump-administration-seeks-to-build-largest-u-s-bitcoin-reserve/">reports</a> indicate that the administration aims to acquire substantial amounts of Bitcoin to establish a national strategic reserve, signaling strong institutional support for digital assets. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Marathon Digital Holdings' strategic expansions and innovative approaches to sustainable mining position it at the forefront of the evolving cryptocurrency landscape. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's alignment with supportive governmental policies and commitment to environmental sustainability underscore its leadership in the sector.</p>
