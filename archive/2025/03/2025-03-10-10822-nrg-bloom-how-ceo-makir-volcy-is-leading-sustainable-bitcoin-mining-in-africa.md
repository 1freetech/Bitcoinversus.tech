<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.nrgbloom.com/team/makir-volcy">NRG Bloom</a>, a Canadian company co-founded and led by CEO Makir Volcy, is pioneering sustainable <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">Bitcoin mining solutions in Africa</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company repurposes <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/28/texas-natural-gas-producers-boost-profits-with-bitcoin-mining/">flared gas</a> and stranded energy to power its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/jack-dorseys-block-inc-expands-bitcoin-mining-operations/">mining operations</a>, addressing environmental concerns while fostering technological advancement. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Headquartered in Quebec, NRG Bloom <a href="https://www.euroquity.com/en/company/nrg-bloom">has established</a> a Bitcoin mining operation in Nigeria, utilizing flared gas to generate electricity for its data centers. This approach not only mitigates environmental pollution but also transforms wasted resources into economic opportunities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-GqbgqxEW0","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-4-3 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The company's mission extends beyond mining; it aims to support prosperity and growth in rural African communities by introducing scalable, efficient, and sustainable energy solutions. NRG Bloom's initiatives align with global efforts to reduce gas flaring and promote renewable energy adoption.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">In collaboration with local partners, NRG Bloom is building a Bitcoin mining hub in Nigeria, deepening community ties and transforming local economies. The company's operations exemplify how technology can drive sustainable development in emerging markets. ​</p>
