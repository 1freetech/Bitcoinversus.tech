<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/coinbase-envisions-crypto-driving-20-of-the-global-gdp/">Coinbase</a>, the leading U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/01/yellow-card-is-the-largest-bitcoin-exchange-in-africa/">cryptocurrency</a> exchange, <a href="https://www.facebook.com/cointelegraph/posts/-now-coinbase-ceo-brian-armstrong-announced-plans-to-hire-1000-us-employees-in-2/958988326407996/">plans to expand</a> its workforce by approximately 1,000 employees in 2025. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/13/coinbase-ceo-brian-armstrong-praises-trumps-crypto-leadership-at-white-house-summit/">CEO Brian Armstrong</a> attributes this expansion to the pro-crypto policies of President Donald Trump's administration. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Armstrong announced the hiring initiative following the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/08/michael-saylor-attends-white-house-digital-assets-summit/">White House Crypto Summit</a>, emphasizing the administration's supportive stance on digital assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He noted that President Trump's actions have revitalized the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/16/japan-proposes-20-flat-tax-on-crypto-transactions/">crypto industry</a>, prompting Coinbase's decision to bolster its U.S. operations. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The planned hiring spree represents a significant increase in Coinbase's workforce, reflecting the company's confidence in the growing cryptocurrency market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move aligns with the administration's efforts to <a href="https://icoholder.com/en/news/coinbase-to-hire-1-000-employees-amid-pro-crypto-shift">position</a> the United States as a leader in the digital asset space. ​</p>
