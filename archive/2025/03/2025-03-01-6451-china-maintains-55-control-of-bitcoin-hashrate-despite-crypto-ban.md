<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite China's 2021 ban on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">cryptocurrency minin</a>g and trading, the nation <a href="https://www.crypto-news-flash.com/chinas-bitcoin-ban-still-55-of-btc-mining-hashrate-under-its-control-will-it-unban-btc/">continues</a> to dominate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Bitcoin mining operations</a>, controlling an estimated 55% of the global <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin hashrate</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This ongoing <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/chinese-bitcoin-miners-control-55-of-network">influence</a> highlights the resilience of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/theory-antminer-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">Chinese miners</a>, who have adapted by either moving their operations underground or relocating to crypto-friendly regions nearby, like Sichuan, which boasts abundant hydroelectric power.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This dominance remains surprising, considering the strict regulations China has enforced against crypto activities. Chinese <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/12/analyzing-centralization-and-monopolies-in-the-bitcoin-industry/">mining pools</a> cater to smaller, more localized miners, while U.S.-based mining firms, which hold about 40% of the global hashrate, are increasingly serving large institutional investors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">There are growing discussions within the crypto community that China might reconsider its ban on Bitcoin by 2025. The nation plans to integrate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency transactions</a> into its Anti-Money Laundering regulations, which could potentially lead to a more regulated but open approach to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">crypto mining</a>​.</p>
