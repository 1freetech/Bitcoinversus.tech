<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/cleanspark-completes-acquisition-of-griid-infrastructure-in-155m-all-stock-transaction/">​CleanSpark, Inc</a>. <!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This inclusion signifies a notable milestone for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/cleanspark-purchases-avalon-a1566i-miners-from-canaan/">CleanSpark</a>, reflecting its consistent performance and growth within the cryptocurrency mining sector.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The S&amp;P SmallCap 600 index measures the small-cap segment of the U.S. equity market, encompassing companies that meet specific financial criteria, <a href="https://cryptohead.io/news/cleansparks-soaring-profits-secure-spot-in-sp-smallcap-600-index/">including</a> profitability and market capitalization. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark's addition to this index not only enhances its visibility within the investment community but also underscores its position as a vertically integrated Bitcoin mining company. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Over the past year, CleanSpark has expanded its operations, including strategic acquisitions and infrastructure development, to enhance its mining capabilities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's focus on energy-efficient mining practices aligns with the growing emphasis on sustainability within the industry. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark's inclusion in the S&amp;P SmallCap 600 index is expected to attract increased attention from institutional investors and may lead to greater liquidity and trading volume for its stock.</p>
