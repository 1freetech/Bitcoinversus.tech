<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>IPv6</strong> is the latest version of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/08/understanding-network-ports-and-their-importance-in-the-it-industry/">Internet Protocol</a>, designed to replace IPv4 due to the exhaustion of available IPv4 addresses. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">IPv6 provides a vastly larger address space by using 128-bit addresses instead of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/15/comparing-cpu-architectures-x64-x86-risc/">32-bit format</a> used by IPv4. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This means IPv6 can support approximately 340 undecillion (3.4 x 10³⁸) unique addresses, which is enough to assign an IP address to every device on earth and beyond. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Besides the larger address space, IPv6 introduces improvements like simplified packet headers, built-in security features (IPSec), and better support for mobile devices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3VIgP9aosY\u0026amp;pp=ygUNaXB2NiBvdmVydmlldw%3D%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">IPv6 operates alongside IPv4 during the transition period, as many networks and devices still rely on IPv4. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, IPv6 is increasingly important for modern networking due to the explosive growth of internet-connected devices, including smartphones, IoT devices, and smart home appliances. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oItwDXraK1M\u0026amp;t=344s\u0026amp;pp=ygUNaXB2NiBvdmVydmlldw%3D%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">IPv6 also supports more efficient routing and reduces the need for Network Address Translation (NAT), allowing devices to communicate more directly over the internet. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As more organizations adopt IPv6, it plays a crucial role in ensuring the continued growth and scalability of the global internet.</p>
