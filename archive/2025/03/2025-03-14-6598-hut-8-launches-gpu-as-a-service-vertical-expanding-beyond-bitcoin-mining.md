<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> giant Hut 8 is <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/hut-8-gpu-service-program-ai-developers">diversifying</a> its revenue streams with the launch of its GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS) program. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Officially <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-09-26/hut-bitcoin-gpu-ai/">announce</a>d on September 26, 2024, this initiative offers high-performance computing solutions to AI developers and cloud computing firms, solidifying Hut 8’s push into the AI space. The program, developed in partnership with AdvizeX, utilizes 1,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs housed in a tier-three <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/17/how-to-manage-inventory-at-a-data-center-salesforce-edition/">data center</a> in Chicago.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Hut 8's shift into AI and cloud computing stems from the increasing challenges faced by traditional <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin miners</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">mining costs</a> rising dramatically due to surges in energy prices and higher network difficulty, Hut 8 aims to offset these hurdles by leveraging its computing infrastructure for new verticals like AI​.</p>
