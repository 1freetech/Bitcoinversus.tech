<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">​<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/09/bitfarms-earned-236-btc-in-october/">Bitfarms Ltd.</a> (NASDAQ/TSX: BITF), a global Bitcoin mining and data center company, <a href="https://investor.bitfarms.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/291/bitfarms-advances-u-s-strategy-with-completion-of">has completed</a> its acquisition of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/27/bitfarms-acquires-stronghold-digital-in-175-million-deal/">Stronghold Digital Mining</a>, Inc. (SDIG), significantly enhancing its U.S. operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/27/bitfarms-acquires-stronghold-digital-in-175-million-deal/">acquisition</a> increases Bitfarms' energy portfolio to 623 megawatts under management, adding 165 MW of active generating capacity and 142 MW of available import capacity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It also <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/03/17/3043573/0/en/Bitfarms-Advances-U-S-Strategy-with-Completion-of-Stronghold-Digital-Mining-Acquisition.html">secures</a> a 1.1-gigawatt growth pipeline in Pennsylvania, positioning Bitfarms as a leading Bitcoin miner in the PJM market. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The integration of Stronghold's assets aligns with Bitfarms' strategy to expand its North American footprint and explore high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) opportunities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company <a href="https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/bitfarms-boosts-ai-and-bitcoin-mining-with-stronghold-acquisition/">plans</a> to develop two power campuses totaling nearly one gigawatt for HPC and AI applications, leveraging strategic partnerships to prioritize these sites for potential conversion. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitfarms' CEO, Ben Gagnon, expressed enthusiasm about the acquisition, stating that it marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for the company. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He <a href="https://investor.bitfarms.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/291/bitfarms-advances-u-s-strategy-with-completion-of">highlighted</a> that the combined assets provide a strong foundation for executing Bitfarms' U.S. strategy and developing scalable HPC/AI businesses. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The transaction was structured as a stock-for-stock merger, with Stronghold shareholders receiving 2.52 shares of Bitfarms for each Stronghold share. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Approximately 59.7 million Bitfarms common shares and 10.6 million warrants were issued, and $44.5 million was paid to retire Stronghold's outstanding loans. Following the merger, Stronghold's common stock ceased trading on Nasdaq</p>
