<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>​The recent release of <strong>AxeOS Bitaxe 2.6.1</strong> <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/21/bios-basic-input-output-system-overview/">firmware</a> introduces several enhancements aimed at improving the user experience for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/28/hydro-cooled-bitaxe-miner-enhances-bitcoin-mining-efficiency/">Bitaxe miners</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11718,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/screenshot-2025-03-31-083952.png?w=629" alt="" class="wp-image-11718" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This update focuses on refining the options on the menu. New modules such as "white paper" and "customization" are available. They also offer the color orange for the background, the latest color added. Instead of "settings" they make the tab much more specific with "Pool Settings" so you know exactly what you're configuring. These are all nice touches to the ever-evolving open-source bitcoin mining universe. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Community feedback has been instrumental in shaping this update. Users have shared their experiences and optimization scripts, contributing to the ongoing improvement of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/proof-of-work-bitcoin-podcaster-builds-custom-dashboard-for-bitaxe-community/">Bitaxe ecosystem</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>To update your Bitaxe device to AxeOS 2.6.1, it's recommended to use the <strong>Bitaxe Web Flasher</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This web-based tool simplifies the firmware flashing process, ensuring that your device is equipped with the latest features and improvements. Detailed instructions and the tool itself are available on GitHub.</p>
