<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/01/michael-saylor-faces-backlash-over-bitcoin-self-custody-stance/">Michael Saylor</a>, founder and executive chairman of Strategy, <a href="https://crypto.news/michael-saylor-invited-to-white-house-digital-assets-summit/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">attended</a> the White House <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/04/politics-president-donald-trump-announces-us-crypto-strategic-reserve/">Digital Assets Summit</a></strong> on March 7, hosted by President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump</a>. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The event brought together industry leaders, investors, and policymakers to discuss the future of digital assets in the United States. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The discussions focused on the role of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/05/uk-to-recognize-cryptocurrency-as-personal-property/">cryptocurrency</a> in national finance, regulatory policies, and the broader economic impact of blockchain innovation.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
