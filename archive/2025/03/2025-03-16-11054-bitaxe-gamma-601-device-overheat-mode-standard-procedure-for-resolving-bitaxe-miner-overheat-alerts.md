<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">When your <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/20/bitaxe-overclock-increases-hashrate-by-35-reduces-energy-consumption-by-24-mining-engineer-report/">Bitaxe Gamma 601</a> periodically overheats, you'll notice an indicator in your <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/25/how-to-fix-a-bricked-suprahex/">firmware</a> UI and a "device overheat" message displayed on the miner's LED screen.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11061,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/device-overheat-mode.jpeg?w=808" alt="" class="wp-image-11061" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Don't worry; Resolving it is straightforward.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">First, open your firmware "settings." </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11072,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-17.png?w=447" alt="" class="wp-image-11072" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Make sure to reset the frequency and core voltage to the default values recommended for your ASIC chip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11066,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/original-overheat-pic-axe-os-e1742165440125.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-11066" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Typically, for the BM 1370 Chip, this means setting your Frequency to '525 (default)' and Core Voltage to '1150 (default).'</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Some chips will have a different setting, slightly lower or higher.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It ultimately depends on the vendor that you buy from or - if your building it yourself, then you'll obviously adjust those settings accordingly. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Click the red "Disable Overheat Mode" button.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11073,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-18.png?w=388" alt="" class="wp-image-11073" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Click "Save" and then "Restart" your bitaxe Machine </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11075,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/firmware-chip-settings-axe-os-e1742166946909.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-11075" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Regardless of the Bitaxe model, I recommend sticking with the 'default' frequency and voltage ratings for your Bitaxe machine until you get comfortable with adjusting any settings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">After restarting, your device will exit overheat mode and return to normal operation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
