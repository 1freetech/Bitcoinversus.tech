<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Tom Lee</a>, co-founder and managing partner of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/08/bitcoin-price-predicted-to-reach-150000-by-year-end-says-fundstrats-tom-lee/">Fundstrat Global Advisors</a>, <a href="https://coingape.com/fundstrats-tom-lee-predicts-bitcoin-price-will-hit-150k-in-2025/">recently</a> shared his optimistic outlook on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/bitcoins-future-price-according-to-industry-experts/">Bitcoin's price trajectory</a> for 2025. <br><br>In an interview on CNBC's 'Squawk Box,' Lee expressed confidence that Bitcoin will surpass the $150,000 mark this year, attributing this anticipated growth to increasing institutional adoption and broader acceptance. ​</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Lee <a href="https://u.today/bitcoin-to-do-better-than-150000-this-year-fundstrat-expert-tom-lee">highlighted</a> that Bitcoin's growing mainstream acceptance is a significant factor driving its potential price surge. He noted that as major financial entities, such as Citadel Securities, begin trading Bitcoin, the cryptocurrency's legitimacy and appeal are further solidified. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Addressing the recent market volatility, where Bitcoin experienced a notable downturn in February 2025, Lee described this fluctuation as a typical cyclical correction. He emphasized that such price movements are inherent to Bitcoin's nature and should not deter long-term investors. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Looking ahead, Lee <a href="https://www.investing.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/bitcoin-price-to-hit-250000-by-the-end-of-2025-tom-lee-3812075">maintains</a> an even more bullish stance for Bitcoin's future. He projects that by the end of 2025, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/22/gamestop-explores-bitcoin-investment-amid-strategic-shift/">Bitcoin</a> could reach prices between $200,000 and $250,000, driven by favorable regulatory environments and the upcoming Bitcoin halving event, which historically has influenced price increases. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lee's predictions are echoed by other industry experts. For instance, Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan has also forecasted that Bitcoin prices could cross the $100,000 threshold by the end of this year, underscoring a broader bullish sentiment within the cryptocurrency market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of March 3, 2025, Bitcoin is trading at approximately $86,751, reflecting a 7.32% decrease from the previous close. The cryptocurrency reached an intraday high of $94,878 and a low of $86,548, indicating ongoing volatility.​<a href="https://www.investing.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/bitcoin-price-to-hit-250000-by-the-end-of-2025-tom-lee-3812075" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Investing</a></p>
