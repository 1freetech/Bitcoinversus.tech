<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In computer networking, a port is a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_(computer_networking)">virtual endpoint</a> for communication, identified by a unique 16-bit number ranging from 0 to 65535. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ports enable multiple network services to operate simultaneously on a single device by directing incoming and outgoing traffic to the appropriate applications. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA) categorizes ports into three ranges: well-known ports (0–1023), registered ports (1024–49151), and dynamic or private ports (49152–65535). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Well-known ports are assigned to standard services such as HTTP (port 80), HTTPS (port 443), SSH (port 22), and DNS (port 53), ensuring consistency across networks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1uAjsK9H2hI","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Registered ports are used by specific applications, while dynamic ports are typically assigned temporarily by the operating system for client-side connections.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Understanding network ports is crucial for IT professionals because ports are fundamental to network security, troubleshooting, and service configuration. Firewalls often filter traffic based on port numbers, allowing administrators to permit or block specific services.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For example, blocking unused ports reduces the attack surface of a system, while opening required ports ensures applications can communicate properly. Network engineers also use port information when diagnosing connectivity issues, configuring routers, and managing servers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A strong understanding of ports is especially important for CompTIA certifications and entry-level IT roles, where recognizing common port numbers is a core networking skill.</p>
