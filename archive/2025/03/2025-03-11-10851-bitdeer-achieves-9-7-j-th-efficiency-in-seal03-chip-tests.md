<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/13/bitdeer-reaches-13-5-j-th-breakthrough-with-seal02-bitcoin-asic-mining-chip/">​Bitdeer Technologies Group</a> (NASDAQ: BTDR) has announced the successful testing of its latest <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin mining chip</a>, SealMiner A3, achieving an exceptional power efficiency of 9.7 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/nerdqaxe-achieves-4-8th-s-with-6-layer-pcb-upgrade/">joules per terahash</a> (J/TH) in low voltage, ultra power-saving mode.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This advancement aligns with the company's SEALMINER technology roadmap and reflects its commitment to enhancing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/18/antminer-s21-pro-leads-bitcoin-mining-efficiency-rankings/">mining efficiency</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Developed in collaboration with Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) using advanced process nodes, SEAL03 will be integrated into Bitdeer's upcoming SEALMINER A3 series <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">mining machines</a>, with mass production scheduled to commence in the latter half of this year. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Concurrently, research and development efforts are underway for the next-generation SEAL04 chip, underscoring Bitdeer's dedication to continuous innovation in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/ethiopia-hosts-groundbreaking-bitcoin-mining-summit-reinforces-role-as-africas-leading-mining-hub/">cryptocurrency mining sector</a>.</p>
