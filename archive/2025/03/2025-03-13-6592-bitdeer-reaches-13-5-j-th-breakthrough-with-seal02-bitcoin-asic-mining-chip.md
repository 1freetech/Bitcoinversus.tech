<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/tag/bitdeer-and-tsmc-to-launch-state-of-the-art-mining-chip-after-successful-test/">Bitdeer</a> Technologies Group has completed a successful test of its SEAL02 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/21/revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-how-ai-and-quantum-computing-meet/">Bitcoin mining chip</a>, achieving an impressive power efficiency ratio of 13.5 J/TH. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This milestone represents a significant advancement in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/13/energy-how-to-enhance-data-center-efficiency/">energy efficiency</a> within the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/10/theory-what-if-blackrock-controlled-bitcoin-mining/">mining industry</a>, placing Bitdeer at the forefront of innovative <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/10/miningstore-ceo-jp-baric-is-revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-with-energy-efficient-solutions/">mining solutions</a>. SEAL02 was developed in partnership with TSMC, one of the world's leading semiconductor manufacturers, and operates in a low-voltage, ultra-power-saving mode, enhancing its energy performance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The SEAL02 chip will be <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-09-26/bitdeer-touts-13-5-j-th-in-successful-test-of-seal02-bitcoin-asic/">integrated</a> into Bitdeer’s upcoming SEALMINER A2 machines, with mass production slated to begin in November 2024. This next-gen ASIC is expected to improve the profitability of mining operations by reducing energy consumption and operational costs, especially as Bitcoin’s price fluctuates. Bitdeer's roadmap includes ongoing research and development for the SEAL03 chip, further solidifying its position in the competitive mining market.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The advancement of SEAL02 could set a new industry standard for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/theory-antminer-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">mining hardware efficiency</a>, offering a competitive edge against similar offerings from rivals like Bitmain and MicroBT, who have also introduced high-efficiency ASIC models. With SEAL02's efficiency and Bitdeer's commitment to innovation, the future of Bitcoin mining looks increasingly energy-conscious​.</p>
