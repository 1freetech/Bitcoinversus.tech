<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Applied Digital (NASDAQ: APLD) has <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-09-06/bitcoin-applied-160m-nvidia/">raised</a> $160 million in strategic financing, with <a href="https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/107/applied-digital-announces-160-million-strategic-financing">backing</a> from notable investors like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/09/nvidia-launches-free-ai-online-courses-for-all-skill-levels/">NVIDIA</a> and Related Companies. This significant capital infusion is set to propel Applied Digital's data center expansion, focused on high-performance computing (HPC) and AI workloads. By leveraging <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">NVIDIA's GPU technologies</a>, the company is positioning itself as a leader in accelerated compute solutions.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/107/applied-digital-announces-160-million-strategic-financing">funding</a> was raised through the issuance of nearly 50 million shares at $3.24 per share, reflecting the closing price before the announcement. This move strengthens Applied Digital’s financial standing and fuels its growth plans, particularly in constructing new data centers and expanding <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/11/economic-challenges-for-bitfufu-cloud-mining-clients/">cloud infrastructure</a>.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These facilities will cater to advanced <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">AI and HPC</a> demands, marking a shift toward AI-driven markets, even as the company continues its significant involvement in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/12/the-mysterious-disappearance-of-jiang-friedcat-xinyu-a-deep-dive-into-the-unsolved-bitcoin-saga/">bitcoin mining</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Wes Cummins expressed excitement about the new chapter, highlighting Applied Digital’s ability to address the growing needs of the compute industry. The company has long-standing expertise in creating scalable, liquid-cooled environments that support both <strong>AI</strong> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/theory-antminer-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">crypto mining operations</a>​.</p>
