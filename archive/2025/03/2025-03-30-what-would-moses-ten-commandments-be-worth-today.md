<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Ten Commandments hold an unparalleled position in the religious, historical, and legal frameworks of society. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These stone tablets, traditionally believed to have been handed down to Moses by God, serve as a foundational artifact for the three major Abrahamic faiths: Judaism, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/18/god-in-cyberspace-understanding-the-christian-reformation-and-the-bitcoin-revolution/">Christianity</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/24/faith-meets-finance-the-islamic-debate-on-bitcoin/">Islam</a>. Their influence on the development of law and moral principles across cultures is profound, making the original stone tablets one of the most significant religious items in human history.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If the original stone tablets of Moses were ever discovered, their theoretical value would likely eclipse that of nearly all other historical and religious artifacts. To understand this value, it’s helpful to compare it to other high-profile historical items that have been sold at auction.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4,"fontSize":"medium"} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading has-medium-font-size">Comparable High-Value Artifacts</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Codex Leicester by Leonardo da Vinci, a 72-page manuscript filled with sketches and scientific observations, was <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/michelatindera/2021/11/21/bill-gates-owns-one-of-leonardo-da-vincis-manuscripts-heres-how-much-its-worth/">sold</a> to Bill Gates in 1994 for $30.8 million. Adjusted for inflation to 2023, this amounts to approximately $60 million​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/creepydotorg/status/1781011438577811545","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Another historic document, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_Carta">the Magna Carta</a> (1297 copy), which laid the groundwork for modern legal systems, fetched $21.3 million at auction in 2007, equivalent to $30.7 million today​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These sales highlight the enduring value of culturally and historically pivotal items.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/18/god-in-cyberspace-understanding-the-christian-reformation-and-the-bitcoin-revolution/">Religious texts</a> also command significant prices, as evidenced by the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/18/god-in-cyberspace-understanding-the-christian-reformation-and-the-bitcoin-revolution/">Gutenberg Bible</a>, one of the earliest printed books, which sold for $5.4 million in 1987, a value that adjusts to approximately $13 million in today’s dollars​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Estimated Value of Moses' Ten Commandment Stone Tablets</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Given the <a href="https://www.patternsofevidence.com/2016/12/14/oldest-known-10-commandments-tablet-sold-at-auction/">historical and religious</a> importance, a conservative estimate places the value of Moses’ original stone tablets at around $1 billion USD.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This estimate takes into account the significance of these tablets compared to the $450 million sale of da Vinci’s <em>Salvator Mundi</em> and other high-value manuscripts and artifacts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If such tablets were ever discovered, their sale would likely shatter all existing records​.</p>
