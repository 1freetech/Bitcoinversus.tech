<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A new HBO documentary titled <em>"Money Electric: The Bitcoin Mystery"</em> has sent ripples through the cryptocurrency world, <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/new-hbo-documentary-unveils-alleged-identity-of-bitcoins-creator-satoshi-nakamoto/">claiming</a> to uncover the true identity of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">Satoshi Nakamoto</a>, the elusive creator of Bitcoin. The film, directed by Emmy-nominated filmmaker Cullen Hoback, promises to reveal evidence pointing to a long-time <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">Bitcoin developer</a>, Peter Todd, as Nakamoto. However, Todd has publicly denied the accusations, calling them far-fetched.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The documentary explores <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/08/bitcoin-leapfrogs-silver-in-etf-markets/">Bitcoin's rise</a> and impact on global finance, while also delving into the mystery of Nakamoto’s identity. Speculation has been rampant, with Todd being the latest in a series of suspects, including other prominent cryptographers like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/27/adam-back-has-a-bullish-prediction-for-bitcoin/">Adam Back</a> and Len Sassaman​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The film suggests Todd's early posts on Bitcoin forums and his involvement in developing key Bitcoin technologies link him to Nakamoto​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, Todd <a href="https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/09/satoshi_nakamoto_suspect_hbo_bitcoin/">refuted</a> the claims, asserting that the documentary's conclusions are not based on concrete evidence. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He argued that if he were Nakamoto, he would have destroyed any proof to avoid being identified, adding that the constant speculation around Satoshi’s identity puts individuals in danger.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/29/bitcoiner-explains-negativity-within-traditional-finance-towards-bitcoin/">Bitcoin enthusiasts</a> eagerly awaited the documentary to see how the community reacts to these fresh allegations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The mystery of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/29/who-is-satoshi-nakamoto-new-emails-with-adam-back-enter-public-domain/">Satoshi Nakamoto</a> remains one of the most intriguing in the world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a>, with implications that could shake the industry if the findings were widely accepted.​</p>
