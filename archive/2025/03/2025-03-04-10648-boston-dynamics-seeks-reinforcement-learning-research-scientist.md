<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/01/boston-dynamics-atlas-humanoid-robot-impresses-with-push-up-routine/">​Boston Dynamics</a> is actively <a href="https://bostondynamics.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/Boston_Dynamics/job/Reinforcement-Learning-Research-Scientist_R1563">recruiting</a> a Reinforcement Learning Research Scientist to join their Atlas team, focusing on advancing mobile manipulation behaviors for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/30/robots-will-eventually-replace-the-human-workforce/">humanoid robots</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">​The salary for a Reinforcement Learning Research Scientist at Boston Dynamics varies based on experience and qualifications. According to ZipRecruiter, the salary range for this position is between $116,000 and $186,600 per year. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, data from H1B filings indicates that Staff Research Scientists at Boston Dynamics have a median salary of $165,000, while Senior Staff Research Scientists earn a median of $176,658. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These figures suggest that the compensation for this role typically falls within the range of $116,000 to $186,600 annually, depending on the candidate's experience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The successful candidate will apply machine learning techniques to address real-world bimanual manipulation challenges, develop high-quality Python and C++ code, lead design reviews, and deploy and debug code on the Atlas platform. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Applicants are required to hold a Master's degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or a related field, and have prior experience in training and deploying reinforcement learning policies for complex robotic behaviors. </p>
