<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Proto, a hardware team within <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/jack-dorseys-block-inc-expands-bitcoin-mining-operations/">Block Inc.</a>, is actively seeking an ASIC Physical Design Engineer <a href="https://block.xyz/careers/jobs/4452461008">to contribute</a> to the development of next-gen <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin mining</a> Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/20/asics-achieve-higher-hashrate-with-mara-2pic-immersion-cooling-system/">ASICs</a>). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At Block, Inc., the salary range for an ASIC Physical Design Engineer position is between $178,000 and $297,000 annually, with a median of approximately $238,000</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10631,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/screenshot-2025-03-03-185928.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-10631" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The role <a href="https://www.builtinchicago.org/job/asic-physical-design-engineer-proto/246178">involves</a> performing block-level place and route (P&amp;R) tasks, integrating designs from early Register-Transfer Level (RTL) stages through to final tapeout. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Collaboration with logic designers is essential for achieving block-level timing closure, and working closely with custom cell development teams is also a key responsibility. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Candidates are <a href="https://www.builtinchicago.org/job/asic-physical-design-engineer-proto/246178">expected</a> to enhance the capacity and scalability of full-chip design flows and develop scripts to improve existing physical design infrastructures and methodologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Effective collaboration across different functions and time zones is crucial for this position. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">An <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/28/bitmains-asic-dominance-faces-new-competition/">Application-Specific Integrated Circuit</a> (ASIC) is a specialized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/31/biden-administration-invests-325-million-in-hemlock-semiconductor-factory/">semiconductor</a> device designed for a specific application or function, offering advantages such as high performance, reduced power consumption, and a smaller physical footprint compared to general-purpose integrated circuits. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">ASICs are widely used across various industries due to their ability to deliver high performance, lower power consumption, and reduced physical footprint. Their specialized nature makes them ideal for specific tasks in diverse environments where precision and efficiency are paramount.</p>
