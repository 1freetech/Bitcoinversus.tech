<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ohio State Senator Niraj Antani has <a href="https://www.daytondailynews.com/local/area-senator-wants-state-local-governments-to-accept-crypto-as-tax-payments/Y4PT3K5E4ZHQNGPPB5IQE2PRRI/">introduced</a> groundbreaking legislation that would allow residents to pay state and local taxes using cryptocurrency, marking a significant step forward in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">digital currency adoption</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If passed, this bill would position Ohio as a leader in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> integration within state financial systems. “Cryptocurrency is not just the future — it’s the present,” Antani declared, showcasing his support for modernizing Ohio’s financial framework to support innovation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposed <a href="https://x.com/NirajAntani/status/1840745739283988761">bill</a>, introduced in late September, aims to allow residents to use Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies for various state fees and taxes. The bill also opens the door for state universities and pensions to invest in cryptocurrencies, further deepening the state’s commitment to this burgeoning financial sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Senator Antani’s new bill seeks to restore Ohio’s leadership in this space and make the state a model for others considering similar legislation.</p>
