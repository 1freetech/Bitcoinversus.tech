<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a historic move, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/goldman-sachs-invests-710-million-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Goldman Sachs</a> has acknowledged the <a href="https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2025/03/17/goldman-sachs-recognizes-cryptocurrencies-for-the-first-time-in-the-letter-to-shareholders/">growing significance</a> of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/02/u-s-treasury-releases-report-on-digital-assets/">digital assets</a> in its latest annual letter to shareholders. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The 2024 report highlights how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/california-dmv-digitizes-car-titles-with-blockchain-technology/">blockchain technology</a> and cryptocurrencies are intensifying competition within the financial sector. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The letter notes that the rise of electronic trading, alongside blockchain and artificial intelligence, has transformed market dynamics. Goldman Sachs <a href="https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2025/03/17/goldman-sachs-recognizes-cryptocurrencies-for-the-first-time-in-the-letter-to-shareholders/">admits</a> that some competitors offer digital asset services that the firm currently does not provide, indicating a shift in client preferences. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite previous caution, Goldman Sachs has made strides in the crypto space. The bank launched a cryptocurrency trading desk in 2021 and introduced its Digital Asset Platform in 2022. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, Goldman participated in testing the Canton Network, a blockchain-based communication system, signaling a growing interest in decentralized technologies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the bank remains vigilant about the risks associated with digital assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The report emphasizes that, although adoption is growing, blockchain technology is still in its early stages and may be susceptible to cyber-attacks and other vulnerabilities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO David Solomon has expressed mixed views on Bitcoin and blockchain. While acknowledging the technology's potential to reduce friction in traditional financial systems, he has described Bitcoin as a speculative investment, though he concedes it could serve as a store of value. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In late 2024, Goldman Sachs increased its holdings in spot Bitcoin ETFs. By December 31, the bank held $1.27 billion in BlackRock's IBIT, an 88% increase from the previous quarter, and $288 million in Fidelity's FBTC, up 105%.</p>
