<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">​President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump</a> has <a href="https://apnews.com/article/e5a6295577275045db3484b71c979bfb">imposed</a> a 25% tariff on all steel and aluminum imports to the United States, effective immediately, aiming to bolster domestic manufacturing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The European Union <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/mar/12/eu-retaliates-against-trump-tariffs-with-26bn-countermeasures">swiftly retaliated</a> with countermeasures targeting $28 billion worth of U.S. goods, including iconic products such as bourbon whiskey, jeans, and Harley-Davidson motorcycles. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Canada, the largest supplier of steel and aluminum to the U.S., announced plans for retaliatory tariffs totaling $20.7 billion, reflecting concerns over potential economic impacts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">South Korean steelmakers, including POSCO and Hyundai Steel, are considering establishing production facilities in the U.S. to mitigate the effects of the tariffs. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The tariffs have raised concerns about increased costs for U.S. manufacturers reliant on imported metals, potentially leading to higher prices for consumer and industrial goods. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Economists warn that the benefits to domestic steel and aluminum industries may be offset by negative impacts on downstream manufacturers. ​</p>
