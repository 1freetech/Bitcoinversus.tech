<p style="text-transform:none">Crypto.com, has <a href="https://knue.com/ixp/186/p/popular-crypto-company-moves-from-miami-to-tyler/">relocated</a> its North American headquarters from Miami to Tyler, Texas. The move places the company at the heart of Texas, a state known for its booming cryptocurrency infrastructure, particularly <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This strategic relocation positions Crypto.com to take advantage of the state’s talent pool and favorable political climate. The new office, located in Plaza Tower in downtown Tyler, is designed to accommodate up to 20 employees, with potential for expansion​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Moving its headquarters to Texas signifies the company's broader strategy of growth beyond just cryptocurrency exchanges, further leveraging the state’s prominence in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/20/prediction-bitcoin-network-will-reach-1-zettahash-by-q4-2024/">Bitcoin mining​</a>.</p>
