<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/11/ark-investment-ceo-cathie-wood-anticipates-parabolic-move-with-bitcoin-in-convergence-with-ai-boom/">Bitcoin’s scarcity</a> is one of its most significant features, with only 21 million coins ever to exist. This scarcity equates to approximately 0.0025 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/02/dubai-officially-approves-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-salary-payments/">BTC</a> per person on the planet, based on the current global population of 8 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to <a href="https://x.com/indianhodl/status/1537748891482963970">INDIANHODL</a>, this allocation isn't an annual amount but a lifetime allotment for each individual. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">When considering the productive value of a human life, which economists and actuaries estimate at around $10 million, <a href="https://coincodex.com/convert/bitcoin/usd/0.0025/">dividing</a> that value by the 0.0025 BTC lifetime allotment brings a valuation of approximately $4 billion per Bitcoin​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of today, 0.0025 BTC is valued at roughly $154.91 in USD, though this number fluctuates with the market​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The scarcity of Bitcoin, combined with its deflationary model, has led many investors to view it as a long-term store of value, often likened to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">digital gold</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With the continued rise of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">Bitcoin adoption</a> globally and its inherent scarcity, this concept of a lifetime allotment provides insight into the potential future value of the cryptocurrency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The comparison of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/28/bitcoins-rising-value-outpaces-traditional-wage-growth/">Bitcoin's value</a> against the productive value of human life offers a unique angle, emphasizing the digital asset’s potential to reach substantial value in the years to come​.</p>
