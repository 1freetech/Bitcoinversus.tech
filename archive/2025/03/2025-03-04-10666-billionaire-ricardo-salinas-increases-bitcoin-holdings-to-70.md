<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mexican billionaire Ricardo Salinas - who has an estimated net worth of $5.8 billion - has <a href="https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/03/04/mexican-billionaire-ricardo-salinas-says-he-has-70-bitcoin-related-exposure">significantly increased</a> his investment in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin</a>, now allocating approximately 70% of his liquid portfolio to the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/05/uk-to-recognize-cryptocurrency-as-personal-property/">cryptocurrency</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10668,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-4.png?w=598" alt="" class="wp-image-10668" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent interview, Salinas stated, "I’ve got about 70% in Bitcoin-related exposure and 30% in gold and gold miners," highlighting his strategic focus on these assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://casebitcoin.com/story/billionaire-ricardo-salinas-pliego-discloses-btc-stake">marks</a> a substantial increase from 2020, when he disclosed that 10% of his liquid portfolio was invested in Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Salinas, known for his critical view of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/08/blackrock-calls-bitcoin-a-hedge-against-global-disorder/">fiat currencies</a>, has previously referred to them as "a fraud" and emphasized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/10/greg-foss-predicts-bitcoins-potential-rise-to-2-million/">Bitcoin's potential</a> as a hedge against currency devaluation. ​</p>
