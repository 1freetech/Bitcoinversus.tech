<p style="text-transform:none">Oneil Cruz, the shortstop turned outfielder for the Pittsburgh Pirates, is set to make history in the 2025 MLB season with a potential 50-home run campaign. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10869,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-11.png?w=720" alt="" class="wp-image-10869" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Oneil Cruz Led the MLB in Maximum Exit Velocity, a clear indicator of how hard he hits the ball. </em></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Known for his <a href="https://www.mlb.com/news/oneil-cruz-hardest-hit-ball-tracked-by-statcast-by-highest-exit-velocity">incredible power</a>, Cruz has recorded some of the <a href="https://www.mlb.com/news/oneil-cruz-hardest-hit-ball-tracked-by-statcast-by-highest-exit-velocity">hardest-hit</a> balls in Statcast history, including a 122.4 mph shot, the fastest ever tracked. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His raw power has already earned him a reputation, and with his performance improving after an injury, he is poised for a breakout year in 2025​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">In 2024, Cruz's longest home run measured 472 feet with an exit velocity of 115.2 mph, adding to his already impressive stat line. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He’s not just a power hitter, though; Cruz has demonstrated an ability to command attention both offensively and defensively. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, his frequent chasing of pitches outside the strike zone has been one area of concern that he has worked to improve​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Since entering the league in 2021, Cruz has quickly become a fan favorite due to his high exit velocities, regularly topping the charts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His blend of power and speed makes him a unique force in the game. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cruz has all the tools necessary to achieve a 50-homer milestone​.</p>
