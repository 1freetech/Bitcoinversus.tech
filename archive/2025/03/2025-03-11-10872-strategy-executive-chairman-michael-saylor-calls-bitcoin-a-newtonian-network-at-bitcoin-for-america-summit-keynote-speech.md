<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/01/michael-saylor-is-the-new-warren-buffet/">Michael Saylor</a>, Executive Chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/03/strategy-chairman-michael-saylor-advocates-for-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">Strategy</a>, delivered a keynote address at the "Bitcoin for America" summit in Washington, D.C., organized by the Bitcoin Policy Institute. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In his speech, Saylor emphasized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Bitcoin's potential</a> to transform the U.S. economy and advocated for the nation to secure a substantial portion of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/05/uk-to-recognize-cryptocurrency-as-personal-property/">cryptocurrency</a>.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Bitcoin as a Strategic Asset</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor characterized Bitcoin as a "superpower," <a href="https://openexo.com/l/33b575d3">suggesting that</a> embracing it could position the United States as a dominant force in the digital economy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He proposed that the U.S. government should aim to own 20% of the Bitcoin network, estimating its future value between $50 trillion and $80 trillion. Such an investment, he argued, could enable the country to pay off its national debt and assert financial leadership in the 21st century. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Advocacy for a Bitcoin Strategic Reserve</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Building on recent developments, Saylor <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/saylor-calls-bitcoin-superpower-urges-142726515.html">supported the establishment</a> of a U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/03/strategy-chairman-michael-saylor-advocates-for-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">strategic Bitcoin reserve</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He likened this initiative to creating a "Digital Fort Knox," securing the nation's financial future by integrating Bitcoin into its reserves. This move, he suggested, would not only stabilize the economy but also encourage innovation within the financial sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bitcoin As a Newtonian network</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Michael Saylor compares Bitcoin to a <strong>Newtonian Network</strong>, using a simple analogy of gravity and planetary systems. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Imagine <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/22/gamestop-explores-bitcoin-investment-amid-strategic-shift/">Bitcoin</a> like a star in space—just as a star's gravity pulls planets into orbit, Bitcoin's "financial gravity" draws in capital. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The more money invested in Bitcoin, the stronger and denser it becomes, creating a reinforcing cycle that continuously boosts its value and stability. Saylor even calls Bitcoin an "Orange Dwarf," highlighting how it grows brighter and hotter financially as it accumulates more resources.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">On the other hand, a <strong>Metcalfean Network</strong>—think social media platforms or messaging apps—depends heavily on user participation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These networks gain value based on how many active users they have; the more users join, the greater the network’s usefulness. But there's a catch: if users leave or engagement drops, the network quickly loses its value. Essentially, these platforms constantly need active growth to remain relevant.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The key difference, according to Saylor, is that Bitcoin isn't about the number of users actively interacting; it's about how much capital it attracts and holds. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Unlike a social media network that can fade away if people lose interest, Bitcoin’s strength increases as more money flows into it, regardless of how frequently people actively transact. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In short, Bitcoin acts like a powerful gravitational force, becoming increasingly robust and secure the more investment it attracts, setting it apart from typical user-driven digital networks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Call to Action for Policymakers</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor urged U.S. policymakers to adopt clear regulatory frameworks that support Bitcoin adoption. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He emphasized that embracing Bitcoin is not just a financial decision but a strategic move to ensure the country's prosperity in the digital age. By leading in Bitcoin adoption, the U.S. could set global standards and drive economic growth. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Conclusion</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Michael Saylor's address at the "Bitcoin for America" summit highlighted his vision of Bitcoin as a transformative asset capable of reshaping the U.S. economy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His proposals for significant national investment and the creation of a strategic reserve reflect a bold strategy aimed at securing America's financial future in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.</p>
