<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Colorado is poised to officially designate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">nuclear energy</a> as a clean energy source, following the recent passage of <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mX-u1iuGvY0">House Bill 25-1040</a> by the state Senate. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mX-u1iuGvY0","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mX-u1iuGvY0
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The bill, which updates statutory definitions of "clean energy" and "clean energy resource" to include nuclear energy, now awaits Governor Jared Polis's signature. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The legislation's approval marks a significant shift in Colorado's energy policy, aligning with the state's <a href="https://tsscolorado.com/for-the-first-time-colorado-legislators-push-forward-a-bill-to-boost-nuclear-energy/">ambitious goals</a> for carbon reduction. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By recognizing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/20/energy-uaes-first-nuclear-power-plant-reaches-full-operational-status/">nuclear energy</a> as a clean resource, the bill enables utilities to incorporate nuclear projects into their clean-energy portfolios and makes such projects eligible for special financing. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://bsky.app/profile/bitcoinversus.bsky.social/post/3lfxg2mzcs22l","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"bluesky-social"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Proponents of the bill, including the grassroots organization Colorado Nuclear Alliance, have advocated for nuclear energy's role in achieving a carbon-free future. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">They argue that nuclear power offers a reliable and high-capacity electricity generation option with zero carbon dioxide emissions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The bill's passage reflects a growing recognition of nuclear energy's potential to contribute to a sustainable and resilient energy infrastructure. As Colorado aims for 100% renewable energy by 2040, the inclusion of nuclear power is seen as a crucial step toward meeting this target. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the bill has garnered substantial support, it has also <a href="https://anthropocenealliance.org/rocky-mountain-peace-and-justice-center/">faced opposition</a> from groups concerned about nuclear energy's safety and environmental impact. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Organizations such as the Rocky Mountain Peace and Justice Center have a long history of advocating against nuclear-powered weapons and have expressed reservations about expanding nuclear energy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Governor Polis's <a href="https://citizenportal.ai/articles/2389614/Colorado/Colorado-advances-nuclear-energy-as-clean-resource-under-new-federal-policy">anticipated signing</a> of the bill will position Colorado among states that recognize nuclear energy as a clean resource, potentially influencing energy policies nationwide. This development underscores the state's commitment to diversifying its energy portfolio and addressing climate change through innovative solutions. ​</p>
