<p style="text-transform:none">​The Avalon Nano 3S, developed by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/19/bitcoin-history-on-this-day-canaan-launched-the-first-asic-miner/">Canaan</a>, is a compact <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/01/bitcoin-miner-defi-django-reveals-modifications-for-airflow-optimization/">Bitcoin miner</a> designed for home use, combining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/03/understanding-mining-algorithms-and-their-role-in-securing-cryptocurrency-networks/">cryptocurrency mining</a> with heating functionality. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://youtu.be/ojLRz6QkmzM?si=HvHkPPiPZj4-yNAP","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It offers a hashrate of 6 terahashes per second (TH/s) while consuming a maximum of 140 watts of power. The device operates on an <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/07/cpu-architecture-and-microarchitecture-explained/">input voltage</a> of 28 volts and supports frequencies of 50/60Hz.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Physically, the Avalon Nano 3S measures 205mm in length, 115mm in width, and 58.5mm in height, with a net weight of approximately 860 grams. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It employs an air-cooling system to maintain optimal operating temperatures between -5°C and 30°C. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The air outlet temperature ranges from 40°C to 60°C, ensuring effective heat dissipation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The device operates quietly, producing noise levels between 33 to 40 decibels, making it suitable for residential environments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ultimately, the Db/TH/s ratio of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/cleanspark-purchases-avalon-a1566i-miners-from-canaan/">Canaan Avalon</a> Nano 3S likely makes this machine the most suitable in-your-bedroom miner on the market today. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Avalon Nano 3S is available in two color options: black and white. It is compatible with multiple power supply units (PSUs), including 65W, 100W, and 140W models. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The "Full Set" package includes an original 140W Canaan power supply, ensuring it meets a variety of mining needs. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Final Grade: A - </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">I'm giving the Nano 3s an A minus. The grammar in the technical documentation was harder to read than it should have been. I had to find a video on youtube to figure out what the credentials were when changing a miner password. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nevertheless, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/24/canaan-inc-expands-self-mining-operations-in-texas-and-pennsylvania/">Avalon</a> Nano 3S serves as an efficient and user-friendly solution for individuals interested in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin mining at home</a>, offering a balance between performance and energy consumption.</p>
