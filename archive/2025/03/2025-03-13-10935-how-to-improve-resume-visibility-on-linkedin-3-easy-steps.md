<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Struggling to get your resume noticed on LinkedIn?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Searching for a job can be overwhelming and at times feels like a job itself. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During this time of applying to hundreds of jobs, I've found that filtering certain job preferences can dramatically increase your resume visibility. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Try this 3-step strategy to boost your visibility instantly:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Turn on "<em><strong>Easy Apply</strong></em>" when job searching. It's a fast, streamlined method that helps submit applications quickly. Despite concerns about receiving a mountain of applications, applying these specific filters greatly reduces competition.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10937,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/screenshot-2025-03-13-165441.png?w=574" alt="" class="wp-image-10937" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">2. Set job filters to <strong><em>"posted in the last week"</em></strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list"></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10938,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/screenshot-2025-03-13-165544.png?w=505" alt="" class="wp-image-10938" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">3. Activate the <strong><em>"less than 10 applicants"</em> </strong>tab.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10940,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/screenshot-2025-03-13-170130.png?w=559" alt="" class="wp-image-10940" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Yes, people often complain about the overwhelming number of applicants with Easy Apply—but turning on these filters significantly increases the likelihood that your resume actually gets seen!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10942,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/screenshot-2025-03-13-165630.png?w=578" alt="" class="wp-image-10942" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It doesn't hurt to try. Give it a shot and best of luck on your job search!</p>
