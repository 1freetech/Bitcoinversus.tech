<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/01/michael-saylor-is-the-new-warren-buffet/">Michael Saylor</a>, Executive Chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/microstrategy-rallies-with-nav-multiple-suggesting-undervaluation/">Strategy</a>, has <a href="https://www.benzinga.com/25/03/44087250/strategys-saylor-calls-crypto-reserve-bullish-for-bitcoin-predicts-100-trillion-opportunity">unveiled</a> a comprehensive digital assets framework, asserting that the United States could unlock a $100 trillion economic opportunity by adopting strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/16/cathie-wood-criticizes-sec-chair-genslers-crypto-policies/">digital asset policies</a>. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor's proposal emphasizes <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/saylor-floats-us-crypto-framework-with-81-trillion-bitcoin-reserve">the establishment</a> of a clear taxonomy for digital assets, categorizing them into digital commodities, currencies, securities, tokens, non-fungible tokens (NFTs), and asset-backed tokens. He believes that such classification would provide regulatory clarity and foster innovation within the digital economy. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://bsky.app/profile/bitcoinversus.bsky.social/post/3lfxg2mzcs22l","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"bluesky-embed"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Central to Saylor's vision is the creation of a strategic Bitcoin reserve, which he claims could generate between $16 trillion to $81 trillion in wealth for the U.S. Treasury. He argues that this reserve would strengthen the U.S. dollar's position as the global reserve currency and help offset national debt. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The framework also advocates for streamlining the issuance and trading of digital assets, aiming to reduce the time and cost associated with bringing assets to market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor suggests that by implementing these measures, the U.S. could expand its digital capital markets from $2 trillion to $280 trillion, thereby solidifying its leadership in the global digital economy. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent interview, Saylor expressed strong support for President Trump's proposed strategic crypto reserve, describing it as potentially "the greatest economic program of the 21st century." <br><br>He dismissed concerns about volatility, drawing parallels to historical territorial acquisitions like the purchase of Alaska. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/24/michael-saylor-defends-institutional-custody-amid-criticism-from-max-keiser-and-vitalik-buterin/">Saylor's advocacy for Bitcoin</a> is well-documented. Under his leadership, Strategy has amassed over 499,000 Bitcoins, acquired for approximately $33.1 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposed digital assets framework has garnered attention from regulatory bodies. Saylor recently presented his plan to the Securities and Exchange Commission's Crypto Task Force, highlighting the potential for a capital markets renaissance driven by digital assets. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Critics, however, caution that such an ambitious strategy carries risks, particularly given <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/23/decoding-bitcoins-price-a-technical-review/">Bitcoin's price volatility</a> and the nascent state of cryptocurrency regulation. Nonetheless, Saylor remains steadfast in his conviction that embracing digital assets is pivotal for America's economic future.</p>
