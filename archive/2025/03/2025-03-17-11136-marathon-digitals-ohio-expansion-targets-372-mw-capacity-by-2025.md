<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">​<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/20/asics-achieve-higher-hashrate-with-mara-2pic-immersion-cooling-system/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a> (MARA) has significantly expanded its operations by acquiring two operational <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/17/data-center-cooling-techniques-immersion-water-and-air-explained/">data centers</a> in Hannibal and Hopedale, Ohio, totaling 222 megawatts (MW) of interconnect-approved capacity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":11142,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/mara-growth-porjections-ohio.jpeg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-11142" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><sup>Marathon Growth Projections. Source: <a href="https://ir.mara.com/">Marathon</a></sup> </figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These sites currently operate at 122 MW, with approval to expand by an additional 100 MW. Additionally, MARA is developing a 150 MW facility in Findlay, Ohio, which already has 30 MW of capacity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Collectively, these facilities offer an interconnect-approved capacity of 372 MW, which MARA aims to fully energize by the end of 2025. ​<a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1375/mara-adds-372-megawatts-of-capacity-in-ohio-with-152-megawatts-operational-and-220-megawatts-in-development" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Mara</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These strategic developments are integral to MARA's objective of achieving a hash rate of 50 exahashes per second (EH/s) by the end of 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company <a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1355/marathon-digital-holdings-increases-2024-hash-rate-target-to-50-exahash">increased</a> its 2024 hash rate target from 35-37 EH/s to 50 EH/s, representing a 100% growth in hash rate during the fiscal year.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By December 2024, MARA's energized hash rate had reached 53.2 EH/s, surpassing its year-end target. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company also <a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1385/mara-announces-bitcoin-production-and-mining-operation-updates-fordecember-2024">increased</a> its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/17/strategy-microstrategy-expands-bitcoin-holdings-with-10-7-million-purchase/">Bitcoin holdings</a> to 44,893 BTC, reflecting a BTC yield per share of 62.7% for the year. ​</p>
