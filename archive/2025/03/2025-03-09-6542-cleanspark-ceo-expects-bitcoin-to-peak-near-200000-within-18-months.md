<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark CEO Zach Bradford <a href="https://www.theblock.co/post/318647/cleanspark-ceo-bitcoin-200000-usd">forecasts</a> that <a href="https://www.theblock.co/post/318647/cleanspark-ceo-bitcoin-200000-usd">Bitcoin</a> could reach a peak of nearly $200,000 within the next 18 months. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bradford's optimism is based on several factors, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/28/bitcoin-rsi-chart-suggests-bullish-outlook/">Bitcoin's historical price trends</a> following <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/14/this-1000-day-bitcoin-trading-strategy-changes-everything/">halving events</a> and the expected market stability after the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/politics-donald-trump-announces-elon-musk-will-lead-government-efficiency-commission/">U.S. presidential election</a> in 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He emphasized that while it's less about who wins the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/14/politics-kamala-harris-accepts-cryptocurrency-donations-via-coinbase/">election</a>, the certainty following it will likely drive the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> toward its next all-time high.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.theblock.co/post/318647/cleanspark-ceo-bitcoin-200000-usd">According to</a> Bradford, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin's price</a> has exhibited extended periods of stability, which he believes signals a sustained upward trend. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
