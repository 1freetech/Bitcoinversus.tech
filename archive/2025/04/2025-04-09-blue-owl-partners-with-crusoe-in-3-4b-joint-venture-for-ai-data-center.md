<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Crusoe Energy Systems has <a href="https://crusoe.ai/newsroom/crusoe-blue-owl-capital-primary-digital-joint-venture/">secured</a> a $3.4 billion joint venture (JV) with Blue Owl Capital and Primary Digital Infrastructure to develop a 200-megawatt AI data center in Texas. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The facility, located at the Lancium Clean Campus in Abilene, will cater to the growing demand for AI computing, housing up to 100,000 GPUs to support high-density workloads. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Crusoe's pivot from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a> to AI infrastructure marks a significant shift in its operational strategy, as it aims to harness renewable energy sources, including wind and potential on-site solar​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The 206 MW facility will incorporate cutting-edge cooling technologies like direct-to-chip liquid cooling, designed for high-performance AI applications. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The site, expected to be operational by the first half of 2025, will <a href="https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/crusoe-signs-34bn-joint-venture-with-blue-owl/">support</a> OpenAI's computing needs through a lease agreement with Oracle. Microsoft, acting as a middleman, reinforces its role as OpenAI’s exclusive cloud provider, ensuring substantial support for generative AI​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This development aligns with Blue Owl's continued investment in digital infrastructure, having already formed partnerships in other major projects, such as a $5 billion AI data center initiative with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">CoreWeave</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The collaboration emphasizes the growing importance of AI-related technology and hyperscale data centers as a key market for future growth​.</p>
