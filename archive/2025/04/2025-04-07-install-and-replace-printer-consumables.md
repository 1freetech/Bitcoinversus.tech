<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-large-font-size"><strong>Paper and Paper Feed Maintenance</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">One of the most common consumables in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/printer-overview-laser-inkjet-thermal-dot-matrix-comptia-a/">printers</a> is paper, which must be loaded correctly to avoid jams and misfeeds. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EzcmCCN6x-0\u0026amp;pp=ygUgUGFwZXIgYW5kIFBhcGVyIEZlZWQgTWFpbnRlbmFuY2U%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Printers often have adjustable trays to accommodate different paper sizes and types, such as plain, photo, or specialty paper. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qM5By8V8IM\u0026amp;pp=ygUgUGFwZXIgYW5kIFBhcGVyIEZlZWQgTWFpbnRlbmFuY2U%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Regularly inspecting and cleaning the paper feed rollers helps prevent paper jams caused by dust, wear, or debris. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wLAqAkU3q10\u0026amp;pp=ygUgUGFwZXIgYW5kIFBhcGVyIEZlZWQgTWFpbnRlbmFuY2XSBwkJvQCDtaTen9Q%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Technicians should also verify the correct paper weight and texture settings within the printer’s configuration to match the media in use, ensuring proper feed and print quality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"className":""} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Ink and Toner Replacement</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/printer-overview-laser-inkjet-thermal-dot-matrix-comptia-a/">Inkjet printers</a> require the installation and periodic replacement of <strong>ink cartridges</strong>, while laser printers rely on <strong>toner cartridges</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">When replacing cartridges, it's essential to use the correct type and model specified by the manufacturer. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eXFepRF7ii8\u0026amp;pp=ygUnSW5zdGFsbCBhbmQgUmVwbGFjZSBQcmludGVyIENvbnN1bWFibGVz","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eXFepRF7ii8&amp;pp=ygUnSW5zdGFsbCBhbmQgUmVwbGFjZSBQcmludGVyIENvbnN1bWFibGVz
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For ink cartridges, the installation typically involves opening the printhead assembly, snapping the new cartridge into place, and running a calibration or print head alignment to ensure print quality. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXd3YqtU-No\u0026amp;pp=ygUZSW5rIGFuZCBUb25lciBSZXBsYWNlbWVudNIHCQm9AIO1pN6f1A%3D%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXd3YqtU-No&amp;pp=ygUZSW5rIGFuZCBUb25lciBSZXBsYWNlbWVudNIHCQm9AIO1pN6f1A%3D%3D
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Toner replacement often requires shaking the cartridge gently to distribute toner evenly before installation, followed by resetting the toner counter or calibration via the printer’s control panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"className":""} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Drums and Imaging Units</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/06/understanding-laser-printer-parts-and-functions/">Laser printers</a> often use <strong>drums</strong> or <strong>imaging units</strong> that work alongside the toner to transfer images onto paper. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUfSGFIcE8k\u0026amp;t=30s\u0026amp;pp=ygUXRHJ1bXMgYW5kIEltYWdpbmcgVW5pdHM%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUfSGFIcE8k&amp;t=30s&amp;pp=ygUXRHJ1bXMgYW5kIEltYWdpbmcgVW5pdHM%3D
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These units degrade over time and must be replaced periodically to maintain print quality. Replacing the drum usually involves opening a specific compartment in the printer, removing the old drum, and carefully inserting the new one without exposing it to light for extended periods, as this can damage the drum's sensitive coating. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pchRnKs3_PU\u0026amp;pp=ygUXRHJ1bXMgYW5kIEltYWdpbmcgVW5pdHM%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pchRnKs3_PU&amp;pp=ygUXRHJ1bXMgYW5kIEltYWdpbmcgVW5pdHM%3D
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">After installation, running a cleaning cycle or calibration is often necessary to ensure the new drum functions correctly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"className":""} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Fuser Units and Maintenance Kits</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <strong>fuser unit</strong> is responsible for heating and bonding toner to paper in laser printers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Over time, the fuser can wear out or become dirty, leading to smudging, ghosting, or poor adhesion of toner. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRpSTyJkYr0\u0026amp;pp=ygUgRnVzZXIgVW5pdHMgYW5kIE1haW50ZW5hbmNlIEtpdHM%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRpSTyJkYr0&amp;pp=ygUgRnVzZXIgVW5pdHMgYW5kIE1haW50ZW5hbmNlIEtpdHM%3D
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Replacing a fuser is a delicate process requiring careful handling to avoid burns, as fusers operate at high temperatures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Many manufacturers provide <strong>maintenance kits</strong> that include the fuser, rollers, and separation pads, which are all designed to be replaced together to extend the printer’s service life. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">After installing these parts, technicians should reset the maintenance counter if required.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"className":""} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Waste Containers and Cleaning Procedures</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Laser printers also use <strong>waste toner bottles</strong> or <strong>waste containers</strong> to collect excess toner that does not make it onto the page. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRmtTFknjNI\u0026amp;pp=ygUxV2FzdGUgQ29udGFpbmVycyBhbmQgQ2xlYW5pbmcgUHJvY2VkdXJlcyBwcmludGVycw%3D%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These must be emptied or replaced when full to prevent spillage or print quality issues. Some inkjet printers have <strong>waste ink pads</strong> or <strong>maintenance boxes</strong> that require attention. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bNrI2_qVRKw\u0026amp;pp=ygUxV2FzdGUgQ29udGFpbmVycyBhbmQgQ2xlYW5pbmcgUHJvY2VkdXJlcyBwcmludGVycw%3D%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"","style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cleaning procedures, including cleaning the print heads, rollers, and interior surfaces, help maintain printer performance and reduce the risk of future failures. Technicians should always follow manufacturer instructions for cleaning to avoid damaging sensitive components.</p>
