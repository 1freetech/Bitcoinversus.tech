<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The NBA <a href="https://www.reuters.com/sports/timberwolves-anthony-edwards-fined-50k-obscene-comment-gesture-toward-fan-2025-04-22/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has fined</a> Minnesota Timberwolves guard Anthony Edwards $50,000 for directing inappropriate language and making an obscene gesture toward a fan during Game 1 of the Western Conference first-round playoff series against the Los Angeles Lakers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Zd6ouil_puQ","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-4-3 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The incident occurred with 4:33 remaining in the third quarter of the Timberwolves' 117–95 victory at Crypto.com Arena. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Edwards responded to heckling by grabbing his crotch and making a vulgar remark, which was captured on video and widely circulated on social media. The league announced the fine on Tuesday, citing unsportsmanlike conduct. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This is not the first time Edwards has faced disciplinary action from the NBA. Earlier in the 2024–25 season, he <a href="https://www.si.com/nba/timberwolves/minnesota-timberwolves-news/nba-fines-anthony-edwards-50k-for-crotch-grab-vulgar-message-to-lakers-fans?utm_source=chatgpt.com">was fined</a> $35,000 for making an obscene gesture during a game against the Sacramento Kings. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Additionally, he has been penalized for using profane language in media interviews and for failing to leave the court promptly after being ejected, bringing his total fines for the season to over $370,000. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Despite the controversy, Edwards delivered a strong performance in Game 1, recording 22 points, eight rebounds, and nine assists. The Timberwolves lead the series 1–0, with Game 2 scheduled to take place in Los Angeles. ​</p>
