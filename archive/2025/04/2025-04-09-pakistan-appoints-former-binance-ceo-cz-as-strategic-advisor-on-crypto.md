<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The government of Pakistan has <a href="https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/technology/pakistan-hires-binance-ex-ceo-as-crypto-adviser-india-left-watching/ar-AA1CtJYa?ocid=BingNewsSerp">officially</a> appointed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/30/binance-ceo-forecasts-bright-future-for-bitcoin-post-halving/">Changpeng Zhao</a> (CZ), the former CEO of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/tag/binance-founder-cz-earned-25-million-daily-while-incarcerated/">Binance</a>, as a strategic advisor on cryptocurrency policy and blockchain innovation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The appointment signals Pakistan’s accelerating interest in integrating digital assets into its financial infrastructure while seeking global expertise to draft regulatory frameworks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>CZ, who stepped down as CEO of Binance in 2023 amid regulatory scrutiny in the United States, has remained a prominent figure in global crypto circles. His new advisory role marks his return to public-facing influence, this time as a consultant to a national government navigating the evolving landscape of digital finance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The announcement has generated international attention, especially within emerging markets where crypto adoption continues to outpace regulatory clarity. Pakistan’s decision to onboard one of the most recognized names in crypto hints at a broader ambition to position itself as a hub for blockchain innovation and cross-border fintech growth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
