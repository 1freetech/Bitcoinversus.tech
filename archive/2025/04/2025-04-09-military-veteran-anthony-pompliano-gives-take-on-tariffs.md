<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Bitcoin Advocate Anthony Pompliano offered a <a href="https://x.com/apompliano/status/1909937159727165668">comprehensive take</a> on the evolving tariff landscape, suggesting that geopolitical tensions between the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/28/smarter-together-u-s-china-breakthrough-in-graphene-semiconductor-technology/">U.S. and China</a> are reshaping global market expectations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In a recent post, he emphasized that China's response—possibly involving the sale of U.S. Treasury holdings—could influence rising yields on the 10-year note, prompting the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/21/u-s-debt-growth-outpaces-economy-warns-fed-chair/">Federal Reserve</a> to consider emergency measures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Pompliano also noted that other nations may enter trade negotiations within the coming week, potentially tempering the initial tariff spikes. Despite short-term volatility, he believes good companies will outperform and sees tariff levels stabilizing between 5–10% over time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>He encouraged investors to remain steady, stating, “Rough seas out there for a little longer. Those who don’t panic will do best.” </p>
