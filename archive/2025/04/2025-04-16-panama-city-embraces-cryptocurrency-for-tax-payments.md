<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Panama City has <a href="https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/22990090508169?utm_source=chatgpt.com">officially adopted</a> cryptocurrency as a method for settling municipal taxes and fees. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Following a city council vote, residents can now use <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/10/bitcoin-expert-debunks-megawatt-land-pricing-myth/">Bitcoin</a> (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USD Coin (USDC), and Tether (USDT) to pay for services such as taxes, licenses, and fines. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Mayor Mayer Mizrachi announced the initiative, <a href="https://www.binance.com/en-IN/square/post/04-16-2025-panama-city-council-approves-cryptocurrency-payments-for-public-services-22996796778265?utm_source=chatgpt.comhttps://www.binance.com/en-IN/square/post/04-16-2025-panama-city-council-approves-cryptocurrency-payments-for-public-services-22996796778265?utm_source=chatgpt.com">highlighting</a> that while cryptocurrencies will be accepted, all funds will be converted to U.S. dollars upon receipt. To facilitate this, the city has partnered with a banking institution to handle the conversion process. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p><a href="https://thedefiant.io/news/regulation/panama-city-council-votes-to-accept-bitcoin-cryptocurrencies-tax-payments-7e8fd006?utm_source=chatgpt.com">This move</a> positions Panama City as a leader in cryptocurrency integration within municipal operations, potentially influencing other cities to consider similar approaches. ​</p>
