<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/21/chase-atm-glitch-is-a-crime-says-experts/">​JPMorgan Chase</a> CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/07/jamie-dimon-warns-trump-tariffs-could-raise-us-prices/">Jamie Dimon</a> has urged the United States to re-engage with China to resolve the escalating trade war, warning that continued economic hostilities could erode U.S. credibility and increase the risk of a global recession.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgTBJ8olCGY&amp;pp=ygU3SmFtaWUgRGltb24gVXJnZXMgVS5TLiB0byBSZXN1bWUgVHJhZGUgVGFsa3Mgd2l0aCBDaGluYQ%3D%3D">recent interview</a> with the Financial Times, Dimon <a href="https://www.pymnts.com/economy/2025/jamie-dimon-urges-immediate-united-states-engagement-with-china-over-tariffs/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emphasized</a> the urgency of resuming dialogue, stating, "I don't think there's any engagement right now … it doesn't have to wait a year. It could start tomorrow." ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Dimon <a href="https://www.moneycontrol.com/world/jpmorgan-chase-ceo-warns-trump-s-tariffs-could-undermine-us-global-leadership-article-12995723.html/amp?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expressed concerns</a> that the U.S.'s aggressive tariff policies, particularly the recent 145% tariffs imposed on Chinese imports, have introduced significant uncertainty into global markets. He noted that such measures challenge the perception of the U.S. as a stable and reliable economic leader.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The CEO's remarks come amid heightened tensions following President Donald Trump's announcement of sweeping tariffs under the "Liberation Day" initiative. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>While the administration has paused some tariffs for 90 days, the increased duties on Chinese goods remain in effect, prompting retaliatory measures from Beijing. <a href="https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-tariffs-china-nvidia-stock-eu-uk-latest-news-b2734136.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">The Independent</a>​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Dimon also highlighted the broader implications of the trade war, cautioning that the U.S. should not assume a "divine right to success" and must actively work to maintain its economic leadership through strategic alliances and fair trade practices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In light of these developments, Dimon called for immediate and respectful engagement with China and other global partners to stabilize markets and restore confidence in the international economic system.</p>
