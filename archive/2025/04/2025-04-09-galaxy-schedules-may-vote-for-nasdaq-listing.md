<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Crypto-focused financial services firm <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/galaxy-digital-ends-argo-bitcoin-hosting-eyes-hpc-shift/">Galaxy Digital</a> has scheduled a Special Meeting of Shareholders on May 9, 2025, to vote on a corporate reorganization and proposed Nasdaq listing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/31/sec-clarifies-bitcoin-mining-not-a-securities-transaction/">U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission</a> has formally approved Galaxy’s registration statement, clearing a major hurdle toward its plan to become a U.S.-listed public company.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Shareholders of record as of April 7, 2025, are eligible to vote at the meeting. If approved, Galaxy will move forward with its domestication from the Cayman Islands to Delaware, facilitating its transition to a U.S. holding company structure. The reorganization is expected to pave the way for Galaxy’s shares to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol <strong>GLXY</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz-predicts-500k-bitcoin-with-u-s-reserve-establishment/">Galaxy</a>, which is currently listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange, aims to unlock broader investor access and enhance liquidity by joining Nasdaq. The company’s leadership says the move aligns with its long-term strategy of deepening its footprint in U.S. capital markets.</p>
