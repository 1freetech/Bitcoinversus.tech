<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <strong>laser printing process</strong> is a precise, multi-step method that relies on electrostatics, heat, and toner to produce high-quality prints quickly and efficiently. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It begins with the <strong>processing</strong> stage, where the printer receives the data from the computer and organizes it into printable instructions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the <strong>charging</strong> phase, a primary corona wire or roller applies a uniform negative charge across the surface of the <strong>photosensitive drum</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <strong>exposing</strong> stage follows, where a laser beam selectively discharges areas of the drum to form the latent image by altering the charge in the pattern of the print job.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next, during the <strong>developing</strong> stage, <strong>toner particles</strong> (which carry a negative charge) are attracted to the discharged areas of the drum, adhering only where the laser has written. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <strong>transferring</strong> stage involves moving the toner from the drum to the paper, which is positively charged by a transfer corona or roller to attract the negatively charged toner. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the <strong>fusing</strong> stage, a heated roller melts and presses the toner into the paper fibers, creating a permanent image. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The final <strong>cleaning</strong> stage wipes residual toner off the drum and resets its charge, preparing it for the next page. Technicians studying for the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/comptia-a/">CompTIA A+ </a>Core 1 exam must understand these stages not only to troubleshoot print issues, but also to maintain drum units, fusers, and toner cartridges effectively.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
