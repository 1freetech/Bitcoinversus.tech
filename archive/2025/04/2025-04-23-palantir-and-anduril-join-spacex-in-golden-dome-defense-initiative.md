<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Elon Musk's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/michael-saylor-declares-we-are-going-to-mars-in-recent-post/">SpaceX</a>, in collaboration with Palantir Technologies and Anduril Industries, <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/musks-spacex-is-frontrunner-build-trumps-golden-dome-missile-shield-2025-04-17/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has emerged</a> as a leading contender to develop President Donald Trump's proposed "Golden Dome" missile defense system. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The initiative aims to deploy a network of 400 to over 1,000 satellites for missile detection and tracking, along with approximately 200 armed satellites to intercept threats. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Musk, a significant Trump supporter and current government advisor, has proposed a subscription-based model for the system, shifting ownership from the government to the provider. This approach has raised concerns within the Pentagon regarding control and long-term costs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The U.S. Missile Defense Agency <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/us-seeks-ideas-golden-dome-missile-interceptors-space-2025-04-04/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has solicited</a> information from defense contractors on space-based interceptors capable of neutralizing incoming missile threats during various phases of flight. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This move indicates a strategic shift towards incorporating space-based elements into national defense. The Pentagon's interest in such technologies underscores the evolving nature of missile threats and the need for advanced defense systems. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The Golden Dome project <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/musks-spacex-is-frontrunner-build-trumps-golden-dome-missile-shield-2025-04-17/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has attracted</a> interest from over 180 companies, including major defense contractors and startups. While SpaceX's advanced infrastructure provides a competitive edge, some experts question the feasibility of such an extensive defense initiative. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Concerns have also been raised in Congress about potential conflicts of interest due to Musk's dual role as a contractor and special government employee. The Pentagon is still in the early stages of decision-making, with initial capabilities expected by 2026 and full deployment anticipated post-2030. </p>
