<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining</a> business development now stretches far beyond just energy costs or <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/29/compass-mining-plans-30-mw-bitcoin-facility-in-iowa/">hardware deployment</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It includes brokering long-term power agreements with utilities, acquiring land with favorable zoning laws, and forming strategic partnerships with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/28/bitmains-asic-dominance-faces-new-competition/">ASIC manufacturers</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/31/axeos-2-6-1-brings-new-features-and-controls-to-bitcoin-mining-machine/">firmware</a> teams, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/27/two-companies-eye-125-billion-ai-data-centers-in-north-dakota/">AI compute</a> providers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
