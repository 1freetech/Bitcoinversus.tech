<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of July 2024, the United States' national debt <a href="https://budget.house.gov/press-release/us-national-debt-surpasses-35-trillion">surpassed</a> $35 trillion, marking a dramatic increase from $17 trillion just a decade ago. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This surge, driven by both structural fiscal policies and pandemic-related expenditures, presents significant concerns for the future of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">U.S. economy</a>. Economists warn of potential long-term consequences, such as rising interest costs that could stunt economic growth and elevate inflation​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/dallc2b7e-2024-09-11-21.07.49-a-cool-retro-video-game-style-image-blending-classic-16-bit-aesthetics-with-modern-elements-depicting-a-futuristic-bitcoin-landscape.-the-scene-shows.webp">Fred Krueger</a>, a noted financial analyst, emphasized the severity of the situation, posting on X (formerly Twitter): "The U.S. debt was $17 trillion 10 years ago; now it is $35 trillion. Double. Graph suggests we are on a path to $100 trillion in 10 years. Houston, we have a problem." Krueger's stark warning reflects the growing alarm among fiscal experts​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The national debt has <a href="https://www.usmoneyreserve.com/news/executive-insights/u-s-national-debt-breaks-records-tops-35-trillion/">grown</a> at an unprecedented rate, with an additional $11.8 trillion added since 2020. Analysts suggest that without substantial intervention, this debt could reach $100 trillion in the next decade, continuing to burden future generations.</p>
