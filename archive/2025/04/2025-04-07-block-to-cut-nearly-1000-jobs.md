<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/cash-app-leads-all-finance-apps-on-apple-os/">​Block</a>, the financial technology company led by CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/culture-jack-dorseys-bluesky-gains-traction-post-election/">Jack Dorsey</a>, has <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/jack-dorseys-block-cuts-931-jobs-7241514/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announced</a> plans to lay off approximately 931 employees, representing about 8% of its workforce. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In an internal email to staff, Dorsey stated that the layoffs are part of organizational changes aimed at addressing strategic priorities, enhancing performance, and flattening the company's hierarchy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The reductions are categorized into three main areas: 391 positions are being eliminated for strategic reasons, 460 due to performance issues, and 80 managerial roles to streamline the organization's structure. Additionally, 193 managers will <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/jack-dorseys-block-cuts-931-jobs-7241514/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">transition</a> to individual contributor roles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Dorsey <a href="https://m.economictimes.com/tech/technology/jack-dorseys-fintech-block-to-lay-off-nearly-1000-employees/articleshow/119520124.cms?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emphasized</a> that these layoffs are not driven by financial targets or efforts to replace employees with artificial intelligence. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Instead, they are intended to raise performance standards and enable the company to operate more efficiently. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This marks the second significant round of layoffs at Block in just over a year, <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jack-dorsey-fintech-venture-lays-185202164.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">following </a>a similar reduction of approximately 1,000 employees in January 2024.</p>
