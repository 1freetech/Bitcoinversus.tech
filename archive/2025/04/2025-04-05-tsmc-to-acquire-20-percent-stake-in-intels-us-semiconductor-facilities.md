<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>​Intel Corporation and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) have reached a <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/intel-tsmc-tentatively-agree-form-chipmaking-joint-venture-information-reports-2025-04-03/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">preliminary agreement</a> to establish a joint venture aimed at operating Intel's semiconductor fabrication facilities in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/25/united-states-wastes-61-of-energy-per-lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory/">United States</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>According to sources cited by <em>The Information</em>, TSMC is expected to acquire a 20% stake in this new entity. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This development follows <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/intel-tsmc-tentatively-agree-form-chipmaking-joint-venture-information-reports-2025-04-03/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">encouragement from </a>U.S. government officials, including those from the White House and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/politics-bitcoin-advocate-howard-lutnick-appointed-u-s-secretary-of-commerce/">Department of Commerce</a>, who have been advocating for collaboration between the two companies to address Intel's ongoing challenges in the semiconductor sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In 2024, Intel reported a net loss of $18.8 billion, marking its first annual net loss since 1986. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The company has faced difficulties in capitalizing on the AI-driven chip boom and has encountered setbacks in customer and technical services compared to competitors like TSMC. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In response to these challenges, Intel appointed industry veteran Lip-Bu Tan as CEO in March 2025, aiming to revitalize the company's fortunes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>TSMC has also been expanding its presence in the United States, recently announcing a $100 billion investment to build five additional chip manufacturing plants. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>As of now, neither Intel nor TSMC have officially commented on the reported joint venture. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The agreement's specifics, including the operational structure and integration of the companies' manufacturing processes, remain under discussion. </p>
