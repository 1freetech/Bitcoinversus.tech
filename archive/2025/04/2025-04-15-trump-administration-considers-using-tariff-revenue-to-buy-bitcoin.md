<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/08/trump-signs-order-to-boost-coal-and-mineral-mining/">Trump administration</a> is exploring the use of tariff revenues to fund the acquisition of Bitcoin, <a href="https://beincrypto.com/trump-administration-bitcoin-reserve-tariff-revenues/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">aiming</a> to expand the nation's strategic cryptocurrency reserves without increasing taxes or national debt. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Bo Hines, Executive Director of the Presidential Council of Advisers on Digital Assets, discussed this initiative in a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6QBMP0pkgQ&amp;pp=ygUVdGhpbmsgY3J5cHRvIGJvIGhpbmVz">recent interview</a>, highlighting the administration's commitment to making the United States a leader in digital assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The reserve <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/currencies/donald-trump-strategic-cryptocurrency-reserve-executive-order-bitcoin-price-2025-3?utm_source=chatgpt.com">is funded</a> by Bitcoin seized in criminal and civil cases, with plans to expand holdings through budget-neutral strategies. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In addition to tariff revenues, the administration is <a href="https://decrypt.co/314816/tariffs-fund-us-bitcoin-reserve-buildup-white-house-advisor-bo-hines?utm_source=chatgpt.com">considering revaluing</a> U.S. Treasury gold certificates to generate funds for Bitcoin purchases. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This approach would leverage the current market value of gold without selling physical reserves, providing another budget-neutral method to increase Bitcoin holdings. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Senator Cynthia Lummis <a href="https://crypto.news/trump-administration-may-potentially-use-tariffs-revenue-to-buy-bitcoin-for-strategic-reserve/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">supports</a> the administration's efforts through the proposed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/07/the-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act-will-need-a-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure-bill-heres-why/">BITCOIN Act</a> of 2025, which aims to acquire up to one million BTC over five years. The legislation seeks to diversify the nation's reserves and strengthen its position in the global digital economy. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>While proponents argue that these measures <a href="https://cryptobriefing.com/tariffs-for-bitcoin-trump-administration/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">will enhance</a> national financial security and innovation, critics express concerns about potential conflicts of interest and the volatility of cryptocurrency markets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The administration maintains that its strategies are designed to protect taxpayers and promote long-term economic stability.</p>
