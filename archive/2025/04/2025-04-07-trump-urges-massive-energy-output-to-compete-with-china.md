<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>He asserts that achieving economic and technological superiority requires substantial energy resources, particularly from fossil fuels. This perspective aligns with his administration's policies aimed at expanding fossil fuel-based energy production. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For instance, Trump has <a href="https://www.axios.com/2025/01/23/trump-ai-power-plants-data-centers?utm_source=chatgpt.com">pledged</a> to open hundreds of coal-fired power plants to bolster the nation's energy capacity. Additionally, his administration has proposed fast-tracking approvals for power plants associated with AI data centers, highlighting the growing energy demands of emerging technologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Critics argue that these moves favor fossil fuel industries and may undermine environmental regulations, while supporters contend they are essential for national competitiveness.</p>
