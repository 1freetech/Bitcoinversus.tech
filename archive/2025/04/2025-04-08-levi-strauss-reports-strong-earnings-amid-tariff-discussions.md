<p class="has-medium-font-size"><strong>Shares Surge 11 Percent as Company Minimizes Impact of Trade Tensions</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Levi Strauss stock <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/5-things-to-know-before-the-stock-market-opens-april-08-2025-11711024?utm_source=chatgpt.com">jumped</a> 11% after the apparel company reported better-than-expected earnings for the first quarter of 2025. The company eased investor concerns by stating that potential pricing impacts tied to ongoing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/06/bitcoin-falls-to-80000-amid-anticipation-of-black-monday/">U.S. tariff disputes</a> would be minimal. </p>
