<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinist.com/blackrock-study-optimal-bitcoin-allocation/">claim</a><em> </em>that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">BlackRock</a> advises an 84.9% <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/dallc2b7e-2024-09-17-09.37.26-a-detailed-comic-book-style-illustration-of-an-official-financial-document-with-the-word-microstrategy-prominently-written-at-the-top.-the-document-.webp">Bitcoin allocation</a> in portfolios originates from a study the asset manager conducted in 2022. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This research analyzed Bitcoin's performance between July 2010 and December 2021 and suggested that for a 60-40 portfolio (60% equities, 40% bonds), the optimal allocation to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> could be as high as 84.9%. The study highlighted <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin's potential</a> for significant returns, despite its volatility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, it's important to note that this allocation recommendation was theoretical and part of an analysis based on historical data. BlackRock emphasized that while <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/01/uptober-momentum-bitcoin-leaps-3-in-15-minute-interval-igniting-market-optimism-and-liquidating-over-70-million-in-shorts/">Bitcoin's unique return profile</a> might justify such a high allocation under certain risk conditions, this does not represent their official advice to all investors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The findings illustrate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/20/bitcoin-whale-michael-saylor-posts-matthew-kratters-video-on-bitcoins-role-in-shaping-the-future-of-global-governance/">Bitcoin's potential</a> in certain investment scenarios rather than an endorsement for widespread portfolio reallocation on this scale.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This claim has sparked discussions due to Bitcoin's extreme volatility, and some experts argue that such a high allocation might not suit most investors, especially those with lower risk tolerance.</p>
