<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Samara Asset Group, a publicly listed German company, is set to <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/samara-asset-group-to-stack-up-bitcoin-with-33-million-bond/">issue</a> €30 million in bonds to bolster its Bitcoin holdings and acquire stakes in alternative investment funds. This move reflects the company’s strategy to enhance its diversified portfolio, with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/14/italian-bank-banca-sella-introduces-bitcoin-trading/">Bitcoin</a> serving as its primary treasury asset. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The bond will be managed by Pareto Securities and is expected to be listed on the Oslo and Frankfurt stock exchanges, targeting high-value investors with a minimum subscription amount of €100,000​</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Patrick Lowry emphasized the firm's long-term commitment to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/04/bernstein-predicts-90000-bitcoin-surge-if-trump-wins-2024-election/">Bitcoin</a>, noting that the bond proceeds will not only reinforce Samara’s balance sheet but also enable further investment in emerging technologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company’s innovative approach aligns with other major players like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/microstrategy-to-raise-700-million-for-bitcoin-acquisition/">MicroStrategy</a>, which similarly leverages Bitcoin as a key financial asset​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Samara AG’s commitment to driving progress through technology and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/04/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board-allocates-2-of-assets-to-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin investments</a> highlights its role as a leader in alternative asset management​.</p>
