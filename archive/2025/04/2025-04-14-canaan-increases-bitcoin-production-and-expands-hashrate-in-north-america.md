<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/24/canaan-inc-expands-self-mining-operations-in-texas-and-pennsylvania/">Canaan Inc</a>. reported a 9.8% increase in Bitcoin mined for March 2025, totaling 90 BTC. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The China-based manufacturer and mining operator continues to strengthen its presence in North America, adding 0.32 exahashes per second (EH/s) to its operational capacity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The company also confirmed plans to deploy an additional 4.7 EH/s in computing power, highlighting a major scaling initiative aimed at offsetting revenue volatility following the Bitcoin halving.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The firm launched its next-generation A16 mining machine during the same period, expected to improve operational efficiency across both internal mining operations and client deployments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Canaan stated that its revenue structure remained steady, with 58.6% derived from $CAN-related income, demonstrating consistency amid fluctuating market conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>According to <a class="">Canaan’s latest update</a>, the firm continues to blend its hardware sales and proprietary mining operations as part of its dual growth strategy.</p>
