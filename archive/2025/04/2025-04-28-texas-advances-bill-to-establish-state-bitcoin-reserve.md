<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/29/texas-legislator-giovanni-capriglione-advocates-for-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">Texas lawmakers</a> are making progress on a bill to create a state-managed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin reserve</a>, aligning with President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/politics-donald-trump-proposes-crypto-advisory-council/">Donald Trump's nationwide crypto reserve initiative</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Senate Bill 21, introduced by Senator Charles Schwertner, proposes the establishment of the Texas Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Senate Committee on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/politics-bitcoin-advocate-howard-lutnick-appointed-u-s-secretary-of-commerce/">Business and Commerce</a> has unanimously passed the bill, which now awaits a Senate floor vote. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A similar bill, HB1598, has been introduced in the House by Representative <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/29/texas-legislator-giovanni-capriglione-advocates-for-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">Giovanni Capriglione</a>. Both bills permit external Bitcoin donations, with varying terms for contributors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative coincides with Trump's push for a U.S. Crypto Strategic Reserve, encompassing cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ether, ADA, SOL, and XRP. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Texas, known for welcoming crypto mining facilities, aims to lead the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">crypto industry</a> in the United States.</p>
