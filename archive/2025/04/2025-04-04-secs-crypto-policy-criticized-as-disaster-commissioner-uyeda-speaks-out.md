<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC) has come <a href="https://coinjournal.net/news/sec-commissioner-says-agencys-approach-to-crypto-has-been-a-disaster-for-the-whole-industry-2/">under fire</a> from within its ranks, as Commissioner Mark Uyeda has openly labeled the agency's approach to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/28/analysis-bitcoin-hyper-regulation-may-stall-global-economic-growth/">cryptocurrency regulation</a> a “disaster for the whole industry.” </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Speaking on Fox Business during an appearance on <em>Mornings with Maria</em>, Uyeda <a href="https://x.com/BitcoinMagazine/status/1844398822195441868">expressed</a> dissatisfaction with the agency’s failure to provide clear regulatory guidance, instead opting for enforcement actions against various <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">cryptocurrency firms</a>. This has left the industry in a state of confusion and conflict​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The lawsuit challenges the SEC’s assertion that most cryptocurrencies qualify as securities, a stance that has sparked controversy across the digital asset sector. Uyeda noted that the SEC has done “nothing” to clarify its stance, leading to inconsistent rulings from courts​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Industry leaders, including Mark Cuban and executives from Coinbase and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/23/politics-ripple-co-founder-chris-larsen-endorses-kamala-harris-for-president/">Ripple Labs</a>, have echoed Uyeda’s concerns. Cuban has gone as far as calling for SEC Chair Gary Gensler’s removal, citing the stifling impact of current policies on innovation within the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/05/rumors-suggest-china-could-reconsider-crypto-ban/">cryptocurrency sector​</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While acknowledging the need for regulation, Uyeda emphasized the importance of clear, actionable guidelines, stating, “We need to lay out some clear guidance and interpretations on what exactly falls within and falls outside the securities laws.”​</p>
