<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>​Hongkong Post has <a href="https://hongkongfp.com/2025/02/07/hong-kong-post-office-to-continue-to-suspend-packages-to-the-us-amid-tariff-war/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announced</a> a suspension of postal services for goods destined for the United States. Effective April 27, 2025, the postal service will cease accepting airmail items containing goods bound for the U.S. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://bsky.app/profile/bitcoinversus.bsky.social/post/3lfxg2mzcs22l","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"bluesky-social"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This decision follows the U.S. government's move to eliminate the duty-free de minimis treatment for postal items from Hong Kong and to increase tariffs on such goods starting May 2. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Hongkong Post has described these actions as "unreasonable" and "bullying," stating that it will not collect the imposed tariffs on behalf of the U.S. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Hong Kong government issued a formal statement through its logistics department expressing “strong opposition” to the U.S. decision. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Officials noted that under the new arrangement, postal services are now required to act as de facto customs agents—a role they refuse to adopt. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Critics argue the tariffs are part of a broader campaign by the U.S. to restrict trade from territories perceived as having close ties to China</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>It's important to note that this suspension applies only to items containing goods; postal items consisting solely of documents are not affected. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>For those in Hong Kong needing to send goods to the U.S., <a href="https://www.businessinsider.com/hongkong-post-suspends-postal-service-for-goods-from-us-2025-4?utm_source=chatgpt.com">alternative shipping</a> methods such as private courier services (e.g., FedEx, UPS, DHL) may be considered, though they might incur higher costs.</p>
