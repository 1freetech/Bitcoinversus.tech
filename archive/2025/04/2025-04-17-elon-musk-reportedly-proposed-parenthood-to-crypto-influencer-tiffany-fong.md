<p>According to a report by <em>The Wall Street Journal</em>, Elon Musk <a href="https://coinness.com/en/news/61728?utm_source=chatgpt.com">privately messaged</a> cryptocurrency influencer Tiffany Fong in late 2024, proposing that she consider having his child. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The two <a href="https://www.vanityfair.com/news/story/elon-musks-children-ashley-st-clair?utm_source=chatgpt.com">had not met</a> in person prior to this exchange. Fong declined the offer, expressing concerns about the implications for her personal and professional life. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Following her refusal, Musk <a href="https://coinness.com/en/news/61728?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reportedl</a>y unfollowed Fong on X (formerly Twitter), leading to a noticeable decline in her engagement and earnings on the platform. Fong had previously experienced a surge in visibility and income due to Musk's interactions with her content. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Fong <a href="https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/elon-musk-asked-influencer-to-carry-his-child-she-worried-about-earnings-report-8184594?utm_source=chatgpt.com">addressed the situation publicly</a>, stating that she had never met Musk in person and was not pregnant with his child, countering circulating rumors. She emphasized her preference for a traditional family structure and expressed discomfort with the unsolicited proposal. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This incident is part of a broader pattern of Musk's <a href="https://www.vanityfair.com/news/story/elon-musks-children-ashley-st-clair?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reported</a> efforts to expand his progeny. The <em>Wall Street Journal</em> detailed Musk's interactions with multiple women, including conservative influencer Ashley St. Clair, with whom he shares a child. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Musk has expressed concerns about declining birth rates and has suggested that increasing the population is essential for civilization's future. ​</p>
