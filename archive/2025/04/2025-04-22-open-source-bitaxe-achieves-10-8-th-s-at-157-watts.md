<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">An individual from the OSMU Discord community has <a href="https://twitter.com/SoloSatoshi/status/1885465689940537594?utm_source=chatgpt.com">engineered</a> a NerdOctaxe <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/argentinas-central-bank-hosts-bitcoin-mining-art-exhibit/">mining rig</a> achieving a hash rate of 10.8 terahashes per second (TH/s) with a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/26/bitcoin-and-energy-the-correlation-between-wealth-and-consumption/">power consumption</a> of 157 watts, resulting in an efficiency of 14.5 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/nerdqaxe-achieves-4-8th-s-with-6-layer-pcb-upgrade/">joules per terahash</a> (J/TH).</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The NerdOctaxe project is an open-source initiative aimed at developing efficient <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/28/rising-demand-bull-market-leads-to-asic-mining-hardware-price-hikes/">cryptocurrency mining hardware</a>. The reported performance metrics indicate a significant advancement in energy efficiency for mining operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Community members have expressed interest in the design and potential scalability of the NerdOctaxe rig, considering its impressive efficiency compared to existing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">mining solutions</a>.</p>
