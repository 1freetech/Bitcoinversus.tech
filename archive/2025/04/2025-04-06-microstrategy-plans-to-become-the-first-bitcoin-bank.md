<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/22/microstrategy-chairman-michael-saylor-highlights-bitcoin-performance-amid-institutional-acceptance/">MicroStrategy</a>, under the leadership of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">Michael Saylor</a>, has <a href="https://www.ibtimes.com/microstrategys-ultimate-goal-become-1-trillion-bitcoin-bank-3746615">developed</a> a gameplan to transform the company into the world’s first Bitcoin bank with a market valuation of $1 trillion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The software firm has been steadily accumulating Bitcoin since 2020, and currently holds 252,220 BTC, making it the largest corporate holder of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/bitcoin-set-for-a-major-surge-with-etf-options-says-galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz/">digital currency</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This aggressive strategy, financed by debt and equity offerings, reflects Saylor's confidence that Bitcoin will eventually reach millions per coin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor envisions <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a> as a financial pioneer that bridges the traditional USD capital markets with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>, positioning itself as a revolutionary force in the emerging Bitcoin-driven economy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Instead of lending out <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/hydro-cooling-technology-in-bitcoin-mining-a-green-solution-for-mexicos-energy-challenges/">Bitcoin</a>, the company’s approach centers on borrowing capital at low interest rates, offering slightly higher returns to lenders, and investing heavily in Bitcoin​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor has emphasized that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> is the top-performing asset of the 21st century and that its long-term value lies in its deflationary nature and ability to hedge against inflation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MicroStrategy’s bold ambition is not only to hold Bitcoin but to create financial instruments like equities and debt tied to the digital currency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://coinjournal.net/news/microstrategys-endgame-is-to-be-the-leading-bitcoin-bank-michael-saylor/">strategy</a>, according to Saylor, could drive the company’s valuation to $1 trillion as Bitcoin becomes an essential part of the global financial landscape​.</p>
