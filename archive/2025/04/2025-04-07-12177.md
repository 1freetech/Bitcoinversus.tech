<p>Vladimir Guerrero Jr. and the Toronto Blue Jays have <a href="https://nypost.com/2025/04/07/sports/vladimir-guerrero-jr-agrees-to-monster-500-million-blue-jays-contract-extension/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">agreed</a> to a monumental 14-year, $500 million contract extension, ensuring the 26-year-old slugger remains with the team through the 2039 season. ​</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This <a href="https://apnews.com/article/0250c8424126930d98d54b03e1fb3182?utm_source=chatgpt.com">agreement</a> ranks as the third-largest in Major League Baseball history, trailing only Juan Soto's $765 million deal with the New York Mets and Shohei Ohtani's $700 million contract with the Los Angeles Dodgers. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Guerrero, a four-time All-Star and the 2021 American League MVP runner-up, has been a cornerstone of the Blue Jays' lineup since his debut in 2019. Over his career, he has compiled a .287 batting average with 160 home runs and 511 RBIs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The contract does not include any deferred payments and features a full no-trade clause, reflecting the organization's commitment to Guerrero as a central figure in their long-term plans. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In terms of cryptocurrency, with Bitcoin currently trading at approximately $78,358, Guerrero's $500 million contract equates to about 6,380 BTC.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This extension not only <a href="https://apnews.com/article/0250c8424126930d98d54b03e1fb3182?utm_source=chatgpt.com">secures</a> Guerrero's future with the Blue Jays but also signifies the franchise's dedication to building a competitive team around their homegrown talent.​</p>
