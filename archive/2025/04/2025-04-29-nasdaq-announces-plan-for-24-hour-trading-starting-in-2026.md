<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nasdaq Inc. has <a href="https://smallcaps.com.au/nasdaq-24-hour-trading-2026-global-demand-us-equities/">announced</a> plans to implement 24-hour trading on its flagship U.S. exchange, aiming to capitalize on the increasing global demand for U.S. equities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The exchange operator has initiated discussions with regulators and expects to launch continuous five-days-a-week trading in the second half of 2026, pending regulatory approval. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10836,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-10.png?w=623" alt="" class="wp-image-10836" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/nasdaq-allow-24-hour-trading-5-days-week">strategic move</a> is driven by a surge in international interest, with foreign holdings of U.S. equities reaching $17 trillion as of June 2024, a 97% increase since 2019. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nasdaq President Tal Cohen highlighted that the global growth of investor demand for U.S. equities presents a pivotal moment to broaden investor access and redefine market functions. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By extending trading hours, Nasdaq <a href="https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/nasdaq-plans-24-hour-trading-tap-into-growing-international-demand-2025-03-07/">aims to attract investors</a> across different time zones, potentially increasing trading volumes and improving market liquidity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Expanding to a 24-hour trading model presents challenges, including ensuring liquidity during overnight hours and addressing concerns about fair market pricing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nasdaq plans to collaborate with regulators, market participants, and infrastructure providers to design systems that maintain market stability and investor confidence. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Currently, some brokerages, such as Charles Schwab and Robinhood, offer limited 24-hour trading services. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nasdaq's move towards round-the-clock trading represents a significant shift in the U.S. equity market landscape, aiming to meet the evolving needs of global investors and adapt to the increasing demand for continuous market access. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
