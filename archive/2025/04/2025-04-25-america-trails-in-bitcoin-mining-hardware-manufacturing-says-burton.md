<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In the Season 14 premiere of <em>BitcoinVersus.Tech</em>, engineer and consultant Matthan “Bill” Burton delivered a pointed message about America's position in the bitcoin mining hardware sector. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://bsky.app/profile/bitcoinversus.bsky.social/post/3lfxg2mzcs22l","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"bluesky-social"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Burton stated that the dominance of Chinese manufacturers continues to define the global landscape, placing the U.S. behind in the critical area of production infrastructure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>“The simple fact is that the three largest #bitcoin mining hardware manufacturers are in China. So when it comes to bitcoin mining #hardware manufacturing specifically — if we were keeping score today, America would be, in fact, losing, folks,” said Burton.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/28/bitmains-asic-dominance-faces-new-competition/">Bitmain</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">MicroBT</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/24/canaan-inc-expands-self-mining-operations-in-texas-and-pennsylvania/">Canaan</a> — all headquartered in China — control the majority of global ASIC production. Despite growing investment in U.S.-based <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/08/business-development-in-bitcoin-mining/">bitcoin mining</a> facilities, Burton emphasized that without domestic hardware manufacturing, America's operational strength remains dependent on overseas supply chains, which could prove vulnerable in geopolitical or supply-related disruptions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>According to industry watchers, Burton’s comments could renew calls for localized fabrication, advanced chip R&amp;D, and greater investment in open-source mining hardware projects across the U.S. and Europe【source: <a class="">CoinDesk</a>】.</p>
