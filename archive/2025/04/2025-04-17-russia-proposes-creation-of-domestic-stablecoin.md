<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The Russian Finance Ministry has <a href="https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/25/04/44849027/russia-reportedly-looking-into-creating-its-own-stablecoin?utm_source=chatgpt.com">proposed</a> the development of a domestically issued stablecoin. This initiative comes in response to recent actions by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/10/tether-surpasses-blackrock-in-2023-profits-by-700-million/">Tether</a>, which froze digital wallets linked to Russian entities, highlighting the vulnerabilities of relying on foreign-controlled digital assets.​</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Osman Kabaloev, Deputy Head of the Financial Policy Department at the Finance Ministry, <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/russia-must-build-its-own-stablecoins-finance-ministry-official-says/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emphasized</a> the need for Russia to establish its own stablecoin infrastructure. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>He suggested that these stablecoins could be pegged to non-dollar currencies, such as the ruble or the Chinese yuan, to mitigate the risks associated with dollar-denominated assets. \</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The proposal aligns with Russia's broader strategy to reduce dependence on Western financial systems, especially in light of ongoing sanctions. By developing a sovereign stablecoin, Russia aims to facilitate international trade and cross-border transactions without the constraints imposed by foreign regulatory bodies. ​<a href="https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/25/04/44849027/russia-reportedly-looking-into-creating-its-own-stablecoin?utm_source=chatgpt.com" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Benzinga</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>While the Central Bank of Russia has traditionally <a href="https://www.bittime.com/en/blog/rusia-berencana-melakukan-regulasi-stablecoin-untuk-transaksi-lintas-perbatasan?utm_source=chatgpt.com">maintained a cautious stance</a> on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">cryptocurrencies</a>, it has shown openness to exploring digital financial assets for international settlements. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The development of a domestic stablecoin could serve as a pivotal step in Russia's efforts to modernize its financial infrastructure and assert greater control over its economic transactions. ​</p>
