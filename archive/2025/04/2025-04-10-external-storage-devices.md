<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>External storage devices, like <strong>USB flash drives</strong>, <strong>external hard drives (HDDs)</strong>, and <strong>portable solid-state drives (SSDs)</strong>, are essential tools for storing and transferring data. USB flash drives are compact, portable, and often used for quick file sharing between devices, with capacities ranging from 4GB to 2TB. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>External HDDs, such as the <strong>WD My Passport</strong>, offer high-capacity storage (up to 20TB) at an affordable cost, making them ideal for backups and archiving. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Portable SSDs, like the <strong>Samsung T7</strong>, provide faster read and write speeds, ensuring quick access to large files like videos and software applications. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Many external storage devices also support encryption and password protection to enhance data security. They connect via interfaces such as <strong>USB-A</strong>, <strong>USB-C</strong>, or <strong>Thunderbolt</strong>. These devices are indispensable for expanding storage and ensuring data portability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
