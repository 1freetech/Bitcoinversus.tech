<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Former <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/22/caroline-ellison-sentenced-to-24-months-for-role-in-ftx-fraud/">FTX</a> CEO Sam Bankman-Fried <a href="https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/crime/sam-bankman-fried-s-latest-california-prison-once-housed-al-capone/ar-AA1D3r17?ocid=BingNewsSerp">has been transferred</a> from a temporary federal holding facility to FCI Herlong, a medium-security federal correctional institution located in northern California. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The facility, which once housed notorious crime figure Al Capone during his era, is now the long-term residence for Bankman-Fried following his conviction and sentencing for fraud and conspiracy tied to the collapse of cryptocurrency exchange FTX</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Bankman-Fried’s legal team had requested placement in a facility with accommodations for his mental health and vegan dietary needs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>While FCI Herlong is not specifically known for those accommodations, federal officials stated that the prison met the Bureau of Prisons’ standards for housing inmates convicted of non-violent white-collar crimes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>His sentence is 25 years, handed down in March 2024, and is being served without eligibility for early parole</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>CNBC reported that prosecutors described Bankman-Fried’s offenses as among the largest financial frauds in American history, noting that he misused over $8 billion in customer funds through deceptive practices and political influence. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>His sentencing judge, Lewis Kaplan, denied leniency and highlighted a lack of remorse. FCI Herlong has now become the focal point for a case that once rocked the global cryptocurrency industry</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
