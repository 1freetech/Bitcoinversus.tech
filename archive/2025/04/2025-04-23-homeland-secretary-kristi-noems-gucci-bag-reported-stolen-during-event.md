<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem's $4,400 Gucci handbag <a href="https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/kristi-noem-gucci-bag-theft-how-security-b2738160.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">was stolen</a> while she dined at The Capital Burger in Washington, D.C. The bag contained approximately $3,000 in cash, a Louis Vuitton wallet, her DHS access badge, passport, driver's license, apartment keys, blank checks, medication, and other personal items. Noem was hosting her children and grandchildren for Easter celebrations. ​</p>

<p>Surveillance footage <a href="https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/kristi-noem-gucci-bag-theft-how-security-b2738160.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reviewed</a> by the Secret Service and D.C. Metropolitan Police shows a white male suspect wearing dark clothing, white sneakers, a black baseball cap, and an N95 mask. He entered the restaurant at 7:52 p.m., sat at a table adjacent to Noem's, and used his foot to slide her bag under his table. He then concealed the bag with his jacket and exited the restaurant within minutes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>At the time of the theft, two plainclothes Secret Service agents assigned to Noem <a href="https://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/kristi-noem-purse-stolen-pass-bag-76sqbr000?utm_source=chatgpt.com">were present</a> in the restaurant but did not witness the incident. The Secret Service is currently investigating the matter. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The theft occurred just days after a similar incident at a nearby restaurant, where another woman's purse was stolen by a suspect matching the same description. Authorities are investigating potential connections between the two cases. </p>
