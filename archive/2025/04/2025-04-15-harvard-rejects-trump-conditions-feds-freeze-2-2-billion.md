<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Harvard University has rejected proposed funding from the Trump-aligned America First Legal Foundation, which attempted to link federal support to compliance with demands such as eliminating diversity programs, cooperating with immigration enforcement, and altering admission policies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The institution issued a public response affirming its commitment to academic freedom and nondiscriminatory practices, making clear it would not be swayed by politically motivated stipulations tied to federal grants. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/sfmcguire79/status/1911830090519871826","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Per the Boston Globe, the federal government responded by freezing approximately $2.2 billion in pending or allocated funding to Harvard University, citing ongoing investigations into alleged misuse of grant funds and foreign influence. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The sudden freeze came shortly after the public dispute, leading some legal analysts to view the measure as politically retaliatory, although federal agencies maintain the action stems from unrelated procedural reviews.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
