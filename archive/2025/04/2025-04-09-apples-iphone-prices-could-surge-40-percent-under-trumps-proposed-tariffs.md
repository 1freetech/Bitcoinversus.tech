<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The plan, which proposes a 10 percent universal baseline tariff on all imports and a 60 percent tariff on goods from China, aims to reduce the U.S. trade deficit but could impact consumer prices significantly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Trump’s sweeping tariff strategy would directly affect Apple, which assembles most of its products in China. Analysts project that a 60 percent tariff on Chinese imports could force Apple to increase iPhone prices in the U.S. market, passing on the cost to consumers to preserve margins. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Apple, which relies heavily on global supply chains, has not publicly commented on how it would respond to the proposed measures.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The 40 percent price hike estimate underscores the potential consequences of tariffs on high-demand consumer tech, raising concerns about inflation and economic volatility during an election cycle that already features rising tensions over trade and manufacturing.</p>
