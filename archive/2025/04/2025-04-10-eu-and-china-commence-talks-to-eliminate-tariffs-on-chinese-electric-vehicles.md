<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The European Union and China have initiated formal negotiations aimed at abolishing EU-imposed tariffs on Chinese electric vehicles (EVs). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>These discussions seek to replace the existing tariffs with a minimum pricing agreement, ensuring fair competition while addressing concerns over market distortions caused by alleged subsidies. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In October 2024, the EU implemented tariffs reaching up to 45.3% on Chinese EV imports, <a href="https://electrek.co/2025/04/10/china-just-won-the-ev-race-due-to-trump-its-now-negotiating-closer-trade-with-eu/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">citing</a> unfair subsidies that undercut European manufacturers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The proposed minimum pricing mechanism would set a floor price for Chinese EVs entering the EU market, aiming to level the playing field without resorting to punitive tariffs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>German automakers and industry associations have <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/eu-china-start-talks-lifting-eu-tariffs-chinese-electric-vehicles-handelsblatt-2025-04-10/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expressed support</a> for the negotiations, emphasizing the importance of removing trade barriers to foster a balanced and competitive market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The Chinese Commerce Ministry confirmed that negotiations would commence immediately, reflecting a mutual interest in resolving trade tensions amicably. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
