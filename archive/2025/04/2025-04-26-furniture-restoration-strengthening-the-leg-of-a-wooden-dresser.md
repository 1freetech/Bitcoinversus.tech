<p style="text-transform:none">This was a project that I wanted to take care of months ago, but things come up, life happens. I was fortunate to have enough time to restore a family member's piece of furniture.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It was important that i do this because, well, working isn't always about the money. Sometimes, you just want to restore something because <em>it can be restored</em>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Woodworking isn't my expertise, but I enjoyed reinforcing the strength of the socket of the back right leg. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">I simply added a new block from the spare wood that I had available. I lodged the wooden block with standard 2-1/2" drywall screws.</p>
