<p>Canaan Inc., a leading provider of high-performance computing solutions, has unveiled the Avalon Q, a professional-grade Bitcoin mining machine tailored for home users. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>This <a href="https://miningdisrupt.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announcement</a> was made at Mining Disrupt 2025, a prominent industry conference held in South Florida.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The Avalon Q delivers up to 90 terahashes per second (TH/s) of hash power with adjustable power consumption ranging from 800W to 1600W. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://youtu.be/i7EjOIqtLGU?si=HMZigxWSVgMu6C6s","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This adaptability allows it to integrate seamlessly with standard household electrical systems operating between 110V and 240V. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Notably, it is the first professional-grade Bitcoin miner to support 110V home power worldwide, enabling users to optimize operations based on electricity costs and mining profitability. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Designed with home environments in mind, the Avalon Q operates at a noise level of just 45 decibels, comparable to a quiet library. This whisper-quiet operation makes it suitable for placement in home offices, garages, or basements without causing significant noise disturbances. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The user-friendly design of the Avalon Q is complemented by the Avalon Family APP, which allows users to monitor and manage their mining operations conveniently. Additionally, Canaan offers a 360-day warranty, providing customers with confidence in the product's reliability and support. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
