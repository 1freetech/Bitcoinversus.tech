<p>CompTIA, short for the Computing Technology Industry Association, is a globally recognized nonprofit trade organization that issues professional IT certifications. It is best known for certifications like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/comptia-a/">A+</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/tag/comptia-network/">Network+</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/linux/">Linux+</a>, which validate foundational and specialized tech skills.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CompTIA plays a major role in setting industry standards for information technology education and workforce development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I really enjoy taking the exams. It's good for training purposes. I scheduled an exam for April 5th and then rescheduled for April 12th...</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I told close relatives about the rescheduling and recall scheduling the exam for the 12th sometime in the Month of March. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I wake up Sunday Morning to check on my exams and their status. The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/linux/">Linux+</a> exam is still there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I went ahead and Scheduled the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/comptia-a/">CompTIA A+</a> Core 2 exam just to make sure that the voucher and retake was still valid as well. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/comptia-a/">CompTIA A+</a> Core 1 exam was gone!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I was lowkey devastated. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To wake up and check up on something you've been preparing for for the last 4 months only for it to be gone or unavailable can be infuriating. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I've put a lot of time and effort in passing Core 1 (I have an entire module on the website dedicated to studying Core 1).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The other thing about it is it obviously costs to take the exam. So I had to pay for the exam again. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But here's the weird thing about that. The bank account (card) I've used to purchase the exam and other exams all of the sudden doesn't work!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I had to purchase a voucher from Professor Messer just to get a chance to take the Exam. The module I have is a simply a study resource. I'm not sure if someone on the staff got wind of my publishing and got upset or what but this is pretty much absolutely ridiculous. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I say all that to say that I just want to pass the exam and then give that knowledge back to other people. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You guys are welcome to review my <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/comptia-a/">CompTIA modules</a> to help you prepare for the exam. I will give an update on this one later. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If I'm not able to complete Core 1101, I will have to just take <a href="https://alison.com/topic/learn/84237/comptia-a-1000-part-1-course-assessment">the free version of the COMPTIA A+</a> exam.</p>
