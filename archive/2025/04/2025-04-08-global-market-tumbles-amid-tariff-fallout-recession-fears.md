<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>U.S. stock markets opened sharply lower on April 7, 2025, continuing the steep sell-off that began the previous week with President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/31/donald-trump-criticizes-bitcoin-taxation-advocates-for-bitcoin-friendly-reforms/">Donald Trump</a> announcing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/06/bitcoin-falls-to-80000-amid-anticipation-of-black-monday/">reciprocal tariffs</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The SPDR S&amp;P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) dropped to $505.28, a decline of 5.75%, while the SPDR Dow Jones Industrial Average ETF (DIA) fell 5.42% to $383.22. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Invesco QQQ Trust Series 1 (QQQ), tracking major tech stocks, plunged 6.09% to $422.67. </p>
