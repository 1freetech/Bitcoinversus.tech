<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>​OpenAI has <a href="https://9to5mac.com/2025/04/07/jony-ives-new-ai-product-is-not-a-phone-but-might-work-like-one/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reportedly</a> considered acquiring io Products, an AI device startup co-founded by its CEO Sam Altman and former Apple design chief Jony Ive, for at least $500 million. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The startup is developing AI-powered personal devices, including concepts such as a screenless "phone" and other AI-enabled household gadgets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>While designs are still in the early stages, some sources emphasize that the product is "not a phone." ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Founded after Ive's departure from Apple in 2019, io Products has attracted key former Apple personnel, including Tang Tan and Evans Hankey, who previously collaborated with Ive on iPhone designs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The startup has also <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/07/openai-reportedly-mulls-buying-jony-ive-and-sam-altmans-ai-hardware-startup/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">secured</a> funding from investors like Laurene Powell Jobs' Emerson Collective. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Discussions between OpenAI and io Products have explored various options, <a href="https://nypost.com/2025/04/07/business/sam-altmans-openai-mulls-buying-ex-apple-design-guru-jony-ives-ai-device-startup-for-500m-report/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">including</a> a full acquisition or a strategic partnership. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>If a deal proceeds, OpenAI would integrate io Products' engineering team, potentially positioning the company to compete more directly with Apple in the AI-integrated hardware market. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>This potential move aligns with OpenAI's broader strategy to expand its presence in consumer hardware, leveraging Ive's renowned design expertise to create innovative AI-driven devices.</p>
