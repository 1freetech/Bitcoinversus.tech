<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/08/bitmain-extends-hashboard-life-with-new-protective-temp-sensor-coating/">Bitmain Technologies</a>, a Beijing-based <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/news/crypto-mining-giant-bitmain-going-public-4050-billion-valuation/">cryptocurrency</a> hardware manufacturer, has become a dominant player in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining industry</a>. As of 2024, the company’s estimated valuation on the open market is approximately $100 Billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This is based off the fact that in 2021, a <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/news/crypto-mining-giant-bitmain-going-public-4050-billion-valuation/">report</a> by Investopedia claimed that Bitmain's then valuation at the time was about $40-50b. Due to the fact that AI has emerged in their gameplan, and adjusting for inflation, it's quite plausible that $100B is a conservative estimate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This is a significant leap from its $12 billion valuation during its 2018 funding round. The company has gained global recognition for its mining hardware, particularly its Antminer series, which is widely used by professional and large-scale Bitcoin miners​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Since its founding in 2013, Bitmain has not only led the charge in Bitcoin mining but has also diversified its business ventures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company operates major mining pools like BTC.com and Antpool, which together account for nearly half of the total mining capacity for Bitcoin. Additionally, Bitmain has <a href="https://craft.co/bitmain">expanded</a> into artificial intelligence (AI) chips, aiming to make 40% of its revenue from AI by 2028​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company faced <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitmain">challenges</a> when its planned IPO in 2018, which aimed for a $40 to $50 billion valuation, was ultimately shelved. Despite this setback, Bitmain continued to thrive by leveraging its expertise in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/bitaxe-ultra-achieves-1-2-th-s-with-immersion-cooling-technology/">ASIC chip development</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitmain's business now spans several countries, including the United States, where it has established data centers to support <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">Bitcoin mining operations</a>.​</p>
