<p><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Metaplanet Inc</a>., a Tokyo-listed investment firm, has expanded its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/semler-scientific-expands-bitcoin-holdings-with-17-7-million-purchase/">Bitcoin holdings</a> by <a href="https://bitbo.io/news/metaplanet-q1-bitcoin-yield/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">acquiring</a> an additional 696 BTC, bringing its total to 4,046 BTC. This acquisition was executed through cash-secured put options, allowing the company to purchase Bitcoin at an effective price of ¥13.48 million per BTC, below the spot price at the time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":12450,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In the first quarter of 2025, Metaplanet's Bitcoin Income Generation business <a href="https://cryptoslate.com/metaplanet-becomes-9th-largest-corporate-bitcoin-holder-after-new-purchase/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reported revenue</a> of ¥770 million, equivalent to approximately 50.26 BTC. This performance resulted in a Bitcoin Yield of 95.6% for the quarter, surpassing the company's 35% quarterly benchmark. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>With these strategic moves, Metaplanet has become the <a href="https://cryptoslate.com/metaplanet-becomes-9th-largest-corporate-bitcoin-holder-after-new-purchase/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">ninth-largest</a> corporate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/12/decoding-the-bitcoin-elite-top-10-holders-revealed/">Bitcoin holder</a> globally, surpassing firms such as Bitcoin Group and Semler Scientific. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>As of April 10, 2025, Bitcoin (BTC) is trading at $81,575.00, reflecting a 6.1% increase from the previous close.</p>
