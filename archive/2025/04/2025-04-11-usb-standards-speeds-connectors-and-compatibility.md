<p><strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/12/serial-communication-and-its-role-in-data-transmission/">Universal Serial Bus</a> (USB)</strong> is a standardized interface used to connect peripheral devices to computers, supporting both <strong>data transfer</strong> and <strong>power delivery</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Since its introduction in the 1990s, USB has evolved through several versions, each offering significant improvements in <strong>bandwidth</strong>, <strong>power output</strong>, and <strong>connector type</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The most common USB standards include <strong>USB 1.1</strong>, <strong>USB 2.0</strong>, <strong>USB 3.0</strong>, <strong>USB 3.1</strong>, <strong>USB 3.2</strong>, and <strong>USB4</strong>. Each version is backward compatible with previous versions, but performance will default to the lowest common standard.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>USB 2.0, often called <strong>Hi-Speed USB</strong>, supports up to <strong>480 Mbps</strong>, while <strong>USB 3.0</strong> (also referred to as <strong>SuperSpeed</strong>) introduced <strong>5 Gbps</strong> transfer rates and <strong>blue-colored ports</strong>. USB 3.1 Gen 2 and USB 3.2 increased speeds to <strong>10 Gbps</strong> and beyond, using both <strong>Type-A</strong> and <strong>Type-C</strong> connectors. The latest, <strong>USB4</strong>, reaches up to <strong>40 Gbps</strong>, supports <strong>Thunderbolt 3 compatibility</strong>, and relies primarily on the <strong>reversible USB-C connector</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In addition to faster speeds, modern USB versions support enhanced <strong>power delivery (PD)</strong>, allowing devices like laptops, monitors, and external drives to be powered entirely through a USB cable. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>For <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/printer-overview-laser-inkjet-thermal-dot-matrix-comptia-a/">CompTIA A+</a> candidates, it's important to identify USB versions by port color, symbol, and physical connector type, while also understanding their roles in <strong>data transfer, charging, and peripheral connectivity</strong>.</p>
