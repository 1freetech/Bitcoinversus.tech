<p>Access Points (APs) are networking devices that extend the wireless coverage of a network by acting as a relay between wired and wireless devices. Unlike routers, which manage entire network traffic, access points focus specifically on connecting Wi-Fi-enabled devices to a wired network infrastructure. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>They are essential in environments requiring wide and reliable wireless coverage, such as offices, schools, or large homes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Modern access points, such as the <strong>Ubiquiti UniFi</strong> or <strong>Cisco Aironet series</strong>, often support features like <strong>dual-band Wi-Fi</strong>, <strong>802.11ac/ax (Wi-Fi 5/6)</strong> standards, and <strong>MU-MIMO technology</strong>, enabling simultaneous connections for multiple devices. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Access Points can be standalone or integrated into mesh systems for seamless connectivity across vast areas.</p>
