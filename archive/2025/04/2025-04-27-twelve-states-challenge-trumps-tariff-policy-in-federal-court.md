<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>A coalition of 12 U.S. states <a href="https://apnews.com/article/0d6531b7f60aaa2f7c6c35e0a944d4a9?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has filed</a> a lawsuit against the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/15/trump-administration-considers-using-tariff-revenue-to-buy-bitcoin/">Trump administration</a>, challenging the legality of its recently imposed tariffs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The suit, lodged in the U.S. Court of International Trade in New York, contends that President Donald Trump exceeded his constitutional authority by unilaterally enacting tariffs without Congressional approval. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The plaintiff states include Oregon, Arizona, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Illinois, Maine, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, New York, and Vermont. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The legal challenge <a href="https://www.npr.org/2025/04/23/g-s1-62417/trump-tariff-lawsuits-states?utm_source=chatgpt.com">centers</a> on the administration's use of the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) to justify the tariffs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The states argue that the IEEPA was misapplied, as it is intended for genuine national emergencies, not for circumventing legislative processes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Arizona Attorney General Kris Mayes described the tariff policy as "insane" and "economically reckless," emphasizing its illegality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Connecticut Attorney General William Tong <a href="https://apnews.com/article/0d6531b7f60aaa2f7c6c35e0a944d4a9?utm_source=chatgpt.com">criticized</a> the tariffs as a "massive tax on Connecticut families" and detrimental to local businesses and employment. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The lawsuit asserts that only Congress possesses the constitutional authority to impose tariffs, and that the president's actions have disrupted the constitutional balance and caused economic instability. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>In response, the Trump administration has defended the tariffs, <a href="https://apnews.com/article/0d6531b7f60aaa2f7c6c35e0a944d4a9?utm_source=chatgpt.com">citing</a> national security concerns and the need to protect American industries. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>White House spokesperson Kush Desai dismissed the lawsuit as a partisan attack, asserting the administration's commitment to addressing national emergencies with all available tools, including tariffs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
