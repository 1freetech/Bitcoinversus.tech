<p>​<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/15/sports-shedeur-sanders-cam-ward-lead-2025-nfl-draft-qb-prospects/">Shedeur Sanders</a>, once projected as a first-round pick in the 2025 NFL Draft, has surprisingly <a href="https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/44844500/shedeur-sanders-not-selected-first-round-nfl-draft?utm_source=chatgpt.com">fallen out</a> of the first three rounds, remaining undrafted after 102 selections. Despite an impressive college career at Colorado, where he posted a 71.8% completion rate and earned Big 12 Offensive Player of the Year honors, several factors contributed to his draft slide. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Concerns among NFL teams included his high sack numbers—95 over two seasons—questions about his arm strength, and the perception that he might struggle outside the coaching environment provided by his father, Deion Sanders . </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Additionally, Sanders' limited participation in pre-draft events, such as the NFL Scouting Combine, and reports of underwhelming interviews may have further impacted teams' evaluations . As the draft continues into its final day, Sanders remains a notable talent still awaiting selection.​</p>
