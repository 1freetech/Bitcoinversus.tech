<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Most of Asia exempt as China absorbs full weight of U.S. trade war shift</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>President Donald Trump has granted a <a href="https://apnews.com/article/trump-tariffs-stock-market-china-recession-deals-e8e54a68397e6829e1d27552a1d7bfb9">90-day pause</a> on new tariffs for “non-retaliating countries,” largely benefiting Asian economies — with one glaring exception: China. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>The world's largest exporter now faces a cumulative 125% tariff on its goods entering the United States, intensifying the long-standing trade rift between the two powers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>According to <strong>Reuters</strong>, this decision <a href="https://www.reuters.com">signals</a> a renewed focus on reshoring critical supply chains while punishing nations seen as manipulating trade or failing to reciprocate on tariff structures.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>China, a dominant supplier of consumer electronics, manufacturing inputs, and machinery, is expected to face sharp contractions in U.S.-bound exports over the coming months. The move also coincides with rising pressure from American domestic manufacturers seeking competitive protection during an election year, per <strong>Bloomberg</strong> <a class="" href="https://www.bloomberg.com">source</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>U.S. allies in the Indo-Pacific, including South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, and the Philippines, were among those exempted under the <a href="https://www.politico.com">new directive</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Trump’s administration, often criticized for its unpredictability, appears to be recalibrating its trade lens to distinguish between adversarial and neutral partners in global supply chains, according to <strong>Politico</strong>.</p>
