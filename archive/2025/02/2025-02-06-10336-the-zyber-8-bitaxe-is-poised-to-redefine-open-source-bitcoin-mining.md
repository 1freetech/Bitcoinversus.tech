<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><br>Sources familiar with the project claim that the Zbyer 8 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Supra</a> (blue) can reach 5-7 TH/s and the Zyber 8 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/25/pierre-rochard-highlights-bitaxe-gamma-for-bitcoin-solo-mining/">Gamma</a> (purple) can reach as high as 13TH/s at about 150W/h.</p>
