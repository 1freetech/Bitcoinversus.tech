<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin mining</a> performance can be significantly improved with <strong>overclocking</strong>, especially during colder seasons like <strong>fall and winter</strong>, when ambient temperatures help regulate heat output. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/27/balance-performance-and-efficiency-underclocking-vs-overclocking/">Overclocking</a> increases a device’s operating frequency and voltage beyond stock settings, enhancing computational power while maintaining safe thermal conditions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In this case, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/17/exclusive-energy-report-using-solar-panels-to-power-micro-bitcoin-data-center/">Bitaxe 601 Gamma </a>default settings of <strong>525 MHz at 1.15V</strong> were increased to <strong>600 MHz at 1.17V</strong>, representing a <strong>14.3% increase in clock speed and a 1.7% voltage boost</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10416,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/screenshot-2025-02-20-002926.png?w=359" alt="" class="wp-image-10416" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As a result, the hash rate has jumped to <strong>1.65 TH/s</strong>, which is <strong>35.25% higher</strong> than the expected <strong>1.22 TH/s</strong>, all while reducing energy consumption to <strong>9.68 J/TH</strong>, a <strong>24.56% improvement</strong> over the expected <strong>12.83 J/TH</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10413,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/screenshot-2025-02-20-002931.png?w=358" alt="" class="wp-image-10413" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Key power and heat metrics indicate that <strong>overclocking under ideal conditions remains efficient and safe</strong>. The miner operates at <strong>15.9W</strong>, drawing <strong>5.2V</strong> of input power while running at <strong>600 MHz</strong> ASIC frequency with a <strong>1.17V measured ASIC voltage</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10411,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/screenshot-2025-02-20-004157-e1740055434933.png" alt="" class="wp-image-10411" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Temperature control plays a vital role in sustaining performance, and with an <strong>ASIC temperature of just 29°C</strong> and a <strong>voltage regulator temperature of 22°C</strong>, the system remains well within optimal limits. The cooling system is also working efficiently, with fan speed set at <strong>35% (5278 RPM)</strong>, balancing airflow and noise levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10420,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/screenshot-2025-02-20-004157-1-e1740055524476.png" alt="" class="wp-image-10420" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This data highlights why <strong>overclocking is best suited for colder seasons</strong>, where <strong>lower ambient temperatures naturally assist heat dissipation</strong>, allowing higher frequencies and voltage without overheating risks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The combination of increased hash rate, lower power consumption, and stable temperatures makes winter an optimal time for miners to push their hardware for maximum profitability.</p>
