<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 1926, the United Kingdom passed the <strong><a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo5/16-17/51/enacted?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Electricity (Supply) Act</a></strong>, a landmark policy that unified the nation’s fragmented power networks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The legislation established the <strong>Central Electricity Board (CEB)</strong> and created a standardized national grid, ensuring reliable <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/16/global-electricity-prices-averaged-0-162-per-kwh-in-march-2024/">electricity distribution</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By consolidating private and regional infrastructure, Britain modernized its energy sector, laying the foundation for widespread industrial and economic growth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A century later, a similar transformation may be unfolding with <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin mining</a> and national strategy</strong>. <br><br><strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/proof-of-work-awards-meet-the-10-most-influential-bitcoin-leaders-in-2024-honorable-mention-included/">Senator Cynthia Lummis</a></strong> has already introduced the <strong>BITCOIN Act</strong>, which proposes the establishment of a <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/17/senator-lummis-aims-to-supercharge-us-dollar-with-strategic-bitcoin-reserve/">Strategic Bitcoin Reserve</a></strong> under the <strong>U.S. Treasury</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Why a Strategic Bitcoin Mining and Compute Act Must Follow</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the <strong>BITCOIN Act</strong> focuses on accumulation, securing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/08/blackrock-calls-bitcoin-a-hedge-against-global-disorder/">Bitcoin</a> at a national level requires more than just reserves—it demands a <strong>robust mining infrastructure and domestic manufacturing of Bitcoin mining hardware</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A <strong>Strategic Bitcoin Mining and Compute Act</strong> has yet to be introduced, but its necessity is clear when examining how quickly initiatives like the <strong>CHIPS and Science Act</strong> and the recently announced <strong>Stargate Initiative</strong> were passed to address national computing and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/31/biden-administration-invests-325-million-in-hemlock-semiconductor-factory/">semiconductor</a> security. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>China</strong> currently dominates <strong>ASIC manufacturing</strong>, leaving the U.S. reliant on foreign supply chains for the backbone of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/23/braiins-mini-miner-offers-bitcoin-mining-and-gaming-fun/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A federally backed initiative could <strong>incentivize domestic ASIC production, immersion and hydro cooling technology, and high-performance mining compute systems</strong>, ensuring a <strong>self-sufficient mining industry</strong> while reinforcing national security and grid stability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b61HINYHDrU&amp;pp=ygUIc3RhcmdhdGU%3D","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <strong>Stargate Initiative</strong>, aimed at <strong>developing a national AI computing infrastructure</strong>, demonstrates how rapidly political momentum can shift when policymakers recognize the strategic importance of compute power. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If AI infrastructure can be prioritized at the federal level, <strong>Bitcoin mining—an industry already operating high-performance computing sites—should also be considered for national integration</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mining sites, often located in energy-abundant regions, could work <strong>in tandem with AI compute sites</strong>, providing <strong>on-demand energy balancing, heat recapture solutions, and distributed compute resources</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Much like the <strong>CHIPS Act revitalized U.S. semiconductor production</strong>, a <strong>Strategic Bitcoin Mining and Compute Act</strong> could establish the framework for securing the <strong>Bitcoin network</strong>, ensuring <strong>energy-efficient operations</strong>, and strengthening the nation’s <strong>digital and financial resilience</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Bitcoin as a Strategic Asset and Infrastructure Priority</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.ft.com/content/73fa6fd9-6f34-4e59-8f0f-04de3be7387a?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Critics</a> of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">the <strong>BITCOIN Act</strong></a> caution that federal involvement in Bitcoin could create distortions in the free market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/73fa6fd9-6f34-4e59-8f0f-04de3be7387a?utm_source=chatgpt.com">supporters</a> argue that <strong>Bitcoin’s decentralized nature and immutable supply make it an unparalleled economic safeguard</strong>, especially in an era of increased monetary uncertainty. <br><br>A <strong>Strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/29/compass-mining-plans-30-mw-bitcoin-facility-in-iowa/">Bitcoin Mining</a> and Compute Act</strong>, alongside <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/02/07/the-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act-will-need-a-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure-bill-heres-why/">the <strong>BITCOIN Act</strong></a>, could establish a <strong>long-term, forward-thinking approach</strong> to securing the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/26/bitcoin-growth-game-power-law-theorist-claims-bitcoin-network-outpaces-internet-growth/">Bitcoin network</a> while driving U.S. technological and financial leadership.</p>
