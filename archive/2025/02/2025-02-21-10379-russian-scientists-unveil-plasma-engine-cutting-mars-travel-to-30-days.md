<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Russian scientists have <a href="https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/mars-in-30-days-russia-unveils-prototype-of-plasma-rocket-engine?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announced</a> the development of a prototype plasma rocket engine capable of reaching Mars in as little as 30 days, a significant reduction from the nearly year-long journey using conventional chemical propulsion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Alexey Voronov, First Deputy Director General for Science at Rosatom’s Research Institute in Troitsk, highlighted the dangers of prolonged exposure to cosmic radiation during extended space travel and emphasized that plasma engines could shorten missions to 30-60 days, facilitating round-trip missions to Mars.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The prototype, <a href="https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/mars-in-30-days-russia-unveils-prototype-of-plasma-rocket-engine?utm_source=chatgpt.com">developed</a> by Rosatom, Russia's state nuclear energy corporation, is currently undergoing ground tests. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The testing setup includes a 14-meter-long, 4-meter-diameter vacuum chamber designed to simulate the conditions of outer space. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This advancement positions Russia at the forefront of efforts to develop advanced propulsion systems aimed at reducing interplanetary travel times.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Plasma engines <a href="https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/mars-in-30-days-russia-unveils-prototype-of-plasma-rocket-engine?utm_source=chatgpt.com">operate</a> by using electromagnetic fields to accelerate charged particles, or plasma, to high speeds, providing continuous thrust over extended periods. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This method contrasts with traditional chemical rockets, which deliver thrust through short, high-intensity bursts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The continuous acceleration offered by plasma propulsion could enable spacecraft to reach higher velocities, thereby reducing travel time to distant destinations like Mars.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The successful development and implementation of plasma propulsion technology could have significant implications for current space exploration strategies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For instance, SpaceX's Starship, which relies on conventional chemical propulsion, is designed for missions to Mars that are expected to take several months. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The advent of plasma engines capable of completing the journey in a fraction of the time could render such traditional approaches less competitive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the prototype represents a promising step forward, it is important to note that the technology is still in the testing phase. Further research and development are necessary to validate the engine's performance and reliability in actual space conditions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If successful, plasma propulsion could revolutionize space travel, making missions to Mars and other distant destinations more feasible and less time-consuming.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
