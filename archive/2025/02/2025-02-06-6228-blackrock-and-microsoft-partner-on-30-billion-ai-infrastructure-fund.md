<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">BlackRock</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/20/microsofts-ai-expert-jaron-lanier-advocates-for-data-dignity-amid-bitcoin-revolution/">Microsoft</a> have <a href="https://thesiliconreview.com/2024/09/blackrock-microsoft-unveil-infrastructure-fund">announced</a> the launch of a $30 billion AI infrastructure investment fund. The Global AI Infrastructure Investment Partnership (GAIIP) will focus on the development of AI-powered <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">data centers</a> and energy projects to meet the growing demand for computational power in AI applications.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This comes at a critical time as deep learning models and large-scale data processing require vast amounts of energy and computing capacity, placing increased pressure on data centers worldwide​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This fund, with Abu Dhabi-based MGX serving as a general partner, also includes <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">Nvidia</a>, a leader in AI chip technology, which will provide the necessary technical expertise.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/microsoft-blackrock-unveil-30-billion-fund-boost-ai-data-centers">collaboration</a> is expected to not only address the infrastructure challenges posed by advanced AI but also ensure sustainable energy sources to power these data centers. As AI evolves, energy-efficient data processing has become a key concern for industry leaders​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/10/theory-what-if-blackrock-controlled-bitcoin-mining/">BlackRock</a> and Microsoft see this investment as an opportunity to drive the next generation of AI technologies while contributing to a more sustainable future.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By strengthening AI supply chains and increasing investment in energy-efficient infrastructure, the fund aims to mobilize up to $100 billion in total investments through a combination of private equity capital and loan funding.</p>
