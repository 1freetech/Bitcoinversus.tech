<p style="text-transform:none">Franklin Templeton, the renowned global investment firm, has officially lodged a proposal with the U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/31/sec-vs-coinbase-bitcoin-stands-strong-amid-crypto-regulatory-storm/">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC) to launch an innovative cryptocurrency exchange-traded fund (ETF). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Trading under the ticker 'EZPZ', the fund aims to provide investors with diversified exposure to the largest digital assets, initially focusing on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> (BTC) and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">Ethereum</a> (ETH).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/08/bitcoin-leapfrogs-silver-in-etf-markets/">ETF</a> seeks to track the performance of the CF Institutional Digital Asset Index, reflecting the firm’s strategic approach to capture the evolving cryptocurrency market dynamics. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/28/coinbase-secures-registration-with-bank-of-spain-boosts-bitcoin-momentum/">Coinbase</a> appointed as the custodian and Bank of New York Mellon managing cash holdings and administrative duties, the fund underscores a robust framework aimed at ensuring security and regulatory compliance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Franklin Templeton's initiative is part of a broader wave among asset managers to introduce crypto ETFs, a segment that is rapidly gaining traction following the earlier launches of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/17/bitcoin-vs-ethereum-gary-gensler-anticipates-ethereum-etf-approval/">Bitcoin and Ethereum spot ETFs</a>.</p>
