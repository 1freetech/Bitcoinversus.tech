<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Essentially, each <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a>, or "coin," that you mine operates on a specific algorithm, which serves as the backbone of its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">cryptographic functions</a>, including the process of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">crypto mining</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mining algorithms are responsible for ensuring the security, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/19/taproot-enables-multi-asset-transactions-on-bitcoins-lightning-network/">decentralization</a>, and integrity of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/03/river-financial-sheds-light-on-lightning-networks-potential/">blockchain network</a> by verifying and adding <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">transactions to the ledger</a>. Each coin uses a specific algorithm tailored to its design goals, including scalability, speed, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">energy efficiency</a>, and security.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Common Algorithms and Their Coins</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">1. <strong>SHA-256 (Secure Hash Algorithm 256-bit)</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DMtFhACPnTY","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Used by</strong>: <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/24/4267/">Bitcoin Cash</a>, Namecoin</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>How it works</strong>: SHA-256 takes an input and produces a 256-bit (32-byte) hash value. It’s highly secure and difficult to reverse.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Pros</strong>: Extremely secure due to its cryptographic strength and extensive use.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Cons</strong>: Requires significant computational power, making it energy-intensive.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">2. Scrypt</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3jtKTWhWO28","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Used by: <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/03/power-is-money-debating-a-litecoin-maximalist/">Litecoin</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">Dogecoin</a>, Verge</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How it works: Scrypt is designed to be more memory-intensive than SHA-256, which helps in decentralizing mining by reducing the efficiency advantage of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">ASIC miners</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pros: More resistant to ASIC domination, favoring CPU and GPU mining.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Cons: Slower transaction verification and uses more memory.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Used by: Ethereum Classic</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How it works: Ethash is a memory-hard algorithm designed to be ASIC-resistant, prioritizing decentralization and favoring GPU mining.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pros: Highly decentralized, favors GPU miners over ASIC miners.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Cons: Requires substantial memory, making it more resource-intensive.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->

<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Used by: Dash, Pura, and several other smaller coins</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How it works: X11 combines 11 different hashing functions (hence the name) in sequence. This increases security by making it harder for specialized hardware to dominate mining.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pros: More energy-efficient than many other PoW algorithms.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Cons: ASIC miners eventually adapted to this algorithm as well.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pros: Strong resistance to ASIC miners, promoting decentralization.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Cons: Heavy memory requirements may limit smaller miners.</li>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Main Features of an Algorithm </h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Security and Decentralization</a>: Different algorithms promote varying levels of security and decentralization based on their design. Coins like Bitcoin focus on computational security (SHA-256), while others like Monero prioritize privacy and resistance to specialized mining hardware.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/03/how-to-replace-a-bitmain-control-board-control-board-overview/">Specialization and Hardware</a>: Algorithms also determine the types of hardware that can effectively mine a coin. For example, SHA-256 has been overtaken by specialized ASIC miners, making it impractical for casual users with regular hardware. On the other hand, algorithms like CryptoNight and Equihash are more ASIC-resistant, keeping mining accessible to a wider range of participants.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/13/energy-how-to-enhance-data-center-efficiency/">Energy Efficiency</a>: With the increasing concern about the energy consumption of PoW algorithms, newer algorithms are being developed to be more energy-efficient. For example, X11 and some variants of Scrypt are designed to reduce power consumption compared to Bitcoin's SHA-256.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/20/intel-edward-snowden-highlights-privacy-risks-at-bitcoin-2024/">Privacy</a>: Certain algorithms, like CryptoNight, enhance privacy features by making it harder to trace transactions on the blockchain, making them popular for privacy-focused coins like Monero.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Different algorithms offer unique trade-offs between security, decentralization, hardware requirements, and energy consumption, which is why each coin selects a specific one based on its goals. Understanding these algorithms helps you identify the kind of hardware you need and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">mining strategies</a> for each coin.</p>
