<p style="text-transform:none">During the flood in southern Poland, The Polish government has turned to Starlink systems to restore critical connectivity for emergency services and aid efforts. These satellite-based communication systems are essential in areas where traditional ground infrastructure has been rendered inoperable due to the floods.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Starlink terminals have been <a href="https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7786/Artykul/3427345,starlink-deployed-to-support-flood-rescue-and-aid-efforts-in-poland">deployed</a> in key flood-affected areas, including Stronie Śląskie and Lądek-Zdrój, to ensure that emergency responders can communicate effectively and coordinate rescue missions. The local pharmacy in Stronie Śląskie, for instance, regained access to IT systems used to dispense life-saving medications, after communication lines were cut off by the disaster. This rapid deployment has been crucial in supporting the WOPR rescuers operating in flooded regions such as Nysa.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Without reliance on traditional network infrastructure, Starlink can be deployed within minutes to re-establish communication lines in challenging environments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The success of Starlink’s deployment in Poland follows similar applications in disaster-stricken areas around the globe. This <a href="https://www.starlink.com/connecting-the-unconnected">technology</a>, provided by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/18/bitcoin-vs-elon-musk-unraveling-the-role-of-spacexs-decisions/">SpaceX</a>, has been increasingly recognized for its ability to close the communication gap during emergencies, delivering broadband internet where it’s most needed.</p>
