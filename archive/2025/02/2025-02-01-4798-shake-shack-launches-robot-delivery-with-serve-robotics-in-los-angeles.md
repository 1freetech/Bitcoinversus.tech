<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Serve Robotics, a prominent player in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/23/autonomous-vehicle-pulled-over-by-phoenix-police-for-traffic-violation/">autonomous delivery</a> sector, has <a href="https://x.com/YahooFinance/status/1823812085043879960">announced</a> a partnership with Shake Shack and Uber Eats.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/YahooFinance/status/1823812085043879960","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The collaboration <a href="https://www.nrn.com/news/shake-shack-rolls-out-sidewalk-robot-delivery-serve-robotics-and-uber-eats">will bring</a> robot-powered food delivery to select Shake Shack locations in Los Angeles. This marks the first time Shake Shack customers in the area can receive their meals via autonomous <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/can-robots-actually-be-considered-criminals/">sidewalk robots</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initiative is part of a broader plan to deploy up to 2,000 of these robots across the U.S. by 2025, expanding beyond the 100 currently operating in Los Angeles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Uber Eats, which holds a 16% stake in Serve Robotics, has been a significant supporter of this robotic delivery platform, reflecting the growing trend towards autonomous solutions in food delivery​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dmzmEyu3AVo","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The robots are designed to navigate busy urban environments, including high-traffic areas like Hollywood Boulevard. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">They complete deliveries at a higher rate than human couriers, with Serve Robotics reporting a 99.94% delivery success rate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The deployment in Los Angeles serves as a testbed for potential nationwide expansion, with the goal of improving efficiency and customer satisfaction through innovation​.</p>
