<p style="text-transform:none">In a surprising financial turn, Tether, the issuer of the USDT stablecoin, surpassed BlackRock, the world's largest asset manager, in 2023 by generating a profit of $6.2 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This achievement highlights a notable shift in the financial landscape, where <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">cryptocurrency companies</a> are increasingly outperforming traditional financial giants. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/10/theory-what-if-blackrock-controlled-bitcoin-mining/">BlackRock</a>, which manages an astounding $10 trillion in assets, earned $5.5 billion during the same period​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tether's success is closely tied to its dominance in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/31/the-end-of-an-era-binance-ends-support-for-busd-stablecoin/">stablecoin market</a>, where USDT holds a 75% market share with a capitalization of $119 billion. Stablecoins like USDT have become an attractive option for investors seeking stability amid the volatility of other <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">cryptocurrencies</a>. This stable market position allowed Tether to focus its investments on highly liquid assets such as U.S. Treasury bills, Bitcoin, and gold, all of which contributed to its record profits​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Another key to Tether's financial triumph is its lean operational structure. With fewer than 100 employees, Tether boasts an extraordinary profit-per-employee ratio, making it one of the most efficient organizations globally. Paolo Ardoino, CEO of Tether, has emphasized the firm's ability to remain agile while managing a massive global operation​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Tether continues to grow, it remains a top 20 holder of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">U.S. Treasury bills</a>, solidifying its influence not only in the crypto world but also in traditional finance. This development further signals the expanding role of stablecoins in the broader financial ecosystem.</p>
