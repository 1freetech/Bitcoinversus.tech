<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">I've decided to rotate my connections on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/linkedin-user-agreement-updates-effective-november-20th/">Linkedin</a> each week to ensure that my network consists of genuine connections and opportunities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Here's why I'm implementing this change:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">1. Real Opportunities: Engaging with individuals who contribute to meaningful discussions and career growth is crucial for me. Passive connections bring no real-time value.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">2. Feed Relevance: My mission is to keep my feed focused and valuable. A cluttered feed diminishes the essence of LinkedIn.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">3. Purposeful Connections: Connecting without any meaningful interaction or introduction is counterproductive. It's important to me that my connections serve a purpose.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If there isn't any meaningful engagement within 5 business days, I will remove the connection to maintain a purpose-driven network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
