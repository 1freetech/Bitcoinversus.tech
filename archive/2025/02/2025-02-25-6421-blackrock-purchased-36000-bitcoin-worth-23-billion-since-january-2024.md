<p style="text-transform:none">According to recent <a href="https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2024/09/26/blackrock-acquires-359279-bitcoin-for-over-23-billion-dollars-the-effect-of-the-news-on-the-market/">reports</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">BlackRock</a> has purchased 359,279 Bitcoin since January 2024, amounting to over $23 billion. This bold investment comes at a time when <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/institutional-interest-in-bitcoin-grows-as-major-banks-invest-in-etfs/">institutional interest</a> in digital currencies is at an all-time high, signaling BlackRock’s confidence in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/27/morgan-stanleys-bullish-bitcoin-prediction/">Bitcoin's future</a> as a store of value and investment asset.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">BlackRock’s significant acquisition of Bitcoin underlines the growing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/04/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board-allocates-2-of-assets-to-bitcoin-etfs/">acceptance of cryptocurrency</a> by mainstream financial institutions. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/24/blackrock-ceo-larry-fink-changes-stance-on-bitcoin-endorses-it-as-financial-instrument/">Larry Fink</a>, CEO of BlackRock, has previously emphasized the importance of Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation and a way to diversify portfolios. By accumulating a vast amount of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a>, BlackRock is setting an example for other major institutional investors, further legitimizing the asset class.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This purchase also coincides with a surge in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Bitcoin's price</a>, which has been experiencing steady growth since the start of 2024. BlackRock’s substantial investment could signal a long-term strategy to capitalize on the scarcity of Bitcoin, which has a fixed supply of 21 million coins. As more institutional investors follow suit, Bitcoin's status as "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">digital gold</a>" is becoming increasingly apparent.</p>
