<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tether has <a href="https://www.cointribune.com/en/exchange-tether-outperforms-blackrock-in-2023-with-record-profits/">indeed</a> outperformed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/23/blackrocks-indirect-bitcoin-investment-through-microstrategy/">BlackRock</a> in profits for 2023, marking a significant shift in the financial landscape. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/21/tether-leverages-stablecoin-success-makes-500-million-investment-in-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure/">Tether</a> reported a profit of $6.2 billion, surpassing BlackRock’s $5.5 billion. This achievement is largely driven by Tether's investments in low-risk, high-yield assets like U.S. Treasury bills, which have benefited from rising interest rates globally. The company’s strategic allocation into stable investments such as Bitcoin and gold has also contributed to this profit boost.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The stablecoin issuer’s remarkable performance highlights the growing influence of digital assets in the broader financial world. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/21/tether-leverages-stablecoin-success-makes-500-million-investment-in-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure/">Tether’s USDT</a> has become the dominant stablecoin, holding a 75% market share, which has allowed it to generate consistent profits from interest on its reserves. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tether's lean operational model, with fewer than 100 employees, contrasts with BlackRock’s massive structure, enabling higher profitability per employee.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This shift demonstrates that traditional financial giants like BlackRock are now competing with digital-native companies that can adapt quickly to market conditions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/02/former-blackrock-director-weighs-in-on-possible-bitcoin-spot-etf/">BlackRock</a> manages trillions of dollars in assets, Tether's nimble business model and focus on stablecoins have given it a competitive edge, especially as stablecoins become an essential tool for liquidity in the cryptocurrency market​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, despite its financial success, Tether continues to face scrutiny regarding its transparency and the true backing of its reserves. Regulatory challenges remain a key issue for Tether as it navigates the increasingly complex financial and legal landscape of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/02/crypto-assets-deemed-securities-by-federal-judge-amplifying-cryptocurrency-regulatory-uncertainty/">crypto sector</a>.</p>
