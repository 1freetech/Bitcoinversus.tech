<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/22/politics-state-of-michigan-adds-6-6-million-bitcoin-etfs-to-state-pension-fund/">U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC) has approved options trading for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/10/blackrocks-bitcoin-etf-surpasses-20-billion/">BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) ETF</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This marks a pivotal moment for institutional and retail investors, providing them with more tools to hedge risks and speculate on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/28/bitcoin-rsi-chart-suggests-bullish-outlook/">Bitcoin's price movements</a>. The options will be listed on the Nasdaq exchange under the ticker IBIT and traded following standard ETF option rules​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The approval is expected to drive increased liquidity and participation from institutional investors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Options trading allows investors to buy or sell Bitcoin at pre-set prices, helping them manage exposure to the often volatile digital asset. According to market analysts, this move could boost <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/08/bitcoin-price-predicted-to-reach-150000-by-year-end-says-fundstrats-tom-lee/">Bitcoin's presence in mainstream financial markets</a>, potentially increasing the total trading volume of IBIT, which has already surpassed $875 million in August​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This regulatory approval represents a broader acceptance of Bitcoin by traditional financial institutions and could signal a future expansion into other cryptocurrencies, such as Ethereum, for which Nasdaq has already filed for ETF options​</p>
