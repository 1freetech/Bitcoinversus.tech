<p style="text-transform:none">Bitdeer and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) are set to <a href="https://www.theblock.co/post/318345/bitdeer-and-tsmc-to-launch-state-of-the-art-mining-chip-after-successful-test">launch</a> a new state-of-the-art mining chip. This innovative chip is designed to enhance the efficiency of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">cryptocurrency mining</a>, promising lower energy consumption and faster processing speeds. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">After extensive testing, the chip has passed all performance benchmarks, indicating that it is ready for mass production. The partnership between Bitdeer and TSMC marks a significant advancement in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> technology, pushing the boundaries of what is possible in the field.</p>

This advancement is crucial for large-scale mining operations looking to optimize energy costs and increase their profitability. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitdeer, a leader in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">cryptocurrency mining industry</a>, has long relied on TSMC’s production capabilities to bring high-performance chips to market.
