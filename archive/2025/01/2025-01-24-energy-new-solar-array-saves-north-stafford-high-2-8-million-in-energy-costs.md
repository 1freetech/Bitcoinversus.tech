<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">North Stafford High School recently<a href="https://interestingengineering.com/energy/virginia-installs-largest-school-rooftop-solar-array"> installed</a> the largest rooftop solar array in Virginia, marking a significant step towards sustainability. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This impressive 1.8-megawatt system, consisting of 3,300 solar panels, is expected to generate 54 million <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/energy/">kilowatt hours</a> of clean energy over its lifespan, significantly reducing the school’s carbon footprint by offsetting 41,583 tons of carbon dioxide emissions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Beyond its environmental impact, the solar project will <a href="https://www.fredericksburgfreepress.com/2024/09/13/solar-project-unlocks-a-brighter-future-at-north-stafford-high-school/">save</a> Stafford County Public Schools an estimated $2.8 million in energy costs over the next 25 years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This financial benefit stems from the on-site generation of energy, which powers a significant portion of the school’s electricity needs​. In fact, the array is projected to cover up to 84% of North Stafford's annual electricity consumption.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The installation is also providing students with valuable hands-on learning opportunities. Real-time data from the solar panels is incorporated into the school’s curriculum, allowing students to monitor energy production and gain insights into renewable energy technologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This unique educational component ensures that students are not only benefiting from a greener school but also gaining practical knowledge in the growing field of clean energy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The solar array is <a href="https://www.smartenergydecisions.com/renewable-energy/2024/09/16/stafford-county-public-schools-launches-solar">maintained</a> under a power purchase agreement with Madison Energy Infrastructure (MEI), allowing the school to benefit from renewable energy without upfront costs.</p>
