<p style="text-transform:none">West Data Group has officially released a teaser to attract potential investors for its expanding data center operations. With over 15 years of experience in Ethiopia, the company is a leader in deploying and maintaining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/13/block-ceo-jack-dorsey-reveals-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining-with-the-mdk-hashboard/">Application Specific Integrated Circuits</a> (ASICs) for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">Bitcoin mining</a> using the SHA-256 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/29/who-is-satoshi-nakamoto-new-emails-with-adam-back-enter-public-domain/">proof-of-work protocol</a>. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/15/growing-institutional-interest-in-bitcoin-despite-death-cross-and-mixed-cpi-data/">crypto data services</a>, West Data Group provides cutting-edge AI data processing through custom-designed hardware and plans to expand to 300 MW of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/14/bitcoin-mining-is-shaping-renewable-energy-frontiers/">renewable energy-powered infrastructure</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">West Data Group’s 30 MW data center, located in Ethiopia, utilizes the country’s abundant renewable energy resources. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The current operation generates over USD 15 million annually from 10,000 deployed miners. With plans to expand to 130 MW, and eventually 300 MW, the company aims to become a global leader in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">Bitcoin mining and AI</a> data processing .</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of September 2024, West Data has developed 30 MW of its planned 130 MW capacity. A Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with Ethiopian Electric Power (EEP) ensures steady electricity supply, enabling the company to pursue large-scale development in the region. Investors can explore opportunities for equity, debt financing, or hosting partnerships as part of West Data’s strategic growth .</p>
