<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">U.S. Senator Cynthia Lummis has <a href="https://www.lummis.senate.gov/press-releases/lummis-announces-revolutionary-proposal-to-supercharge-the-dollar-bolster-u-s-economy/">announced</a> plans to establish a strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">Bitcoin reserve</a>, aiming to strengthen the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/04/bitcoin-and-the-history-of-the-u-s-dollar/">U.S. dollar</a> and address the national debt.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposal suggests that the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/07/bitcoins-value-strengthens-in-relation-to-the-us-national-debt/">U.S. government</a> acquire approximately 1 million bitcoins over five years, representing about 5% of the total Bitcoin supply.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/SenLummis/status/1854208373740458432","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-rich is-provider-twitter wp-block-embed-twitter"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposed reserve would be managed by the U.S. Treasury through a decentralized network of secure Bitcoin vaults, ensuring high levels of physical and cybersecurity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Senator Lummis emphasized that the Bitcoin holdings would be retained for a minimum of 20 years, with the primary purpose of reducing the national debt.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative has garnered bipartisan support, with over 2,200 letters sent to U.S. Senators within 48 hours of the bill's introduction, urging co-sponsorship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposal reflects a growing interest among policymakers in integrating digital assets into national financial strategies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Critics have raised concerns about Bitcoin's volatility and its suitability as a reserve asset.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, proponents argue that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/blackrocks-growing-bitcoin-holdings-a-strategic-bet-on-digital-assets/">Bitcoin's finite supply</a> and decentralized nature make it a valuable hedge against inflation and economic instability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The introduction of the Bitcoin Strategic Reserve bill marks a significant step in the U.S. government's exploration of digital currencies as tools for economic policy. The proposal's progress will be closely monitored by both financial markets and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ercot-approves-riots-1-gw-bitcoin-mining-capacity-in-corsicana/">cryptocurrency community</a>.</p>
