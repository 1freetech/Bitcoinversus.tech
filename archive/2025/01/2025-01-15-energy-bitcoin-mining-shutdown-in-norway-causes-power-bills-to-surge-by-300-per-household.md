<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a small Norwegian town, residents have seen their annual power bills <a href="https://www.blocktrainer.de/en/blog/without-a-bitcoin-mining-plant-in-norway-will-the-price-of-electricity-rise">rise</a> by approximately $300 after the local <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/new-bitcoin-mining-facility-emerges-in-nigeria/">Bitcoin mining facility</a> shut down. The mining operation, which had been contributing 20% of the area’s grid fees, acted as a financial subsidy for local electricity consumers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With the closure of the mining site, households now face significantly higher <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/antminer-al1-pro-generates-over-100-in-daily-bitcoin-profits-at-0-07-kwh/">electricity costs</a>, as the burden of grid maintenance and fees has shifted back to consumers​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining facility</a> in question had been a major consumer of electricity in the town, helping to stabilize energy prices by covering a substantial portion of grid operating costs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://www.minerlist.com/blog/best-bitcoin-asic-miner">closure</a> has highlighted the sometimes unexpected benefits that large-scale Bitcoin mining operations can bring to local economies by reducing overall electricity expenses.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the financial benefits, the facility was ultimately shut down due to complaints from residents about noise pollution generated by the mining machines. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the town looks for alternative ways to fill the financial gap left by the facility, the situation demonstrates both the pros and cons of hosting <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/12/swan-bitcoin-launches-mining-operations-and-eyes-public-listing/">Bitcoin mining operations</a> in local communities.</p>
