<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/23/suprahex-bitcoin-mining-machine-review/">Bitcoin</a> has experienced a significant decline, dropping nearly 8% in the past 24 hours, currently trading at approximately $101,518. This downturn is part of a broader market selloff influenced by concerns over the Chinese AI startup DeepSeek.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">DeepSeek has <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/tech-stock-sell-off-deepseek-ai-chatgpt-china-nvidia-chips-2025-1?utm_source=chatgpt.com">developed</a> an AI model that rivals existing technologies at a fraction of the cost, raising concerns about the competitive landscape in the tech industry. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">This development has led to a selloff in tech stocks, with the Nasdaq Composite falling over 3% and major companies like Nvidia experiencing significant losses.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/bitcoin-drops-11-day-low-amid-tech-selloff-2025-01-27/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">apprehension</a> surrounding DeepSeek's advancements has also impacted the cryptocurrency market. Analysts suggest that the uncertainty has prompted investors to move away from riskier assets, contributing to Bitcoin's decline.</p>
