<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The SupraHex is an open-source <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/top-secret-bitcoin-mining-machine-revealed-in-west-texas/">Bitcoin mining</a> machine designed for enthusiasts and developers seeking a hands-on approach to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">mining hardware</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Featuring a modular and compact design, the machine allows easy  integration into various setups, making it a practical choice for those interested in optimizing mining performance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10275,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-1.png?w=594" alt="" class="wp-image-10275" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The SupraHex <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitaxe-gamma-redefines-asic-mining-with-advanced-cooling-features/">Bitaxe</a> supports standalone operation, meaning it can solo mine or mine directly to a pool over Wi-Fi, enhancing its usability for hobbyists and "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/quantum-bitcoin-mining-futuristic-heatsink-enhances-bitaxe-asic-chip-performance/">quantum mining</a>" operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The machine demonstrates efficient power consumption, drawing 98.7 watts at an input voltage of 11.7 volts, with an ASIC voltage request of 1.25 volts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://bsky.app/profile/bitcoinversus.bsky.social/post/3lfxg2mzcs22l","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"bluesky-embed"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These figures highlight the energy-efficient nature of the SupraHex Bitaxe, making it suitable for home-based mining applications or research purposes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Effective thermal management ensures stable performance, with the ASIC temperature recorded at 43°C and the voltage regulator reaching 71°C, while fan speed is maintained at 60% to facilitate optimal cooling. Such thermal characteristics contribute to the machine's long-term reliability and operational stability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10280,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/screenshot-2025-01-23-171647.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-10280" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em><sup>SupraHex Dashboard</sup></em></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In terms of performance, the SupraHex Bitaxe operates at an ASIC frequency of 550 MHz, with an ASIC voltage measured at 1.25 volts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10279,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/screenshot-2025-01-23-171701.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-10279" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These specifications enable the machine to achieve a current hash rate of 4.34 TH/s, slightly surpassing the expected 4.21 TH/s. The reported efficiency stands at 22.79 J/TH, closely aligning with the expected 23.47 J/TH, showcasing the machine's capability to deliver consistent results. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The cooling system features two high-speed fans mounted on top to ensure adequate heat dissipation from the ASIC chips. Heatsinks are strategically placed on critical components, further enhancing thermal management. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10283,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/img_2270-1.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-10283" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The machine is mounted on a transparent acrylic frame with red metal standoffs, providing structural support while keeping internal components visible and accessible for easy modifications or troubleshooting.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Connectivity options include a wireless antenna, which allows for remote management and seamless integration with mining pools.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The power connectors and data interfaces are designed to facilitate efficient operation, contributing to a smooth mining experience. I would have like the LED screen to be somewhere easily visible but other than that, the board has some pretty unique features. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10287,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/screenshot-2025-01-23-173955-1.png?w=511" alt="" class="wp-image-10287" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The device features a blue PCB that houses the ASIC chips and critical components necessary for mining, with heat sinks and voltage regulators enhancing stability and performance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The SupraHex Bitaxe offers an engaging platform for Bitcoin mining, delivering an open-source solution that empowers users to experiment and innovate. With a hash rate of 4.34 TH/s and an efficiency rating of 22.79 J/TH, the machine presents a viable option for small-scale miners seeking an affordable yet efficient solution. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The combination of effective cooling solutions, modular design, and accessible customization options makes it an attractive choice for developers and mining enthusiasts aiming to optimize their mining operations.</p>
