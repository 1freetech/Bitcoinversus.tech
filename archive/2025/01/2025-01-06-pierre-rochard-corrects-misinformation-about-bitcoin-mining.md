<p style="text-transform:none">Senior <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining expert</a> Pierre Rochard took to Twitter today to correct a widely shared Wired article that claimed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/28/bitcoin-miners-aid-texas-during-heatwave-curtail-energy-consumption-by-90/">ERCOT</a>, Texas' grid operator, pays <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/bitcoin-miner-revenue-bottoming-out-signals-potential-bull-market/">Bitcoin miners</a> to curtail during high demand periods. The false claim suggests that miners were financially incentivized to turn off their operations to ease grid stress.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Rochard, pointing out the misinformation, clarified that ERCOT does not, in fact, pay Bitcoin miners to switch off for a fee. Instead, ERCOT operates through a competitive, offer-based system known as Ancillary Services (AS), designed to maintain grid reliability during disturbances. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/politics-putin-signs-law-legalizing-bitcoin-mining-in-russia/">Bitcoin miners</a> and other loads are compensated to stay on standby, ready to provide frequency support.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The misunderstanding arose from a misinterpretation of ERCOT's energy procurement methods, which do involve paying generators like peaker plants and batteries to remain offline in reserve, but the same logic does not apply to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/energy-demand-points-to-undervaluation-of-bitcoin-mining-companies/">Bitcoin miners</a>. Rather than receiving payments to power down, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin miners</a> operate as a load in reserve under ERCOT’s market mechanisms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pierre Rochard emphasized that this kind of factual accuracy is critical, particularly given the significant media coverage surrounding Bitcoin mining's role in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/14/bitcoin-mining-is-shaping-renewable-energy-frontiers/">energy grids</a>. He further stated that correcting misinformation helps maintain transparency and an informed public discourse around the intersection of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">energy management and Bitcoin mining</a>.</p>
