<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Fred Krueger, a prominent <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin advocate</a> with a strong background in Wall Street, recently issued a bold statement targeting altcoin and NFT enthusiasts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a social media <a href="https://x.com/dotkrueger/status/1833265749336723641?s=51">post</a>, Krueger declared, “Altcoiners, I hate to break it to you. Your coin will be worthless in 10 years. Your NFT will have zero bidders. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/28/bitcoin-rsi-chart-suggests-bullish-outlook/">Bitcoin</a> will be 1 million. You will have squandered an enormous opportunity.” </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This prediction highlights his belief in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/bitcoin-market-dominance-hits-new-cycle-high/">Bitcoin's dominance</a> over other digital assets in the long term.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Krueger’s <a href="https://cryptonews.net/news/ethereum/28594287/">remarks</a> come amid increasing institutional interest in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a>, as companies like Japan’s Metaplanet are further solidifying Bitcoin’s position as a strategic reserve asset. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With an additional $2 million investment, Metaplanet’s total <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a> now stand at $26 million, further fueling market speculation about <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin’s future</a> as the leading cryptocurrency. Krueger’s comments align with his broader stance that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a> is the only digital asset with true staying power.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As institutional support for Bitcoin grows, Krueger’s warning serves as a reminder of the risks associated with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/01/kaspa-most-profitable-machine-but-low-hashrate-and-difficulty-still-a-concern-for-miners/">altcoins and NFTs</a>, which he believes will not retain their value over time. </p>
