<p style="text-transform:none">First Lady Melania Trump has <a href="https://www.businessinsider.com/trump-launches-cryptocurrency-days-before-inauguration-2025-1?utm_source=chatgpt.com">introduced</a> her own meme coin, $MELANIA, following the recent launch of her husband Donald Trump's $TRUMP cryptocurrency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The $TRUMP coin experienced a volatile surge, reaching a market capitalization of approximately $32 billion shortly after its release. Similarly, $MELANIA quickly amassed <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2025/01/20/what-to-know-about-melania-trumps-new-meme-coin-as-market-cap-crosses-2-billion/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">a market cap exceeding $2 billion</a>.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Despite these impressive figures, the rapid proliferation of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/nfl-lineman-russell-okungs-bitcoin-investment-pays-off/">celebrity-endorsed cryptocurrencies</a> has raised concerns among investors and industry experts. <p style="text-transform:none">Notably, the $HAWK token, <a href="https://www.news.com.au/finance/markets/world-markets/how-to-avoid-the-next-hawk-tuah-crypto-investment-disaster/news-story/fc27a14539ca4062bd872f8a550970b1?utm_source=chatgpt.com">promoted by internet personality</a> Haliey Welch, saw its value plummet over 90% shortly after launch, leading to substantial investor losses and legal actions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The cryptocurrency community remains cautious, emphasizing the importance of thorough research and skepticism toward celebrity-backed tokens. The lack of intrinsic value and potential for market manipulation in these coins often leave retail investors vulnerable to significant financial setbacks.</p>
