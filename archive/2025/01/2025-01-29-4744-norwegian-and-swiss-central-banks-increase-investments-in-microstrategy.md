<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The central banks of Norway and Switzerland have notably increased their investments in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/22/microstrategy-chairman-michael-saylor-highlights-bitcoin-performance-amid-institutional-acceptance/">MicroStrategy</a>, a firm that holds a substantial amount of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/08/bitcoin-strategic-reserve-bill-proposal-by-wyoming-senator-cynthia-lummis/">Bitcoin</a>. Norges Bank, responsible for managing Norway's Government Pension Fund, now holds over 1.12 million shares, while the Swiss National Bank has acquired 466,000 shares, marking a 60% increase from the previous quarter.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The shares of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/microstrategy-plans-2-billion-equity-offering-to-purchase-bitcoin/">MicroStrategy</a> have shown a fluctuation aligned closely with Bitcoin's market price. Despite recent declines in share price, the company's aggressive <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Bitcoin acquisition strategy</a> has allowed it to outperform many stocks in the S&amp;P 500 index over the long term. This maneuver is part of a broader trend where institutional investors use publicly traded companies like MicroStrategy as a proxy to gain exposure to digital assets.</p>
