<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">On January 19, 2013, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a> history was made as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Canaan</a> Creative, through its Avalon project, introduced the world’s first consumer application-specific integrated circuit (<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/sound-engineer-to-mine-bitcoin-at-live-concert/">ASIC</a>) miners. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Known as Avalon1, these initial ASIC miners revolutionized the mining landscape by introducing highly efficient and specialized hardware designed solely for Bitcoin’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">SHA-256</a> hashing algorithm, significantly outperforming the general-purpose CPUs and GPUs that miners previously used. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Avalon1’s introduction allowed for substantial growth in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/19/taproot-enables-multi-asset-transactions-on-bitcoins-lightning-network/">Bitcoin network’s security</a> and hashing power, effectively starting the ASIC mining era and shaping the infrastructure of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/23/f2pool-launches-fractal-bitcoin-mining-pool/">Bitcoin mining</a> that continues today​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">Vitalik Buterin</a>, co-founder of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Ethereum</a> and an early contributor to Bitcoin Magazine, <a href="https://bitcoinmagazine.com/business/avalon-ships-bitcoins-first-consumer-asics-1358905223">documented</a> the release and implications of ASIC mining in a 2013 article. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His work highlighted the importance of Avalon’s innovation, as the new hardware provided a way for miners to increase profitability and network security. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Bitcoin’s shift to ASICs was met with some concerns about centralization, the technology represented a critical leap forward in efficiency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Today, ASIC miners, continuously refined in performance and energy consumption, are integral to securing Bitcoin’s decentralized network​.</p>
