<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a> has once again <a href="https://www.cryptoglobe.com/latest/2024/09/microstrategy-buys-another-18300-bitcoins-to-add-to-its-massive-btc-holdings/">expanded</a> its Bitcoin portfolio, acquiring an additional 18,300 BTC for $1.11 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This latest purchase brings the company’s total <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">Bitcoin holdings</a> to 244,800 BTC, making it the largest corporate holder of Bitcoin globally. The company’s CEO, Michael Saylor, remains a strong proponent of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">Bitcoin</a>, consistently reinforcing its value as a hedge against inflation and a key long-term asset​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The latest <a href="https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2024/09/229998-microstrategy-buys-18300-bitcoin-company-now-holds-244800-btc/">acquisition</a>, conducted between August and September 2024, comes at an average price of $60,408 per Bitcoin. MicroStrategy's total investment in Bitcoin now amounts to $9.45 billion, with an average purchase price of $38,585 per Bitcoin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/11/michael-saylor-of-microstrategy-forecasts-bitcoin-as-the-leading-asset-class/">Saylor</a> has <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">publicly stated</a> his belief that Bitcoin’s value could soar into the millions over the next two decades, reflecting the company’s unwavering confidence in the cryptocurrency’s future.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MicroStrategy’s aggressive Bitcoin strategy underscores its commitment to the digital asset, even as its stock prices fluctuate. As of now, the company has seen a Bitcoin yield of 17% year-to-date, further emphasizing the benefits of its bold investment approach​.</p>
