<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/13/marathon-digital-invests-100-million-in-bitcoin/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a> has introduced the MARA 2PIC system, a two-phase immersion cooling solution designed to enhance data center efficiency and performance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.mara.com/2pic/home?utm_source=chatgpt.com">The system</a> submerges mining hardware in a dielectric fluid with a higher boiling point, which absorbs heat and vaporizes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The vapor then rises and condenses upon contacting chilled condenser coils cooled by a water loop connected to a dry cooler, allowing the liquid coolant to return and maintain a continuous heat transfer cycle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10155,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/screenshot-2025-01-16-195054.png?w=746" alt="" class="wp-image-10155" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This sealed setup prevents dust and debris from affecting the hardware while efficiently dissipating heat, leading to optimal miner performance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The MARA <a href="https://www.mara.com/2pic/home?utm_source=chatgpt.com">2PIC system</a> enables substantial overclocking of ASIC miners, with XP miners capable of being overclocked by up to 100% and Auradine miners achieving higher hash rates in a compact form factor.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Energy efficiency is significantly improved, as the system requires 80% less energy for cooling overhead compared to traditional air-cooled or single-phase immersion systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, the system supports remote management, facilitating streamlined and low-maintenance operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/23/mara-holdings-raises-850-million-through-convertible-note-offering-for-bitcoin-purchases/">MARA</a> 2PIC system is heat capture ready, utilizing high-temperature water for heat exchange, which can be beneficial for operations in various climates.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By adopting this advanced cooling technology, data centers can achieve higher performance, reduced energy consumption, and lower operational costs.</p>
