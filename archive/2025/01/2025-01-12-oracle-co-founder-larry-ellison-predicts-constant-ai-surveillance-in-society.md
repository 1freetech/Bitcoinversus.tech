<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison envisions a world <a href="https://www.msn.com/en-us/autos/news/billionaire-larry-ellison-says-a-vast-ai-fueled-surveillance-system-can-ensure-citizens-will-be-on-their-best-behavior/ar-AA1qCbNJ?ocid=socialshare#image=1">dominated</a> by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/20/microsofts-ai-expert-jaron-lanier-advocates-for-data-dignity-amid-bitcoin-revolution/">AI surveillance</a>, where constant monitoring ensures citizens are always observed. He predicts that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/energy-new-machine-learning-model-predicts-heat-movement-through-materials/">AI systems</a> will oversee everything from security footage to body and dashboard cameras, aiming to maintain order through automated oversight.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Speaking to investors, Ellison <a href="https://www.msn.com/en-us/autos/news/billionaire-larry-ellison-says-a-vast-ai-fueled-surveillance-system-can-ensure-citizens-will-be-on-their-best-behavior/ar-AA1qCbNJ?ocid=socialshare#image=1">described</a> a future where AI analyzes data from cameras and drones, supervising everything from police officers to public spaces. He believes this level of surveillance will promote better behavior among citizens. Ellison also envisions AI drones replacing police cars in high-speed chases. Oracle, aggressively pursuing AI, has partnered with companies like SpaceX to develop these technologies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This ambitious vision raises ethical concerns about privacy and civil liberties. As <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/26/gradually-then-suddenly-how-ai-and-robots-are-reshaping-the-workforce/">AI technologies</a> continue to evolve, the balance between safety and personal freedom will become a critical conversation.</p>
