<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/08/blackrock-calls-bitcoin-a-hedge-against-global-disorder/">BlackRock</a> CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/24/blackrock-ceo-larry-fink-changes-stance-on-bitcoin-endorses-it-as-financial-instrument/">Larry Fink</a> and G42 CEO Peng Xiao recently engaged in a discussion at <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/03/gold-bars-worth-1-million-for-the-first-time/">Bloomberg</a> House in Davos, focusing on the future of artificial intelligence infrastructure and its energy requirements. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Bloomberg, Peng Xiao emphasized that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/02/bit-digital-expands-ai-infrastructure-with-46-million-acquisition/">AI infrastructure</a> could require a minimum of 200 to 300 gigawatts of power to meet global demand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During the discussion with Bloomberg's Francine Lacqua, Fink and Xiao explored various critical factors influencing AI expansion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The conversation touched on US restrictions on semiconductor technology, the feasibility of addressing energy needs through natural gas, and the evolving role of cryptocurrencies in the broader economic landscape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Peng Xiao stressed the importance of energy security, citing natural gas as a potential solution to AI's escalating power consumption. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He noted that AI advancements will significantly impact infrastructure planning, urging policymakers and industry leaders to strategize accordingly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Fink shared insights on macroeconomic concerns, warning that sustained investment in AI could contribute to elevated inflation levels. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He also highlighted the potential of crypto assets in diversifying investment portfolios amid rapid technological advancements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The session in Davos underscored the growing attention AI infrastructure demands are receiving from global financial and technology leaders. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As AI continues to evolve, balancing energy needs with sustainable development remains a top priority for industry stakeholders.</p>
