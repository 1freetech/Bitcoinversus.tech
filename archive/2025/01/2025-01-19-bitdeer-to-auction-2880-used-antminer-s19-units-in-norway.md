<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitdeer, a prominent NASDAQ-listed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> giant, is <a href="https://www.livebitcoinnews.com/bitdeer-announces-auction-for-used-mining-equipment/">gearing up</a> for a major auction featuring 2,880 used Antminer <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/19/hive-digital-prioritizes-efficiency-with-major-antminer-s19k-pro-investment/">S19 units</a>. This auction, scheduled for September 24, 2024, in Norway, is part of the company’s strategy to upgrade its fleet ahead of the upcoming <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/30/binance-ceo-forecasts-bright-future-for-bitcoin-post-halving/">Bitcoin halving</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Antminer <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">S19 devices</a>, previously used in Bitdeer’s Norwegian data center, have been recognized for their efficiency and have contributed significantly to the company’s mining capacity​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The starting bid for the <a href="https://fxdailyreport.com/bitdeer-to-auction-2880-used-antminer-s19-mining-machines/">entire batch</a> is set at $270,000, making it an attractive option for small and medium-sized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">mining operations</a>. The auction will feature two models: the Antminer S19-90T and S19-95T, known for their high hashrate and energy efficiency. Participants must submit a deposit of $10,000 in USDT to take part in the auction​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This auction is a crucial opportunity for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/28/bitcoin-miners-aid-texas-during-heatwave-curtail-energy-consumption-by-90/">miners</a> looking to scale their operations at a reduced cost, especially as the Bitcoin halving event approaches. The Antminer S19 models are capable of mining cryptocurrencies like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a>, Bitcoin Cash, and Bitcoin SV, making them versatile for different <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/21/tether-leverages-stablecoin-success-makes-500-million-investment-in-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure/">mining operations</a>.</p>
