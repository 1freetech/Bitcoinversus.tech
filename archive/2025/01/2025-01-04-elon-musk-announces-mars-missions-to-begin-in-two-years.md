<p style="text-transform:none">SpaceX CEO Elon Musk <a href="https://twitter.com/elonmusk/status/1832550322293837833">has declared</a> plans to launch the first uncrewed Starships to Mars in the next two years, aligning with the upcoming Earth-Mars transfer window. These missions aim to test the reliability of landing spacecraft safely on the Martian surface.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"The first Starships to Mars will launch in two years when the next Earth-Mars transfer window opens," Musk stated. "These will be uncrewed to test the reliability of landing intact on Mars."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If these initial missions prove successful, Musk anticipates that the first crewed flights to Mars could commence in four years. The ultimate objective is to establish a self-sustaining city on Mars within the next 20 years. <a>Source: SpaceX Mars Plans</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"Being multiplanetary will vastly increase the probable lifespan of consciousness, as we will no longer have all our eggs, literally and metabolically, on one planet," Musk added. <a href="https://twitter.com/elonmusk">Source: Elon Musk's Statements</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The flight rate is expected to grow exponentially, contributing to the development of sustainable human presence on Mars. SpaceX continues to develop the Starship spacecraft, designed for long-duration interplanetary flights. </p>
