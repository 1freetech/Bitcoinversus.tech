<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark, a leading U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/20/smi-vantage-boosts-malaysian-economy-with-bitcoin-mining-facility-in-sarawak/">Bitcoin mining firm</a>, has announced its <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/cleanspark-acquires-tennessee-crypto-mining-facilities-hashrate-expansion">acquisition</a> of seven cryptocurrency mining sites in Knoxville, Tennessee. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The $27.5 million deal includes the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">mining facilities</a> and associated land, adding a total power capacity of 85 megawatts (MW). This acquisition will boost CleanSpark’s mining capacity by 5 exahashes per second (EH/s), marking a 22% increase in the company’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/08/prediction-bitcoin-hashrate-to-hit-new-all-time-highs-by-year-end/">hashrate​</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The new sites range from 10 MW to 20 MW in capacity and will be powered by Bitmain’s S21 Pro miners, which are set to be installed soon.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark’s focus on scaling its mining operations demonstrates its commitment to sustainable growth. The company expects to hit 37 EH/s by the end of 2024, making it one of the largest <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/03/how-to-replace-a-bitmain-control-board-control-board-overview/">Bitcoin mining operators</a> in the U.S. This acquisition follows its previous expansion efforts, including significant investments in Wyoming and Georgia​..</p>
