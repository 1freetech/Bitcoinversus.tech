<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">OCEAN</a>, a leading <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">Bitcoin mining pool</a>, has recently made headlines for its growing influence over the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/20/prediction-bitcoin-network-will-reach-1-zettahash-by-q4-2024/">Bitcoin mining network</a>. In a recent announcement, OCEAN revealed that it now accounts for 56% of unique miners receiving newly generated <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Bitcoin rewards</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This development raises significant concerns about the centralization of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a>, a process that was originally intended to remain decentralized.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.blockchainmagazine.com/the-centralization-of-bitcoin-mining-how-limited-supply-breeds-powerful-miners/">According</a> to the <a href="https://x.com/ocean_mining/status/1832450315053896065">mining pool</a>, their latest block paid out to 50 unique <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/30/mastercard-launches-crypto-credential-to-simplify-bitcoin-transfers/">Bitcoin addresses</a> directly from the coinbase, meaning that these miners received their rewards straight from the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/24/4267/">Bitcoin network</a> rather than through an intermediary. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Over the past six months, a total of 174 addresses have received payouts from the blockchain, and 97 of those have been associated with OCEAN.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While OCEAN <a href="https://www.blockchainmagazine.com/the-centralization-of-bitcoin-mining-how-limited-supply-breeds-powerful-miners/">touts</a> this as a step towards transparency and efficiency, critics argue that such concentration of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/23/nodal-power-turns-landfill-methane-into-green-energy-for-bitcoin-mining/">mining power</a> could undermine Bitcoin’s foundational principles of decentralization. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If a single entity controls more than half of the miners, it could potentially lead to network manipulation or even a 51% attack, where a majority of miners could theoretically double-spend or block transactions​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> continues to grow, monitoring the concentration of mining power will be essential to preserving the security and decentralized nature of the network.</p>
