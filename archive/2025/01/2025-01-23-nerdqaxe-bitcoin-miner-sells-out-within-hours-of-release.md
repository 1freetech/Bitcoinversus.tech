<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The NerdQAxe+, a cutting-edge <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/28/hydro-cooled-bitaxe-miner-enhances-bitcoin-mining-efficiency/">Bitcoin ASIC miner</a> sold by SoloSatoshi.com, has garnered significant attention in the cryptocurrency community.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10261,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/screenshot-2025-01-23-153620.png?w=595" alt="" class="wp-image-10261" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Equipped with four BM1368 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/12/07/i-got-my-bitaxe-machine-to-hash-exclusive-review/">ASIC chips</a>, the device delivers a hash rate of 2.5 TH/s while maintaining an energy efficiency of approximately 55 watts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This innovative miner is a derivative of the Bitaxe project, originally created by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/proof-of-work-awards-meet-the-10-most-influential-bitcoin-leaders-in-2024-honorable-mention-included/">Skot9000</a>, and combines the advanced, intuitive user interface and display of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/16/troubleshooting-a-bricked-nerdaxe/">NerdAxe</a> with the powerful, quad-chip architecture of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/nerdqaxe-achieves-4-8th-s-with-6-layer-pcb-upgrade/">QAxe</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10269,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/screenshot-2025-01-23-153937.png?w=337" alt="" class="wp-image-10269" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><sub><em>screen shot from solosatoshi.com</em></sub></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Upon its release, the NerdQAxe+ experienced overwhelming demand, leading to a rapid sell-out. SoloSatoshi.com has acknowledged the high interest and is working diligently to restock the product to meet consumer demand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The open-source nature of the NerdQAxe+ allows for extensive customization, appealing to both hobbyist and professional miners seeking efficient and powerful mining solutions.</p>
