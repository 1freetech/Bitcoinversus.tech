<p style="text-transform:none">In a significant development for the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/12/analyzing-centralization-and-monopolies-in-the-bitcoin-industry/">Bitcoin industry</a>, it was revealed that Bhutan holds $750 million worth of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a>, with these addresses now publicly identified on Arkham, a blockchain intelligence platform. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">This public disclosure of Bhutan's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a> marks a new chapter in the government's ongoing mining operations. Managed through Druk Holdings, Bhutan has been <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/">mining Bitcoin</a> as part of its broader investment strategy, positioning itself as a notable player in the digital currency space.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Arkham Intelligence is the first platform to publicly label and confirm these <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/30/mastercard-launches-crypto-credential-to-simplify-bitcoin-transfers/">Bitcoin addresses</a>, bringing transparency to Bhutan's growing involvement in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/15/pennsylvania-greenlights-bitcoin-mining-with-revised-bill/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The decision to make this information public also underscores Bhutan's commitment to alternative energy sources, which are integral to its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">Bitcoin mining operations</a>, leveraging its vast hydropower resources to fuel the mining process.</p>
