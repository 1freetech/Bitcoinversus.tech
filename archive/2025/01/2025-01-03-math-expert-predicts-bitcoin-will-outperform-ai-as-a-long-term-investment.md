<p style="text-transform:none">As the tech world debates <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">the future of AI and Bitcoin</a>, Fred Krueger has emerged as a leading voice, advocating for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/07/crucial-graphic-highlights-dollar-depreciation-relative-to-real-estate-and-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> as the superior investment choice. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At the recent <em>All In Conference</em>, Krueger emphasized that despite AI’s transformational potential, only a few companies, such as Nvidia, are profiting from it. His outlook echoes caution, suggesting the AI space bears resemblance to the 1999 tech bubble, where many companies failed to capitalize on the dot-com boom.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Krueger believes <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/28/bitcoin-rsi-chart-suggests-bullish-outlook/">Bitcoin</a> offers a safer long-term investment, citing its role as a hedge against the growing fiscal challenges in the U.S., Europe, and China. “Bitcoin is the only protection against the U.S. fiscal situation,” Krueger noted, pointing to Bitcoin’s 800% rise since August 2020. He predicted a meteoric rise in Bitcoin’s price, suggesting it could reach $1 million by 2033, driven by the cryptocurrency's adherence to the power law model.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Krueger’s analysis highlights the resilience of Bitcoin as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/23/blackrocks-indirect-bitcoin-investment-through-microstrategy/">institutional investors</a> continue to accumulate the asset, with recent ETF inflows boosting <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin’s market potential</a>. He contrasts this stability with AI, where despite advancements, clear investment opportunities remain uncertain​.</p>
