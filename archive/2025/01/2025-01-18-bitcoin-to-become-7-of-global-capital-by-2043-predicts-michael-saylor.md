<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a bold prediction, Michael Saylor, the co-founder of MicroStrategy, has <a href="https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/markets/bitcoin-could-reach-13-million-in-21-years-says-microstrategys-michael-saylor">forecasted</a> that Bitcoin will reach a price of $13 million per coin over the next 21 years. Saylor believes <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">Bitcoin's global importance</a> will continue to rise, growing to represent 7% of all global capital, up from its current share of 0.1%.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He outlined these predictions during a recent interview on CNBC’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/30/anthony-pompliano-discusses-bitcoins-market-dominance/">Squawk Box</a>, where he explained that Bitcoin's annual growth rate, historically around 44%, will lead to massive gains in the future.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor’s <a href="https://www.hedgewithcrypto.com/news/michael-saylor-forecasts-bitcoin-price-to-soar-to-13-million-by-2045/">optimism</a> is driven by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">Bitcoin’s liquidity </a>and its open, global capital market, which he argues makes it superior to traditional assets like gold. Under his leadership, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a> has become one of the largest corporate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/12/decoding-the-bitcoin-elite-top-10-holders-revealed/">holders of Bitcoin</a>, with 226,500 BTC valued at over $12.8 billion. According to Saylor, Bitcoin's market cap could reach $257 trillion by 2045, assuming a continued 23,417% growth from current prices​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While many share <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/03/bitcoin-whale-michael-saylor-poised-to-acquire-more-btc-through-microstrategy/">Saylor’s bullish outlook</a>, some remain skeptical due to Bitcoin’s volatility. However, long-term investors and Bitcoin enthusiasts are closely following these predictions as global adoption continues to grow. As more institutional players enter the space, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Bitcoin’s potential</a> as a dominant global asset continues to be a topic of intense discussion.</p>
