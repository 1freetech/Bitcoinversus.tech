<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The evolution of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/22/bitcoin-miner-drew-vosk-unveils-secret-immersion-cooling-facility-in-viriginia/">Bitcoin mining hardware</a> has shown a significant shift in both power and efficiency, with each new generation becoming slightly less impactful than the previous one. Early models like the Antminer S1 introduced a massive leap in processing power, but recent iterations reveal diminishing returns in both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/01/kaspa-most-profitable-machine-but-low-hashrate-and-difficulty-still-a-concern-for-miners/">hashrate and energy efficiency</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":5895,"width":"647px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/screenshot-2024-09-14-122800.png?w=858" alt="" class="wp-image-5895" style="width:647px;height:auto" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Image Source: Blockware Intelligence</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Blockware Intelligence’s <a href="https://45454036.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/45454036/blockwareHashrateReport.pdf?utm_medium=email&amp;_hsmi=322908100&amp;utm_content=322908100&amp;utm_source=hs_automation">September report</a> highlights how the latest ASIC machines, such as the S19 and S21 models, show reduced gains in efficiency. The Antminer S1, for instance, was followed by its successor, the S3, which demonstrated a dramatic 60% improvement in efficiency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, by the time of the Antminer S19 and S21 series, these improvements had dropped to around 14% and 10%, respectively. This trend indicates that while <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining technology</a> continues to advance, the efficiency gains with each new model are becoming less substantial, leaving miners facing diminishing returns on hardware investments​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":5900,"width":"672px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/screenshot-2024-09-14-122834.png?w=964" alt="" class="wp-image-5900" style="width:672px;height:auto" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Image Source: Blockware Intelligence</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Blockware Intelligence commented on this trend in the report, stating, <em>“In the early days of ASICs, each machine was orders of magnitude more powerful and efficient than its predecessor. Nowadays, the marginal efficiency gains for each new ASIC are diminishing</em>."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This reduced rate of improvement in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/">mining efficiency</a> directly impacts profitability, especially as the costs of operating these machines rise alongside the growing difficulty of mining Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Miners are left with tough choices—either continue upgrading to marginally better hardware or focus on operational efficiency to sustain profitability in an increasingly competitive market​.</p>
