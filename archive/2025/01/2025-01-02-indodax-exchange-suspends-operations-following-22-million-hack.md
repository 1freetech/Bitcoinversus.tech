<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Indonesian <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/03/india-enforces-strict-compliance-on-offshore-crypto-exchanges/">cryptocurrency exchange</a> Indodax <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/indodax-crypto-exchange-hacked-22-million-loss">suffered</a> a significant breach, resulting in the theft of $22 million worth of cryptocurrencies, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Ether</a>, and Tron. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The attack targeted Indodax’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/01/while-sec-mulls-over-etfs-elon-musks-x-carves-a-path-with-crypto-licenses/">hot wallets</a>, forcing the exchange to suspend operations and launch an investigation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Blockchain security firms PeckShield and Cyvers were among the first to detect the breach, linking the attack to possible North Korean hacking groups. Indodax is working to restore platform security and ensure users' assets remain protected.</p>
