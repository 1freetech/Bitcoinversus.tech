<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent interview with <a href="https://www.benzinga.com/24/11/41915099/cannabis-stocks-pop-following-chris-christies-prediction-on-trumps-policy-shift">Benzinga</a>, former New Jersey Governor Chris Christie discussed President-elect Donald Trump's potential plans regarding federal marijuana policy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Christie suggested that Trump might seek to reclassify marijuana to a non-scheduled status, effectively decriminalizing it at the federal level.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Christie highlighted the challenges faced by the cannabis industry due to marijuana's current Schedule I classification, which deters financial institutions from engaging with cannabis businesses. He noted that descheduling could alleviate these issues by removing the stigma associated with federally illegal substances.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
