<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/10/miningstore-ceo-jp-baric-is-revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-with-energy-efficient-solutions/">Bitcoin mining industry</a> faces increasing financial strain due to declining block rewards and rising operational costs, Loka Mining has <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-hashrate-contracts-could-offset-skyrocketing-mining-costs">unveiled</a> a forward-thinking solution. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company’s CEO, Andy Fajar Handika, introduced forward <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/06/the-luxor-hashprice-formula-is-a-new-way-to-interpret-bitcoins-value/">hashrate contracts</a> as a novel way for miners to secure short-term financing by selling their <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">future hashrate</a>. This approach aims to provide smaller mining operations with immediate liquidity, enabling them to maintain operations and pursue growth even in challenging market conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/VersusInstitute/status/1831139005192089914","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These hashrate forward contracts are offered in 3-month, 6-month, and 1-year terms, allowing miners to obtain fiat-denominated loans against their future mining power. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph:59b801a17094b:0-bitcoin-mining-ceo-eyes-hashrate-contracts-to-offset-rising-costs/">strategy</a> not only helps miners purchase new equipment but also shifts the risk of Bitcoin price volatility to the investors purchasing these contracts. By doing so, miners can hedge against market fluctuations while securing the capital needed for expansion​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The introduction of these contracts is particularly timely, as the recent <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/23/the-bitcoin-halving-is-otw-heres-what-you-need-to-know/">Bitcoin halving</a> in April 2024 reduced <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/09/yzers-gamified-app-rewards-learners-with-sats/">block rewards</a> from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC, putting additional pressure on miners' profit margins. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Many smaller operations, which lack the fundraising capabilities of larger companies, have been forced to sell their <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a> or use them as collateral for loans on decentralized finance platforms. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By offering a more secure and predictable financing option, Loka Mining’s forward hashrate contracts could prevent widespread miner capitulation and help stabilize the industry during this period of economic uncertainty.</p>
