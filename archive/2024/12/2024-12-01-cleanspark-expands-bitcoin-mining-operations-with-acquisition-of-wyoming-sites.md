<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark Inc., a prominent player in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining industry</a>, has <a href="https://www.cleanspark.com/blog/cleanspark-executes-75-mw-of-power-contracts-and-completes-acquisition-of-bitcoin-mining-site-in-wyoming?pid=11582&amp;highlight=2024">completed</a> the acquisition of two Bitcoin mining sites in Cheyenne, Wyoming. The $18.75 million transaction marks a significant expansion of CleanSpark’s operational footprint, bringing an additional 75 megawatts (MW) of power capacity to the company’s portfolio.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The first of these sites, which features a power capacity of 30 MW, is expected to add over 2 exahashes per second (EH/s) to CleanSpark’s total hashrate once fully operational. The site will utilize the latest S21 immersion XP <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/29/bitcoin-mining-hardware-prices-surge-amid-hashrate-increase/">mining machines</a>, designed for enhanced efficiency and performance. Groundbreaking is slated for immediate commencement, with full operations expected by the end of 2024.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to this, CleanSpark is in the final stages of <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-08-01/cleanspark-cheyenne-bitcoin-mining/">securing</a> the underlying real estate for a second site in Wyoming. This location is projected to bring an additional 45 MW online, contributing an extra 3 EH/s to the company’s total hashrate. Wyoming now joins Georgia, Mississippi, Tennessee, and New York as part of CleanSpark’s expanding network of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">Bitcoin mining data centers</a>​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark’s CEO, Zach Bradford, expressed excitement about the acquisition, stating, “On the heels of the University of Wyoming announcing the launch of its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/28/university-of-wyoming-establishes-bitcoin-research-institute/">Bitcoin Research Institute</a> and Senator Lummis introducing the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">BITCOIN Act</a>, we are thrilled to expand in a state so publicly supportive of our industry.” </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move underscores CleanSpark’s strategic focus on expanding its operations in regions that support the growth and sustainability of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Bitcoin mining industry</a>​.</p>
