<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to <a href="https://www.coinspeaker.com/bitcoin-worth-1-billion-2038-fidelity/">multiple sources</a>, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/07/fidelity-expert-jurrien-timmer-endorses-bitcoin-calls-asset-exponential-gold/">Jurrien Timmer</a>, Director of Global Macro at <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/11/fidelitys-wise-origin-bitcoin-fund-holds-over-34000-btc-worth-1-3-billion/">Fidelity</a>, the investment firm has made several bold forecasts regarding <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/05/bitcoin-poised-for-peak-in-2025-says-analyst-rekt-capital/">Bitcoin’s future value</a>. One such prediction is that one <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> may be worth $1 billion by 2038​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This prediction is based on Timmer’s analysis of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">Bitcoin’s growth potential</a>, drawing parallels with the historical performance of stocks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He notes that $1 invested in stocks in the 18th century is now worth approximately $4 billion today. By extrapolating this concept, Timmer suggests that if $1 million today were to experience similar growth rates over the next twenty years, it could potentially reach a value of $1 billion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/25/fidelity-digital-assets-officially-acknowledges-bitcoins-potential/">Fidelity</a> has also <a href="https://u.today/fidelitys-timmer-makes-surprising-market-prediction-that-might-impact-cryptocurrencies">previously predicted</a> that Bitcoin will reach $100 million price levels by 2035 using the Stock-to-Flow model. More recently, Jurrien Timmer forecasted that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> will reach $100,000 by the end of 2023.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While these predictions are ambitious and subject to market fluctuations, they demonstrate Fidelity’s confidence in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Bitcoin’s long-term potential</a>. As the cryptocurrency continues to evolve and gain mainstream acceptance, only time will tell if these forecasts become a reality​.</p>
