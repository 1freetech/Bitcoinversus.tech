<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/teslas-bitcoin-holdings-reach-1b-amid-market-surge/">Arkham Intelligence</a>, a blockchain analytics firm, <a href="https://cryptobriefing.com/arkham-spot-trading-platform-launch/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has announced</a> the official launch of its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/proof-of-work-awards-meet-the-10-most-influential-bitcoin-leaders-in-2024-honorable-mention-included/">cryptocurrency exchange</a> in the United States. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The platform aims to provide U.S. traders with access to a range of digital assets, positioning itself as a competitor to established exchanges like Coinbase and Crypto.com. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The exchange will offer spot trading services, allowing users to buy and sell cryptocurrencies directly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/arkham-intelligence-launches-crypto-perpetuals-083334439.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">The launch</a> follows Arkham's recent introduction of a crypto derivatives exchange targeting retail investors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This platform features live on-chain audits and proof-of-reserve systems to enhance transparency in cryptocurrency trading. However, due to regulatory considerations, the derivatives exchange is not available to U.S. traders.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Founded in 2020, Arkham Intelligence specializes in blockchain data analysis using artificial intelligence. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The firm <a href="https://cryptobriefing.com/arkham-spot-trading-platform-launch/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has received</a> backing from notable investors, including Sam Altman, co-founder of OpenAI. The expansion into the U.S. market reflects Arkham's strategy to broaden its services and compete with major industry players.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The U.S. cryptocurrency market has seen significant growth, with increasing demand for diverse trading platforms. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Arkham's entry introduces a new option for traders seeking advanced analytics and transparent trading environments. The firm's focus on security and compliance aims to build trust among U.S. users as it establishes its presence in the competitive exchange landscape.</p>
