<p style="text-transform:none">OpenAI has <a href="https://techround.co.uk/news/chatgpt-4o-upgrade/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announced</a> a significant update to its GPT-4o model, enhancing its creative writing capabilities and improving its handling of uploaded files. <p style="text-transform:none">The update also <a href="https://techround.co.uk/news/chatgpt-4o-upgrade/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">enhances</a> GPT-4o's ability to process uploaded documents, such as PDFs and text files.
