<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">TeraWulf, a leading player in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining industry</a>, is <a href="https://ultramining.com/news/en/terawulf-unveils-cutting-edge-bitcoin-mining-facility-ahead-of-major-expansion/">preparing to launch</a> its latest 2-megawatt facility, WULF Den, at the start of September. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This state-of-the-art facility features a liquid-cooled system and was rapidly constructed within an existing building to speed up the deployment process. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The WULF Den facility is part of TeraWulf's broader strategy to expand its operations, with plans to bring a larger 20-megawatt facility online by the end of the year.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to this expansion, TeraWulf is actively engaged <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/terawulf-bitcoin-mining-facility-launch-tech-partnership">in discussions</a> with major technology companies, including members of the Magnificent Seven—Alphabet, Amazon, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/26/how-to-connect-your-ipad-with-a-uag-keyboard-bluetooth-lite-review/">Apple</a>, Meta, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/20/microsofts-ai-expert-jaron-lanier-advocates-for-data-dignity-amid-bitcoin-revolution/">Microsoft</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">Nvidia</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Tesla</a>. These potential partnerships could involve leveraging TeraWulf’s substantial computing power for data centers or large AI firms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Financially, TeraWulf is recognized as one of the most profitable <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin miners</a> on a per-share basis, with an average production cost of around $40,000 per Bitcoin. Unlike <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a>, which opts to hold <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/11/michael-saylor-explains-how-bitcoin-will-reshape-corporate-balance-sheets/">Bitcoin as a reserve asset</a>, TeraWulf’s strategy involves daily sales to maximize profit margins.</p>
