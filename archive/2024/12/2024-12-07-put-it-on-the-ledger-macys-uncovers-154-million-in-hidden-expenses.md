<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Macy's Inc. has <a href="https://www.barrons.com/articles/macys-stock-price-earnings-accounting-employee-a805efa3?utm_source=chatgpt.com">uncovered</a> a significant accounting discrepancy involving a former employee who allegedly concealed between $132 million and $154 million in delivery expenses over nearly three years.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The individual <a href="https://www.marketwatch.com/story/macys-stock-drops-after-erroneous-accounting-entries-found-leading-to-delay-of-quarterly-results-4da28190?utm_source=chatgpt.com">responsible</a> for tracking small-package delivery costs is no longer with the company.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">An independent investigation <a href="https://www.barrons.com/articles/macys-stock-price-earnings-accounting-employee-a805efa3?utm_source=chatgpt.com">revealed</a> that the employee made erroneous accounting entries to hide these expenses from the fourth quarter of 2021 through November 2, 2024.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the substantial amount, there is no indication that the accounting errors impacted Macy's cash management or vendor payments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As a result of the findings, Macy's has <a href="https://www.barrons.com/articles/macys-stock-price-earnings-accounting-employee-a805efa3?utm_source=chatgpt.com">postponed</a> its third-quarter earnings release to December 11, 2024, to complete the independent investigation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Preliminary results <a href="https://www.marketwatch.com/story/macys-stock-drops-after-erroneous-accounting-entries-found-leading-to-delay-of-quarterly-results-4da28190?utm_source=chatgpt.com">indicate</a> a 2.4% decline in net sales to $4.742 billion, aligning with analysts' expectations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's stock experienced a 2.6% decline following the announcement, reflecting investor concerns over the internal controls and financial oversight.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Macy's assures stakeholders that measures are being implemented to prevent such incidents in the future.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The incident underscores the importance of robust accounting practices and internal controls in large corporations. Macy's commitment to transparency and corrective action aims to restore confidence among investors and customers.</p>
