<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At CNBC's Delivering Alpha 2024 conference, Ben Affleck, co-founder and CEO of Artists Equity, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypURoMU3P3U">expressed confidence</a> that artificial intelligence (AI) will not replace actors or writers in the foreseeable future. He <a href="https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/delivering-alpha-live-peltz-einhorn-to-discuss-post-election-stock-market-economy/5981573/">emphasized</a> that while AI can generate imitative content, it lacks the creative nuance and taste inherent in human artistry.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypURoMU3P3U","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Affleck <a href="https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/delivering-alpha-live-peltz-einhorn-to-discuss-post-election-stock-market-economy/5981573/">highlighted</a> the unique value of human collaboration in filmmaking, noting that the collective discernment and construction of narratives by actors are beyond AI's current capabilities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He stated that AI's role might be limited to handling more laborious and less creative aspects of production, potentially lowering costs and barriers to entry for emerging filmmakers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9119,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During the conference, Affleck also discussed the potential for AI to personalize entertainment experiences. He <a href="https://www.vulture.com/article/ben-affleck-ai-succession-fanfic.html?utm_campaign=feed-part&amp;utm_medium=social_acct&amp;utm_source=chatgpt.com">envisioned</a> a future where audiences could request custom episodes of their favorite shows, tailored to specific plotlines, thereby creating new revenue streams and partnerships between Hollywood and Silicon Valley.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Affleck's perspective aligns with a broader industry sentiment that, despite technological advancements, the essence of storytelling and performance remains a distinctly human endeavor. He believes that while AI can assist in certain areas, it cannot replicate the depth and authenticity that human creators bring to the arts.</p>
