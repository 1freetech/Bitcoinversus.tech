<p>Examining the Power Law Model’s Structure</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Unlike conventional investment models, Perrenod’s approach aligns Bitcoin’s market performance with power laws observed across various fields, proposing that Bitcoin’s growth is not just exponential but follows a stable, long-term trend. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Proponents of this model believe that, much like other natural phenomena, Bitcoin’s progression is less random than expected, forecasting a predictable price increase fueled by a 5.8x power law relationship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This model could redefine how investors perceive Bitcoin, offering an alternative to the stock-to-flow model, which has faced criticism in recent years for its lack of reliability during market downturns.</p>
