<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Braiins has <a href="https://www.blocktrainer.de/en/blog/braiins-introduces-bmm-100-mini-miner">introduced</a> the BMM 100 Mini Miner, an innovative device allowing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/29/bitcoiner-explains-negativity-within-traditional-finance-towards-bitcoin/">Bitcoin enthusiasts</a> to not only mine but also engage in gaming activities. This miner, with a hash rate of 1 terahash per second and a power consumption of just 40 watts, is ideal for home-based miners who want to decentralize the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">Bitcoin network</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">What sets the BMM 100 apart is its ability to play the classic game DOOM, giving users a unique blend of utility and entertainment.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The BMM 100 comes <a href="https://braiins.com/hardware/bmm-100-mini-miner">pre-installe</a>d with Braiins OS+, a lightweight operating system designed to optimize Bitcoin mining. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With its compact size and low noise output (40 dB), it’s suitable for small spaces, allowing users to mine quietly at home. The built-in display lets users monitor essential mining data like hashrate, temperature, and even the current <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/07/intel-vaneck-releases-major-prediction-for-bitcoin-price/">price of Bitcoin</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Braiins continues to push forward with innovative hardware, this dual-function miner reflects their commitment to making <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> accessible, while adding an element of fun for tech enthusiasts.</p>
