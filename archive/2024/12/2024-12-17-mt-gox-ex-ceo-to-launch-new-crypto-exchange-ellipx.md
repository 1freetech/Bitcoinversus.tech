<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mark Karpelès, the former CEO of the defunct Mt. Gox cryptocurrency exchange, is <a href="https://www.cryptonewsz.com/ex-mt-gox-ceo-launch-new-cryptoexchange-ellipx/">preparing to launch</a> a new platform called EllipX by the end of September 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Based in Poland, this new exchange aims to serve European users initially, with plans for global expansion. EllipX will focus on transparency and user-friendliness, addressing the growing demand for secure and reliable trading platforms. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>https://x.com/coingecko/status/1831212760077873220</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The platform is also expected to comply with the EU’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/28/analysis-bitcoin-hyper-regulation-may-stall-global-economic-growth/">Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation</a> (MiCA), ensuring strict oversight and regular third-party audits to maintain operational transparency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move comes as Karpelès <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/former-mt-gox-ceo-ellipx-crypto-exchange-europe">looks to regain trust</a> in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">crypto community</a>, and as part of this effort, former Mt. Gox users will receive at least a 50% discount on trading fees at EllipX​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">EllipX will initially offer <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/26/shopinbit-is-leading-the-bitcoin-e-commerce-revolution-in-europe/">crypto-only services</a>, with potential future expansions into banking and fiat currency services. The platform aims to provide an intuitive experience for both newcomers and seasoned traders alike​.</p>
