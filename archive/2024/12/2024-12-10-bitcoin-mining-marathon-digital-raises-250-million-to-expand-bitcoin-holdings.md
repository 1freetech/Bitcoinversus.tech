<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin mining company <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a> has <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/marathon-to-raise-usd250-million-to-buy-more-bitcoin-8693811">announced</a> plans to raise $250 million through a debt offering to acquire additional <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Marathon already holds over 20,000 BTC on its balance sheet and is continuing to pursue an aggressive strategy of increasing its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">cryptocurrency reserves</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The funds from this convertible note offering will be used primarily for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/31/gta-bitcoin-monetizing-video-games/">Bitcoin purchases</a> and general corporate purposes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move follows a similar approach first popularized by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/microstrategy-plans-2-billion-equity-offering-to-purchase-bitcoin/">MicroStrategy</a> in 2020, when the software company began raising debt to accumulate Bitcoin as a reserve asset. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/13/marathon-digital-invests-100-million-in-bitcoin/">Marathon's recent purchase of $100 million</a> worth of Bitcoin brought its total holdings to over $1.28 billion​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The convertible notes, set to mature in 2031, offer institutional investors a chance to buy additional notes as part of the offering..</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As more companies adopt Bitcoin as part of their strategic financial planning, Marathon continues to bet big on the cryptocurrency, reinforcing its position as one of the largest holders of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> among publicly traded companies.</p>
