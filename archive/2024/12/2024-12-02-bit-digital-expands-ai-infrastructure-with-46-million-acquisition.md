<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bit Digital, Inc. (NASDAQ: BTBT), a leader in digital asset and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">AI infrastructure</a>, has made a significant acquisition, <a href="https://bit-digital.com/press-releases/bit-digital-inc-vertically-integrates-acquiring-tier-3-hpc-datacenter-company-280-mw-pipeline-in-major-metropolitan-areas/">purchasing</a> Enovum Data Centers for approximately $46 million. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The acquisition adds a Tier 3 data center in Canada to Bit Digital's portfolio, bolstering its infrastructure with 4 MW of fully operational capacity and providing a potential expansion pipeline of 288 MW​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The acquisition reflects Bit Digital's strategy of diversifying its operations beyond <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a>, while remaining asset-light in terms of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/03/how-to-replace-a-bitmain-control-board-control-board-overview/">mining hardware</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By expanding into the HPC space, Bit Digital is well-positioned to tap into the growing demand for AI and machine learning processing power. The Tier 3 facility is located in a major metropolitan area, which will allow the company to service a broader array of clients seeking high-reliability data center solutions​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This deal was structured with both cash and stock components, with the total consideration amounting to CAD $62.8 million, equivalent to around USD $46 million. As Bit Digital moves forward with its growth strategy, this acquisition marks a pivotal step in solidifying its presence in the AI and HPC sectors​.</p>
