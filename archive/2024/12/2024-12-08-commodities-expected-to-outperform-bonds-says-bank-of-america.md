<p style="text-transform:none">Bank of America <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/commodities/commodity-bull-rally-begins-gold-oil-outlook-high-inflation-2024-8">has forecasted</a> that commodities could generate annualized returns of approximately 11%, nearly doubling the recent performance of bonds. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">This projection comes as the bank identifies several factors driving this trend, including rising debt, demographic changes, and reverse-globalization. According to the bank's strategists, these elements are all contributing to a structural rise in inflation, which is expected to fuel demand for commodities like oil and gold.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Among commodities, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">gold</a> has been a particularly strong performer, surging by 21% this year alone. Oil prices have remained relatively stable, trading at levels similar to those of two years ago. Despite this, <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/commodities/commodity-bull-rally-begins-gold-oil-outlook-high-inflation-2024-8">the overall commodities index</a> has outperformed bonds, with annualized returns ranging between 10% and 14%, even amidst falling inflation and a dovish <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/23/federal-reserve-chair-jerome-powell-signals-shift-in-monetary-policy-at-jackson-hole/">Federal Reserve</a>.</p>
