<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Two unidentified companies have <a href="https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/two-companies-seek-to-develop-125bnai-data-centers-in-north-dakota/">proposed</a> building <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">artificial intelligence</a> (AI) data centers in North Dakota, with each project potentially costing up to $125 billion. This massive investment could transform the state's tech infrastructure, marking it as a critical hub for AI-driven operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During an August meeting with the North Dakota Public Service Commission, Commissioner of Commerce Josh Teigen <a href="https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/two-companies-seek-to-develop-125bnai-data-centers-in-north-dakota/">revealed</a> the companies' plans, noting that discussions have involved both him and Governor Doug Burgum. The proposed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">AI data centers</a> could start with 500 MW to 1 GW projects, with a possibility to scale up to 5-10 GW in the future.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the companies' identities remain undisclosed, it's speculated that they are trillion-dollar enterprises, with firms like Microsoft, Amazon, and Google being potential contenders. Microsoft has shown interest in similar projects, having previously explored a $100 billion AI supercomputer campus dubbed 'Stargate'​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Governor Burgum’s background as a former Microsoft executive adds credibility to these speculations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">North Dakota’s current data center market is relatively small, but the state has seen a 37% surge in electricity demand from data centers over the past four years, according to the Energy Information Administration. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The state’s ample energy production, especially as the third-largest crude oil producer in the U.S., makes it an attractive location for such energy-intensive projects​.</p>
