<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/image.png?w=594" alt="" class="wp-image-9653" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Bitaxe is a fully open-source Bitcoin miner, allowing anyone to access and modify its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/how-to-build-an-embedded-system-hardware-edition/">hardware</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/command-1-sudo-linux-os/">software</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Ultra model is the third major version of Bitaxe and now includes the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/bitaxe-ultra-achieves-1-2-th-s-with-immersion-cooling-technology/">BM1366 chip</a>, which is known for its efficiency and is also used in Bitmain's S19XP mining rigs. This update makes the Ultra model even more powerful and accessible for Bitcoin miner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":5581,"width":"631px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/screenshot-2024-09-03-140650.png?w=623" alt="" class="wp-image-5581" style="width:631px;height:auto" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em><sup>Bitaxe Web Installer Availablity for different model Bitaxe Machines. Image Source: <a href="https://wantclue.github.io/bitaxe-web-flasher/">Wantclue, Github</a></sup></em></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><br>Bitaxe Machines are currently <em>not compatible</em> with 5G frequency status. You need to make sure you have a 2.4GHz frequency to get your Bitaxe machine online and hashing. I currently have the Bitaxe v2.0.1 there are several versions of Bitaxe Machines.<br><br>The <a href="https://www.bitaxe.org/">Bitaxe</a> lineup features a variety of models, each designed to cater to different user needs and mining setups. Among the options are the Bitaxe Supra, available in board versions 401 and 402, and the Bitaxe Ultra, which comes in several board versions, including 202, 204, and 205.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The availability of multiple board versions and models highlights the Bitaxe's adaptability in the competitive field of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a>. Personally, I have the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/bitaxe-startup-efficiency-peaks-explained/">Bitaxe</a> Version 201.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It serves as a solid option for certain applications. Each model and version has its own unique features, making it essential for miners to select the one that best aligns with their specific goals and technical expertise</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The BM1366 is an <a href="https://github.com/skot/bitaxe">undocumented</a> SHA256 mining ASIC from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/03/how-to-replace-a-bitmain-control-board-control-board-overview/">Bitmain</a>, primarily utilized in the Antminer S19XP and S19k Pro models. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitmain claims that the BM1366 achieves an impressive efficiency of 0.021J/GH, making it one of the most energy-efficient chips available for Bitcoin mining.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This ASIC is available for around $15 each in small quantities, making it a relatively affordable option for those looking to build or upgrade mining equipment. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/bitaxe-ultra-achieves-1-2-th-s-with-immersion-cooling-technology/">BM1366</a> differs in footprint and pinout from its predecessors, the BM1397 and BM1387, which were used in previous <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/quantum-bitcoin-mining-futuristic-heatsink-enhances-bitaxe-asic-chip-performance/">Bitaxe models</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, the BM1366 has the capability to roll more than just the nonce on the chip, a feature that enhances its ability to create longer serial chains of ASICs, reducing the frequency at which new work needs to be sent to the chip​.</p>
