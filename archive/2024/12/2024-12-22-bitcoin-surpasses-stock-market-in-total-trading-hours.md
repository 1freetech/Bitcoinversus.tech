<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin, the world’s leading cryptocurrency, has now <a href="https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/24/09/40745688/bitcoin-bull-says-king-crypto-logged-more-trading-hours-than-equities-since-nixon-shock-ol">logged</a> more trading hours than the U.S. stock market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This milestone highlights the continuous 24/7 nature of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/28/bitcoin-rsi-chart-suggests-bullish-outlook/">cryptocurrency markets</a> compared to traditional stock exchanges, which operate on fixed trading hours. Since its inception in 2010, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a> has clocked an estimated 123,774 hours of trading, surpassing the stock market's 100,737 hours during the same period.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The difference in trading hours underlines Bitcoin’s appeal as a global, decentralized currency accessible at any time, offering flexibility beyond the confines of traditional equity markets. This shift is reflective of the growing influence of digital assets on the financial landscape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/07/intel-vaneck-releases-major-prediction-for-bitcoin-price/">Matthew Sigel</a>, Head of Digital Assets Research at <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">Van Eck</a>, emphasized how Bitcoin's continuous availability has attracted more investors worldwide, further reinforcing the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/09/bitcoins-growing-dominance-a-forecast-of-the-spot-etf-approval/">cryptocurrency’s dominance</a> in the financial world. As the crypto market grows, this gap in trading hours between Bitcoin and the stock market is likely to increase.</p>
