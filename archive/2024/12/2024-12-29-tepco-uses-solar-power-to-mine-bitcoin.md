<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Japan's largest power company, TEPCO, is <a href="https://insidebitcoins.com/news/tepco-utilizes-excess-renewable-energy-for-bitcoin-mining">leveraging</a> its surplus renewable energy to mine <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> through its subsidiary Agile Energy X. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative addresses a major issue in Japan's energy sector, where solar and wind energy production often exceeds demand, leading to wasted power. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 2023, the country curtailed 1,920 gigawatt-hours of renewable energy, enough to power hundreds of thousands of households. Agile Energy X has strategically placed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/03/how-to-replace-a-bitmain-control-board-control-board-overview/">Bitcoin mining rigs</a> near solar farms in Gunma and Tochigi prefectures, using this otherwise wasted energy to fuel mining operations​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Agile Energy X’s President, Kenji Tateiwa, highlighted the potential for Bitcoin mining to create new revenue streams for green energy producers, particularly those facing overinvestment risks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By utilizing even a <a href="https://cryptohead.io/news/power-giant-tepco-eyes-bitcoin-mining-to-capture-wasted-solar-energy/">fraction</a> of Japan's renewable energy surplus, Bitcoin mining could generate significant profits—up to $2.5 billion annually if just 10% of surplus power is used​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move aligns with global trends, as other countries like El Salvador are also repurposing excess renewable energy for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining</a>, which helps dispel the myth that mining is inherently harmful to the environment.</p>
