<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The U.S. Bankruptcy Court has <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/ftx-reorganization-moves-forward-as-us-bankruptcy-judge-grants-approval/">approved</a> FTX's plan to repay creditors a minimum of 118% of their claims. The decision, delivered by Judge John Dorsey on October 7, 2024, marks a pivotal moment in the ongoing fallout from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/28/culture-sean-diddy-combs-and-sam-bankman-fried-now-share-high-profile-jail-unit/">FTX's collapse</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The repayment, estimated to involve between $14.5 billion and $16.5 billion in assets, will prioritize smaller creditors with claims of $50,000 or less, who are expected to receive payments within the next 60 days. Larger claims may take longer, potentially being resolved by mid-2025​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The approved plan is noteworthy because it will repay creditors in cash rather than <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a>, a decision that has stirred mixed reactions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Some creditors had <a href="https://www.tronweekly.com/ftx-bankruptcy-return-creditors/">hoped</a> for in-kind distributions, particularly given Bitcoin's surge from $16,000 at the time of FTX's collapse to over $63,000 today. However, FTX's current holdings of Bitcoin and other <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/14/stocks-on-steroids-market-enter-goldilocks-zone-amid-strong-jobs-report-fed-policies/">cryptocurrencies</a> were insufficient to honor such requests​.</p>
