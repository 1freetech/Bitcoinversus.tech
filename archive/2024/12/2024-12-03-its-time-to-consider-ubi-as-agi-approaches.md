<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As advancements in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/intel-ai-could-take-over-coding-in-two-years-says-aws-ceo-matt-garman/">artificial general intelligence</a> (AGI) progress rapidly, some experts predict its arrival by 2026-27. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The potential impact of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/can-robots-actually-be-considered-criminals/">AGI</a> on employment and economic stability is immense, with machines capable of performing a wide range of human tasks autonomously. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">With this transformative shift on the horizon, now is the time to start discussing the benefits of universal basic income (UBI).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Anticipation of AGI has sparked concerns about job displacement, as entire industries may become automated. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">By introducing UBI, society can provide a financial safety net, helping individuals transition through this shift without a sudden economic shock. UBI would ensure everyone receives a basic income to cover essential needs, potentially stabilizing markets and reducing income inequality. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Addressing UBI now could help smooth the adaptation to a future where AGI plays a significant role, allowing society to focus on re-skilling and new opportunities rather than widespread economic disruption.</p>
