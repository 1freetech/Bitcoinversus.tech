<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/19/hive-digital-prioritizes-efficiency-with-major-antminer-s19k-pro-investment/">HIVE Digital</a> Technologies has <a href="https://hivedigitaltechnologies.com/news/hive-digital-technologies-announces-august-2024-production-of-112-bitcoin-and-1-hodl-growth-to-2533-bitcoins-and-acquires-1000-bitmain-s21-pro-for-immediate-delivery/">announced</a> the purchase of 1,000 Bitmain S21 Pro Antminers, marking a significant upgrade to its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/29/bitcoin-mining-hardware-prices-surge-amid-hashrate-increase/">mining fleet</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This acquisition is expected to increase HIVE's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/">mining efficiency</a> and capacity as part of its broader strategy to optimize operations. The newly acquired miners will contribute an additional 200 PH/s to HIVE’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/08/prediction-bitcoin-hashrate-to-hit-new-all-time-highs-by-year-end/">hashrate</a>, boosting the company's total mining capacity to 5.3 EH/s.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">HIVE's purchase of the S21 Pro Antminers aligns with its ongoing efforts to improve <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/13/energy-how-to-enhance-data-center-efficiency/">energy efficiency</a>. The company's global fleet now operates with an efficiency of 23 joules per terahash (J/TH), and the integration of these new miners is expected to further enhance this. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, HIVE remains committed to using green energy sources, with a focus on hydroelectricity, to power its mining operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This purchase not only enhances HIVE’s mining capacity but also underscores its focus on maintaining low general and administrative expenses while maximizing returns on investment. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of August 31, 2024, HIVE’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a> amounted to 2,533 BTC, with a total value exceeding $164 million​.</p>
