<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/born-to-win-the-privileged-success-behind-alexandr-wang-and-bronny-james/">Scale AI</a> has <a href="https://scale.com/blog/defense-llama">unveiled</a> Defense Llama, a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/22/citi-and-jpmorgan-lead-10b-financial-package-for-openai/">Large Language Model</a> (LLM) developed in collaboration with Meta and defense experts, tailored specifically for U.S. national security applications. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This model is accessible exclusively within controlled U.S. government environments through Scale Donovan, enabling military and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/08/massive-data-breach-exposes-social-security-numbers/">national security</a> personnel to leverage generative AI for tasks such as military operations planning and intelligence analysis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Defense Llama is built upon Meta's Llama 3 model and has been fine-tuned using Scale's proprietary Data Engine. The training dataset encompasses military doctrines, international humanitarian law, and Department of Defense (DoD) guidelines, ensuring the model's outputs align with DoD ethical principles and provide accurate, relevant responses.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The model's responses adhere to the writing style and tone prescribed by the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), enhancing its applicability for national security professionals. Defense Llama has undergone evaluation using DoD-specific benchmarks to verify its understanding of military terminology and protocols.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Scale AI's CEO, Alexandr Wang, emphasized the company's commitment to supporting American national security, stating that Defense Llama enables service members to harness generative AI to meet their specific mission needs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The introduction of Defense Llama follows Meta's recent decision to make its Llama models available to U.S. government agencies and defense contractors, marking a significant development in the integration of AI technologies within national security frameworks.</p>
