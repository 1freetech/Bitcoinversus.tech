<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Amid escalating <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/u-s-housing-prices-hit-record-highs-in-2024/">housing prices</a> and elevated mortgage rates, a significant number of first-time homebuyers are increasingly relying on inheritances to secure their initial properties. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A <a href="https://nypost.com/2024/03/28/nepo-housing-market-gen-z-millennials-parents-helping-with-downpayment/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">recent survey</a> by Redfin revealed that 16% of Gen Z and millennial buyers anticipate using an inheritance for their down payment, highlighting the growing dependence on familial financial support in the current housing market.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The trend of utilizing inheritances for home purchases underscores the <a href="https://fortune.com/recommends/mortgages/how-to-buy-a-house/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">challenges faced</a> by younger generations in accumulating sufficient funds for down payments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/12/u-s-homebuyer-age-hits-record-56/">median home price</a> in the United States reaching $420,800, a 20% down payment would require approximately $85,000, a daunting figure for many prospective buyers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This reliance on inherited wealth not only reflects the financial hurdles confronting first-time buyers but also raises concerns about widening economic disparities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As wealth transfers become a <a href="https://www.marketwatch.com/story/the-biggest-wealth-transfer-in-american-history-is-upon-us-some-will-benefit-far-more-than-others-1f6c213e?utm_source=chatgpt.com">pivotal factor</a> in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/28/understanding-the-income-required-for-a-home-in-the-us/">homeownership</a>, individuals without access to such resources may find it increasingly difficult to enter the housing market, potentially exacerbating existing inequalities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In response to these challenges, experts advocate for policies aimed at enhancing housing affordability and accessibility. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Proposed measures include first-generation down-payment assistance programs and initiatives to increase housing supply, which could provide critical support to aspiring homeowners lacking familial financial backing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
