<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Japanese government is set to <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/japan-considers-lowering-cryptocurrency-tax-rate-to-unified-20-from-current-55/">reform</a> its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/01/kaspa-most-profitable-machine-but-low-hashrate-and-difficulty-still-a-concern-for-miners/">cryptocurrency</a> tax policies by significantly reducing the maximum tax rate on crypto earnings from 55% to 20%. This proposed change is aimed at aligning the tax treatment of cryptocurrencies with that of traditional financial assets, such as stocks. Currently, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/unpopular-opinion-gary-gensler-is-the-perfect-fit-for-crypto-oversight/">crypto profits</a> are taxed under a miscellaneous income category, with the highest tax rate applied to earnings over 200,000 yen (approximately $1,377)​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Financial Services Agency (FSA) of Japan is spearheading this initiative as part of a broader fiscal overhaul planned for 2025. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The new tax regime would introduce a flat 20% rate for individual <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/03/india-enforces-strict-compliance-on-offshore-crypto-exchanges/">crypto investors</a>, addressing long-standing concerns from industry advocates and the Japan Blockchain Association. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to benefiting individuals, the reform would also ease the tax burden on corporations holding cryptocurrency, which are currently subject to a flat 30% tax​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposed tax reduction is seen as a positive step for encouraging further <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">crypto adoption</a> and investment within Japan, particularly as the country looks to position itself as a leader in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/24/faith-meets-finance-the-islamic-debate-on-bitcoin/">digital finance</a>​.</p>
