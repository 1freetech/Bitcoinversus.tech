<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to a recent <a href="https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/digital-assets/matthew-sigel-bitcoin-2050-valuation-scenarios-global-medium-of-exchange-and-reserve-asset/">analysis</a> by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/07/intel-vaneck-releases-major-prediction-for-bitcoin-price/">VanEck</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/28/bitcoins-rising-value-outpaces-traditional-wage-growth/">Bitcoin's value</a> could skyrocket to $2.9 million per coin by 2050. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This bold prediction is grounded in the expectation that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> will gain significant traction as both a global medium of exchange and a reserve asset. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/digital-assets/matthew-sigel-bitcoin-2050-valuation-scenarios-global-medium-of-exchange-and-reserve-asset/">report </a>suggests that as traditional fiat currencies such as the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/04/bitcoin-and-the-history-of-the-u-s-dollar/">U.S. dollar</a>, euro, yen, and pound lose their dominance due to declining economic relevance and fiscal challenges, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> could fill the void in international trade​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">VanEck’s projection assumes that by 2050, Bitcoin could account for 10% of global cross-border payments and 5% of domestic trade. The analysis also anticipates that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/24/bitmex-founder-arthur-hayes-predicts-historic-bitcoin-surge-by-2026/">central banks</a> will hold 2.5% of their assets in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/03/bitcoin-whale-michael-saylor-poised-to-acquire-more-btc-through-microstrategy/">Bitcoin</a>, signaling widespread acceptance and legitimacy of the cryptocurrency in global financial systems​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/digital-assets/matthew-sigel-bitcoin-2050-valuation-scenarios-global-medium-of-exchange-and-reserve-asset/">report</a> highlights that the potential for Bitcoin's ascent is closely tied to the development of Layer-2 (L2) solutions, which are expected to resolve the cryptocurrency’s scalability issues. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">VanEck</a> projects that these <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/03/river-financial-sheds-light-on-lightning-networks-potential/">L2 solutions</a> could be valued at approximately $7.6 trillion, making up 12% of Bitcoin’s total value.</p>
