<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/25/apple-opens-iphone-nfc-chip-to-third-parties-paving-way-for-bitcoin-payments/">Apple</a> is <a href="https://9to5mac.com/2024/11/10/apple-vision-pro-2-fall-2025-spring-2026/">reportedly</a> preparing to release the second generation of its mixed-reality headset, the Vision Pro 2, between fall 2025 and spring 2026. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, the development is on track for this timeline, with the new model anticipated to feature enhanced performance and capabilities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Industry analyst Ming-Chi Kuo suggests that the Vision Pro 2 will be equipped with Apple's M5 chip, offering a significant upgrade over the current M2 processor. This advancement is expected to support more demanding applications and improve overall user experience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to the high-end model, Apple is reportedly working on a more affordable version of the Vision Pro headset. However, the release of this budget-friendly option may be delayed until 2027, as the company focuses on refining its features and market positioning.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The original Vision Pro, <a href="https://nypost.com/2024/11/12/tech/apples-vision-pro-flop-company-scales-back-production-of-3500-vr-headset-amid-lackluster-sales-customer-complaints/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">launched</a> in February 2024, received mixed reviews, with some users citing discomfort and a high price point as concerns. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Apple aims to address these issues in the upcoming iterations, striving to make spatial computing more accessible and comfortable for a broader audience.</p>
