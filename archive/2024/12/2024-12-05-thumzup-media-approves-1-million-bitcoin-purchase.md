<p style="text-transform:none">Thumzup Media Corporation, a Nasdaq-listed social media marketing firm, has <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/15/2981922/0/en/Thumzup-Board-of-Directors-Approves-Bitcoin-as-Treasury-Reserve-Asset.html">announced</a> plans to purchase up to $1 million in Bitcoin to bolster its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">treasury reserves</a>.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Robert Steele <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/thumzup-board-of-directors-approves-bitcoin-as-treasury-reserve-asset-1034025239">highlighted</a> Bitcoin's finite supply and inflation-resistant properties as key factors in this strategic move.</p>
