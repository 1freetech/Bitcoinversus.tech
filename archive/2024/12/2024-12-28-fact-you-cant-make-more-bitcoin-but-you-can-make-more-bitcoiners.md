<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent <a href="https://x.com/asanoha_gold/status/1831912589212971165">viral tweet</a> by @asanoha_gold, an image circulates with the bold statement: “You Can’t Make More <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a>, You Can Make More <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/29/bitcoiner-explains-negativity-within-traditional-finance-towards-bitcoin/">Bitcoiners</a>.” This message captures the spirit of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin’s unique scarcity</a> and the growing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/31/to-the-moon-literally-bitmex-plans-on-sending-1-bitcoin-to-the-moon/">Bitcoin community</a>. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">While Bitcoin has a <a href="https://river.com/learn/can-bitcoins-hard-cap-of-21-million-be-changed/">hard cap</a> of 21 million coins, this tweet underscores the idea that although no more Bitcoin will ever be created, new adopters and enthusiasts—“Bitcoiners”—can continue to emerge, contributing to Bitcoin’s rising global adoption​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The tweet also hints at broader socio-economic concerns, particularly in light of declining birth rates across Western countries. Encouraging people to create more Bitcoiners is not only a nod to expanding the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/22/autumn-domingo-discusses-mission-of-generation-bitcoin-with-charlene-faredepo/">Bitcoin community</a> but also subtly touches on the <a href="https://x.com/mnolangray/status/1477533015899361283">growing conversations</a> about societal trends such as declining population growth.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/29/bitcoin-mining-hardware-prices-surge-amid-hashrate-increase/">Bitcoin’s value</a>, both monetary and cultural, is founded on its finite supply and decentralized architecture. With only 21 million coins ever to exist, Bitcoin’s appeal lies in its scarcity. This scarcity is a core tenet of its value proposition, enticing investors who see Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation and a secure, long-term store of value​.</p>
