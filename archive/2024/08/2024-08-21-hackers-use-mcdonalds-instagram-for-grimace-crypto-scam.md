<p style="text-transform:none">Earlier this week, McDonald's Instagram account was <a href="https://mycryptoparadise.com/fast-food-fraud-mcdonalds-instagram-hack-siphons-700k-in-solana-scam/">compromised</a> to promote a Solana-based memecoin named GRIMACE. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The fraudulent post claimed the token was a McDonald’s experiment, leading to a rapid surge in GRIMACE’s value to a $20 million market cap before plummeting to under $1 million.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The hackers not only manipulated the platform to promote the memecoin but also made bold claims, <a href="https://x.com/watcherguru/status/1826300798726189340?s=51">stating</a>, "sorry mah [explicit] you have just been rug pulled...thank you for the $700,000 in Solana," indicating a successful rug pull. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Although McDonald's quickly removed the post, the incident left behind a trail of financial losses for investors who got caught in the hype​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This situation underscores the vulnerability of even major corporate social media accounts to hacking attempts aimed at fueling <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/26/space-force-guardian-jason-p-lowerys-twitter-account-hacked/">crypto scams</a>. GRIMACE, which was initially portrayed as an experiment by McDonald's, became another <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/06/sam-bankman-fried-found-guilty-in-high-profile-crypto-fraud-trial/">cautionary tale for the crypto community</a>. As <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">meme coins</a> continue to attract both legitimate and fraudulent attention, users are advised to remain vigilant and skeptical of sudden opportunities that seem too good to be true​.</p>
