<h4 class="wp-block-heading">A Significant Move Toward Digital Assets in Public Pensions</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The State of Michigan Retirement System has made a notable investment in Bitcoin by purchasing $6.6 million worth of ARK 21Shares <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/06/prediction-a-bitcoin-etf-will-outperform-the-success-of-gold-etfs/">Bitcoin Exchange-Traded Funds</a> (ETFs). This investment, disclosed in a recent filing with the U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/unpopular-opinion-gary-gensler-is-the-perfect-fit-for-crypto-oversight/">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC), marks Michigan as the third U.S. state to include <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/02/bitcoin-etfs-attract-over-720-million-on-first-day-of-trading/">Bitcoin ETFs</a> in its pension fund portfolio​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Michigan Retirement System bought 110,000 shares of the ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF, each valued at about $68.04 at the time. Relatively speaking, this represents a small portion of the $143.9 billion in total assets managed by the Michigan pension fund.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Growing Trend Among U.S. Pension Funds</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move follows a growing trend among public pension funds to explore investments in digital assets. Earlier this year, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/tag/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board/">State of Wisconsin Investment Board</a> (SWIB) disclosed a $164 million investment in Bitcoin through <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/09/blackrock-invests-over-1-billion-in-bitcoin/">BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust</a> and Grayscale's Bitcoin Trust. In addition, Jersey City's pension fund is also considering allocating funds to Bitcoin ETFs, as announced by Mayor <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/politics-jersey-city-pension-fund-to-invest-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Steven Fulop​</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Encouraging Institutional Adoption</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The approval of spot Bitcoin ETFs by the SEC in January has encouraged more institutional investors to consider Bitcoin as a viable investment option. The ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF, traded on the Cboe BZX Exchange, has been performing well, with total assets under management reaching $3.2 billion. This trend is expected to continue as more state pension funds explore similar investment opportunities​.</p>
