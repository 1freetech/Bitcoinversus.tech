<p style="text-transform:none">Students in a public school in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">El Salvador</a> are learning about <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining</a> using a <a href="https://www.bitaxe.org/">Bitaxe</a> ASIC miner. The <a href="https://www.bitaxe.org/">Bitaxe</a>, a handheld <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/17/amid-high-demand-for-asic-repair-techs-zeus-mining-launches-asic-repair-course/">ASIC miner</a>, offers models with a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/08/prediction-bitcoin-hashrate-to-hit-new-all-time-highs-by-year-end/">hashrate</a> ranging from 400 to 700 GH/s.</p>
