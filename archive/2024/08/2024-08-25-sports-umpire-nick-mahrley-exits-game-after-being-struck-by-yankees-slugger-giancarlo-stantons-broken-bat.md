<p style="text-transform:none">During the fifth inning of the New York Yankees' game against the Colorado Rockies on August 25, 2024, a frightening incident occurred when home plate umpire Nick Mahrley was hit by a piece of Giancarlo Stanton's broken bat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Stanton's bat shattered as he hit a bloop single to left field, and the barrel struck Mahrley on the left side of his mask, knocking it off. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mahrley immediately collapsed and was attended to by New York's athletic training staff. The game paused as a medic came onto the field, and Mahrley was carted off, drawing applause from the concerned crowd​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Following the Yankees' 10-3 victory, it was <a href="https://www.aol.com/sports/plate-umpire-nick-mahrley-exits-193124422.html">confirmed</a> that Mahrley had suffered a concussion and would miss his next assigned series. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The game resumed with crew chief Marvin Hudson taking over behind the plate​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Mahrley, a 41-year-old veteran of 473 games as a callup umpire, had only recently become a full-time umpire. His injury serves as a reminder of the unpredictable dangers faced by officials on the field​.</p>
