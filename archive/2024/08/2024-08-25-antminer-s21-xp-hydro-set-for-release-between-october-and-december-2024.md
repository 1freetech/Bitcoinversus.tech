<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/08/bitmain-extends-hashboard-life-with-new-protective-temp-sensor-coating/">Bitmain</a> Antminer S21 XP Hyd is poised for release between October and December 2024, setting a new standard in the world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining</a>. Designed to maximize efficiency, this miner boasts a remarkable <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/20/prediction-bitcoin-network-will-reach-1-zettahash-by-q4-2024/">hashrate</a> of 473 terahashes per second (TH/s) while maintaining a power consumption of 5676 watts, making it one of the most powerful options available for SHA-256 algorithm mining.​</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Featuring advanced <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">hydro cooling</a> technology, the Antminer S21 XP Hyd efficiently dissipates heat, enhancing its overall performance and longevity. The device is designed to operate in temperatures ranging from 5 to 45 degrees Celsius, with a humidity tolerance of 5% to 95%. Measuring 410 x 170 x 209mm and weighing approximately 12.8 kg, this miner ensures a compact and efficient mining experience​​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Profitability projections for the Antminer S21 XP Hyd indicate significant returns for miners. At current electricity rates, daily profits are estimated at $10.66 (assuming your power consumption is at 0.08/KWh), with yearly earnings projected to reach roughly $3900. This level of profitability is achieved through the machine's exceptional energy efficiency of 12 joules per terahash (J/TH), setting a new benchmark in the mining industry​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The Antminer S21 XP Hyd is available for purchase at a unit price of $14,521.10, with potential discounts lowering the price to $10,165. This competitive pricing reflects its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/28/smarter-together-u-s-china-breakthrough-in-graphene-semiconductor-technology/">advanced technology</a> and high performance. Miners can choose from payment methods including USD, BTC, ETC, and USDT (ERC20/TRC20)​. The device supports multiple cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash, and Bitcoin SV, offering flexibility for diverse mining operations​.</p>
