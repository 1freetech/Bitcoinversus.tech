<p style="text-transform:none">Republican presidential candidate Vivek Ramaswamy <a href="https://coingape.com/vivek-ramaswamy-demands-kamala-harris-crypto-stand-gary-genslers-sec-future/">predicted</a> that Vice President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">Kamala Harris</a> would likely embrace the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/morgan-stanley-becomes-first-major-bank-to-offer-bitcoin-etfs-to-clients/">Bitcoin</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">cryptocurrency</a> industry as part of her political strategy. Ramaswamy, a strong advocate for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> and a key supporter of former President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/25/trumps-exclusive-bitcoin-2024-fundraiser-seats-priced-at-844600/">Donald Trump</a>, stated that Harris might adopt a pro-crypto stance to attract the growing base of crypto enthusiasts and tech-savvy voters.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Ramaswamy emphasized that Harris’s engagement with the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">crypto community</a> could be a strategic move to distinguish herself from other <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/14/bitcoin-advocate-rfk-jr-sets-independent-course-for-2024-presidential-run/">political figures</a>, particularly in light of the recent pushback against the crypto industry by regulatory bodies like the SEC. He suggested that Harris's potential support for crypto could include advocating for self-custody of digital assets and reassessing the role of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/unpopular-opinion-gary-gensler-is-the-perfect-fit-for-crypto-oversight/">Gary Gensler</a> as SEC Chairman. Ramaswamy views this shift as a positive development for the political discourse surrounding cryptocurrency, highlighting the importance of clear and supportive policies for the future of digital assets in the U.S.</p>
