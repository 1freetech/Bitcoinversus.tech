<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Vice President Kamala <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/27/kamala-harris-aims-to-build-democratic-ties-with-crypto-community/">Harris</a> has <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/crypto4harris-launches-mark-cuban-town-hall-for-kamala-harris.htm">officially</a> launched her campaign's pro-crypto initiative, known as <em>Crypto4Harris</em>. This new advocacy group is designed to strengthen ties between the Harris campaign and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/10/theory-what-if-blackrock-controlled-bitcoin-mining/">cryptocurrency industry</a>, which has seen a strained relationship under the Biden administration. The group's primary aim is to position Kamala Harris as a leader who can balance innovation in digital assets with necessary consumer protections.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <em>Crypto4Harris</em> initiative is expected to host its first virtual town hall meeting on August 14, 2024, featuring high-profile figures such as billionaire entrepreneur Mark Cuban. These events will focus on discussing policy approaches that can foster a regulatory environment supportive of cryptocurrency growth while addressing market stability and consumer protection concerns​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The formation of <em>Crypto4Harris</em> is a strategic move by Harris’s campaign to appeal to a growing number of crypto investors and enthusiasts. The <a href="https://blockonomi.com/kamala-harris-campaign-reaches-out-to-crypto-heavies/">initiative</a> also represents an effort by the Democratic Party to rebrand itself as pro-business and to rebuild its relationship with the tech industry, particularly in Silicon Valley​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mark Cuban, who was approached by Harris’s team, expressed optimism about the campaign's willingness to engage with the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/29/who-is-satoshi-nakamoto-new-emails-with-adam-back-enter-public-domain/">crypto community</a>, seeing it as a positive step towards a more crypto-friendly regulatory environment. This move comes at a critical time as the 2024 presidential election draws near, with Harris expected to clarify her stance on Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies in the coming weeks​.</p>
