<p style="text-transform:none">Auradine, Inc. has <a href="https://auradine.com/auradine-teraflux-bitcoin-miner/">announced</a> the initial deliveries of its Teraflux 2800 series, a groundbreaking advancement in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining technology</a>. This platform, built with 3-nanometer silicon <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/08/bitmain-extends-hashboard-life-with-new-protective-temp-sensor-coating/">ASIC technology</a>, aims to set new benchmarks in performance, energy efficiency, resilience, and operational efficiency. Designed and manufactured in the U.S., the Teraflux platform is the fastest and most resilient solution available today. The company plans to showcase live demonstrations of the Teraflux platform at the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/25/trumps-exclusive-bitcoin-2024-fundraiser-seats-priced-at-844600/">Bitcoin 2024 conference</a> in Nashville, allowing attendees to experience its capabilities firsthand <a href="https://auradine.com/auradine-teraflux-bitcoin-miner/">Auradine</a>​​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The Teraflux 2800 series features both air-cooled and single-phase immersion-cooled miners. The AT2880 air-cooled miner delivers a peak output of 260 terahashes per second (TH/s), while the AI3680 immersion-cooled miner achieves up to 375 TH/s with an efficiency as low as 14 joules per terahash (J/TH) in optimal mode. These miners incorporate Auradine’s EnergyTune technology, providing unparalleled flexibility and economics in energy demand response and integration with leading Virtual Power Plants such as CPower and Voltus.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The Teraflux platform includes several unique features that contribute to its superior total cost of ownership (TCO). The SMART System Design enhances system durability and security, allowing miners to continue operating even if multiple chips fail on a hashboard. This feature maximizes uptime and reduces repair costs. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The platform also has a wide operating range, capable of functioning in temperatures from -20°C to 50°C, ensuring reliability in diverse environmental conditions. Additionally, Auradine’s Zero-Touch Provisioning software allows for pre-configuration of miners, fast onboarding, and instant hashing following deployment​​.</p>
