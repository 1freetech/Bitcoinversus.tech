<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) technology is one of the most intriguing innovations today. Companies like Synchron, BlackRock Neurotech, and Tesla's Neuralink are at the forefront of this rapidly developing field, aiming to transform the lives of individuals with severe medical conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Synchron's Stentrode BCI uses a <a href="https://synchron.com/">minimally invasive</a> procedure to insert the device without requiring open brain surgery. This technology has been successfully implanted in the first U.S. patient, marking a significant milestone in scalable BCI devices​​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">BlackRock Neurotech's NeuroPort system, which has been in development for the longest, has gathered the most data. This BCI technology helps patients with paralysis control external devices like robotic arms or wheelchairs through their thoughts. "Restoring movement and sensation is crucial for improving the quality of life for patients with severe disabilities," said Marcus Gerhardt, CEO of BlackRock​​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Tesla's Neuralink, led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/01/while-sec-mulls-over-etfs-elon-musks-x-carves-a-path-with-crypto-licenses/">Elon Musk</a>, aims to develop a fully implantable BCI that is cosmetically invisible and designed to let users control computers or mobile devices anywhere they go. Neuralink's recent human trials represent a critical step towards integrating BCIs into everyday life.</p>
