<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/28/bitcoins-rising-value-outpaces-traditional-wage-growth/">Bitcoin's value</a> recently dipped below the $50,000 mark for the first time since February, reaching a low of $49,351. This <a href="https://decrypt.co/242990/bitcoin-drop-below-50000-us-economy-sours-10x-research">decline</a> is part of a broader market sell-off, contributing to a 17% reduction in the overall cryptocurrency market capitalization. Factors influencing this downturn include increasing interest rates, concerns over inflation, and regulatory pressures on cryptocurrency exchanges.</p>
