<p style="text-transform:none">The "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">Crypto4Harris</a>" advocacy group has announced a town hall featuring influential figures such as billionaire Mark Cuban and Representative Wiley Nickel. <a href="https://lu.ma/dpgcaxsi">Starting at 7PM ET</a>, this event marks a pivotal attempt by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">Vice President Kamala Harris</a>' supporters to court the crypto industry, aiming to reset and strengthen her policy stance on digital assets ahead of the 2024 presidential election.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The town hall is expected to draw a diverse group of participants, including elected officials, policy leaders, and industry heavyweights, to discuss strategies to support Harris' campaign and to foster a regulatory environment conducive to the growth of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/04/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board-allocates-2-of-assets-to-bitcoin-etfs/">digital assets in the U.S</a>. This initiative underscores a significant shift as Harris seeks to build bridges with a sector that has felt overlooked by current regulatory approaches.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This engagement with the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/politics-jersey-city-pension-fund-to-invest-in-bitcoin-etfs/">crypto community</a> comes at a critical time, with less than 90 days to the U.S. election, where Harris is positioning herself as a candidate aware of the burgeoning importance of technology and innovation in American economics and politics. The involvement of figures like Mark Cuban highlights the campaign’s commitment to involving key industry players in policy dialogues that could shape the future of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">cryptocurrency regulation in the U.S</a>.</p>
