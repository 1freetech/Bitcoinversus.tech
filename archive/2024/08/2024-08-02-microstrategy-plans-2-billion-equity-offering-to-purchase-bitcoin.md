<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy Inc</a>., the software company led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/11/michael-saylor-of-microstrategy-forecasts-bitcoin-as-the-leading-asset-class/">Michael Saylor</a>, has announced plans to raise up to $2 billion through an at-the-market equity offering to fund further purchases of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/28/university-of-wyoming-establishes-bitcoin-research-institute/">Bitcoin</a>. The company, known for its aggressive investment in Bitcoin, intends to continue expanding its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/01/kaspa-most-profitable-machine-but-low-hashrate-and-difficulty-still-a-concern-for-miners/">cryptocurrency holdings</a>, which already stand at 152,800 BTC, valued at approximately $5.9 billion.<br></p>

<p style="text-transform:none">This move comes as part of MicroStrategy's ongoing strategy to leverage its position in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/18/bitcoin-ordinals-market-surges-outpacing-top-two-nft-markets-combined/">Bitcoin market</a>. The firm has been systematically buying Bitcoin to strengthen its balance sheet and position itself as a leader in corporate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/31/unpopular-opinion-michael-saylor-is-the-best-candidate-for-secretary-of-the-treasury/">Bitcoin adoption</a>. </p>
