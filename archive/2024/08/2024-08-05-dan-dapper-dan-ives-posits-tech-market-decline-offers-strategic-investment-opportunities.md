<p style="text-transform:none">Dan "Dapper Dan" Ives, managing partner at Wedbush Securities, discussed the global <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/05/bitcoin-dips-under-50000-as-economic-concerns-rise/">market sell-off</a> on CNBC's "Squawk Box," highlighting opportunities within the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/28/smarter-together-u-s-china-breakthrough-in-graphene-semiconductor-technology/">tech sector</a>. Ives emphasized that the decline in technology stocks is not a cause for panic but rather an opportunity for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/23/blackrocks-indirect-bitcoin-investment-through-microstrategy/">strategic investment</a>. He noted that despite market volatility, companies like Apple and Microsoft remain strong due to innovations in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">AI and cloud computing</a>​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Ives pointed out that the sell-off is influenced by broader economic concerns, including inflation and interest rates, but he urged investors to focus on long-term potential. According to Ives, "I believe this is just a massive fear of panic that will create the opportunity to own $NVDA, $AAPL, $MSFT, $AMZN and $GOOGL, and that's always been our playbook." He emphasized that the fundamentals of tech companies are robust, and their growth prospects remain intact​.</p>
