<p><strong>Cynthia Lummis Proposes Bitcoin as a Strategic Reserve Asset</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">U.S. Senator Cynthia Lummis, alongside Senator Kirsten Gillibrand, has introduced a groundbreaking bill aimed at integrating Bitcoin into the U.S. financial system. This proposal, which is expected to be presented shortly, seeks to establish <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin</a> as a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/18/intel-bitcoin-og-dennis-porter-says-claims-trump-wants-bitcoin-reserve-in-nashville/">strategic reserve asset</a> for the United States. The bill addresses various aspects of cryptocurrency regulation, including tax policies, stablecoin regulation, and consumer protections​.</p>

<p><strong>Potential Endorsement by Donald Trump</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a related development, Dennis Porter, CEO of the Satoshi Act Fund, has suggested that former President Donald Trump might endorse this bill. Porter has highlighted the strategic importance of Bitcoin, proposing its integration into the U.S. Exchange Stabilization Fund (ESF). This move could help stabilize the U.S. dollar and provide liquidity during financial crises​</p>
