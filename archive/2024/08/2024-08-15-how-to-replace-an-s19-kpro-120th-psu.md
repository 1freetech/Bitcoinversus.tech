<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><em>The <strong>S19 KPro </strong>is an exclusive model PSU designed by <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">Bitmain</a></strong>. </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><em>The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/03/01/computer-muscle-understanding-the-power-supply-unit/">power supply unit</a> (PSU) is a fundamental component of the Bitcoin <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitaxe-gamma-redefines-asic-mining-with-advanced-cooling-features/">ASIC machine</a>. The <strong>S19 KPro </strong>(pictured below) is a specific PSU for the <strong> S19 KPro </strong>model <strong>ASIC</strong> machine. </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><em>Generally, there are specific model PSU’s that are compatible with specific <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin Mining Machines</a>.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4770} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-1.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4770" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong><em>Hardware Review:</em></strong> <strong><em>Final Grade - 77/100 C</em></strong>+</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the S19Kpro, like most next-gen <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/08/bitmain-extends-hashboard-life-with-new-protective-temp-sensor-coating/">Bitmain</a> machines, is an adaptable machine due to it's ability to install different types of control boards, the design of this particular model is of lower quality than it's JPro constituents, simply in part because the JPro model PSU's are usually of a simpler design, which make them easier to assemble and disassemble. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">I'm giving this one a solid C+ because, while it's not the worst machine in production, it's definitely not the best in terms of technician-assembly friendliness. <strong><br></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4787} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-17.png" alt="" class="wp-image-4787" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1.</strong> <strong>Materials needed:</strong><br>A. Drill (w/phillips head)&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>B. Phillips head screwdriver.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":5040,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/img_2005-1.png?w=795" alt="" class="wp-image-5040" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">When assembling the PSU,<strong><em> always remember</em></strong>, “<strong><em>Righty Tighty Lefty Loosey</em></strong>.” <strong>Turn the screw right to tighten</strong> and <strong>turn it left to loosen</strong> the screw.<br><br></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>2. </strong>Remove the top <strong>3 screw</strong>s so that both lids are completely off.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4780} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-9.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4780" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4774} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-4-1.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4774" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4781} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-9-1.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4781" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>3. </strong>Remove the <strong>4 larger screws</strong> from the battery terminal and remove the the <strong>2 power cables </strong>connected to the control board.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4785} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-11-1.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4785" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>4. </strong>Remove the fan gate on the backside of the server. (4 screws at each corner)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4783} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-11.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4783" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4771} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-2.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4771" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4777} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-6-1.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4777" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4773} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-4.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4773" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4772} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-3.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4772" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>5. </strong>Gently slide the old psu off. Once removed, This is what the PSU will look like.<strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4778} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-7.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4778" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>6. </strong>Gently<strong> </strong>Slide the New PSU on.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4786} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-12.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4786" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>7. </strong>Put the fan gate back on with <strong>4 screws.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4782} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-10.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4782" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>8. </strong>Attach the terminal screws to the top of the psu and connect the 2 power cables.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4784} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-10-1.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4784" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>9</strong>. For your convenience, put the smaller of the two top lids on first</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4779} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-8.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4779" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4775} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-5.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4775" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4776} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-6.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4776" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>10. <strong>Great Job!</strong> You’ve just replaced the psu on an <strong>S19Kpro</strong></p>
