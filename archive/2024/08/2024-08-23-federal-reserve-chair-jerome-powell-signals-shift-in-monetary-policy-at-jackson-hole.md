<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">Federal Reserve</a> Chair <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/25/bitcoin-financial-markets-anticipate-jerome-powells-jackson-hole-address/">Jerome Powell</a> announced that the time has come for <a href="https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/jackson-hole-fed-meeting-powell-speech">policy adjustments</a>, indicating a shift towards cutting interest rates. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
