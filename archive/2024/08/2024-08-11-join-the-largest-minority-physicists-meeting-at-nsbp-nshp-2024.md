<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://nsbp.org/">The National Society of Black Physicists</a> (NSBP) and the <a href="https://hispanicphysicists.org/">National Society of Hispanic Physicists</a> (NSHP) are co-hosting their largest academic meeting of minority physicists from November 13 to 17, 2024, at the Hyatt Regency Houston. This joint conference aims to foster diversity, inclusion, and excellence in the field of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/25/explore-realistic-space-physics-with-space-engine-on-steam-os/">physics</a>. It will feature sessions for students and professionals, providing a platform for networking, collaboration, and knowledge sharing in the physics community​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The NSBP-NSHP 2024 Conference in Houston marks the first joint meeting of these societies in over a decade. The event will include keynote addresses, panel discussions, and poster sessions designed to highlight recent advancements in physics and promote career development for attendees​​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Attendees can <a href="https://hispanicphysicists.org/jointmeeting/">register</a> through the NSBP website, with options available for both students and professionals​.</p>
