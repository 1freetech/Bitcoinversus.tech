<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Marathon Digital</a> Holdings has recently made a significant investment by purchasing $100 million worth of Bitcoin. This acquisition aligns with Marathon's strategy to increase its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/09/blackrock-invests-over-1-billion-in-bitcoin/">Bitcoin holdings</a> and adopt a "full HODL" approach, meaning they plan to retain their Bitcoin without selling it in the near future. This move comes amid a broader trend of institutional adoption and favorable macroeconomic conditions for Bitcoin. According to Marathon, this purchase underscores their confidence in Bitcoin's long-term value.</p>
