<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://x.com/dennis_porter_/status/1813949707368149329?s=51">Dennis Porter</a> claims that former President Donald Trump plans to announce a strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/11/michael-saylor-explains-how-bitcoin-will-reshape-corporate-balance-sheets/">Bitcoin reserve</a> in Nashville, Tennessee. <p style="text-transform:none">Trump's recent involvement in the Bitcoin community includes commitments to support Bitcoin mining and to advocate for the cryptocurrency on a national level. He has met with major U.S. Bitcoin miners and pledged to protect Bitcoin and crypto rights if elected president​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The announcement of a Bitcoin strategic reserve would mark a significant step in integrating cryptocurrency into the national economic strategy. This development aligns with Trump's efforts to position the U.S. as a leader in the Bitcoin and crypto space​.</p>
