<p style="text-transform:none">The University of Wyoming (UW) has launched the UW Bitcoin Research Institute, the first academic institute focused exclusively on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/22/autumn-domingo-discusses-mission-of-generation-bitcoin-with-charlene-faredepo/">Bitcoin studies</a>. Dr. Bradley Rettler, an Associate Professor of Philosophy and author of "Resistance Money: A Philosophical Case for Bitcoin," has been appointed as the institute's inaugural director. The announcement was made at the Bitcoin 2024 Conference in Nashville.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/uwcbdi_congratulations-to-bradley-rettler-on-the-activity-7223348548040323073-an3Z?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_desktop">post</a> on LinkedIn, The UW Bitcoin Research Institute stated, “Congratulations to Bradley Rettler on the establishment of the UW Bitcoin Research Institute! The BRI's focus on high-quality peer-reviewed publications will significantly advance Bitcoin scholarship. At the CBDI, we’re excited to complement the BRI through our applied approach - offering a minor degree, mining bitcoin, facilitating student internship/job placements, forging industry partnerships, and driving Bitcoin project development. Together, we will ensure the University of Wyoming leads in academic institutional support of Bitcoin and its growing industry!"</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The institute aims to enhance Bitcoin research quality by creating faculty positions dedicated to Bitcoin studies, rewarding excellent academic research, and encouraging young scholars to explore Bitcoin-related projects. It will also offer workshops and provide peer-reviewed research for journalists, policymakers, and the public. This initiative addresses the current need for more rigorous and accurate academic studies on Bitcoin.</p>
