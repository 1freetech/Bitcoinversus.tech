<p style="text-transform:none">The UAG Rugged Bluetooth Keyboard with Trackpad is designed for durability and performance. With a non-slip exterior that withstands 8-foot drops, it provides reliable protection. The backlit keys offer a laptop-like typing experience, and multi-touch trackpad supports gestures like swiping and pinching. The adjustable magnetic stand provides four viewing angles, and the rechargeable battery lasts up to 6 months. Plus, it includes a slot for the Apple Pencil. The hardware configuration is fairly simple. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Overall Grade: 76 (C+)</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The UAG Rugged Bluetooth Keyboard with Trackpad is a convenient accessory for iPad users, but it has its limitations. The keyboard is rather small, and the trackpad does not move as fluidly as one might like. Overall, I give the product a C+.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
