<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Azure Cloud Drama</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The outage also extended to Microsoft’s Azure cloud, specifically affecting the Central US region based in Iowa. All three availability zones in this region went offline, causing major disruptions for businesses relying on Azure’s cloud services. Companies had to quickly implement disaster recovery plans to manage the impact,</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Airlines Grounded</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The outage's ripple effect reached the aviation industry, with airlines like Frontier grounding their planes due to the chaos. Passengers experienced delays as the airline's operations were disrupted by the IT issues. The widespread impact of the outage highlighted the critical role cloud services play in various sectors​ </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Businesses in Limbo</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Globally, businesses found themselves in a state of disarray, with services down and IT teams scrambling to troubleshoot. Major incidents like this are akin to a digital fire drill, with everyone working urgently to restore normalcy. The outage underscored the vulnerability of relying heavily on cloud services and the importance of robust disaster recovery plans​.</p>
