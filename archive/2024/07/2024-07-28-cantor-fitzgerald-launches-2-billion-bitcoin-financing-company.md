<p style="text-transform:none">Cantor Fitzgerald, a prominent global financial services group, has <a href="https://www.coindesk.com/business/2024/07/27/wall-streets-cantor-fitzgerald-to-open-bitcoin-financing-lending-business/">announced</a> the launch of a new Bitcoin financing business. The initiative begins with an initial $2 billion in lending, with plans to offer additional $2 billion increments to provide leverage to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/12/decoding-the-bitcoin-elite-top-10-holders-revealed/">Bitcoin owners</a>.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Howard Lutnick, the CEO of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_Fitzgerald">Cantor Fitzgerald</a> stated, “Bitcoin should trade the same as gold everywhere in the world without exception and without limitation.” This perspective highlights the firm’s belief in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/10/greg-foss-predicts-bitcoins-potential-rise-to-2-million/">Bitcoin's potential</a> to become a universally recognized and valuable asset​​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cantor Fitzgerald’s entry into Bitcoin financing is expected to significantly impact the market, providing new opportunities for Bitcoin investors to leverage their holdings. The firm has a history of innovation and leadership in financial services, and this new venture continues its tradition of pioneering in emerging markets​​.</p>
