<p style="text-transform:none">Vice President Kamala Harris is <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2024/05/17/crypto-congress-democrats-00158630">aiming</a> to mend the strained relationship between the Democratic Party and the US cryptocurrency community. Recent <a href="https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2024/07/27/democrats-pushing-harris-campaign-for-reset-on-crypto-stance-house-rep-says/">developments</a> on Capitol Hill indicate a shift in the Democratic stance toward digital currencies, with notable figures like Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer and former House Speaker Nancy Pelosi showing unexpected support for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">bitcoin-friendly legislation</a>​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">In a significant move, 32 Democrats joined Republicans to advance a rollback of SEC guidelines perceived as restrictive towards digital assets. This move came despite opposition from President Joe Biden and Senator <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/21/passage-of-elizabeth-warrens-bill-would-likely-cause-the-first-digital-civil-war/">Elizabeth Warren</a>, who are known for their critical stance on cryptocurrencies​. This shift is seen as an attempt to balance regulatory concerns with the growing influence and advocacy of the crypto industry within the US political landscape.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Crypto-friendly legislation has been gaining bipartisan support, highlighting the industry's increasing political clout. House Financial Services Chair Patrick McHenry, an advocate for the industry, referred to this period as a "high watermark" for crypto in Washington. This sentiment reflects the growing acceptance and legitimization of digital assets in federal financial regulations.</p>
