<p style="text-transform:none">In 2020, For me to fully understand what <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin</a> was, I knew that I’d need to understand <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/28/how-to-download-applications-via-powershell-a-step-by-step-guide/">computer code</a>. When the pandemic was in full effect, I had time to study my first language, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/computer-programming/c/">C++</a>. Bitcoin is primarily written in C++, so that’s where I wanted to start. It was recommended that I study <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/09/my-red-tea-infusion-full-python-course-review-final-score/">Python</a> as well. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/computer-programming/html/">HTML</a> also seemed practical in case I needed to build a website.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During this time, I also discovered that you could contribute to the <a href="https://github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin">Bitcoin source code</a>. I thought that was cool and had ambitions to contribute. Even if no rewards were given, volunteering to improve the Bitcoin source code would certainly be an accomplishment in my eyes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With all this in mind, I figured I might as well become a full stack developer. That’s basically what I’m doing over the next 2-8 years. </p>
