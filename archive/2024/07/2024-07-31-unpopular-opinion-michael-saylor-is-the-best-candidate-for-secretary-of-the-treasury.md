<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">Michael Saylor</a>, the executive chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a>, has long advocated for Bitcoin as a primary reserve asset. He believes that integrating Bitcoin into corporate balance sheets can provide significant financial stability and growth.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">If the US Treasury were to adopt Bitcoin under the guidance of Michael Saylor, it could potentially fortify the dollar by diversifying the nation’s reserves with a highly appreciating asset. This move would align with Saylor’s strategy at MicroStrategy, where the company has aggressively accumulated Bitcoin to safeguard its treasury against the devaluation of fiat currencies​​. By holding Bitcoin, the Treasury could leverage its appreciating value, thus reinforcing the strength of the dollar in the global market​.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Global Speculation on Bitcoin Ownership</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The increasing adoption of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/30/which-countries-hold-the-most-bitcoin-in-2024/">Bitcoin globally</a> suggests that more countries might be accumulating this digital asset. Analysts predict a significant increase in the number of countries holding Bitcoin if its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">adoption</a> continues at the current pace​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Given <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/25/bitcoins-growth-potential-spotlighted-on-x-com/">Bitcoin's potential</a> to act as a store of value, countries may see it as a strategic asset to hedge against economic instability and international sanctions. The assumption is that as Bitcoin's velocity and market integration increase, more nations will recognize its benefits, potentially leading to a doubling of state-level Bitcoin holders.</p>
