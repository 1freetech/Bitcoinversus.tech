<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/06/the-luxor-hashprice-formula-is-a-new-way-to-interpret-bitcoins-value/">hashprice</a> refers to the revenue a miner earns for contributing computational power (measured in hashes) to the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin network</a>. It's often expressed as dollars per petahash per day ($/PH/day). The hashprice is crucial for miners, as it directly relates to the profitability of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/">Bitcoin mining operations</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent <a href="https://twitter.com/luxortechnology/status/1734273102924915148?s">tweet</a>, Luxor highlighted a decrease in hashprice, marking a significant shift in the Bitcoin Mining Landscape.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The tweet by Luxor suggests an expectation about the average revenue miners can expect in that future period. This figure is particularly significant because it gives miners, investors, and analysts an insight into the expected market conditions post-halving. It can inform decisions on mining investment, the viability of mining operations, and expectations about the broader Bitcoin market dynamics following the halving event.<br><br>The significance of the post-halving hashprice curve dropping can be better understood in the context of Bitcoin's "halving" events. The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/16/j-p-morgan-predicts-bitcoins-hash-rate-to-drop-by-20-ahead-of-2024-halving/">Bitcoin halving</a>, which happens approximately every four years, cuts the reward for mining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/16/honda-embraces-bitcoin-starts-new-chapter-in-automotive-transactions/">Bitcoin transactions</a> in half. This reduction in rewards leads to a decrease in the amount of new <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> entering circulation, which can impact the supply-demand balance and potentially drive up the price of Bitcoin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The immediate effect the next halving will most likely be a decrease in hashprice. Since the mining reward is halved, unless the price of Bitcoin increases proportionally, miners earn less per hash they contribute. This can squeeze the profitability of less efficient miners and lead to a consolidation in the mining industry.</p>
