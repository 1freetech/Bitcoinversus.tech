<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/26/record-high-ordinal-volumes-hint-at-new-bitcoin-bull-market/">Bitcoin Ordinals</a> market has shown significant growth in 2023, but the claim that it is now four times larger than the next two NFT markets combined may not be accurate. According to a <a href="https://nftnow.com/news/research-and-mining-report-highlights-bitcoins-enduring-impact/">report</a> by NFT Now, by the end of 2023, Ordinals are projected to reach a trading volume of approximately $725 million. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the dominance of Bitcoin Ordinals in the NFT market needs to be contextualized with the performance of other NFT marketplaces. For instance, Blur, a new NFT marketplace, has seen a significant increase in trading volume, surpassing OpenSea by 120% in cumulative trading volume from January to April 2023​<a href="https://coinmarketcap.com/alexandria/article/2023-nft-market-analysis:-an-insider-look" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"></a>​. This suggests that while Bitcoin Ordinals are growing, other NFT marketplaces are also experiencing significant activity and growth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, Galaxy Research <a href="https://nftnow.com/news/research-and-mining-report-highlights-bitcoins-enduring-impact/">estimates</a> that the market size of Bitcoin NFTs will reach $4.5 billion by 2025, underscoring the potential for further growth in the Bitcoin Ordinals market​<a href="https://nftnow.com/news/research-and-mining-report-highlights-bitcoins-enduring-impact/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"></a>​​<a href="https://www.coindesk.com/consensus-magazine/2023/12/19/the-bitcoin-fee-flippening-is-upon-us/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"></a>​. This forecast indicates an increasing interest and potential in the Bitcoin NFT space but doesn't necessarily confirm the current market size compared to other NFT markets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the Bitcoin Ordinals market is growing rapidly and is a significant player in the NFT space, its current size relative to other NFT markets is not definitively four times larger. The NFT market is dynamic, with multiple platforms experiencing growth and shifts in trading volumes.</p>
