<p style="text-transform:none">The remarks by Ken Griffin have sparked debate and concern among retail investors and market observers, as it implies that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/27/morgan-stanleys-bullish-bitcoin-prediction/">large financial institutions </a>like Citadel might have significant influence over market prices. The implications of such a statement are significant, considering the role of market makers and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/09/billionaire-paul-tudor-jones-sees-promise-in-bitcoin-amid-economic-uncertainty/">hedge funds</a> in financial markets and the potential impact on market integrity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">It's important to note that these remarks have also been associated with discussions about market manipulation and regulatory compliance, particularly in the context of Citadel's past with the SEC.</p>
