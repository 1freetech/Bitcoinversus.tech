<p style="text-transform:none"><br><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/24/bitmex-founder-arthur-hayes-predicts-historic-bitcoin-surge-by-2026/">BitMex</a> is undertaking a historic mission to send a physical Bitcoin to the Moon. This mission, slated for launch on January 8, 2024, at 02:18 am ET from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, Florida, marks a significant milestone in both space exploration and the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/27/engineer-max-harvey-analyzes-bitcoins-financial-escape-velocity/">evolution of Bitcoin</a>. Collaborating with Astrobotic, Bitcoin Magazine, and Oxcart Assembly, the mission aims to demonstrate the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/10/greg-foss-predicts-bitcoins-potential-rise-to-2-million/">potential of Bitcoin</a> in creating a global financial system that transcends Earth's boundaries. This initiative is part of the Peregrine-1 mission, representing the first Western soft lunar landing since NASA's Apollo 17 mission in 1972.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The physical <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/28/beef-gets-pricier-with-dollars-cheaper-with-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>, weighing 43 grams and valued at approximately US$45,000 as of January 8, 2024, is among the 201 payloads from various governments, companies, universities, and NASA’s Commercial Lunar Payload Services initiative. This coin, engraved with a public address and a private key, is the first-ever financial asset to be sent to the Moon. The Bitcoin will remain on the lunar surface with Peregrine-1, allowing the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/07/bitcoins-value-strengthens-in-relation-to-the-us-national-debt/">Bitcoin community</a> and future generations to interact with it, whether on Earth or in space. This includes the ability to send personal messages and their own satoshis to the Moon.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">By sending Bitcoin to the Moon, BitMex is fostering a future where cryptocurrency and space exploration go hand in hand, paving the way to explore uncharted territories and fuel the evolution of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/31/bitcoin-on-mars-monetizing-the-red-frontier/">Bitcoin beyond Earth</a>. The tentative arrival date of the Bitcoin on the Moon is February 23, 2024, after completing a 7-week lunar orbit phase.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">BitMex's venture into space is more than just a scientific and technological achievement; it's a bold statement about the potential and future of cryptocurrency in our expanding universe. As we witness this historic event, it's clear that we are standing at the threshold of a new era, where the realms of finance, technology, and space exploration intersect in unprecedented ways.</p>
