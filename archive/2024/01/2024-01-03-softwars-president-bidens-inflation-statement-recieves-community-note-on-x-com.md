<p style="text-transform:none">President Biden's recent tweet on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/25/bitcoins-growth-potential-spotlighted-on-x-com/">x.com</a> claiming  that "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/31/haircuts-and-hyperinflation-ruben-galantes-unique-record-of-argentinas-economic-struggles/">inflation</a> was 0%" has sparked widespread discussion about the significant role of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">software</a> in influencing public perception of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/09/billionaire-paul-tudor-jones-sees-promise-in-bitcoin-amid-economic-uncertainty/">economic data</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The tweet from the @POTUS account <a href="https://twitter.com/POTUS/status/1730949376904688046">claimed</a>, "Inflation was 0% last month – and our economy grew by more than 5% last quarter. Bidenomics."</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">President Biden explained further that The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/20/fednow-instant-payment-network-set-to-almost-revolutionize-financial-transactions/">Federal Reserve</a> aims for a 2% PCE, which currently stands at 3%. Another <a href="https://t.co/QU5jj3B2Qu">measure</a>, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/15/growing-institutional-interest-in-bitcoin-despite-death-cross-and-mixed-cpi-data/">Consumer Price Index</a> (CPI), shows an annual inflation rate of 3.2%. The community note - which is utilized to put inaccurate or untrue posts in context - highlighted that the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index indicates a month-over-month change. Additionally, the note pointed out that inflation is usually assessed via the CPI metric.</p>

They possess the capability to either clarify or complicate public understanding of economic trends, playing a role that is sometimes more influential than traditional government channels. </p>
