<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/24/bitcoin-vs-xrp-technical-analysis-reveals-bitcoin-is-supreme/">Ripple</a> co-founder and executive chairman Chris Larsen has <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/ripple-chris-larsen-endorses-kamala-harris-president">officially</a> endorsed <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/politics-vivek-ramaswamy-predicts-kamala-harris-will-embrace-bitcoin-in-2024/">Vice President Kamala Harris</a> for the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/politics-donald-trump-announces-elon-musk-will-lead-government-efficiency-commission/">2024 U.S. presidential election</a>. Larsen, alongside 87 other prominent business leaders, signed a letter supporting Harris, citing her commitment to creating a stable business environment and advancing fair policies that support economic growth. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Larsen’s <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2024/09/08/ripple-chairman-chris-larsen-endorses-kamala-harris-for-president-in-new-letter-with-american-ceos-report/">endorsement</a> stands out within the crypto community, where political loyalties have varied. His move aligns with his longstanding views on business-friendly policies and technological advancement. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Larsen’s support of Harris further cements the involvement of the cryptocurrency sector in U.S. politics. Meanwhile, Ripple's general counsel, Stuart Alderoty, has shown support for former President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/politics-donald-trump-announces-elon-musk-will-lead-government-efficiency-commission/">Donald Trump</a>, illustrating the diverse political positions within Ripple's leadership.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With this endorsement, Larsen joins notable figures such as Yelp CEO Jeremy Stoppelman, Snap chairman Michael Lynton, and billionaire investor Mark Cuban, all of whom have backed Harris for her pro-business stance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The letter asserts that Harris will continue to foster a business-friendly environment, critical for sectors like cryptocurrency and technology​.</p>
