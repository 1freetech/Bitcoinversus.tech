<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">Vice President Kamala Harris’s </a>2024 presidential campaign <a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/kamala-harris-accepts-crypto-donations/">has started accepting</a> cryptocurrency donations, but not directly. Instead, the Future Forward political action committee (PAC), which supports her candidacy, is accepting these donations via Coinbase Commerce. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move marks a significant step for Harris in embracing the growing influence of digital currencies in political fundraising. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Harris herself has not explicitly taken a stance on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/22/omans-new-350m-bitcoin-mining-hub-sets-the-pace-for-digital-economy/">cryptocurrency</a>, her campaign’s openness to crypto donations highlights an effort to engage with tech-savvy voters.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Notably, former President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Donald Trump</a> has also integrated cryptocurrency into his 2024 campaign fundraising efforts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Trump began <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/25/trumps-exclusive-bitcoin-2024-fundraiser-seats-priced-at-844600/">accepting Bitcoin donations</a> through his website months before Harris. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He has made a concerted effort to attract voters in the cryptocurrency space, even promising to establish a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">strategic Bitcoin reserve</a> if re-elected. As more candidates begin to embrace digital currencies, cryptocurrency’s role in future elections is expected to grow​.</p>
