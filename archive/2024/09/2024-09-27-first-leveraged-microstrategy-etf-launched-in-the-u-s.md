<p style="text-transform:none">On August 15, 2024, Defiance ETFs <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/08/15/2930650/0/en/Defiance-Launches-MSTX-The-First-Leveraged-Single-Stock-ETF-on-MicroStrategy-in-the-US.html">introduced</a> the first-ever leveraged ETF focused on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a>, known as the Defiance Daily Target 1.75X Long MSTR ETF (MSTX). This ETF is designed to offer investors 1.75 times the daily percentage change in MicroStrategy's stock price. Given MicroStrategy's substantial <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/08/bitcoin-strategic-reserve-bill-proposal-by-wyoming-senator-cynthia-lummis/">Bitcoin holdings</a>, this ETF provides a unique opportunity for investors seeking amplified exposure to the cryptocurrency market through MicroStrategy's performance​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">MicroStrategy, led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/20/michael-saylor-shares-insight-on-bitcoins-impact-at-btc23-conference-in-germany/">Executive Chairman Michael Saylor</a>, has become one of the largest holders of Bitcoin, with a current treasury of over 226,500 BTC. The launch of MSTX allows traders to gain leveraged exposure to both MicroStrategy's stock and its indirect connection to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a>. The ETF is designed for sophisticated investors, given its higher risk due to leverage and market volatility.</p>
