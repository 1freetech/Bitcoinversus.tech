<p style="text-transform:none">The company, known for its services in precious metals IRAs, has faced increasing scrutiny due to these complaints. Some clients have pointed out the lack of transparency and delayed delivery times as significant issues. While Oxford Gold Group promotes itself as a leader in the industry, these recent developments have cast doubt on the firm's reliability​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the company's claims of upholding high standards of integrity and transparency, the unresolved complaints have sparked concerns among both existing and potential investors. The ongoing situation serves as a reminder for investors to thoroughly research and verify the credentials of financial firms before engaging in any significant transactions​.</p>
