<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Marathon Digital Holdings</a> has announced that every Bitcoin block it mines will now carry the inscription "Made in USA." This move highlights Marathon's commitment to domestic operations, emphasizing that its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/bitcoin-miner-revenue-bottoming-out-signals-potential-bull-market/">Bitcoin production</a> is entirely based in the United States.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This decision is part of a broader push by the company to reinforce its identity as a fully American operation and aligns with Marathon's other recent initiatives, including a significant $250 million offering to purchase more Bitcoin and expand its operational footprint in the U.S.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company has also emphasized that this step sets them apart from other large-scale miners who may operate internationally, ensuring that Marathon's Bitcoin is 100% American-made, a unique selling point in the industry. This move comes amid increasing interest in the origins of Bitcoin mining, particularly in the context of global regulatory scrutiny and environmental concerns.</p>
