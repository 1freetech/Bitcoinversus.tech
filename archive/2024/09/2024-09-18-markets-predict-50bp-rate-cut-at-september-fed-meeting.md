<p style="text-transform:none">As the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/23/federal-reserve-chair-jerome-powell-signals-shift-in-monetary-policy-at-jackson-hole/">Federal Reserve</a> approaches its September 18th meeting, market sentiment has shifted, with traders now predicting a 50 basis point (bp) rate cut. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The shift in expectations follows economic data suggesting that the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/25/barack-obama-compares-bitcoin-to-portable-swiss-bank-accounts/">central bank</a> may need to ease monetary policy to manage inflation and stimulate growth. Currently, the Fed's target interest rate stands at 525-550 basis points (bps), and there is increasing anticipation that it could be lowered to the 475-500 bps range​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to the CME Group’s FedWatch Tool, there is a 52% probability that the Federal Reserve will lower the target rate by 50 basis points during the meeting. The other 48% of traders expect a smaller cut of 25 basis points, bringing the target range to 500-525 bps​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This potential rate cut is significant for markets, particularly for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">cryptocurrencies</a> like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">Bitcoin</a>. Lower interest rates generally encourage more risk-taking, as investors search for higher yields outside traditional bonds or savings accounts. As a result, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> and other digital assets may see renewed interest as traders look for alternative stores of value.</p>
