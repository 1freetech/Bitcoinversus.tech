<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Former U.S. President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Donald Trump</a> has <a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/trump-vows-to-remove-all-sanctions-on-russia/">vowed</a> to lift all sanctions against Russia, claiming they are harming the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/07/crucial-graphic-highlights-dollar-depreciation-relative-to-real-estate-and-bitcoin/">strength of the U.S. dollar</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During his presidential campaign, Trump has emphasized the importance of maintaining the dollar's status as the global reserve currency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He argued that prolonging sanctions could diminish the dollar’s influence, potentially leading to the U.S. losing its economic power.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He also warned that countries like Russia, China, and Iran are <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/03/04/intel-brics-nations-accelerate-plans-for-common-currency/">seeking alternatives to the U.S. dollar</a>, which could result in a significant shift in global economic power. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This stance has raised concerns about the future of U.S. foreign policy and its impact on global trade.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In contrast, Trump’s stance on sanctions has sparked international debate about the long-term effects on U.S. influence in the world. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While many support the economic leverage that sanctions provide, Trump views them as detrimental to the dollar's value, potentially accelerating its decline on the global stage.</p>
