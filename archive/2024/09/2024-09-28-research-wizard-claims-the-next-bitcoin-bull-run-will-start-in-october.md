<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent statement, a self-proclaimed "research wizard," known as Ardizor, predicted that Bitcoin's next bull market will begin in October 2024, just in time for what many in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/21/hackers-use-mcdonalds-instagram-for-grimace-crypto-scam/">crypto community</a> refer to as "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/01/uptober-momentum-bitcoin-leaps-3-in-15-minute-interval-igniting-market-optimism-and-liquidating-over-70-million-in-shorts/">Uptober</a>." </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ardizor's claim is based on the historical timing of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/14/this-1000-day-bitcoin-trading-strategy-changes-everything/">Bitcoin's price cycles</a>, particularly in relation to halving events. He confidently <a href="https://twitter.com/ardizor/status/1837243243307708578">stated</a>, "The next bull run starts in 2 weeks. $BTC cycles begin 170 days post-halving and hit their peak 310 days later".</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Historically, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/30/binance-ceo-forecasts-bright-future-for-bitcoin-post-halving/">halving events</a> have been pivotal in shaping Bitcoin's market cycles. Bitcoin halving reduces the rewards miners receive by half, decreasing the rate at which new <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> enters circulation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This mechanism creates scarcity, which historically leads to upward price movements in the months following the halving. Ardizor’s prediction aligns with these cycles, positioning Bitcoin for a substantial rise in the upcoming months.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Many market participants view October as a turning point, echoing the sentiment of "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/01/uptober-momentum-bitcoin-leaps-3-in-15-minute-interval-igniting-market-optimism-and-liquidating-over-70-million-in-shorts/">Uptober</a>," a time when digital assets typically perform well. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With Bitcoin’s increasing mainstream acceptance, this upcoming surge could attract a wave of new participants and renewed interest in the crypto market.</p>
