<p style="text-transform:none">As we head into Week 4 of the college football season, the quarterback rankings for the 2025 NFL Draft are beginning to take shape. Leading <a href="https://www.sportingnews.com/us/nfl/news/nfl-draft-2025-quarterbacks-rankings-shedeur-sanders-quinn-ewers/0f86fd930ea1ff706a26570b">the pack </a>is Shedeur Sanders, son of Deion Sanders, who has demonstrated his talent with standout performances at Colorado. Sanders has showcased exceptional pocket presence and decision-making, which make him a top contender for the first overall pick in the upcoming draft​.</p>
