<p style="text-transform:none">A recent <a href="https://x.com/BitcoinMagazine/status/1819670545870274878">graphic</a> has brought to light the significant <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/07/bitcoins-value-strengthens-in-relation-to-the-us-national-debt/">depreciation of the U.S. dollar</a> when compared to both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/26/bitcoin-vs-property-the-return-of-the-final-boss/">real estate</a> values and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/31/unpopular-opinion-michael-saylor-is-the-best-candidate-for-secretary-of-the-treasury/">Bitcoin</a> over the past eight years. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The image illustrates how the price of a median home in the United States has increased from $288,400 in 2016 to $434,700 in 2024. However, the amount of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/08/bitcoin-strategic-reserve-bill-proposal-by-wyoming-senator-cynthia-lummis/">Bitcoin</a> required to purchase the same home has dramatically decreased from 664 BTC to just 6.6 BTC during this period.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The depreciation of the dollar can have various effects on real estate. While it may lead to higher property values due to increased costs of construction and materials, it also raises concerns about affordability and higher interest rates. Conversely, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/bitcoin-appreciates-over-time-relative-to-the-iphone/">Bitcoin's appreciation</a> has outpaced these effects, making it a compelling alternative for those looking to hedge against inflation and currency depreciation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This trend is likely to continue as more investors consider <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/25/fidelity-digital-assets-officially-acknowledges-bitcoins-potential/">Bitcoin a viable store of value</a>, especially as it becomes increasingly recognized in mainstream finance. The comparison between real estate and Bitcoin illustrates the changing dynamics of wealth preservation in the modern economy.</p>
