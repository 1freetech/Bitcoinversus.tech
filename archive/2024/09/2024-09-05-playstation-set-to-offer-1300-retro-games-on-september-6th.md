<p style="text-transform:none">PlayStation enthusiasts have <a href="https://opencritic.com/news/4965/playstation-getting-more-than-1-300-retro-games-on-september-6-exclusive-">something special</a> to look forward to on September 6th, as the console giant will introduce a massive catalog of over 1,300 retro games through a partnership with Antstream Arcade. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The service will allow PlayStation users to access a vast array of classic games, ranging from the '80s to the early 2000s, on both PS4 and PS5 consoles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The games <a href="https://www.tweaktown.com/news/99922/playstation-getting-new-cloud-streaming-service-with-1300-classic-games/index.html">will be available</a> via a cloud streaming platform, providing a nostalgic experience for those who remember the early days of gaming. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Users can choose between a subscription model, priced at $39.99 annually, or opt for lifetime access with a one-time fee of $99.99.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Antstream Arcade, already <a href="https://www.neogaf.com/threads/gamerant-playstation-will-be-getting-more-than-1-300-retro-games-on-september-6-through-antstream-arcade.1674516/">established</a> on other platforms like Xbox and PC, is known for its extensive collection of officially licensed retro titles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The service includes games from legendary systems such as the Atari 2600, Commodore 64, and SNES, alongside early PlayStation titles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move marks the first time a third-party cloud gaming service has been integrated into Sony's ecosystem, highlighting PlayStation's commitment to expanding its digital offerings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With the inclusion of community-driven features like tournaments and challenges, Antstream Arcade promises to not only revive these classic titles but also offer new ways for players to engage with them. This could be an exciting opportunity for both seasoned gamers and newcomers to explore the roots of video gaming history.</p>
