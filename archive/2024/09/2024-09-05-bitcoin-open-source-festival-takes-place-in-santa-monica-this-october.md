<p style="text-transform:none">The City of Santa Monica, California, will be the center of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/tag/coinbase-registration-spain-bank-of-spain-cryptocurrency-bitcoin-momentum-authorized-crypto-exchange-cryptocurrency-expansion-strategy-international-cryptocurrency-acceptance-cryptocurrency-services-s/">cryptocurrency world</a> this October as it hosts a Bitcoin open-source festival. This event aims to bring together developers, enthusiasts, and industry leaders to discuss and showcase advancements in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/28/cantor-fitzgerald-launches-2-billion-bitcoin-financing-company/">Bitcoin</a> technology and open-source contributions.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Scheduled for October 12-14, 2024, the festival will feature a series of workshops, presentations, and networking opportunities. Attendees can expect to engage in discussions about the latest developments in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/04/core-scientific-receives-major-investment-from-bitmain/">Bitcoin infrastructure</a>, security protocols, and community-driven projects. The event will serve as a platform for sharing knowledge and fostering collaboration among participants from various sectors of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/12/analyzing-centralization-and-monopolies-in-the-bitcoin-industry/">cryptocurrency industry</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The festival is organized by <a href="https://www.santamonicatech.com">Santa Monica Tech</a> and will be held at the Santa Monica Civic Auditorium, a venue known for hosting significant tech events. According to the event organizers, “This festival represents a unique opportunity to connect with the vibrant <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/29/bitcoiner-explains-negativity-within-traditional-finance-towards-bitcoin/">Bitcoin community</a> and contribute to the evolution of open-source technologies.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For more details about the event schedule and registration, visit the official <a href="https://www.santamonikabitcoinfestival.com">Santa Monica Bitcoin Festival website</a>.</p>
