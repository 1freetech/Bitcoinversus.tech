<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Fundstrat’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Tom Lee</a> predicts a significant rise in Bitcoin’s value, potentially reaching $150,000 by the end of 2024, driven by increasing demand and market dynamics.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tom Lee, the managing partner and head of research at Fundstrat Global Advisors, has <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-price-150k-in-2024-base-case-tom-lee">reaffirmed</a> his bullish outlook for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/microstrategy-plans-2-billion-equity-offering-to-purchase-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>, predicting that the cryptocurrency could reach $150,000 by the end of 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Lee, the approval of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/06/prediction-a-bitcoin-etf-will-outperform-the-success-of-gold-etfs/">Bitcoin spot ETFs</a> by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) could play a pivotal role in this surge.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He also highlighted the possibility of easing monetary policy by the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/20/fednow-instant-payment-network-set-to-almost-revolutionize-financial-transactions/">Federal Reserve</a>, which could benefit risk assets like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/vaneck-ceo-claims-bitcoins-value-could-reach-350000/">Bitcoin</a>, contributing to its price increase​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite some caution from other market analysts, who warn of potential volatility, Lee remains confident that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">Bitcoin</a> will achieve new all-time highs within the next year​.</p>
