<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">The BitAxe series</a> is at the forefront of the open-source <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/18/bitcoin-ordinals-market-surges-outpacing-top-two-nft-markets-combined/">Bitcoin mining revolution</a>, offering a range of ASIC miners that are both powerful and accessible to a broad spectrum of miners, from beginners to seasoned enthusiasts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The BitAxe Ultra, one of the standout models in this series, utilizes the BM1366 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">ASIC chip</a> from Antminer's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/15/how-to-replace-an-s19-kpro-120th-psu/">S19</a> XP, delivering exceptional hash rates that make it a prime choice for serious miners seeking efficiency and high performance.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">open-source nature</a> of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">BitAxe</a> series allows for unprecedented customization and enhancement by users, fostering a community-driven approach to development and innovation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A key feature of the BitAxe Ultra is its standalone capability, which allows it to mine directly to a pool over <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/03/02/a-beginners-guide-to-network-topologies/">WiFi</a> without the need for an external computer.</p>
