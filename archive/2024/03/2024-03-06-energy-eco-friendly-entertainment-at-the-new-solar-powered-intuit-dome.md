<p style="text-transform:none">The Intuit Dome, the future home of the Los Angeles Clippers, is setting a new standard for energy sustainability in sports arenas. The total construction cost of the Intuit Dome is $2 billion. The solar panels are a critical component of the arena's energy system, which also includes 13 Megawatt hours of “on-site battery storage.”</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">This combination allows the Intuit Dome to operate entirely carbon-free from day one of its public opening. The capability of the solar panels and battery storage system is impressive, as it enables the venue to run an NBA game or concert in full operation directly from the batteries​<a href="https://www.enr.com/articles/57216-iconic-intuit-dome-realizes-a-sustainable-vision" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"></a>​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The sustainability features of the Intuit Dome extend beyond its solar panels and battery storage. The arena is also the world's first sports arena to be built with 100% embodied carbon concrete, significantly reducing its environmental impact. Moreover, the venue is designed to have zero net greenhouse gas emissions, operating as a fully electric venue with batteries and solar power. It will have sufficient on-site energy storage capacity to meet the electricity needs of a basketball game or a concert, showcasing its commitment to environmental sustainability​<a href="https://www.worldconstructionnetwork.com/projects/intuit-dome-inglewood-california-us/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"></a>​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While specific details about the cost savings directly attributable to the solar panels and energy storage system are not provided, the overall design and construction approach of the Intuit Dome emphasize efficiency, sustainability, and long-term environmental stewardship. These features not only contribute to reducing the operational carbon footprint of the venue but also set a precedent for future sports arenas and large-scale construction projects aiming for sustainability and carbon neutrality.</p>
