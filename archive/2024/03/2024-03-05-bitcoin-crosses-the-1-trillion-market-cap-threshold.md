<p style="text-transform:none">On February 14, 2024, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/20/prediction-bitcoin-network-will-reach-1-zettahash-by-q4-2024/">Bitcoin</a> achieved a significant milestone as its total market value <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/total-amount-invested-bitcoin-back-over-1-trillion-2024-02-14/">exceeded</a> $1 trillion, a peak not seen since November 2021. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">This surge in Bitcoin's market cap was largely fueled by the continuous inflow into U.S. spot <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/02/bitcoin-etfs-attract-over-720-million-on-first-day-of-trading/">Bitcoin exchange-traded funds</a> (ETFs), which have played a pivotal role in bolstering the prices of cryptocurrencies. On that Wednesday, Bitcoin's price soared to a 25-month high of $52,079, showcasing the growing investor confidence and the cryptocurrency's robust performance in the financial market​<a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/total-amount-invested-bitcoin-back-over-1-trillion-2024-02-14/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"></a>​.</p>
