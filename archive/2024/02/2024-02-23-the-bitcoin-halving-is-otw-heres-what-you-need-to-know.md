<p style="text-transform:none">This Halving occurs approximately every four years and reduces the rate at which new bitcoin are generated, thereby influencing the supply and potentially its price.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">To understand the numerical methodology of the Bitcoin halving, consider that the network aims to produce one block every 10 minutes. This translates to approximately 6 blocks per hour, 144 blocks per day (That’s currently 900 Bitcoin per day), and thus around 52,560 blocks per year under ideal conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The halving is significant as it influences Bitcoin's supply, potentially affecting its price and the miners' profitability, thereby playing a crucial role in Bitcoin's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">economic model</a> and its long-term sustainability.</p>
