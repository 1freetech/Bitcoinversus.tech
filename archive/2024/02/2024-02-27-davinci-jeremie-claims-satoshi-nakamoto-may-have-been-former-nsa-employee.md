<p style="text-transform:none"><br>The claim made by early <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/06/analyzing-the-bitcoin-god-candle-theory-by-aleksander-svetski/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin</a> adopter Davinci Jeremie introduces an intriguing angle to the already mysterious origins of Bitcoin, suggesting a possible connection between its creator, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Satoshi</a> Nakamoto, and the National Security Agency (NSA).
