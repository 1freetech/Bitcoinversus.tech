<p><em><strong>Bitwise, Fidelity, and BlackRock Lead Historic Inflows into Bitcoin ETFs</strong></em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">BITB by Bitwise led all new Spot <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/10/a-brief-analysis-on-luxors-hashprice-predictions-for-bitcoins-future/">Bitcoin</a> ETFs with the highest net inflow on its first day of trading, amounting to $237.9 million. This remarkable performance can be attributed to its competitive pricing structure, offering the lowest fee among spot <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/12/latest-court-rulings-reshape-gary-genslers-perspective-on-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin ETFs</a> at 0.20%, which is waived for the first six months on the first $1 billion in assets​​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Following closely was FBTC by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/07/fidelity-expert-jurrien-timmer-endorses-bitcoin-calls-asset-exponential-gold/">Fidelity</a>, attracting $226.9 million, and IBIT by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/02/former-blackrock-director-weighs-in-on-possible-bitcoin-spot-etf/">BlackRock</a>, which saw an influx of $111.6 million. A grand total of $720.85 million was invested in spot Bitcoin ETFs on their first day. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A <a href="https://coinmarketcap.com/headlines/news/crypto-funds-151-million-inflows-spot-bitcoin-etf/">report</a> by CoinMarketCap highlighted that crypto funds at asset managers like CoinShares, Bitwise, Grayscale, ProShares, and 21Shares saw inflows totaling $151 million in the first week of 2024. This surge was predominantly driven by bitcoin-based funds, which contributed $113 million to the total inflow.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The launch of these ETFs marks a significant milestone in the integration of cryptocurrencies into mainstream financial markets, offering investors a regulated and potentially safer avenue to invest in digital currencies. However, as with any investment, potential investors should be aware of the various risks involved, including regulatory and technological risks associated with blockchain technology, as well as the nondiversification risk due to the concentration in a relatively small number of securities​​.</p>
