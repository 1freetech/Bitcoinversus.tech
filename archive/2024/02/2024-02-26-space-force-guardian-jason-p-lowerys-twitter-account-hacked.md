<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><br>Space Force Guardian <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/21/power-is-money-jason-p-lowery-and-the-banning-of-the-most-important-book-about-bitcoin/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Jason P. Lowery</a> has faced a challenging situation since last July, as he's been <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jasonplowery_dear-linkedin-community-since-last-july-activity-7161339431742803968-TJVK/?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_ios" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">locked out</a> of his Twitter account due to a phone swap hack. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jasonplowery_dear-linkedin-community-since-last-july-activity-7161339431742803968-TJVK?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_ios" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">letter</a> shared by Space Force Guardian Jason P. Lowery, he details the challenges he faces after being locked out of his <a href="http://x.com" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">X.com</a> account due to hacking. Lowery highlights the frustration of being charged for a verification service he cannot access, as the hacker altered both the phone number and password associated with his account. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This situation underscores the vulnerabilities within social media verification processes and the difficulties in regaining control of compromised accounts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite efforts to regain access, he remains unable to use his verified account, which continues to incur charges for a subscription that he cannot utilize. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The situation raises concerns about the security of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/15/max-keiser-starts-beef-with-sam-altman-calls-worldcoin-project-this-years-sbf/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">private-owned software</a> networks and the effectiveness of Twitter's verification feature, particularly for users with significant followings who find themselves vulnerable to such security breaches. </p>
