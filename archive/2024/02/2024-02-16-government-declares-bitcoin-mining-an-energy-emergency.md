<p style="text-transform:none">The electricity consumption of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining</a> has been classified as a federal emergency. This decision underlines the government's concern over the environmental impact and energy use associated with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin mining operations</a>. To address these concerns, the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) is set to launch a detailed survey targeting select <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Bitcoin miners</a>. This initiative, authorized by the Office of Management and Budget on January 26, aims to collect comprehensive data on the energy consumption patterns of these operations.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The urgency of this survey stems from the extensive electricity requirements of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin mining activities</a>, which have drawn criticism for their potential impact on power grids and carbon emissions. By gathering data on how power demand for Bitcoin mining is evolving, the EIA seeks to identify the regions where mining growth is concentrated and the types of electricity sources utilized by these operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">This step represents the government's latest effort to better understand and potentially regulate the Bitcoin mining industry's energy use. It follows heightened legislative and environmental concerns regarding the sector's reliance on fossil fuels and its implications for climate change. Notably, the U.S. has emerged as a primary destination for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">Bitcoin miners</a> after China's crackdown on the practice in 2021, highlighting the need for detailed insights into the industry's energy consumption and environmental impact.</p>
