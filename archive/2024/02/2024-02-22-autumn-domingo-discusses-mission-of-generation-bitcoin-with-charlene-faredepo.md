<p style="text-transform:none">In an interview on Charlene Fadirepo YouTube channel, Autumn Domingo shares her insights and experiences as a young trailblazer in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin community</a>. A student at the University of New Hampshire&nbsp;and already a UX designer, Domingo's foray into the world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/31/to-the-moon-literally-bitmex-plans-on-sending-1-bitcoin-to-the-moon/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bitcoin</a> is not just a personal journey but a mission to educate her peers. As one of the co-founders of Generation Bitcoin, she, along with Ella Hough, Arsh Molu, and Ishaana Misra, is driving an education community that hosts weekly virtual Bitcoin workshops and curates internships for students. This initiative is crucial in building the next generation of Bitcoin professionals.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The interview with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/29/ceo-charlene-fadirepo-shares-insight-on-africas-bitcoin-revolution/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Charlene</a> Fadirepo delves into Domingo's contributions and the collective efforts of the Generation Bitcoin leadership team. Domingo and her team are not just participants but are shaping the narrative and functionality of Bitcoin for the 21st century.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The significance of such platforms and discussions cannot be understated. They offer a window into the potential of Bitcoin to revolutionize not just finance but also the way we think about value, ownership, and the decentralization of power. Autumn Domingo's story serves as an inspiration for young people everywhere to become informed, involved, and innovative in the evolving world of Bitcoin.</p>
