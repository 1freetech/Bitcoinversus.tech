<p style="text-transform:none">On July 3, 2002, MicroStrategy's stock (NASDAQ: MSTR) reached an all-time low of $0.42. As of November 22, 2024, <a href="https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/stock/mstr?utm_source=chatgpt.com">the stock closed</a> at $421.88, marking a significant recovery over the past two decades.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Michael Saylor</a>, Executive Chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/microstrategy-purchases-an-additional-27200-btc/">MicroStrategy</a>, highlighted this remarkable turnaround in a recent tweet, <a href="https://x.com/saylor/status/1860307458645778583">stating</a>, </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"On July 3, 2002, $MSTR stock fell to an all-time low of $0.42. Yesterday, it closed at $421.88. Never. Never. Never give up."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor's leadership has been instrumental in the company's strategic decisions, particularly its substantial investments in Bitcoin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Under Saylor's guidance, MicroStrategy has become <a href="https://www.fool.com/investing/2024/09/18/according-to-microstrategys-michael-saylor-now-is/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">the largest</a> corporate holder of Bitcoin, with holdings exceeding $14.6 billion as of September 2024. The company's aggressive Bitcoin acquisition strategy has been a key factor in its stock price appreciation.</p>
