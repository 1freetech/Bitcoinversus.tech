<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A recent opinion from Judge Sun Jie of the Shanghai Songjiang People's Court has <a href="https://www.scmp.com/tech/blockchain/article/3287415/shanghai-court-says-crypto-ownership-legal-under-china-law-amid-bitcoin-price-surge?utm_source=chatgpt.com">clarified</a> that, under Chinese law, individuals are permitted to own <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/elon-musk-endorses-howard-lutnick-for-u-s-treasury-secretary/">cryptocurrencies</a> like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/24/ixtechcrypto-announces-bitaxe-whitepaper-release-and-giveaway/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Judge Sun stated that cryptocurrencies are considered virtual commodities with property attributes, making personal ownership legal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, this recognition does not extend to commercial activities involving cryptocurrencies. Chinese regulations continue to prohibit businesses from engaging in cryptocurrency investments, token issuance, and related transactions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These measures aim to prevent potential disruptions to economic and financial order and to curb illegal activities such as money laundering and fraud.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This legal perspective emerged from a case involving a dispute over an initial coin offering (ICO), which is considered illicit financing in China. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The court's stance underscores the distinction between lawful personal ownership and prohibited commercial use of cryptocurrencies within the country.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
