<p style="text-transform:none">Uber Technologies has <a href="https://www.engadget.com/big-tech/uber-is-reportedly-exploring-an-expedia-takeover-120038754.html">reportedly</a> explored acquiring Expedia, a leading travel booking platform valued at around $20 billion. The discussions, as revealed by the <em>Financial Times</em>, are in preliminary stages with no formal bid yet. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Uber's interest aligns with its long-term strategy to transform into a comprehensive travel and logistics "super app." </p>
