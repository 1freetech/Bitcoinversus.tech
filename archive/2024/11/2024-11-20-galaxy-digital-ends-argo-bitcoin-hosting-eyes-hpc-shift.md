<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/bitcoin-set-for-a-major-surge-with-etf-options-says-galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz/">Galaxy Digital</a>, the financial services and investment firm led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/bitcoin-set-for-a-major-surge-with-etf-options-says-galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz/">Mike Novogratz</a>, is reportedly planning to end its hosting services for Argo Blockchain’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/natural-gas-finds-new-value-in-bitcoin-mining-operations/">Bitcoin mining operations</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move marks a strategic pivot for Galaxy as it considers repurposing its Helios mining facility into a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/09/mawson-infrastructure-group-expands-to-ohio-boosting-ai-and-hpc-capacities/">high-performance computing</a> (HPC) center, a decision that reflects the company’s interest in exploring broader applications in the tech sector, particularly in AI and other compute-heavy sectors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p id="block-875b9d92-f534-4898-8371-ccf5ac3ceaf3" style="text-transform:none">Galaxy Digital, the financial services and investment firm led by Mike Novogratz, is reportedly planning to end its hosting services for Argo Blockchain’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a> operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p id="block-875b9d92-f534-4898-8371-ccf5ac3ceaf3" style="text-transform:none">This move marks a strategic pivot for Galaxy as it considers repurposing its Helios mining facility into a high-performance computing (HPC) center, a decision that reflects the company’s interest in exploring broader applications in the tech sector, particularly in AI and other compute-heavy sectors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p id="block-3ff58fe2-9c67-47c1-91b4-e6989b71c4e8" style="text-transform:none">Galaxy initially acquired the Helios facility from Argo Blockchain in December 2022 for $65 million and also provided a $35 million loan to assist with Argo’s liquidity needs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p id="block-3ff58fe2-9c67-47c1-91b4-e6989b71c4e8" style="text-transform:none">This arrangement allowed Galaxy to host Argo’s Bitcoin miners at Helios while maintaining operational control over the facility. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p id="block-3ff58fe2-9c67-47c1-91b4-e6989b71c4e8" style="text-transform:none">However, with the evolving landscape and increasing interest in HPC to support artificial intelligence, scientific research, and other data-intensive applications, Galaxy appears to be re-evaluating its focus. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p id="block-3ff58fe2-9c67-47c1-91b4-e6989b71c4e8" style="text-transform:none">The pivot aligns with a broader trend among Bitcoin miners, where firms are exploring alternative revenue streams through computational infrastructure suited for emerging technologies beyond cryptocurrency.</p>
