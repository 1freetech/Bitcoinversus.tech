<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Norway’s central bank, Norges Bank, and the Swiss National Bank (SNB) have both <a href="https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/2024-08-15-norway-and-switzerland-central-banks-reveal-significant-microstrategy-holdings-12197601991409">strategically</a> invested in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/microstrategy-purchases-an-additional-27200-btc/">MicroStrategy</a> (MSTR), a company renowned for its substantial <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With an investment of 1,578,900 shares, Norway’s Norges Bank demonstrates its approach to gain indirect exposure to the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">cryptocurrency sector</a> by leveraging MicroStrategy’s aggressive Bitcoin acquisition strategy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Through this shareholding, Norges Bank positions itself to benefit from the potential growth in digital assets while maintaining a traditional investment approach. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a similar strategic move, Switzerland’s SNB owns 468,200 shares of MicroStrategy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By investing in a corporate entity heavily invested in Bitcoin, the SNB aligns with an emerging trend among financial institutions seeking cryptocurrency exposure without direct Bitcoin purchases. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As MicroStrategy continues expanding its cryptocurrency holdings, the Swiss bank’s investment reflects a cautious but calculated step into digital asset territories. </p>
