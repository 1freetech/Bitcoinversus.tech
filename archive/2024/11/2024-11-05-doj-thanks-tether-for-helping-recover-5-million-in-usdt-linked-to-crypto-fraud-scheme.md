<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) has successfully seized $5 million worth of Tether (USDT) linked to a crypto investment fraud scheme, with significant assistance from Tether. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2024/08/25/doj-thanks-tether-for-assistance-in-seizure-of-5000000-usdt-in-fraud-scheme/">seizure</a> is part of a broader effort to combat international crypto scams, commonly referred to as "pig butchering." </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/VersusInstitute/status/1828184859564814579","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These schemes typically involve criminals gaining victims' trust through fake romantic relationships and then persuading them to invest in fraudulent crypto platforms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">FBI agents, with help from Tether, tracked the stolen funds across multiple wallets. Tether froze the wallets involved, allowing the DOJ to reclaim the assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This operation is one of several recent efforts by the DOJ to tackle crypto-related fraud and return funds to victims. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This case highlights the growing cooperation between crypto companies and law enforcement agencies to address cybercrime​.</p>
