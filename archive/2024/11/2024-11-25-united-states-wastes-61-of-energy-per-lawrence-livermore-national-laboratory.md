<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Livermore_National_Laboratory">Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory</a> (LLNL), the United States wasted approximately 61% of its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/category/energy/">energy</a> in 2023.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9938,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/energy-2023-united-states.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-9938" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This inefficiency is primarily due to losses in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/hydro-cooling-technology-in-bitcoin-mining-a-green-solution-for-mexicos-energy-challenges/">electricity generation</a> and transportation sectors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The LLNL's energy flow charts <a href="https://www.energyvanguard.com/blog/the-meaning-of-rejected-energy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reveal</a> that a significant portion of energy is lost as waste heat during <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/energy-wasted-styrofoam-generates-power-through-static-electricity/">electricity production</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Traditional power plants, which rely on fossil fuels, operate at efficiencies between 33% and 60%, leading to substantial energy losses.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OBuAzxp3EE0","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the transportation sector, internal combustion engines in vehicles <a href="https://www.greenbuildingadvisor.com/article/the-meaning-of-rejected-energy?utm_source=chatgpt.com">convert</a> only about 25% of the fuel's energy into movement, with the remainder lost as heat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Efforts to reduce energy waste <a href="https://www.llnl.gov/article/50451/national-economy-continues-reenergize-post-pandemic-while-pushing-toward-new-decarbonized-normal?utm_source=chatgpt.com">include</a> transitioning to renewable energy sources and improving energy efficiency in various sectors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Renewable energy technologies, such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/record-high-energy-curtailments-suggests-california-needs-bitcoin-mining/">wind</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/energy-sahara-desert-solar-farms-could-meet-global-electricity-needs/">solar power</a>, have higher efficiencies and lower waste compared to fossil fuel-based systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The LLNL's annual energy flow charts serve as a valuable tool for understanding energy consumption patterns and identifying areas for improvement. </p>
