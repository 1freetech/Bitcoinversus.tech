<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Michael Saylor</a>, Executive Chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/record-high-for-microstrategy-stock-fueled-by-bitcoin-treasury/">MicroStrategy</a>, recently <a href="https://www.theblock.co/post/326791/michael-saylor-bitcoin-manifest-destiny-us-100k-nye-party">discussed</a> the ongoing cryptocurrency rally and the potential for more pro-Bitcoin policies in the United States. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In an interview with CNBC's "Closing Bell: Overtime," Saylor expressed optimism about the future of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/new-york-supreme-court-supports-greenidge-generations-operations/">Bitcoin</a>, citing recent political developments and MicroStrategy's strategic investments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M1e7xNdCgM4","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor highlighted the impact of the recent U.S. elections, noting that the Republican victory could lead to a more favorable regulatory environment for cryptocurrencies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He emphasized that the "red wave" is likely to result in pro-Bitcoin policies and a supportive digital assets framework.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Regarding the leadership of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Saylor refrained from speculating on potential successors to current Chair Gary Gensler. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, he expressed confidence that the incoming administration would appoint a pro-business and pro-Bitcoin individual to lead the SEC, which could further bolster the cryptocurrency market.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MicroStrategy has continued its aggressive Bitcoin acquisition strategy, recently purchasing an additional 27,200 bitcoins for approximately $2.03 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor also addressed the potential for Bitcoin to reach new price milestones, stating that he is planning a celebration for when <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/12/van-eck-ceo-anticipates-bitcoin-reaching-100000-amid-institutional-adoption/">Bitcoin surpasses $100,000</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He expressed confidence that this milestone could be achieved by the end of the year, reflecting his bullish outlook on Bitcoin's future performance.</p>
