<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/27/first-leveraged-microstrategy-etf-launched-in-the-u-s/">MicroStrategy</a> has revealed an ambitious capital gameplan, <a href="https://www.microstrategy.com/press/microstrategy-announces-third-quarter-2024-financial-results-and-announces-42-billion-capital-plan_10-30-2024">aiming</a> to raise $42 billion, split between an at-the-market (ATM) equity offering and fixed-income securities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company plans to secure $21 billion through each channel, allowing it to expand its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a> while supporting its business intelligence operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.microstrategy.com/press/microstrategy-announces-third-quarter-2024-financial-results-and-announces-42-billion-capital-plan_10-30-2024">announcement</a> underscores MicroStrategy’s enduring commitment to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/the-evolution-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a> as a treasury asset, aligning with its goal of using excess cash to accumulate more of the digital currency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The company's quarterly report, scheduled for 5 p.m. ET, will further outline its financials and provide insights into its forward strategy. With Bitcoin prices continuing to fluctuate, CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/microstrategys-michael-saylor-declares-bitcoin-as-the-universal-merger-partner/">Michael Saylor</a> remains a vocal proponent of Bitcoin as a dependable store of value. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This dual approach of equity offerings and debt issuance not only supports MicroStrategy's balance sheet but strengthens its foothold in the volatile <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/28/cantor-fitzgerald-launches-2-billion-bitcoin-financing-company/">cryptocurrency market</a>, where it now holds approximately 190,000 BTC valued at billions in total.</p>
