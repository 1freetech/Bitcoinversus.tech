<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cosmos Health Inc., a <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/cosmos-health-integrates-bitcoin-ethereum-130000895.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">diversified</a> global healthcare company, has announced the integration of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/nerdqaxe-achieves-4-8th-s-with-6-layer-pcb-upgrade/">Bitcoin</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Ethereum</a> into its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">treasury reserve assets</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to holding these digital assets, Cosmos Health is working to accommodate customers who wish to make payments in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">cryptocurrencies</a>. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Greg Siokas <a href="https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/cosmos-health-integrates-bitcoin-and-ethereum-treasury-reserve-assets-2024-11-18?utm_source=chatgpt.com">stated</a> that the company views select crypto assets as potential hedges against inflation and currency devaluation, as well as diversification tools offering substantial upside potential.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This decision aligns with Cosmos Health's forward-thinking approach to investing in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/21/revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-how-ai-and-quantum-computing-meet/">new technologies</a>, including the recent acquisition of Cloudscreen, an AI-driven drug repurposing platform. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company <a href="https://www.accesswire.com/943902/cosmos-health-integrates-bitcoin-and-ethereum-as-treasury-reserve-assets?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expects</a> that adding Bitcoin and Ethereum, along with potentially other cryptocurrencies and blockchain-related assets, will help diversify its balance sheet and offer significant upside potential as these assets increasingly gain global adoption.</p>
