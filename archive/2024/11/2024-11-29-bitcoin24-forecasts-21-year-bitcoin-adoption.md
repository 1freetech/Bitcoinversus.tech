<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://github.com/bitcoin-model/bitcoin_model/blob/main/README.md">Bitcoin24</a> is an innovative open-source macro model designed to forecast asset growth and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">Bitcoin adoption</a> over the next 21 years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This tool is equipped with micro models to assess various Bitcoin strategies tailored for individuals, corporations, institutions, and nation-states. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The model allows users to input their assumptions or adjust existing parameters to explore different scenarios. It provides a simplified yet robust simulation to project potential long-term outcomes of adopting a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">Bitcoin standard</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://github.com/bitcoin-model/bitcoin_model/blob/main/README.md">Bitcoin24</a> operates by enabling users to experiment with different strategies, comparing results automatically via scenario comparison charts within the micro models' section. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Although <a href="https://github.com/bitcoin-model/bitcoin_model/blob/main/README.md">Bitcoin24</a> does not explicitly account for Bitcoin's notorious volatility, it offers a clear perspective on potential growth trends under different economic conditions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The model's flexibility makes it an excellent resource for anyone interested in understanding how different <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/25/bitcoins-growth-potential-spotlighted-on-x-com/">Bitcoin adoption</a> strategies could play out in the future.</p>
