<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Austin's rental market is <a href="https://www.newsweek.com/austin-rental-market-collapsing-real-estate-expert-says-1986647">experiencing challenges</a>, with vacancy rates climbing and rental prices decreasing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of September 2023, the city's rental vacancy rate <a href="https://x.com/nickgerli1/status/1857185059779825846">reached</a> 11%, surpassing the national average of 6.6% and indicating a substantial increase from previous years.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9109,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/apartment-vacancies-austin.png?w=673" alt="" class="wp-image-9109" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Rental prices have also declined. Austin's median apartment rent has fallen significantly, with a 15% decline from its peak of $1,635 in August 2022. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of October 2024, the median rent has dropped to $1,399, approaching pre-pandemic levels. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9110,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/apartment-demand-drop-austin-texas.jpg?w=680" alt="" class="wp-image-9110" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The surge in vacancies and drop in rents have led landlords to offer concessions to attract tenants. </p>
