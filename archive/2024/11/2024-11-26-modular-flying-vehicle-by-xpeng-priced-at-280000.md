<p style="text-transform:none">Chinese electric vehicle manufacturer <a href="https://www.xpeng.com/brand">Xpeng</a> has <a href="https://www.yahoo.com/news/photos-xpengs-land-aircraft-carrier-163415643.html">unveiled</a> its innovative modular flying vehicle, the Land Aircraft Carrier, priced at approximately $280,000.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company has already secured 2,000 orders for this groundbreaking vehicle, which features a detachable aircraft capable of accommodating two passengers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The aircraft <a href="https://www.yahoo.com/news/photos-xpengs-land-aircraft-carrier-163415643.html">can be reattached</a> to the ground module within five minutes, enhancing its practicality for users.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Designed for short-distance flights, the Land Aircraft Carrier aims to revolutionize personal transportation by seamlessly integrating driving and flying capabilities.</p>
