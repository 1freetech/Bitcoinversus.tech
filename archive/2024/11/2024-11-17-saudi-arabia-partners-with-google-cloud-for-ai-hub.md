<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/31/saudi-cleric-declares-bitcoin-permissible-under-islamic-law/">Saudi Arabia</a> is embarking on a transformative journey to establish itself as a global leader in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/26/germany-debuts-official-ai-tour-guide-emma-for-tourism-campaign/">artificial intelligence</a> through a series of strategic initiatives and partnerships. The Public Investment Fund (PIF) has <a href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2577417/business-econo">announced</a> a collaboration with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/30/google-fined-20-decillion-by-moscow-court/">Google</a> Cloud to develop an advanced AI hub near Dammam in the Eastern Province. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The AI center is <a href="https://www.arabnews.com/node/2577417/business-economy">projected</a> to create thousands of jobs and contribute approximately $71 billion to the nation's gross domestic product over the next eight years.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to the AI hub, Saudi Arabia is <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6AkgtlaC4Fs">planning</a> "Project Transcendence," a state-backed initiative with potential investments reaching up to $100 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The project aims to develop a technological hub that rivals neighboring United Arab Emirates by investing in data centers, startups, and other infrastructure to advance AI capabilities. It also focuses on recruiting talent, fostering a local ecosystem, and encouraging tech companies to establish a presence in the country.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The PIF's strategy includes the creation of Alat, a fund chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, which seeks to co-invest with large international companies. Alat is dedicated to developing AI and semiconductor technologies, reflecting Saudi Arabia's commitment to diversifying its economy and reducing reliance on oil revenues.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The partnership with Google Cloud is expected to involve investments ranging from $5 to $10 billion, focusing on developing Arabic-language AI models and enhancing the region's digital infrastructure. This collaboration underscores Saudi Arabia's ambition to become a regional tech hub by the end of the decade.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These initiatives align with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 plan, which aims to diversify the economy and position the kingdom as a leader in technological innovation. By investing heavily in AI and forming strategic partnerships, Saudi Arabia is poised to make significant contributions to the global digital economy.</p>
