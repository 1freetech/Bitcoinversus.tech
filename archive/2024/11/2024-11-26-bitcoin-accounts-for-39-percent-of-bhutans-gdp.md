<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/proof-of-work-awards-meet-the-10-most-influential-bitcoin-leaders-in-2024-honorable-mention-included/">Bhutan's strategic investment in Bitcoin</a> has led to the cryptocurrency comprising 39% of the nation's Gross Domestic Product (GDP), amounting to $1.14 billion.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.worldcoinindex.com/news/bhutan-s-bitcoin-boom-39-of-gdp-tied-to-1-14-billion-crypto-investment?utm_source=chatgpt.com" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">World Coin Index</a> This significant allocation underscores the country's commitment to integrating digital assets into its economic framework.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Himalayan kingdom's substantial Bitcoin holdings have been <a href="https://decrypt.co/249876/bhutan-has-even-more-bitcoin-than-el-salvador-thanks-to-its-mining-operation?utm_source=chatgpt.com">accumulated</a> through state-run mining operations, leveraging its abundant hydroelectric resources.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This approach has positioned Bhutan as a notable player in the global cryptocurrency landscape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Inspired by El Salvador's adoption of Bitcoin as legal tender in 2021, Bhutan has <a href="https://www.worldcoinindex.com/news/bhutan-s-bitcoin-boom-39-of-gdp-tied-to-1-14-billion-crypto-investment?utm_source=chatgpt.com">demonstrated</a> the potential of cryptocurrencies to reshape financial landscapes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/el-salvadors-bitcoin-investment-yields-over-525-million-in-profit/">El Salvador's Bitcoin investments</a> have surpassed $550 million, yet Bhutan's bold commitment has set a new standard for national crypto adoption.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The government's proactive stance on digital assets reflects a broader trend among nations exploring the integration of cryptocurrencies into their economies.</p>
