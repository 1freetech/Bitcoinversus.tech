<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pennsylvania has made history by <a href="https://www.foxbusiness.com/money/pennsylvania-house-introduces-bill-implement-strategic-bitcoin-reserve">introducing</a> the Pennsylvania Bitcoin Strategic Reserve Act, a bill that would allow the state treasury to allocate up to 10% of its $7 billion <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">reserves into Bitcoin</a>. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Republican State Representative Mike Cabell, the bill's sponsor, described the act as a "visionary step toward securing our state's financial future." He <a href="https://www.foxbusiness.com/money/pennsylvania-house-introduces-bill-implement-strategic-bitcoin-reserve">emphasized</a> that integrating Bitcoin into the state's reserves could protect Pennsylvania from inflation's impact and position it as a leader in financial innovation.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The bill's introduction <a href="https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/pennsylvania-house-passes-bipartisan-bill-bring-regulatory-clarity-digital-assets">follows</a> the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Pennsylvania House's</a> recent passage of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin Rights bill</a>, which safeguards residents' rights to self-custody digital assets and use Bitcoin as a payment method. This earlier legislation received strong bipartisan support, passing with a majority of 176 votes to 26.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Advocacy groups like the Satoshi Action Fund have played a significant role in crafting both pieces of legislation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The fund's founder, Dennis Porter, <a href="https://www.foxbusiness.com/money/pennsylvania-house-introduces-bill-implement-strategic-bitcoin-reserve">noted</a> that similar Bitcoin reserve initiatives are being considered in at least ten other states, indicating a growing trend toward state-level adoption of digital assets.</p>
