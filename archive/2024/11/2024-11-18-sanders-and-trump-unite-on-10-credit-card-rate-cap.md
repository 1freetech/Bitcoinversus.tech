<p style="text-transform:none">Senator Bernie Sanders has expressed support for working with former President Donald Trump to cap credit card interest rates at 10%. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Highlighting bipartisan potential, Sanders called out excessive rates charged by banks as "usury," a stance aligning with Trump’s prior pledge to curb financial exploitation. Per <em>Newsweek</em>, the average credit card interest rate now exceeds 21%, worsening consumer debt.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Sanders has previously pushed for a 15% cap, Trump’s proposal at 10% underscores shared priorities in tackling predatory lending. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Critics warn that strict caps could tighten credit access, but both leaders see opportunity for meaningful reform. According to <em>Yahoo Finance</em>, bipartisan action could drive significant changes for American consumers.</p>
