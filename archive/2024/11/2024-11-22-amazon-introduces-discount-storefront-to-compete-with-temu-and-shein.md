<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/amazon-invests-500m-in-nuclear-power-for-5000-megawatts-of-ai-energy/">Amazon</a> has <a href="https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/affordable-products-amazon-20-dollars-and-under">introduced</a> "Amazon Haul," a new storefront offering a wide range of products priced at $20 or less, aiming to compete with discount retailers like Temu and Shein.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Accessible via Amazon's mobile app, Amazon Haul features categories such as fashion, home goods, and electronics, with many items priced under $10 and some as low as $1.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">To maintain low prices, Amazon Haul items are shipped directly from a warehouse in China, resulting in delivery times of one to two weeks</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Orders over $25 qualify for free shipping; otherwise, a $3.99 fee applies. Returns are accepted for items priced above $3 within 15 days of delivery.</p>
