<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Prominent figures in the financial and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> sectors have made ambitious predictions regarding <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/06/prediction-a-bitcoin-etf-will-outperform-the-success-of-gold-etfs/">Bitcoin's future value</a>, reflecting their perspectives on its potential role in the global economy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/18/ark-invests-cathie-wood-foresees-a-golden-era-for-bitcoin/">Cathie Wood</a>, CEO of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/11/ark-investment-ceo-cathie-wood-anticipates-parabolic-move-with-bitcoin-in-convergence-with-ai-boom/">Ark Invest</a>, projects Bitcoin reaching <strong>$1 million by 2030</strong>. Her optimistic <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/currencies/bitcoin-price-outlook-cathie-wood-cryptocurrency-investors-target-markets-etf-2024-3">outlook</a> is based on anticipated widespread adoption and Bitcoin's potential to become a standard store of value.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/microstrategys-michael-saylor-declares-bitcoin-as-the-universal-merger-partner/">Michael Saylor</a>, CEO of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a>, <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2024/07/28/microstrategys-michael-saylor-says-bitcoin-will-hit-13000000-in-base-case-scenario-heres-his-timeline/">envisions</a> Bitcoin attaining <strong>$13 million by 2045</strong>. Saylor views Bitcoin as "digital property" and a hedge against inflation, leading his company to accumulate substantial Bitcoin reserves.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/24/bitmex-founder-arthur-hayes-predicts-historic-bitcoin-surge-by-2026/">Arthur Hayes</a>, co-founder of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/31/to-the-moon-literally-bitmex-plans-on-sending-1-bitcoin-to-the-moon/">BitMEX</a>, predicts a Bitcoin price of <strong>$750,000 by 2026</strong>. Hayes attributes this potential growth to increased institutional interest and favorable market conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Robert Kiyosaki, author of "Rich Dad Poor Dad," anticipates <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/the-evolution-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a> reaching <strong>$500,000 by 2025</strong>. Kiyosaki promotes Bitcoin as a hedge against economic instability and inflation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/28/tim-draper-leads-2-5m-round-for-bitcoin-payment-startup-ark-labs/">Tim Draper</a>, venture capitalist, forecasts <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/the-evolution-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a> hitting <strong>$250,000 by 2025</strong>. Draper's confidence stems from his belief in Bitcoin's capacity to disrupt traditional banking and financial systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/22/pete-rizzo-challenges-bitcoin-stock-analyst-planb-s2f-model-is-dead/">PlanB</a>, creator of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/22/pete-rizzo-challenges-bitcoin-stock-analyst-planb-s2f-model-is-dead/">Stock-to-Flow model</a>, anticipates a price of <strong>$400,000 by 2026</strong>. His model evaluates Bitcoin's scarcity and supply reduction through halving events.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/bitcoin-set-for-a-major-surge-with-etf-options-says-galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz/">Mike Novogratz</a>, CEO of Galaxy Digital Holdings, projects <strong>$500,000 by 2028</strong>. Novogratz has expressed confidence in Bitcoin's trajectory and its place within the broader financial ecosystem.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Peter Brandt, chart trader and author, estimates Bitcoin will reach <strong>$135,000 by September 2025</strong>. Brandt bases his forecast on chart patterns and technical analysis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mark Yusko, CEO of Morgan Creek Capital, predicts <strong>$150,000 by 2025</strong>. Yusko views Bitcoin as a digital alternative to gold, focusing on its deflationary qualities and appeal to institutional investors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tyler Winklevoss, founder of Gemini projects a price of <strong>$500,000 by 2030</strong>. Winklevoss emphasizes Bitcoin's potential to act as "digital gold."</p>
