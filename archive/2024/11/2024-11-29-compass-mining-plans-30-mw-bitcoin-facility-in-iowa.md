<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://compassmining.io/">Compass Mining</a>, a prominent provider of Bitcoin mining hardware and services, has announced plans to establish a new mining facility in Iowa. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company has acquired property and secured power agreements for the state-of-the-art site, which is set to begin operations with an initial capacity of 8 megawatts (MW) in January 2025. Future expansions aim to increase capacity up to 30 MW as additional infrastructure investments are pursued.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9776,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-53.png?w=597" alt="" class="wp-image-9776" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Situated on a 5-acre greenfield site, the Iowa facility <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/compass-mining-expands-into-iowa-with-new-30-mw-bitcoin-mining-facility-302308018.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">represents</a> a significant advancement in Compass Mining's strategy to expand its self-owned infrastructure portfolio. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">An Electric Service Agreement (ESA) is in place to ensure the energization of the initial 8 MW capacity by early 2025. Once operational, customers will have the option to select "Iowa 4" as the deployment location for new machines purchased through Compass's platform.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Traditionally partnering with third-party facilities, Compass Mining is now focusing on reducing counterparty risk by owning and operating its own sites. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The new Iowa facility exemplifies this approach, allowing the company to enhance control over operations and offer greater flexibility to customers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Chief Mining Officer Shanon Squires <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/compass-mining-expands-into-iowa-with-new-30-mw-bitcoin-mining-facility-302308018.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emphasized</a> that increasing self-owned capacity provides customers with greater operational control, while partnerships with reliable third-party facilities continue to offer deployment flexibility, reflecting both power market dynamics and geographic diversity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/simple-mining-targets-125-mw-bitcoin-mining-hasrate-by-q1-2025/">Iowa facility</a> is part of Compass Mining's broader U.S. expansion efforts. Earlier this year, the company successfully energized facilities in Kentucky (10 MW) and Nebraska (7.5 MW). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 2024 alone, Compass has added nearly 50 MW of new power capacity across Indiana, Iowa, Ohio, Kentucky, Nebraska, and Texas, with plans for an additional 20 MW by January 2025.</p>
