<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mawson Infrastructure Group has <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/08/27/2936263/0/en/Mawson-Infrastructure-Group-Expands-to-Ohio-for-AI-HPC-Expansion.html">announced</a> its expansion into Ohio, aiming to bolster its AI and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">high-performance computing</a> (HPC) infrastructure. This strategic move is expected to increase the company’s total operational capacity from 129 megawatts (MW) to approximately 153 MW. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The expansion will add 24 MW of capacity and extend their footprint within the PJM market, North America's largest wholesale electric market.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Mawson’s Ohio expansion follows a successful project in Pennsylvania and aligns with its commitment to carbon-free energy, including nuclear power, to support the growing demands of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/05/culture-ai-transforms-modern-hip-hop-into-old-school-rb/">AI</a>, HPC, and digital assets markets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company is also inviting additional AI and HPC partners for further collaboration and innovation opportunities.</p>
