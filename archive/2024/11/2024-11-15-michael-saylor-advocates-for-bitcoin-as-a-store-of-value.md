<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">Michael Saylor</a>, Executive Chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a>, has reiterated his belief that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">Bitcoin will surpass gold</a> as a premier store of value. He stated, "Bitcoin is going to eat gold. It's got all the great attributes of gold and none of the defects of gold."</p>

<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/22/microstrategy-chairman-michael-saylor-highlights-bitcoin-performance-amid-institutional-acceptance/">MicroStrategy</a> has significantly increased its Bitcoin holdings. As of September 20, 2024, the company owns 252,220 bitcoins, acquired at an average price of $39,292.18 per bitcoin, totaling approximately $9.91 billion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Saylor's confidence in Bitcoin's potential</a> stems from its digital nature, scarcity, and ease of transfer, which he believes address the limitations of physical gold. He has previously emphasized that Bitcoin possesses all the positive attributes of gold without its drawbacks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MicroStrategy's aggressive <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">Bitcoin acquisition</a> strategy has positioned it as the largest corporate holder of the cryptocurrency. The company's stock performance has been closely tied to Bitcoin's price movements, reflecting investor sentiment toward its digital asset strategy..</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The broader financial community remains divided on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/bitcoin-appreciates-over-time-relative-to-the-iphone/">Bitcoin's role as a store of value</a>. While proponents like Saylor advocate for its superiority over traditional assets like gold, critics caution about its volatility and regulatory uncertainties.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite differing opinions, MicroStrategy continues to expand its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/03/u-s-bitcoin-etfs-set-to-surpass-satoshi-nakamotos-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a>, signaling a strong commitment to its digital asset strategy. The company's recent financial disclosures highlight its ongoing investments in Bitcoin, underscoring its belief in the cryptocurrency's long-term value proposition.</p>
