<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/18/ark-invests-cathie-wood-foresees-a-golden-era-for-bitcoin/">ARK Invest</a>, led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/18/ark-invests-cathie-wood-foresees-a-golden-era-for-bitcoin/">Cathie Wood</a>, made headlines on August 27, 2024, by selling a significant portion of its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Bitcoin holdings</a>.
