<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A recent HBO documentary, <em>Money Electric: The Bitcoin Mystery</em>, has sparked controversy by <a href="https://insidebitcoins.com/news/hbo-documentary-claims-peter-todd-is-bitcoin-creator-satoshi-nakamoto-but-he-denies-it">suggesting</a> that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">Bitcoin Core</a> developer Peter Todd is the elusive Satoshi Nakamoto, the creator of Bitcoin. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The film, directed by Cullen Hoback, points to Todd's early involvement in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/06/analyzing-the-bitcoin-god-candle-theory-by-aleksander-svetski/">Bitcoin community</a> and his cryptographic expertise as circumstantial evidence linking him to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/29/who-is-satoshi-nakamoto-new-emails-with-adam-back-enter-public-domain/">Nakamoto</a>. Despite these assertions, Todd has vehemently denied the claims.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://crypto2community.com/crypto-news/hbo-documentary-claims-bitcoin-creator-satoshi-nakamoto-is-peter-todd-who-denies-it/">statement</a> following the documentary’s release, Todd responded on social media, <a href="https://x.com/peterktodd/status/1843789750983110697">saying</a>, "I'm not Satoshi," and dismissed the accusations as "ludicrous" and based on conspiracy theories. He critiqued the filmmaker for resorting to sensationalism, noting that the timeline of his involvement with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/14/italian-bank-banca-sella-introduces-bitcoin-trading/">Bitcoin</a> does not align with Nakamoto's initial activity in 2008​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The documentary <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/hbo-documentary-peter-todd-satoshi-nakamoto">highlighted</a> key points, such as Todd’s interaction with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">Nakamoto</a> on the BitcoinTalk forum in 2010 and his introduction of the “replace-by-fee” concept in 2014, but none of this constitutes solid proof. Experts have been quick to critique the film’s lack of concrete evidence, with many in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/11/michael-saylor-of-microstrategy-forecasts-bitcoin-as-the-leading-asset-class/">crypto community</a> expressing skepticism about the claims​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Some <a href="https://www.ibtimes.com/im-not-satoshi-peter-todd-denies-hbo-documentary-claim-hes-bitcoin-creator-crypto-goes-wild-3746068">argue</a> that the real Satoshi would deny their identity to avoid government scrutiny and maintain anonymity​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Peter Todd remains firm in his denial, and the true identity of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">Satoshi Nakamoto</a> remains a mystery.</p>
