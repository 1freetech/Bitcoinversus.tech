<p style="text-transform:none">Bluesky, the emerging social media platform, has experienced a remarkable surge in <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/19/bluesky-tops-20m-users-narrowing-gap-with-instagram-threads/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">user engagement</a>, with its user base surpassing 20 million in November 2024.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This rapid growth has prompted analysis of user behavior, particularly the relationship between content creation and follower acquisition.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The 10:1 post-to-follower ratio observed on Bluesky can be classified as a behavioral social media growth theory or a user engagement theory. </p>
