<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/02/boeing-starliner-astronauts-stranded-in-space-wont-return-until-2025/">Astronauts</a> aboard the International Space Station (ISS) are set to <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/general/how-nasa-astronauts-vote-from-space-aboard-international-space-station/">vote</a> in the upcoming <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/04/data-points-to-bitcoin-price-rally-following-u-s-elections/">U.S. elections</a> by casting their ballots from space. This initiative ensures that even those in orbit can exercise their voting rights.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The process <a href="https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/how-do-astronauts-vote-space">begins</a> with astronauts completing a Federal Post Card Application before their mission, indicating their intent to vote from space. Subsequently, a secure electronic ballot is transmitted to the ISS, which the astronaut fills out and returns via a secure channel to be counted.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This system was first utilized in 1997 when Texas passed legislation allowing astronauts to vote electronically from space. Most American astronauts reside in Texas, making this provision particularly relevant.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Notably, NASA astronaut Kate Rubins cast her ballot from the ISS during the 2016 and 2020 elections, highlighting the program's success.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The secure transmission of ballots is facilitated by NASA's Space Communications and Navigation (SCaN) infrastructure, ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of the voting process.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative underscores NASA's commitment to enabling astronauts to fulfill their civic duties, regardless of their location. The program exemplifies the seamless integration of technology and democracy.</p>
