<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Hut 8 Corp., a leading Bitcoin mining company, has <a href="https://hut8.com/2024/11/06/hut-8-announces-initial-asic-fleet-upgrade/">announced</a> a significant upgrade to its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/19/hive-digital-prioritizes-efficiency-with-major-antminer-s19k-pro-investment/">mining fleet</a>, aiming to enhance operational efficiency and expand mining capacity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company plans to acquire 31,145 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/bitmains-new-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">BITMAIN Antminer</a> S21+ <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">ASIC machines</a>, with delivery expected in early Q1 2025. This strategic move is anticipated to increase Hut 8's self-mining hashrate by approximately 3.7 exahashes per second (EH/s), bringing the total to around 9.3 EH/s—a 66% increase. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, the upgrade is set to improve fleet efficiency from 31.7 to 19.9 joules per terahash (J/TH), marking a 37% enhancement.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://marketchameleon.com/PressReleases/i/1976005/HUT/hut-8-operations-update-for-september-2024">previous announcement</a>, Hut 8 revealed a partnership with BITMAIN to host U3S21EXPH ASIC miners at its Vega site in the Texas Panhandle. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This collaboration includes a purchase option for approximately 15 EH/s of hosted miners, with deployment targeted for the first half of 2025. The agreement is expected to generate around $135 million in annualized hosting revenue once fully operational.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Combining the fleet upgrade with the Vega site deployment positions Hut 8 to achieve a total self-mining hashrate of approximately 24 EH/s by Q2 2025. The average fleet efficiency is projected to improve to 15.7 J/TH, underscoring the company's commitment to optimizing energy consumption and operational performance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Hut 8's strategic initiatives reflect a broader industry trend toward enhancing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/13/energy-how-to-enhance-data-center-efficiency/">mining efficiency</a> and scaling operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Other companies, such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/19/hive-digital-prioritizes-efficiency-with-major-antminer-s19k-pro-investment/">HIVE Digital</a>, have also upgraded their fleets with advanced ASIC miners to improve performance and reduce energy costs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The planned upgrades and partnerships underscore Hut 8's dedication to maintaining a competitive edge in the rapidly evolving <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">Bitcoin mining landscape</a>. By investing in cutting-edge technology and expanding operational capacity, the company aims to strengthen its position as a leading player in the industry.</p>
