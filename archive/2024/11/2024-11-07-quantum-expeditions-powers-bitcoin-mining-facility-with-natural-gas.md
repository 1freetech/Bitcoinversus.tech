<p style="text-transform:none">Quantum Expeditions has established a 0.5MW facility that operates on natural gas to power 188 Bitcoin mining machines. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This includes 44 of the more powerful S21 machines and 144 SK19 Pros. They have a total hashrate of about 25PH/s. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The CEO, Doug Hardwick, emphasized the importance of treating the gas before using it to avoid any potential damage to the generators. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He mentioned that you don't want "dirty gas" hooked up to your expensive generator, which could lead to costly repairs or inefficiencies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, he pointed out that it's more cost-effective to utilize gas that has already been found, rather than incurring additional expenses in exploration​.</p>
