<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter and its subsequent rebranding to X has significantly impacted the platform's integration capabilities with WordPress.com. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Historically, WordPress.com users could seamlessly share their content on Twitter through the Jetpack plugin. However, following Musk's takeover, X introduced substantial price increases for its API access, leading WordPress.com to discontinue its Twitter integration due to the prohibitive costs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2022/4/6/23012913/twitter-tweet-embeds-deleted-tweets-empty-iframe-broken">termination</a> of this integration has broader implications for content verification and preservation. Embedded tweets on websites have traditionally served as verifiable records of statements or events. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, if a tweet is deleted by its author, the embedded content may become inaccessible or display as blank spaces, complicating the verification process for journalists and researchers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In response to these challenges, it's more efficient to use unaltered screenshots of tweets as a more reliable method of documentation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Screenshots capture the content as it appeared at a specific moment, preserving the information even if the original tweet is later deleted or altered. This approach ensures that the context and content remain intact for future reference.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The evolving dynamics between social media platforms and content management systems underscore the importance of reliable content preservation methods. As platforms like X modify their integration policies and API access, content creators and journalists must adapt their strategies to maintain the integrity and verifiability of shared information.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
