<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Texas State Representative Giovanni Capriglione has expressed a desire for Texas to establish a Strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">Bitcoin Reserve</a> ahead of federal initiatives. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Speaking at the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/bitkey-wallet-applauded-by-texas-blockchain-council-leader-lee-bratcher/">Texas Blockchain Summit</a> in Dallas, Capriglione stated, "It’s destined to happen somewhere. I’d rather it happen here first."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10023,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-28-063224.png?w=594" alt="" class="wp-image-10023" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This sentiment aligns with recent discussions among Texas lawmakers about creating a state-held Bitcoin reserve. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/18/intel-bitcoin-og-dennis-porter-says-claims-trump-wants-bitcoin-reserve-in-nashville/">Dennis Porter</a>, CEO of the Satoshi Action Fund, revealed that preliminary conversations with a Texas legislator regarding such legislation have begun.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These state-level initiatives mirror federal efforts, such as U.S. Senator Cynthia Lummis's introduction of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">BITCOIN Act of 2024</a>, which proposes the establishment of a national Bitcoin strategic reserve.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Texas <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/texas-discussion-introduce-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-satoshi-action-fund?utm_source=chatgpt.com">boasts</a> the world's eighth-largest economy, with a GDP of $2.4 trillion in 2022, the creation of a Strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz-predicts-500k-bitcoin-with-u-s-reserve-establishment/">Bitcoin Reserve</a> could significantly impact both the state's economic resilience and the broader adoption of digital assets.</p>
