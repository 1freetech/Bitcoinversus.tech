<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">President-elect <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump </a>has <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Government_Efficiency">announced</a> the formation of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), appointing Elon Musk and Vivek Ramaswamy as its leaders. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initiative aims to streamline federal operations by reducing bureaucracy, eliminating excessive regulations, and cutting wasteful expenditures.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Elon Musk</a>, CEO of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/elon-musks-optimus-robot-set-for-mass-adoption-at-20k-30k-price/">Tesla</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/spacex-launch-sends-shock-waves-through-the-upper-ionosphere/">SpaceX</a>, along with entrepreneur and former presidential candidate Vivek Ramaswamy, will co-chair the commission. Their combined expertise is expected to drive significant reforms in government efficiency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The acronym DOGE, a nod to the popular internet meme and cryptocurrency Dogecoin, reflects the unconventional approach of the initiative. The commission is set to conclude its work by July 4, 2026, aligning with the 250th anniversary of the U.S. Declaration of Independence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Musk and Ramaswamy have proposed substantial reductions in federal spending, with Musk suggesting cuts of up to $2 trillion from the $6.75 trillion budget. Ramaswamy has advocated for significant downsizing of the federal workforce and the elimination of certain agencies.<br>VOX</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Critics express concerns that such drastic measures could adversely affect essential government services and economic stability. Ethics experts also highlight potential conflicts of interest, given Musk's extensive business dealings with the federal government.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Historically, similar efforts to reduce government waste have faced challenges. The success of DOGE will depend on its ability to implement reforms without compromising critical services.<br>VOX</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The commission's recommendations are anticipated to influence the incoming administration's budgetary decisions, with the potential to reshape the federal government's operational landscape.</p>
