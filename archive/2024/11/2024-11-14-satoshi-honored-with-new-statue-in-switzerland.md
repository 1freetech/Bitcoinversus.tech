<p style="text-transform:none">The Plan ₿ Initiative, a collaboration between <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/21/tether-leverages-stablecoin-success-makes-500-million-investment-in-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure/">Tether</a> and the city of Lugano, Switzerland, has introduced a remarkable statue honoring Bitcoin’s creator, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">Satoshi Nakamoto</a>. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Revealed during the 3rd Annual Plan ₿ Forum, this statue <a href="https://coinjournal.net/news/tether-and-city-of-lugano-unveil-satoshi-nakamoto-statue/">features</a> a disappearing effect designed to symbolize Nakamoto’s mystery and anonymity in the cryptocurrency world. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Located at Villa Ciani in Lugano, the statue attracted Bitcoin advocates, government officials, and technology enthusiasts, who gathered to celebrate the influence of Nakamoto’s work on financial innovation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino, the statue is more than a tribute; it <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2024/10/25/tethers-plan-b-initiative-unveils-new-satoshi-nakamoto-statue-in-lugano-switzerland/">embodies</a> the spirit of decentralization that Bitcoin represents, pointing to Nakamoto’s choice to fade from public life and let the cryptocurrency community take the lead. </p>
