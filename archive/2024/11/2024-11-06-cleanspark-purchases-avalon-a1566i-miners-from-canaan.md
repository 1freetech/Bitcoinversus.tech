<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Canaan</a> U.S. Inc. has announced that it secured an order from CleanSpark for the delivery of 3,800 Avalon A1566i <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/bitaxe-ultra-achieves-1-2-th-s-with-immersion-cooling-technology/">Immersion Cooling Miners</a>. Each of these high-performance miners operates with an average compute power of 249 Terahash per second (TH/s) without overclocking. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The delivery is set to take place in the fourth quarter of 2024, highlighting CleanSpark's strategic investment in advanced immersion mining operations. Sources confirm CleanSpark's dedication to enhancing energy efficiency and output through innovative technologies like Canaan's latest miner models.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Canaan’s Avalon A1566i model is designed for immersion cooling, which significantly optimizes operational temperatures and power consumption, enabling miners to achieve high levels of compute power sustainably. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark's focus on immersion cooling aligns with its mission to incorporate sustainable and cost-effective methods in Bitcoin mining. The investment in these miners supports CleanSpark's commitment to maintaining its position as an environmentally conscious leader in the mining sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Immersion cooling technology is becoming an industry standard due to its ability to manage heat more effectively than traditional air-cooling methods. CleanSpark's decision to expand its mining operations with Canaan's equipment demonstrates a forward-thinking approach to improving performance while reducing energy overheads. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The upcoming deployment of 3,800 miners reinforces CleanSpark's status as a prominent player in the North American mining landscape.</p>
