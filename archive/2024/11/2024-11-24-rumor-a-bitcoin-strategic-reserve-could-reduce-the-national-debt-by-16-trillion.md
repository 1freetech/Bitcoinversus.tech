<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Senator <a href="https://www.lummis.senate.gov/press-releases/lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-legislation/">Cynthia Lummis</a> has <a href="https://www.lummis.senate.gov/press-releases/lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-legislation/">introduced</a> the BITCOIN Act, proposing that the U.S. Treasury acquire 1 million bitcoins over five years to establish a strategic reserve aimed at bolstering the dollar and countering inflation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/microstrategy-rallies-with-nav-multiple-suggesting-undervaluation/">MicroStrategy</a> Chairman <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/01/michael-saylor-faces-backlash-over-bitcoin-self-custody-stance/">Michael Saylor</a> supports the initiative, <a href="https://coingape.com/how-bitcoin-reserve-will-cut-us-national-debt-by-16-trillion-michael-saylor-explains/">suggesting</a> that such an acquisition could potentially reduce the national debt by $16 trillion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.lummis.senate.gov/press-releases/lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-legislation/">proposed purchase</a> represents approximately 5% of Bitcoin's total supply, aligning with the scale of the nation's gold reserves.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of November 12, 2024, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/07/bitcoins-value-strengthens-in-relation-to-the-us-national-debt/">U.S. national debt</a> has surpassed $35.9 trillion.</p>
