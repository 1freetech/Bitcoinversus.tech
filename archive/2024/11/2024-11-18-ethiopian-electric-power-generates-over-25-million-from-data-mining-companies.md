<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ethiopian Electric Power (EEP) <a href="https://addisstandard.com/ethiopian-electric-power-generates-over-25-million-in-revenue-from-data-mining-companies/">announced</a> that it generated over $25 million in revenue during the 2023/2024 fiscal year from electricity supplied to companies involved in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">data mining</a>. This substantial income is part of EEP's strategy to increase foreign currency earnings by billing domestic customers in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/01/bitcoin-advocate-femi-longe-wants-the-world-to-price-their-goods-and-services-in-bitcoin/">U.S. dollars</a> rather than Ethiopian Birr​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Minilik Getahun, Manager of Sales and Customer Management at EEP, <a href="https://ethiopianmonitor.com/2024/02/23/eep-begins-selling-electric-power-to-data-miners-in-foreign-currency/">highlighted</a> that since December 2022, nine data mining companies have been making payments in U.S. dollars, a shift from the previous practice of local currency billing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initial target was to supply 882.48 gigawatt-hours of electricity, aiming for a revenue of $27.71 million. However, due to operational challenges, only 796.28 gigawatt-hours were supplied, which still resulted in more than 90% of the projected income​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This development is a crucial part of Ethiopia's broader effort to attract foreign investment and leverage its hydropower resources for economic growth. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The switch to dollar billing reflects Ethiopia's commitment to boosting foreign exchange reserves, especially as the country continues to position itself as a favorable destination for energy-intensive industries like data mining​.</p>
