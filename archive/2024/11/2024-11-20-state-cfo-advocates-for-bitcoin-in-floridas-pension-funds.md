<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Florida's Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Jimmy Patronis, has <a href="https://coinjournal.net/news/florida-holds-800-million-in-crypto-state-cfo-says/">announced </a>that the state holds approximately $800 million in cryptocurrency-related investments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Patronis <a href="https://x.com/BlockwareTeam/status/1852013676804325406">emphasized </a>the state's commitment to embracing digital assets, stating, "Crypto's not going anywhere. We'd be a fool if we're not prepared to do everything we can to harness the opportunities there."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z0BEQevMJik&amp;t=2s">interview</a> with CNBC's Squawk Box, Patronis discussed the potential for expanding Florida's cryptocurrency portfolio, particularly under a potential Trump administration. He noted that he "would not be shocked" to see the state's crypto investments grow in such a scenario.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Patronis has also proposed integrating Bitcoin into Florida's state pension funds. He sent a letter to the Florida State Board of Administration, requesting a report on the feasibility and benefits of including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/the-evolution-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a> and other digital assets in the state's retirement system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Other states have begun exploring cryptocurrency investments as well. The State of Wisconsin Investment Board reported holdings of $163 million in spot <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/institutional-interest-in-bitcoin-grows-as-major-banks-invest-in-etfs/">Bitcoin ETFs</a>, and Jersey City, New Jersey, has also ventured into crypto assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Patronis expressed concerns about central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), suggesting that cryptocurrencies could serve as a hedge against potential federal overreach. He stated, "We need to be able to have a hedge against this massive overreach by the federal government with a centralized currency."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The CFO's advocacy aligns with former President Donald Trump's views on Bitcoin. Trump has proposed the U.S. establish a national "stockpile" of Bitcoin and crypto reserves, citing concerns about the Communist Party of China's increasing influence over the global <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/15/bitcoin-vs-politics-the-2024-elections-will-be-a-decisive-moment-for-bitcoin-spot-etfs/">cryptocurrency landscape</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Florida's proactive stance on cryptocurrency investments reflects a broader trend among U.S. states to integrate digital assets into their financial strategies. As the cryptocurrency market continues to evolve, Florida's approach may serve as a model for other states considering similar investments.</p>
