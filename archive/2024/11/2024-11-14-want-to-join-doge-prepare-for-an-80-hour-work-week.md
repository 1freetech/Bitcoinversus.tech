<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">President-elect <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump</a> has unveiled the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/trump-appoints-musk-ramaswamy-to-lead-government-efficiency-initiative/">Department of Government Efficiency</a> (DOGE), a bold initiative focused on streamlining federal operations and reducing wasteful spending. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The department will be led by tech mogul <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Elon Musk</a> and entrepreneur <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/trump-appoints-musk-ramaswamy-to-lead-government-efficiency-initiative/">Vivek Ramaswamy</a>, who have committed to transforming the structure of government through intensive cost-cutting. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8657,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-5.png?w=668" alt="" class="wp-image-8657" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">To accomplish this, DOGE is actively recruiting individuals they describe as “super high-IQ small-government revolutionaries” willing to work over 80 hours per week on challenging, unglamorous tasks that contribute to minimizing bureaucratic inefficiencies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent post, DOGE stated, “We are very grateful to the thousands of Americans who have expressed interest in helping us at DOGE. We don’t need more part-time idea generators. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">We need super high-IQ small-government revolutionaries willing to work 80+ hours per week on unglamorous cost-cutting.” This announcement has generated considerable attention from Americans intrigued by the department’s uncompromising approach to efficiency, with the unusual demands drawing both support and skepticism.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8655,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-4.png?w=659" alt="" class="wp-image-8655" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">DOGE’s mission under Musk and Ramaswamy involves dismantling bureaucracy, slashing regulatory excess, and overhauling federal agencies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The department will coordinate with the White House and the Office of Management &amp; Budget to pursue large-scale structural reforms, all designed to create a more streamlined and cost-effective government.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The demand for 80-hour workweeks underscores the department’s emphasis on commitment, and DOGE has already attracted a range of interest from professionals eager to contribute to a new government model. However, critics have voiced concerns about the feasibility of implementing such a model and have questioned the sustainability of its intense demands. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As DOGE ramps up its activities, its effectiveness in reducing federal spending and administrative redundancy will be closely watched by the public and other government officials.</p>
