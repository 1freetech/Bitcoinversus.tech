<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/12/bitcoins-surge-toward-90000-reflects-market-confidence/">Bitcoin's price</a> has surpassed $90,000 for the first time, driven by market optimism following President-elect Donald Trump's victory and his pro-cryptocurrency stance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> reached a record high of $91,110, marking a 32% increase since the November 5 election.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Galaxy Digital CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/bitcoin-set-for-a-major-surge-with-etf-options-says-galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz/">Mike Novogratz</a> suggests that if the U.S. establishes a Bitcoin reserve, the cryptocurrency's value could soar to $500,000. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He argues that such a move would prompt other nations to acquire Bitcoin, significantly boosting demand and price.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The recent surge in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/28/bitcoins-rising-value-outpaces-traditional-wage-growth/">Bitcoin's value</a> is also attributed to substantial inflows into spot Bitcoin ETFs, which saw $1.2 billion in net inflows on Monday. Technical analysis indicates a strong bullish trend, with a 'golden cross' formation and an RSI reading above 80, suggesting overbought conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/microstrategy-purchases-an-additional-27200-btc/">MicroStrategy Inc</a>., the largest corporate holder of Bitcoin, announced plans to raise $42 billion over the next three years to purchase additional Bitcoin. This strategy aims to enhance shareholder value through the digital transformation of capital.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The cryptocurrency market's resurgence contrasts sharply with its 2022 downturn following the collapse of major companies like FTX. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Both institutional and individual investors are increasingly engaging, reflected in record futures contracts at CME and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/13/google-drops-bitcoin-chart-from-search/">rising Google searches</a>.</p>
