<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pierre Rochard, a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">Bitcoin</a> advocate from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ercot-approves-riots-1-gw-bitcoin-mining-capacity-in-corsicana/">Riot Platforms</a>, shared a post showcasing the new Bitaxe Gamma, developed by D-Central Technologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His tweet <a href="https://x.com/bitcoinpierre/status/1858717807355064648">highlighted</a> the simplicity of the setup and expressed enthusiasm for experimenting with this innovative solo mining device.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9825,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-23-085424.png?w=591" alt="" class="wp-image-9825" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitaxe-gamma-redefines-asic-mining-with-advanced-cooling-features/">Bitaxe Gamma</a> is an open-source Bitcoin ASIC miner developed by D-Central Technologies. It utilizes the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/quantum-bitcoin-mining-futuristic-heatsink-enhances-bitaxe-asic-chip-performance/">BM1370 ASIC chip</a>, the same chip found in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">Antminer S21 Pro</a>, delivering a hash rate of 1.0 to 1.2 TH/s with an impressive power efficiency of just 15 J/TH.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Designed for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/network-effect-solo-mining-pool-surpasses-9000-users-amid-hashrate-decline/">solo mining</a> enthusiasts, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitaxe-gamma-redefines-asic-mining-with-advanced-cooling-features/">Bitaxe Gamma</a> features built-in Wi-Fi, allowing for seamless wireless connectivity and easy management of mining operations. It also includes a 0.91" OLED display for real-time monitoring of hash rate, temperature, and power consumption.<br>AMAZON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The miner operates on a global voltage standard, making it suitable for use worldwide. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Its compact and portable design, combined with energy efficiency, makes it an attractive option for both novice and experienced miners.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For those interested in customization, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/how-to-build-your-own-bitcoin-mining-data-center-bitaxe-edition-spread-sheet-included/">Bitaxe Gamma</a> is powered by AxeOS, an open-source firmware that provides full control over mining settings and performance monitoring through a modern web interface.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Overall, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/fold-mining-club-offers-limited-edition-bitaxe-bitcoin-miner-bundle/">Bitaxe Gamma</a> represents a significant advancement in desktop Bitcoin mining technology, offering high performance, energy efficiency, and user-friendly features in an open-source package.</p>
