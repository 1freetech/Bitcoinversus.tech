<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/27/bitfarms-acquires-stronghold-digital-in-175-million-deal/">Bitfarms</a> Ltd. reported earning 236 BTC in October 2024, enhancing its total <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin treasury</a> to 1,188 BTC. This figure underlines the company's robust mining activity across its 12 facilities spread across four countries. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The expansion comes alongside an agreement with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/27/bitfarms-acquires-stronghold-digital-in-175-million-deal/">Stronghold Digital Mining</a>, involving the hosting of 10,000 miners at the Panther Creek site in Pennsylvania. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The partnership features a profit-sharing mechanism, allowing both companies to capitalize on increased operational efficiency. Bitfarms indicated some delays in reaching its target hashrate, which will impact production timelines and performance metrics.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Stronghold, known for its environmentally focused coal refuse power generation, aligns with Bitfarms' pursuit of sustainable energy solutions for its mining operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The deployment under this deal will contribute to Bitfarms' strategic scale-up of energy capacity and mining output. The company’s efforts reflect its broader initiative to optimize <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">mining efficiency</a> through strategic partnerships and regional diversification.</p>
