<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Thumzup Media Corporation, a <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/15/2981922/0/en/Thumzup-Board-of-Directors-Approves-Bitcoin-as-Treasury-Reserve-Asset.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">publicly traded</a> company on Nasdaq under the ticker TZUP, has announced its decision to allocate up to $1 million of its treasury reserves into <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/goldman-sachs-invests-710-million-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Robert Steele emphasized that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/31/unpopular-opinion-michael-saylor-is-the-best-candidate-for-secretary-of-the-treasury/">Bitcoin's finite supply</a> and resistance to inflation make it a robust addition to the company's financial strategy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The board's approval <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/15/2981922/0/en/Thumzup-Board-of-Directors-Approves-Bitcoin-as-Treasury-Reserve-Asset.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reflects</a> a growing trend among corporations to incorporate digital assets into their balance sheets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Steele <a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/thumzup-approves-1-million-bitcoin/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">highlighted</a> the influence of newly sanctioned Bitcoin ETFs and increasing institutional support as factors enhancing Bitcoin's role as a reliable asset for preserving value.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Following the announcement, Thumzup's stock experienced a 4% increase, reaching $3.97 per share, and bringing the company's market capitalization to approximately $36 million. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move aligns Thumzup with other firms adopting Bitcoin to diversify reserves and hedge against economic uncertainties.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to its treasury strategy, Thumzup plans to offer <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">Bitcoin payments</a> to its gig economy workforce through its Account Specialist Program. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative reflects the company's commitment to innovative compensation solutions and acknowledges the growing demand for cryptocurrency payments among gig workers.</p>
