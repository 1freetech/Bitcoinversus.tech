<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Andre Ingram, a seasoned NBA G League player, has been a vocal <a href="https://www.theeagleonline.com/article/2021/04/10-years-in-the-g-league-an-oral-history-of-andre-ingrams-call-up-to-the-lakers">advocate</a> for increased salaries for G League athletes. Ingram, who has spent over a decade in the G League, has firsthand experience with the financial challenges players face. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He has consistently highlighted the need for better compensation to support players' livelihoods and career development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9468,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-37.png?w=932" alt="" class="wp-image-9468" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ingram's advocacy gained significant attention when he was <a href="https://hoopshype.com/player/andre-ingram/">elected</a> as the first president of the NBA G League Players Union in 2020. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In this role, he has worked tirelessly to negotiate better terms for players, focusing on salary increases, improved benefits, and enhanced working conditions. His leadership has been instrumental in bringing players' concerns to the forefront of league discussions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7UD-98Ly-v8","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The NBA G League has responded to these efforts by implementing salary increases and offering more comprehensive benefits packages. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 2021, the league <a href="https://www.sportingnews.com/ca/nba/news/g-league-players-salaries-contracts-how-much/jzdmveihgvjjutcqq1jvqqea">announced</a> a new salary structure that raised base salaries and introduced performance bonuses, reflecting a commitment to addressing players' financial concerns. These changes have been seen as a direct result of Ingram's persistent advocacy and the union's negotiations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ingram's dedication to improving conditions for G League players has not only enhanced their financial stability but also elevated the overall professionalism of the league. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His efforts have paved the way for future players to pursue their basketball careers with greater security and support, underscoring the importance of strong leadership and collective bargaining in professional sports.</p>
