<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Asian <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/20/smi-vantage-boosts-malaysian-economy-with-bitcoin-mining-facility-in-sarawak/">private wealth managers</a> are increasingly embracing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">cryptocurrencies</a>, with 76% of family offices and high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) already investing in digital assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A recent <a href="https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2024/10/17/asian-private-wealth-managers-embrace-crypto-some-foresee-bitcoin-at-100k-by-year-end/">report</a> by Aspen Digital highlights a surge in crypto adoption across Asia, driven by the region's growing recognition of digital assets as a strategic investment. Among these investors, 31% expect <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/21/bitcoin-power-law-suggests-bitcoin-price-will-reach-100k-by-jan-2025/">Bitcoin</a> to reach $100,000 by the end of the year​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The potential for high returns is a significant motivator for crypto investments in Asia, alongside diversification benefits. Wealth managers are diversifying portfolios by investing in decentralized finance (DeFi) and artificial intelligence (AI), with 67% and 61% of respondents showing interest, respectively​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite this interest, the majority still allocate less than 5% of their portfolios to digital assets, primarily due to regulatory uncertainty and fragmented market conditions​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As spot-based <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/17/blackrock-pushes-bitcoin-as-key-asset-at-brazils-digital-conference/">Bitcoin exchange-traded funds</a> (ETFs) gain traction in markets like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/27/hong-kongs-futu-securities-launches-bitcoin-and-crypto-trading/">Hong Kong</a>, optimism about crypto continues to grow. Some wealth managers are preparing to increase their exposure, with investments in cryptocurrencies expected to rise above 10%​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">​The launch of institutional-grade custody solutions is also encouraging more involvement, providing a sense of security and regulatory compliance for future investments​</p>
