<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Michael Saylor</a>, Executive Chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/record-high-for-microstrategy-stock-fueled-by-bitcoin-treasury/">MicroStrategy</a>, has <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/rumble-rises-founder-hints-buy-bitcoin?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expressed</a> interest in assisting Rumble Inc., a video-sharing platform, with integrating Bitcoin into its financial strategy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Rumble's CEO, Chris Pavlovski, <a href="https://x.com/chrispavlovski/status/1858943901983498298">recently conducted</a> a poll on X (formerly Twitter), inquiring whether the company should add Bitcoin to its balance sheet. The poll garnered significant support, with approximately 29,000 respondents voting in favor.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9805,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Following the poll, Saylor <a href="https://x.com/BitcoinMagazine/status/1858950474977972444">responded</a> affirmatively and offered to discuss the potential benefits and implementation strategies of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">adopting Bitcoin</a>. Pavlovski acknowledged Saylor's offer, indicating a willingness to explore the proposal further. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Rumble's consideration of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin adoption</a> reflects a broader movement among companies exploring cryptocurrency to diversify assets and hedge against traditional market volatility. </p>
