<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/19/hive-digital-prioritizes-efficiency-with-major-antminer-s19k-pro-investment/">HIVE Digital</a> Technologies Ltd. has <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/hive-digital-technologies-ltd.-announces-order-of-6500-canaan-avalon-a1566-asic-miners-and-groundbreaking-of-100-mw-in-paraguay-1033989767">acquired</a> 6,500 Canaan Avalon A1566 ASIC miners, each with a hashrate of 185 TH/s, totaling approximately 1.2 EH/s in additional <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/simple-mining-targets-125-mw-bitcoin-mining-hasrate-by-q1-2025/">mining capacity</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This strategic investment aims to enhance HIVE's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/gryphon-digital-leverages-methane-for-low-cost-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining operations</a> by replacing older, less efficient equipment, thereby increasing overall efficiency and output.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Avalon A1566 miners are known for their high performance and energy efficiency, operating at 185 TH/s with a power consumption of 3,420W, resulting in an efficiency of 18.5 J/TH.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This upgrade is expected to significantly boost HIVE's mining capabilities and profitability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to the hardware acquisition, HIVE has commenced construction of a 100 MW green energy facility in Paraguay, with plans to bring 30 MW online by early Q2 2025, further expanding their sustainable mining operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These developments underscore HIVE's commitment to enhancing its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">mining infrastructure</a> and operational efficiency in the competitive Bitcoin mining industry.</p>
