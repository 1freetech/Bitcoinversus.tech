<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Billionaire investor <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/09/billionaire-paul-tudor-jones-sees-promise-in-bitcoin-amid-economic-uncertainty/">Paul Tudor Jones</a> has significantly increased his fund's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/bhutans-bitcoin-holdings-soar-to-1-billion-with-help-of-hydroelectric-mining/">Bitcoin holdings</a> through BlackRock's iShares <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/proof-of-work-awards-meet-the-10-most-influential-bitcoin-leaders-in-2024-honorable-mention-included/">Bitcoin</a> Trust ETF. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://beincrypto.com/paul-tudor-jones-bitcoin-stake/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Recent filings</a> with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) reveal that Tudor Investment Corporation now holds over 4.4 million shares in the ETF, a substantial rise from the 869,565 shares reported in June. This expansion reflects Jones's growing confidence in Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation and economic uncertainty.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The value of Tudor Investment's <a href="https://watcher.guru/news/paul-tudor-jones-hedge-fund-holds-230m-worth-of-blackrocks-bitcoin-etf?utm_source=chatgpt.com">stake</a> in BlackRock's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin ETF</a> has surged alongside Bitcoin's price increase. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of September 30, the holdings were valued at approximately $160 million; with Bitcoin's recent rally, the current valuation stands at around $230 million. This strategic move aligns with Jones's previous endorsements of Bitcoin as a valuable asset in diversified portfolios.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">BlackRock's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/10/blackrocks-bitcoin-etf-surpasses-20-billion/">iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF</a> has experienced significant growth, attracting substantial institutional investments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The fund's assets under management have reached $33.1 billion, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BlackRock?utm_source=chatgpt.com">surpassing</a> BlackRock's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/12/blackrocks-bitcoin-etf-surpasses-gold-etf-in-assets-under-management/">iShares Gold Trust</a>. This milestone underscores the increasing acceptance of Bitcoin among traditional financial institutions and highlights the evolving landscape of digital asset investments.</p>
