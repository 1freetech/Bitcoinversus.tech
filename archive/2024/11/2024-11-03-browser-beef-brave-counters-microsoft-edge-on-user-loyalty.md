<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Brave and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/24/microsoft-to-vote-on-bitcoin-investment-proposal-in-december-meeting/">Microsoft</a> Edge have sparked a humorous social media rivalry, as seen in Brave's playful response to a recent post from the Microsoft Edge account. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The exchange began when Microsoft Edge posted an image, depicting a familiar pop culture character looking surprised, captioned, “When users come back after trying a new browser.” </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In an apparent jab at other browsers, Edge’s <a href="https://x.com/brave/status/1848472205870698902">tweet</a> implies users often return after experimenting with alternatives.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, Brave was quick to join the conversation, <a href="https://x.com/brave/status/1848472205870698902">retweeting</a> the image with a sharp reply:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">“They’re not switching back to Edge. They’re just downloading Brave on a new PC.” </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Brave’s response suggests that Edge users who return are merely using Edge as the default browser on new devices—only to promptly replace it with Brave.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
