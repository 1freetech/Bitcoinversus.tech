<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/28/smarter-together-u-s-china-breakthrough-in-graphene-semiconductor-technology/">Scientists</a> at the U.S. Department of Energy’s Brookhaven National Laboratory have <a href="https://newatlas.com/physics/gun-particle-accelerator-electrons/">reached</a> a new milestone with their development of the world's highest voltage electron gun. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This advancement is crucial to the Electron-Ion Collider (EIC), a groundbreaking project designed to explore the inner structures of atomic nuclei. Over a rigorous six-month test, the electron gun achieved unprecedented results, propelling electrons from zero to 500 million miles per hour in just two inches.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The EIC, housed at Brookhaven, is a 2.4-mile circumference circular collider aimed at producing high-speed collisions between electrons and ions. The goal of these collisions is to disintegrate protons and neutrons, unveiling insights into gluons, the particles that bind quarks within atomic nuclei. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Gluons exert the strongest known force in nature, yet their properties remain elusive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Brookhaven physicist Erdong Wang, the primary developer since 2017, stated, “We speed up the velocity of electrons to 80% the speed of light. That’s an acceleration from zero to over 500 million miles per hour in about two ten-billionths of a second.” </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This rapid acceleration over a short distance is essential for achieving the collision speeds needed for such intricate experiments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The successful testing of the electron gun signals a major step forward in achieving the EIC’s ambitious mission of unraveling the mysteries of atomic matter. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Understanding the forces at play within protons and neutrons could transform fundamental physics and deepen our knowledge of the universe’s most basic elements.</p>
