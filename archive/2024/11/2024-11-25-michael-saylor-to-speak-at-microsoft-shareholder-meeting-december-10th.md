<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Michael Saylor</a>, CEO of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/microstrategy-plans-2-billion-equity-offering-to-purchase-bitcoin/">MicroStrategy</a>, has publicly offered to assist Microsoft in developing a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/15/michael-saylor-advocates-for-bitcoin-as-a-store-of-value/">Bitcoin investment strategy</a>. <br><br>In a recent post on X (formerly Twitter), Saylor addressed Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, stating, "If you want to make the next trillion dollars for [Microsoft] shareholders, call me."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9473,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This proposal follows <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/24/microsoft-to-vote-on-bitcoin-investment-proposal-in-december-meeting/">Microsoft's announcement</a> of a shareholder vote scheduled for December 10, 2024, to assess the potential of investing in Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's board has <a href="https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/markets/microsoft-to-consider-bitcoin-investment-in-december-despite-board-opposition">recommended</a> against the proposal, citing existing investment strategies that already consider a range of assets, including cryptocurrencies.<br><br>Today, news has circulated online that suggests Michael Saylor will conduct some sort of presentation and the shareholder meeting December 10th, 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor's company, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/microstrategy-to-raise-700-million-for-bitcoin-acquisition/">MicroStrategy</a>, is <a href="https://cryptobriefing.com/microsoft-bitcoin-strategy-investment/">known</a> for its substantial <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/microstrategy-purchases-an-additional-27200-btc/">Bitcoin holdings</a>, totaling over 252,000 BTC. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This investment has significantly boosted MicroStrategy's stock performance, leading to a 313% outperformance over Microsoft's stock this year.</p>
