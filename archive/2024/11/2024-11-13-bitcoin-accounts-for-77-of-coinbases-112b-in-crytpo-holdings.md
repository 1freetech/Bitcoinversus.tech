<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">Coinbase</a>, one of the world’s largest <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> exchanges and custodial platforms, showcases an undeniable preference for Bitcoin among its users, as reflected in the sheer volume of Bitcoin under its custodial care. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With a total holding of $112.66 billion in assets, Coinbase’s portfolio is significantly shaped by Bitcoin, which alone accounts for $86.61 billion, translating to approximately 76.88% of the entire custodial value. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Founded in 2012, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/28/coinbase-secures-registration-with-bank-of-spain-boosts-bitcoin-momentum/">Coinbase</a> has grown from a simple platform to buy and sell Bitcoin into a full-fledged financial services company, offering a range of products from advanced trading features to secure asset custody. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As a regulated exchange based in the United States, Coinbase has built a reputation for security and compliance, which has attracted both retail and institutional investors. Its custodial services have particularly gained traction with large institutions and high-net-worth individuals who prefer the convenience and security that Coinbase provides.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The overwhelming presence of Bitcoin in Coinbase’s holdings highlights a trend that has become increasingly evident in the cryptocurrency market: Bitcoin remains the most trusted and sought-after asset. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Investors view Bitcoin as a "digital gold," a store of value amid economic uncertainty, which has driven its demand across <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/el-salvadors-bitcoin-investment-yields-over-525-million-in-profit/">multiple demographics</a>. Additionally, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/31/unpopular-opinion-michael-saylor-is-the-best-candidate-for-secretary-of-the-treasury/">Bitcoin's finite supply</a> and widespread adoption make it an attractive asset for those seeking long-term security in a rapidly evolving financial landscape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Coinbase’s significant Bitcoin holdings indicate not just a user preference but also the platform's positioning within the cryptocurrency ecosystem. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By holding such a vast amount of Bitcoin on behalf of its customers, Coinbase is reinforcing its image as a secure gateway into the world of digital assets, particularly for those interested in the stability and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">legacy of Bitcoin</a>.</p>
