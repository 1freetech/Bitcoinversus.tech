<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Anthony Pompliano, a prominent Bitcoin advocate and investor, has reignited debate over the idea of a United States <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin strategic reserve</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent post on X.com, Pompliano suggested that the United States print $250 billion and allocate 100% of it to purchasing Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10028,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-28-064033.png?w=585" alt="" class="wp-image-10028" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His reasoning centers on Bitcoin’s unique characteristics as a hedge against inflation and a potential safeguard for U.S. financial sovereignty.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Pompliano, Bitcoin has become the preferred balance sheet asset for companies like MicroStrategy, which has consistently demonstrated the cryptocurrency’s value in shielding against currency devaluation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4sIy5jBj8ng","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4sIy5jBj8ng
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He emphasized that Bitcoin’s decentralized structure and limited supply of 21 million coins make it an asset with long-term scarcity-driven value appreciation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pompliano also pointed out that other proposals for a U.S. Bitcoin reserve, such as Senator Cynthia Lummis’s legislation and RFK Jr.’s daily Bitcoin buying plan, do not go far enough. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He proposed a bolder approach—allocating the entire $250 billion to Bitcoin acquisition on the first day of Donald Trump’s hypothetical return to the presidency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pompliano <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/18/god-in-cyberspace-understanding-the-christian-reformation-and-the-bitcoin-revolution/">believes</a> that a strategic reserve of Bitcoin would solidify the nation’s financial footing while signaling leadership in a global financial transformation.</p>
