<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://ir.semlerscientific.com/news-releases/news-release-details/semler-scientificr-announces-btc-and-atm-activity-raised-215">Semler Scientific</a>, a healthcare technology firm, has <a href="https://ir.semlerscientific.com/news-releases/news-release-details/semler-scientificr-announces-btc-and-atm-activity-raised-215">expanded</a> its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">Bitcoin</a> holdings by purchasing an additional 215 bitcoins for $17.7 million between November 6 and November 15, 2024. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The acquisition was made at an average price of $82,502 per bitcoin, inclusive of fees and expenses.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Since adopting a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">Bitcoin treasury</a> strategy on May 28, 2024, Semler Scientific has accumulated a total of 1,273 bitcoins at an aggregate purchase price of $88.7 million, averaging $69,682 per bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company views <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/19/how-to-explain-bitcoin-as-digital-energy/">Bitcoin</a> as a reliable store of value and a compelling investment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://ir.semlerscientific.com/news-releases/news-release-details/semler-scientificr-announces-btc-and-atm-activity-raised-215?utm_source=chatgpt.com">The company</a> has introduced a key performance indicator (KPI) called "BTC Yield" to assess the performance of its Bitcoin acquisition strategy. From July 1, 2024, to November 15, 2024, Semler Scientific reported a BTC Yield of 37.3%, indicating substantial Bitcoin accretion for stakeholders.</p>
