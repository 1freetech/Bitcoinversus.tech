<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the demand for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">artificial intelligence</a> (AI) and cloud computing continues to surge, U.S. technology companies are increasingly competing with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/energy-demand-points-to-undervaluation-of-bitcoin-mining-companies/">bitcoin miners</a> for energy resources. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This competition has intensified as data centers drive the fastest U.S. power demand growth since the early 2000s, far outpacing the expansion of the power grid. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Major companies like Amazon and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/20/microsofts-ai-expert-jaron-lanier-advocates-for-data-dignity-amid-bitcoin-revolution/">Microsoft</a> are aggressively securing energy assets, often targeting those held by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">bitcoin miners</a> to fuel their growing AI infrastructure needs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The energy consumption of data centers is <a href="https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/24/08/40599343/amazon-microsoft-and-other-us-tech-giants-compete-with-bitcoin-miners-over-scarce-energy-s?utm_source=snapi">projected</a> to more than double by the end of the decade, potentially accounting for 9% of total U.S. electricity usage. In contrast, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">bitcoin mining</a> currently represents approximately 0.4% of global electricity consumption. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With the increasing competition, some bitcoin miners are pivoting to AI and cloud services to maximize their energy assets. Analysts estimate that up to 20% of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">bitcoin mining power capacity</a> could transition to AI by 2027.</p>
