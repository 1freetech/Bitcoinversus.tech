<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/12/u-s-homebuyer-age-hits-record-56/">median price of new homes</a> in the United States, when measured in Bitcoin, has experienced a significant decline over the past decade. In 2012, purchasing a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/07/crucial-graphic-highlights-dollar-depreciation-relative-to-real-estate-and-bitcoin/">median-priced new home</a> required approximately 50,616 BTC. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By 2024, this figure had dropped to just 5 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/03/fidelitys-fbtc-fund-now-holds-184219-btc/">BTC</a>. This <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/currencies/bitcoin-versus-us-home-prices-performed-5-years-crypto-housing-2021-12">trend</a> reflects Bitcoin's substantial appreciation against the U.S. dollar, outpacing the rise in home prices. For instance, in 2021, the median sales price for new houses in the U.S. was $404,700, while Bitcoin's value surged from around $998 in 2017 to $61,900 in 2021.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The data <a href="https://www.pricedinbitcoin21.com/chart/consumer-goods/MSPNHSUS">indicates</a> that as Bitcoin's value increased, fewer BTC were needed to purchase a median-priced new home. In 2013, approximately 19,127 BTC were required, decreasing to 351 BTC in 2014, and further down to 10 BTC in 2021. This trend underscores Bitcoin's rapid appreciation relative to the U.S. housing market</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite <a href="https://inflationchart.com/btc-in-home">fluctuations</a> in both Bitcoin's value and U.S. home prices, the overall trajectory shows a consistent decrease in the number of Bitcoins needed to buy a median-priced new home. This pattern highlights the cryptocurrency's growing purchasing power over traditional assets like real estate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Analysts <a href="https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/article/crypto-went-mainstream-drove-up-housing-prices">suggest</a> that Bitcoin's limited supply and increasing adoption have contributed to its value surge, making significant purchases, such as homes, more accessible for those holding the cryptocurrency. However, the volatility inherent in digital currencies necessitates careful consideration for potential buyers and investors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
