<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In July 2024, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/26/germany-debuts-official-ai-tour-guide-emma-for-tourism-campaign/">German government</a> sold approximately 50,000 Bitcoin at an average price of $54,000 per coin, totaling around $2.7 billion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By November 12, 2024, Bitcoin's price had <a href="https://decrypt.co/236329/germany-begins-selling-bitcoin-billions-volatility-fears">surged</a> to approximately $88,000, significantly higher than the sale price.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Had the government retained its holdings until November, the value would have been approximately $4.4 billion, indicating a missed profit opportunity of about $1.7 billion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The decision to sell was influenced by legal and financial considerations, including asset seizure protocols and market volatility concerns.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Critics argue that holding the assets longer could have yielded greater returns, while others support the sale as a prudent move to mitigate risks associated with cryptocurrency volatility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The sale's impact on the market was notable, contributing to short-term price fluctuations and sparking discussions on governmental roles in cryptocurrency markets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This event underscores the complexities governments face in managing digital assets and the challenges in timing market transactions for optimal financial outcomes.</p>
