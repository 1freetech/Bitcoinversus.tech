<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.aol.com/fda-employees-consider-handle-rfk-174914918.html">Reports</a> indicate that some employees at the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) are contemplating resignation in response to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/20/kennedys-bitcoin-revolution-a-new-era-for-the-u-s-dollar/">Robert F. Kennedy Jr</a>.'s anticipated role in President-elect Donald Trump's administration. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Kennedy, known for his critical views on vaccines and the pharmaceutical industry, has expressed intentions to overhaul the FDA, including the potential elimination of entire departments he deems ineffective.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://www.econotimes.com/Mass-Exodus-Looms-FDA-Employees-Consider-Quitting-as-Robert-F-Kennedy-Jr-Joins-Trumps-Administration-1693973">development</a> has raised concerns among FDA staff about the future direction of the agency and its commitment to science-based decision-making. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The potential for significant policy shifts under Kennedy's leadership has led to discussions among employees about the possibility of a "mass exodus" from the agency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The situation underscores the tension between proposed administrative changes and the existing framework of federal health agencies, highlighting the challenges in balancing reform initiatives with the preservation of established scientific standards.</p>
