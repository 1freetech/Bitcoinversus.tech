<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In March 2024, the average global electricity price <a href="https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/electricity_prices/">stood</a> at USD 0.162 per <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/ethiopia-hosts-groundbreaking-bitcoin-mining-summit-reinforces-role-as-africas-leading-mining-hub/">kilowatt-hour</a> (kWh) for residential users and USD 0.181 per kWh for businesses. Europe <a href="https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/electricity_prices/">reported</a> the highest residential electricity prices at USD 0.228 per kWh, while Asia had the lowest at USD 0.082 per kWh.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The International Energy Agency (IEA) <a href="https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2024/executive-summary">forecasts</a> a 3.4% annual increase in global <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">electricity demand</a> through 2026, driven by economic growth and the electrification of residential and transport sectors. Emerging economies, particularly <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/05/rumors-suggest-china-could-reconsider-crypto-ban/">China</a> and India, are expected to contribute significantly to this surge.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8747,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/sdge-recorded-and-forecasted-electric-rates.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-8747" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Technological advancements, notably in artificial intelligence and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">data centers</a>, are <a href="https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2024/executive-summary">projected</a> to double electricity consumption by 2026. Data centers alone could consume over 1,000 terawatt-hours, equivalent to Japan's current electricity usage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8751,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Falling fossil fuel prices are anticipated to reduce electricity costs globally. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, prices may remain above historical averages due to persistent inflationary pressures. Europe <a href="https://www.fitchsolutions.com/bmi/power-renewables/global-electricity-prices-falling-fuel-prices-weigh-electricity-prices-24-05-2024">continues</a> to experience some of the highest electricity prices worldwide.</p>
