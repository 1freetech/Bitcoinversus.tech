<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">President-elect <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/20/trumps-world-liberty-token-sale-faces-rocky-launch/">Donald Trump</a> is moving forward with plans to establish a "<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/21/hackers-use-mcdonalds-instagram-for-grimace-crypto-scam/">crypto</a> advisory council" aimed at reshaping U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/elon-musk-endorses-howard-lutnick-for-u-s-treasury-secretary/">cryptocurrency policy</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Major industry players, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ripple-ceo-predicts-inevitable-approval-of-xrp-etf/">Ripple</a>, Kraken, Circle, Paradigm, and Andreessen Horowitz's crypto arm a16z, are actively seeking positions on this council, which is expected to operate under the National Economic Council or a similar White House entity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The council's responsibilities will encompass advising on digital asset policy, legislation, and coordination among regulatory bodies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In line with his campaign promises, Trump has <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/cryptoverse-trumps-bitcoin-stockpile-plan-stirs-debate-2024-08-06/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">proposed</a> the creation of a national bitcoin reserve. This initiative involves retaining all bitcoins currently held by the U.S. government, estimated at approximately $11.1 billion, including 203,239 bitcoin tokens from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/02/marine-veteran-fights-to-end-civil-forfeiture-after-87000-seized-by-police/">criminal seizures</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The proposal has sparked debate among experts regarding its potential impact on bitcoin's price and market dynamics, as it could reduce the number of tokens available for trading.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The anticipation of a more crypto-friendly regulatory environment under the incoming administration has contributed to a significant surge in bitcoin's value. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The cryptocurrency recently surpassed $96,000 for the first time, reflecting investor optimism about supportive policies and the establishment of a national bitcoin reserve. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/bitcoin-breaks-95000-first-time-optimism-over-trump-crypto-plans-2024-11-21/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">substantial investment</a>s from companies like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/microstrategy-to-raise-700-million-for-bitcoin-acquisition/">MicroStrategy</a>, which plans to purchase $42 billion more in bitcoin, have further bolstered the market.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While the crypto industry views these developments as a positive shift, some consumer groups express concerns about the potential for the industry to influence its own regulatory framework. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Proponents a<a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/crypto-industry-jockeys-seats-trumps-promised-council-2024-11-21/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">rgue</a> that involving industry experts is crucial for strategic policy development, whereas critics caution against the risks of self-regulation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The establishment of the crypto advisory council and the national bitcoin reserve will likely play pivotal roles in shaping the future of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> regulation in the United States.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
