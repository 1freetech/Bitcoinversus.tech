<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><br>A recent <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959652624029652?via%3Dihub">peer-reviewed study</a> published in the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959652624029652?via%3Dihub">Journal of Cleaner Production</a> explores a groundbreaking approach to reducing methane emissions through the integration of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/23/nodal-power-turns-landfill-methane-into-green-energy-for-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining with landfill gas-to-energy</a> (LFGTE) projects. This <a href="https://www.murrayrudd.pro/an-integrated-landfill-gas-to-energy-and-bitcoin-mining-framework/">novel method</a> leverages the energy produced by methane, a greenhouse gas 28 times more potent than carbon dioxide, emitted from landfills to power Bitcoin mining operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The study demonstrates that this integration not only helps mitigate methane emissions but also proves economically viable for both the environment and the mining industry.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/10/transforming-waste-to-watts-marathon-digitals-eco-friendly-bitcoin-mining-project/">Methane</a>, a significant contributor to global warming, is often released from landfills and can be captured through LFGTE systems. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The research highlights that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/energy-demand-points-to-undervaluation-of-bitcoin-mining-companies/">Bitcoin mining</a> offers the ideal incentive structure for such capital-intensive projects, providing sustainable revenue generation where traditional enterprises may fall short. By utilizing this methane, the study estimates significant reductions in global methane emissions, contributing positively to environmental sustainability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Moreover, the economic benefits of this integration are substantial. The study used a Monte Carlo simulation model to evaluate the financial viability, revealing that even a small landfill could reduce methane emissions by over 2,000 tons, equivalent to more than 61,200 tons of CO2. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This reduction could yield environmental credits valued at approximately $9.18 million, making it a financially attractive proposition for both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin miners</a> and landfill operators.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The research further suggests that Bitcoin’s economic incentives could drive similar technologies in other sectors, including orphaned oil wells, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/16/bitcoin-mining-finds-a-friend-in-u-s-renewable-energy/">wastewater treatment plants</a>, and agricultural processing operations, expanding the potential for methane mitigation across various industries.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The integration of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining</a> with LFGTE projects presents a scalable, innovative solution for reducing methane emissions while generating economic value, positioning Bitcoin mining as a tool for environmental sustainability.</p>
