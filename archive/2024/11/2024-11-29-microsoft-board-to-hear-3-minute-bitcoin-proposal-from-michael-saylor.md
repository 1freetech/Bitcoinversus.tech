<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/michael-saylor-declares-bitcoin-a-100-solution-amid-price-rebound/">Michael Saylor</a>, co-founder and executive chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/record-high-for-microstrategy-stock-fueled-by-bitcoin-treasury/">MicroStrategy</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinist.com/michael-saylor-bitcoin-microsoft-board/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">is scheduled</a> to present a Bitcoin investment strategy to Microsoft's board of directors before December 10.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor, a prominent advocate for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">Bitcoin adoption</a>, has publicly <a href="https://decrypt.co/288347/microsoft-trillion-dollars-bitcoin-michael-saylor?utm_source=chatgpt.com">encouraged</a> Microsoft to integrate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">Bitcoin</a> into its financial strategy, suggesting it could significantly enhance shareholder value.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent X Spaces discussion hosted by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/vanecks-matthew-sigel-foresees-bitcoin-hitting-180000/">VanEck</a>, Saylor confirmed his agreement to deliver a concise, three-minute presentation to Microsoft's leadership.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Microsoft's board has <a href="https://cryptobriefing.com/michael-saylor-bitcoin-investment-microsoft/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">previously expressed</a> a focus on stability and risk minimization, evaluating various assets, including Bitcoin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The upcoming <a href="https://decrypt.co/288347/microsoft-trillion-dollars-bitcoin-michael-saylor?utm_source=chatgpt.com">presentation</a> by Saylor aims to provide insights into the potential benefits of incorporating <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">Bitcoin</a> into Microsoft's treasury, aligning with his belief that such a move could substantially increase the company's market value.</p>
