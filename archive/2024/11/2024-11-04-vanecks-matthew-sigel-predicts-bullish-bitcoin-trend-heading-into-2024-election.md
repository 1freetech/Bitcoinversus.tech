<p style="text-transform:none">VanEck’s head of digital assets research, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/07/intel-vaneck-releases-major-prediction-for-bitcoin-price/">Matthew Sigel</a>, expressed optimism regarding Bitcoin’s potential in the lead-up to the upcoming U.S. presidential election. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During an interview on CNBC’s Squawk Box, Sigel highlighted market trends that suggest an advantageous position for the cryptocurrency. Sigel pointed out that Bitcoin’s current setup may pave the way for significant gains as the 2024 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/15/bitcoin-vs-politics-the-2024-elections-will-be-a-decisive-moment-for-bitcoin-spot-etfs/">Presidential election</a> approaches.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Sigel suggested that potential political outcomes are playing a key role in shaping Bitcoin’s market outlook. He emphasized that the possibility of a victory by Republican candidate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Donald Trump</a> could accelerate positive momentum for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/the-evolution-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Trump’s track record includes policies perceived as more favorable to the crypto industry, which could attract investor confidence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Contrastingly, Sigel noted that Vice President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/culture-president-barack-obama-raps-eminem-lyrics-at-kamala-harris-presidential-rally-in-detroit/">Kamala Harris</a>, the Democratic candidate, has yet to address cryptocurrency directly. This absence of a clear stance from Harris raises questions about her understanding of the digital asset space. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Market participants are watching closely as the political climate shifts in anticipation of election outcomes that could impact <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/italy-to-raise-bitcoin-capital-gains-tax-to-42/">cryptocurrency policies</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Analysts in the sector are aligning with the notion that the election’s outcome could significantly impact Bitcoin’s trajectory. A win for Trump may align with increased adoption and positive regulatory measures for digital assets, adding to the bullish narrative for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/31/illegal-bitcoin-mine-raided-in-thailand/">Bitcoin</a>.</p>
