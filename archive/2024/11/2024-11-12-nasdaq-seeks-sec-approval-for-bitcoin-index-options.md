<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nasdaq has officially filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) to list and trade <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/28/beef-gets-pricier-with-dollars-cheaper-with-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> Index Options, marking a significant step toward expanding the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/13/marathon-digital-invests-100-million-in-bitcoin/">cryptocurrency market</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If approved, these options will allow institutional and retail investors to hedge their Bitcoin exposure and manage risks more effectively, following the CME CF Bitcoin Real-Time Index​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/nasdaq-bitcoin-index-options-sec-approval">The move</a> aligns Nasdaq with its competitor, the New York Stock Exchange, which also aims to offer Bitcoin-related derivatives. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The options will be cash-settled and provide an alternative tool for investors, enabling them to leverage their positions in the volatile <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/bitcoin-market-dominance-hits-new-cycle-high/">cryptocurrency market</a>. According to Greg Ferrari, Vice President and Head of Exchange Business Management at Nasdaq, this development will contribute to the maturation of digital assets.</p>
