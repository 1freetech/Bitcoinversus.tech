<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/13/marathon-digital-invests-100-million-in-bitcoin/">MARA Holdings</a>, Inc. has <a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1378/mara-holdings-inc-announces-pricing-of-oversubscribed-and-upsized-offering-of-zero-coupon-convertible-senior-notes-due-2030">successfully acquired</a> 5,771 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/20/genius-group-invests-10-million-in-bitcoin-launches-bitcoin-treasury-strategy/">bitcoin</a> at an average price of $95,554 per BTC, utilizing funds from its recent zero-coupon <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/microstrategy-to-raise-700-million-for-bitcoin-acquisition/">convertible note offering</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This strategic acquisition increases the company's total <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/bitcoin-accounts-for-77-of-coinbases-112b-in-crytpo-holdings/">bitcoin holdings</a> to approximately 33,875 BTC, currently valued at $3.4 billion based on a spot price of $99,000 per BTC.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The convertible note offering, initially set at $700 million, <a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1378/mara-holdings-inc-announces-pricing-of-oversubscribed-and-upsized-offering-of-zero-coupon-convertible-senior-notes-due-2030?utm_source=chatgpt.com">was upsized</a> to $850 million due to strong investor demand. The notes, maturing on March 1, 2030, are convertible into cash, shares of MARA's common stock, or a combination thereof, at the company's discretion. Proceeds from the offering are earmarked for bitcoin acquisitions and repurchasing existing convertible notes due in 2026.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MARA's strategic move reflects a growing trend among corporations to incorporate bitcoin into their balance sheets, aiming to leverage the cryptocurrency's potential for value appreciation. The company's substantial bitcoin holdings position it as a significant player in the digital asset space, aligning with its broader strategy to support the energy transformation through digital asset compute.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1378/mara-holdings-inc-announces-pricing-of-oversubscribed-and-upsized-offering-of-zero-coupon-convertible-senior-notes-due-2030?utm_source=chatgpt.com">acquisition</a> also results in a 35% bitcoin yield per share, enhancing shareholder value and demonstrating MARA's commitment to integrating digital assets into its financial strategy. This approach underscores the company's confidence in bitcoin's long-term prospects and its role in the evolving financial landscape.</p>
