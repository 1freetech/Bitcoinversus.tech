<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">a solo <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Bitcoin miner</a> has successfully mined a block worth 3.275 BTC, equivalent to $199,098. The miner achieved this feat by solving block number 858,978, a rare occurrence in the increasingly competitive world of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/15/pennsylvania-greenlights-bitcoin-mining-with-revised-bill/">Bitcoin mining</a>. The block, containing 2,391 transactions, was mined through ckpool, a popular solo mining pool.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The success of this <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/01/bitcoin-miner-defi-django-reveals-modifications-for-airflow-optimization/">solo miner</a> is a significant example of decentralization in action. Despite the dominance of large mining pools, this event proves that individual miners can still achieve substantial rewards, albeit rarely. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2024/08/30/bitcoin-mining-news-a-solitary-individual-earns-199098-with-hashrate-below-700eh-s/">event</a> comes at a time when Bitcoin's overall hashrate has seen fluctuations, with August recording levels below 700 EH/s. The current hashrate reflects the impact of the recent <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/23/the-bitcoin-halving-is-otw-heres-what-you-need-to-know/">Bitcoin halving</a>, which caused a notable decrease in mining activity and a subsequent drop in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/04/bitcoins-september-challenge-benjamin-cohen-discusses-bearish-data-trends/">Bitcoin's price</a>. </p>
