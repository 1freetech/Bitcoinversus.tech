<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Riot Platforms Inc. is approaching the <a href="https://www.riotplatforms.com/bitcoin-mining/corsicana/">completion</a> of Phase 1 of its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">Bitcoin mining facility</a> in Corsicana, Texas, which will add 400 megawatts (MW) of capacity. Upon full development, the facility is expected to reach a total capacity of 1 gigawatt (GW), positioning it as one of the largest <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/hut-8-corp-enhances-mining-fleet-with-bitmain-s21-acquisition/">Bitcoin mining operations</a> globally.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Corsicana facility's development began with the energization of its substation in April 2024, marking a significant milestone in Riot's expansion efforts. The initial phase includes four 100 MW buildings utilizing immersion cooling technology, enhancing operational efficiency and sustainability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Riot's investment in Corsicana is projected to create approximately 270 jobs, contributing positively to the local economy. The company has emphasized its commitment to fostering community partnerships and delivering economic benefits to Navarro County.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) has <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/10/19/2537494/0/en/Riot-Announces-Groundbreaking-at-1-GW-Data-Center-Facility-in-Corsicana-Navarro-County-Texas.html">approved</a> the facility's total capacity of 1 GW, marking a first for the council. This approval underscores Riot's collaborative approach and its dedication to transparent and responsible development within the energy sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Riot's expansion in Corsicana aligns with its broader strategy to enhance <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/natural-gas-finds-new-value-in-bitcoin-mining-operations/">Bitcoin mining operations</a> across Texas. The company also operates a 700 MW facility in Rockdale, Texas, further solidifying its position as a leader in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">cryptocurrency mining industry</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Corsicana project has garnered <a href="https://www.riotplatforms.com/bitcoin-mining/corsicana/">attention</a> for its scale and potential impact on the Bitcoin mining landscape. Riot's advancements in immersion cooling technology and large-scale operations reflect ongoing innovation and growth within the sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the facility nears the completion of its first phase, Riot continues to focus on operational excellence and community engagement. The company's efforts in Corsicana are anticipated to set new benchmarks for efficiency and capacity in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a>.</p>
