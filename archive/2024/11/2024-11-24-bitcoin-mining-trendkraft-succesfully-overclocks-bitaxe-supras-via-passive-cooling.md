<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the same thread, he compared passive setups to actively cooled Supras, sharing specifics:</p>

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}},"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size" style="text-transform:none">"The same Supras in the Swarm with the same settings are actively cooled with Noctua NF-A4x20 5V PWM and Ice Cooler Tower and get around 63°C warm at 29°C in the office. 139.5 grams of pure happiness made from 100% copper. It's a Northbridge cooler from 2011."</p>
