<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/23/federal-reserve-chair-jerome-powell-signals-shift-in-monetary-policy-at-jackson-hole/">Federal Reserve</a> has <a href="https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/federal-reserve-cuts-interest-rates-by-a-quarter-point/5964313/">reduced</a> its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points, adjusting the federal funds rate to a target range of 4.50% to 4.75%.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This decision follows a previous 50 basis point cut in September, reflecting the central bank's ongoing efforts to balance economic growth with inflation control.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Recent economic indicators suggest a solid expansion, though job gains have slowed, and the unemployment rate has risen slightly, remaining low overall.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Inflation has progressed toward the Federal Reserve's 2% objective but remains somewhat elevated, prompting the rate adjustment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Analysts <a href="https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2024-10-29/federal-reserve-to-cut-rates-by-25-basis-points-at-next-two-meetings-reuters-poll">anticipate </a>that the Federal Reserve will implement additional 25 basis point cuts in its upcoming meetings to further support economic stability.</p>
