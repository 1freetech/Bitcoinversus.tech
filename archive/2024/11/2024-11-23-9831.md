<p style="text-transform:none">CoreSite Realty Corporation has achieved a significant milestone at its VA3 data center in Reston, Virginia, with the completion of a new 38,000-square-foot, 8-megawatt data hall suite. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20191014005106/en/CoreSite-Celebrates-VA3-Data-Center-Grand-Opening?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expansion underscores</a> CoreSite's commitment to providing scalable and resilient colocation capacity in Fairfax County. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cANPuTcas","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The project was executed in collaboration with DPR Construction, a partner known for delivering high-quality data center facilities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The newly upgraded data hall <a href="https://www.coresite.com/data-center/va3-reston-va?utm_source=chatgpt.com">features</a> advanced structural reinforcements, a new roof, two generators, and a complete interior fit-out. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These enhancements are designed to support high-performance capabilities, including 400G AWS Direct Connect cloud access, catering to the growing demand for robust and efficient data center services.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CoreSite's VA3 <a href="https://www.coresite.com/news/coresites-va3-data-center-powered-100-renewable-energy?utm_source=chatgpt.com">facility</a> is part of the company's Northern Virginia campus, which includes VA1 and VA2 data centers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=54cANPuTcas","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The campus offers over 100 megawatts of capacity at full build-out, providing direct, low-latency access to major cloud service providers. This interconnected ecosystem enables businesses to deploy hybrid and multi-cloud solutions effectively.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The expansion aligns with CoreSite's ongoing efforts to support digital transformation initiatives by offering scalable infrastructure and advanced connectivity options. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By enhancing the VA3 facility, CoreSite continues to meet the evolving needs of enterprises seeking high-performance data center solutions in the Northern Virginia region.</p>
