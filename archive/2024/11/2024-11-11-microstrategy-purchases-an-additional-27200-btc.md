<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/blackrock-now-owns-5-2-of-microstrategy/">MicroStrategy</a>, under the leadership of Executive Chairman <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/01/michael-saylor-faces-backlash-over-bitcoin-self-custody-stance/">Michael Saylor</a>, has expanded its Bitcoin holdings by <a href="https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/24/11/41878981/microstrategy-acquires-another-27200-bitcoin-worth-2-billion">acquiring</a> an additional 27,200 BTC for approximately $2.03 billion. This purchase, completed at an average price of $74,463 per Bitcoin, brings the company's total holdings to 279,420 BTC, acquired at an aggregate cost of about $11.9 billion, averaging $42,692 per <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/03/fidelitys-fbtc-fund-now-holds-184219-btc/">Bitcoin</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The acquisition was financed through the sale of 7,854,647 shares under agreements with financial institutions, including TD Securities and Barclays Capital. This strategy aligns with MicroStrategy's ongoing approach to leverage equity sales for Bitcoin accumulation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In October, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/engineer-mark-harvey-explains-math-behind-microstrategy-3-year-bitcoin-plan/">MicroStrategy</a> announced plans to raise $42 billion over the next three years to purchase additional Bitcoin, aiming to enhance shareholder value through the digital transformation of capital.</p>
