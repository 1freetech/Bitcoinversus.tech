<ol class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word count: needs to be 250 words or more. We encourage the use of AI (chat gpt, microsoft co pilot). to help curate stories. I have a <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/10Kmb1dYmWcozB9gqqKYFvT7ZZOzpgRSYxu9aSSjoV1g/edit">template</a> available to put into the AI terminal (you can also create your own <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/10Kmb1dYmWcozB9gqqKYFvT7ZZOzpgRSYxu9aSSjoV1g/edit">template</a>!)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>To add a new post click on the “NEW” icon at the top left and corner of the dashboard.<br><img width="446" height="33" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcPkctL-UTfIQqFosAXexRzMzQfljUgHLMB_BHCw5ZyZxmKGAdybqJn3J0O3Ht7VgGBiHnhvt7ii9oOPbFSayaA9gZdh2NzDpTm9BO2z9Q_L_j0i8MQtkUdAx70gCtCjzMXs9uU6JDsdNqFOhudqJoEsD5g?key=OeJ_M_23umEmXNyHykENfQ"></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"start":3} -->
<ol start="3" class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Relevant vidoes/posts for stories</strong> - add youtube and/or twitter posts to the news story to further verify your tech news story. Do this by clicking on the <strong><em>blue</em></strong> “+” sign.  You must add one relevant social media source to the story. If the embed doesn't work, screen shot the post and post the screen shot instead. </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>

<li><a href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn/Common_questions/Web_mechanics/What_are_hyperlinks">Hyperlinks</a>! <img width="52" height="34" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfcNcCrHauzyevQWe_h02Tb8zMH5Kciua7NsLHOxB_EEo8vbwqpIPnVlBSwq61X_thaYpa4DtwkoXKO50eQqj7DUKoSBky3KY7mwopLbpAjFmSJHzKjyTVax2UZR7S6T0TapyQwVnXA2STPzi5YRucgJ4U?key=OeJ_M_23umEmXNyHykENfQ">We want you to hyperlink 2 previous relevant stories from the bitcoinversus.tech website and one outside source. For instance if it’s a story about Microstrategy. Highlight the word microstrategy, then search for and hyperlink a previous story that was previously written on the bitcoin versus platform. Keywords, companies, and assets should be hyperlinked to Bitcoinversus.tech stories. </li>

<p style="text-transform:none">Keywords like “bitcoin” or “crypto” or “Blackrock” or “Satoshi Nakomoto”. Stories from a 3rd party should be hyperlinked into non key words like “reported” or “launched” etc. if you cannot find a story to hyperlink your word to, that’s okay just complete the story.<img src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcFQc4Joplr2rDo5Gb65NQYhUJOX9U1JxzdY9XS0h7D-3Gt3VhoIYVsiK48kdheYRcPXnNpVAjhnsyRZLEAwNKmFDg_TTNmJGIEfIcjwzuhWK8GknDHF60lS4kQuzAd6DF90cSYMw_sfiHmlmz9soP7BeKy?key=OeJ_M_23umEmXNyHykENfQ" width="267" height="219"><img src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdouV3NmHMnDr7IMY3vmU9G7a5xNdxcljI2pquo7ncShwhHDhyNXyILdK0gK0TOLNPGsmEA417Q1qd2jVaLtLcQ0Zyp3i_xGMFaAtMVct2BnJmBc0ixwyfOdS_DPcNjjRwxKlQiGUFEGFVuJtLgfGLpYmP7?key=OeJ_M_23umEmXNyHykENfQ" width="261" height="224"></p>

<ol start="7" class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Please make sure you put the appropriate tag. We have 5 main subjects that we utilize for categorizing the story 1.Intel (exclusive story or relatively unknown story 2. Trending News (all trending bitcoin/tech news) 3. Bitcoin Mining 4. Sports and 4.Culture (Music, Entertainment, Celebrities). Your news story/opinion piece may not fit neatly into the main categories but please highlight whichever one is the most relevant to your story. If a new category needs to be added, please add a new category. <img width="234" height="315" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcGUxVXYrsgeLymTehoWn5niuJBEuvqm4qOzWAdbdaB3eGwsqEfO9xar0NLZVKsOjkN8ItHVVLGGoi-7qrrNbIzRkadBvcHteTMOtN9v0aFC2rkDmywA5B_RFcKp9fdG5chVwCKCtRP3gQjVz4yZS_vaaWp?key=OeJ_M_23umEmXNyHykENfQ"><img width="170" height="236" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdHRMbMrGC0eXwPpCiicsCpqu4jYA9DRLFe8TK0Wqc2AlboQPc6XErgIwoeSLtTi-E_pdGn0OklsrehciFiFtCGZoWyM8A-RXnPbeBhoLZojDvIYI3iKlOHRVoV1FxGXOPeQiVF01Uir0Jmnfyxj3TeIr41?key=OeJ_M_23umEmXNyHykENfQ"></li>
