<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent critique, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/21/galaxy-digital-ceo-mike-novogratz-predicts-500k-bitcoin-with-u-s-reserve-establishment/">Bitcoin</a> advocate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/sports-max-keiser-to-host-bitcoin-golf-event-in-january-2025/">Max Keiser</a> questioned Warren Buffett's relevance in the evolving financial landscape, highlighting the cryptocurrency's ascent over traditional investment vehicles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In recent <a href="https://x.com/maxkeiser/status/1858659898524905586">post</a> Keiser remarked, <br>"Does anyone remeber Warrne Buffet?"</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9744,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-22-135300.png?w=592" alt="" class="wp-image-9744" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin's market capitalization has recently surpassed that of Berkshire Hathaway, the conglomerate led by Buffett. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of November 22, 2024, Bitcoin's market cap stands at approximately $1.9 trillion, while Berkshire Hathaway's valuation is around $1 trillion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This milestone reflects Bitcoin's growing acceptance and the increasing interest in digital assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Buffett has <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Buffett?utm_source=chatgpt.com">historically</a> expressed skepticism toward Bitcoin, famously labeling it "rat poison squared" in 2018. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Despite his reservations, the cryptocurrency's performance has outpaced many traditional investments, prompting discussions about the future of finance and the role of digital currencies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Keiser's comments and Bitcoin's market performance highlight a broader trend of digital assets gaining prominence. Investors are increasingly considering cryptocurrencies as viable components of diversified portfolios, signaling a potential shift in investment strategies.</p>
