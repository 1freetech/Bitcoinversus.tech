<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The traditional <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/14/this-1000-day-bitcoin-trading-strategy-changes-everything/">four-year Bitcoin cycle</a>, characterized by predictable boom and bust phases, is undergoing significant transformation due to increased <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/teslas-bitcoin-holdings-reach-1b-amid-market-surge/">adoption by corporations</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/bhutans-bitcoin-holdings-soar-to-1-billion-with-help-of-hydroelectric-mining/">nation-states</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9173,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-22.png?w=594" alt="" class="wp-image-9173" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mark Harvey, a prominent engineer and cryptocurrency analyst, asserts that this cycle is now obsolete, citing several key developments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/blackrock-now-owns-5-2-of-microstrategy/">MicroStrategy Inc</a>. (MSTR) has been at the forefront of corporate Bitcoin acquisition. The company has <a href="https://www.fidelitydigitalassets.com/research-and-insights/adding-bitcoin-corporate-treasury">consistently</a> purchased substantial amounts of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/bitcoin-bull-nba-legend-scottie-pippen-draws-parallels-between-bitcoin-and-bulls-dynasty/">Bitcoin</a>, effectively absorbing a significant portion of new supply. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This strategy has inspired other corporations to follow suit, further increasing demand and reducing market volatility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nation-states are also entering the Bitcoin market. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">El Salvador</a>, under President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/13/el-salvadors-bitcoin-investment-yields-over-525-million-in-profit/">Nayib Bukele</a>, became the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender in 2021. The nation has since mined approximately 474 bitcoins using <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">geothermal energy</a>, adding to its state holdings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">Senator Cynthia Lummis</a> introduced legislation proposing the establishment of a strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin reserve</a>, aiming to acquire up to 1 million bitcoins over a set period.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The approval of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/florida-cfo-proposes-bitcoin-investment-for-state-pension-funds/">Bitcoin Exchange-Traded Funds</a> (ETFs) has facilitated greater investment from institutional investors, including baby boomers. These ETFs provide a regulated and accessible avenue for investing in Bitcoin, contributing to increased market stability and liquidity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The recent re-election of pro-crypto U.S. President Donald Trump has <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/bitcoin-surges-record-high-trump-bets-2024-11-11/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">further increased</a> confidence in the cryptocurrency market. His administration's favorable stance towards digital assets is expected to encourage broader adoption and integration of Bitcoin into the financial system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These developments collectively suggest a departure from the traditional Bitcoin market cycles, indicating a more sustained and stable growth trajectory for the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a>.</p>
