<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/01/michael-saylor-faces-backlash-over-bitcoin-self-custody-stance/">Bitcoin</a> surged to a new all-time high of $77,000 on Friday, reflecting heightened investor enthusiasm following <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump</a>'s recent election victory and <a href="https://www.econotimes.com/Trump-Win-Drives-Bitcoin-to-Record-77K-as-Spot-ETF-Inflows-Soar-Investors-Bet-on-Fed-Cut-1693287">his commitment</a> to fostering a more <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/alliance-bernstein-calls-bitcoin-at-200000-in-2025-conservative/">cryptocurrency</a>-friendly administration.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The influx of capital into spot <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin exchange-traded funds</a> (ETFs) on Wednesday and Thursday significantly contributed to this price escalation. These ETFs, which directly hold <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/state-backed-bitcoin-mining-expands-in-uae-argentina-and-ethiopia/">Bitcoin</a>, have been instrumental in driving the cryptocurrency's value to unprecedented levels earlier this year.</p>
