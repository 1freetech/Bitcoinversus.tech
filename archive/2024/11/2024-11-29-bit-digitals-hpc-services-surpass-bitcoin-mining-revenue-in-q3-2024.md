<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bit Digital, Inc., a New York-based company, has <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-11-19/bit-digital-hpc-bitcoin-mining/">reported</a> a significant shift in its revenue streams, with high-performance computing (HPC) services surpassing Bitcoin mining for the first time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the third quarter of 2024, the company generated $12.2 million from HPC services, compared to $10.1 million from Bitcoin mining.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20241118cn58547/bit-digital-inc-announces-third-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2024-financial-results?utm_source=chatgpt.com">The decline</a> in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/jack-dorseys-block-inc-expands-bitcoin-mining-operations/">Bitcoin mining revenue</a>, down 11% from the previous year, is attributed to the April 2024 Bitcoin halving event, which reduced block rewards, and increased network difficulty.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In contrast, the HPC segment <a href="https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20241118cn58547/bit-digital-inc-announces-third-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2024-financial-results?utm_source=chatgpt.com">has shown</a> robust growth, contributing significantly to the company's overall revenue increase of 96% year-over-year, totaling $22.7 million for the quarter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Strategic initiatives <a href="https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20241118cn58547/bit-digital-inc-announces-third-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2024-financial-results?utm_source=chatgpt.com">have bolstered</a> Bit Digital's HPC capabilities, including the acquisition of Enovum Data Centers and a substantial agreement with cloud gaming provider Boosteroid, potentially generating over $700 million in revenue over five years.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Sam Tabar <a href="https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20241118cn58547/bit-digital-inc-announces-third-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2024-financial-results?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emphasized</a> the company's focus on expanding its HPC business, viewing it as a foundation for sustainable growth and long-term value creation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite <a href="https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20241118cn58547/bit-digital-inc-announces-third-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2024-financial-results?utm_source=chatgpt.com">challenges</a> in the Bitcoin mining sector, Bit Digital's strategic pivot towards HPC services positions the company to capitalize on the growing demand for high-performance computing infrastructure.</p>
