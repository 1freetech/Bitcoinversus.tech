<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The New York Supreme Court has <a href="https://earthjustice.org/press/2024/victory-new-york-court-rescinds-approval-of-fracked-gas-power-plant-sale-to-cryptocurrency-mining-company-in-north-tonawanda">ordered</a> the state's Public Service Commission (PSC) to reassess the approval of Digihost International Inc.'s acquisition of the Fortistar North Tonawanda gas power plant, intended for cryptocurrency mining operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9213,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-26.png?w=581" alt="" class="wp-image-9213" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The court <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-11-18/new-york-digihost-bitcoin-mining/">found</a> that the PSC failed to evaluate the transaction's compliance with New York's Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (CLCPA), which mandates reductions in greenhouse gas emissions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 2021, Digihost entered into an <a href="https://www.wgrz.com/article/tech/science/environment/lawsuit-seeks-to-challenge-transfer-of-north-tonawanda-natural-gas-plant-to-crypto-company/71-1ad7d7d1-77f0-4e55-b973-f23d8174daea">agreement</a> to purchase the 55-megawatt Fortistar plant from North Tonawanda Holdings, aiming to repurpose it for continuous <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/argentinas-central-bank-hosts-bitcoin-mining-art-exhibit/">cryptocurrency mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The PSC approved the transfer in September 2022 without conducting a thorough environmental impact assessment, prompting legal challenges from environmental groups.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The court's decision <a href="https://earthjustice.org/press/2024/victory-new-york-court-rescinds-approval-of-fracked-gas-power-plant-sale-to-cryptocurrency-mining-company-in-north-tonawanda">emphasizes</a> the necessity for state agencies to consider environmental consequences in their approvals, particularly concerning projects that could significantly increase greenhouse gas emissions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The PSC is now required to re-evaluate the transaction, ensuring alignment with the state's climate objectives.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This ruling <a href="https://www.news10.com/news/ny-supreme-court-cases-pit-crypto-against-climate/">highlights</a> the ongoing tension between economic development initiatives, such as cryptocurrency mining, and environmental sustainability goals. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The outcome of the PSC's reassessment may set a precedent for future energy-intensive projects in New York.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
