<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/09/nvidia-launches-free-ai-online-courses-for-all-skill-levels/">NVIDIA</a>, the world's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/nvidia-is-the-worlds-most-valuable-company/">most valuable company</a>, has entered the nuclear energy sector by <a href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/californias-only-nuclear-plant-use-ai-help-comply-with-new-licensing-challenges-2024-11-13/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">partnering</a> with Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&amp;E) to implement artificial intelligence (AI) solutions at California's Diablo Canyon Nuclear Power Plant. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This collaboration aims to enhance operational efficiency and document management within the facility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/ai-solution-deployed-at-diablo-canyon-in-nuclear-industry-first">initiative</a> involves deploying Atomic Canyon's Neutron Enterprise, a generative AI solution built on NVIDIA's full-stack AI platform. This system is designed to streamline document search and retrieval processes, significantly reducing the time required to access critical information.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Diablo Canyon, California's only remaining nuclear power plant, contributes nearly 9% of the state's electricity and generates 17% of its zero-carbon energy. The integration of AI technologies is <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pge-launches-first-commercial-deployment-of-on-site-generative-ai-solution-for-the-nuclear-energy-sector-at-diablo-canyon-302304285.html">expected</a> to bolster the plant's role in providing clean and reliable energy to meet the state's growing demands.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/pg-e-launches-first-commercial-183000027.html">partnership</a> marks a significant milestone as the first commercial deployment of on-site generative AI at a U.S. nuclear power plant, setting a precedent for future technological advancements in the nuclear energy sector.</p>
