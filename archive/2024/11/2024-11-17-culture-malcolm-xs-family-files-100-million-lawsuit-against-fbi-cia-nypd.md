<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The family of civil rights leader Malcolm X has <a href="https://apnews.com/article/682c71e26cbeaf293d0ea783999c0c5e?utm_source=chatgpt.com">filed</a> a $100 million lawsuit against the FBI, CIA, and New York Police Department, alleging these agencies were complicit in his 1965 assassination.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The lawsuit, <a href="https://www.the-sun.com/news/12903715/malcolm-x-daughters-lawsuit-cia-fbi-assassination-plot/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">submitted</a> in Manhattan federal court, contends that the agencies were aware of and involved in the assassination plot but failed to prevent his death.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Attorney Ben Crump, representing Malcolm X's daughters, <a href="https://people.com/malcolm-x-family-files-lawsuit-against-us-agencies-over-assassination-8746613?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expressed</a> hope that federal officials would review the case to address and rectify past wrongdoings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8822,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-8.png?w=655" alt="" class="wp-image-8822" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The lawsuit <a href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/malcolm-xs-family-sues-us-law-enforcement-agencies-over-assassination-2024-11-15/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">seeks</a> to uncover the extent of the agencies' knowledge and complicity in the assassination plot against Malcolm X.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Malcolm X, a prominent Black civil rights leader and former Nation of Islam spokesperson, was assassinated on February 21, 1965, in New York City.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Although three Nation of Islam members were convicted, two were exonerated in 2021, leading to renewed scrutiny of the case.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The lawsuit aims to reveal the truth and seek justice for Malcolm X's family, who have long sought answers about his death.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The family <a href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/malcolm-xs-family-sues-us-law-enforcement-agencies-over-assassination-2024-11-15/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">asserts</a> significant and irreversible damage caused by the alleged conspiracy and cover-up, emphasizing the need for accountability and transparency.</p>
