<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent <a href="https://plebbase.com/research/king-dead-long-live-king-low-profile-plus-bitaxe-gamma-63">analysis</a>, the <a href="https://52pi.com/products/low-profile-plus-cpu-cooler-for-raspberry-pi-5-aluminum-heatsink-with-cooling-fan-for-raspberry-pi-5">52Pi</a> Low-Profile Plus cooler has <a href="https://plebbase.com/research/king-dead-long-live-king-low-profile-plus-bitaxe-gamma-63">demonstrated</a> exceptional performance when paired with the Bitaxe <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/how-to-build-your-own-bitcoin-mining-data-center-bitaxe-edition-spread-sheet-included/">Gamma Bitcoin miner</a>. The cooler's design, featuring four copper heat pipes and a substantial base plate, effectively dissipates heat, maintaining optimal operating temperatures for the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitaxe-gamma-redefines-asic-mining-with-advanced-cooling-features/">Bitaxe Gamma</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Bitaxe Gamma, equipped with the BM1370 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/hut-8-corp-enhances-mining-fleet-with-bitmain-s21-acquisition/">ASIC</a> from the Antminer S21 Pro, achieves a hashrate between 1.0 to 1.2 TH/s with an energy efficiency of approximately 15 J/TH. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Proper cooling is essential to sustain these performance levels and prevent overheating.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Testing revealed that the 52Pi Low-Profile Plus cooler, when combined with a Noctua NF-A6x15 fan, outperformed other cooling solutions, including the 'Ötzi' cooler. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The enhanced design of the 52Pi cooler allows for more efficient heat transfer, resulting in lower ASIC temperatures and improved miner stability.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The integration of the 52Pi Low-Profile Plus cooler with the Bitaxe Gamma underscores the importance of selecting appropriate cooling solutions to maximize mining hardware performance and longevity. </p>
