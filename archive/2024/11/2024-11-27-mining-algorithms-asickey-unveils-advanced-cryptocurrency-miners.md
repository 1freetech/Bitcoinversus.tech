<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://asickey.com/">ASICKey</a> has <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/13/2980425/0/en/ASICKey-Launches-High-Performance-Cryptocurrency-Miners-with-Advanced-Technology.html">introduced</a> a new lineup of cryptocurrency miners, including the KEYMINER A1, KEYMINER X, and KEYMINER PRO, all utilizing advanced 3nm <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/quantum-bitcoin-mining-futuristic-heatsink-enhances-bitaxe-asic-chip-performance/">ASIC technology</a> to deliver high <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/bitaxe-startup-efficiency-peaks-explained/">hash rates</a> with reduced power consumption.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The entry-level KEYMINER A1 <a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/asickey-unveils-new-bitcoin-miners-as-price-surge-restores-interest-in-mining/">offers</a> a hash rate of 1100 TH/s for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Bitcoin</a> while consuming just 650 W of power, reportedly generating monthly profits of $1,200 for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin</a>, $2,900 for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/03/power-is-money-debating-a-litecoin-maximalist/">Litecoin</a>, and $3,800 for Dash at current rates.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">ASICKey <a href="https://99bitcoins.com/news/asickey-introduces-new-bitcoin-miners-with-focus-on-energy-efficiency-and-low-noise/">claims</a> that the new miners feature ultra-low noise levels, making them suitable for use in various environments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The launch of these miners comes amid a resurgence in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/top-secret-bitcoin-mining-machine-revealed-in-west-texas/">Bitcoin mining</a> interest, driven by a rebound in cryptocurrency prices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">ASICKey's new models aim to set a new standard in the mining industry by offering unparalleled hash rates, low power consumption, and maximum profitability for users.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company emphasizes the user-friendly design of the miners, stating that no prior IT or mining knowledge is required for setup, making them accessible to both seasoned professionals and newcomers alike.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">ASICKey also offers a 0% fee mining pool option for users, further enhancing the profitability potential of their mining equipment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/13/2980425/0/en/ASICKey-Launches-High-Performance-Cryptocurrency-Miners-with-Advanced-Technology.html">guarantees</a> delivery within seven business days and covers all delivery and customs fees, ensuring a seamless experience for customers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's <a href="https://99bitcoins.com/news/asickey-introduces-new-bitcoin-miners-with-focus-on-energy-efficiency-and-low-noise/">focus</a> on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">energy efficiency</a> and low noise levels addresses some of the environmental concerns associated with crypto mining.</p>
