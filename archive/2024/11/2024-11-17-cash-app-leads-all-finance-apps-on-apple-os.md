<p style="text-transform:none">As of November 16, 2024, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">Cash App</a> has <a href="https://x.com/VersusInstitute/status/1857790331925709303">secured</a> the top position in the U.S. Apple App Store's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/microstrategy-purchases-an-additional-27200-btc/">finance</a> category, surpassing competitors such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/11/bitcoin-vs-paypal-new-company-ventures-verify-bitcoins-significance/">PayPal</a> and Coinbase.</p>
