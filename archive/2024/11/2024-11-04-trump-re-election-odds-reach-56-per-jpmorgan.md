<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/22/jpmorgan-ceo-jamie-dimon-reportedly-backs-kamala-harris-in-private-considers-role-in-her-administration/">JPMorgan Chase</a> has <a href="https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/markets-are-pricing-in-trump-win-surging-gop-sweep-probability-jpmorgan-3670241">observed</a> a significant shift in market sentiment, with increasing odds of a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/10/politics-trump-pledges-to-lift-sanctions-on-russia-to-protect-the-dollars-dominance/">Donald Trump</a> victory in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/23/politics-ripple-co-founder-chris-larsen-endorses-kamala-harris-for-president/">2024 U.S. Presidential Electio</a>n. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to recent market data, betting odds now place Trump's re-election chances at 56%, and the probability of a Republican-controlled Senate has surpassed 80%. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This uptick in expectations has been reflected in financial markets, with stronger U.S. equities, particularly in the banking sector, a strengthening U.S. dollar, and tighter credit spreads over the past two weeks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the recent rise in market activity tied to a potential Trump victory, JPMorgan's strategists caution that the current market movements have yet to fully match the scale of the "Trump trade" from 2016.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This previous market phenomenon saw significant outperformance in U.S. equities, stronger Treasury yields, and tighter credit spreads. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the strategists suggest that the market is still some way from fully pricing in the expected impact of a Republican sweep​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The implications of a Republican victory could extend beyond just market performance. JPMorgan notes that industries such as gold and Bitcoin may benefit from the so-called "debasement trade," driven by expected fiscal expansion and tariffs under a Trump-led administration​.</p>
