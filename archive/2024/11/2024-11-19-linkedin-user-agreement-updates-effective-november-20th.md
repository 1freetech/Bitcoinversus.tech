<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">LinkedIn has recently <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/legal/preview/user-agreement#rights">updated</a> its user agreement, introducing significant changes to how user content is managed and shared on the platform. One of the most notable updates involves granting LinkedIn a non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, copy, and distribute the content you post. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This includes sharing content across third-party services without additional consent or compensation. However, users retain ownership of their original content, and the license can be revoked by deleting the content or closing the account, though some exceptions apply, such as legal obligations or previous sublicenses​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A major update focuses on LinkedIn’s integration of artificial intelligence (AI), which is now used to recommend content and connections based on user data. Users have the option to opt out of having their data used for AI training. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Privacy and data security are being emphasized more than ever, with enhanced control over content visibility and protection against data breaches​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These updates are part of LinkedIn’s continuous efforts to adapt to market demands and improve the user experience, especially as it introduces more AI features and virtual event functionalities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Users can now conduct live events with features like Q&amp;A sessions and real-time polls. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, the platform is focusing more on the creator economy, offering monetization tools and improved engagement opportunities​.​</p>
