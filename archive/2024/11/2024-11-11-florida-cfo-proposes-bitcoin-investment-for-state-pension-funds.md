<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Florida's Chief Financial Officer, Jimmy Patronis, has formally <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/florida-state-cfo-wants-bitcoin-included-state-pension-funds">requested</a> the State Board of Administration (SBA) to evaluate the feasibility of incorporating <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/26/blockware-explains-how-bitcoin-price-drives-mining-profit-multiple/">Bitcoin</a> into the state's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/politics-jersey-city-pension-fund-to-invest-in-bitcoin-etfs/">pension fund investments</a>. In a letter dated October 29, 2024, Patronis emphasized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/17/fundstrats-tom-lee-unveils-bitcoins-potential-surge-in-2024/">Bitcoin's potential</a> to diversify the state's portfolio and act as a hedge against the volatility of traditional asset classes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Patronis <a href="https://myfloridacfo.com/news/newsletter/past-issues/news-details/2024/10/29/cfo-jimmy-patronis-to-sba--florida-needs-to-lean-forward-on-cryptocurrency">highlighted </a>Florida's robust economic standing, noting its Triple-A bond rating and record reserves. He suggested that a "Digital Currency Investment Pilot Program" could align with the state's innovative investment strategies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The CFO's proposal aligns with recent national discussions on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency adoption</a>. Former President Donald Trump, at the Bitcoin 2024 convention, proposed establishing a national cryptocurrency reserve to position the U.S. as a global leader in the crypto space.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Patronis also referenced Governor Ron DeSantis's efforts to protect Floridians from central bank digital currencies, viewing decentralized digital currencies like Bitcoin as alternatives to centralized monetary systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Other states have begun exploring cryptocurrency investments for their pension funds. The State of Wisconsin Investment Board invested $164 million in Bitcoin exchange-traded funds, while the State of Michigan Retirement System acquired shares in a Bitcoin ETF.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Patronis's request to the SBA includes a comprehensive report on the feasibility, risks, and potential benefits of directing a portion of state retirement funds into digital assets. He emphasized the importance of Florida staying ahead of the curve in considering new investments to provide the best returns for its residents.</p>
