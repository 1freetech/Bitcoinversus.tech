<p style="text-transform:none">Soluna Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: SLNH) has <a href="https://www.solunacomputing.com/news/q324-results/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reported </a>robust financial results for the third quarter of 2024, achieving a year-to-date revenue of $29.7 million, marking a significant increase from the $10.9 million reported during the same period in 2023.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A key development <a href="https://www.solunacomputing.com/news/soluna-announces-sustainable-ai-soluna-cloud-supported-by-hewlett-packard-enterprise/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">contributing</a> to this growth is the launch of Soluna Cloud AI, a sustainable cloud computing service designed to support AI training and customization. This initiative is bolstered by a strategic partnership with Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), utilizing HPE's direct liquid-cooled infrastructure powered by nearly 100% renewable energy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to technological advancements, Soluna has <a href="https://www.solunacomputing.com/news/q324-results/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">secured</a> $25 million in growth capital to expand Project Dorothy, its flagship behind-the-meter data center. This expansion aims to enhance the company's capacity to convert surplus renewable energy into scalable computing resources.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Progress has also <a href="https://www.solunacomputing.com/news/q324-results/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">been made</a> on Project Kati, with several important milestones achieved, and Project Rosa is accelerating with the signing of term sheets for power and land. These projects are integral to Soluna's mission to make renewable energy a global superpower by transforming surplus renewable energy into scalable computing resources.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO John Belizaire <a href="https://www.solunacomputing.com/news/q324-results/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expressed optimism</a> about the company's trajectory, stating, "Soluna continues to move at a fast pace and has achieved several important milestones this quarter." The company's strategic initiatives and financial performance underscore its commitment to advancing renewable computing solutions.</p>
