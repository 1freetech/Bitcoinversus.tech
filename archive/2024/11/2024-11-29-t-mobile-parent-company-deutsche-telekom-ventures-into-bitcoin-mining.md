<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Deutsche Telekom, Europe’s largest telecom, has started mining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/26/bitcoin-growth-game-power-law-theorist-claims-bitcoin-network-outpaces-internet-growth/">Bitcoin</a> using surplus renewable energy in a pilot project called "Digital Monetary Photosynthesis." </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.telekom.com/en/media/media-information/archive/test-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure-for-surplus-energy-1082684?utm_source=chatgpt.com">initiative</a>, in partnership with Bankhaus Metzler, aims to stabilize energy grids by converting excess energy into digital assets, per Deutsche Telekom.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/natural-gas-finds-new-value-in-bitcoin-mining-operations/">mining operations</a>, hosted by Riva Engineering in Backnang, Germany, utilize surplus power from photovoltaic systems. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Oliver Nyderle of Deutsche Telekom MMS, the project addresses grid stability challenges caused by fluctuating <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/record-high-energy-curtailments-suggests-california-needs-bitcoin-mining/">renewable energy</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bankhaus Metzler is leveraging the project to gain insights into digital asset applications, with a focus on advancing blockchain innovation in Germany. By balancing supply and demand in the energy grid, the pilot follows similar models used in the U.S. and Finland.</p>
