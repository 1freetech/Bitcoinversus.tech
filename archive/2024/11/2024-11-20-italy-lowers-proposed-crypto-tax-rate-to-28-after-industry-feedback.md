<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Italy's government has <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/italy-eyes-drastic-tax-hike-223947254.html">decided</a> to reduce the proposed capital gains tax on cryptocurrency transactions from 42% to 28%, following significant feedback from industry stakeholders and political figures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initial proposal, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/italy-to-raise-bitcoin-capital-gains-tax-to-42/">introduced</a> in October 2024, aimed to increase the tax rate from the existing 26% to 42%, sparking widespread concern among investors and businesses within the cryptocurrency sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Deputy Economy Minister Maurizio Leo announced the revised tax plan, emphasizing the government's commitment to fostering a more favorable environment for digital asset investments while ensuring appropriate taxation. The adjustment reflects a balance between generating revenue and supporting the growth of the cryptocurrency industry in Italy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initial 42% tax proposal faced criticism from various quarters, including members of the ruling coalition. Lawmaker Giulio Centemero described the higher tax rate as "counterproductive" and called for a comprehensive dialogue with market participants to develop a more effective taxation framework.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Industry experts have welcomed the reduction to 28%, viewing it as a positive step toward maintaining Italy's competitiveness in the rapidly evolving digital economy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://crypto2community.com/crypto-news/italy-mulls-reducing-proposed-crypto-tax-hike-from-42-to-28/">revised</a> tax rate is expected to encourage both domestic and international investors to engage more actively in the Italian cryptocurrency market.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The government's decision aligns with broader European Union efforts to standardize cryptocurrency regulations across member states. The Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) framework aims to provide clear guidelines for digital asset operations, promoting transparency and investor protection within the EU.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The revised tax policy is set to be included in Italy's 2025 budget, which requires parliamentary approval by the end of December 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lawmakers are <a href="https://www.reuters.com/markets/currencies/italy-minister-open-reviewing-tax-hike-cryptocurrencies-2024-11-07/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">expected</a> to deliberate on the proposal in the coming weeks, with the outcome likely to influence Italy's position in the global cryptocurrency landscape.</p>
