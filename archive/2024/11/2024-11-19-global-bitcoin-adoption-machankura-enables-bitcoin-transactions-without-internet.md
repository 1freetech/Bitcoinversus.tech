<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">Bitcoin adoption in Africa</a> faces unique challenges, particularly due to limited internet connectivity. About 30% of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/ethiopia-hosts-groundbreaking-bitcoin-mining-summit-reinforces-role-as-africas-leading-mining-hub/">Africans</a> have access to the internet, making it difficult to leverage traditional <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/01/yellow-card-is-the-largest-bitcoin-exchange-in-africa/">Bitcoin platforms</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Kgothatso Ngako, the founder of Machankura, addresses this issue by enabling <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/16/honda-embraces-bitcoin-starts-new-chapter-in-automotive-transactions/">Bitcoin transactions</a> via basic mobile phones using USSD technology. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This service allows users to send and receive <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> without needing a smartphone or internet connection, effectively bringing financial services to the unbanked population across multiple African countries​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ngako emphasizes the concept of sovereignty, stating that it is not just about individual autonomy but also about the collective future. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He compares the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/19/3373/">Bitcoin movement</a> to the struggle against <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid">apartheid</a>, highlighting the need for resilience against regulatory challenges.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He likens American financial regulations to "Frieza" from Dragon Ball Z, viewing them as obstacles that must be overcome to ensure economic freedom for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/07/bitcoin-dada-is-empowering-ugandan-women-with-bitcoin-education/">African entrepreneurs</a>​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> offers a transformative opportunity for African entrepreneurs, particularly in regions with strict capital controls. Machankura’s platform enables users to participate in both local and international economic transactions without the need for traditional banking infrastructure. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><br>Machankura is currently <a href="https://coinvoice.net/article/4733831467452338177">operational</a> in several African countries, including South Africa, Nigeria, and Kenya, with nearly 15,000 users. As <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/01/yellow-card-is-the-largest-bitcoin-exchange-in-africa/">Bitcoin adoption</a> grows, Ngako envisions Machankura expanding its reach to millions more who lack internet access, potentially revolutionizing the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/29/ceo-charlene-fadirepo-shares-insight-on-africas-bitcoin-revolution/">financial landscape in Africa</a>. </p>
