<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Former President Trump’s <a href="https://businessmirror.com.ph/2024/08/26/made-in-usa-bitcoin-threatens-chinas-bitmain-rule/">push</a> for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/03/bitcoin-whale-michael-saylor-poised-to-acquire-more-btc-through-microstrategy/">Bitcoin</a> to be “mined, minted, and made” in the U.S. is challenging the dominance of Chinese companies <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/08/bitmain-extends-hashboard-life-with-new-protective-temp-sensor-coating/">Bitmain</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/12/analyzing-centralization-and-monopolies-in-the-bitcoin-industry/">Canaan</a>, and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">Whatsminer</a>, which control the majority of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">Bitcoin mining hardware</a> market. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite these efforts, Chinese firms continue to lead, while U.S. companies like Block, led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">Jack Dorsey</a>, are making strides with innovations like the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/13/block-ceo-jack-dorsey-reveals-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining-with-the-mdk-hashboard/">MDK hashboard</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, proprietary control boards developed by American firms such as Foundry and Epic are emerging as potential alternatives to Chinese hardware​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The shift towards American-made <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin mining hardware</a> aligns with broader U.S. goals of reducing dependency on foreign technology, especially as geopolitical tensions with China rise. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Bitmain and its counterparts remain dominant, the emergence of U.S.-based solutions could pave the way for a more diversified and secure global supply chain. However, scaling these efforts to match the well-established Chinese manufacturers will be a significant challenge for American companies.</p>
