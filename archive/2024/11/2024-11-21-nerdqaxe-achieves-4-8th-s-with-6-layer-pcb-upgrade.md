<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://github.com/shufps/qaxe">NerdQAxe++</a> has undergone a significant upgrade, transitioning from a 4-layer to a 6-layer <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/new-siemens-software-integrates-ai-for-pcb-design-efficiency/">printed circuit board</a> (PCB). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9054,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/nerd-q-axe-6-layer-pcb.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-9054" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"0.6rem","textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:0.6rem;text-transform:none"><em>All photos belong to <a href="https://github.com/shufps?tab=overview&amp;from=2024-11-01&amp;to=2024-11-17">Thomas Shufps</a></em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This enhancement has resulted in a stable hashrate of approximately 4.8 terahashes per second (TH/s) while maintaining power consumption around 76 watts, equating to an efficiency of about 15.8 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/how-to-build-your-own-bitcoin-mining-data-center-bitaxe-edition-spread-sheet-included/">joules per terahash</a> (J/TH). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9059,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/nerd-q-axe-1-e1731884582709.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-9059" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://github.com/shufps/qaxe">improved design </a>also allows for the use of a smaller cooling system, effectively maintaining the board's temperature at 55°C, a notable improvement from the previous 63°C with a larger cooler.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In comparative tests, the same smaller cooler was applied to the earlier 4-layer PCB design. The firmware initiated a shutdown when temperatures reached 70°C, indicating that the previous design was less efficient in heat management. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The transition to a 6-layer PCB has not only enhanced performance metrics but also improved thermal regulation, underscoring the importance of PCB architecture in optimizing the efficiency and reliability of mining hardware.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9055,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-17-175330.png?w=361" alt="" class="wp-image-9055" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><sup>4 x BM1370 chips</sup></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/bitaxe-startup-efficiency-peaks-explained/">Bitaxe</a> miner's achievement of over 4.8TH/s represents a significant advancement in the realm of compact, open-source Bitcoin mining solutions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This development highlights the potential for small-scale miners to achieve higher performance levels through thoughtful design enhancements, contributing to the broader accessibility and decentralization of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/proto-releases-updates-from-bitcoin-mining-development-kit/">Bitcoin mining</a>.</p>
