<p style="text-transform:none">NBA legend <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/scottie-pippen-inquires-about-bitcoin-on-social-media/">Scottie Pippen</a> has once again grabbed attention in both the sports and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">cryptocurrency</a> worlds. In a recent tweet to his 670,400 followers, Pippen shared a vivid dream in which Bitcoin's mysterious creator, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">Satoshi Nakamoto</a>, predicted a monumental shift for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-advocate-davinci-jeremie-predicts-early-retirement-with-one-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Pippen, the real Bitcoin bull run will begin on November 15, 2024, coinciding with a supermoon event. The dream foretold that this celestial occurrence would mark the rise of the "Bulls" and bring significant changes to the cryptocurrency landscape.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">This isn’t the first time Pippen has stirred excitement in the crypto community. Earlier in September, he predicted Bitcoin would reach $84,650 by November 5, 2024, based on another dream where Satoshi visited him. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While some have taken these claims with skepticism, the reactions from the crypto community have been overwhelmingly positive, with exchanges like Kraken playfully engaging with the star’s predictions​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin’s price has been steadily rising, with analysts pointing to factors like potential U.S. Federal Reserve interest rate cuts that could push prices even higher. At the time of Pippen's latest tweet, Bitcoin was trading around $68,000, reflecting its recent upward trend​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Whether Pippen’s dream will come true remains to be seen, but his bullish outlook has certainly ignited conversation and excitement in the cryptocurrency world.</p>
