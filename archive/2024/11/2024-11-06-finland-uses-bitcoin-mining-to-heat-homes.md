<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In an innovative project led by Hashlabs Mining, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/10/miningstore-ceo-jp-baric-is-revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-with-energy-efficient-solutions/">Bitcoin mining</a> is <a href="https://x.com/HashlabsMining/status/1828311081372000413">being utilized</a> to provide heat to homes in Finland. The system <a href="https://bitcoinnews.com/mining/hashlabs-finland-bitcoin-heaters/">leverages</a> the WhatsMiner M63S, a hydro-cooled <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">ASIC mining device</a>, which generates hot water during the mining process. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This hot water, reaching temperatures of 70°C, is <a href="https://www.districtenergy.org/blogs/district-energy/2024/04/25/finlands-households-turn-to-bitcoin-mining-to-heat">integrated</a> into Finland's district heating system and distributed through a network of insulated pipes to multiple buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This project not only makes use of the excess energy from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/15/pennsylvania-greenlights-bitcoin-mining-with-revised-bill/">Bitcoin mining</a> but also <a href="https://payspacemagazine.com/news/bitcoin-mining-energy-to-heat-homes-in-finland/">promotes</a> sustainability by reducing reliance on traditional fuels like biomass, peat, and hard coal, which are commonly used in district heating systems​. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initiative is seen as a step toward a more sustainable energy economy in Finland, aligning with the country's goals to increase the share of climate-neutral energy sources​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Jaran Mellerud, co-founder of Hashlabs Mining, emphasized that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">Bitcoin mining</a> provides a reliable source of high-temperature water for district heating. The project has already integrated two <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin mines</a> into the district heating system, with plans for further expansion.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By utilizing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> in this way, Finland is exploring new methods to balance the energy-intensive nature of mining with societal needs, making it a powerful tool in the global transition to renewable energy.</p>
