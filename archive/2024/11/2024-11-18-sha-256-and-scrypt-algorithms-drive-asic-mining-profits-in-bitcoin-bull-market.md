<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to <a href="https://www.asicminervalue.com/">Asicminervalue.com</a>, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/bitmains-new-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">Bitmain Antminer</a> L9 (17.6Gh) has <a href="https://archive.asicminervalue.com/">emerged</a> as a leading performer. Released in May 2024, it offers a hashrate of 17.6 Gh/s and consumes 3,260 W of power, generating an estimated daily profit of $55.42.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Another notable miner is the Bitmain Antminer L9 (16.2Gh), also <a href="https://archive.asicminervalue.com/">launched</a> in May 2024. With a hashrate of 16.2 Gh/s and the same power consumption of 3,260 W, it yields approximately $50.26 per day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Bitmain Antminer L7 (9.5Gh), <a href="https://archive.asicminervalue.com/">introduced</a> in November 2021, continues to be a strong contender. It delivers a hashrate of 9.5 Gh/s with a power consumption of 3,425 W, resulting in a daily profit of about $25.12.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">ASIC mining continues to demonstrate its profitability, with several machines standing out due to their performance and efficiency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to ASIC Miner Value, the Bitmain Antminer L9 (17Gh), set to release in May 2024, leads the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/03/power-is-money-debating-a-litecoin-maximalist/">Scrypt algorithm</a> mining category with a hashrate of 17 Gh/s, a power consumption of 3,260 W, and a daily profit of $35.31. Its competitive noise level of 75 dB further enhances its appeal. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Another notable contender, the <strong>VolcMiner D1</strong>, launched in October 2024, delivers similar performance metrics with 17 Gh/s at a slightly higher power consumption of 3,900 W, achieving $34.99 per day. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, its design prioritizes noise reduction at 75 dB, ideal for large-scale mining operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/bitmains-new-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">Bitmain Antminer S21e XP Hyd 3U</a>, debuting in January 2025, breaks records in SHA-256 mining with a staggering 860 Th/s, consuming 11,180 W. Despite its high power usage, its superior hashrate delivers a solid profit of $28.78 per day, cementing its dominance in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Meanwhile, the <strong>Jasminer X44-P</strong>, <a href="https://www.asicminervalue.com/">designed</a> for EtHash mining, achieves profitability of $21.27 per day with a power-efficient design of 2,550 W and a higher noise level of 80 dB. Its release in December 2024 caters to miners seeking high-performance Ethereum mining machines.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These machines represent the forefront of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/14/bitcoin-mining-is-shaping-renewable-energy-frontiers/">mining innovation</a>, with their profitability highlighting the importance of power efficiency and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">hashrate optimization</a>.</p>
