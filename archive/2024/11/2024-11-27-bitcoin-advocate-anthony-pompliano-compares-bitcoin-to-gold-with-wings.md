<p style="text-transform:none">Anthony Pompliano, Founder and CEO of Professional Capital Management, recently offered an insightful perspective. Pompliano describes <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> as "gold with wings," emphasizing its potential to protect against market downturns while offering substantial growth.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/video/bitcoin-gold-wings-anthony-pompliano-113037445.html">recent appearance</a> on Market Domination Overtime, Pompliano discussed why he remains optimistic about Bitcoin's future, despite the recent price volatility. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He highlighted Bitcoin's impressive 62% compound annual growth rate over the past decade as evidence of its long-term potential. "You can think of Bitcoin as gold with wings," he explained, noting that it combines the security of gold with the growth potential of a high-performing asset.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pompliano also addressed the growing interest in Bitcoin ETFs, which he believes provide institutional investors with a low-cost, liquid method of gaining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/morgan-stanley-becomes-first-major-bank-to-offer-bitcoin-etfs-to-clients/">exposure to Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This increased institutional interest, according to Pompliano, will continue to support <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin's growth</a> in the long term​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the recent dip, Pompliano remains bullish, asserting that Bitcoin is well-positioned for future gains, driven by both its unique properties and the increasing adoption by financial institutions.</p>
