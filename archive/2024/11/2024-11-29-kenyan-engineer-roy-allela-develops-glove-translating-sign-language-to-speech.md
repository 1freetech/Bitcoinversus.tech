<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Kenyan engineer Roy Allela has <a href="https://afrotech.com/roy-allela-creates-smart-gloves-for-hearing-impaired/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">developed</a> Sign-IO, a smart glove that translates sign language into audible speech, significantly enhancing communication for the deaf community. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The glove employs sensors on each finger to detect hand movements, which are then transmitted via Bluetooth to a mobile application that vocalizes the gestures in real-time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b9a03JNF-dg","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Allela's inspiration <a href="https://www.passiton.com/passiton-blog/255-inventor-creates-glove-that-translate-sign?utm_source=chatgpt.com">stemmed</a> from his six-year-old niece, who was born deaf. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Observing her challenges in communicating with non-sign language users, he sought to create a device that would bridge this gap. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Sign-IO glove allows users to customize the voice's gender, language, and pitch, making it adaptable to individual preferences.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The innovative glove has <a href="https://www.blackenterprise.com/kenyan-engineer-creates-high-tech-gloves-sign-language-into-speech/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">garnered</a> international recognition, winning the Hardware Trailblazer Award at the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) global finals. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Allela aims to distribute the gloves to special needs schools across Kenya, promoting inclusivity and improving the quality of life for those with hearing impairments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
