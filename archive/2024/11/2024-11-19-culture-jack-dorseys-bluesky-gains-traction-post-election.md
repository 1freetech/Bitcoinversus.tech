<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bluesky, a decentralized social <a href="https://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/the-rise-of-bluesky-and-what-it-could-mean-for-elon-musks-x-nnz3mcp88?utm_source=chatgpt.com">media platform</a> founded by Twitter co-founder Jack Dorsey, has emerged as a significant alternative to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/17/x-com-launches-new-job-search-platform/">X</a> (formerly <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/16/culture-stephen-king-departs-x-cites-platforms-dark-turn-on-threads/">Twitter</a>), especially following the U.S. presidential election on November 5, 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The platform has gained over a million new users since the election, driven by concerns over Elon Musk's influence in Donald Trump's circle, leading many anti-Republican users to seek alternatives.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9311,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-29.png?w=721" alt="" class="wp-image-9311" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The migration to Bluesky has been fueled by dissatisfaction with X's environment, which has seen an increase in bots and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/16/culture-stephen-king-departs-x-cites-platforms-dark-turn-on-threads/">offensive content</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bluesky offers an advertisement-free experience and customizable algorithms, allowing users greater control over their content. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Its decentralized model enables users to move their social identities between apps, aligning with its vision of an open social network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9313,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-19-060804.png?w=582" alt="" class="wp-image-9313" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite its smaller user base of 15 million compared to X's 600 million, Bluesky's growth is notable. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The platform plans to <a href="https://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/the-rise-of-bluesky-and-what-it-could-mean-for-elon-musks-x-nnz3mcp88?utm_source=chatgpt.com">introduce</a> a paid subscription service to enhance user experience, aiming to attract more users seeking a community free from the issues reported on X.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The shift to Bluesky reflects a broader trend of users seeking platforms that prioritize user control and a safer online environment. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Bluesky continues to develop, it presents a viable alternative for those disillusioned with X's current trajectory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
