<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Russell Okung, a former NFL offensive tackle, made headlines in 2020 when he converted $6.5 million—half of his $13 million contract with the Carolina Panthers—into <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitcoin-mining-could-shape-future-energy-strategies/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His move, initially seen as unconventional, has turned out to be a lucrative investment as Bitcoin has surged in value. Today, Okung’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/blackrocks-growing-bitcoin-holdings-a-strategic-bet-on-digital-assets/">Bitcoin holdings</a> are worth around $20 million, more than the total value of his original contract.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Okung’s financial acumen and commitment to Bitcoin have made waves both in the sports and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/silk-road-founder-ross-ulbricht-pleads-for-second-chance-in-tweet-from-federal-prison/">cryptocurrency communities</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Recently, he <a href="https://x.com/RussellOkung/status/1856692125343088651">shared</a> a humorous and relatable story via a tweet, revealing how he managed his family’s Bitcoin holdings. He mentioned his mother’s request to sell her Bitcoin position a year ago due to concerns over the currency's volatility. <br><br>Rather than selling, Okung opted to keep her investment intact, jokingly calling himself a “good son” for this decision.</p>
