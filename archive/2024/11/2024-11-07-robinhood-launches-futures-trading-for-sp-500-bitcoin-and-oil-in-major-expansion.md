<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Robinhood Markets, Inc. has <a href="https://newsroom.aboutrobinhood.com/the-legend-awakens/">announced</a> the rollout of in-app futures trading, allowing users to trade contracts based on the S&amp;P 500, oil, and Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This latest addition to the platform comes as part of a broader effort to attract seasoned traders by providing advanced tools at competitive fees.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company’s mobile app now supports futures and index options trading, giving users access to high-demand assets like Bitcoin and S&amp;P 500 futures, traditionally dominated by larger financial institutions​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The move is accompanied by the launch of Robinhood Legend, a desktop platform aimed at active traders. It offers real-time data, customizable layouts, and advanced charting capabilities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Robinhood Gold members benefit from trading futures contracts with fees as low as 50 cents per contract, while non-Gold members pay 75 cents. Index options are similarly competitive, with fees set at 35 cents for Gold members​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With its user base of 11.8 million monthly active users, Robinhood <a href="https://decrypt.co/286802/robinhood-targets-seasoned-traders-with-bitcoin-futures-new-desktop-platform">aims</a> to compete directly with established brokerages like Schwab and Morgan Stanley​.</p>
