<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/mawson-infrastructure-group-inc-announces-monthly-operational-update-for-october-2024-1034041645?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reported</a> a significant financial upturn for October 2024, with total monthly revenue reaching approximately $4.89 million. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This figure represents a 49% year-over-year increase from October 2023 and an 18% month-over-month rise from September 2024.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's Digital Colocation business <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/mawson-infrastructure-group-inc-announces-monthly-operational-update-for-october-2024-1034041645?utm_source=chatgpt.com">contributed</a> $3.96 million to the monthly revenue, marking a substantial growth from $0.08 million in October 2023 and an 18% increase compared to September 2024.</p>
