<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">Bitcoin's adoption rate</a>, per Giovanni Santostasi’s detailed analysis titled <em>The Bitcoin Growth Game</em>, is <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">surpassing the early internet era</a>, marking a significant milestone in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/04/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board-allocates-2-of-assets-to-bitcoin-etfs/">digital finance</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Santostasi's work explains Bitcoin's growth dynamics using a mathematical model that emphasizes how <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">network participation</a> enhances its overall value. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He draws comparisons to non-zero-sum games where cooperation benefits all participants, reinforcing Bitcoin's unique standing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/21/bitcoin-power-law-suggests-bitcoin-price-will-reach-100k-by-jan-2025/">Santostasi</a>, Bitcoin adoption follows a compounding pattern where each new participant adds exponential value to the system. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For example, by his calculations, new participants invest their external resources to gain access, which in turn grows the network. His theory underscores why Bitcoin’s cooperative system is neither exploitative nor centralized. (<a href="https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-on-track-for-mass-adoption-as-it-grows-faster-than-the-internet-202102100825">fxstreet.com</a>)</p>
