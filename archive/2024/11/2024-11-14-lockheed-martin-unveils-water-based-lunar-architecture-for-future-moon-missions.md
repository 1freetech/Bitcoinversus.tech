<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lockheed Martin has <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KbK6MpFvWw&amp;embeds_referring_origin=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia-src.linkedin.com">unveiled</a> its vision for a sustainable lunar presence with its water-based lunar architecture, which aims to revolutionize how humanity approaches space exploration. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://spacenews.com/lockheed-martin-offers-architecture-for-2024-human-lunar-landing/">The architecture</a> is designed to support long-term missions on the Moon, focusing on utilizing water as a key resource for both human habitation and operational needs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This new approach is part of the broader Artemis program, which aims to return humans to the Moon and establish a lasting presence there. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Lockheed Martin's water-based lunar architecture integrates the use of lunar water resources to support life and fuel production. The architecture includes a polar moon station that utilizes water ice deposits found at the lunar poles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company’s focus on sustainability and resource utilization aligns with the long-term goals of the Artemis program, which aims to pave the way for human missions to Mars and beyond. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lockheed Martin's innovative approach highlights the importance of preparing for future space exploration while addressing the immediate challenges of lunar habitation​.</p>
