<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/09/nvidia-launches-free-ai-online-courses-for-all-skill-levels/">NVIDIA</a> has <a href="https://companiesmarketcap.com/assets-by-market-cap/">surpassed</a> Apple Inc. to become the world's most valuable company, with a market capitalization exceeding $3.5 trillion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This milestone reflects the growing influence of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/05/culture-ai-transforms-modern-hip-hop-into-old-school-rb/">artificial intelligence</a> (AI) in the technology sector.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">NVIDIA's stock has <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/nvidia-overtakes-apple-as-most-valuable-company-in-world-8731936?utm_source=chatgpt.com">surged</a> over 850% since the end of 2022, driven by high demand for its AI-capable chips. Major tech companies, including Microsoft and Alphabet, have invested heavily in AI infrastructure, contributing to NVIDIA's rapid growth.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Analysts attribute NVIDIA's success to its leadership in AI technology and strategic partnerships.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Apple, now the second most valuable company, maintains a market capitalization of approximately $3.4 trillion. Despite strong sales, including a 5.5% year-on-year increase in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/bitcoin-appreciates-over-time-relative-to-the-iphone/">iPhone</a> revenue, Apple's net income declined due to a substantial income tax charge.</p>
