<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Major <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/03/space-force-guardian-jason-lowery-gives-crucial-insight-on-innovation-in-bitcoin-sector/">Jason Lowery</a> of the U.S. Space Force has applied for a role as a military advisor to the U.S. government, aiming to advocate for the establishment of a "US Hash Force" and a strategic <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">Bitcoin reserve</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9529,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-45.png?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-9529" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}},"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size" style="text-transform:none">Image Source: beincrypto.com</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent open letter to the U.S. Defense Innovation Board, Lowery <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-national-strategic-importance-space-force-major-jason-lowery?utm_source=chatgpt.com">urged</a> the Department of Defense to investigate the national strategic importance of proof-of-work systems like Bitcoin.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His proposal <a href="https://beincrypto.com/softwar-author-white-house-bid/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emphasizes</a> the integration of Bitcoin's proof-of-work technology into national defense strategies, highlighting its potential to enhance cybersecurity and secure digital assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He contends that such technologies could play a crucial role in maintaining the United States' position as a global superpower in an increasingly digital and interconnected world.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9532,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/jason-lowery-letter-to-defense-innovation-board.jpg?w=811" alt="" class="wp-image-9532" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Source: Dennis Porter, Satoshi Action Fund</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Department of Defense has <a href="https://cryptonews.net/news/bitcoin/29957890/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">initiated</a> a security and policy review of Lowery's thesis, indicating a growing interest in the potential applications of Bitcoin within national defense frameworks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lowery's thesis, "Softwar: A Novel Theory on Power Projection and the National Strategic Significance of Bitcoin," explores how Bitcoin's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/14/understanding-the-byzantine-generals-problem-a-foundation-of-cryptography-and-distributed-computing/">proof-of-work</a> mechanism <a href="https://archive.org/details/lowery-jason-p.-major-softwar-a-novel-theory-on-power-projection-and-the-nationa?utm_source=chatgpt.com">can serve</a> as a form of cyber power projection. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He <a href="https://archive.org/details/lowery-jason-p.-major-softwar-a-novel-theory-on-power-projection-and-the-nationa?utm_source=chatgpt.com">argues</a> that this technology could transform cybersecurity and modern warfare by imposing significant physical costs on potential cyber attackers, thereby deterring malicious activities.</p>
