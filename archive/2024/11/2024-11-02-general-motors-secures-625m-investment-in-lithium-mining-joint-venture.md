<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">General Motors (GM) is <a href="https://www.ttnews.com/articles/gm-invests-lithium-project">committing</a> $625 million to expand its lithium production capacity through a joint venture with Lithium Americas Corp., aiming to secure domestic lithium supplies critical to electric vehicle (EV) battery production. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The investment focuses on the Thacker Pass project in Nevada, which holds North America's largest known lithium deposit, targeting an annual output of around 40,000 metric tons once operational. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">GM will hold a 38% ownership stake in the project, marking one of the largest U.S. investments in lithium mining​.</p>
