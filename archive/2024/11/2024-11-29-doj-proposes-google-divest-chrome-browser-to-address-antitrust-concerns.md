<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) has proposed that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/30/google-fined-20-decillion-by-moscow-court/">Google</a> divest its Chrome browser to address antitrust concerns regarding the company's dominance in the online search market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The DOJ <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/google-should-be-forced-to-divest-chrome-browser-doj-says-8749189?utm_source=chatgpt.com">argues</a> that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/18/googles-advertising-policy-update-paves-the-way-for-bitcoin-trusts/">Google's control</a> over Chrome reinforces its search monopoly by directing users to its search engine, thereby limiting competition.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to the <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/90a1f661-b6ef-46fc-897d-ebb3ff3bc045?utm_source=chatgpt.com">proposed sale</a> of Chrome, the DOJ recommends that Google share search data with competitors and cease agreements that set Google as the default search engine on devices like Apple's iPhone. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ONNjnK8NZ1U","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Google has expressed opposition to the DOJ's proposals, stating that such actions could harm consumers by undermining the security and privacy of its services. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company plans to present its counterarguments in December, with court hearings scheduled for April 2025 and a final ruling expected by August.</p>
