<h3 class="wp-block-heading"></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent development, Brazil's Supreme Court has <a href="https://tribune.com.pk/story/2492457/brazil-bans-elon-musks-x-8874-per-day-fines-for-vpn-users">issued</a> an order imposing fines of up to $8,874 per day on individuals using virtual private networks (VPNs) to access <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/17/x-com-launches-new-job-search-platform/">X.com</a>, the platform formerly known as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/26/space-force-guardian-jason-p-lowerys-twitter-account-hacked/">Twitter</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This decision follows a broader ban on X in Brazil, initiated after the platform, under <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/18/bitcoin-vs-elon-musk-unraveling-the-role-of-spacexs-decisions/">Elon Musk's leadership</a>, failed to appoint a local legal representative, as required by Brazilian law. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The court's decision marks an escalation in the ongoing tensions between Musk and Brazilian authorities, particularly Justice Alexandre de Moraes, who has been a vocal critic of X's content moderation policies and its role in spreading misinformation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://www.slashgear.com/1654673/brazil-x-twitter-ban-vpn-fines/">court's ruling</a> has sparked significant debate, as approximately 40 million Brazilians, nearly 20% of the population, are estimated to use X monthly. With the platform now inaccessible through conventional means, the use of VPNs has become a popular alternative. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/RadarHits/status/1829977081314173259","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the daily fines have been implemented as a deterrent, aiming to enforce the platform's suspension until it complies with local regulations​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The ban and subsequent fines are part of a broader conflict involving content regulation and the spread of far-right ideologies on X. Justice Moraes has also ordered tech giants like Apple and Google to remove the X app from their respective stores, further tightening the restrictions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the crackdown, Musk and X have maintained a defiant stance, refusing to comply with what they describe as "illegal orders" for censorship, thus prolonging the standoff.</p>
