<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/04/vanecks-matthew-sigel-predicts-bullish-bitcoin-trend-heading-into-2024-election/">VanEck</a>, a prominent asset management firm overseeing $100 billion in assets, has <a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/vaneck-targets-bitcoin-price-of-180000/">projected</a> Bitcoin's price to reach $180,000 in the current market cycle. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/04/vanecks-matthew-sigel-predicts-bullish-bitcoin-trend-heading-into-2024-election/">Matthew Sigel</a>, VanEck's head of digital assets research, expressed confidence in Bitcoin's trajectory, stating, "We think it’s just getting started."</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Sigel highlighted the absence of technical resistance, indicating a favorable environment for Bitcoin's growth. He noted, "We are now in blue sky territory, no technical resistance."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The recent surge in Bitcoin's value, which saw it approach $93,490 on November 13, has been partly attributed to pro-crypto sentiments following the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/15/fbi-confiscates-electronics-from-polymarket-ceo-shayne-coplan-following-trump-election-prediction/">U.S. presidential election</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">VanEck's ambitious target reflects a tenfold increase from the cycle's lowest point, marking a significant milestone in Bitcoin's market performance. Sigel emphasized that indicators tracked by VanEck "are still flashing green," suggesting sustained momentum.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The firm also anticipates that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/15/tiaas-disclose-investment-in-bitcoin-etfs-in-latest-sec-filing/">Bitcoin</a> could achieve repeated all-time highs over the next two quarters, driven by growing institutional interest and favorable political developments. Sigel mentioned receiving increased inquiries from investment advisors looking to expand their Bitcoin holdings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a long-term outlook, VanEck envisions Bitcoin becoming a reserve asset by 2050, potentially reaching a price of $3 million per coin. This projection assumes Bitcoin's integration into global trade and central bank reserves at a modest 2% weight.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of November 16, 2024, Bitcoin is trading at approximately $91,041, reflecting a slight decrease from the previous close. The cryptocurrency's intraday high reached $91,914, with a low of $90,068.</p>
