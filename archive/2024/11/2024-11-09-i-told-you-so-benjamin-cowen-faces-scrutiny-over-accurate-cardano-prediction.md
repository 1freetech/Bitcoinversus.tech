<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In April 2023, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> analyst <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/04/bitcoins-september-challenge-benjamin-cohen-discusses-bearish-data-trends/">Benjamin Cowen</a> forecasted that Cardano (ADA) would decline to 400 satoshis (sats) against Bitcoin (BTC). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At that time, some Cardano supporters dismissed his analysis, with one individual reportedly taunting Cowen and subsequently blocking him on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/scottie-pippen-inquires-about-bitcoin-on-social-media/">social media</a>. As of October 2024, Cowen's prediction has materialized, with ADA/BTC trading near 490 sats.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Following the accuracy of his forecast, Cowen <a href="https://twitter.com/intocryptoverse/status/1853516434355155269">faced criticism </a>from certain Cardano proponents who accused him of contributing to ADA's decline. In response, Cowen expressed frustration over being blamed for the market's movements, emphasizing the importance of acknowledging one's own investment decisions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cowen <a href="https://thecryptobasic.com/2024/10/28/market-veteran-taunts-skeptics-after-cardano-prediction-comes-to-fruition/">highlighted</a> the inconsistency in reactions from some community members, noting that when his predictions are incorrect, he is mocked, and when correct, he is blamed for the outcomes. He advocates for personal accountability in investment choices and stresses the need for constructive discourse within the cryptocurrency community.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The situation underscores the challenges analysts face when sharing market predictions, as well as the broader issue of accountability among investors. Cowen's experience serves as a reminder of the complexities inherent in financial forecasting and the varied responses such analyses can elicit.</p>
