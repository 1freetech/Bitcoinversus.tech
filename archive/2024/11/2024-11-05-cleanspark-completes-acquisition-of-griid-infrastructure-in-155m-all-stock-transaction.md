<p style="text-transform:none">CleanSpark Inc. (Nasdaq: CLSK) <a href="https://x.com/CleanSpark_Inc/status/1851956783733944793">officially</a> acquired GRIID Infrastructure Inc. (Nasdaq: GRDI) on October 30, 2024, following shareholder approval from GRIID’s stakeholders two days earlier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":7768,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/screenshot-2024-10-31-084026.png?w=688" alt="" class="wp-image-7768" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This $155 million all-stock deal will significantly expand CleanSpark’s infrastructure, contributing over 400 MW of capacity over the coming years. CleanSpark also issued a $5 million loan to GRIID and allocated $50.9 million to clear outstanding obligations, ensuring robust growth for both entities.</p>
