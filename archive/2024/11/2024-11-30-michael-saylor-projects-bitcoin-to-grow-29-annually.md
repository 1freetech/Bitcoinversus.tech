<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent interview, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/24/michael-saylor-defends-institutional-custody-amid-criticism-from-max-keiser-and-vitalik-buterin/">Michael Saylor</a>, the executive chairman of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">MicroStrategy</a> and prominent <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-advocate-davinci-jeremie-predicts-early-retirement-with-one-bitcoin/">Bitcoin advocate</a>, reaffirmed his confident stance on Bitcoin’s potential, <a href="https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2024/10/12/michael-saylor-reveals-microstrategy-endgame-worlds-leading-bitcoin-bank/">projecting</a> a 29% annual growth rate for the digital asset over the next 21 years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With a current holding of over 252,000 BTC—worth approximately $15 billion—MicroStrategy leads the corporate Bitcoin market, planning to extend this strategy with the goal of transforming the firm into a trillion-dollar entity focused on Bitcoin investments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor envisions Bitcoin as a top-performing asset, bolstered by its deflationary properties, positioning it as a key financial reserve for the future. Should Bitcoin’s growth align with his estimates, its value could reach $13 million per coin by 2045​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MicroStrategy’s investment approach diverges from traditional banking by leveraging low-interest debt to buy Bitcoin rather than issuing loans, a method Saylor believes is safer and more profitable. He describes Bitcoin as the ultimate asset, unaffected by inflation, providing long-term value without the counterparty risks typical in fiat-driven investments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His ambitious goal includes managing up to $150 billion in Bitcoin while aiming for a 50% return by borrowing from the fixed-income market and placing these funds directly into BTC​.</p>
