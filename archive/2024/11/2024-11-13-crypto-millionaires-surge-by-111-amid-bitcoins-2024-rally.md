<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The recent surge in Bitcoin's value has led to a <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/currencies/bitcoin-millionaires-crypto-rally-cryptocurrency-etf-billionaires-sec-ethereum-wealth-2024-8">significant increase</a> in the number of crypto millionaires. According to the latest data, the number of Bitcoin millionaires has more than doubled over the past year, rising by 111% to reach 85,400 individuals. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This dramatic growth has been fueled by Bitcoin's rally, which has seen the cryptocurrency's value climb by approximately 45% in 2024, following the introduction of spot <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/04/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board-allocates-2-of-assets-to-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin ETFs</a> in the US.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In total, the population of crypto millionaires worldwide <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-millionaires-surge-111-in-year-amid-crypto-rally">has grown</a> to 172,300, up from 88,200 last year. This surge is not limited to millionaires; the number of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/tag/coinbase-registration-spain-bank-of-spain-cryptocurrency-bitcoin-momentum-authorized-crypto-exchange-cryptocurrency-expansion-strategy-international-cryptocurrency-acceptance-cryptocurrency-services-s/">crypto</a> billionaires also increased, with six new billionaires minted, five of whom owe their fortunes to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/07/intel-vaneck-releases-major-prediction-for-bitcoin-price/">Bitcoin​</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The report <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/currencies/bitcoin-millionaires-crypto-rally-cryptocurrency-etf-billionaires-sec-ethereum-wealth-2024-8">highlights</a> that the broader adoption of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin ETFs</a> and the influx of institutional capital have been key drivers of this rally. The total market value of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">cryptocurrencies</a> has jumped to $2.3 trillion, marking an 89% increase from the previous year​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Experts point out that this trend underscores <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Bitcoin's dominant position</a> in the crypto market, particularly among long-term investors seeking large holdings.</p>
