<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lume has secured a $4.2 million seed round led by General Catalyst, with participation from Khosla Ventures, Floodgate, Y Combinator, and Soma Capital.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Founded in January 2023, Lume has <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/12/general-catalyst-and-khosla-ventures-back-data-mapping-startup-lume/">rapidly developed</a> an AI-driven platform that automates data mapping, significantly reducing the time required for tasks such as customer data onboarding and API payload normalization.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9194,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-24.png?w=622" alt="" class="wp-image-9194" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://lume.ai/blog/lume-raises-4-2m-seed-round-led-by-general-catalyst">The company's</a> innovative approach addresses the longstanding challenge of manual data integration, which has persisted for decades. By leveraging AI, Lume's platform understands the nuances between different data systems, enabling seamless data translation and integration.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lume's co-founders, Nicolas Machado, Nebyou Zewde, and Robert Ross, bring a wealth of experience and expertise to the company. Their leadership has been instrumental in developing a platform that serves a diverse clientele, from startups to large enterprises.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Lume continues to expand, the company is actively seeking engineers who are passionate about solving complex problems at the intersection of data and AI. This presents an exciting opportunity for professionals eager to contribute to a transformative solution in the data integration space.</p>
