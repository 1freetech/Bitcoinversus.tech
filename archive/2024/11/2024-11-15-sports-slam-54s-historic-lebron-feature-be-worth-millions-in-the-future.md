<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 2001, SLAM magazine's <a href="https://www.slamonline.com/news/nba/ohio-player-lebron-james-first/">54th issue</a> featured Shaquille O'Neal on the cover, but its true significance lies in introducing a young LeBron James to a national audience. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8721,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-15-145719-1.png?w=573" alt="" class="wp-image-8721" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At just 16 years old, LeBron was highlighted in a feature that marked his first appearance in a major publication, setting the stage for his future stardom.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8713,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-15-155408.png?w=434" alt="" class="wp-image-8713" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of November 15, 2024, LeBron James is <a href="https://www.statmuse.com/nba/ask/lebron-james-stats-in-2025-season">averaging</a> 23.2 points, 7.7 rebounds, and 8.9 assists per game in the 2024–2025 NBA season.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8716,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-15-151230.png?w=846" alt="" class="wp-image-8716" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><sup><em>The opening paragraph of the feature article, written by Ryan Jones, is something of legend: <br>"'Are you the one I’m asking for the autograph?'<br>This from the waitress working the dining room at the Diamond Grille, the old-school steakhouse standing on a hill just seconds from downtown Akron, OH. There are four people seated in the booth, only one of whom is 16 years old, 6 feet, 7 inches tall, and dressed like he just stepped off a basketball court. That would be LeBron James, and yes, he is the one."</em></sup></figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lebron James, the father of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/born-to-win-the-privileged-success-behind-alexandr-wang-and-bronny-james/">Bronny James</a>, has also accounted for three consecutive triple-doubles this season, becoming the oldest player in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/scottie-pippen-inquires-about-bitcoin-on-social-media/">NBA</a> history to do so.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While <a href="https://www.slamonline.com/news/nba/ohio-player-lebron-james-first/">SLAM 54</a> may not currently hold significant monetary value, its role in introducing LeBron James to the national stage could enhance its worth over time, especially as his legacy continues to grow.</p>
