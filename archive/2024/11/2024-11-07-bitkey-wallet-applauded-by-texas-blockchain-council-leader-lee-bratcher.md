<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Lee Bratcher, President of the Texas Blockchain Council, recently praised Bitkey, a self-custody Bitcoin wallet developed by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/proto-releases-updates-from-bitcoin-mining-development-kit/">Block, Inc</a>., which is led by <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">Jack Dorsey</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bratcher highlighted the wallet's advanced user interface, its seamless NFC integration, and intuitive design, describing it as a significant advancement for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/31/donald-trump-criticizes-bitcoin-taxation-advocates-for-bitcoin-friendly-reforms/">Bitcoin users</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Bitkey wallet is designed to enhance user control over Bitcoin assets through its three-part security system that includes a mobile app, hardware device, and recovery tools. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This system supports true self-custody, allowing users to manage their Bitcoin securely without relying on custodial platforms, which can pose risks related to hacks and restricted access.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/lee_bratcher/status/1849553883095335260","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitkey leverages a multi-signature security model involving three keys: a mobile key for convenience, a hardware key for larger transactions, and a server key that aids in wallet recovery while maintaining user control over funds. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This combination ensures that no single key breach can compromise the wallet, promoting both ease of use and robust security. The wallet's strategic partnerships with platforms like Cash App, Coinbase, and MoonPay further enhance its accessibility and functionality, solidifying its position in the market as a user-friendly self-custody solution.</p>
