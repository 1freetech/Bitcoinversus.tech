<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://ir.geniusgroup.net/">Genius Group Limited</a> (NYSE American: GNS), an AI-powered education and acceleration firm, has <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/genius-group-launches-bitcoin-treasury-with-purchase-of-110-bitcoin-for-10-million-1034030129">purchased</a> 110 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/16/screven-county-ga-secures-votes-with-bitcoin-blockchain/">Bitcoin</a> for $10 million, averaging $90,932 per Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This acquisition marks the commencement of the company's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin treasury strategy</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://ir.geniusgroup.net/news-events/press-releases/detail/156/genius-group-adopts-bitcoin-treasury-reserve-strategy">The company</a> plans to allocate 90% or more of its current and future reserves to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/mt-gox-transfers-2-2-billion-in-bitcoin-amid-repayment-delay/">Bitcoin</a>, with an initial target of $120 million. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This strategy aims to strengthen Genius Group's balance sheet and enhance shareholder returns.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Roger Hamilton will host a podcast on November 19 to discuss the company's Bitcoin-first plan, featuring insights from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/california-dmv-digitizes-car-titles-with-blockchain-technology/">blockchain</a> experts Thomas Power and Ian Putter. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The session will provide guidance for other companies considering <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Bitcoin</a> as a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/engineer-mark-harvey-explains-math-behind-microstrategy-3-year-bitcoin-plan/">treasury reserve</a>.</p>
