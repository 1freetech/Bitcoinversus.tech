<p style="text-transform:none">Former U.S. President Donald Trump has officially announced his plan to establish a government efficiency commission if he wins the 2024 presidential election. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative will focus on reducing federal waste, and tech billionaire Elon Musk is set to lead the commission. Trump emphasized the importance of conducting a complete audit of federal agencies, stating that this measure is necessary to identify inefficiencies and cut back on unnecessary spending.</p>
