<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Coinbase has successfully <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/coinbase-artificial-intelligence-ai-crypto-transactions-wallet-brian-armstrong?ai">conducted</a> its first <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/30/mastercard-launches-crypto-credential-to-simplify-bitcoin-transfers/">crypto transaction</a> between <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/23/max-keisers-radical-take-on-bitcoins-role-in-an-ai-dominated-future/">artificial intelligence</a> (AI) agents on its platform. This historic event marks a significant milestone in the integration of AI with blockchain technology. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The transaction, which took place on August 30, 2024, involved one AI agent using crypto tokens to acquire AI tokens from another AI agent. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These AI tokens, essentially data strings, enhance machine learning algorithms, thereby demonstrating the potential for AI to perform financial operations autonomously.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/28/coinbase-secures-registration-with-bank-of-spain-boosts-bitcoin-momentum/">Coinbase</a> CEO Brian Armstrong emphasized the importance of this development, stating that AI agents have traditionally been limited by their inability to engage in financial transactions. "They don't have a payment method to book you the plane ticket or hotel for your upcoming trip. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">They can't get through paywalls, promote their post on X with a paid ad, or use the growing network of paid APIs to integrate data they need," Armstrong explained. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, with the introduction of crypto wallets, AI agents can now utilize <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/14/ferrari-accelerates-into-the-future-with-bitcoin-payments/">USDC</a> on the Base network to transact with humans, merchants, and other AI systems. This advancement makes transactions instant, global, and fee-free, potentially revolutionizing how AI systems operate across various industries​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/21/analyzing-the-coinbase-legal-tussle-with-the-sec/">Coinbase</a> is pushing for further integration of AI models into <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/30/mastercard-launches-crypto-credential-to-simplify-bitcoin-transfers/">crypto wallets</a>, which could unlock new capabilities, such as AI-enabled checkouts. This progress is part of a broader trend where companies are exploring ways to enhance AI capabilities, particularly in handling tasks that involve financial transactions without human intervention​.</p>
