<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">LQR House Inc., a publicly traded <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/26/shopinbit-is-leading-the-bitcoin-e-commerce-revolution-in-europe/">e-commerce</a> platform specializing in the spirits and beverage industry, has <a href="https://invest.lqrhouse.com/press/lqr-house-inc-board-of-directors-approves-purchase-of-up-to-1m-bitcoin/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announced</a> plans to purchase up to $1 million worth of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/jack-dorseys-block-inc-expands-bitcoin-mining-operations/">Bitcoin</a> as part of its treasury management strategy. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, the company will begin accepting <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/detroit-to-accept-cryptocurrency-payments-for-taxes-and-fees/">cryptocurrency payments</a> on its platform, CWSpirits.com, and intends to retain up to $10 million of these crypto payments in Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">CEO Sean Dollinger stated that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/07/bitcoins-future-price-according-to-industry-experts/">Bitcoin's scarcity</a> and finite supply position it as a modern hedge against inflation and a safe haven in times of economic uncertainty.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move aligns with a broader trend of companies integrating Bitcoin into their financial strategies. For instance, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/18/microstrategy-to-raise-700-million-for-bitcoin-acquisition/">MicroStrategy</a> has significantly increased its Bitcoin holdings, recently acquiring 51,780 BTC for $4.6 billion, bringing its total reserves to 331,200 BTC.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Similarly, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Metaplanet</a> has issued bonds to finance additional Bitcoin acquisitions, reflecting a growing corporate confidence in digital assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By adopting Bitcoin, LQR House aims to strengthen its treasury and offer customers more flexibility in payment options, demonstrating a forward-thinking approach in the evolving financial landscape.</p>
