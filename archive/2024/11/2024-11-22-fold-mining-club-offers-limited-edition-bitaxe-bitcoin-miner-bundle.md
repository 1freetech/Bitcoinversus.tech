<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://blog.foldapp.com/fold-mining-club/">Fold</a> has launched the Fold Mining Club in collaboration with <a href="https://x.com/CryptoCloaks">CryptoCloaks</a>. This initiative aims to make home <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/quantum-bitcoin-mining-futuristic-heatsink-enhances-bitaxe-asic-chip-performance/">Bitcoin mining</a> accessible to a broader audience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8892,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/fold-bitaxe-1.webp?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-8892" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Fold Mining Club offers a limited-edition Bitaxe Supra 403 ASIC Solo Miner bundle, priced at $259. The package includes the miner, a custom Fold Mining Club case, a one-year Fold+ subscription, and coverage for one year of electricity costs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":8893,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/fold-bitaxe-back-1.webp?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-8893" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Bitaxe Supra 403 is an open-source, single ASIC chip Bitcoin miner featuring the BM1368 chip from the Antminer S21. It delivers a hash rate of approximately 733 GH/s at overclocked settings while consuming between 15 to 18 watts of power, making it one of the most efficient Bitcoin mining devices available.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Setting up the Bitaxe Supra 403 is straightforward. <a href="https://blog.foldapp.com/fold-mining-club/">Users</a> can connect the device to a standard power outlet, link it to their home Wi-Fi network, and begin mining. The miner supports both solo mining and pool mining configurations, providing flexibility for users.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Fold Mining Club aims to bring 100 solo miners online, contributing an additional 0.00006 EH/s to the Bitcoin network's total hashrate. This effort underscores a commitment to decentralization and community-driven mining initiatives.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/bitaxe-gamma-redefines-asic-mining-with-advanced-cooling-features/">Bitaxe</a> project, initiated by Skot, has gained popularity within the Bitcoin community for its open-source design and user-friendly approach to home mining. It offers enthusiasts an opportunity to engage with Bitcoin mining without the need for industrial-scale equipment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The collaboration between Fold and CryptoCloaks reflects a shared vision of making Bitcoin mining more accessible and decentralized, aligning with the broader goals of the cryptocurrency community.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Fold Mining Club <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/quantum-bitcoin-mining-futuristic-heatsink-enhances-bitaxe-asic-chip-performance/">Bitaxe</a> bundle is available for purchase through the CryptoCloaks store, with initial batches selling out rapidly, indicating strong interest from the community.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative represents a significant step toward democratizing Bitcoin mining, allowing individuals to participate in the network's security and operation from their homes.</p>
