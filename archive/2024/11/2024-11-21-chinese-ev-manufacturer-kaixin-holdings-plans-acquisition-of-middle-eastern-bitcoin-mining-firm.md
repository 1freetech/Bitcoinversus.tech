<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Kaixin Holdings, a prominent Chinese <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/02/general-motors-secures-625m-investment-in-lithium-mining-joint-venture/">electric vehicle manufacturer</a>, is in <a href="https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/kaixin-holdings-explores-strategic-alternatives-potential-acquisition-crypto-mining">advanced discussions</a> to acquire a controlling stake in a leading <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/11/economic-challenges-for-bitfufu-cloud-mining-clients/">Bitcoin mining</a> company based in the Middle East, as reported by Nasdaq.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The target company operates cost-efficient <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining machines</a> and offers comprehensive cloud hosting services, benefiting from a stable, long-term energy supply. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Kaixin Holdings, listed on NASDAQ under the ticker KXIN, is recognized for producing electric passenger and logistics vehicles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Middle Eastern <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ercot-approves-riots-1-gw-bitcoin-mining-capacity-in-corsicana/">cryptocurrency mining company</a> under consideration is noted for its efficient Bitcoin mining operations and end-to-end cloud hosting services. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Its access to a stable energy supply enhances operational resilience and cost efficiency, making it an attractive prospect for Kaixin's expansion into the crypto mining industry.</p>
