<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Trump Media &amp; Technology Group (TMTG), the parent company of Truth Social, is <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/d7f921d5-3668-4b6b-a98d-2681ad73610f?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reportedly</a> in advanced discussions to acquire <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/fold-mining-club-offers-limited-edition-bitaxe-bitcoin-miner-bundle/">cryptocurrency</a> trading platform Bakkt. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Financial Times <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/d7f921d5-3668-4b6b-a98d-2681ad73610f?utm_source=chatgpt.com">cites sources</a> familiar with the matter, indicating that the deal would be an all-stock transaction.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bakkt, established in 2018 by Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), <a href="https://nypost.com/2024/11/19/business/trump-media-nearing-deal-to-buy-crypto-firm-bakkt-report/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">offers</a> a platform for trading digital assets and currently lists 16 cryptocurrencies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company has faced profitability challenges, leading to considerations of winding down certain operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The potential acquisition aligns with President-elect Donald Trump's increasing involvement in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/22/jack-dorseys-block-inc-expands-bitcoin-mining-operations/">cryptocurrency sector</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Recently, Trump promoted World Liberty Financial, a decentralized finance venture, signaling a broader interest in digital assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Market reactions to the news have been significant. Bakkt's shares surged by 162% following reports of the acquisition talks, while TMTG's stock experienced a 16.7% increase. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Analysts suggest that the deal <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/d7f921d5-3668-4b6b-a98d-2681ad73610f?utm_source=chatgpt.com">could provide</a> TMTG with a foothold in the growing cryptocurrency market, potentially diversifying its revenue streams beyond social media.</p>
