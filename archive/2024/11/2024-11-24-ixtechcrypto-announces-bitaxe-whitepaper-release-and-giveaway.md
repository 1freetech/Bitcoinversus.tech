<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://ixtech.xyz/products/bitaxe-supra?utm_source=chatgpt.com">IxTechCrypto</a> has announced the release of the Limited Edition Bitaxe Whitepaper release, with stock expected to be available this week. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Interested individuals are encouraged to sign up on the company's website to receive notifications, as the whitepapers are anticipated to sell quickly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to the release, IxTechCrypto is <a href="https://discord.com/invite/osmu">hosting a giveaway</a> of the Bitaxe Whitepaper. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The first giveaway will take place on the Open Source Miners United (OSMU) Discord server, a community dedicated to open-source cryptocurrency mining solutions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The ITX <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/bitaxe-ultra-achieves-1-2-th-s-with-immersion-cooling-technology/">Bitaxe project</a>, developed by IxTechCrypto, <a href="https://ixtech.xyz/pages/about">focuses</a> on creating open-source ASIC miners for Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initiative aims to make cryptocurrency mining more accessible and decentralized by providing open-source hardware and software solutions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The OSMU community actively engages with global miners, developers, and tech enthusiasts to promote collaboration and knowledge sharing in the field of cryptocurrency mining. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By hosting the giveaway on the OSMU Discord server, IxTechCrypto seeks to foster greater community involvement and support for open-source mining projects.</p>
