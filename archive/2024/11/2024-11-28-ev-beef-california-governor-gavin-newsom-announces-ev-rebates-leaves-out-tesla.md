<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">California Governor Gavin Newsom has <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/california-governor-newsom-propose-clean-vehicle-rebate-if-trump-cuts-ev-tax-2024-11-25/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">announced</a> plans to reinstate state-level electric vehicle (EV) rebates if the incoming Trump administration eliminates the federal EV tax credit. This initiative aims to maintain support for EV adoption within the state. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Notably, the proposed program may exclude major manufacturers like Tesla to encourage competition and innovation among smaller EV producers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tesla CEO <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/24/the-dogefather-judge-rules-in-favor-of-elon-musk-in-dogecoin-case/">Elon Musk</a> has <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/california-governor-newsom-propose-clean-vehicle-rebate-if-trump-cuts-ev-tax-2024-11-25/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">criticized</a> this potential exclusion, highlighting that Tesla manufactures its EVs in California. He described the move as "insane" on social media.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">California has been a <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/4474c2a2-aa18-4a8e-b461-87961af464db?utm_source=chatgpt.com">significant market</a> for Tesla, with the Model Y being the top-selling vehicle in the state. However, Tesla's market share in California has declined this year, which some attribute to Musk's controversial political views and support for Trump.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The specifics of the <a href="https://apnews.com/article/b55ab3d35145384c1bb192cbda536b0a?utm_source=chatgpt.com">proposed rebate program</a>, including the criteria for manufacturer eligibility, are still under negotiation with the California Legislature. The program is expected to be funded by the state's Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund.</p>
