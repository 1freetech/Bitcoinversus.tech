<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Peter Todd shared a critical perspective on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ercot-approves-riots-1-gw-bitcoin-mining-capacity-in-corsicana/">Riot's upcoming 1 GW mining facility</a> in Corsicana, Texas, which is on track to become the world's largest. Todd suggests that, over time, such large-scale centralized facilities are economically and logistically inefficient compared to more distributed mining solutions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Todd, centralizing so much hash power in one location involves significant costs associated with transporting electricity from distant generation sites. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, these facilities face challenges in managing excess heat, necessitating costly immersion cooling systems due to the density of operations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Todd <a href="https://x.com/peterktodd/status/1854662584613401063">envisions</a> a future where Bitcoin mining could be decentralized and integrated into renewable energy systems, such as solar panels and wind turbines, potentially even as a heat source for homes or other facilities. He believes this approach will gain traction if <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/30/bitaxe-ultra-achieves-1-2-th-s-with-immersion-cooling-technology/">ASIC technology</a> matures and becomes manageable remotely. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, he acknowledges that regulatory environments will play a crucial role, as large-scale mining facilities like Riot’s could attract stricter oversight. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Todd contrasts this with the concept of smaller, decentralized mining setups, where 400,000 individuals might each run a 1kW miner, reducing regulatory exposure and increasing the resilience of Bitcoin's network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The original <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/10/ercot-approves-riots-1-gw-bitcoin-mining-capacity-in-corsicana/">announcement</a>, made by Bitcoin advocate Pierre Rochard, highlighted Riot's progress in developing Phase 1 of this facility, which will provide an initial 400 MW, with an ultimate target of 1 GW capacity.</p>
