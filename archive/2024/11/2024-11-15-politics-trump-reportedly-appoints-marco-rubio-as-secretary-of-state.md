<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">President-elect <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/donald-trump-up-6m-in-crypto-holdings-since-january/">Donald Trump </a>is <a href="https://www.wsj.com/politics/national-security/trump-selects-florida-rep-mike-waltz-as-national-security-adviser-104414fe?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reportedly</a> set to appoint Senator Marco Rubio of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/florida-cfo-proposes-bitcoin-investment-for-state-pension-funds/">Florida</a> as Secretary of State, marking the first time a Latino would hold this position. The New York Times <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2024/11/11/us/politics/trump-rubio-secretary-of-state.html">reported</a> on  that Trump appeared to have settled on Rubio for the role, though the decision is not yet final.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Rubio, who has served as a U.S. Senator since 2011, brings extensive experience in foreign policy, particularly concerning Latin America. He has been a vocal critic of authoritarian regimes in the region and has advocated for a strong U.S. presence to counter adversarial influences.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The potential appointment has elicited mixed reactions. Some supporters praise Rubio's foreign policy expertise and view his selection as a strategic move to strengthen U.S. diplomacy. Conversely, certain factions within the MAGA movement have expressed skepticism, questioning Rubio's alignment with Trump's "America First" agenda.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">If confirmed, Rubio's appointment would necessitate the selection of a successor for his Senate seat in Florida. Governor Ron DeSantis would be responsible for appointing an interim senator until a special election is held.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This development is part of Trump's broader efforts to assemble his cabinet ahead of his second term, with other notable appointments including Susie Wiles as White House Chief of Staff and Representative Mike Waltz as National Security Adviser.<br></p>
