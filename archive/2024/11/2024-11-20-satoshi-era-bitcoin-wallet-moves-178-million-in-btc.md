<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">A <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/18/visa-partners-with-coinbase-for-bitcoin-crypto-transactions/">Bitcoin wallet</a> dormant <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">since 2010</a> has <a href="https://coinatory.com/cryptocurrency-news/bitcoin-news/satoshi-era-bitcoin-wallet-moves-2000-btc-after-14-years-25283/">transferred</a> 2,000 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/03/fidelitys-fbtc-fund-now-holds-184219-btc/">BTC</a>, valued at approximately $178 million, to Coinbase on November 15, 2024.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This movement, tracked by Lookonchain, highlights the reactivation of Satoshi-era wallets, a rare occurrence that often captures significant attention in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/15/fbi-confiscates-electronics-from-polymarket-ceo-shayne-coplan-following-trump-election-prediction/">cryptocurrency market</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The wallet originally received <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/pennsylvania-house-proposes-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-act/">Bitcoin</a> when the price hovered below $0.10, a stark contrast to today’s valuation nearing $90,000 per BTC.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The owner, likely an early miner, maintained possession for 14 years, encompassing a period when Bitcoin’s creator, Satoshi Nakamoto, was still active online.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Such long-term holding <a href="https://coinatory.com/cryptocurrency-news/bitcoin-news/satoshi-era-bitcoin-wallet-moves-2000-btc-after-14-years-25283/">underscores</a> the value trajectory of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Bitcoin</a> and the resilience of early adopters who held through years of volatility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While millions of bitcoins mined in this era are considered lost, movements from dormant wallets remind the market of Bitcoin’s storied history.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Transfers of Satoshi-era coins to exchanges <a href="https://coinatory.com/cryptocurrency-news/bitcoin-news/satoshi-era-bitcoin-wallet-moves-2000-btc-after-14-years-25283/">frequently signal</a> selling intentions, prompting market speculation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the broader cryptocurrency market appeared unfazed by this whale’s activity.</p>
