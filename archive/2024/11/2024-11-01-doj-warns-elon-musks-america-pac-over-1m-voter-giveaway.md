<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/17/td-bank-slapped-with-record-3-billion-fine-for-money-laundering/">Department of Justice</a> (DOJ) has issued a warning to Elon Musk's America PAC, cautioning that its $1 million giveaway to registered voters in battleground states might breach federal election law. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The DOJ’s Election Crimes Branch, part of its Public Integrity Section, reportedly sent a letter addressing the legality of the PAC’s daily cash award, which targets registered voters who sign a petition supporting the First and Second Amendments. Legal experts have voiced concerns, noting that federal laws prohibit financial inducements for voter registration or turnout, a potential issue given the PAC’s focus on key swing states where voter influence could sway electoral outcomes​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">America PAC’s approach, which includes a $1 million daily award for signatories, raises specific legal questions since the offer is only available to residents of seven battleground states.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of now, the DOJ has yet to confirm if formal charges will follow or if recipients might be required to return their winnings. Representatives for Musk and his PAC have not issued a public response to the DOJ’s communication, and the DOJ has declined further comment on its legal stance​.</p>
