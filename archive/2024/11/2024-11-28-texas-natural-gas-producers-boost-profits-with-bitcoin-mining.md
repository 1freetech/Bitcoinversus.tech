<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the current <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/06/record-high-energy-curtailments-suggests-california-needs-bitcoin-mining/">energy market</a>, Texas natural gas producers are facing a significant disparity between selling gas to pipelines and utilizing it for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/29/the-evolution-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to recent data, selling natural gas to pipelines in Texas yields less than $2 per thousand cubic feet (Mcf). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9989,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-59.png?w=598" alt="" class="wp-image-9989" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In contrast, using the same gas to power Bitcoin mining operations with three-year-old hardware, such as the S19j Pro or M30s+ models, can generate approximately $5 per Mcf. This approach offers over 120% more value for the same energy input.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Energy Information Administration (EIA) <a href="https://ycharts.com/indicators/txnatural_gas_industrial_price?utm_source=chatgpt.com">reports</a> that as of October 2024, the industrial price of natural gas in Texas was $1.88 per Mcf, reflecting a 24.5% decrease from the previous month and a 30.63% decline from the same period last year.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This decline in natural gas prices has prompted producers to explore alternative revenue streams.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin mining has <a href="https://braiins.com/blog/from-flare-to-fortune-mining-bitcoin-off-grid-with-stranded-gas?utm_source=chatgpt.com">emerged</a> as a viable solution for monetizing stranded or flared natural gas. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By converting natural gas into electricity on-site, producers can power <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/05/argentinas-central-bank-hosts-bitcoin-mining-art-exhibit/">mining rigs</a> and generate Bitcoin, effectively turning otherwise wasted energy into a profitable asset. This method not only provides financial benefits but also contributes to environmental sustainability by reducing gas flaring.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Companies like 360 Mining have successfully <a href="https://coxtoday.smu.edu/2024/08/08/360-mining/?utm_source=chatgpt.com">implemented</a> this strategy. In the spring of 2024, when natural gas prices were around $1.60 per Mcf and Bitcoin prices exceeded $60,000, 360 Mining reported that 1,000 cubic feet of gas used in their Bitcoin mining operations produced approximately $22 in revenue.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/26/blockware-explains-how-bitcoin-price-drives-mining-profit-multiple/">profitability of Bitcoin mining</a> is influenced by several factors, including the price of Bitcoin, mining difficulty, and energy costs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As of November 27, 2024, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/27/bitcoin-advocate-anthony-pompliano-compares-bitcoin-to-gold-with-wings/">Bitcoin</a> is trading at $94,421, with an intraday high of $94,635 and a low of $90,748. This price level enhances the attractiveness of using natural gas for mining operations.</p>
