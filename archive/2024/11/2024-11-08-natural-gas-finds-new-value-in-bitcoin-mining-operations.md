<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/top-secret-bitcoin-mining-machine-revealed-in-west-texas/">Bitcoin mining operations</a> in Texas using natural gas have gained traction as a profitable alternative for oil and gas producers dealing with stranded gas or limited market access. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Recent data <a href="https://x.com/denverbitcoin/status/1830295600094486816">highlights</a> that in Texas, selling natural gas through traditional pipelines often yields less than $2 per thousand cubic feet (mcf) due to market dynamics. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By comparison, mining <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/gryphon-digital-leverages-methane-for-low-cost-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin</a> with older mining hardware, such as the S19j Pro or M30s+, can generate approximately $5 per mcf, showcasing a significant value increase of over 120% when utilizing gas directly onsite for mining instead of conventional sales​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The operational strategy behind using natural gas for Bitcoin mining involves mobile data centers or generators that convert gas into electricity to power mining equipment. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This method not only boosts revenue but also offers a practical solution to managing flared or excess gas, aligning with environmental and regulatory needs​.</p>
