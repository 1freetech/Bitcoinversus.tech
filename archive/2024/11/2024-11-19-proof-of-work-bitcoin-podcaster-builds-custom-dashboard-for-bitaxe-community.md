<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://x.com/Bart_Mol">Bart Mol</a>, a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/17/rumor-michael-saylor-wants-a-bitcoin-dividend-for-microstrategy-shareholdersvernment-sells-33m-btc-through-binance/">Bitcoin</a> Podcaster and innovator, has introduced a custom-built "Home Assistant dashboard" designed to streamline the monitoring of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/11/how-to-build-your-own-bitcoin-mining-data-center-bitaxe-edition-spread-sheet-included/">BitAxe mining</a> operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Unveiled on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/14/want-to-join-doge-prepare-for-an-80-hour-work-week/">X.com</a>, Mol shared the development, stating that his motivation stemmed from the challenge of tracking multiple BitAxes efficiently.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":9126,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/screenshot-2024-11-18-062911.png?w=666" alt="" class="wp-image-9126" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Key Features of the Dashboard</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The dashboard showcases advanced metrics, such as aggregated hashrate, power consumption, efficiency, and detailed stats for individual <a href="https://www.bitaxe.org/">BitAxe miners</a>. It also integrates data from the Satoshi Radio mining pool, presenting crucial insights into pool performance and operational status.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Each <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/08/bitaxe-startup-efficiency-peaks-explained/">BitAxe</a> miner's information is organized, featuring hashrate, power usage, temperature, and fan speed, ensuring that users can manage their devices with precision. Additionally, the firmware supports detailed performance tracking for different miner models, including BitAxe Supra and BitAxe Gamma.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Firmware Upgrade for Enhanced Functionality</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mol highlighted that the dashboard firmware is a work in progress and is built to enable customization for miners with varied setups. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As part of this ongoing project, feedback is welcomed from the mining community to refine its features further. By leveraging open-source technologies, this initiative aims to empower <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/28/bitcoin-miners-aid-texas-during-heatwave-curtail-energy-consumption-by-90/">Bitcoin miners</a> with a tailored and accessible solution.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Community Collaboration Encouraged</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In his post, Mol shared the <a href="https://github.com/satoshiradio/bitaxe-HAS-dashboard?tab=readme-ov-file">GitHub repository</a> for the dashboard, inviting other enthusiasts to join in the development process. "Monitor miner stats, pool performance, and more with ease—all for free and open source," he emphasized. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His approach underscores the collaborative spirit within the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/proto-releases-updates-from-bitcoin-mining-development-kit/">Bitcoin mining ecosystem</a>, fostering innovation through shared knowledge and tools.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For those eager to explore this development, the project can be accessed via <a href="https://github.com/satoshiradio/bitaxe-HAS-dashboard?tab=readme-ov-file">Bart Mol's GitHub</a>. Feedback and contributions are encouraged as this firmware continues to evolve.</p>
