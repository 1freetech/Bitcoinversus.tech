<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">TeraWulf Inc., a leader in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/gryphon-digital-leverages-methane-for-low-cost-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin Mining</a>, <a href="https://investors.terawulf.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/89/terawulf-appoints-john-larkin-as-director-of-investor">announced</a> the appointment of John Larkin as Senior Vice President and Director of Investor Relations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With over 25 years in capital markets, Larkin will report to CEO Paul Prager, leveraging his expertise to foster stronger institutional investor relations and enhance strategic communication for TeraWulf's growing operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His background includes pivotal roles at Connacht Asset Management as COO and at Susquehanna International Group, where he managed special situations and event-driven investment strategies​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">TeraWulf’s digital infrastructure, including the Lake Mariner facility in New York, positions it strategically in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/energy-demand-points-to-undervaluation-of-bitcoin-mining-companies/">Bitcoin mining</a> sector, utilizing renewable energy sources to support high-performance computing and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">cryptocurrency mining</a>​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As TeraWulf continues its growth trajectory, Prager highlighted Larkin's role as crucial for advancing the company's investor engagement strategies and communicating TeraWulf's financial goals and operational milestones. </p>
