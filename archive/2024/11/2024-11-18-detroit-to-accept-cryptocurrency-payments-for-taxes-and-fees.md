<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Detroit has <a href="https://www.usatoday.com/story/money/business/2024/11/07/detroit-to-accept-cryptocurrency-taxes-fees/76112596007/">announced</a> plans to accept <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency payments</a> for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/25/italy-to-raise-bitcoin-capital-gains-tax-to-42/">taxes</a> and city fees starting in mid-2025, positioning itself as the largest U.S. city to embrace <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/02/u-s-treasury-releases-report-on-digital-assets/">digital currencies</a> for municipal transactions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The city will collaborate with PayPal to facilitate these payments. PayPal will handle the acceptance of cryptocurrencies such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/11/09/bitcoin-hits-record-77000/">Bitcoin</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">Ethereum</a>, converting them into U.S. dollars before remitting the funds to the city. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This approach ensures that Detroit remains insulated from the volatility associated with digital currencies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">City Treasurer Nikhil Patel emphasized that the initiative aims to modernize payment options and attract technological innovation to Detroit. By offering diverse payment methods, the city seeks to enhance convenience for residents and businesses, while signaling its openness to emerging technologies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Detroit's decision aligns with a broader trend of municipalities exploring cryptocurrency for public payments. States like Colorado and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">Louisiana</a> have already implemented similar measures, accepting digital currencies for taxes and certain state services.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initiative reflects Detroit's commitment to embracing technological advancements and fostering an environment conducive to innovation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By integrating cryptocurrency payments, the city aims to position itself at the forefront of digital transformation in municipal services.</p>
