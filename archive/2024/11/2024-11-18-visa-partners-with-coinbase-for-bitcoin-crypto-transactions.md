<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/30/mastercard-launches-crypto-credential-to-simplify-bitcoin-transfers/">Visa</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/28/coinbase-secures-registration-with-bank-of-spain-boosts-bitcoin-momentum/">Coinbase</a> have <a href="https://crypto2community.com/crypto-news/visa-and-coinbase-partner-to-enable-faster-crypto-transactions/">announced</a> a partnership to enable instant <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency transactions</a> for U.S. and EU cardholders. This integration, leveraging Visa Direct, aims to enhance the efficiency of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/05/rumors-suggest-china-could-reconsider-crypto-ban/">crypto transactions</a> by allowing real-time funding of Coinbase wallets directly from Visa debit cards. </p>
