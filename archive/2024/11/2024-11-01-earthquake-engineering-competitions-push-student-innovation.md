<p style="text-transform:none">A recent earthquake-resistant <a href="https://www.hku.edu.tr/eng/news/the-excitement-of-the-final-has-begun-at-the-2024-dask-earthquake-resistant-building-design-competition/">model building </a><a href="https://www.hku.edu.tr/eng/news/the-excitement-of-the-final-has-begun-at-the-2024-dask-earthquake-resistant-building-design-competition/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">competition</a> brought together civil engineering students to design and construct structures that can withstand simulated seismic activity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The focus is on assessing how well these structures withstand seismic forces, which is crucial in determining the winning designs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The models that successfully pass the shake tests without collapsing are further evaluated on economic and aesthetic criteria to select the best-performing structure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The DASK Earthquake-Resistant Building Design Competition, emphasized the importance of earthquake-resilient infrastructure in disaster-prone regions. Models that successfully passed the earthquake simulation tests were judged on economic and aesthetic criteria.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The event <a href="https://www.hku.edu.tr/eng/news/the-excitement-of-the-final-has-begun-at-the-2024-dask-earthquake-resistant-building-design-competition/">highlighted</a> the critical role that innovative engineering solutions play in reducing earthquake-related damage and ensuring safety.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The competition provides a platform for students to showcase their skills. It also contributes to the broader goal of developing technologies and methodologies that can be used to build safer, more resilient structures in earthquake-prone areas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The hands-on experience gained from these events is invaluable in preparing the next generation of engineers to tackle real-world challenges​.</p>
