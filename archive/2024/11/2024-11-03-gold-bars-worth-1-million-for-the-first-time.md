<p style="text-transform:none">For the first time in history, a single gold bar has <a href="https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/investing/2024/08/19/gold-bars-are-worth-a-million-dollars-for-the-first-time/">reached the milestone</a> value of $1 million. This occurred when the spot price of gold surged past $2,500 per troy ounce. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Typically, gold bars weigh around 400 troy ounces, making each worth over $1 million at the current price level.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The increase in gold prices is influenced by several factors, including <a href="https://preservegold.com/gold-bars-are-worth-a-million-dollars-for-the-first-time/">significant</a> purchases by central banks, which have accumulated nearly 483.3 tons of gold in the first half of 2024 alone. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, expectations of more lenient monetary policies from the U.S. Federal Reserve have contributed to the metal's price rally​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Gold bars vary slightly in weight, usually ranging from 350 to 430 ounces in the London market, the global hub for gold trading. </p>
