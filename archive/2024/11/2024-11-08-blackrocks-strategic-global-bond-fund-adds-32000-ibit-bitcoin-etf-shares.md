<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/05/blackrock-leads-the-way-in-bitcoin-etf-market/">BlackRock</a>, the world's largest asset manager, has made a significant move in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/bitcoin-market-dominance-hits-new-cycle-high/">cryptocurrency market</a> by increasing its holdings in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/09/blackrock-invests-over-1-billion-in-bitcoin/">iShares Bitcoin Trust</a> (IBIT). As of its latest filing with the U.S. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/31/the-end-of-an-era-binance-ends-support-for-busd-stablecoin/">Securities and Exchange Commission</a> (SEC), BlackRock's Strategic Global Bond Fund now holds 32,000 shares of IBIT, up from previous levels.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The iShares <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> Trust has quickly risen to prominence, surpassing Grayscale's Bitcoin Trust (GBTC) to become the largest Bitcoin ETF globally, with over $20 billion in assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">BlackRock’s integration of IBIT into its bond-focused funds marks a strategic pivot, highlighting the firm's belief in the long-term value of Bitcoin. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The rise of BlackRock's IBIT also comes amid increasing global demand for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin ETFs</a>, with inflows reaching significant levels across various markets, including <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/27/hong-kongs-futu-securities-launches-bitcoin-and-crypto-trading/">Hong Kong</a>. BlackRock's dominance in this space reflects its ability to meet the growing demand for regulated cryptocurrency products.</p>
