<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><br>Jiang Xinyu, widely <a href="https://coingape.com/trending/friedcat-shocking-bitcoin-mystery-biggest-btc-miner-vanished/">known as</a> "Friedcat," was an illustrious figure in the early <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/14/bitcoin-mining-is-shaping-renewable-energy-frontiers/">Bitcoin community</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He entered the University of Science and Technology of China at just 14, earning a PhD in computer science by the time he was 15. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He swiftly became a cornerstone in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/15/max-keiser-starts-beef-with-sam-altman-calls-worldcoin-project-this-years-sbf/">cryptocurrency world</a> through his pioneering work with ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) miner machines starting in 2012, dramatically enhancing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/">Bitcoin mining efficiency</a> and profitability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Under his leadership, his company achieved remarkable success, capturing a substantial portion of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/08/bitcoin-leapfrogs-silver-in-etf-markets/">Bitcoin mining market</a> by 2013. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The value of the shares in his company soared, reflecting the booming interest and investment in the Bitcoin sector at the time. However, the pressure and expectations mounted as rapidly as his fame and fortune.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In 2014, Jiang's life took a dramatic and distressing turn. He vanished without a trace, leaving behind a cloud of rumors and speculation about his mental state, financial troubles, and possible motives for disappearing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His last known activity was related to his mention of a documentary about American history, which some speculated could be a clue to his whereabouts or state of mind.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite some activity in his <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/01/yellow-card-is-the-largest-bitcoin-exchange-in-africa/">cryptocurrency wallets</a> in 2017, Jiang has not been seen or heard from since his disappearance. The mystery of Friedcat remains one of the most intriguing and unsolved puzzles in the cryptocurrency community, leaving many to wonder about the fate of the once-revered Bitcoin pioneer.</p>
