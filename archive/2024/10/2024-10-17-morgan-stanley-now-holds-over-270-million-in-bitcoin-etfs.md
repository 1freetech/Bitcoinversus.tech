<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/morgan-stanley-becomes-first-major-bank-to-offer-bitcoin-etfs-to-clients/">Morgan Stanley</a> has <a href="https://bitcoinnews.com/markets/morgan-stanley-270m-bitcoin-etfs/">disclosed</a> that it has invested over $272 million in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/04/state-of-wisconsin-investment-board-allocates-2-of-assets-to-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin exchange-traded funds </a>(ETFs), reflecting a growing confidence in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/14/italian-bank-banca-sella-introduces-bitcoin-trading/">cryptocurrency</a> among traditional financial institutions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The investment is seen as part of a broader institutional shift toward <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/04/bernstein-predicts-90000-bitcoin-surge-if-trump-wins-2024-election/">Bitcoin</a>, with firms like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/06/blackrock-discloses-164-million-bitcoin-purchase/">BlackRock</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/11/fidelitys-wise-origin-bitcoin-fund-holds-over-34000-btc-worth-1-3-billion/">Fidelity</a> also heavily involved in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">Bitcoin ETF space</a>​.</p>
