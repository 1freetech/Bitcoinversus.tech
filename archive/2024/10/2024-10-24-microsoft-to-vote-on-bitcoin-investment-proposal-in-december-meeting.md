<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/20/microsofts-ai-expert-jaron-lanier-advocates-for-data-dignity-amid-bitcoin-revolution/">Microsoft</a> is set to vote on a shareholder proposal related to Bitcoin at its upcoming annual meeting in December. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://microsoft.gcs-web.com/node/33151/html">proposal</a>, which will be decided during the meeting, is titled "Assessment of Investing in Bitcoin," and was officially revealed in a recent SEC filing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This proposal reflects growing interest among shareholders regarding cryptocurrency, particularly <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-advocate-davinci-jeremie-predicts-early-retirement-with-one-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>, as an investment opportunity for Microsoft, which has already ventured into <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/21/revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-how-ai-and-quantum-computing-meet/">blockchain technologies</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency partnerships​</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The meeting is expected to include discussions on several other key shareholder proposals, but the Bitcoin investment assessment has gained notable attention due to Bitcoin’s volatile but expanding role in institutional finance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Microsoft has not indicated its position on the proposal yet, but it is expected that both sides—those in favor of Bitcoin adoption and those cautioning against it—will present strong arguments​.</p>
