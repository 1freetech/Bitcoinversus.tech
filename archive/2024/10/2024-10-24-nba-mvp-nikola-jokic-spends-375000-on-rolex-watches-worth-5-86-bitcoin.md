<p style="text-transform:none">After winning a bronze medal at the 2024 Paris Olympics, Nikola Jokic decided to <a href="https://basketnews.com/news-210869-nikola-jokic-reportedly-bought-11-rolex-watches-for-his-teammates.html">reward</a> his Serbian National teammates with an impressive gift. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Denver Nuggets superstar purchased 11 Rolex watches, spending a total of $375,500. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In Bitcoin terms, this purchase equates to approximately 5.86 BTC, given the current price of Bitcoin at around $64,000. </p>
