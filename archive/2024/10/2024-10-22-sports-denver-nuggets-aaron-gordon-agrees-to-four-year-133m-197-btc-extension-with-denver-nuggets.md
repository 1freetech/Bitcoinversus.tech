<p style="text-transform:none">Aaron Gordon has secured a four-year, $133 million contract extension with the Denver Nuggets, reaffirming his position as a cornerstone of the team. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Gordon’s new deal amounts to approximately 197 Bitcoin at <a href="https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/bitcoin/">current market rates</a>, highlighting the strengthening of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/07/crucial-graphic-highlights-dollar-depreciation-relative-to-real-estate-and-bitcoin/">bitcoin against the dollar</a>.</p>
