<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/05/culture-ai-transforms-modern-hip-hop-into-old-school-rb/">Artificial intelligence</a> may soon replace human coding tasks, <a href="https://www.geekwire.com/2024/aws-exec-explains-how-ai-agents-are-driving-a-radical-shift-in-the-world-of-coding/">according to</a> Amazon Web Services CEO Matt Garman<strong>.</strong> </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent internal discussion, Garman suggested that within the next 24 months, AI could automate much of the coding work currently done by developers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This potential shift would allow developers to focus more on innovative and creative tasks rather than routine coding work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Garman emphasized that coding is merely a language used to communicate with computers, not the core skill itself. Instead, he <a href="https://futurism.com/the-byte/aws-ceo-human-devs-ai">highlighted</a> the importance of innovation and building valuable solutions for end users. </p>
