<p style="text-transform:none">A <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/05/culture-ai-transforms-modern-hip-hop-into-old-school-rb/">sound engineer</a> is getting ready to go live and mine <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-advocate-davinci-jeremie-predicts-early-retirement-with-one-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> at the same time by setting up a portable <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/28/university-of-wyoming-establishes-bitcoin-research-institute/">Bitcoin mining rig</a>. The rig, which is mounted on a rack, uses free electricity from the venue.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The setup involves several crucial components to ensure <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/10/miningstore-ceo-jp-baric-is-revolutionizing-bitcoin-mining-with-energy-efficient-solutions/">efficient mining</a> during live events.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The picture shown on the thumbnail features high-performance Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs) built for SHA-256 hashing, which allows <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These ASIC miners are placed in a rack-mounted configuration along with power distribution units (PDUs) to manage and monitor power flow. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Given the high energy demands of mining, venues often provide the advantage of free electricity, reducing operational costs significantly​.</p>
