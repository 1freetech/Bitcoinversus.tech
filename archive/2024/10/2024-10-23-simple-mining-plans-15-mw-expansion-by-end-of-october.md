<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/simple-mining-targets-125-mw-bitcoin-mining-hasrate-by-q1-2025/">Simple Mining</a>, a major player in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/proto-releases-updates-from-bitcoin-mining-development-kit/">Bitcoin mining</a> space, is on track for significant expansion by the end of October 2024.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Chief Operating Officer Nick Garland, Simple Mining will complete an additional 5 MW of capacity next week, bringing their total hashrate under management to 50 MW. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Garland also shared that another 10+ MW will come online in November, keeping the company on course to meet its ambitious goal of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/simple-mining-targets-125-mw-bitcoin-mining-hasrate-by-q1-2025/">managing 125+ MW of hashrate by the end of Q1 2025</a>​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Simple Mining, headquartered in Cedar Falls, Iowa, has been a standout in the industry due to its focus on providing cost-effective mining solutions, with electricity rates as low as $0.08/kWh​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company's operations are known for their transparency, offering flexible contracts and precision billing, ensuring clients only pay for the time their miners are operational​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Simple Mining’s facilities also offer robust 24/7 onsite support and free repairs for the first 12 months of service​.</p>
