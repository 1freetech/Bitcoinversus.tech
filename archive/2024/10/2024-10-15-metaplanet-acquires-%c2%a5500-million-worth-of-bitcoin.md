<p style="text-transform:none">Japanese publicly traded company Metaplanet has <a href="https://www.tftc.io/">recently purchased</a> an additional ¥500 million (approximately $3.7 million) worth of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This acquisition boosts the company’s total holdings to 360.368 bitcoins. The investment is part of Metaplanet's strategy to increase its <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/11/michael-saylor-explains-how-bitcoin-will-reshape-corporate-balance-sheets/">Bitcoin reserves</a>, following a ¥1 billion loan secured for this purpose. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This move aligns with the growing trend of public companies using <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/microstrategy-plans-2-billion-equity-offering-to-purchase-bitcoin/">Bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset</a>. Metaplanet's total investment in Bitcoin now amounts to ¥3.45 billion.</p>
