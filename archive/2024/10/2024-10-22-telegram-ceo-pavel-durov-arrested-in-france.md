<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pavel Durov, the founder and CEO of Telegram, <a href="https://www.lemonde.fr/en/pixels/article/2024/08/25/telegram-ceo-pavel-durov-arrested-in-france-in-world-first-case_6721434_13.html">was arrested</a> on August 24, 2024, at Le Bourget airport near Paris. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">French authorities detained Durov as part of an ongoing investigation into the platform's lack of moderation, which they allege has allowed criminal activities, including drug trafficking, cyberbullying, and organized crime, to flourish. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Durov, who was traveling on a private jet, was apprehended shortly after landing and is expected to face legal proceedings in France​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The arrest marks a significant moment in global tech, as Telegram is one of the world's leading messaging apps, with nearly a billion users. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">French authorities initiated the investigation following concerns over Telegram's minimal cooperation with law enforcement, which had led to widespread criminal use of the platform. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The situation has sparked reactions from various governments, including Russia, which criticized France for detaining Durov without proper cooperation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Durov, a vocal opponent of government censorship, had previously left Russia after refusing to shut down opposition groups on VKontakte, a social media platform he co-founded.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His arrest in France underscores the increasing scrutiny tech platforms face over content moderation, particularly as Telegram continues to be used as a key communication tool in conflict zones and for illegal activities​.</p>
