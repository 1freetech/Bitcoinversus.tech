<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">South Korea’s Financial Services Commission (FSC) has taken a significant step towards approving spot cryptocurrency <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/vaneck-ceo-claims-bitcoins-value-could-reach-350000/">exchange-traded funds</a> (ETFs), <a href="https://thenewscrypto.com/south-korea-virtual-assets-committee-set-to-review-crypto-etfs/">officially</a> announcing the formation of a Virtual Asset Committee on October 10, 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The committee, composed of public and private sector experts, will act as an advisory body to review and approve <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/13/strike-ceo-jack-mallers-criticizes-ethereum-and-other-cryptocurrencies/">Bitcoin and Ethereum</a> spot ETFs, alongside addressing corporate virtual accounts for digital assets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The introduction of spot ETFs in South Korea could have a <a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/south-korea-reconsider-bitcoin-etfs/">profound</a> impact on the market, particularly in reducing the so-called "Kimchi premium," a phenomenon where <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/politics-putin-signs-law-legalizing-bitcoin-mining-in-russia/">cryptocurrencies</a> trade at higher prices domestically than in international markets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to CryptoQuant CEO Ki Young Ju, the approval of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/19/decoding-bitcoin-with-max-keiser-spotlight-on-blackrock-and-spot-etfs/">spot Bitcoin ETFs</a> will help mitigate this gap by attracting more market makers and arbitrage funds​.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In addition to its focus on ETFs, the FSC has <a href="https://www.blockstreet.co.kr/news/view?ud=2024101110003860426">launched</a> the Digital Asset User Protection Foundation, a non-profit aimed at assisting users in recovering assets when service providers cease operations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative forms part of broader regulatory efforts designed to enhance investor protection in South Korea’s evolving <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/bitcoin-market-dominance-hits-new-cycle-high/">cryptocurrency market</a>.​</p>
