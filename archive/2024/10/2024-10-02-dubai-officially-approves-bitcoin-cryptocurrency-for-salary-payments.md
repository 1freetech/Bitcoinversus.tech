<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a landmark decision, the Dubai Court of First Instance has officially recognized <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> as a valid form of salary payment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This groundbreaking ruling signifies a major shift in the legal approach to digital currencies in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/03/04/intel-brics-nations-accelerate-plans-for-common-currency/">United Arab Emirates</a>, particularly in employment contracts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://cryptonews.com/news/dubai-court-rules-in-favor-of-salaries-payment-in-crypto.htm">The case</a> centered on an employment dispute where an employee claimed unpaid wages in the form of EcoWatt tokens, a cryptocurrency specified in their contract. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The court <a href="https://cryptoslate.com/dubai-approves-crypto-salaries-in-landmark-court-ruling/">ruled</a> in favor of the employee, thereby setting a precedent that supports the use of digital currencies like Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies for salary payments under <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/24/faith-meets-finance-the-islamic-debate-on-bitcoin/">UAE law​</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This decision not only opens doors for the broader adoption of cryptocurrencies in the region but also aligns with the evolving nature of financial transactions in the global economy.</p>
