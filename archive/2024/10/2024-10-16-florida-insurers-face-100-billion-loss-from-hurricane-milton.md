<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/hurricane-season-sparks-interest-in-aquadam-flood-protection/">Hurricane Milton</a> could <a href="https://www.foxweather.com/weather-news/tracking-hurricane-milton-florida-southeast">deliver</a> up to $100 billion in losses for global insurers, driving a surge in reinsurance premiums by 2025. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Category 4 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/hurricane-season-sparks-interest-in-aquadam-flood-protection/">hurricane</a> is expected to make landfall near Florida’s Gulf Coast, with Tampa in its path. Milton follows closely behind Hurricane Helene, which struck just two weeks ago. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Analysts at Morningstar DBRS estimate insured losses between $60 billion and $100 billion, putting pressure on smaller insurers, though large national firms remain more shielded​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Citizens Property Insurance, Florida’s public insurer, is set to <a href="https://www.foxweather.com/weather-news/tracking-hurricane-milton-florida-southeast">cover</a> much of the damage, while Moody's RMS Event Response estimates combined losses from Milton and Helene to range from $35 billion to $55 billion. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Smaller regional insurers face increased earnings pressure, as many national insurers have scaled back in Florida due to profitability issues​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Governor Ron DeSantis has declared a state of emergency for 51 counties, with evacuations underway across Florida. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With airports and roads packed, state resources are being mobilized for recovery efforts, while storm surges and destructive winds threaten catastrophic damage​.</p>
