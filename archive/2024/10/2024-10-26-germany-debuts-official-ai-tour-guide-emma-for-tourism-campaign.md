<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Germany has <a href="https://www.travelweek.ca/news/gntb-launches-emma-its-first-ai-generated-influencer/">introduced</a> a groundbreaking AI-generated travel influencer, Emma, as part of a strategic move to boost its tourism sector. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The German National Tourist Board (GNTB) developed Emma to serve as a virtual guide, offering visitors personalized travel recommendations through social media platforms like Instagram.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x0EMXhk60CM","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative marks a significant step in digital tourism, where technology meets cultural promotion. Emma, an engaging brand ambassador, interacts with travelers, offering insights into Germany’s rich history, iconic landmarks, and hidden gems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Developed in partnership with the agency Startup Creator, Emma represents a blend of advanced artificial intelligence and influencer marketing. Fluent in over 20 languages, Emma is designed to engage with potential tourists, answering queries and providing recommendations around the clock. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/visitgermanyuk/status/1846915850298630523","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Her role extends beyond being a mere information source, as she aims to act as a personal travel companion who can craft itineraries based on individual preferences and interests. This dynamic approach seeks to revolutionize how tourists plan and experience their trips to Germany.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Emma’s debut has received positive feedback for her potential to personalize tourism, she has also faced criticism. Some users argue that AI-generated influencers lack the authenticity of human influencers, who form emotional connections with their audiences. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the GNTB has clarified that Emma will complement, not replace, real-life influencers. Emma’s social media presence is expected to grow, especially with her ability to access real-time data from GNTB's open data project, enabling her to provide up-to-the-minute travel information by year’s end.</p>
