<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">National Public Data <a href="https://www.engadget.com/cybersecurity/national-public-data-confirms-breach-that-exposed-americans-social-security-numbers-100046695.html">confirmed </a>that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/26/space-force-guardian-jason-p-lowerys-twitter-account-hacked/">hackers</a> have stolen a vast trove of personal information, including the Social Security numbers of nearly all Americans. The breach involved about 2.7 billion personal records, which were subsequently leaked online, posing a severe risk of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/26/california-to-join-states-supporting-digital-drivers-licenses-and-ids-in-apple-wallet/">identity theft</a> and fraud.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">National Public Data noted the intrusion was due to a third-party's actions, with the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/21/hackers-use-mcdonalds-instagram-for-grimace-crypto-scam/">hackers</a> first accessing the data in late December 2023. They then began selling it in April 2024, with parts of the data surfacing over the summer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company coordinated with law enforcement to address the incident and advised affected individuals on protective measures against potential financial fraud​.</p>
