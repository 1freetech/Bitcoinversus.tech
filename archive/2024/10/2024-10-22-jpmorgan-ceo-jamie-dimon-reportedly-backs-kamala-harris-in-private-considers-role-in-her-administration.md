<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/22/citi-and-jpmorgan-lead-10b-financial-package-for-openai/">JPMorgan Chase</a> CEO Jamie Dimon, known for his significant influence in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/28/analysis-bitcoin-hyper-regulation-may-stall-global-economic-growth/">global finance</a>, is privately backing Vice President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/14/politics-kamala-harris-accepts-cryptocurrency-donations-via-coinbase/">Kamala Harris</a> as she eyes a potential 2024 run. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to a <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2024/10/22/business/jamie-dimon-jpmorgan-harris-trump.html">report</a> from the New York Times, Dimon has maintained a close relationship with both Democratic and Republican leaders, providing counsel on various financial and policy matters. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While he has not publicly endorsed any candidate, <a href="https://x.com/carlquintanilla/status/1848749423922471387">insiders</a> suggest he is open to playing a role in a future Harris administration if she wins​.</p>
