<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Northwood Space, a <a href="https://www.ainvest.com/news/northwood-innovating-ground-stations-for-faster-satellite-communication-2410100055330cae5c55531f/">startup</a> led by CEO Bridgit Mendler, is making strides in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/03/31/bitcoin-on-mars-monetizing-the-red-frontier/">aerospace industry </a>by developing scalable ground stations capable of quickly communicating with satellites. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These stations, equipped with phased-array antennas, are designed for fast setup—within days—offering satellite operators rapid access to orbiting systems. Northwood's innovation is centered on enabling seamless satellite communication for data transfer between Earth and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/31/to-the-moon-literally-bitmex-plans-on-sending-1-bitcoin-to-the-moon/">space</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Based in El Segundo, California, Northwood focuses on mass-producing ground stations that are smaller, scalable, and easier to deploy than traditional models. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company recently passed a key test by successfully connecting with Planet Labs satellites, demonstrating the effectiveness of its technology. The goal is to roll out these systems by mid-2025, ensuring they meet the growing demands of the space industry​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With a background in both entertainment and academia, Mendler—who co-founded Northwood with Griffin Cleverly and Shaurya Luthra—has positioned the company as a disruptor in satellite communications.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Investors such as Founders Fund, Andreessen Horowitz, and Also Capital back Northwood, highlighting its potential to redefine satellite connectivity on a global scale​.</p>
