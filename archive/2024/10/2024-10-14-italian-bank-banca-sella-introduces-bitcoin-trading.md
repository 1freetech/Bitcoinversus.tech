<p style="text-transform:none">Italy's Banca Sella, one of the oldest financial institutions in the country, has officially <a href="https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2024/08/19/old-italian-bank-banca-sella-goes-all-into-bitcoin-trading/">introduced</a> Bitcoin trading services through its mobile banking platform, Hype. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/banca-sella-bitcoin-trading-to-customers/">This move</a> marks a significant milestone in the integration of traditional <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">banking with cryptocurrency</a>, providing more than 1.3 million customers direct access to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/03/05/bitcoin-crosses-the-1-trillion-market-cap-threshold/">Bitcoin trading</a> without needing external exchanges. The service is currently in beta testing for HypePlus customers but is expected to expand to all users in the coming days​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Customers will be able to manage their <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/16/honda-embraces-bitcoin-starts-new-chapter-in-automotive-transactions/">Bitcoin transactions</a> securely within a regulated environment, and Banca Sella has implemented tiered trading caps to ensure a balanced trading experience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Banca Sella continues to innovate, other financial institutions may follow suit, further integrating cryptocurrencies into the mainstream financial ecosystem​.</p>
