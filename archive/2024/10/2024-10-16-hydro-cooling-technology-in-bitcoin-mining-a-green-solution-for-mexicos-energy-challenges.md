<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>MicroBT</strong> and <strong>HeatCore</strong> have partnered to introduce a groundbreaking hydro cooling system designed to make <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/26/exclusive-hydro-cooling-is-the-future-of-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining</a> more energy-efficient and environmentally friendly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This innovative technology, named <strong>Methane Green</strong>, aims to reduce methane emissions while optimizing power consumption. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The system is particularly beneficial in Mexico, where <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/23/nodal-power-turns-landfill-methane-into-green-energy-for-bitcoin-mining/">natural flare gas</a> can be harnessed for energy production, reducing air contamination and promoting sustainable practices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">HeatCore's Integrated Energy and Hashing System (IEHS) <a href="https://heatcore.tech/2022/10/20/microbt-and-heat-core-inaugurate-first-hydro-cooling-and-heat-recovery-pilot-in-usa/">offers</a> a dual benefit by using water cooling to dissipate heat and recycle it for various applications, such as heating residential buildings, agricultural greenhouses, and even seawater desalination.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">MicroBT and HeatCore's initiative is part of a larger effort to make Bitcoin mining more sustainable globally. By leveraging flaring gas for electricity generation and utilizing advanced cooling technologies, this system represents a significant step toward eco-friendly mining. </p>
