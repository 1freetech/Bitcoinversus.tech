<p style="text-transform:none">As Florida and Texas faced multiple hurricanes in September and October of 2024, Randy Wagner’s <a href="https://youtu.be/ednxK3JPsWQ">actions</a> from eight years ago are proving more relevant than ever. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Back in 2016, Wagner used an $8,300 AquaDam to shield his Texas home from rising floodwaters, saving it from severe damage. His innovative approach highlights how water-filled barriers can serve as a cost-effective solution to protect homes during flood events. As hurricanes continue to wreak havoc, AquaDam technology is now being considered as an option for those facing similar natural disasters</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As water levels rose to 27 inches around his home, Wagner’s AquaDam held strong, protecting his home from an estimated $150,000 in damages.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The water-filled tubes, made of sturdy plastic and fabric, formed a flexible barrier that adapted to the contours of the ground, preventing floodwaters from penetrating his property.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The AquaDam relies on the principle of displacement—by filling its tubes with water, it creates a stable barrier against incoming floods. The weight of the water inside the AquaDam presses the tubes firmly against the ground, which prevents floodwaters from seeping underneath. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">This innovative solution, which can be deployed quickly and moved as needed, offers a cost-effective option for flood-prone regions like Texas and Florida. Emergency management agencies might consider broader adoption of AquaDam-like barriers as hurricanes and other natural disasters continue to wreak havoc in vulnerable areas.</p>
