<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Amazon is making a significant <a href="https://observer.com/2024/10/amazon-google-microsoft-nuclear-power-ai-ambitions/">investment</a> in nuclear energy by committing $500 million to build <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">small modular reactors</a> (SMRs), aiming to provide up to 5,000 megawatts of power for its AI data centers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This unprecedented move highlights Amazon's efforts to meet the rising electricity demands driven by artificial intelligence while maintaining its commitment to achieving carbon neutrality by 2040. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By investing in SMRs through partnerships with Energy Northwest and Dominion Energy, Amazon is setting the stage for a more sustainable, AI-powered future​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The initial <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/15/small-modular-reactors-are-the-future-of-nuclear-energy/">SMR</a> deployments will generate up to 320 megawatts, enough to power over 770,000 homes, with potential expansion to nearly 1 gigawatt. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Amazon's growing reliance on AI has increased the need for clean, scalable energy sources, and nuclear power offers a promising solution without contributing to carbon emissions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This initiative mirrors similar moves by tech companies like Google and Microsoft, all aiming to power AI-driven operations with reliable, carbon-free energy​.</p>
