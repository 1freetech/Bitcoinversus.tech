<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/18/googles-advertising-policy-update-paves-the-way-for-bitcoin-trusts/">Google</a> has quietly removed the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/29/major-institutions-control-12-of-all-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> price chart from its search results. While <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">crypto enthusiasts</a> rely heavily on real-time data, Google’s decision leaves users seeking alternatives. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Both Bing and Brave continue to display <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/28/bitcoin-rsi-chart-suggests-bullish-outlook/">Bitcoin charts</a>, a bullish sign for investors looking for consistent updates.</p>
