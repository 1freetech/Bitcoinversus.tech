<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Presidential candidate <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Donald Trump</a> has seen a significant rise in his <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/29/coinbase-secures-its-dominance-with-25-billion-in-bitcoin-holdings/">cryptocurrency holdings</a> in 2024, with a total increase of $6.1 million since the start of the year. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This surge has largely been driven by his investment in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/21/hackers-use-mcdonalds-instagram-for-grimace-crypto-scam/">meme coins</a>, which now make up over 65% of his portfolio​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Among his most prominent <a href="https://platform.arkhamintelligence.com/explorer/entity/donald-trump">holdings</a> is the MAGA Memecoin, which alone accounts for a substantial portion of his portfolio's growth​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Trump’s portfolio also <a href="https://coinpedia.org/news/trump-crypto-holdings-analyzing-the-impact-of-meme-coins-on-his-portfolio/">includes</a> other notable assets such as Wrapped Ether (WETH) and Ethereum (ETH), valued collectively at over $2 million. These holdings reflect a broader diversification strategy in the cryptocurrency space, combining speculative meme coins with more established digital assets.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The increase in his crypto assets highlights the growing interest in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/bitfinex-named-sole-entity-for-restitution-in-2016-hack-of-120000-btc/">cryptocurrencies</a> tied to high-profile figures like Trump, particularly as the 2024 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/23/politics-ripple-co-founder-chris-larsen-endorses-kamala-harris-for-president/">U.S. presidential election</a> approaches. </p>
