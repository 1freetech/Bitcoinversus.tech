<p style="text-transform:none">A Moscow court has <a href="https://bmmagazine.co.uk/news/russia-fines-google-20-decillion-more-than-all-the-money-in-the-world-in-escalating-sanctions-dispute/">imposed</a> an unprecedented $20 decillion fine on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/13/google-drops-bitcoin-chart-from-search/">Google</a> for allegedly blocking Russian pro-Kremlin YouTube channels. This fine—a number almost inconceivably large—exceeds the global economy's worth.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The lawsuit, driven by various Russian broadcasters, stemmed from Google’s removal of Russian state media channels under Western sanctions, intensifying legal tensions between the tech giant and Russian authorities. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the penalty, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/18/googles-advertising-policy-update-paves-the-way-for-bitcoin-trusts/">Google</a> appears unfazed, continuing its essential services in Russia.</p>
