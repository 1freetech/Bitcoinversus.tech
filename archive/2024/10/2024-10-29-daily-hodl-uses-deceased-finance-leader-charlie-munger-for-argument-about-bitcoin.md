<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Charlie Munger, who passed away recently, has long been a significant figure in the financial world, known for his outspoken criticism of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite his passing, Munger's harsh views on the cryptocurrency <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2023/10/09/berkshire-hathaways-charlie-munger-slams-bitcoin-says-most-crypto-investments-going-to-zero-report/">continue to be referenced</a> in discussions about Bitcoin's future. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Daily Hodl recently <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2024/08/23/bitcoin-price-by-2050-52-million-treasure-or-zero-dollar-trap/">highlighted</a> Munger’s strong stance against <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/09/blackrock-invests-over-1-billion-in-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>, using his critiques to argue that the digital currency might eventually collapse to zero.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Munger’s opinions were well-documented during his lifetime, relying on a deceased figure's critiques to shape current debates can seem problematic​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/saylor/status/1494044455678132224","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Munger believed that Bitcoin undermined <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/04/bitcoin-and-the-history-of-the-u-s-dollar/">national currencies</a> and could lead to irrational speculative behavior. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the continued use of his posthumous critiques in arguments about <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin</a> raises questions about the relevance of past opinions in a rapidly evolving financial landscape. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">On the other side of the debate, predictions for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/07/bitcoins-value-strengthens-in-relation-to-the-us-national-debt/">Bitcoin's value</a> in the coming decades continue to be wide-ranging. Some experts, such as those at <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/vaneck-ceo-claims-bitcoins-value-could-reach-350000/">VanEck</a>, suggest that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/27/bitcoin-outpaces-internets-adoption-rate/">Bitcoin</a> could reach as high as $52 million per coin by 2050, driven by factors like de-dollarization and its potential role as a global reserve currency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/07/intel-vaneck-releases-major-prediction-for-bitcoin-price/">bullish outlook presented by VanEck</a> is counterbalanced by the lingering echoes of Munger's skepticism, which resonate with those wary of Bitcoin's speculative nature​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While Munger's warnings remain part of the conversation, it is important to recognize that these perspectives reflect a generational take that may not fully capture the evolving dynamics of the digital currency market​.</p>
