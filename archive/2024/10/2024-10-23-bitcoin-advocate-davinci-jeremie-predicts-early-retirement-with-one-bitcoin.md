<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/27/davinci-jeremie-claims-satoshi-nakamoto-may-have-been-former-nsa-employee/">Davinci Jeremie</a>, a well-known figure in the cryptocurrency world, recently made a bold statement on social media: owning just one <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/blackrocks-growing-bitcoin-holdings-a-strategic-bet-on-digital-assets/">Bitcoin</a> could be enough to secure early retirement within a decade. In a <a href="https://x.com/davincij15/status/1845277571757506801">post</a> on X (formerly Twitter), Jeremie urged followers to work hard until they acquire at least one Bitcoin, as the cryptocurrency's future value may provide financial independence. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Jeremie has long been an advocate for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a>, famously <a href="https://www.unilad.com/news/money/bitcoin-davinci-jeremie-where-is-he-now-320729-20230803">advising</a> people to invest when Bitcoin was just $1 per coin back in 2013. Now, with <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/08/bitcoin-price-predicted-to-reach-150000-by-year-end-says-fundstrats-tom-lee/">Bitcoin’s value</a> fluctuating around $63,000, his prediction continues to draw attention, especially as financial institutions project significant growth for the cryptocurrency in the next decade​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Industry experts have highlighted Bitcoin’s limited supply of 21 million coins as a key factor that could drive its value higher. Given the global adoption of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/16/hydro-cooling-technology-in-bitcoin-mining-a-green-solution-for-mexicos-energy-challenges/">Bitcoin</a> and the growing interest from institutional investors, some analysts believe Jeremie's prediction holds weight​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/21/microstrategy-is-a-synthetic-bitcoin-miner-according-to-max-harvey/">Bitcoin remains volatile</a>, its increasing acceptance by governments and corporations may contribute to long-term value appreciation.</p>
