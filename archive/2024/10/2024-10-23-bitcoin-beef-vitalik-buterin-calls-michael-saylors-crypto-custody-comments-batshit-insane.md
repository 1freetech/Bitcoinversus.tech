<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Ethereum</a> co-founder Vitalik Buterin has publicly <a href="https://decrypt.co/287881/vitalik-buterin-michael-saylors-bitcoin-self-custody-comments-are-batshit-insane">criticized</a> MicroStrategy’s <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/11/michael-saylor-explains-how-bitcoin-will-reshape-corporate-balance-sheets/">Michael Saylor</a> for advocating that crypto assets, such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/21/bitcoin-power-law-suggests-bitcoin-price-will-reach-100k-by-jan-2025/">Bitcoin</a>, should be held by large financial institutions like BlackRock and Fidelity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Buterin referred to Saylor’s approach as “batshit insane,” arguing that crypto's foundational value lies in decentralization, not institutional control​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Buterin emphasized that a regulatory capture strategy, where lawmakers and law enforcement would inherently support entities like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/17/blackrock-pushes-bitcoin-as-key-asset-at-brazils-digital-conference/">BlackRock</a> due to their custody of crypto assets, has historical precedents for failure. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":7437,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/image-1.png?w=663" alt="" class="wp-image-7437" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For Buterin, centralized custody undermines what <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/crypto4harris-town-hall-to-shape-us-crypto-policy/">crypto</a> is fundamentally about—individual self-sovereignty and decentralized control​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The remarks come as part of a broader backlash against Saylor, who in recent years has amassed over 250,000 <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/22/microstrategy-chairman-michael-saylor-highlights-bitcoin-performance-amid-institutional-acceptance/">Bitcoin for MicroStrategy</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Many in the crypto community, including Buterin, see Saylor’s shift towards institutional control as a step in the wrong direction, potentially jeopardizing the very ethos that gave birth to Bitcoin​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Buterin also acknowledged that some of his past views, such as promoting the "mountain man" trope of self-sufficiency in crypto, may be outdated due to advancements like zero-knowledge proofs and other technologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, he remains firm that regulatory capture, as endorsed by Saylor, is a dangerous route for the future of digital currencies​</p>
