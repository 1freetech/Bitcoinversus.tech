<p style="text-transform:none">NBA legend <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/06/scottie-pippen-inquires-about-bitcoin-on-social-media/">Scottie Pippen</a> recently made waves in the cryptocurrency community by predicting a significant rise in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin’s price</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://x.com/ScottiePippen/status/1831074924217438310">post</a> shared on X (formerly Twitter), Pippen claimed that Bitcoin’s anonymous creator, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/06/satoshi-nakamoto-stood-on-the-shoulders-of-cypherpunks/">Satoshi Nakamoto</a>, appeared to him in a dream, forecasting that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin</a> would hit $84,650 on November 5, 2024. His tweet quickly sparked excitement and skepticism alike.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pippen’s <a href="https://cryptopotato.com/interesting-bitcoin-price-prediction-from-nba-legend-scottie-pippen/">prediction</a> has stirred a range of reactions. Some fans embraced the forecast with optimism, while others questioned whether Pippen’s account had been hacked. Notably, at the time of his prediction, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> was trading around $56,800, meaning it would need to experience a significant rally of over 45% to hit the $84,650 mark.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The timing of this prediction is intriguing, as Bitcoin’s current market activity has been fluctuating, with some analysts suggesting that a potential Federal Reserve rate cut and the upcoming <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/crypto4harris-town-hall-to-shape-us-crypto-policy/">U.S. presidential election</a> could influence a Bitcoin rally. Former President <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/25/trumps-exclusive-bitcoin-2024-fundraiser-seats-priced-at-844600/">Donald Trump’s pro-crypto stance</a> and proposals to establish a national <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin reserve</a> might contribute to this upward trend if he wins the election. Conversely, even if <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/27/kamala-harris-aims-to-build-democratic-ties-with-crypto-community/">Kamala Harris </a>wins, the economy may go into a slight "panic" allowing bitcoin to rise because of perceived economic uncertainty. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Whether Pippen’s prediction will become reality remains to be seen, but it certainly adds an interesting twist to the conversation about <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/10/a-brief-analysis-on-luxors-hashprice-predictions-for-bitcoins-future/">Bitcoin's future</a>​.</p>
