<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) recently <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/chip-stocks-rally-as-tsmc-earnings-point-to-strong-ai-demand-8729657">projected</a> strong <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/chip-stocks-rally-as-tsmc-forecasts-strong-ai-demand-for-many-years-155420433.html">AI-driven demand</a> that is expected to persist for 'many years,' in a <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/news/chip-stocks-rally-as-tsmc-forecasts-strong-ai-demand-for-many-years-155420433.html">report</a> from yahoo finance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">TSMC's third-quarter earnings exceeded expectations, with net income surging over 50%. The company's optimistic forecast indicates that AI-related sales could triple in 2024, accounting for 15% of its revenue. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This announcement reassured investors following a brief sell-off in semiconductor stocks earlier in the week due to weaker projections from Dutch firm ASML​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">TSMC’s performance has been buoyed by growing demand for high-performance chips used in AI applications, especially as key industry players like Nvidia and Broadcom also experienced gains. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Nvidia, a leader in AI chip development, saw a 2% rise, while Broadcom gained more than 3% on the back of TSMC’s robust outlook​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the semiconductor industry navigates fluctuating demand in other sectors, the AI market continues to be a significant growth driver, with TSMC's outlook playing a key role in restoring market confidence​</p>
