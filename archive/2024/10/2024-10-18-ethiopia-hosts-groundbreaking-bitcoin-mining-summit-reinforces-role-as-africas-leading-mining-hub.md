<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, is buzzing with energy as the <a href="https://africanbitcoinmining.com/">Africa Bitcoin Mining Summit</a> kicked off on October 3, 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This exclusive event has drawn <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin miners</a>, manufacturers, and energy experts from across Africa and beyond, reflecting the country’s growing prominence in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/22/el-salvadors-lava-pool-a-volcanic-bitcoin-mining-facility/">cryptocurrency mining</a> landscape. Industry leaders like Gridless and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/19/luxor-releases-details-on-latest-os-update/">Luxor Technologies</a> are present, with Luxor highlighting Ethiopia’s leadership in Bitcoin hashrate, utilizing 600 megawatts (MW) of power for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">mining operations</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The summit offers a platform for African miners from countries like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/07/nigeria-emerges-as-a-global-leader-in-bitcoin-awareness/">Nigeria</a>, Malawi, and Zambia to exchange insights, alongside large-scale Chinese miners who continue to dominate the sector. Additionally, manufacturers such as Whatsminer are showcasing advancements in mining technology, crucial for maintaining efficiency in the face of rising demand​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ethiopia’s strategic push into Bitcoin mining stems from its abundant <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">renewable energy resources</a>, particularly hydroelectric power. With low energy costs—reported at approximately 3.14 cents per kilowatt-hour—Ethiopia has attracted international miners seeking affordable alternatives to regions like China, which has implemented strict regulations on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">crypto mining</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As the government continues to support digital infrastructure development, such as a recent $250 million partnership aimed at enhancing data mining capabilities, Ethiopia is poised to be a key player in the global Bitcoin market. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">While challenges remain, particularly around balancing mining activity with public energy needs, the summit is a clear signal that Ethiopia is embracing its role as a leader in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/29/ceo-charlene-fadirepo-shares-insight-on-africas-bitcoin-revolution/">African crypto mining revolution</a>​.</p>
