<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Pedro Domingos, a professor emeritus at the University of Washington and author of <em>The Master Algorithm</em> and <em>2040</em>, envisions a future where humans may achieve unprecedented lifespans, potentially living up to 500 years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/pmddomingos/status/1848067623457554884","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He suggests that such longevity could redefine human development stages, with people remaining in childhood until around 50. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In recent public statements, Domingos explained that rapid advancements in artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and nanotechnology might converge to slow or even reverse aging, leading to these prolonged life expectancies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Domingos, extended lifespans would allow humanity to adapt with larger cognitive capacities, expanding both the depth and duration of learning and self-discovery.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Domingos sees AI and medical innovations as central to this transformation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By integrating algorithms capable of diagnosing and even treating diseases at genetic and cellular levels, scientists could theoretically eliminate or dramatically reduce age-related diseases. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This approach would not only enhance health but could fundamentally change our understanding of aging, potentially enabling the body to rejuvenate itself. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Research continues to explore possibilities in this domain, as scientists aim to pinpoint cellular processes that accelerate degeneration and test experimental treatments that promote cellular repair.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
