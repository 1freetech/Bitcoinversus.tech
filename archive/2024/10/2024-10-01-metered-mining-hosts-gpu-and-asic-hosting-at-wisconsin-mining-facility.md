<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://www.meteredmining.io/">Metered Mining</a>, a notable player in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/22/energy-bitcoin-mining-facility-breaks-ground-in-ethiopia/">cryptocurrency mining</a> hosting arena, is setting growth plans into motion by doubling their facility capacity from 4MW to 8MW in Wisconsin.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This expansion reflects their commitment to advancing the scalability of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> operations for both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">GPU</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">ASIC miners</a>. By providing reliable infrastructure and efficient resources, they aim to support the thriving community of cryptocurrency miners. </p>
