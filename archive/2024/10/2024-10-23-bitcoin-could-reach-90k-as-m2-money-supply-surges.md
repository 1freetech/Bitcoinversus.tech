<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin continues to track global M2 money supply trends, and analysts are <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/m2-money-supply-bitcoin-cycle-market-analyst">forecasting</a> a potential surge in its price to $90,000 by the end of 2024. The M2 money supply, which measures cash and short-term bank deposits, has been a key indicator of Bitcoin's performance in past cycles. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">As liquidity accelerates, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin</a> is poised to benefit, with experts noting that the growing M2 supply historically correlates with major price movements for the cryptocurrency. This is particularly relevant as institutional investors are reportedly adding substantial amounts of Bitcoin to their portfolios, similar to the behavior seen before the 2020 rally​</p>
