<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cipher Mining Inc. has <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/en/news-release/2024/10/15/2963299/0/en/Cipher-Mining-Announces-the-Signing-of-Option-Agreements-to-Acquire-1-5-GW-of-Data-Center-Sites-in-West-and-North-Texas.html">secured</a> option agreements to acquire 1.5 gigawatts (GW) of data center sites in West and North Texas. This strategic move strengthens the company’s presence in large-scale infrastructure, focusing on both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">high-performance computing</a> (HPC) and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/22/expert-panel-debates-best-bitcoin-mining-cooling-methods-at-pacific-bitcoin-festival/">Bitcoin mining</a>. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The agreements target three distinct locations, each designed to host a capacity of 500 megawatts (MW), setting Cipher up for massive operational scalability.​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With this acquisition, Cipher is poised to expand its self-mining hashrate, expected to reach approximately 13.5 EH/s by the end of 2024 and potentially 35 EH/s by 2025. This operational growth aligns with the company’s broader objective to lead in both <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/metaplanet-acquires-%c2%a5500-million-worth-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/02/coreweave-core-scientific-deal-key-indicator-ai-boosts-profitability-for-bitcoin-miners/">HPC</a> sectors​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">The company's developments also <a href="https://app.researchpool.com/company/cifr-us17253j1060-cipher-mining-inc-sYmpLs92P0/press/cipher-mining-inc-cifr-cipher-mining-provides-second-quarter-2024-business-update-RG56SDgBRy">include</a> ongoing construction at its Black Pearl site, which is scheduled to deliver an additional 300 MW of capacity by mid-2025. Cipher Mining remains on track to finalize the acquisition of the Reveille data center, further bolstering its infrastructure by another 200 MW​.</p>
