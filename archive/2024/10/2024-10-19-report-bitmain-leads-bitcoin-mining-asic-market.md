<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/08/bitmain-extends-hashboard-life-with-new-protective-temp-sensor-coating/">Bitmain</a> remains the dominant leader in the ASIC market with a substantial 65-75% share. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/18/bitfarms-releases-footage-offers-virtual-tour-of-hydro-cooled-mining-site-in-paraguay/">MicroBT</a> is the closest competitor, with market share ranging from 20-35%. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/12/analyzing-centralization-and-monopolies-in-the-bitcoin-industry/">Canaan</a> holds a smaller share of the market but continues to compete through its AvalonMiner series.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Each company leverages their relationships with chip foundries and their latest models to cater to miners globally. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The current market is shaped by technological<br>advancements in efficiency and the competitive pricing of units based on <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/13/energy-how-to-enhance-data-center-efficiency/">energy efficiency</a>,<br>which is measured in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/25/antminer-s21-xp-hydro-set-for-release-between-october-and-december-2024/">Joules per Terahash</a> (J/TH).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Furthermore, the main factor impacting the pricing of an <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/15/how-to-replace-an-s19-kpro-120th-psu/">ASIC machine</a> is its energy efficiency.<br>As shown in the figure below, we can determine that the energy efficiency of the chip directly<br>affects the price of the machine. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The lower the J/TH ratio, the higher the price of the machine. Conversely, the higher the J/TH (i.e., the less efficient the machine), the lower the price.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The price of the most efficient machine (under 19 J/TH) is 85.77% higher than the least efficient<br>machines currently on the market (38 to 68 J/TH). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><strong>Additional Factors Impacting Bitcoin ASIC Prices</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitcoin Market Price<strong>: </strong>When <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/28/the-bullish-implications-of-bitcoins-rising-hashrate/">Bitcoin's price</a> is high, mining becomes more profitable, which increases the demand for mining hardware, driving up prices. Conversely, when Bitcoin's price drops, demand for mining machines can fall, causing a decline in prices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mining Difficulty:<strong> </strong>As more miners join the network, the difficulty to mine Bitcoin increases, which drives demand for more efficient ASICs. A higher difficulty can lead to higher prices for machines that provide a competitive edge in efficiency</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Chip Supply and Manufacturing<strong>:</strong> Dependence on chip foundries (TSMC, Samsung, SMIC) can cause price fluctuations based on supply chain disruptions and technological advancements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Shipping and Import Costs: Logistics, shipping rates, and insurance contribute to overall costs, especially for international transactions.</p>
