<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Schneider Electric, a global leader in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/dallc2b7e-2024-10-20-17.20.09-a-modern-nuclear-power-plant-depicted-in-a-comic-book-style-with-bold-outlines-vibrant-colors-and-dynamic-shading.-the-cooling-towers-are-sleek-and.webp">energy management</a> and automation, has <a href="https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/17/schneider_850m_stake_motivair/">announced</a> a significant acquisition of a 75% controlling stake in Motivair Corp, a specialist in liquid cooling technology for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/15/cipher-mining-expands-with-1-5-gw-data-center-sites-option-in-texas/">high-performance computing systems</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The $850 million all-cash deal reflects Schneider Electric’s ongoing strategy to strengthen its portfolio in data center technologies and meet the growing demands of high-density computing systems fueled by artificial intelligence and large-scale data processing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Motivair, based in Buffalo, New York, brings decades of expertise in liquid cooling systems designed for supercomputers and data centers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The company’s technology enables efficient thermal management for high-power computing environments, ensuring energy-efficient operations that align with Schneider Electric’s sustainability initiatives. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The acquisition also positions Schneider Electric to offer advanced cooling solutions in response to increasing power demands, driven by AI models and next-gen data centers​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The deal is expected to close in the coming quarters, subject to regulatory approvals. Schneider Electric plans to fully acquire the remaining 25% stake in Motivair by 2028, enhancing its competitive edge in the data center cooling market​.</p>
