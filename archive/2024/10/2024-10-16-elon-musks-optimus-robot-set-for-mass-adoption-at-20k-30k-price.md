<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tesla’s Optimus humanoid <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/07/26/gradually-then-suddenly-how-ai-and-robots-are-reshaping-the-workforce/">robot</a> took the spotlight at the "We, Robot" event in October 2024, offering a glimpse into the future of robotics. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Optimus was <a href="https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/tesla-unveils-optimus-robot-elon-musk-claims-biggest-product-ever-with-everyday-human-task-capabilities-449619-2024-10-11">designed</a> for both personal and industrial use, capable of performing tasks like serving drinks, carrying packages, and walking pets. Elon Musk emphasized the robot’s versatility, predicting widespread adoption in households and industries​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Optimus is <a href="https://www.techopedia.com/tesla-optimus-robot-all-you-need-to-know">projected</a> to cost between $20,000 and $30,000, making it more affordable than many robots in the market today. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Tesla</a> aims to scale production and eventually make the robot available for middle-class homes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Key features include tactile sensors, faster walking speeds, and 22-movement hand dexterity, all powered by Tesla’s advanced AI technology​.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Beyond domestic tasks, Optimus could revolutionize industries like manufacturing and logistics. Tesla is already testing the robot in its factories, using it to assist with repetitive tasks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Musk believes Optimus could tap into a $20 trillion market, generating over $1 trillion in annual profits once mass production is achieved​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Tesla's Optimus humanoid robot is expected to begin rolling out for public purchase by the end of 2025, with a broader release and higher production planned for 2026. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/trending-elon-musk-interviews-trump-on-x-com-et/">Elon Musk</a> has stated that Optimus will initially be used within Tesla's own factories by the end of this year, where it will assist with tasks like moving parts and handling repetitive functions.</p>
