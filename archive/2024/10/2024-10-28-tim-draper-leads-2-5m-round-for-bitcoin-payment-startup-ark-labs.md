<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Billionaire investor Tim Draper has <a href="https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/tim-draper-leads-2-5-130800350.html">spearheaded</a> a $2.5 million pre-seed funding round for Ark Labs, a promising startup focused on advancing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/19/taproot-enables-multi-asset-transactions-on-bitcoins-lightning-network/">Bitcoin transactions</a> by making them faster and more cost-effective. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Ark Labs, is working on a protocol designed to simplify <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/14/ferrari-accelerates-into-the-future-with-bitcoin-payments/">Bitcoin payments</a>, making the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/28/bitcoins-rising-value-outpaces-traditional-wage-growth/">cryptocurrency</a> more accessible for everyday global commerce.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Draper, known for his investments in companies like SpaceX, Tesla, and Coinbase, emphasized the importance of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/29/vaneck-will-donate-5-of-bitcoin-etf-profits-to-support-bitcoin-core-developers/">Bitcoin</a> as a medium of exchange. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He believes that Ark Labs' technology will play a pivotal role in making <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/07/bitcoin-dada-is-empowering-ugandan-women-with-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> a viable option for daily transactions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The funding round also saw participation from notable investors like Fulgur Ventures, Axiom Capital, and angel investor Stephen Cole​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://x.com/ArkLabsHQ">Ark Labs</a> plans to use the funds to expand its team, enhance its technology, and establish strategic partnerships with merchants and developers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The startup's innovative Ark protocol aims to provide seamless, non-custodial <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/03/river-financial-sheds-light-on-lightning-networks-potential/">Bitcoin payments</a> without requiring deep technical knowledge from users.</p>
