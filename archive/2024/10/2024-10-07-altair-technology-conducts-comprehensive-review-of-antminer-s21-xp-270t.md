<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Antminer S21 XP 270T, announced in June 2024 by Bitmain, has quickly made waves in the cryptocurrency mining sector. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This latest iteration of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/21/theory-antminer-s21e-xp-hyd-3u-will-be-the-first-bitcoin-miner-capable-of-1-ph-s/">Antminer</a> series improves mining efficiency by an impressive 37% compared to its predecessor, the S19 XP. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The release of the first batch was met with high expectations, as it sets new performance standards for competitors such as Bitdeer, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/01/ocean-unveiled-jack-dorseys-new-wave-in-bitcoin-mining/">Block</a>, Auradine, and MicroBT. The full release is expected in the first week of October.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">One of the standout features of the Antminer S21 XP is its enhanced hashboards. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These aluminum-backed hashboards are equipped with 91 chips per board, significantly more than the 65 chips found on the S21 Pro. Each chip hashes at approximately 1 Th, although this is about 20% lower than the per-chip hashrate on the S21 Pro. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Bitmain's focus on improved efficiency at lower clock frequencies is evident in this design choice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, the S21 XP uses an Amlogic A113D control board, similar to previous models, but with one notable difference: the inclusion of a 0.91" I2C OLED display.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Performance tests show the Antminer S21 XP hashing at 273T while consuming 3684W, achieving a real-world efficiency of 13.49 J/T, which is about 12% better than the S21 Pro. Moreover, the fans were operating at only 60% capacity during testing, suggesting potential for overclocking.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This unit is part of a small, early-release batch, with global shipments beginning soon. It has undoubtedly raised the bar in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/10/theory-what-if-blackrock-controlled-bitcoin-mining/">Bitcoin mining industry</a>.</p>
