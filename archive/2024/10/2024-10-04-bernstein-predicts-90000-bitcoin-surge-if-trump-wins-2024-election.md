<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a <a href="https://finbold.com/bernstein-predicts-bitcoin-price-to-90000-on-trump-victory-best-crypto-to-buy-now/">recent analysis</a> from the $725 billion asset management firm Bernstein, Bitcoin may reach unprecedented levels if <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/09/politics-donald-trump-announces-elon-musk-will-lead-government-efficiency-commission/">Donald Trump</a> secures the U.S. presidency again in 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The firm projects Bitcoin could surge to $90,000, significantly exceeding its previous all-time high of $73,000, which was set earlier this year. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This prediction is rooted in Trump’s pro-crypto stance, with his administration promising a more favorable regulatory environment for cryptocurrencies. Trump has also hinted at establishing Bitcoin as a key strategic reserve asset​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">On the other hand, if <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">Kamala Harris</a> were to win, Bernstein warns that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/01/bitcoiner-shares-michael-saylors-epic-quote-on-bitcoin-education/">Bitcoin</a> could fall to as low as $30,000 due to her administration’s less enthusiastic stance on cryptocurrencies. Harris’s lack of clear crypto policy leaves many in the industry concerned about future regulatory headwinds.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This significant market reaction highlights the impact the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/11/politics-kamala-harris-campaign-launches-pro-crypto-initiative/">2024 election</a> could have on the cryptocurrency space, with Trump's victory seen as a potential game-changer for Bitcoin and the broader industry.</p>
