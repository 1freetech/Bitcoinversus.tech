<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/05/bitcoin-is-the-lebron-james-of-digital-assets/">Los Angeles Lakers</a> Rookie <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/26/born-to-win-the-privileged-success-behind-alexandr-wang-and-bronny-james/">Bronny James</a> continues to <a href="https://www.sportingnews.com/us/nba/news/bronny-james-preseason-stats-game-logs-lakers-2024/87d4824330471a64c97c2fb8">face challenges</a> in his first preseason with the Los Angeles Lakers as he heads into the upcoming game against the Phoenix Suns. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the 2024 preseason so far, he has logged 44 minutes across four games, scoring just four points while committing five turnovers. His shooting performance has been a particular struggle, going 2-for-16 from the field, including 0-for-7 from beyond the arc. His shooting struggles and turnovers have contributed to a -40 plus/minus, reflecting his impact on the court.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His draft profile emphasized his strong defensive instincts and basketball IQ, projecting him as a versatile 3-and-D guard with the ability to contribute in transition and on the defensive end. Despite these strengths, concerns about his shooting consistency and offensive efficiency were highlighted, signaling areas for improvement as he adjusts to the NBA level​.</p>
