<p style="text-transform:none">BlackRock <a href="https://www.techopedia.com/news/blackrock-jay-jacobs-cryptocurrency-adoption-fastest-growing-tech-in-years">made headline</a>s at the 2024 Digital Assets Conference in Brazil by showcasing the increasing <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/03/senator-cynthia-lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill/">importance of Bitcoin</a> in modern investment portfolios. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Jay Jacobs, BlackRock's U.S. Head of Thematic and Active ETFs, emphasized that Bitcoin adoption is outpacing the growth of other major technologies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">He <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnZSRQjkRos&amp;t=1s">highlighted</a> how digital currencies are rapidly emerging as a monetary alternative, driven by growing skepticism towards traditional financial institutions​</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At the event, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/02/former-blackrock-director-weighs-in-on-possible-bitcoin-spot-etf/">BlackRock</a> underscored the role of Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation, presenting it alongside traditional assets like gold. The company detailed how the purchasing power of fiat currencies has steadily declined, positioning Bitcoin as a valuable alternative in the face of economic instability. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Jacobs explained, the introduction of regulated <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin exchange-traded funds</a> (ETFs) like IBIT in Brazil has democratized access to cryptocurrency investments​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Brazil becomes a pivotal market for Bitcoin through <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/10/blackrocks-bitcoin-etf-surpasses-20-billion/">BlackRock's ETF offerings</a>, institutional and retail investors alike are beginning to integrate Bitcoin into their portfolios, signaling a major shift in global financial markets​.</p>
