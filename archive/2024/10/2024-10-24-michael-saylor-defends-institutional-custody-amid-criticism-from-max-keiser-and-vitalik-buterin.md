<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the wake of strong reactions from key figures in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">Bitcoin community</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">Michael Saylor</a> has reaffirmed his stance on Bitcoin self-custody. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Notably, Saylor faced backlash from prominent figures like Max Keiser, a senior Bitcoin advisor to <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">El Salvador</a>, and Ethereum founder <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">Vitalik Buterin</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The debate centers around Saylor's views on how countries like El Salvador manage their Bitcoin holdings without generating yield, a point that Max Keiser sharply criticized. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a post from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/sports-max-keiser-to-host-bitcoin-golf-event-in-january-2025/">Max Keiser</a>, he stated: </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"Saylor's the type of guy who think the stripper really likes him (or, he has Stockholm Syndrome) Dude!!! Wake up! They tried to put you in prison in 2020, Remember?"</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://twitter.com/VersusInstitute/status/1848412134457090140","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"twitter","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor <a href="https://x.com/saylor/status/1849132234692833436">responded</a> on social media, reaffirming his belief in a flexible approach to Bitcoin custody. He stated, </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">"I support self-custody for those willing &amp; able, the right to self-custody for all, and freedom to choose the form of custody &amp; custodian for individuals &amp; institutions globally. #Bitcoin benefits from all forms of investment by all types of entities, and should welcome everyone"​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Meanwhile, Vitalik Buterin weighed in on Saylor's stance on self-custody calling him "batshit insane."</p>
