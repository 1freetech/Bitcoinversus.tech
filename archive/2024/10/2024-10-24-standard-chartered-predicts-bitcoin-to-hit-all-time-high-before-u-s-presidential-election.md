<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">According to Standard Chartered, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin</a> may see a new all-time high of $74,000 before the U.S. presidential election in November 2024. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Geoff Kendrick, the head of digital assets research at the bank, attributes this <a href="https://www.cryptopolitan.com/bitcoin-all-time-high-before-us-election/">forecast</a> to several key factors, including a steeper U.S. Treasury yield curve, growing interest in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/08/wells-fargo-invests-in-bitcoin-etfs/">Bitcoin exchange-traded funds</a> (ETFs), and the possibility of Donald Trump’s reelection​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">One of the driving forces behind Bitcoin's price surge is institutional investment, with large bets placed on Bitcoin reaching $80,000 by the end of December 2024. The increasing popularity of Bitcoin ETFs has also resulted in significant inflows, further supporting upward price momentum​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Additionally, recent market activity suggests that short-term holders who were previously at a loss are now seeing profits, which could push more traders into the market. However, Bitcoin may face resistance at the $68,000 level, and if it fails to break through, a price correction could occur​.</p>
