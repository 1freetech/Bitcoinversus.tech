<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In the early days of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/top-secret-bitcoin-mining-machine-revealed-in-west-texas/">Bitcoin mining</a>, hobbyists and crypto enthusiasts mined <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/14/italian-bank-banca-sella-introduces-bitcoin-trading/">Bitcoin</a> using just a standard computer equipped with a multi-core CPU. Back in 2009, these <a href="https://thenextweb.com/news/a-brief-history-of-bitcoin-mining-hardware">setups</a> earned miners 50 BTC per block with minimal competition and computing power requirements. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Mining was relatively simple and accessible, allowing participants to secure the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/19/taproot-enables-multi-asset-transactions-on-bitcoins-lightning-network/">Bitcoin network</a> and earn rewards using basic hardware​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Bitcoin gained popularity, miners quickly transitioned to more powerful Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) around 2010. GPUs, initially designed for rendering video game graphics, significantly outperformed CPUs in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">mining efficiency</a> by performing more parallel calculations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">This shift increased the network's difficulty, making <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">GPU mining </a>the norm as miners sought better performance​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By 2013, the introduction of Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs) revolutionized Bitcoin mining. <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">ASICs</a>, built exclusively for Bitcoin mining, dramatically outpaced <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">GPUs</a> in terms of both speed and energy efficiency. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The advent of ASICs marked a shift toward industrial-scale mining operations, with higher barriers to entry due to increased costs and hardware specialization. </p>
