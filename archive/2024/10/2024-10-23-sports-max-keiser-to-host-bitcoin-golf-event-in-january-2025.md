<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">El Salvador</a> is set to host a landmark event for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/29/bitcoiner-explains-negativity-within-traditional-finance-towards-bitcoin/">Bitcoin enthusiasts</a>, the first-ever Max &amp; Stacy Bitcoin Golf Invitational. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Scheduled for January 10th and 11th, 2025, the event will take place at the luxurious El Encanto Country Club. El Encanto offers a blend of finesse and challenge with its natural hazards and breathtaking landscapes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The event is more than just a golf tournament; it's a celebration of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/08/16/bitcoin-city-institutional-titans-rally-behind-el-salvadors-bitcoin-vision/">Bitcoin's growing influence in the country</a>. Max Keiser and Stacy Herbert, prominent Bitcoin advocates and advisors to President Nayib Bukele, have been pivotal in shaping El Salvador's Bitcoin-centric policies. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">Their efforts to position the country as a global hub for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/01/bitcoin-advocate-femi-longe-wants-the-world-to-price-their-goods-and-services-in-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> have attracted international attention, and this golf invitational is set to further strengthen the bond between <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/05/can-bitcoin-make-el-salvador-the-singapore-of-central-america/">Bitcoin and El Salvador</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With the growing interest in Bitcoin pioneer<a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/02/theory-bitcoin-mining-in-outer-space-will-increase-hashrate-efficiency/">Theory: Bitcoin Mining in Outer Space Will Increase Hashrate Efficiency</a> and El Salvador's unique position as a <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/04/power-is-money-the-economical-physics-of-bitcoin/">Bitcoin pioneer</a>, the event is expected to draw participants from around the world.</p>
