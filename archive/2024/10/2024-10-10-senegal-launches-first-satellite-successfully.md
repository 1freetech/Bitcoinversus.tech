<p style="text-transform:none">Senegal <a href="https://x.com/PR_Diomaye/status/1824567409107411252">achieved</a> a historic milestone on August 16, 2024, by launching its first satellite, GAINDESAT-1A, into orbit. The satellite was launched from Vandenberg Space Force Base in California, USA, aboard SpaceX’s Falcon 9 rocket as part of the Transporter-11 rideshare mission.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">GAINDESAT-1A was <a href="https://www.techafricanews.com/2024/08/19/senegal-launches-first-satellite-gaindesat-1a-enters-the-space-age/">developed</a> by Senegalese engineers in collaboration with the Montpellier University Space Centre (CSUM) in France. The satellite is expected to play a crucial role in various sectors, including climate monitoring, natural disaster management, and agricultural oversight. It will also support national security and improve air and water quality tracking​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">President Bassirou Diomaye Faye highlighted the significance of this achievement, emphasizing that it marks a major step towards Senegal's "technological sovereignty." He expressed his pride and gratitude to all those involved in the project, which has been in development for five years​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The launch of GAINDESAT-1A is expected to have far-reaching benefits, including enhanced weather forecasting, better management of water resources, and strengthened defense capabilities. Senegal's space agency, SENSAT, led the project, which aims to further the country's technological progress and independence​.</p>
