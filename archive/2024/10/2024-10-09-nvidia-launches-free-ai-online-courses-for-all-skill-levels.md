<p style="text-transform:none">NVIDIA has just released a series of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/09/my-red-tea-infusion-full-python-course-review-final-score/">free online courses</a> designed to provide comprehensive training in <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/20/microsofts-ai-expert-jaron-lanier-advocates-for-data-dignity-amid-bitcoin-revolution/">Artificial Intelligence</a>. These courses cater to a wide range of learners, from beginners to advanced professionals, and focus on topics such as deep learning, accelerated computing, and data science. With no payment required, these courses offer an accessible opportunity to enhance skills in AI and related fields.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">One of the standout courses is "Building a Brain in 10 Minutes," which guides learners through the creation of a simple neural network. This course is particularly notable for its user-friendly approach, making it ideal for those new to AI concepts. Additionally, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/09/soluna-unleashes-nvidia-gpus-for-ai-targets-80-million/">NVIDIA</a> offers courses on generative AI, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/12/ai-and-bitcoin-mining-sectors-drive-new-energy-demands-for-data-centers/">AI in data centers</a>, and advanced techniques for AI-driven video analytics. Each course is crafted to ensure that participants gain both theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience using NVIDIA’s cutting-edge tools​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">NVIDIA's initiative extends beyond individual learning. The company has also partnered with the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/02/california-dmv-digitizes-car-titles-with-blockchain-technology/">state of California</a> to provide AI training sessions aimed at students, educators, and workers. This partnership highlights NVIDIA's commitment to promoting AI literacy and job creation, ensuring that residents of California have the skills necessary to succeed in an AI-driven future​.</p>
