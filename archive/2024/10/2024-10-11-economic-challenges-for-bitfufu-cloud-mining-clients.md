<h3 class="wp-block-heading"></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">BitFuFu, a leading provider of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/06/the-luxor-hashprice-formula-is-a-new-way-to-interpret-bitcoins-value/">cloud-based bitcoin mining</a>, faced <a href="https://theminermag.com/news/2024-08-20/bitfufu-cloud-mining-bitcoin-q2/">significant</a> economic challenges in the second quarter of 2024, as revealed in their latest financial report. Despite achieving considerable revenue growth, the company's cloud mining clients experienced substantial losses, highlighting the volatile nature of the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/bitcoin-miner-revenue-bottoming-out-signals-potential-bull-market/">cryptocurrency mining market</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">During this quarter, BitFuFu <a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/08/20/2932687/0/en/BitFuFu-Reports-Unaudited-Second-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results.html">reported</a> that its cloud mining clients incurred an average production cost of $60,534 per <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">bitcoin</a>, at a time when the average market price of bitcoin was approximately $65,000. This resulted in a gross loss margin of 38%, underscoring the financial pressures faced by clients in the current crypto-economic environment. These figures reflect the broader industry trend of increasing operational and electricity costs, further exacerbated by the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/23/the-bitcoin-halving-is-otw-heres-what-you-need-to-know/">bitcoin halving event</a> earlier in April, which reduced mining rewards by half.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The report also indicated a significant rise in the company's total revenue, reaching $129.4 million, up from $76.3 million in the same period last year. This growth was supported by an expansion in both the company’s hosting capacity and total mining capacity, which now stands at 24.7 exahashes per second, marking an increase of over 60%.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite these challenges, BitFuFu <a href="https://uk.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/BITFUFU-INC-166577824/news/BitFuFu-Reports-Unaudited-Second-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results-47690978/">continues</a> to expand its user base and operational scale, suggesting a resilient business model in the face of ongoing market fluctuations​.</p>
