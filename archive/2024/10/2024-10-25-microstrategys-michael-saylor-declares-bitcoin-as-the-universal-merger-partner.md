<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In a recent <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj6x0mcENYo">interview</a> with "Scarce Assets," <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/15/michael-saylor-quantifies-why-bitcoin-outshines-golds-stability/">Michael Saylor</a> emphasized Bitcoin’s critical role in revitalizing struggling companies. During the discussion, Saylor argued that <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/11/michael-saylor-of-microstrategy-forecasts-bitcoin-as-the-leading-asset-class/">Bitcoin</a> is the "universal merger partner" and a key solution for "zombie companies" in the Russell 2000. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">His recommendation? Recapitalize these businesses using Bitcoin to ensure their survival. According to Saylor, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin</a> provides an unparalleled financial advantage, solidifying its place as a long-term asset that will continue to dominate global markets​.</p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor's views align with the mission of "Scarce Assets," a podcast hosted by finance professionals Andy Edstrom and Jesse Myers. The show explores Bitcoin's emerging role in financial strategies, especially as wealth managers seek to outperform their peers in the coming decades. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Saylor's insights highlight Bitcoin as a viable hedge against inflation and a store of value that could surpass traditional assets like gold and real estate​</p>

He mentioned that demand for these financial products significantly exceeds the daily supply from miners, setting the stage for further growth in Bitcoin’s value​.</p>
