<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Earlier this year, Ethiopian Investment Holdings (EIH), a state-owned entity, <a href="https://www.forbesafrica.com/crypto/2024/02/22/ethiopia-to-become-the-first-african-country-to-start-bitcoin-mining/">initiated a partnership</a> with Hong Kong's West Data Group’s Center Service PLC, marking a significant leap into <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/07/01/bitcoin-miner-defi-django-reveals-modifications-for-airflow-optimization/">Bitcoin mining</a>. This collaboration is poised to bolster Ethiopia's presence in the global data center market, a sector projected to expand to $5.4 billion by 2027. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The project is set against the backdrop of Ethiopia's vast energy resources, including plans for expansive <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">hydroelectric power</a>. The country is constructing one of the world's largest hydro plants, which is expected to substantially increase its power generation capacity, thereby supporting high-energy-consuming operations like <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/14/auradine-announces-teraflux-2800-series-bitcoin-miners/">Bitcoin mining</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the promising outlook, there are challenges, particularly concerning the regulation of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/10/bitcoin-scholarship-el-salvador-implements-bitcoin-mining-into-school-curriculum/">Bitcoin mining</a> and its impact on local energy supplies. Critics argue that while the sector could bring significant economic benefits, it might also strain Ethiopia’s electrical grid, which is still inaccessible to a large segment of the population.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">As Ethiopia navigates these waters, the potential economic uplift from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/08/12/bitcoin-miner-revenue-bottoming-out-signals-potential-bull-market/">Bitcoin mining</a> could be transformative, not just for the country but as a model for other <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/20/micro-grid-hydroelectric-bitcoin-mining-facilities-emerge-in-kenya/">African nations</a> with similar resources.</p>
