<p style="text-transform:none">Nick Garland, Chief Operating Officer at <a href="https://simplemining.io/">Simple Mining</a>, announced: <br><br>"Next week we will complete an additional 5 MW expansion which takes our total <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/08/prediction-bitcoin-hashrate-to-hit-new-all-time-highs-by-year-end/">hashrate</a> under management up to 50 MW.In November, we have an additional 10+ MW coming online as well, which keeps us on track for our target of 125+ MW by the end of Q1 2025." <br><br>Garland's statement reflects the company's aggressive growth strategy, signaling significant expansion in the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/01/metered-mining-hosts-gpu-and-asic-hosting-at-wisconsin-mining-facility/">Bitcoin mining industry</a>​.</p>
