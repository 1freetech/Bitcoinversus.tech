<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Sony Block Solutions Labs, in collaboration with Startale Labs, <a href="https://cryptobriefing.com/sony-ethereum-l2-soneium/">has announced</a> the development of Soneium, a cutting-edge <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/06/24/why-ethereum-is-the-brian-scalabrine-of-the-nba/">Ethereum</a> Layer-2 blockchain platform. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The platform will integrate with Sony’s diverse product lineup, including Sony Music, Sony Pictures, and Sony Bank, within the next two years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The testnet is expected to go live soon, providing developers with a robust environment for blockchain-based applications​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none">The Soneium platform offers innovative tools for developers and aims to push Web3 adoption across various sectors, including gaming, entertainment, and finance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Soneium's mission is to decentralize power and make blockchain technology a part of everyday life, promising lower transaction fees and faster processing speeds compared to traditional blockchain networks.</p>
