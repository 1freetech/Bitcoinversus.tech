<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Shedeur Sanders from Colorado has impressed with his passing skills, accumulating 2,018 passing yards with 17 touchdowns and a 72.6% completion rate. However, his rushing game has been less effective, totaling negative rushing yards. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Meanwhile, Jalen Milroe from Alabama showcases a more balanced attack, with 1,722 passing yards and 330 rushing yards, scoring 11 rushing touchdowns in addition to 13 through the air. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Cam Ward from Miami has been a standout and is my favorite qb of this year's draft with 2,538 passing yards, 24 touchdowns, and a strong 9.71 yards per play.</p>
