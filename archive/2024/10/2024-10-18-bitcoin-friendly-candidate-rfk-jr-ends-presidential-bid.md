<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/10/14/bitcoin-advocate-rfk-jr-sets-independent-course-for-2024-presidential-run/">Robert F. Kennedy Jr.</a> has <a href="https://www.jpost.com/breaking-news/article-815788">officially</a> ended his campaign for the U.S. Presidency. The decision was solidified after Kennedy withdrew his petition to appear on the Arizona ballot. According to the Arizona Secretary of State’s office, his campaign filed the necessary paperwork to remove his name from consideration. </p>

<p style="text-transform:none">Kennedy, who initially entered the race as a Democrat, faced challenges throughout his campaign. His support in the polls had steadily declined in recent months, falling from 10% to just 4%. The move to withdraw from the race also follows reports that he may be considering endorsing another candidate, though he has not confirmed these speculations​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">As Kennedy steps away from the campaign trail, he has left open the possibility of future involvement in the political sphere. "We are not talking about any of that," Kennedy commented when asked about potential endorsements, leaving the door open for further speculation on his next move​. </p>
