<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/07/crucial-graphic-highlights-dollar-depreciation-relative-to-real-estate-and-bitcoin/">Bitcoin</a> has <a href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/currencies/bitcoin-versus-us-home-prices-performed-5-years-crypto-housing-2021-12">shown</a> remarkable resilience and growth compared to traditional assets such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/07/crucial-graphic-highlights-dollar-depreciation-relative-to-real-estate-and-bitcoin/">real estate</a>, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/11/oxford-gold-group-faces-investor-complaints-over-missing-gold/">gold</a>, and consumer technology, like the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/09/25/bitcoin-appreciates-over-time-relative-to-the-iphone/">iPhone</a> and MacBook. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Over the past decade, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin</a> has significantly outperformed many of these assets, with the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/09/19/louisiana-adopts-bitcoin-cryptocurrency-for-state-payments/">cryptocurrency</a> gaining over 6,100% between 2017 and 2021, while US home prices increased by just 29% during the same period​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The significant gap in value seen between a 2010 MacBook Pro worth 17.129 BTC and a 2023 MacBook Pro valued at just 0.034 BTC underscores how far <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/19/report-bitmain-leads-bitcoin-mining-asic-market/">Bitcoin</a> has come in maintaining purchasing power against consumer technology​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In recent years, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/02/08/bitcoin-leapfrogs-silver-in-etf-markets/">Bitcoin's market cap</a> has been growing closer to that of gold and real estate. Analysts predict that, especially after the next Bitcoin halving in 2024, Bitcoin's market cap could approach the $10 trillion mark, a valuation currently held by gold. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Some <a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2021/11/08/heres-when-bitcoin-could-overtake-gold-and-real-estate-according-to-quant-analyst-planb/">believe</a> that by 2028, Bitcoin could surpass both gold and real estate due to its increasing scarcity and the growing demand for digital assets as stores of value​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The limited supply and high returns of Bitcoin are attracting investors who once solely focused on gold and real estate.</p>
