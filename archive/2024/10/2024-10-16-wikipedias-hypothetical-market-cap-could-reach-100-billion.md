<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In recent <a href="https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/how-much-is-wikipedia-worth-704865/">discussions</a> surrounding the monetization of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/26/shopinbit-is-leading-the-bitcoin-e-commerce-revolution-in-europe/">online platforms</a>, Wikipedia, one of the most visited websites globally, has emerged as a focal point. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Wikipedia attracts an impressive 1.7 billion unique visitors each month, generating roughly 240 billion page views annually. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">With this level of user engagement, Wikipedia ranks alongside major tech giants in terms of daily web traffic. If the platform implemented advertising, even conservatively, it could generate substantial revenue. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">At an <a href="https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/how-much-is-wikipedia-worth-704865/">estimate</a>d Revenue Per Mille (RPM) of $5, Wikipedia could achieve $1.2 billion in yearly revenue. With operating costs estimated at $200 million annually, it could potentially see $1 billion in net profit each year​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Valuing Wikipedia based on a Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio of 25, similar to other tech companies, could position its market capitalization at $25 billion. However, a Price-to-Sales (P/S) ratio of 9 might yield a lower valuation of around $10.8 billion​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These figures are supported by comparisons to similar platforms like Reddit, which was valued at $10 billion, and Twitter, which reached a market cap of $30-40 billion before its acquisition​.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The hypothetical valuation of Wikipedia not only underscores the immense worth of <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/29/bitcoiner-explains-negativity-within-traditional-finance-towards-bitcoin/">user-generated content</a> but also highlights the potential revenue streams untapped by its current non-profit model.</p>
