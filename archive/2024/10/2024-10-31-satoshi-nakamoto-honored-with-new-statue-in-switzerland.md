<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The Plan ₿ Initiative, a collaboration between <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/12/21/tether-leverages-stablecoin-success-makes-500-million-investment-in-bitcoin-mining-infrastructure/">Tether</a> and the city of Lugano, Switzerland, has <a href="https://bitcoinmagazine.com/takes/satoshi-nakamoto-statue-in-lugano-is-inspiring-">introduced</a> a remarkable statue honoring Bitcoin’s enigmatic creator, Satoshi Nakamoto. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Revealed during the 3rd Annual Plan ₿ Forum, this statue features a disappearing effect designed to symbolize Nakamoto’s mystery and anonymity in the cryptocurrency world. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Located at Villa Ciani in Lugano, the statue attracted <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2023/11/25/bitcoins-growth-potential-spotlighted-on-x-com/">Bitcoin advocates</a>, government officials, and technology enthusiasts, who gathered to celebrate the influence of Nakamoto’s work on financial innovation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://dailyhodl.com/2024/10/25/tethers-plan-b-initiative-unveils-new-satoshi-nakamoto-statue-in-lugano-switzerland/">According</a> to Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino, the statue is more than a tribute; it embodies the spirit of decentralization that Bitcoin represents, pointing to Nakamoto’s choice to fade from public life and let the <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/10/23/bitcoin-beef-vitalik-buterin-calls-michael-saylors-crypto-custody-comments-batshit-insane/">Bitcoin community</a> take the lead. Lugano Mayor Michele Foletti also remarked on the statue’s symbolism, seeing it as a fitting tribute in a city that aims to become a blockchain and cryptocurrency hub in Europe​.</p>
