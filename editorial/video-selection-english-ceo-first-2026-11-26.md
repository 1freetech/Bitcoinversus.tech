# Video selection — English and executive context first

Effective November 26, 2026. Owner-directed editorial rule for BitcoinVersus.tech news and lessons.

- Default to English-language audio, not merely English titles or subtitles. Confirm language through reliable metadata/transcript or playback where available; do not guess. If no suitable English video can be verified, use a credited primary-source alternative or omit the video and disclose the limitation.
- For company stories, first search for relevant CEO/founder interviews, prioritizing popular (compare available public view counts), recent and substantive English-language interviews. Relevance and credibility outrank popularity alone. If no suitable interview exists, prefer official keynotes, engineering presentations, product demonstrations or reputable English-language interviews with technical leaders.
- Distinguish broad company context from evidence supporting specific benchmark or product claims. Caption the speaker, source, date and relevance accurately.
- Use a native Gutenberg YouTube block and direct source link. Confirm save by rereading the post. Claim playback and responsive rendering only after actual desktop/mobile testing; disclose unavailable tests.
- Reference example: Intel D270 post 35582, replaced video with https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TMGyj5BFHv0 (Lip-Bu Tan interviewed by Gavin Baker, TomorrowX Summit, iConnections).
