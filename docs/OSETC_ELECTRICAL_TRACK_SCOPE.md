# OSETC Track Scope — Electrical Technician Only

Effective: 2026-10-22. Applies to BitcoinVersus.Tech / Open-Source Electrical Technician Certification.

**OSETC means Open-Source Electrical Technician Certification.** Its core instruction must be electrical: circuits, meters, safe work practices, conductors, wiring, grounding/bonding, overcurrent protection, relays, contactors, electric motors, starters, electrical panels, controls, insulation resistance, calibration of electrical instruments, electrical troubleshooting, and electrical maintenance.

**Do not classify networking or industrial communications as a primary OSETC topic.** Dedicated lessons on Ethernet cabling/switching, TCP/IP, Modbus TCP, OPC UA, PROFINET, EtherCAT, EtherNet/IP, SNMP, and protocol frames/client-server systems belong in the appropriate networking or industrial-communications curriculum. Discuss a protocol in OSETC only where incidental to an electrical measurement/control skill, not as the main topic.

Before any new OSETC publication:
1. Compare the proposed subject with the definition above; route out-of-scope subjects elsewhere.
2. Check all OSETC lesson numbers and the WordPress/GitHub archive for duplication and preserve 000–999 progression.
3. Verify a unique electrical-specific 1200 × 630 featured cover, at least one working YouTube embed, at least one authentic functioning social embed, relevant internal references, accessible formatting and mandatory donation QR footer.
4. Check prepublication content and postpublication rendered media. Do not equate a link with a playable player, or a script reference with confirmed interactive playback.
5. Archive the final Gutenberg text and metadata in GitHub and confirm WordPress matches.
