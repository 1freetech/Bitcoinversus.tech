How to read this map: Each dot represents a ranked project, not a measurement of online generating capacity. State-ranking markers are presently positioned as approximate state-level locators, spaced to prevent collisions, rather than claiming an exact project address. America's Top 10 markers have locality-level positioning where identified, except where the article names only a state. Project capacity may represent generation, electricity service, gross site development, critical IT power, or another distinct figure. Never sum these different measurements as actual production. Original article links provide supporting evidence and status updates. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors. Mapping library: Leaflet.
